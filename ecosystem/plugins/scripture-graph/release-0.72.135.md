Adds four Book of Mormon events, each with portrait and landscape artwork:

- Mosiah 27: Alma and the Angel.
- Alma 14: Alma and Amulek Delivered.
- Helaman 5: Nephi and Lehi Encircled by Fire.
- 3 Nephi 1: The Night Without Darkness.

The events appear in Events → Event Backgrounds and automatically match their scripture chapters. Small accurate offline previews are included; full images load on demand.

Also delivers the previously prepared Three Days of Darkness, redone Christ at Bountiful, First Vision, Covenant of Peace, and Sacrament at Bountiful artwork. All artwork URLs were verified by size and SHA-256 before release.
