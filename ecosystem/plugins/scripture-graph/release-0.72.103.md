Add the requested laptop photo shortcuts.

Ctrl+P toggles photo-only view, hiding reading text and app controls and removing the reading tint. Press Ctrl+P again or Escape to return to the same page and scroll position. Ctrl+N cycles to the next background photo and saves that scene choice, including while photo-only view is active.

These shortcuts intentionally replace Obsidian's Ctrl+P/Ctrl+N behavior. Key repeat does not toggle repeatedly or skip photographs.

Validation: production build/typecheck and shortcut regression checks for capture, repeat handling, Escape, focus restoration and cleanup.
