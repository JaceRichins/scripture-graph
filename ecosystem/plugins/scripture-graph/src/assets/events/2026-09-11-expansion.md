# More Book of Mormon event backgrounds — September 11, 2026

Eight new images made with the built-in `image_gen` tool: four events, each independently composed for portrait (1024 × 1536) and landscape (1536 × 1024). Full-size JPEGs load on demand; 256 × 384 previews are bundled for offline use. These are devotional artistic interpretations, not documentary or archaeological reconstructions.

The night without darkness is shown as light as midday, following 3 Nephi 1:19, with a new star. The prison scene shows Alma and Amulek delivered after the collapse, with no victims shown. Helaman 5 shows the brothers unharmed within the fire. Mosiah 27 shows Alma and the four sons of Mosiah with the angel.

Chapter assignments feed the existing Events → Event Backgrounds gallery automatically. No large library download is required.

Exact prompts, original generated PNG paths, and scripture source links are saved in [the generation manifest](2026-09-11-expansion.json). JPEG encoding and preview resizing only change storage size; compositions were generated by the image tool.
