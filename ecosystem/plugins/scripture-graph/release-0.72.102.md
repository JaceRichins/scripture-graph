Laptop arrow keys now scroll and turn reading pages.

Up/Down scroll the active page; Left/Right move between scripture chapters across book boundaries, or through history on other library pages. Missing personal chapter notes fall back to the scripture itself, fetched on demand and opened in reading mode.

Typing, selections, dialogs, modified shortcuts and sidebar controls retain normal keyboard behavior. Held horizontal arrows do not skip chapters.

Validation: production build/typecheck, keyboard navigation and updater regression checks; chapter-opening checks with and without personal notes and with an unavailable server.
