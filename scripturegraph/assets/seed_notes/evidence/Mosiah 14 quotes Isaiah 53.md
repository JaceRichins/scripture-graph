# Mosiah 14 quotes Isaiah 53

> [!textual] The observation
> [[Mosiah 14]] reproduces the Suffering Servant song of [[Isaiah 53]]
> essentially verbatim (KJV wording), placed in Abinadi's trial speech as the
> scriptural core of his testimony of Christ — see the parallel verse map in
> the study guide's Related Scriptures section.

<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
**Observation (certain):** the quotation is verbatim-level; the engine's
deterministic parallel detector independently confirms dense verse-level
overlap between the two chapters.

**Significance (modest, honestly framed):** this demonstrates the Book of
Mormon's *internal claim* — Nephite access to Isaiah via the brass plates —
and shows how Abinadi reads Isaiah 53 christologically ([[Mosiah 15]]).
Because the wording tracks the KJV (including its translation choices), the
quotation is *not* independent evidence of an ancient Isaiah text tradition;
it is a datum about the translation's relationship to the English Bible,
discussed under the Translation evidence area.

**Does not establish:** an independent ancient textual witness to Isaiah.

**Alternative framings:** KJV dependence in quotation blocks is expected
under both believing translation models ("cultural translation" into the
English Bible register) and skeptical composition models — so by itself it
discriminates between them weakly.
<!-- SG:END summary -->
