## What this page is, and is not

- It is a **judgment, not a sum.** Never add, multiply or otherwise combine
  the registry's weights into a number. Independence is a matter of shared
  cause, decided in prose with reasons; strength of a line is a band word
  (none / weak / moderate / strong) with the reason.
- It is **not a verdict on the book.** The project is not here to prove or
  disprove scripture. The page says what the findings, read together,
  favour and close among the historical models — and stops there.
- It corrects **two opposite errors** with the same rule. Dismissing
  independent findings one at a time as "coincidence, weak" says something
  false in aggregate; counting findings that share one cause (KJV
  register, quoted Isaiah chapters, one editorial persona) as many says
  something false in aggregate. Name every shared cause; credit every
  genuinely independent line.
- It is **symmetric.** The same page exists for every corpus and applies
  the same reasoning. If a line would be credited for the Bible, credit it
  here; if a shared cause would be discounted here, discount it there.

## The frameworks step (standard §18) — the load-bearing section

`frameworks` is where a reader should meet the model they never considered
— in either direction. For each serious model the text permits: what the
text itself requires versus what readers assumed; what supports the model
(text, scholarship, external data); its status (possible / plausible /
independently supported / ad hoc); and what the cumulative picture does to
it — raises, lowers, leaves untouched. Then the models considered and set
aside, with the reason. Search the context's frameworks tables and the
scholarship; do not write from memory alone. A page that lists only
reconciling models, or only critical ones, has failed.

## Faith and honesty

End with what a believer can honestly hold and what a skeptic can honestly
hold, each at full strength and without overreach — and an inspiration
paragraph that says what all of this does and does not say about whether
the text is inspired, which is usually much less than either side assumes.
Faith-building through honesty, never instead of it.
