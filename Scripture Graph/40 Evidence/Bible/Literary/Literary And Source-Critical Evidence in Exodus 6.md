# Literary And Source-Critical Evidence in Exodus 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Exodus 6:3 says that God appeared to the patriarchs as God Almighty but was not known to them by the name JEHOVAH. This stands in prima facie literary tension with Genesis passages in which the narrative uses LORD and portrays earlier figures invoking the LORD's name. The observation is secure, while explanations of the relationship are disputed.

**Does not establish:** The tension alone does not prove a contradiction, identify exact documentary sources, or establish when the relevant passages were composed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statement may mean that the patriarchs did not know the full significance or experienced character associated with the name, rather than that they had never heard its syllables.
- Genesis may use the divine name retrospectively in the narrator's voice or in wording adapted for later readers.
- Source-critical models explain the difference through the combination of distinct textual traditions.
