# Internal Numerical Coherence in Numbers 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The redemption subsection is numerically coherent: 22,273 Israelite firstborn exceed the stated 22,000 Levites by 273, and 273 payments of five shekels equal the reported 1,365 shekels. This strongly supports deliberate accounting within this subsection.

**Does not establish:** The calculation does not by itself establish the historicity, date, scale, or circumstances of the census.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An author or editor could construct internally consistent figures without preserving a historical census.
- The arithmetic may reflect literary or administrative schematization rather than exact population data.
