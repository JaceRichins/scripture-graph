# Explicit Canonical Typology in Leviticus 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Paul explicitly identifies Christ with Passover and immediately applies unleavened-feast imagery to Christian conduct. This provides strong canonical evidence for a Christian typological reading of the adjacent Passover and Unleavened Bread ordinances.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every detail of Leviticus 23 was intended as a one-to-one prediction of Jesus's life or fix the historical chronology of the Crucifixion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be adapting familiar festival imagery pastorally without claiming that every detail in Leviticus predicts Christ.
- The analogy can depend on the broader Passover tradition rather than on Leviticus 23 alone.
