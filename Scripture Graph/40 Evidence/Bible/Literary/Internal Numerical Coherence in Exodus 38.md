# Internal Numerical Coherence in Exodus 38

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The silver figures are internally coherent under the account's implied conversion of 3,000 shekels per talent: 603,550 half-shekels equal 301,775 shekels, the same as 100 talents plus 1,775 shekels. The 100 talents are assigned to 100 sockets, while the remaining 1,775 shekels are assigned to pillar fittings.

**Does not establish:** The arithmetic does not independently establish the historicity, date, authorship, or precise modern weight of the materials.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor or compiler could have deliberately harmonized the totals.
- The numbers could function as idealized or stylized accounting rather than a surviving field record.
- Internal consistency alone cannot verify that the reported census or material quantities are historical.
