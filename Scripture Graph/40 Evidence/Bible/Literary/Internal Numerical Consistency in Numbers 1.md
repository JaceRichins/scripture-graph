# Internal Numerical Consistency in Numbers 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The twelve reported tribal subtotals add arithmetically to 603,550, exactly matching the chapter's stated aggregate. This establishes internal numerical consistency in the transmitted figures, but not their historical accuracy.

**Does not establish:** The agreement does not independently establish the census's date, scale, historicity, or the modern demographic meaning of the numbers.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor or copyist could have preserved or adjusted the aggregate to agree with the subtotals.
- Internally consistent figures may still be schematic, idealized, differently defined, or historically inaccurate.
