# Canonical Conceptual Connection in Leviticus 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Leviticus 17:11 supplies clear conceptual background for Hebrews 9's association of sacrificial blood with remission, although Hebrews incorporates that language into a distinct Christian argument about Christ.

**Does not establish:** The connection does not establish that Leviticus 17's original audience understood the verse as an explicit prediction of Jesus Christ.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Hebrews draws on the sacrificial system broadly rather than demonstrably quoting Leviticus 17:11 alone.
- Shared terminology may reflect wider Torah traditions concerning blood and sacrifice.
