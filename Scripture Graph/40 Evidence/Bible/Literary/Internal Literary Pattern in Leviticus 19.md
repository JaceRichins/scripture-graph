# Internal Literary Pattern in Leviticus 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated declarations identifying the lawgiver as the LORD or "the LORD your God" punctuate otherwise diverse commands. This is strong internal evidence that divine identity and authority provide the chapter's recurring rhetorical rationale.

**Does not establish:** The refrain does not establish single authorship, a precise date of composition, or that every law originally belonged to one literary unit.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The refrain may chiefly be a conventional legal formula rather than a deliberate device unifying the chapter.
- An editor could have used the repeated formula to join originally separate laws.
