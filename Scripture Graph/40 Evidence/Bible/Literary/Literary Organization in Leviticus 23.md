# Literary Organization in Leviticus 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chronological movement from the first month to the seventh month, together with repeated introductory and convocation formulas, is strong internal evidence that the chapter functions as an intentionally ordered ritual calendar.

**Does not establish:** The organization does not establish a particular date, author, compositional history, or mode of transmission.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor could have arranged originally separate ritual instructions into the present calendar sequence.
- Repeated formulas may reflect conventional legal style rather than composition as a single literary unit.
