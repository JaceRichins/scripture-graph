# Literary Pattern in Exodus 40

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close command-performance correspondence and repeated obedience formula provide strong literary evidence that exact compliance with divine instruction is a principal theme of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish the historical date, authorship, or precise compositional process of the narrative.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may partly serve as a conventional record of task completion rather than conveying a distinct theology of obedience.
- Some of the correspondence results naturally from narrating fulfillment of previously listed instructions.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 40#^ex-40-1|Exodus 40:1-16]], [[Exodus 40#^ex-40-17|Exodus 40:17-33]]
