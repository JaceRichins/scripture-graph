# Internal Narrative Fulfillment in 1 Kings 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrated outcome supports Micaiah's warning over the unanimous favorable prediction: Ahab does not return in peace, he dies, and Israel disperses from the battle.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical sequence of prediction and fulfillment, nor a universal rule that a lone dissenter is necessarily correct.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may have been shaped retrospectively so that Micaiah's prediction corresponds closely to the reported outcome.
- The passage establishes Micaiah's reliability within this narrative but does not by itself authenticate every later claimant to prophetic authority.
