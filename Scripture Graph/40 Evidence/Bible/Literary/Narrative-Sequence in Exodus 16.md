# Narrative-Sequence in Exodus 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In Exodus's literary sequence, explicit Sabbath instructions and references to divine commandments and laws occur in Exodus 16, before the Decalogue is narrated in Exodus 20. This is strong evidence for the placement of Sabbath observance before Sinai's Decalogue within the final narrative, but only limited evidence about when the practice or text originated historically.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historical origin of the Sabbath, the date of this passage, or that Israel already possessed the complete legal corpus later narrated at Sinai.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The final narrative may place Sabbath instruction before the Decalogue without preserving the historical order in which the laws developed.
- Exodus 16 may presuppose Sabbath traditions familiar to the narrator or intended audience.
- Later editorial shaping could account for the present placement and terminology.
