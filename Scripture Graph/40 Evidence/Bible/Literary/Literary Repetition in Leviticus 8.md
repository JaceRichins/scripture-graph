# Literary Repetition in Leviticus 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The opening declaration, repeated statements that individual acts occurred as the LORD commanded Moses, and the closing summary strongly support reading obedience to revealed instructions as a deliberate emphasis of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The repetition does not by itself establish the ceremony's historicity, date of composition, or authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The formulas may primarily be conventional language characteristic of priestly ritual narrative.
- The repetition may provide compositional structure without implying that obedience is the chapter's sole or dominant theological theme.
