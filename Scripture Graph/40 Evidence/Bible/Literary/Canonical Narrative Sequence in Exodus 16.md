# Canonical Narrative Sequence in Exodus 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In the present canonical narrative, detailed Sabbath instruction and observance appear in Exodus 16 before Israel reaches Sinai and before the Sabbath command in the Decalogue. This is strong evidence about the literary sequence and theological prominence of Sabbath, but not about when the practice or passage historically originated.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historical origin, precise antiquity, or compositional date of Sabbath observance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may be presenting a practice familiar from later Israel within an earlier narrative setting.
- The episode may preserve an earlier Sabbath tradition subsequently incorporated into the Sinai legislation.
- Canonical placement alone cannot determine the historical or compositional date of either passage.
