# Internal Literary-Ritual Pattern in Leviticus 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The systematic alignment of offender category, animal, blood location, and carcass disposal provides substantial internal evidence that the ritual intentionally differentiates cases by office and communal scope.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish that some persons possess greater human worth, nor does it fully define the symbolism of blood, animals, or altar locations.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The differences may reflect ritual status or sanctuary access more than the comparative moral seriousness of the offenders.
- Differences in animals may partly reflect established ceremonial convention rather than a single symbolic hierarchy.
- The text does not explicitly explain why each category receives its particular procedure.
