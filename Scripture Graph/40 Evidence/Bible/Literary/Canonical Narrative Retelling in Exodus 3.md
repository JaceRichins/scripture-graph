# Canonical Narrative Retelling in Exodus 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 7 retells the bush encounter, including the angelic appearance, God's patriarchal identification, holy ground, Israel's affliction, and Moses's commission. The overlap securely establishes early Christian reception of Exodus 3 as a pivotal call narrative.

**Does not establish:** Literary dependence or shared tradition does not constitute an independent witness proving the episode's historicity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Acts summarizes and adapts the episode for Stephen's speech rather than reproducing every detail.
- The retelling demonstrates reception and interpretation, not independent historical corroboration.
