# Direct Textual Statement in Leviticus 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Leviticus 17:11 is strong textual evidence that this chapter grounds the altar's atoning use of blood in two assertions: the life of flesh is in blood, and God has assigned blood to the altar for atonement. The verse itself does not fully explain how atonement operates or identify a future messianic fulfillment.

**Does not establish:** This verse alone does not establish a complete theory of atonement, the universal necessity of blood for every kind of forgiveness, or an explicit identification of the blood with Jesus Christ.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Readers differ over whether the relationship between blood and atonement should be explained chiefly through representation, substitution, purification, or some combination of these ideas.
- The reference to life in blood may supply a ritual-theological rationale rather than a comprehensive physiological statement.
