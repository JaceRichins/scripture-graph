# Internal Textual Pattern in Leviticus 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Leviticus 4 supplies strong internal textual evidence that the prescribed rite is calibrated according to the offender's communal role: both the animal and the location of blood application vary systematically. The reason for that calibration is not stated.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish unequal human worth, a precise scale of moral blame, the historical frequency of these rites, or a single explanation for the ritual differences.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The distinctions may primarily reflect the degree to which different offenses affect sanctuary space.
- The pattern may express representative responsibility rather than greater personal guilt.
- Differences in animals may include economic or practical accommodation, although the chapter does not state that rationale.
- The arrangement may partly serve literary or legal classification.
