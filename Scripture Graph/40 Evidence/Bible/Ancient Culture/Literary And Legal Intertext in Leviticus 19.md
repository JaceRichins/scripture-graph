# Literary And Legal Intertext in Leviticus 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Leviticus 19 contains a substantial cluster of commands resembling the Ten Commandments: honoring parents, Sabbath observance, rejection of idols, prohibitions on stealing and lying, and protection of God's name from false use. This strongly supports reading the chapter as an application of core covenant obligations to communal life.

**Does not establish:** The cluster does not by itself establish literary dependence, compositional date, authorship, or the direction in which one legal collection influenced another.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallels may reflect shared Israelite legal tradition rather than a deliberate line-by-line reuse of the Decalogue.
- Some correspondences are thematic and do not preserve the exact wording or order of Exodus 20.
