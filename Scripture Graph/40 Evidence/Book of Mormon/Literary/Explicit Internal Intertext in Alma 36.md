# Explicit Internal Intertext in Alma 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit comparison with Lehi's vision, reinforced by shared throne-and-angels imagery, is strong internal evidence that Alma 36 intentionally recalls the narrative in 1 Nephi 1 and participates in the book's intertextual cohesion.

**Does not establish:** The intertext establishes a deliberate textual relationship, not independent corroboration of either vision or the book's ancient origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence could have been created by a single later author or editor familiar with both passages.
- Literary dependence does not determine whether the narrated visions were historical experiences.
