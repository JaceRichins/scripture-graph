# Literary Structure in Alma 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained framing and reversals are meaningful evidence of careful literary composition. They provide, at most, weak and non-exclusive support for claims of specifically ancient or Hebrew authorship because concentric rhetoric is not unique to ancient Hebrew writing and some detailed mappings depend on interpretive selection.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not by itself establish ancient origin, Hebrew-language composition, historicity, or the identity of the author.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A modern author could intentionally compose a concentric narrative.
- Oral repetition and thematic return can produce broad symmetry without a formal chiastic blueprint.
- An editor could strengthen correspondences during transmission or redaction.
- Highly detailed chiastic outlines may partly reflect an analyst's flexible choice of matching units.
