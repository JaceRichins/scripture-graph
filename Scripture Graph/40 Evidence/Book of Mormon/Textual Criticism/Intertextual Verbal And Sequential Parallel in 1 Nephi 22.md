# Intertextual Verbal And Sequential Parallel in 1 Nephi 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The paired images of the wicked becoming stubble and the righteous being led up as calves of the stall closely parallel the adjacent images in Malachi 4:1-2. Their shared sequence provides strong evidence of a textual or translation-level relationship.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of dependence, Nephi's access to Malachi, the date of composition, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The English translator may have rendered related ideas in familiar King James biblical language.
- A modern author or editor could have borrowed directly from Malachi.
- Both passages could draw on a broader stock of biblical judgment and pastoral imagery, though the paired sequence makes coincidence less persuasive.
