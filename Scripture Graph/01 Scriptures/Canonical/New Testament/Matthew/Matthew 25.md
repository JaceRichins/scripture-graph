# Matthew 25

**1** Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom. ^matt-25-1

**2** And five of them were wise, and five were foolish. ^matt-25-2

**3** They that were foolish took their lamps, and took no oil with them: ^matt-25-3

**4** But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps. ^matt-25-4

**5** While the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept. ^matt-25-5

**6** And at midnight there was a cry made, Behold, the bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet him. ^matt-25-6

**7** Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps. ^matt-25-7

**8** And the foolish said unto the wise, Give us of your oil; for our lamps are gone out. ^matt-25-8

**9** But the wise answered, saying, Not so; lest there be not enough for us and you: but go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves. ^matt-25-9

**10** And while they went to buy, the bridegroom came; and they that were ready went in with him to the marriage: and the door was shut. ^matt-25-10

**11** Afterward came also the other virgins, saying, Lord, Lord, open to us. ^matt-25-11

**12** But he answered and said, Verily I say unto you, I know you not. ^matt-25-12

**13** Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh. ^matt-25-13

**14** For the kingdom of heaven is as a man travelling into a far country, who called his own servants, and delivered unto them his goods. ^matt-25-14

**15** And unto one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one; to every man according to his several ability; and straightway took his journey. ^matt-25-15

**16** Then he that had received the five talents went and traded with the same, and made them other five talents. ^matt-25-16

**17** And likewise he that had received two, he also gained other two. ^matt-25-17

**18** But he that had received one went and digged in the earth, and hid his lord's money. ^matt-25-18

**19** After a long time the lord of those servants cometh, and reckoneth with them. ^matt-25-19

**20** And so he that had received five talents came and brought other five talents, saying, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me five talents: behold, I have gained beside them five talents more. ^matt-25-20

**21** His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord. ^matt-25-21

**22** He also that had received two talents came and said, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me two talents: behold, I have gained two other talents beside them. ^matt-25-22

**23** His lord said unto him, Well done, good and faithful servant; thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord. ^matt-25-23

**24** Then he which had received the one talent came and said, Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man, reaping where thou hast not sown, and gathering where thou hast not strawed: ^matt-25-24

**25** And I was afraid, and went and hid thy talent in the earth: lo, there thou hast that is thine. ^matt-25-25

**26** His lord answered and said unto him, Thou wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sowed not, and gather where I have not strawed: ^matt-25-26

**27** Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers, and then at my coming I should have received mine own with usury. ^matt-25-27

**28** Take therefore the talent from him, and give it unto him which hath ten talents. ^matt-25-28

**29** For unto every one that hath shall be given, and he shall have abundance: but from him that hath not shall be taken away even that which he hath. ^matt-25-29

**30** And cast ye the unprofitable servant into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth. ^matt-25-30

**31** When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory: ^matt-25-31

**32** And before him shall be gathered all nations: and he shall separate them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats: ^matt-25-32

**33** And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left. ^matt-25-33

**34** Then shall the King say unto them on his right hand, Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world: ^matt-25-34

**35** For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in: ^matt-25-35

**36** Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me. ^matt-25-36

**37** Then shall the righteous answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or thirsty, and gave thee drink? ^matt-25-37

**38** When saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and clothed thee? ^matt-25-38

**39** Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee? ^matt-25-39

**40** And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me. ^matt-25-40

**41** Then shall he say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels: ^matt-25-41

**42** For I was an hungred, and ye gave me no meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me no drink: ^matt-25-42

**43** I was a stranger, and ye took me not in: naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not. ^matt-25-43

**44** Then shall they also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee? ^matt-25-44

**45** Then shall he answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me. ^matt-25-45

**46** And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous into life eternal. ^matt-25-46
