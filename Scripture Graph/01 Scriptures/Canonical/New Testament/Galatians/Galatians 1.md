# Galatians 1

**1** Paul, an apostle, (not of men, neither by man, but by Jesus Christ, and God the Father, who raised him from the dead;) ^gal-1-1

**2** And all the brethren which are with me, unto the churches of Galatia: ^gal-1-2

**3** Grace be to you and peace from God the Father, and from our Lord Jesus Christ, ^gal-1-3

**4** Who gave himself for our sins, that he might deliver us from this present evil world, according to the will of God and our Father: ^gal-1-4

**5** To whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen. ^gal-1-5

**6** I marvel that ye are so soon removed from him that called you into the grace of Christ unto another gospel: ^gal-1-6

**7** Which is not another; but there be some that trouble you, and would pervert the gospel of Christ. ^gal-1-7

**8** But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach any other gospel unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be accursed. ^gal-1-8

**9** As we said before, so say I now again, If any man preach any other gospel unto you than that ye have received, let him be accursed. ^gal-1-9

**10** For do I now persuade men, or God? or do I seek to please men? for if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ. ^gal-1-10

**11** But I certify you, brethren, that the gospel which was preached of me is not after man. ^gal-1-11

**12** For I neither received it of man, neither was I taught it, but by the revelation of Jesus Christ. ^gal-1-12

**13** For ye have heard of my conversation in time past in the Jews' religion, how that beyond measure I persecuted the church of God, and wasted it: ^gal-1-13

**14** And profited in the Jews' religion above many my equals in mine own nation, being more exceedingly zealous of the traditions of my fathers. ^gal-1-14

**15** But when it pleased God, who separated me from my mother's womb, and called me by his grace, ^gal-1-15

**16** To reveal his Son in me, that I might preach him among the heathen; immediately I conferred not with flesh and blood: ^gal-1-16

**17** Neither went I up to Jerusalem to them which were apostles before me; but I went into Arabia, and returned again unto Damascus. ^gal-1-17

**18** Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to see Peter, and abode with him fifteen days. ^gal-1-18

**19** But other of the apostles saw I none, save James the Lord's brother. ^gal-1-19

**20** Now the things which I write unto you, behold, before God, I lie not. ^gal-1-20

**21** Afterwards I came into the regions of Syria and Cilicia; ^gal-1-21

**22** And was unknown by face unto the churches of Judæa which were in Christ: ^gal-1-22

**23** But they had heard only, That he which persecuted us in times past now preacheth the faith which once he destroyed. ^gal-1-23

**24** And they glorified God in me. ^gal-1-24
