# Romans 11

**1** I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin. ^rom-11-1

**2** God hath not cast away his people which he foreknew. Wot ye not what the scripture saith of Elias? how he maketh intercession to God against Israel, saying, ^rom-11-2

**3** Lord, they have killed thy prophets, and digged down thine altars; and I am left alone, and they seek my life. ^rom-11-3

**4** But what saith the answer of God unto him? I have reserved to myself seven thousand men, who have not bowed the knee to the image of Baal. ^rom-11-4

**5** Even so then at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace. ^rom-11-5

**6** And if by grace, then is it no more of works: otherwise grace is no more grace. But if it be of works, then is it no more grace: otherwise work is no more work. ^rom-11-6

**7** What then? Israel hath not obtained that which he seeketh for; but the election hath obtained it, and the rest were blinded ^rom-11-7

**8** (According as it is written, God hath given them the spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see, and ears that they should not hear;) unto this day. ^rom-11-8

**9** And David saith, Let their table be made a snare, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them: ^rom-11-9

**10** Let their eyes be darkened, that they may not see, and bow down their back alway. ^rom-11-10

**11** I say then, Have they stumbled that they should fall? God forbid: but rather through their fall salvation is come unto the Gentiles, for to provoke them to jealousy. ^rom-11-11

**12** Now if the fall of them be the riches of the world, and the diminishing of them the riches of the Gentiles; how much more their fulness? ^rom-11-12

**13** For I speak to you Gentiles, inasmuch as I am the apostle of the Gentiles, I magnify mine office: ^rom-11-13

**14** If by any means I may provoke to emulation them which are my flesh, and might save some of them. ^rom-11-14

**15** For if the casting away of them be the reconciling of the world, what shall the receiving of them be, but life from the dead? ^rom-11-15

**16** For if the firstfruit be holy, the lump is also holy: and if the root be holy, so are the branches. ^rom-11-16

**17** And if some of the branches be broken off, and thou, being a wild olive tree, wert grafted in among them, and with them partakest of the root and fatness of the olive tree; ^rom-11-17

**18** Boast not against the branches. But if thou boast, thou bearest not the root, but the root thee. ^rom-11-18

**19** Thou wilt say then, The branches were broken off, that I might be grafted in. ^rom-11-19

**20** Well; because of unbelief they were broken off, and thou standest by faith. Be not highminded, but fear: ^rom-11-20

**21** For if God spared not the natural branches, take heed lest he also spare not thee. ^rom-11-21

**22** Behold therefore the goodness and severity of God: on them which fell, severity; but toward thee, goodness, if thou continue in his goodness: otherwise thou also shalt be cut off. ^rom-11-22

**23** And they also, if they abide not still in unbelief, shall be grafted in: for God is able to graft them in again. ^rom-11-23

**24** For if thou wert cut out of the olive tree which is wild by nature, and wert grafted contrary to nature into a good olive tree: how much more shall these, which be the natural branches, be grafted into their own olive tree? ^rom-11-24

**25** For I would not, brethren, that ye should be ignorant of this mystery, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits; that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in. ^rom-11-25

**26** And so all Israel shall be saved: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob: ^rom-11-26

**27** For this is my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins. ^rom-11-27

**28** As concerning the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes: but as touching the election, they are beloved for the fathers' sakes. ^rom-11-28

**29** For the gifts and calling of God are without repentance. ^rom-11-29

**30** For as ye in times past have not believed God, yet have now obtained mercy through their unbelief: ^rom-11-30

**31** Even so have these also now not believed, that through your mercy they also may obtain mercy. ^rom-11-31

**32** For God hath concluded them all in unbelief, that he might have mercy upon all. ^rom-11-32

**33** O the depth of the riches both of the wisdom and knowledge of God! how unsearchable are his judgments, and his ways past finding out! ^rom-11-33

**34** For who hath known the mind of the Lord? or who hath been his counsellor? ^rom-11-34

**35** Or who hath first given to him, and it shall be recompensed unto him again? ^rom-11-35

**36** For of him, and through him, and to him, are all things: to whom be glory for ever. Amen. ^rom-11-36
