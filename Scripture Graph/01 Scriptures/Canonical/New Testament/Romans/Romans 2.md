# Romans 2

**1** Therefore thou art inexcusable, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest: for wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest doest the same things. ^rom-2-1

**2** But we are sure that the judgment of God is according to truth against them which commit such things. ^rom-2-2

**3** And thinkest thou this, O man, that judgest them which do such things, and doest the same, that thou shalt escape the judgment of God? ^rom-2-3

**4** Or despisest thou the riches of his goodness and forbearance and longsuffering; not knowing that the goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance? ^rom-2-4

**5** But after thy hardness and impenitent heart treasurest up unto thyself wrath against the day of wrath and revelation of the righteous judgment of God; ^rom-2-5

**6** Who will render to every man according to his deeds: ^rom-2-6

**7** To them who by patient continuance in well doing seek for glory and honour and immortality, eternal life: ^rom-2-7

**8** But unto them that are contentious, and do not obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, indignation and wrath, ^rom-2-8

**9** Tribulation and anguish, upon every soul of man that doeth evil, of the Jew first, and also of the Gentile; ^rom-2-9

**10** But glory, honour, and peace, to every man that worketh good, to the Jew first, and also to the Gentile: ^rom-2-10

**11** For there is no respect of persons with God. ^rom-2-11

**12** For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law: and as many as have sinned in the law shall be judged by the law; ^rom-2-12

**13** (For not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified. ^rom-2-13

**14** For when the Gentiles, which have not the law, do by nature the things contained in the law, these, having not the law, are a law unto themselves: ^rom-2-14

**15** Which shew the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience also bearing witness, and their thoughts the mean while accusing or else excusing one another;) ^rom-2-15

**16** In the day when God shall judge the secrets of men by Jesus Christ according to my gospel. ^rom-2-16

**17** Behold, thou art called a Jew, and restest in the law, and makest thy boast of God, ^rom-2-17

**18** And knowest his will, and approvest the things that are more excellent, being instructed out of the law; ^rom-2-18

**19** And art confident that thou thyself art a guide of the blind, a light of them which are in darkness, ^rom-2-19

**20** An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes, which hast the form of knowledge and of the truth in the law. ^rom-2-20

**21** Thou therefore which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself? thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal? ^rom-2-21

**22** Thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege? ^rom-2-22

**23** Thou that makest thy boast of the law, through breaking the law dishonourest thou God? ^rom-2-23

**24** For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you, as it is written. ^rom-2-24

**25** For circumcision verily profiteth, if thou keep the law: but if thou be a breaker of the law, thy circumcision is made uncircumcision. ^rom-2-25

**26** Therefore if the uncircumcision keep the righteousness of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision? ^rom-2-26

**27** And shall not uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfil the law, judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law? ^rom-2-27

**28** For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh: ^rom-2-28

**29** But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter; whose praise is not of men, but of God. ^rom-2-29
