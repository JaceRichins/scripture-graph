# 2 Timothy 3

**1** This know also, that in the last days perilous times shall come. ^2tim-3-1

**2** For men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy, ^2tim-3-2

**3** Without natural affection, trucebreakers, false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good, ^2tim-3-3

**4** Traitors, heady, highminded, lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God; ^2tim-3-4

**5** Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away. ^2tim-3-5

**6** For of this sort are they which creep into houses, and lead captive silly women laden with sins, led away with divers lusts, ^2tim-3-6

**7** Ever learning, and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth. ^2tim-3-7

**8** Now as Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so do these also resist the truth: men of corrupt minds, reprobate concerning the faith. ^2tim-3-8

**9** But they shall proceed no further: for their folly shall be manifest unto all men, as theirs also was. ^2tim-3-9

**10** But thou hast fully known my doctrine, manner of life, purpose, faith, longsuffering, charity, patience, ^2tim-3-10

**11** Persecutions, afflictions, which came unto me at Antioch, at Iconium, at Lystra; what persecutions I endured: but out of them all the Lord delivered me. ^2tim-3-11

**12** Yea, and all that will live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution. ^2tim-3-12

**13** But evil men and seducers shall wax worse and worse, deceiving, and being deceived. ^2tim-3-13

**14** But continue thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, knowing of whom thou hast learned them; ^2tim-3-14

**15** And that from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus. ^2tim-3-15

**16** All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: ^2tim-3-16

**17** That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works. ^2tim-3-17
