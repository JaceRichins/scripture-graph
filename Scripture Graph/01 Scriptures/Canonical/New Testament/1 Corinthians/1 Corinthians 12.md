# 1 Corinthians 12

**1** Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you ignorant. ^1cor-12-1

**2** Ye know that ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led. ^1cor-12-2

**3** Wherefore I give you to understand, that no man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Jesus accursed: and that no man can say that Jesus is the Lord, but by the Holy Ghost. ^1cor-12-3

**4** Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit. ^1cor-12-4

**5** And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord. ^1cor-12-5

**6** And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all. ^1cor-12-6

**7** But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal. ^1cor-12-7

**8** For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit; ^1cor-12-8

**9** To another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit; ^1cor-12-9

**10** To another the working of miracles; to another prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another divers kinds of tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues: ^1cor-12-10

**11** But all these worketh that one and the selfsame Spirit, dividing to every man severally as he will. ^1cor-12-11

**12** For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ. ^1cor-12-12

**13** For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit. ^1cor-12-13

**14** For the body is not one member, but many. ^1cor-12-14

**15** If the foot shall say, Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body? ^1cor-12-15

**16** And if the ear shall say, Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body? ^1cor-12-16

**17** If the whole body were an eye, where were the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where were the smelling? ^1cor-12-17

**18** But now hath God set the members every one of them in the body, as it hath pleased him. ^1cor-12-18

**19** And if they were all one member, where were the body? ^1cor-12-19

**20** But now are they many members, yet but one body. ^1cor-12-20

**21** And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee: nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you. ^1cor-12-21

**22** Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary: ^1cor-12-22

**23** And those members of the body, which we think to be less honourable, upon these we bestow more abundant honour; and our uncomely parts have more abundant comeliness. ^1cor-12-23

**24** For our comely parts have no need: but God hath tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to that part which lacked: ^1cor-12-24

**25** That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another. ^1cor-12-25

**26** And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it. ^1cor-12-26

**27** Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular. ^1cor-12-27

**28** And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues. ^1cor-12-28

**29** Are all apostles? are all prophets? are all teachers? are all workers of miracles? ^1cor-12-29

**30** Have all the gifts of healing? do all speak with tongues? do all interpret? ^1cor-12-30

**31** But covet earnestly the best gifts: and yet shew I unto you a more excellent way. ^1cor-12-31
