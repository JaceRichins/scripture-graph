# 1 John 3

**1** Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not. ^1jn-3-1

**2** Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is. ^1jn-3-2

**3** And every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure. ^1jn-3-3

**4** Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law. ^1jn-3-4

**5** And ye know that he was manifested to take away our sins; and in him is no sin. ^1jn-3-5

**6** Whosoever abideth in him sinneth not: whosoever sinneth hath not seen him, neither known him. ^1jn-3-6

**7** Little children, let no man deceive you: he that doeth righteousness is righteous, even as he is righteous. ^1jn-3-7

**8** He that committeth sin is of the devil; for the devil sinneth from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil. ^1jn-3-8

**9** Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin; for his seed remaineth in him: and he cannot sin, because he is born of God. ^1jn-3-9

**10** In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil: whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of God, neither he that loveth not his brother. ^1jn-3-10

**11** For this is the message that ye heard from the beginning, that we should love one another. ^1jn-3-11

**12** Not as Cain, who was of that wicked one, and slew his brother. And wherefore slew he him? Because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous. ^1jn-3-12

**13** Marvel not, my brethren, if the world hate you. ^1jn-3-13

**14** We know that we have passed from death unto life, because we love the brethren. He that loveth not his brother abideth in death. ^1jn-3-14

**15** Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer: and ye know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him. ^1jn-3-15

**16** Hereby perceive we the love of God, because he laid down his life for us: and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren. ^1jn-3-16

**17** But whoso hath this world's good, and seeth his brother have need, and shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him, how dwelleth the love of God in him? ^1jn-3-17

**18** My little children, let us not love in word, neither in tongue; but in deed and in truth. ^1jn-3-18

**19** And hereby we know that we are of the truth, and shall assure our hearts before him. ^1jn-3-19

**20** For if our heart condemn us, God is greater than our heart, and knoweth all things. ^1jn-3-20

**21** Beloved, if our heart condemn us not, then have we confidence toward God. ^1jn-3-21

**22** And whatsoever we ask, we receive of him, because we keep his commandments, and do those things that are pleasing in his sight. ^1jn-3-22

**23** And this is his commandment, That we should believe on the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another, as he gave us commandment. ^1jn-3-23

**24** And he that keepeth his commandments dwelleth in him, and he in him. And hereby we know that he abideth in us, by the Spirit which he hath given us. ^1jn-3-24
