# 2 Corinthians 2

**1** But I determined this with myself, that I would not come again to you in heaviness. ^2cor-2-1

**2** For if I make you sorry, who is he then that maketh me glad, but the same which is made sorry by me? ^2cor-2-2

**3** And I wrote this same unto you, lest, when I came, I should have sorrow from them of whom I ought to rejoice; having confidence in you all, that my joy is the joy of you all. ^2cor-2-3

**4** For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote unto you with many tears; not that ye should be grieved, but that ye might know the love which I have more abundantly unto you. ^2cor-2-4

**5** But if any have caused grief, he hath not grieved me, but in part: that I may not overcharge you all. ^2cor-2-5

**6** Sufficient to such a man is this punishment, which was inflicted of many. ^2cor-2-6

**7** So that contrariwise ye ought rather to forgive him, and comfort him, lest perhaps such a one should be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow. ^2cor-2-7

**8** Wherefore I beseech you that ye would confirm your love toward him. ^2cor-2-8

**9** For to this end also did I write, that I might know the proof of you, whether ye be obedient in all things. ^2cor-2-9

**10** To whom ye forgive any thing, I forgive also: for if I forgave any thing, to whom I forgave it, for your sakes forgave I it in the person of Christ; ^2cor-2-10

**11** Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices. ^2cor-2-11

**12** Furthermore, when I came to Troas to preach Christ's gospel, and a door was opened unto me of the Lord, ^2cor-2-12

**13** I had no rest in my spirit, because I found not Titus my brother: but taking my leave of them, I went from thence into Macedonia. ^2cor-2-13

**14** Now thanks be unto God, which always causeth us to triumph in Christ, and maketh manifest the savour of his knowledge by us in every place. ^2cor-2-14

**15** For we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ, in them that are saved, and in them that perish: ^2cor-2-15

**16** To the one we are the savour of death unto death; and to the other the savour of life unto life. And who is sufficient for these things? ^2cor-2-16

**17** For we are not as many, which corrupt the word of God: but as of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God speak we in Christ. ^2cor-2-17
