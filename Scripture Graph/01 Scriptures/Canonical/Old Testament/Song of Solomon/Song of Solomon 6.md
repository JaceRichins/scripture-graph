# Song of Solomon 6

**1** Whither is thy beloved gone, O thou fairest among women? whither is thy beloved turned aside? that we may seek him with thee. ^song-6-1

**2** My beloved is gone down into his garden, to the beds of spices, to feed in the gardens, and to gather lilies. ^song-6-2

**3** I am my beloved's, and my beloved is mine: he feedeth among the lilies. ^song-6-3

**4** Thou art beautiful, O my love, as Tirzah, comely as Jerusalem, terrible as an army with banners. ^song-6-4

**5** Turn away thine eyes from me, for they have overcome me: thy hair is as a flock of goats that appear from Gilead. ^song-6-5

**6** Thy teeth are as a flock of sheep which go up from the washing, whereof every one beareth twins, and there is not one barren among them. ^song-6-6

**7** As a piece of a pomegranate are thy temples within thy locks. ^song-6-7

**8** There are threescore queens, and fourscore concubines, and virgins without number. ^song-6-8

**9** My dove, my undefiled is but one; she is the only one of her mother, she is the choice one of her that bare her. The daughters saw her, and blessed her; yea, the queens and the concubines, and they praised her. ^song-6-9

**10** Who is she that looketh forth as the morning, fair as the moon, clear as the sun, and terrible as an army with banners? ^song-6-10

**11** I went down into the garden of nuts to see the fruits of the valley, and to see whether the vine flourished, and the pomegranates budded. ^song-6-11

**12** Or ever I was aware, my soul made me like the chariots of Amminadib. ^song-6-12

**13** Return, return, O Shulamite; return, return, that we may look upon thee. What will ye see in the Shulamite? As it were the company of two armies. ^song-6-13
