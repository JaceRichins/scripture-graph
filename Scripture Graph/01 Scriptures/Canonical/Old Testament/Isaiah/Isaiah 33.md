# Isaiah 33

**1** Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! when thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; and when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee. ^isa-33-1

**2** O LORD, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble. ^isa-33-2

**3** At the noise of the tumult the people fled; at the lifting up of thyself the nations were scattered. ^isa-33-3

**4** And your spoil shall be gathered like the gathering of the caterpiller: as the running to and fro of locusts shall he run upon them. ^isa-33-4

**5** The LORD is exalted; for he dwelleth on high: he hath filled Zion with judgment and righteousness. ^isa-33-5

**6** And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the LORD is his treasure. ^isa-33-6

**7** Behold, their valiant ones shall cry without: the ambassadors of peace shall weep bitterly. ^isa-33-7

**8** The highways lie waste, the wayfaring man ceaseth: he hath broken the covenant, he hath despised the cities, he regardeth no man. ^isa-33-8

**9** The earth mourneth and languisheth: Lebanon is ashamed and hewn down: Sharon is like a wilderness; and Bashan and Carmel shake off their fruits. ^isa-33-9

**10** Now will I rise, saith the LORD; now will I be exalted; now will I lift up myself. ^isa-33-10

**11** Ye shall conceive chaff, ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath, as fire, shall devour you. ^isa-33-11

**12** And the people shall be as the burnings of lime: as thorns cut up shall they be burned in the fire. ^isa-33-12

**13** Hear, ye that are far off, what I have done; and, ye that are near, acknowledge my might. ^isa-33-13

**14** The sinners in Zion are afraid; fearfulness hath surprised the hypocrites. Who among us shall dwell with the devouring fire? who among us shall dwell with everlasting burnings? ^isa-33-14

**15** He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from holding of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil; ^isa-33-15

**16** He shall dwell on high: his place of defence shall be the munitions of rocks: bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure. ^isa-33-16

**17** Thine eyes shall see the king in his beauty: they shall behold the land that is very far off. ^isa-33-17

**18** Thine heart shall meditate terror. Where is the scribe? where is the receiver? where is he that counted the towers? ^isa-33-18

**19** Thou shalt not see a fierce people, a people of a deeper speech than thou canst perceive; of a stammering tongue, that thou canst not understand. ^isa-33-19

**20** Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tabernacle that shall not be taken down; not one of the stakes thereof shall ever be removed, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken. ^isa-33-20

**21** But there the glorious LORD will be unto us a place of broad rivers and streams; wherein shall go no galley with oars, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby. ^isa-33-21

**22** For the LORD is our judge, the LORD is our lawgiver, the LORD is our king; he will save us. ^isa-33-22

**23** Thy tacklings are loosed; they could not well strengthen their mast, they could not spread the sail: then is the prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey. ^isa-33-23

**24** And the inhabitant shall not say, I am sick: the people that dwell therein shall be forgiven their iniquity. ^isa-33-24
