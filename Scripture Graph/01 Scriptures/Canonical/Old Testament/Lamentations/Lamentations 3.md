# Lamentations 3

**1** I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath. ^lam-3-1

**2** He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light. ^lam-3-2

**3** Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand against me all the day. ^lam-3-3

**4** My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my bones. ^lam-3-4

**5** He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail. ^lam-3-5

**6** He hath set me in dark places, as they that be dead of old. ^lam-3-6

**7** He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy. ^lam-3-7

**8** Also when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer. ^lam-3-8

**9** He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked. ^lam-3-9

**10** He was unto me as a bear lying in wait, and as a lion in secret places. ^lam-3-10

**11** He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate. ^lam-3-11

**12** He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow. ^lam-3-12

**13** He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins. ^lam-3-13

**14** I was a derision to all my people; and their song all the day. ^lam-3-14

**15** He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath made me drunken with wormwood. ^lam-3-15

**16** He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes. ^lam-3-16

**17** And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I forgat prosperity. ^lam-3-17

**18** And I said, My strength and my hope is perished from the LORD: ^lam-3-18

**19** Remembering mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall. ^lam-3-19

**20** My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is humbled in me. ^lam-3-20

**21** This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope. ^lam-3-21

**22** It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. ^lam-3-22

**23** They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness. ^lam-3-23

**24** The LORD is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him. ^lam-3-24

**25** The LORD is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him. ^lam-3-25

**26** It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the LORD. ^lam-3-26

**27** It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth. ^lam-3-27

**28** He sitteth alone and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it upon him. ^lam-3-28

**29** He putteth his mouth in the dust; if so be there may be hope. ^lam-3-29

**30** He giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproach. ^lam-3-30

**31** For the Lord will not cast off for ever: ^lam-3-31

**32** But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his mercies. ^lam-3-32

**33** For he doth not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men. ^lam-3-33

**34** To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth, ^lam-3-34

**35** To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the most High, ^lam-3-35

**36** To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not. ^lam-3-36

**37** Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not? ^lam-3-37

**38** Out of the mouth of the most High proceedeth not evil and good? ^lam-3-38

**39** Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins? ^lam-3-39

**40** Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the LORD. ^lam-3-40

**41** Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens. ^lam-3-41

**42** We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned. ^lam-3-42

**43** Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied. ^lam-3-43

**44** Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that our prayer should not pass through. ^lam-3-44

**45** Thou hast made us as the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the people. ^lam-3-45

**46** All our enemies have opened their mouths against us. ^lam-3-46

**47** Fear and a snare is come upon us, desolation and destruction. ^lam-3-47

**48** Mine eye runneth down with rivers of water for the destruction of the daughter of my people. ^lam-3-48

**49** Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission, ^lam-3-49

**50** Till the LORD look down, and behold from heaven. ^lam-3-50

**51** Mine eye affecteth mine heart because of all the daughters of my city. ^lam-3-51

**52** Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird, without cause. ^lam-3-52

**53** They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon me. ^lam-3-53

**54** Waters flowed over mine head; then I said, I am cut off. ^lam-3-54

**55** I called upon thy name, O LORD, out of the low dungeon. ^lam-3-55

**56** Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry. ^lam-3-56

**57** Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee: thou saidst, Fear not. ^lam-3-57

**58** O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life. ^lam-3-58

**59** O LORD, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause. ^lam-3-59

**60** Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their imaginations against me. ^lam-3-60

**61** Thou hast heard their reproach, O LORD, and all their imaginations against me; ^lam-3-61

**62** The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day. ^lam-3-62

**63** Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their musick. ^lam-3-63

**64** Render unto them a recompence, O LORD, according to the work of their hands. ^lam-3-64

**65** Give them sorrow of heart, thy curse unto them. ^lam-3-65

**66** Persecute and destroy them in anger from under the heavens of the LORD. ^lam-3-66
