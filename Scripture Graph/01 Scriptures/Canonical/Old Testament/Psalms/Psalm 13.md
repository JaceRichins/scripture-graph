# Psalm 13

**1** How long wilt thou forget me, O LORD? for ever? how long wilt thou hide thy face from me? ^ps-13-1

**2** How long shall I take counsel in my soul, having sorrow in my heart daily? how long shall mine enemy be exalted over me? ^ps-13-2

**3** Consider and hear me, O LORD my God: lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death; ^ps-13-3

**4** Lest mine enemy say, I have prevailed against him; and those that trouble me rejoice when I am moved. ^ps-13-4

**5** But I have trusted in thy mercy; my heart shall rejoice in thy salvation. ^ps-13-5

**6** I will sing unto the LORD, because he hath dealt bountifully with me. ^ps-13-6
