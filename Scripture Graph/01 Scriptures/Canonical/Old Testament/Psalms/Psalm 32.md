# Psalm 32

**1** Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered. ^ps-32-1

**2** Blessed is the man unto whom the LORD imputeth not iniquity, and in whose spirit there is no guile. ^ps-32-2

**3** When I kept silence, my bones waxed old through my roaring all the day long. ^ps-32-3

**4** For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me: my moisture is turned into the drought of summer. Selah. ^ps-32-4

**5** I acknowledged my sin unto thee, and mine iniquity have I not hid. I said, I will confess my transgressions unto the LORD; and thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin. Selah. ^ps-32-5

**6** For this shall every one that is godly pray unto thee in a time when thou mayest be found: surely in the floods of great waters they shall not come nigh unto him. ^ps-32-6

**7** Thou art my hiding place; thou shalt preserve me from trouble; thou shalt compass me about with songs of deliverance. Selah. ^ps-32-7

**8** I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye. ^ps-32-8

**9** Be ye not as the horse, or as the mule, which have no understanding: whose mouth must be held in with bit and bridle, lest they come near unto thee. ^ps-32-9

**10** Many sorrows shall be to the wicked: but he that trusteth in the LORD, mercy shall compass him about. ^ps-32-10

**11** Be glad in the LORD, and rejoice, ye righteous: and shout for joy, all ye that are upright in heart. ^ps-32-11
