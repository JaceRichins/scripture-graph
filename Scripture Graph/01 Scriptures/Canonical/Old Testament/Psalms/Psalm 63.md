# Psalm 63

**1** O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is; ^ps-63-1

**2** To see thy power and thy glory, so as I have seen thee in the sanctuary. ^ps-63-2

**3** Because thy lovingkindness is better than life, my lips shall praise thee. ^ps-63-3

**4** Thus will I bless thee while I live: I will lift up my hands in thy name. ^ps-63-4

**5** My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness; and my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips: ^ps-63-5

**6** When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches. ^ps-63-6

**7** Because thou hast been my help, therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice. ^ps-63-7

**8** My soul followeth hard after thee: thy right hand upholdeth me. ^ps-63-8

**9** But those that seek my soul, to destroy it, shall go into the lower parts of the earth. ^ps-63-9

**10** They shall fall by the sword: they shall be a portion for foxes. ^ps-63-10

**11** But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that sweareth by him shall glory: but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped. ^ps-63-11
