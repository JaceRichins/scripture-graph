# Psalm 85

**1** LORD, thou hast been favourable unto thy land: thou hast brought back the captivity of Jacob. ^ps-85-1

**2** Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of thy people, thou hast covered all their sin. Selah. ^ps-85-2

**3** Thou hast taken away all thy wrath: thou hast turned thyself from the fierceness of thine anger. ^ps-85-3

**4** Turn us, O God of our salvation, and cause thine anger toward us to cease. ^ps-85-4

**5** Wilt thou be angry with us for ever? wilt thou draw out thine anger to all generations? ^ps-85-5

**6** Wilt thou not revive us again: that thy people may rejoice in thee? ^ps-85-6

**7** Shew us thy mercy, O LORD, and grant us thy salvation. ^ps-85-7

**8** I will hear what God the LORD will speak: for he will speak peace unto his people, and to his saints: but let them not turn again to folly. ^ps-85-8

**9** Surely his salvation is nigh them that fear him; that glory may dwell in our land. ^ps-85-9

**10** Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other. ^ps-85-10

**11** Truth shall spring out of the earth; and righteousness shall look down from heaven. ^ps-85-11

**12** Yea, the LORD shall give that which is good; and our land shall yield her increase. ^ps-85-12

**13** Righteousness shall go before him; and shall set us in the way of his steps. ^ps-85-13
