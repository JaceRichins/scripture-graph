# Psalm 29

**1** Give unto the LORD, O ye mighty, give unto the LORD glory and strength. ^ps-29-1

**2** Give unto the LORD the glory due unto his name; worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness. ^ps-29-2

**3** The voice of the LORD is upon the waters: the God of glory thundereth: the LORD is upon many waters. ^ps-29-3

**4** The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is full of majesty. ^ps-29-4

**5** The voice of the LORD breaketh the cedars; yea, the LORD breaketh the cedars of Lebanon. ^ps-29-5

**6** He maketh them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young unicorn. ^ps-29-6

**7** The voice of the LORD divideth the flames of fire. ^ps-29-7

**8** The voice of the LORD shaketh the wilderness; the LORD shaketh the wilderness of Kadesh. ^ps-29-8

**9** The voice of the LORD maketh the hinds to calve, and discovereth the forests: and in his temple doth every one speak of his glory. ^ps-29-9

**10** The LORD sitteth upon the flood; yea, the LORD sitteth King for ever. ^ps-29-10

**11** The LORD will give strength unto his people; the LORD will bless his people with peace. ^ps-29-11
