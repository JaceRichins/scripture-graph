# Psalm 25

**1** Unto thee, O LORD, do I lift up my soul. ^ps-25-1

**2** O my God, I trust in thee: let me not be ashamed, let not mine enemies triumph over me. ^ps-25-2

**3** Yea, let none that wait on thee be ashamed: let them be ashamed which transgress without cause. ^ps-25-3

**4** Shew me thy ways, O LORD; teach me thy paths. ^ps-25-4

**5** Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day. ^ps-25-5

**6** Remember, O LORD, thy tender mercies and thy lovingkindnesses; for they have been ever of old. ^ps-25-6

**7** Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions: according to thy mercy remember thou me for thy goodness' sake, O LORD. ^ps-25-7

**8** Good and upright is the LORD: therefore will he teach sinners in the way. ^ps-25-8

**9** The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he teach his way. ^ps-25-9

**10** All the paths of the LORD are mercy and truth unto such as keep his covenant and his testimonies. ^ps-25-10

**11** For thy name's sake, O LORD, pardon mine iniquity; for it is great. ^ps-25-11

**12** What man is he that feareth the LORD? him shall he teach in the way that he shall choose. ^ps-25-12

**13** His soul shall dwell at ease; and his seed shall inherit the earth. ^ps-25-13

**14** The secret of the LORD is with them that fear him; and he will shew them his covenant. ^ps-25-14

**15** Mine eyes are ever toward the LORD; for he shall pluck my feet out of the net. ^ps-25-15

**16** Turn thee unto me, and have mercy upon me; for I am desolate and afflicted. ^ps-25-16

**17** The troubles of my heart are enlarged: O bring thou me out of my distresses. ^ps-25-17

**18** Look upon mine affliction and my pain; and forgive all my sins. ^ps-25-18

**19** Consider mine enemies; for they are many; and they hate me with cruel hatred. ^ps-25-19

**20** O keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I put my trust in thee. ^ps-25-20

**21** Let integrity and uprightness preserve me; for I wait on thee. ^ps-25-21

**22** Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles. ^ps-25-22
