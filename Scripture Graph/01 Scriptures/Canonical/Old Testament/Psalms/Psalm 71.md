# Psalm 71

**1** In thee, O LORD, do I put my trust: let me never be put to confusion. ^ps-71-1

**2** Deliver me in thy righteousness, and cause me to escape: incline thine ear unto me, and save me. ^ps-71-2

**3** Be thou my strong habitation, whereunto I may continually resort: thou hast given commandment to save me; for thou art my rock and my fortress. ^ps-71-3

**4** Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the wicked, out of the hand of the unrighteous and cruel man. ^ps-71-4

**5** For thou art my hope, O Lord GOD: thou art my trust from my youth. ^ps-71-5

**6** By thee have I been holden up from the womb: thou art he that took me out of my mother's bowels: my praise shall be continually of thee. ^ps-71-6

**7** I am as a wonder unto many; but thou art my strong refuge. ^ps-71-7

**8** Let my mouth be filled with thy praise and with thy honour all the day. ^ps-71-8

**9** Cast me not off in the time of old age; forsake me not when my strength faileth. ^ps-71-9

**10** For mine enemies speak against me; and they that lay wait for my soul take counsel together, ^ps-71-10

**11** Saying, God hath forsaken him: persecute and take him; for there is none to deliver him. ^ps-71-11

**12** O God, be not far from me: O my God, make haste for my help. ^ps-71-12

**13** Let them be confounded and consumed that are adversaries to my soul; let them be covered with reproach and dishonour that seek my hurt. ^ps-71-13

**14** But I will hope continually, and will yet praise thee more and more. ^ps-71-14

**15** My mouth shall shew forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof. ^ps-71-15

**16** I will go in the strength of the Lord GOD: I will make mention of thy righteousness, even of thine only. ^ps-71-16

**17** O God, thou hast taught me from my youth: and hitherto have I declared thy wondrous works. ^ps-71-17

**18** Now also when I am old and grayheaded, O God, forsake me not; until I have shewed thy strength unto this generation, and thy power to every one that is to come. ^ps-71-18

**19** Thy righteousness also, O God, is very high, who hast done great things: O God, who is like unto thee! ^ps-71-19

**20** Thou, which hast shewed me great and sore troubles, shalt quicken me again, and shalt bring me up again from the depths of the earth. ^ps-71-20

**21** Thou shalt increase my greatness, and comfort me on every side. ^ps-71-21

**22** I will also praise thee with the psaltery, even thy truth, O my God: unto thee will I sing with the harp, O thou Holy One of Israel. ^ps-71-22

**23** My lips shall greatly rejoice when I sing unto thee; and my soul, which thou hast redeemed. ^ps-71-23

**24** My tongue also shall talk of thy righteousness all the day long: for they are confounded, for they are brought unto shame, that seek my hurt. ^ps-71-24
