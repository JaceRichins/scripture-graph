# Psalm 6

**1** O LORD, rebuke me not in thine anger, neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure. ^ps-6-1

**2** Have mercy upon me, O LORD; for I am weak: O LORD, heal me; for my bones are vexed. ^ps-6-2

**3** My soul is also sore vexed: but thou, O LORD, how long? ^ps-6-3

**4** Return, O LORD, deliver my soul: oh save me for thy mercies' sake. ^ps-6-4

**5** For in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks? ^ps-6-5

**6** I am weary with my groaning; all the night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears. ^ps-6-6

**7** Mine eye is consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all mine enemies. ^ps-6-7

**8** Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for the LORD hath heard the voice of my weeping. ^ps-6-8

**9** The LORD hath heard my supplication; the LORD will receive my prayer. ^ps-6-9

**10** Let all mine enemies be ashamed and sore vexed: let them return and be ashamed suddenly. ^ps-6-10
