# Psalm 125

**1** They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever. ^ps-125-1

**2** As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever. ^ps-125-2

**3** For the rod of the wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous; lest the righteous put forth their hands unto iniquity. ^ps-125-3

**4** Do good, O LORD, unto those that be good, and to them that are upright in their hearts. ^ps-125-4

**5** As for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, the LORD shall lead them forth with the workers of iniquity: but peace shall be upon Israel. ^ps-125-5
