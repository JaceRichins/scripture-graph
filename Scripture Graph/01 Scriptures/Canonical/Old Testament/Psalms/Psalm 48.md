# Psalm 48

**1** Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness. ^ps-48-1

**2** Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, is mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King. ^ps-48-2

**3** God is known in her palaces for a refuge. ^ps-48-3

**4** For, lo, the kings were assembled, they passed by together. ^ps-48-4

**5** They saw it, and so they marvelled; they were troubled, and hasted away. ^ps-48-5

**6** Fear took hold upon them there, and pain, as of a woman in travail. ^ps-48-6

**7** Thou breakest the ships of Tarshish with an east wind. ^ps-48-7

**8** As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish it for ever. Selah. ^ps-48-8

**9** We have thought of thy lovingkindness, O God, in the midst of thy temple. ^ps-48-9

**10** According to thy name, O God, so is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of righteousness. ^ps-48-10

**11** Let mount Zion rejoice, let the daughters of Judah be glad, because of thy judgments. ^ps-48-11

**12** Walk about Zion, and go round about her: tell the towers thereof. ^ps-48-12

**13** Mark ye well her bulwarks, consider her palaces; that ye may tell it to the generation following. ^ps-48-13

**14** For this God is our God for ever and ever: he will be our guide even unto death. ^ps-48-14
