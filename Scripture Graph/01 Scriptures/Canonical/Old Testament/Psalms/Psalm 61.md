# Psalm 61

**1** Hear my cry, O God; attend unto my prayer. ^ps-61-1

**2** From the end of the earth will I cry unto thee, when my heart is overwhelmed: lead me to the rock that is higher than I. ^ps-61-2

**3** For thou hast been a shelter for me, and a strong tower from the enemy. ^ps-61-3

**4** I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever: I will trust in the covert of thy wings. Selah. ^ps-61-4

**5** For thou, O God, hast heard my vows: thou hast given me the heritage of those that fear thy name. ^ps-61-5

**6** Thou wilt prolong the king's life: and his years as many generations. ^ps-61-6

**7** He shall abide before God for ever: O prepare mercy and truth, which may preserve him. ^ps-61-7

**8** So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, that I may daily perform my vows. ^ps-61-8
