# Psalm 64

**1** Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy. ^ps-64-1

**2** Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity: ^ps-64-2

**3** Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words: ^ps-64-3

**4** That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not. ^ps-64-4

**5** They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them? ^ps-64-5

**6** They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep. ^ps-64-6

**7** But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded. ^ps-64-7

**8** So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away. ^ps-64-8

**9** And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing. ^ps-64-9

**10** The righteous shall be glad in the LORD, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory. ^ps-64-10
