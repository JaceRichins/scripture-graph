# Psalm 22

**1** My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring? ^ps-22-1

**2** O my God, I cry in the daytime, but thou hearest not; and in the night season, and am not silent. ^ps-22-2

**3** But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel. ^ps-22-3

**4** Our fathers trusted in thee: they trusted, and thou didst deliver them. ^ps-22-4

**5** They cried unto thee, and were delivered: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded. ^ps-22-5

**6** But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised of the people. ^ps-22-6

**7** All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying, ^ps-22-7

**8** He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him: let him deliver him, seeing he delighted in him. ^ps-22-8

**9** But thou art he that took me out of the womb: thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother's breasts. ^ps-22-9

**10** I was cast upon thee from the womb: thou art my God from my mother's belly. ^ps-22-10

**11** Be not far from me; for trouble is near; for there is none to help. ^ps-22-11

**12** Many bulls have compassed me: strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round. ^ps-22-12

**13** They gaped upon me with their mouths, as a ravening and a roaring lion. ^ps-22-13

**14** I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint: my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels. ^ps-22-14

**15** My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death. ^ps-22-15

**16** For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet. ^ps-22-16

**17** I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me. ^ps-22-17

**18** They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture. ^ps-22-18

**19** But be not thou far from me, O LORD: O my strength, haste thee to help me. ^ps-22-19

**20** Deliver my soul from the sword; my darling from the power of the dog. ^ps-22-20

**21** Save me from the lion's mouth: for thou hast heard me from the horns of the unicorns. ^ps-22-21

**22** I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee. ^ps-22-22

**23** Ye that fear the LORD, praise him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; and fear him, all ye the seed of Israel. ^ps-22-23

**24** For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; neither hath he hid his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard. ^ps-22-24

**25** My praise shall be of thee in the great congregation: I will pay my vows before them that fear him. ^ps-22-25

**26** The meek shall eat and be satisfied: they shall praise the LORD that seek him: your heart shall live for ever. ^ps-22-26

**27** All the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the LORD: and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee. ^ps-22-27

**28** For the kingdom is the LORD's: and he is the governor among the nations. ^ps-22-28

**29** All they that be fat upon earth shall eat and worship: all they that go down to the dust shall bow before him: and none can keep alive his own soul. ^ps-22-29

**30** A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation. ^ps-22-30

**31** They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this. ^ps-22-31
