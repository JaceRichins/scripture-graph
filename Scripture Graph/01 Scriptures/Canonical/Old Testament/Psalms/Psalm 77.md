# Psalm 77

**1** I cried unto God with my voice, even unto God with my voice; and he gave ear unto me. ^ps-77-1

**2** In the day of my trouble I sought the Lord: my sore ran in the night, and ceased not: my soul refused to be comforted. ^ps-77-2

**3** I remembered God, and was troubled: I complained, and my spirit was overwhelmed. Selah. ^ps-77-3

**4** Thou holdest mine eyes waking: I am so troubled that I cannot speak. ^ps-77-4

**5** I have considered the days of old, the years of ancient times. ^ps-77-5

**6** I call to remembrance my song in the night: I commune with mine own heart: and my spirit made diligent search. ^ps-77-6

**7** Will the Lord cast off for ever? and will he be favourable no more? ^ps-77-7

**8** Is his mercy clean gone for ever? doth his promise fail for evermore? ^ps-77-8

**9** Hath God forgotten to be gracious? hath he in anger shut up his tender mercies? Selah. ^ps-77-9

**10** And I said, This is my infirmity: but I will remember the years of the right hand of the most High. ^ps-77-10

**11** I will remember the works of the LORD: surely I will remember thy wonders of old. ^ps-77-11

**12** I will meditate also of all thy work, and talk of thy doings. ^ps-77-12

**13** Thy way, O God, is in the sanctuary: who is so great a God as our God? ^ps-77-13

**14** Thou art the God that doest wonders: thou hast declared thy strength among the people. ^ps-77-14

**15** Thou hast with thine arm redeemed thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah. ^ps-77-15

**16** The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee; they were afraid: the depths also were troubled. ^ps-77-16

**17** The clouds poured out water: the skies sent out a sound: thine arrows also went abroad. ^ps-77-17

**18** The voice of thy thunder was in the heaven: the lightnings lightened the world: the earth trembled and shook. ^ps-77-18

**19** Thy way is in the sea, and thy path in the great waters, and thy footsteps are not known. ^ps-77-19

**20** Thou leddest thy people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron. ^ps-77-20
