# Psalm 113

**1** Praise ye the LORD. Praise, O ye servants of the LORD, praise the name of the LORD. ^ps-113-1

**2** Blessed be the name of the LORD from this time forth and for evermore. ^ps-113-2

**3** From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same the LORD's name is to be praised. ^ps-113-3

**4** The LORD is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens. ^ps-113-4

**5** Who is like unto the LORD our God, who dwelleth on high, ^ps-113-5

**6** Who humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heaven, and in the earth! ^ps-113-6

**7** He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, and lifteth the needy out of the dunghill; ^ps-113-7

**8** That he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people. ^ps-113-8

**9** He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise ye the LORD. ^ps-113-9
