# Psalm 86

**1** Bow down thine ear, O LORD, hear me: for I am poor and needy. ^ps-86-1

**2** Preserve my soul; for I am holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that trusteth in thee. ^ps-86-2

**3** Be merciful unto me, O Lord: for I cry unto thee daily. ^ps-86-3

**4** Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul. ^ps-86-4

**5** For thou, Lord, art good, and ready to forgive; and plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon thee. ^ps-86-5

**6** Give ear, O LORD, unto my prayer; and attend to the voice of my supplications. ^ps-86-6

**7** In the day of my trouble I will call upon thee: for thou wilt answer me. ^ps-86-7

**8** Among the gods there is none like unto thee, O Lord; neither are there any works like unto thy works. ^ps-86-8

**9** All nations whom thou hast made shall come and worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name. ^ps-86-9

**10** For thou art great, and doest wondrous things: thou art God alone. ^ps-86-10

**11** Teach me thy way, O LORD; I will walk in thy truth: unite my heart to fear thy name. ^ps-86-11

**12** I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with all my heart: and I will glorify thy name for evermore. ^ps-86-12

**13** For great is thy mercy toward me: and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell. ^ps-86-13

**14** O God, the proud are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent men have sought after my soul; and have not set thee before them. ^ps-86-14

**15** But thou, O Lord, art a God full of compassion, and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth. ^ps-86-15

**16** O turn unto me, and have mercy upon me; give thy strength unto thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid. ^ps-86-16

**17** Shew me a token for good; that they which hate me may see it, and be ashamed: because thou, LORD, hast holpen me, and comforted me. ^ps-86-17
