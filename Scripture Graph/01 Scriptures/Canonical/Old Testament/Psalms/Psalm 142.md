# Psalm 142

**1** I cried unto the LORD with my voice; with my voice unto the LORD did I make my supplication. ^ps-142-1

**2** I poured out my complaint before him; I shewed before him my trouble. ^ps-142-2

**3** When my spirit was overwhelmed within me, then thou knewest my path. In the way wherein I walked have they privily laid a snare for me. ^ps-142-3

**4** I looked on my right hand, and beheld, but there was no man that would know me: refuge failed me; no man cared for my soul. ^ps-142-4

**5** I cried unto thee, O LORD: I said, Thou art my refuge and my portion in the land of the living. ^ps-142-5

**6** Attend unto my cry; for I am brought very low: deliver me from my persecutors; for they are stronger than I. ^ps-142-6

**7** Bring my soul out of prison, that I may praise thy name: the righteous shall compass me about; for thou shalt deal bountifully with me. ^ps-142-7
