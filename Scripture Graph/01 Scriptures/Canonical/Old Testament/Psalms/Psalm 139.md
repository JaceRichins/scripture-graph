# Psalm 139

**1** O LORD, thou hast searched me, and known me. ^ps-139-1

**2** Thou knowest my downsitting and mine uprising, thou understandest my thought afar off. ^ps-139-2

**3** Thou compassest my path and my lying down, and art acquainted with all my ways. ^ps-139-3

**4** For there is not a word in my tongue, but, lo, O LORD, thou knowest it altogether. ^ps-139-4

**5** Thou hast beset me behind and before, and laid thine hand upon me. ^ps-139-5

**6** Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; it is high, I cannot attain unto it. ^ps-139-6

**7** Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence? ^ps-139-7

**8** If I ascend up into heaven, thou art there: if I make my bed in hell, behold, thou art there. ^ps-139-8

**9** If I take the wings of the morning, and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea; ^ps-139-9

**10** Even there shall thy hand lead me, and thy right hand shall hold me. ^ps-139-10

**11** If I say, Surely the darkness shall cover me; even the night shall be light about me. ^ps-139-11

**12** Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee; but the night shineth as the day: the darkness and the light are both alike to thee. ^ps-139-12

**13** For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother's womb. ^ps-139-13

**14** I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well. ^ps-139-14

**15** My substance was not hid from thee, when I was made in secret, and curiously wrought in the lowest parts of the earth. ^ps-139-15

**16** Thine eyes did see my substance, yet being unperfect; and in thy book all my members were written, which in continuance were fashioned, when as yet there was none of them. ^ps-139-16

**17** How precious also are thy thoughts unto me, O God! how great is the sum of them! ^ps-139-17

**18** If I should count them, they are more in number than the sand: when I awake, I am still with thee. ^ps-139-18

**19** Surely thou wilt slay the wicked, O God: depart from me therefore, ye bloody men. ^ps-139-19

**20** For they speak against thee wickedly, and thine enemies take thy name in vain. ^ps-139-20

**21** Do not I hate them, O LORD, that hate thee? and am not I grieved with those that rise up against thee? ^ps-139-21

**22** I hate them with perfect hatred: I count them mine enemies. ^ps-139-22

**23** Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: ^ps-139-23

**24** And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting. ^ps-139-24
