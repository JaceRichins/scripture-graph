# Psalm 15

**1** LORD, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill? ^ps-15-1

**2** He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart. ^ps-15-2

**3** He that backbiteth not with his tongue, nor doeth evil to his neighbour, nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbour. ^ps-15-3

**4** In whose eyes a vile person is contemned; but he honoureth them that fear the LORD. He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not. ^ps-15-4

**5** He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved. ^ps-15-5
