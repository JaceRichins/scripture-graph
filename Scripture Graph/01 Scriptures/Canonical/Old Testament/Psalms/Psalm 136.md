# Psalm 136

**1** O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-1

**2** O give thanks unto the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-2

**3** O give thanks to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-3

**4** To him who alone doeth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-4

**5** To him that by wisdom made the heavens: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-5

**6** To him that stretched out the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-6

**7** To him that made great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-7

**8** The sun to rule by day: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-8

**9** The moon and stars to rule by night: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-9

**10** To him that smote Egypt in their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-10

**11** And brought out Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-11

**12** With a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-12

**13** To him which divided the Red sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-13

**14** And made Israel to pass through the midst of it: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-14

**15** But overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red sea: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-15

**16** To him which led his people through the wilderness: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-16

**17** To him which smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-17

**18** And slew famous kings: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-18

**19** Sihon king of the Amorites: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-19

**20** And Og the king of Bashan: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-20

**21** And gave their land for an heritage: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-21

**22** Even an heritage unto Israel his servant: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-22

**23** Who remembered us in our low estate: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^ps-136-23

**24** And hath redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-24

**25** Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-25

**26** O give thanks unto the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^ps-136-26
