# Psalm 66

**1** Make a joyful noise unto God, all ye lands: ^ps-66-1

**2** Sing forth the honour of his name: make his praise glorious. ^ps-66-2

**3** Say unto God, How terrible art thou in thy works! through the greatness of thy power shall thine enemies submit themselves unto thee. ^ps-66-3

**4** All the earth shall worship thee, and shall sing unto thee; they shall sing to thy name. Selah. ^ps-66-4

**5** Come and see the works of God: he is terrible in his doing toward the children of men. ^ps-66-5

**6** He turned the sea into dry land: they went through the flood on foot: there did we rejoice in him. ^ps-66-6

**7** He ruleth by his power for ever; his eyes behold the nations: let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah. ^ps-66-7

**8** O bless our God, ye people, and make the voice of his praise to be heard: ^ps-66-8

**9** Which holdeth our soul in life, and suffereth not our feet to be moved. ^ps-66-9

**10** For thou, O God, hast proved us: thou hast tried us, as silver is tried. ^ps-66-10

**11** Thou broughtest us into the net; thou laidst affliction upon our loins. ^ps-66-11

**12** Thou hast caused men to ride over our heads; we went through fire and through water: but thou broughtest us out into a wealthy place. ^ps-66-12

**13** I will go into thy house with burnt offerings: I will pay thee my vows, ^ps-66-13

**14** Which my lips have uttered, and my mouth hath spoken, when I was in trouble. ^ps-66-14

**15** I will offer unto thee burnt sacrifices of fatlings, with the incense of rams; I will offer bullocks with goats. Selah. ^ps-66-15

**16** Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will declare what he hath done for my soul. ^ps-66-16

**17** I cried unto him with my mouth, and he was extolled with my tongue. ^ps-66-17

**18** If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me: ^ps-66-18

**19** But verily God hath heard me; he hath attended to the voice of my prayer. ^ps-66-19

**20** Blessed be God, which hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me. ^ps-66-20
