# Psalm 141

**1** LORD, I cry unto thee: make haste unto me; give ear unto my voice, when I cry unto thee. ^ps-141-1

**2** Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; and the lifting up of my hands as the evening sacrifice. ^ps-141-2

**3** Set a watch, O LORD, before my mouth; keep the door of my lips. ^ps-141-3

**4** Incline not my heart to any evil thing, to practise wicked works with men that work iniquity: and let me not eat of their dainties. ^ps-141-4

**5** Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities. ^ps-141-5

**6** When their judges are overthrown in stony places, they shall hear my words; for they are sweet. ^ps-141-6

**7** Our bones are scattered at the grave's mouth, as when one cutteth and cleaveth wood upon the earth. ^ps-141-7

**8** But mine eyes are unto thee, O GOD the Lord: in thee is my trust; leave not my soul destitute. ^ps-141-8

**9** Keep me from the snares which they have laid for me, and the gins of the workers of iniquity. ^ps-141-9

**10** Let the wicked fall into their own nets, whilst that I withal escape. ^ps-141-10
