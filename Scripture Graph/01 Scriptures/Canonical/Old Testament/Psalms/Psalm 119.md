# Psalm 119

**1** Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the LORD. ^ps-119-1

**2** Blessed are they that keep his testimonies, and that seek him with the whole heart. ^ps-119-2

**3** They also do no iniquity: they walk in his ways. ^ps-119-3

**4** Thou hast commanded us to keep thy precepts diligently. ^ps-119-4

**5** O that my ways were directed to keep thy statutes! ^ps-119-5

**6** Then shall I not be ashamed, when I have respect unto all thy commandments. ^ps-119-6

**7** I will praise thee with uprightness of heart, when I shall have learned thy righteous judgments. ^ps-119-7

**8** I will keep thy statutes: O forsake me not utterly. ^ps-119-8

**9** Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word. ^ps-119-9

**10** With my whole heart have I sought thee: O let me not wander from thy commandments. ^ps-119-10

**11** Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee. ^ps-119-11

**12** Blessed art thou, O LORD: teach me thy statutes. ^ps-119-12

**13** With my lips have I declared all the judgments of thy mouth. ^ps-119-13

**14** I have rejoiced in the way of thy testimonies, as much as in all riches. ^ps-119-14

**15** I will meditate in thy precepts, and have respect unto thy ways. ^ps-119-15

**16** I will delight myself in thy statutes: I will not forget thy word. ^ps-119-16

**17** Deal bountifully with thy servant, that I may live, and keep thy word. ^ps-119-17

**18** Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law. ^ps-119-18

**19** I am a stranger in the earth: hide not thy commandments from me. ^ps-119-19

**20** My soul breaketh for the longing that it hath unto thy judgments at all times. ^ps-119-20

**21** Thou hast rebuked the proud that are cursed, which do err from thy commandments. ^ps-119-21

**22** Remove from me reproach and contempt; for I have kept thy testimonies. ^ps-119-22

**23** Princes also did sit and speak against me: but thy servant did meditate in thy statutes. ^ps-119-23

**24** Thy testimonies also are my delight and my counsellors. ^ps-119-24

**25** My soul cleaveth unto the dust: quicken thou me according to thy word. ^ps-119-25

**26** I have declared my ways, and thou heardest me: teach me thy statutes. ^ps-119-26

**27** Make me to understand the way of thy precepts: so shall I talk of thy wondrous works. ^ps-119-27

**28** My soul melteth for heaviness: strengthen thou me according unto thy word. ^ps-119-28

**29** Remove from me the way of lying: and grant me thy law graciously. ^ps-119-29

**30** I have chosen the way of truth: thy judgments have I laid before me. ^ps-119-30

**31** I have stuck unto thy testimonies: O LORD, put me not to shame. ^ps-119-31

**32** I will run the way of thy commandments, when thou shalt enlarge my heart. ^ps-119-32

**33** Teach me, O LORD, the way of thy statutes; and I shall keep it unto the end. ^ps-119-33

**34** Give me understanding, and I shall keep thy law; yea, I shall observe it with my whole heart. ^ps-119-34

**35** Make me to go in the path of thy commandments; for therein do I delight. ^ps-119-35

**36** Incline my heart unto thy testimonies, and not to covetousness. ^ps-119-36

**37** Turn away mine eyes from beholding vanity; and quicken thou me in thy way. ^ps-119-37

**38** Stablish thy word unto thy servant, who is devoted to thy fear. ^ps-119-38

**39** Turn away my reproach which I fear: for thy judgments are good. ^ps-119-39

**40** Behold, I have longed after thy precepts: quicken me in thy righteousness. ^ps-119-40

**41** Let thy mercies come also unto me, O LORD, even thy salvation, according to thy word. ^ps-119-41

**42** So shall I have wherewith to answer him that reproacheth me: for I trust in thy word. ^ps-119-42

**43** And take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth; for I have hoped in thy judgments. ^ps-119-43

**44** So shall I keep thy law continually for ever and ever. ^ps-119-44

**45** And I will walk at liberty: for I seek thy precepts. ^ps-119-45

**46** I will speak of thy testimonies also before kings, and will not be ashamed. ^ps-119-46

**47** And I will delight myself in thy commandments, which I have loved. ^ps-119-47

**48** My hands also will I lift up unto thy commandments, which I have loved; and I will meditate in thy statutes. ^ps-119-48

**49** Remember the word unto thy servant, upon which thou hast caused me to hope. ^ps-119-49

**50** This is my comfort in my affliction: for thy word hath quickened me. ^ps-119-50

**51** The proud have had me greatly in derision: yet have I not declined from thy law. ^ps-119-51

**52** I remembered thy judgments of old, O LORD; and have comforted myself. ^ps-119-52

**53** Horror hath taken hold upon me because of the wicked that forsake thy law. ^ps-119-53

**54** Thy statutes have been my songs in the house of my pilgrimage. ^ps-119-54

**55** I have remembered thy name, O LORD, in the night, and have kept thy law. ^ps-119-55

**56** This I had, because I kept thy precepts. ^ps-119-56

**57** Thou art my portion, O LORD: I have said that I would keep thy words. ^ps-119-57

**58** I entreated thy favour with my whole heart: be merciful unto me according to thy word. ^ps-119-58

**59** I thought on my ways, and turned my feet unto thy testimonies. ^ps-119-59

**60** I made haste, and delayed not to keep thy commandments. ^ps-119-60

**61** The bands of the wicked have robbed me: but I have not forgotten thy law. ^ps-119-61

**62** At midnight I will rise to give thanks unto thee because of thy righteous judgments. ^ps-119-62

**63** I am a companion of all them that fear thee, and of them that keep thy precepts. ^ps-119-63

**64** The earth, O LORD, is full of thy mercy: teach me thy statutes. ^ps-119-64

**65** Thou hast dealt well with thy servant, O LORD, according unto thy word. ^ps-119-65

**66** Teach me good judgment and knowledge: for I have believed thy commandments. ^ps-119-66

**67** Before I was afflicted I went astray: but now have I kept thy word. ^ps-119-67

**68** Thou art good, and doest good; teach me thy statutes. ^ps-119-68

**69** The proud have forged a lie against me: but I will keep thy precepts with my whole heart. ^ps-119-69

**70** Their heart is as fat as grease; but I delight in thy law. ^ps-119-70

**71** It is good for me that I have been afflicted; that I might learn thy statutes. ^ps-119-71

**72** The law of thy mouth is better unto me than thousands of gold and silver. ^ps-119-72

**73** Thy hands have made me and fashioned me: give me understanding, that I may learn thy commandments. ^ps-119-73

**74** They that fear thee will be glad when they see me; because I have hoped in thy word. ^ps-119-74

**75** I know, O LORD, that thy judgments are right, and that thou in faithfulness hast afflicted me. ^ps-119-75

**76** Let, I pray thee, thy merciful kindness be for my comfort, according to thy word unto thy servant. ^ps-119-76

**77** Let thy tender mercies come unto me, that I may live: for thy law is my delight. ^ps-119-77

**78** Let the proud be ashamed; for they dealt perversely with me without a cause: but I will meditate in thy precepts. ^ps-119-78

**79** Let those that fear thee turn unto me, and those that have known thy testimonies. ^ps-119-79

**80** Let my heart be sound in thy statutes; that I be not ashamed. ^ps-119-80

**81** My soul fainteth for thy salvation: but I hope in thy word. ^ps-119-81

**82** Mine eyes fail for thy word, saying, When wilt thou comfort me? ^ps-119-82

**83** For I am become like a bottle in the smoke; yet do I not forget thy statutes. ^ps-119-83

**84** How many are the days of thy servant? when wilt thou execute judgment on them that persecute me? ^ps-119-84

**85** The proud have digged pits for me, which are not after thy law. ^ps-119-85

**86** All thy commandments are faithful: they persecute me wrongfully; help thou me. ^ps-119-86

**87** They had almost consumed me upon earth; but I forsook not thy precepts. ^ps-119-87

**88** Quicken me after thy lovingkindness; so shall I keep the testimony of thy mouth. ^ps-119-88

**89** For ever, O LORD, thy word is settled in heaven. ^ps-119-89

**90** Thy faithfulness is unto all generations: thou hast established the earth, and it abideth. ^ps-119-90

**91** They continue this day according to thine ordinances: for all are thy servants. ^ps-119-91

**92** Unless thy law had been my delights, I should then have perished in mine affliction. ^ps-119-92

**93** I will never forget thy precepts: for with them thou hast quickened me. ^ps-119-93

**94** I am thine, save me; for I have sought thy precepts. ^ps-119-94

**95** The wicked have waited for me to destroy me: but I will consider thy testimonies. ^ps-119-95

**96** I have seen an end of all perfection: but thy commandment is exceeding broad. ^ps-119-96

**97** O how love I thy law! it is my meditation all the day. ^ps-119-97

**98** Thou through thy commandments hast made me wiser than mine enemies: for they are ever with me. ^ps-119-98

**99** I have more understanding than all my teachers: for thy testimonies are my meditation. ^ps-119-99

**100** I understand more than the ancients, because I keep thy precepts. ^ps-119-100

**101** I have refrained my feet from every evil way, that I might keep thy word. ^ps-119-101

**102** I have not departed from thy judgments: for thou hast taught me. ^ps-119-102

**103** How sweet are thy words unto my taste! yea, sweeter than honey to my mouth! ^ps-119-103

**104** Through thy precepts I get understanding: therefore I hate every false way. ^ps-119-104

**105** Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path. ^ps-119-105

**106** I have sworn, and I will perform it, that I will keep thy righteous judgments. ^ps-119-106

**107** I am afflicted very much: quicken me, O LORD, according unto thy word. ^ps-119-107

**108** Accept, I beseech thee, the freewill offerings of my mouth, O LORD, and teach me thy judgments. ^ps-119-108

**109** My soul is continually in my hand: yet do I not forget thy law. ^ps-119-109

**110** The wicked have laid a snare for me: yet I erred not from thy precepts. ^ps-119-110

**111** Thy testimonies have I taken as an heritage for ever: for they are the rejoicing of my heart. ^ps-119-111

**112** I have inclined mine heart to perform thy statutes alway, even unto the end. ^ps-119-112

**113** I hate vain thoughts: but thy law do I love. ^ps-119-113

**114** Thou art my hiding place and my shield: I hope in thy word. ^ps-119-114

**115** Depart from me, ye evildoers: for I will keep the commandments of my God. ^ps-119-115

**116** Uphold me according unto thy word, that I may live: and let me not be ashamed of my hope. ^ps-119-116

**117** Hold thou me up, and I shall be safe: and I will have respect unto thy statutes continually. ^ps-119-117

**118** Thou hast trodden down all them that err from thy statutes: for their deceit is falsehood. ^ps-119-118

**119** Thou puttest away all the wicked of the earth like dross: therefore I love thy testimonies. ^ps-119-119

**120** My flesh trembleth for fear of thee; and I am afraid of thy judgments. ^ps-119-120

**121** I have done judgment and justice: leave me not to mine oppressors. ^ps-119-121

**122** Be surety for thy servant for good: let not the proud oppress me. ^ps-119-122

**123** Mine eyes fail for thy salvation, and for the word of thy righteousness. ^ps-119-123

**124** Deal with thy servant according unto thy mercy, and teach me thy statutes. ^ps-119-124

**125** I am thy servant; give me understanding, that I may know thy testimonies. ^ps-119-125

**126** It is time for thee, LORD, to work: for they have made void thy law. ^ps-119-126

**127** Therefore I love thy commandments above gold; yea, above fine gold. ^ps-119-127

**128** Therefore I esteem all thy precepts concerning all things to be right; and I hate every false way. ^ps-119-128

**129** Thy testimonies are wonderful: therefore doth my soul keep them. ^ps-119-129

**130** The entrance of thy words giveth light; it giveth understanding unto the simple. ^ps-119-130

**131** I opened my mouth, and panted: for I longed for thy commandments. ^ps-119-131

**132** Look thou upon me, and be merciful unto me, as thou usest to do unto those that love thy name. ^ps-119-132

**133** Order my steps in thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me. ^ps-119-133

**134** Deliver me from the oppression of man: so will I keep thy precepts. ^ps-119-134

**135** Make thy face to shine upon thy servant; and teach me thy statutes. ^ps-119-135

**136** Rivers of waters run down mine eyes, because they keep not thy law. ^ps-119-136

**137** Righteous art thou, O LORD, and upright are thy judgments. ^ps-119-137

**138** Thy testimonies that thou hast commanded are righteous and very faithful. ^ps-119-138

**139** My zeal hath consumed me, because mine enemies have forgotten thy words. ^ps-119-139

**140** Thy word is very pure: therefore thy servant loveth it. ^ps-119-140

**141** I am small and despised: yet do not I forget thy precepts. ^ps-119-141

**142** Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth. ^ps-119-142

**143** Trouble and anguish have taken hold on me: yet thy commandments are my delights. ^ps-119-143

**144** The righteousness of thy testimonies is everlasting: give me understanding, and I shall live. ^ps-119-144

**145** I cried with my whole heart; hear me, O LORD: I will keep thy statutes. ^ps-119-145

**146** I cried unto thee; save me, and I shall keep thy testimonies. ^ps-119-146

**147** I prevented the dawning of the morning, and cried: I hoped in thy word. ^ps-119-147

**148** Mine eyes prevent the night watches, that I might meditate in thy word. ^ps-119-148

**149** Hear my voice according unto thy lovingkindness: O LORD, quicken me according to thy judgment. ^ps-119-149

**150** They draw nigh that follow after mischief: they are far from thy law. ^ps-119-150

**151** Thou art near, O LORD; and all thy commandments are truth. ^ps-119-151

**152** Concerning thy testimonies, I have known of old that thou hast founded them for ever. ^ps-119-152

**153** Consider mine affliction, and deliver me: for I do not forget thy law. ^ps-119-153

**154** Plead my cause, and deliver me: quicken me according to thy word. ^ps-119-154

**155** Salvation is far from the wicked: for they seek not thy statutes. ^ps-119-155

**156** Great are thy tender mercies, O LORD: quicken me according to thy judgments. ^ps-119-156

**157** Many are my persecutors and mine enemies; yet do I not decline from thy testimonies. ^ps-119-157

**158** I beheld the transgressors, and was grieved; because they kept not thy word. ^ps-119-158

**159** Consider how I love thy precepts: quicken me, O LORD, according to thy lovingkindness. ^ps-119-159

**160** Thy word is true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever. ^ps-119-160

**161** Princes have persecuted me without a cause: but my heart standeth in awe of thy word. ^ps-119-161

**162** I rejoice at thy word, as one that findeth great spoil. ^ps-119-162

**163** I hate and abhor lying: but thy law do I love. ^ps-119-163

**164** Seven times a day do I praise thee because of thy righteous judgments. ^ps-119-164

**165** Great peace have they which love thy law: and nothing shall offend them. ^ps-119-165

**166** LORD, I have hoped for thy salvation, and done thy commandments. ^ps-119-166

**167** My soul hath kept thy testimonies; and I love them exceedingly. ^ps-119-167

**168** I have kept thy precepts and thy testimonies: for all my ways are before thee. ^ps-119-168

**169** Let my cry come near before thee, O LORD: give me understanding according to thy word. ^ps-119-169

**170** Let my supplication come before thee: deliver me according to thy word. ^ps-119-170

**171** My lips shall utter praise, when thou hast taught me thy statutes. ^ps-119-171

**172** My tongue shall speak of thy word: for all thy commandments are righteousness. ^ps-119-172

**173** Let thine hand help me; for I have chosen thy precepts. ^ps-119-173

**174** I have longed for thy salvation, O LORD; and thy law is my delight. ^ps-119-174

**175** Let my soul live, and it shall praise thee; and let thy judgments help me. ^ps-119-175

**176** I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek thy servant; for I do not forget thy commandments. ^ps-119-176
