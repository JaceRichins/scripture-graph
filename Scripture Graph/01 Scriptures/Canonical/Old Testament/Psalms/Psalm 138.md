# Psalm 138

**1** I will praise thee with my whole heart: before the gods will I sing praise unto thee. ^ps-138-1

**2** I will worship toward thy holy temple, and praise thy name for thy lovingkindness and for thy truth: for thou hast magnified thy word above all thy name. ^ps-138-2

**3** In the day when I cried thou answeredst me, and strengthenedst me with strength in my soul. ^ps-138-3

**4** All the kings of the earth shall praise thee, O LORD, when they hear the words of thy mouth. ^ps-138-4

**5** Yea, they shall sing in the ways of the LORD: for great is the glory of the LORD. ^ps-138-5

**6** Though the LORD be high, yet hath he respect unto the lowly: but the proud he knoweth afar off. ^ps-138-6

**7** Though I walk in the midst of trouble, thou wilt revive me: thou shalt stretch forth thine hand against the wrath of mine enemies, and thy right hand shall save me. ^ps-138-7

**8** The LORD will perfect that which concerneth me: thy mercy, O LORD, endureth for ever: forsake not the works of thine own hands. ^ps-138-8
