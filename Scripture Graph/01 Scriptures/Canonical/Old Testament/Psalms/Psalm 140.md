# Psalm 140

**1** Deliver me, O LORD, from the evil man: preserve me from the violent man; ^ps-140-1

**2** Which imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they gathered together for war. ^ps-140-2

**3** They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent; adders' poison is under their lips. Selah. ^ps-140-3

**4** Keep me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from the violent man; who have purposed to overthrow my goings. ^ps-140-4

**5** The proud have hid a snare for me, and cords; they have spread a net by the wayside; they have set gins for me. Selah. ^ps-140-5

**6** I said unto the LORD, Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications, O LORD. ^ps-140-6

**7** O GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head in the day of battle. ^ps-140-7

**8** Grant not, O LORD, the desires of the wicked: further not his wicked device; lest they exalt themselves. Selah. ^ps-140-8

**9** As for the head of those that compass me about, let the mischief of their own lips cover them. ^ps-140-9

**10** Let burning coals fall upon them: let them be cast into the fire; into deep pits, that they rise not up again. ^ps-140-10

**11** Let not an evil speaker be established in the earth: evil shall hunt the violent man to overthrow him. ^ps-140-11

**12** I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted, and the right of the poor. ^ps-140-12

**13** Surely the righteous shall give thanks unto thy name: the upright shall dwell in thy presence. ^ps-140-13
