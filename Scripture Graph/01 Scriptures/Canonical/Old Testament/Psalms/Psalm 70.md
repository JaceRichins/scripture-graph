# Psalm 70

**1** Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O LORD. ^ps-70-1

**2** Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt. ^ps-70-2

**3** Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha. ^ps-70-3

**4** Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified. ^ps-70-4

**5** But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer; O LORD, make no tarrying. ^ps-70-5
