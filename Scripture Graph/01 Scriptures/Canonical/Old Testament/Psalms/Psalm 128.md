# Psalm 128

**1** Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD; that walketh in his ways. ^ps-128-1

**2** For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee. ^ps-128-2

**3** Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine by the sides of thine house: thy children like olive plants round about thy table. ^ps-128-3

**4** Behold, that thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the LORD. ^ps-128-4

**5** The LORD shall bless thee out of Zion: and thou shalt see the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life. ^ps-128-5

**6** Yea, thou shalt see thy children's children, and peace upon Israel. ^ps-128-6
