# Psalm 16

**1** Preserve me, O God: for in thee do I put my trust. ^ps-16-1

**2** O my soul, thou hast said unto the LORD, Thou art my Lord: my goodness extendeth not to thee; ^ps-16-2

**3** But to the saints that are in the earth, and to the excellent, in whom is all my delight. ^ps-16-3

**4** Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god: their drink offerings of blood will I not offer, nor take up their names into my lips. ^ps-16-4

**5** The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot. ^ps-16-5

**6** The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places; yea, I have a goodly heritage. ^ps-16-6

**7** I will bless the LORD, who hath given me counsel: my reins also instruct me in the night seasons. ^ps-16-7

**8** I have set the LORD always before me: because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved. ^ps-16-8

**9** Therefore my heart is glad, and my glory rejoiceth: my flesh also shall rest in hope. ^ps-16-9

**10** For thou wilt not leave my soul in hell; neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption. ^ps-16-10

**11** Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore. ^ps-16-11
