# Psalm 130

**1** Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O LORD. ^ps-130-1

**2** Lord, hear my voice: let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications. ^ps-130-2

**3** If thou, LORD, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand? ^ps-130-3

**4** But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared. ^ps-130-4

**5** I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope. ^ps-130-5

**6** My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning. ^ps-130-6

**7** Let Israel hope in the LORD: for with the LORD there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption. ^ps-130-7

**8** And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities. ^ps-130-8
