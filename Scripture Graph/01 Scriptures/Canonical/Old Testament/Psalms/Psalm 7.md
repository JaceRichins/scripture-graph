# Psalm 7

**1** O LORD my God, in thee do I put my trust: save me from all them that persecute me, and deliver me: ^ps-7-1

**2** Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending it in pieces, while there is none to deliver. ^ps-7-2

**3** O LORD my God, if I have done this; if there be iniquity in my hands; ^ps-7-3

**4** If I have rewarded evil unto him that was at peace with me; (yea, I have delivered him that without cause is mine enemy:) ^ps-7-4

**5** Let the enemy persecute my soul, and take it; yea, let him tread down my life upon the earth, and lay mine honour in the dust. Selah. ^ps-7-5

**6** Arise, O LORD, in thine anger, lift up thyself because of the rage of mine enemies: and awake for me to the judgment that thou hast commanded. ^ps-7-6

**7** So shall the congregation of the people compass thee about: for their sakes therefore return thou on high. ^ps-7-7

**8** The LORD shall judge the people: judge me, O LORD, according to my righteousness, and according to mine integrity that is in me. ^ps-7-8

**9** Oh let the wickedness of the wicked come to an end; but establish the just: for the righteous God trieth the hearts and reins. ^ps-7-9

**10** My defence is of God, which saveth the upright in heart. ^ps-7-10

**11** God judgeth the righteous, and God is angry with the wicked every day. ^ps-7-11

**12** If he turn not, he will whet his sword; he hath bent his bow, and made it ready. ^ps-7-12

**13** He hath also prepared for him the instruments of death; he ordaineth his arrows against the persecutors. ^ps-7-13

**14** Behold, he travaileth with iniquity, and hath conceived mischief, and brought forth falsehood. ^ps-7-14

**15** He made a pit, and digged it, and is fallen into the ditch which he made. ^ps-7-15

**16** His mischief shall return upon his own head, and his violent dealing shall come down upon his own pate. ^ps-7-16

**17** I will praise the LORD according to his righteousness: and will sing praise to the name of the LORD most high. ^ps-7-17
