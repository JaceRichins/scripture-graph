# Job 32

**1** So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. ^job-32-1

**2** Then was kindled the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram: against Job was his wrath kindled, because he justified himself rather than God. ^job-32-2

**3** Also against his three friends was his wrath kindled, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job. ^job-32-3

**4** Now Elihu had waited till Job had spoken, because they were elder than he. ^job-32-4

**5** When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, then his wrath was kindled. ^job-32-5

**6** And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I am young, and ye are very old; wherefore I was afraid, and durst not shew you mine opinion. ^job-32-6

**7** I said, Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom. ^job-32-7

**8** But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding. ^job-32-8

**9** Great men are not always wise: neither do the aged understand judgment. ^job-32-9

**10** Therefore I said, Hearken to me; I also will shew mine opinion. ^job-32-10

**11** Behold, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasons, whilst ye searched out what to say. ^job-32-11

**12** Yea, I attended unto you, and, behold, there was none of you that convinced Job, or that answered his words: ^job-32-12

**13** Lest ye should say, We have found out wisdom: God thrusteth him down, not man. ^job-32-13

**14** Now he hath not directed his words against me: neither will I answer him with your speeches. ^job-32-14

**15** They were amazed, they answered no more: they left off speaking. ^job-32-15

**16** When I had waited, (for they spake not, but stood still, and answered no more;) ^job-32-16

**17** I said, I will answer also my part, I also will shew mine opinion. ^job-32-17

**18** For I am full of matter, the spirit within me constraineth me. ^job-32-18

**19** Behold, my belly is as wine which hath no vent; it is ready to burst like new bottles. ^job-32-19

**20** I will speak, that I may be refreshed: I will open my lips and answer. ^job-32-20

**21** Let me not, I pray you, accept any man's person, neither let me give flattering titles unto man. ^job-32-21

**22** For I know not to give flattering titles; in so doing my maker would soon take me away. ^job-32-22
