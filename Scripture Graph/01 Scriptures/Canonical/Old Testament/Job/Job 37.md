# Job 37

**1** At this also my heart trembleth, and is moved out of his place. ^job-37-1

**2** Hear attentively the noise of his voice, and the sound that goeth out of his mouth. ^job-37-2

**3** He directeth it under the whole heaven, and his lightning unto the ends of the earth. ^job-37-3

**4** After it a voice roareth: he thundereth with the voice of his excellency; and he will not stay them when his voice is heard. ^job-37-4

**5** God thundereth marvellously with his voice; great things doeth he, which we cannot comprehend. ^job-37-5

**6** For he saith to the snow, Be thou on the earth; likewise to the small rain, and to the great rain of his strength. ^job-37-6

**7** He sealeth up the hand of every man; that all men may know his work. ^job-37-7

**8** Then the beasts go into dens, and remain in their places. ^job-37-8

**9** Out of the south cometh the whirlwind: and cold out of the north. ^job-37-9

**10** By the breath of God frost is given: and the breadth of the waters is straitened. ^job-37-10

**11** Also by watering he wearieth the thick cloud: he scattereth his bright cloud: ^job-37-11

**12** And it is turned round about by his counsels: that they may do whatsoever he commandeth them upon the face of the world in the earth. ^job-37-12

**13** He causeth it to come, whether for correction, or for his land, or for mercy. ^job-37-13

**14** Hearken unto this, O Job: stand still, and consider the wondrous works of God. ^job-37-14

**15** Dost thou know when God disposed them, and caused the light of his cloud to shine? ^job-37-15

**16** Dost thou know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him which is perfect in knowledge? ^job-37-16

**17** How thy garments are warm, when he quieteth the earth by the south wind? ^job-37-17

**18** Hast thou with him spread out the sky, which is strong, and as a molten looking glass? ^job-37-18

**19** Teach us what we shall say unto him; for we cannot order our speech by reason of darkness. ^job-37-19

**20** Shall it be told him that I speak? if a man speak, surely he shall be swallowed up. ^job-37-20

**21** And now men see not the bright light which is in the clouds: but the wind passeth, and cleanseth them. ^job-37-21

**22** Fair weather cometh out of the north: with God is terrible majesty. ^job-37-22

**23** Touching the Almighty, we cannot find him out: he is excellent in power, and in judgment, and in plenty of justice: he will not afflict. ^job-37-23

**24** Men do therefore fear him: he respecteth not any that are wise of heart. ^job-37-24
