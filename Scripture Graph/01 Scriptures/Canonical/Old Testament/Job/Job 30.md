# Job 30

**1** But now they that are younger than I have me in derision, whose fathers I would have disdained to have set with the dogs of my flock. ^job-30-1

**2** Yea, whereto might the strength of their hands profit me, in whom old age was perished? ^job-30-2

**3** For want and famine they were solitary; fleeing into the wilderness in former time desolate and waste. ^job-30-3

**4** Who cut up mallows by the bushes, and juniper roots for their meat. ^job-30-4

**5** They were driven forth from among men, (they cried after them as after a thief;) ^job-30-5

**6** To dwell in the clifts of the valleys, in caves of the earth, and in the rocks. ^job-30-6

**7** Among the bushes they brayed; under the nettles they were gathered together. ^job-30-7

**8** They were children of fools, yea, children of base men: they were viler than the earth. ^job-30-8

**9** And now am I their song, yea, I am their byword. ^job-30-9

**10** They abhor me, they flee far from me, and spare not to spit in my face. ^job-30-10

**11** Because he hath loosed my cord, and afflicted me, they have also let loose the bridle before me. ^job-30-11

**12** Upon my right hand rise the youth; they push away my feet, and they raise up against me the ways of their destruction. ^job-30-12

**13** They mar my path, they set forward my calamity, they have no helper. ^job-30-13

**14** They came upon me as a wide breaking in of waters: in the desolation they rolled themselves upon me. ^job-30-14

**15** Terrors are turned upon me: they pursue my soul as the wind: and my welfare passeth away as a cloud. ^job-30-15

**16** And now my soul is poured out upon me; the days of affliction have taken hold upon me. ^job-30-16

**17** My bones are pierced in me in the night season: and my sinews take no rest. ^job-30-17

**18** By the great force of my disease is my garment changed: it bindeth me about as the collar of my coat. ^job-30-18

**19** He hath cast me into the mire, and I am become like dust and ashes. ^job-30-19

**20** I cry unto thee, and thou dost not hear me: I stand up, and thou regardest me not. ^job-30-20

**21** Thou art become cruel to me: with thy strong hand thou opposest thyself against me. ^job-30-21

**22** Thou liftest me up to the wind; thou causest me to ride upon it, and dissolvest my substance. ^job-30-22

**23** For I know that thou wilt bring me to death, and to the house appointed for all living. ^job-30-23

**24** Howbeit he will not stretch out his hand to the grave, though they cry in his destruction. ^job-30-24

**25** Did not I weep for him that was in trouble? was not my soul grieved for the poor? ^job-30-25

**26** When I looked for good, then evil came unto me: and when I waited for light, there came darkness. ^job-30-26

**27** My bowels boiled, and rested not: the days of affliction prevented me. ^job-30-27

**28** I went mourning without the sun: I stood up, and I cried in the congregation. ^job-30-28

**29** I am a brother to dragons, and a companion to owls. ^job-30-29

**30** My skin is black upon me, and my bones are burned with heat. ^job-30-30

**31** My harp also is turned to mourning, and my organ into the voice of them that weep. ^job-30-31
