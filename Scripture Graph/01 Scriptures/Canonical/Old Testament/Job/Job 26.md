# Job 26

**1** But Job answered and said, ^job-26-1

**2** How hast thou helped him that is without power? how savest thou the arm that hath no strength? ^job-26-2

**3** How hast thou counselled him that hath no wisdom? and how hast thou plentifully declared the thing as it is? ^job-26-3

**4** To whom hast thou uttered words? and whose spirit came from thee? ^job-26-4

**5** Dead things are formed from under the waters, and the inhabitants thereof. ^job-26-5

**6** Hell is naked before him, and destruction hath no covering. ^job-26-6

**7** He stretcheth out the north over the empty place, and hangeth the earth upon nothing. ^job-26-7

**8** He bindeth up the waters in his thick clouds; and the cloud is not rent under them. ^job-26-8

**9** He holdeth back the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud upon it. ^job-26-9

**10** He hath compassed the waters with bounds, until the day and night come to an end. ^job-26-10

**11** The pillars of heaven tremble and are astonished at his reproof. ^job-26-11

**12** He divideth the sea with his power, and by his understanding he smiteth through the proud. ^job-26-12

**13** By his spirit he hath garnished the heavens; his hand hath formed the crooked serpent. ^job-26-13

**14** Lo, these are parts of his ways: but how little a portion is heard of him? but the thunder of his power who can understand? ^job-26-14
