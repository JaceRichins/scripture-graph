# Job 7

**1** Is there not an appointed time to man upon earth? are not his days also like the days of an hireling? ^job-7-1

**2** As a servant earnestly desireth the shadow, and as an hireling looketh for the reward of his work: ^job-7-2

**3** So am I made to possess months of vanity, and wearisome nights are appointed to me. ^job-7-3

**4** When I lie down, I say, When shall I arise, and the night be gone? and I am full of tossings to and fro unto the dawning of the day. ^job-7-4

**5** My flesh is clothed with worms and clods of dust; my skin is broken, and become loathsome. ^job-7-5

**6** My days are swifter than a weaver's shuttle, and are spent without hope. ^job-7-6

**7** O remember that my life is wind: mine eye shall no more see good. ^job-7-7

**8** The eye of him that hath seen me shall see me no more: thine eyes are upon me, and I am not. ^job-7-8

**9** As the cloud is consumed and vanisheth away: so he that goeth down to the grave shall come up no more. ^job-7-9

**10** He shall return no more to his house, neither shall his place know him any more. ^job-7-10

**11** Therefore I will not refrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. ^job-7-11

**12** Am I a sea, or a whale, that thou settest a watch over me? ^job-7-12

**13** When I say, My bed shall comfort me, my couch shall ease my complaint; ^job-7-13

**14** Then thou scarest me with dreams, and terrifiest me through visions: ^job-7-14

**15** So that my soul chooseth strangling, and death rather than my life. ^job-7-15

**16** I loathe it; I would not live alway: let me alone; for my days are vanity. ^job-7-16

**17** What is man, that thou shouldest magnify him? and that thou shouldest set thine heart upon him? ^job-7-17

**18** And that thou shouldest visit him every morning, and try him every moment? ^job-7-18

**19** How long wilt thou not depart from me, nor let me alone till I swallow down my spittle? ^job-7-19

**20** I have sinned; what shall I do unto thee, O thou preserver of men? why hast thou set me as a mark against thee, so that I am a burden to myself? ^job-7-20

**21** And why dost thou not pardon my transgression, and take away mine iniquity? for now shall I sleep in the dust; and thou shalt seek me in the morning, but I shall not be. ^job-7-21
