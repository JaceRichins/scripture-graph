# 1 Kings 4

**1** So king Solomon was king over all Israel. ^1kgs-4-1

**2** And these were the princes which he had; Azariah the son of Zadok the priest, ^1kgs-4-2

**3** Elihoreph and Ahiah, the sons of Shisha, scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder. ^1kgs-4-3

**4** And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the host: and Zadok and Abiathar were the priests: ^1kgs-4-4

**5** And Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers: and Zabud the son of Nathan was principal officer, and the king's friend: ^1kgs-4-5

**6** And Ahishar was over the household: and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the tribute. ^1kgs-4-6

**7** And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, which provided victuals for the king and his household: each man his month in a year made provision. ^1kgs-4-7

**8** And these are their names: The son of Hur, in mount Ephraim: ^1kgs-4-8

**9** The son of Dekar, in Makaz, and in Shaalbim, and Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan: ^1kgs-4-9

**10** The son of Hesed, in Aruboth; to him pertained Sochoh, and all the land of Hepher: ^1kgs-4-10

**11** The son of Abinadab, in all the region of Dor; which had Taphath the daughter of Solomon to wife: ^1kgs-4-11

**12** Baana the son of Ahilud; to him pertained Taanach and Megiddo, and all Beth-shean, which is by Zartanah beneath Jezreel, from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah, even unto the place that is beyond Jokneam: ^1kgs-4-12

**13** The son of Geber, in Ramoth-gilead; to him pertained the towns of Jair the son of Manasseh, which are in Gilead; to him also pertained the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, threescore great cities with walls and brasen bars: ^1kgs-4-13

**14** Ahinadab the son of Iddo had Mahanaim: ^1kgs-4-14

**15** Ahimaaz was in Naphtali; he also took Basmath the daughter of Solomon to wife: ^1kgs-4-15

**16** Baanah the son of Hushai was in Asher and in Aloth: ^1kgs-4-16

**17** Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah, in Issachar: ^1kgs-4-17

**18** Shimei the son of Elah, in Benjamin: ^1kgs-4-18

**19** Geber the son of Uri was in the country of Gilead, in the country of Sihon king of the Amorites, and of Og king of Bashan; and he was the only officer which was in the land. ^1kgs-4-19

**20** Judah and Israel were many, as the sand which is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking, and making merry. ^1kgs-4-20

**21** And Solomon reigned over all kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines, and unto the border of Egypt: they brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life. ^1kgs-4-21

**22** And Solomon's provision for one day was thirty measures of fine flour, and threescore measures of meal, ^1kgs-4-22

**23** Ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and an hundred sheep, beside harts, and roebucks, and fallowdeer, and fatted fowl. ^1kgs-4-23

**24** For he had dominion over all the region on this side the river, from Tiphsah even to Azzah, over all the kings on this side the river: and he had peace on all sides round about him. ^1kgs-4-24

**25** And Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beer-sheba, all the days of Solomon. ^1kgs-4-25

**26** And Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen. ^1kgs-4-26

**27** And those officers provided victual for king Solomon, and for all that came unto king Solomon's table, every man in his month: they lacked nothing. ^1kgs-4-27

**28** Barley also and straw for the horses and dromedaries brought they unto the place where the officers were, every man according to his charge. ^1kgs-4-28

**29** And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore. ^1kgs-4-29

**30** And Solomon's wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east country, and all the wisdom of Egypt. ^1kgs-4-30

**31** For he was wiser than all men; than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his fame was in all nations round about. ^1kgs-4-31

**32** And he spake three thousand proverbs: and his songs were a thousand and five. ^1kgs-4-32

**33** And he spake of trees, from the cedar tree that is in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall: he spake also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes. ^1kgs-4-33

**34** And there came of all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, which had heard of his wisdom. ^1kgs-4-34
