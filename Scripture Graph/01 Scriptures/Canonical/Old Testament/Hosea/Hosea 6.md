# Hosea 6

**1** Come, and let us return unto the LORD: for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up. ^hosea-6-1

**2** After two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight. ^hosea-6-2

**3** Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the LORD: his going forth is prepared as the morning; and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth. ^hosea-6-3

**4** O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee? O Judah, what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away. ^hosea-6-4

**5** Therefore have I hewed them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of my mouth: and thy judgments are as the light that goeth forth. ^hosea-6-5

**6** For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings. ^hosea-6-6

**7** But they like men have transgressed the covenant: there have they dealt treacherously against me. ^hosea-6-7

**8** Gilead is a city of them that work iniquity, and is polluted with blood. ^hosea-6-8

**9** And as troops of robbers wait for a man, so the company of priests murder in the way by consent: for they commit lewdness. ^hosea-6-9

**10** I have seen an horrible thing in the house of Israel: there is the whoredom of Ephraim, Israel is defiled. ^hosea-6-10

**11** Also, O Judah, he hath set an harvest for thee, when I returned the captivity of my people. ^hosea-6-11
