# 1 Samuel 3

**1** And the child Samuel ministered unto the LORD before Eli. And the word of the LORD was precious in those days; there was no open vision. ^1sam-3-1

**2** And it came to pass at that time, when Eli was laid down in his place, and his eyes began to wax dim, that he could not see; ^1sam-3-2

**3** And ere the lamp of God went out in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was, and Samuel was laid down to sleep; ^1sam-3-3

**4** That the LORD called Samuel: and he answered, Here am I. ^1sam-3-4

**5** And he ran unto Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou calledst me. And he said, I called not; lie down again. And he went and lay down. ^1sam-3-5

**6** And the LORD called yet again, Samuel. And Samuel arose and went to Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou didst call me. And he answered, I called not, my son; lie down again. ^1sam-3-6

**7** Now Samuel did not yet know the LORD, neither was the word of the LORD yet revealed unto him. ^1sam-3-7

**8** And the LORD called Samuel again the third time. And he arose and went to Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou didst call me. And Eli perceived that the LORD had called the child. ^1sam-3-8

**9** Therefore Eli said unto Samuel, Go, lie down: and it shall be, if he call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, LORD; for thy servant heareth. So Samuel went and lay down in his place. ^1sam-3-9

**10** And the LORD came, and stood, and called as at other times, Samuel, Samuel. Then Samuel answered, Speak; for thy servant heareth. ^1sam-3-10

**11** And the LORD said to Samuel, Behold, I will do a thing in Israel, at which both the ears of every one that heareth it shall tingle. ^1sam-3-11

**12** In that day I will perform against Eli all things which I have spoken concerning his house: when I begin, I will also make an end. ^1sam-3-12

**13** For I have told him that I will judge his house for ever for the iniquity which he knoweth; because his sons made themselves vile, and he restrained them not. ^1sam-3-13

**14** And therefore I have sworn unto the house of Eli, that the iniquity of Eli's house shall not be purged with sacrifice nor offering for ever. ^1sam-3-14

**15** And Samuel lay until the morning, and opened the doors of the house of the LORD. And Samuel feared to shew Eli the vision. ^1sam-3-15

**16** Then Eli called Samuel, and said, Samuel, my son. And he answered, Here am I. ^1sam-3-16

**17** And he said, What is the thing that the LORD hath said unto thee? I pray thee hide it not from me: God do so to thee, and more also, if thou hide any thing from me of all the things that he said unto thee. ^1sam-3-17

**18** And Samuel told him every whit, and hid nothing from him. And he said, It is the LORD: let him do what seemeth him good. ^1sam-3-18

**19** And Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him, and did let none of his words fall to the ground. ^1sam-3-19

**20** And all Israel from Dan even to Beer-sheba knew that Samuel was established to be a prophet of the LORD. ^1sam-3-20

**21** And the LORD appeared again in Shiloh: for the LORD revealed himself to Samuel in Shiloh by the word of the LORD. ^1sam-3-21
