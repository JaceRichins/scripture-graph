# Proverbs 12

**1** Whoso loveth instruction loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof is brutish. ^prov-12-1

**2** A good man obtaineth favour of the LORD: but a man of wicked devices will he condemn. ^prov-12-2

**3** A man shall not be established by wickedness: but the root of the righteous shall not be moved. ^prov-12-3

**4** A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband: but she that maketh ashamed is as rottenness in his bones. ^prov-12-4

**5** The thoughts of the righteous are right: but the counsels of the wicked are deceit. ^prov-12-5

**6** The words of the wicked are to lie in wait for blood: but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them. ^prov-12-6

**7** The wicked are overthrown, and are not: but the house of the righteous shall stand. ^prov-12-7

**8** A man shall be commended according to his wisdom: but he that is of a perverse heart shall be despised. ^prov-12-8

**9** He that is despised, and hath a servant, is better than he that honoureth himself, and lacketh bread. ^prov-12-9

**10** A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel. ^prov-12-10

**11** He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth vain persons is void of understanding. ^prov-12-11

**12** The wicked desireth the net of evil men: but the root of the righteous yieldeth fruit. ^prov-12-12

**13** The wicked is snared by the transgression of his lips: but the just shall come out of trouble. ^prov-12-13

**14** A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth: and the recompence of a man's hands shall be rendered unto him. ^prov-12-14

**15** The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise. ^prov-12-15

**16** A fool's wrath is presently known: but a prudent man covereth shame. ^prov-12-16

**17** He that speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit. ^prov-12-17

**18** There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise is health. ^prov-12-18

**19** The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue is but for a moment. ^prov-12-19

**20** Deceit is in the heart of them that imagine evil: but to the counsellors of peace is joy. ^prov-12-20

**21** There shall no evil happen to the just: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief. ^prov-12-21

**22** Lying lips are abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight. ^prov-12-22

**23** A prudent man concealeth knowledge: but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness. ^prov-12-23

**24** The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute. ^prov-12-24

**25** Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad. ^prov-12-25

**26** The righteous is more excellent than his neighbour: but the way of the wicked seduceth them. ^prov-12-26

**27** The slothful man roasteth not that which he took in hunting: but the substance of a diligent man is precious. ^prov-12-27

**28** In the way of righteousness is life; and in the pathway thereof there is no death. ^prov-12-28
