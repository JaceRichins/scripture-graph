# Proverbs 17

**1** Better is a dry morsel, and quietness therewith, than an house full of sacrifices with strife. ^prov-17-1

**2** A wise servant shall have rule over a son that causeth shame, and shall have part of the inheritance among the brethren. ^prov-17-2

**3** The fining pot is for silver, and the furnace for gold: but the LORD trieth the hearts. ^prov-17-3

**4** A wicked doer giveth heed to false lips; and a liar giveth ear to a naughty tongue. ^prov-17-4

**5** Whoso mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker: and he that is glad at calamities shall not be unpunished. ^prov-17-5

**6** Children's children are the crown of old men; and the glory of children are their fathers. ^prov-17-6

**7** Excellent speech becometh not a fool: much less do lying lips a prince. ^prov-17-7

**8** A gift is as a precious stone in the eyes of him that hath it: whithersoever it turneth, it prospereth. ^prov-17-8

**9** He that covereth a transgression seeketh love; but he that repeateth a matter separateth very friends. ^prov-17-9

**10** A reproof entereth more into a wise man than an hundred stripes into a fool. ^prov-17-10

**11** An evil man seeketh only rebellion: therefore a cruel messenger shall be sent against him. ^prov-17-11

**12** Let a bear robbed of her whelps meet a man, rather than a fool in his folly. ^prov-17-12

**13** Whoso rewardeth evil for good, evil shall not depart from his house. ^prov-17-13

**14** The beginning of strife is as when one letteth out water: therefore leave off contention, before it be meddled with. ^prov-17-14

**15** He that justifieth the wicked, and he that condemneth the just, even they both are abomination to the LORD. ^prov-17-15

**16** Wherefore is there a price in the hand of a fool to get wisdom, seeing he hath no heart to it? ^prov-17-16

**17** A friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity. ^prov-17-17

**18** A man void of understanding striketh hands, and becometh surety in the presence of his friend. ^prov-17-18

**19** He loveth transgression that loveth strife: and he that exalteth his gate seeketh destruction. ^prov-17-19

**20** He that hath a froward heart findeth no good: and he that hath a perverse tongue falleth into mischief. ^prov-17-20

**21** He that begetteth a fool doeth it to his sorrow: and the father of a fool hath no joy. ^prov-17-21

**22** A merry heart doeth good like a medicine: but a broken spirit drieth the bones. ^prov-17-22

**23** A wicked man taketh a gift out of the bosom to pervert the ways of judgment. ^prov-17-23

**24** Wisdom is before him that hath understanding; but the eyes of a fool are in the ends of the earth. ^prov-17-24

**25** A foolish son is a grief to his father, and bitterness to her that bare him. ^prov-17-25

**26** Also to punish the just is not good, nor to strike princes for equity. ^prov-17-26

**27** He that hath knowledge spareth his words: and a man of understanding is of an excellent spirit. ^prov-17-27

**28** Even a fool, when he holdeth his peace, is counted wise: and he that shutteth his lips is esteemed a man of understanding. ^prov-17-28
