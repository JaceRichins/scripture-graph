# Proverbs 30

**1** The words of Agur the son of Jakeh, even the prophecy: the man spake unto Ithiel, even unto Ithiel and Ucal, ^prov-30-1

**2** Surely I am more brutish than any man, and have not the understanding of a man. ^prov-30-2

**3** I neither learned wisdom, nor have the knowledge of the holy. ^prov-30-3

**4** Who hath ascended up into heaven, or descended? who hath gathered the wind in his fists? who hath bound the waters in a garment? who hath established all the ends of the earth? what is his name, and what is his son's name, if thou canst tell? ^prov-30-4

**5** Every word of God is pure: he is a shield unto them that put their trust in him. ^prov-30-5

**6** Add thou not unto his words, lest he reprove thee, and thou be found a liar. ^prov-30-6

**7** Two things have I required of thee; deny me them not before I die: ^prov-30-7

**8** Remove far from me vanity and lies: give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me: ^prov-30-8

**9** Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the LORD? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain. ^prov-30-9

**10** Accuse not a servant unto his master, lest he curse thee, and thou be found guilty. ^prov-30-10

**11** There is a generation that curseth their father, and doth not bless their mother. ^prov-30-11

**12** There is a generation that are pure in their own eyes, and yet is not washed from their filthiness. ^prov-30-12

**13** There is a generation, O how lofty are their eyes! and their eyelids are lifted up. ^prov-30-13

**14** There is a generation, whose teeth are as swords, and their jaw teeth as knives, to devour the poor from off the earth, and the needy from among men. ^prov-30-14

**15** The horseleach hath two daughters, crying, Give, give. There are three things that are never satisfied, yea, four things say not, It is enough: ^prov-30-15

**16** The grave; and the barren womb; the earth that is not filled with water; and the fire that saith not, It is enough. ^prov-30-16

**17** The eye that mocketh at his father, and despiseth to obey his mother, the ravens of the valley shall pick it out, and the young eagles shall eat it. ^prov-30-17

**18** There be three things which are too wonderful for me, yea, four which I know not: ^prov-30-18

**19** The way of an eagle in the air; the way of a serpent upon a rock; the way of a ship in the midst of the sea; and the way of a man with a maid. ^prov-30-19

**20** Such is the way of an adulterous woman; she eateth, and wipeth her mouth, and saith, I have done no wickedness. ^prov-30-20

**21** For three things the earth is disquieted, and for four which it cannot bear: ^prov-30-21

**22** For a servant when he reigneth; and a fool when he is filled with meat; ^prov-30-22

**23** For an odious woman when she is married; and an handmaid that is heir to her mistress. ^prov-30-23

**24** There be four things which are little upon the earth, but they are exceeding wise: ^prov-30-24

**25** The ants are a people not strong, yet they prepare their meat in the summer; ^prov-30-25

**26** The conies are but a feeble folk, yet make they their houses in the rocks; ^prov-30-26

**27** The locusts have no king, yet go they forth all of them by bands; ^prov-30-27

**28** The spider taketh hold with her hands, and is in kings' palaces. ^prov-30-28

**29** There be three things which go well, yea, four are comely in going: ^prov-30-29

**30** A lion which is strongest among beasts, and turneth not away for any; ^prov-30-30

**31** A greyhound; an he goat also; and a king, against whom there is no rising up. ^prov-30-31

**32** If thou hast done foolishly in lifting up thyself, or if thou hast thought evil, lay thine hand upon thy mouth. ^prov-30-32

**33** Surely the churning of milk bringeth forth butter, and the wringing of the nose bringeth forth blood: so the forcing of wrath bringeth forth strife. ^prov-30-33
