# Exodus 23

**1** Thou shalt not raise a false report: put not thine hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness. ^ex-23-1

**2** Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a cause to decline after many to wrest judgment: ^ex-23-2

**3** Neither shalt thou countenance a poor man in his cause. ^ex-23-3

**4** If thou meet thine enemy's ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again. ^ex-23-4

**5** If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lying under his burden, and wouldest forbear to help him, thou shalt surely help with him. ^ex-23-5

**6** Thou shalt not wrest the judgment of thy poor in his cause. ^ex-23-6

**7** Keep thee far from a false matter; and the innocent and righteous slay thou not: for I will not justify the wicked. ^ex-23-7

**8** And thou shalt take no gift: for the gift blindeth the wise, and perverteth the words of the righteous. ^ex-23-8

**9** Also thou shalt not oppress a stranger: for ye know the heart of a stranger, seeing ye were strangers in the land of Egypt. ^ex-23-9

**10** And six years thou shalt sow thy land, and shalt gather in the fruits thereof: ^ex-23-10

**11** But the seventh year thou shalt let it rest and lie still; that the poor of thy people may eat: and what they leave the beasts of the field shall eat. In like manner thou shalt deal with thy vineyard, and with thy oliveyard. ^ex-23-11

**12** Six days thou shalt do thy work, and on the seventh day thou shalt rest: that thine ox and thine ass may rest, and the son of thy handmaid, and the stranger, may be refreshed. ^ex-23-12

**13** And in all things that I have said unto you be circumspect: and make no mention of the name of other gods, neither let it be heard out of thy mouth. ^ex-23-13

**14** Three times thou shalt keep a feast unto me in the year. ^ex-23-14

**15** Thou shalt keep the feast of unleavened bread: (thou shalt eat unleavened bread seven days, as I commanded thee, in the time appointed of the month Abib; for in it thou camest out from Egypt: and none shall appear before me empty:) ^ex-23-15

**16** And the feast of harvest, the firstfruits of thy labours, which thou hast sown in the field: and the feast of ingathering, which is in the end of the year, when thou hast gathered in thy labours out of the field. ^ex-23-16

**17** Three times in the year all thy males shall appear before the Lord GOD. ^ex-23-17

**18** Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leavened bread; neither shall the fat of my sacrifice remain until the morning. ^ex-23-18

**19** The first of the firstfruits of thy land thou shalt bring into the house of the LORD thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother's milk. ^ex-23-19

**20** Behold, I send an Angel before thee, to keep thee in the way, and to bring thee into the place which I have prepared. ^ex-23-20

**21** Beware of him, and obey his voice, provoke him not; for he will not pardon your transgressions: for my name is in him. ^ex-23-21

**22** But if thou shalt indeed obey his voice, and do all that I speak; then I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries. ^ex-23-22

**23** For mine Angel shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Amorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites: and I will cut them off. ^ex-23-23

**24** Thou shalt not bow down to their gods, nor serve them, nor do after their works: but thou shalt utterly overthrow them, and quite break down their images. ^ex-23-24

**25** And ye shall serve the LORD your God, and he shall bless thy bread, and thy water; and I will take sickness away from the midst of thee. ^ex-23-25

**26** There shall nothing cast their young, nor be barren, in thy land: the number of thy days I will fulfil. ^ex-23-26

**27** I will send my fear before thee, and will destroy all the people to whom thou shalt come, and I will make all thine enemies turn their backs unto thee. ^ex-23-27

**28** And I will send hornets before thee, which shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite, from before thee. ^ex-23-28

**29** I will not drive them out from before thee in one year; lest the land become desolate, and the beast of the field multiply against thee. ^ex-23-29

**30** By little and little I will drive them out from before thee, until thou be increased, and inherit the land. ^ex-23-30

**31** And I will set thy bounds from the Red sea even unto the sea of the Philistines, and from the desert unto the river: for I will deliver the inhabitants of the land into your hand; and thou shalt drive them out before thee. ^ex-23-31

**32** Thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor with their gods. ^ex-23-32

**33** They shall not dwell in thy land, lest they make thee sin against me: for if thou serve their gods, it will surely be a snare unto thee. ^ex-23-33
