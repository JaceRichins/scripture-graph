# Genesis 5

**1** This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him; ^gen-5-1

**2** Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created. ^gen-5-2

**3** And Adam lived an hundred and thirty years, and begat a son in his own likeness, after his image; and called his name Seth: ^gen-5-3

**4** And the days of Adam after he had begotten Seth were eight hundred years: and he begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-4

**5** And all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred and thirty years: and he died. ^gen-5-5

**6** And Seth lived an hundred and five years, and begat Enos: ^gen-5-6

**7** And Seth lived after he begat Enos eight hundred and seven years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-7

**8** And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years: and he died. ^gen-5-8

**9** And Enos lived ninety years, and begat Cainan: ^gen-5-9

**10** And Enos lived after he begat Cainan eight hundred and fifteen years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-10

**11** And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years: and he died. ^gen-5-11

**12** And Cainan lived seventy years, and begat Mahalaleel: ^gen-5-12

**13** And Cainan lived after he begat Mahalaleel eight hundred and forty years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-13

**14** And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years: and he died. ^gen-5-14

**15** And Mahalaleel lived sixty and five years, and begat Jared: ^gen-5-15

**16** And Mahalaleel lived after he begat Jared eight hundred and thirty years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-16

**17** And all the days of Mahalaleel were eight hundred ninety and five years: and he died. ^gen-5-17

**18** And Jared lived an hundred sixty and two years, and he begat Enoch: ^gen-5-18

**19** And Jared lived after he begat Enoch eight hundred years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-19

**20** And all the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty and two years: and he died. ^gen-5-20

**21** And Enoch lived sixty and five years, and begat Methuselah: ^gen-5-21

**22** And Enoch walked with God after he begat Methuselah three hundred years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-22

**23** And all the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty and five years: ^gen-5-23

**24** And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him. ^gen-5-24

**25** And Methuselah lived an hundred eighty and seven years, and begat Lamech: ^gen-5-25

**26** And Methuselah lived after he begat Lamech seven hundred eighty and two years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-26

**27** And all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty and nine years: and he died. ^gen-5-27

**28** And Lamech lived an hundred eighty and two years, and begat a son: ^gen-5-28

**29** And he called his name Noah, saying, This same shall comfort us concerning our work and toil of our hands, because of the ground which the LORD hath cursed. ^gen-5-29

**30** And Lamech lived after he begat Noah five hundred ninety and five years, and begat sons and daughters: ^gen-5-30

**31** And all the days of Lamech were seven hundred seventy and seven years: and he died. ^gen-5-31

**32** And Noah was five hundred years old: and Noah begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^gen-5-32
