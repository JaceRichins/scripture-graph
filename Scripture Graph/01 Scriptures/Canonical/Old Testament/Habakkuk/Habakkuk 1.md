# Habakkuk 1

**1** The burden which Habakkuk the prophet did see. ^hab-1-1

**2** O LORD, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear! even cry out unto thee of violence, and thou wilt not save! ^hab-1-2

**3** Why dost thou shew me iniquity, and cause me to behold grievance? for spoiling and violence are before me: and there are that raise up strife and contention. ^hab-1-3

**4** Therefore the law is slacked, and judgment doth never go forth: for the wicked doth compass about the righteous; therefore wrong judgment proceedeth. ^hab-1-4

**5** Behold ye among the heathen, and regard, and wonder marvellously: for I will work a work in your days, which ye will not believe, though it be told you. ^hab-1-5

**6** For, lo, I raise up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hasty nation, which shall march through the breadth of the land, to possess the dwellingplaces that are not theirs. ^hab-1-6

**7** They are terrible and dreadful: their judgment and their dignity shall proceed of themselves. ^hab-1-7

**8** Their horses also are swifter than the leopards, and are more fierce than the evening wolves: and their horsemen shall spread themselves, and their horsemen shall come from far; they shall fly as the eagle that hasteth to eat. ^hab-1-8

**9** They shall come all for violence: their faces shall sup up as the east wind, and they shall gather the captivity as the sand. ^hab-1-9

**10** And they shall scoff at the kings, and the princes shall be a scorn unto them: they shall deride every strong hold; for they shall heap dust, and take it. ^hab-1-10

**11** Then shall his mind change, and he shall pass over, and offend, imputing this his power unto his god. ^hab-1-11

**12** Art thou not from everlasting, O LORD my God, mine Holy One? we shall not die. O LORD, thou hast ordained them for judgment; and, O mighty God, thou hast established them for correction. ^hab-1-12

**13** Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil, and canst not look on iniquity: wherefore lookest thou upon them that deal treacherously, and holdest thy tongue when the wicked devoureth the man that is more righteous than he? ^hab-1-13

**14** And makest men as the fishes of the sea, as the creeping things, that have no ruler over them? ^hab-1-14

**15** They take up all of them with the angle, they catch them in their net, and gather them in their drag: therefore they rejoice and are glad. ^hab-1-15

**16** Therefore they sacrifice unto their net, and burn incense unto their drag; because by them their portion is fat, and their meat plenteous. ^hab-1-16

**17** Shall they therefore empty their net, and not spare continually to slay the nations? ^hab-1-17
