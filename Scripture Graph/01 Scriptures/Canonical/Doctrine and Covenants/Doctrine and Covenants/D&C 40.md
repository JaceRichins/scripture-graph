# D&C 40

**1** Behold, verily I say unto you, that the heart of my servant James Covel was right before me, for he covenanted with me that he would obey my word. ^dc-40-1

**2** And he received the word with gladness, but straightway Satan tempted him; and the fear of persecution and the cares of the world caused him to reject the word. ^dc-40-2

**3** Wherefore he broke my covenant, and it remaineth with me to do with him as seemeth me good. Amen. ^dc-40-3
