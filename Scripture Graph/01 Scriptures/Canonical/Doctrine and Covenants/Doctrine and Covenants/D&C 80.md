# D&C 80

**1** Verily, thus saith the Lord unto you my servant Stephen Burnett: Go ye, go ye into the world and preach the gospel to every creature that cometh under the sound of your voice. ^dc-80-1

**2** And inasmuch as you desire a companion, I will give unto you my servant Eden Smith. ^dc-80-2

**3** Wherefore, go ye and preach my gospel, whether to the north or to the south, to the east or to the west, it mattereth not, for ye cannot go amiss. ^dc-80-3

**4** Therefore, declare the things which ye have heard, and verily believe, and know to be true. ^dc-80-4

**5** Behold, this is the will of him who hath called you, your Redeemer, even Jesus Christ. Amen. ^dc-80-5
