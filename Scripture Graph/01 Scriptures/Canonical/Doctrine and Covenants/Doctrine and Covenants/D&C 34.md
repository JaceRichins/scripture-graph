# D&C 34

**1** My son Orson, hearken and hear and behold what I, the Lord God, shall say unto you, even Jesus Christ your Redeemer; ^dc-34-1

**2** The light and the life of the world, a light which shineth in darkness and the darkness comprehendeth it not; ^dc-34-2

**3** Who so loved the world that he gave his own life, that as many as would believe might become the sons of God. Wherefore you are my son; ^dc-34-3

**4** And blessed are you because you have believed; ^dc-34-4

**5** And more blessed are you because you are called of me to preach my gospel— ^dc-34-5

**6** To lift up your voice as with the sound of a trump, both long and loud, and cry repentance unto a crooked and perverse generation, preparing the way of the Lord for his second coming. ^dc-34-6

**7** For behold, verily, verily, I say unto you, the time is soon at hand that I shall come in a cloud with power and great glory. ^dc-34-7

**8** And it shall be a great day at the time of my coming, for all nations shall tremble. ^dc-34-8

**9** But before that great day shall come, the sun shall be darkened, and the moon be turned into blood; and the stars shall refuse their shining, and some shall fall, and great destructions await the wicked. ^dc-34-9

**10** Wherefore, lift up your voice and spare not, for the Lord God hath spoken; therefore prophesy, and it shall be given by the power of the Holy Ghost. ^dc-34-10

**11** And if you are faithful, behold, I am with you until I come— ^dc-34-11

**12** And verily, verily, I say unto you, I come quickly. I am your Lord and your Redeemer. Even so. Amen. ^dc-34-12
