# D&C 22

**1** Behold, I say unto you that all old covenants have I caused to be done away in this thing; and this is a new and an everlasting covenant, even that which was from the beginning. ^dc-22-1

**2** Wherefore, although a man should be baptized an hundred times it availeth him nothing, for you cannot enter in at the strait gate by the law of Moses, neither by your dead works. ^dc-22-2

**3** For it is because of your dead works that I have caused this last covenant and this church to be built up unto me, even as in days of old. ^dc-22-3

**4** Wherefore, enter ye in at the gate, as I have commanded, and seek not to counsel your God. Amen. ^dc-22-4
