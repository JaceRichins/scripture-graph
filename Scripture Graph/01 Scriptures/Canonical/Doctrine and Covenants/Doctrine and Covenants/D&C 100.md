# D&C 100

**1** Verily, thus saith the Lord unto you, my friends Sidney and Joseph, your families are well; they are in mine hands, and I will do with them as seemeth me good; for in me there is all power. ^dc-100-1

**2** Therefore, follow me, and listen to the counsel which I shall give unto you. ^dc-100-2

**3** Behold, and lo, I have much people in this place, in the regions round about; and an effectual door shall be opened in the regions round about in this eastern land. ^dc-100-3

**4** Therefore, I, the Lord, have suffered you to come unto this place; for thus it was expedient in me for the salvation of souls. ^dc-100-4

**5** Therefore, verily I say unto you, lift up your voices unto this people; speak the thoughts that I shall put into your hearts, and you shall not be confounded before men; ^dc-100-5

**6** For it shall be given you in the very hour, yea, in the very moment, what ye shall say. ^dc-100-6

**7** But a commandment I give unto you, that ye shall declare whatsoever thing ye declare in my name, in solemnity of heart, in the spirit of meekness, in all things. ^dc-100-7

**8** And I give unto you this promise, that inasmuch as ye do this the Holy Ghost shall be shed forth in bearing record unto all things whatsoever ye shall say. ^dc-100-8

**9** And it is expedient in me that you, my servant Sidney, should be a spokesman unto this people; yea, verily, I will ordain you unto this calling, even to be a spokesman unto my servant Joseph. ^dc-100-9

**10** And I will give unto him power to be mighty in testimony. ^dc-100-10

**11** And I will give unto thee power to be mighty in expounding all scriptures, that thou mayest be a spokesman unto him, and he shall be a revelator unto thee, that thou mayest know the certainty of all things pertaining to the things of my kingdom on the earth. ^dc-100-11

**12** Therefore, continue your journey and let your hearts rejoice; for behold, and lo, I am with you even unto the end. ^dc-100-12

**13** And now I give unto you a word concerning Zion. Zion shall be redeemed, although she is chastened for a little season. ^dc-100-13

**14** Thy brethren, my servants Orson Hyde and John Gould, are in my hands; and inasmuch as they keep my commandments they shall be saved. ^dc-100-14

**15** Therefore, let your hearts be comforted; for all things shall work together for good to them that walk uprightly, and to the sanctification of the church. ^dc-100-15

**16** For I will raise up unto myself a pure people, that will serve me in righteousness; ^dc-100-16

**17** And all that call upon the name of the Lord, and keep his commandments, shall be saved. Even so. Amen. ^dc-100-17
