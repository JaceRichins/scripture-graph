# New Testament Allusion in D&C 81

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The promise of eternal life “in the mansions” prepared “in the house of my Father” closely echoes John 14:2, where Jesus speaks of mansions in his Father's house.

**Does not establish:** The echo does not by itself define the number, physical character, or relationship of the mansions to later teachings about kingdoms of glory.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may reflect a conventional Christian description of heaven derived generally from John 14 rather than a deliberate verse-level allusion.
- The shared language does not require every interpretive detail of John 14 to be imported into D&C 81.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 81#^dc-81-6|D&C 81:6]], [[John 14#^john-14-2|John 14:2]]
