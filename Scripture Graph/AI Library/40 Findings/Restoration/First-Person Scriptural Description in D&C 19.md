# First-Person Scriptural Description in D&C 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 19:18–19 is strong textual evidence that this revelation portrays Christ's redemptive suffering as involving both body and spirit, intense pain, bleeding, trembling, and willing completion. The chapter itself does not identify the geographical location or divide these sufferings into a precise chronology.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish that every detail occurred exclusively in Gethsemane or determine its relation in time to the Crucifixion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The description may encompass Christ's passion as a whole rather than referring exclusively to Gethsemane.
- The bitter-cup language may be theological and literary as well as descriptive of a particular episode.
