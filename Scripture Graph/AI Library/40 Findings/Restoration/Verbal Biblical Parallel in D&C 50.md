# Verbal Biblical Parallel in D&C 50

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The invitation to come and "reason together" closely parallels Isaiah 1:18. D&C 50 expands that inherited phrase into a face-to-face, question-driven explanation designed to produce understanding.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not establish the revelation's historical origin, authorship mechanism, or theological authority.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The phrase may have entered the revelation through familiar English biblical idiom without conscious citation of Isaiah.
- The shared wording alone does not determine whether the dependence is direct, remembered, or understood as revelatory continuity.
