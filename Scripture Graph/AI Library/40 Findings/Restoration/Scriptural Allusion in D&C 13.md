# Scriptural Allusion in D&C 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The distinctive combination of the sons of Levi, an offering, and righteousness makes dependence upon or conscious reuse of Malachi 3:3 highly likely.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not determine the precise nature, timing, or location of the promised offering.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages could draw on a shared biblical vocabulary about Levites and acceptable offerings.
- The allusion may function typologically rather than predict a restoration of every feature of ancient Levitical sacrifice.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 13#^dc-13-1|D&C 13:1]], [[Malachi 3#^mal-3-3|Malachi 3:3]]
