# Canonical Sequence in D&C 103

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence from the vineyard parable in D&C 101, through the mobilizing instructions in D&C 103, to the later postponement and interpretation in D&C 105 strongly supports reading D&C 103 as a preparation-stage revelation rather than as a narrative of completed redemption.

**Does not establish:** The sequence does not by itself prove either prophetic failure or supernatural fulfillment, and it does not show that the territorial redemption envisioned in D&C 103 occurred.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A skeptical historical reading may understand D&C 105 as a retrospective theological explanation for an expedition that did not accomplish its announced objective.
- A believing reading may understand the later postponement as an anticipated consequence of conditional promises and inadequate collective preparation.
- The sections arose at different stages of a developing crisis, so later language need not exhaust the original meaning of D&C 103.
