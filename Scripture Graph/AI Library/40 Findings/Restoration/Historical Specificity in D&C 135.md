# Historical Specificity in D&C 135

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The names, date, approximate time, location, sequence of deaths, and reported wounds make the account historically specific and testable, but specificity within one memorial account is not independent corroboration of every detail.

**Does not establish:** Textual specificity alone does not establish the accuracy of every reported circumstance or theological interpretation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some details could reflect imperfect recollection or rapid post-event reporting.
- The account's commemorative purpose may shape its selection and characterization of events.
- Independent contemporary records would be needed to corroborate particular details.
