# Literary And Theological in D&C 43

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The deliberate pairing of the voices of judgment and mercy within the catalogue supports reading the warning as both punitive and invitational, not as an announcement of destruction alone.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every natural disaster is a particular divine punishment or that humans can reliably identify such punishments.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The catalogue may primarily intensify the rhetoric of warning rather than define a systematic relationship between mercy and judgment.
- Individual calamities in the list may be conventional apocalyptic imagery rather than identifiable historical judgments.
