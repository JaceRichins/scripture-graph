# Literary Structure in D&C 96

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter has two discernible instruction units: verses 1–5 concern the place and its purposes, while verses 6–9 concern John Johnson. Each unit closes with “Even so. Amen,” and both connect material administration to bringing forth the word.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not establish whether the two units were delivered together, composed according to a formal literary pattern, or later joined editorially.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two closings may preserve originally distinct revelatory segments.
- The shared vocabulary may be formulaic rather than evidence of carefully planned composition.
