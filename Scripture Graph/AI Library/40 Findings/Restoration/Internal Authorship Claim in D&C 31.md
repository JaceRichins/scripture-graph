# Internal Authorship Claim in D&C 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter explicitly represents its words as coming from Jesus Christ rather than from human beings. This is a textual claim of divine authorship; the assertion by itself is not independent historical proof of that authorship.

**Does not establish:** An internal claim cannot, without additional evidence, independently demonstrate supernatural authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A believer may accept the statement as authentic divine self-identification.
- A historical-critical reader may understand it as a revelatory claim mediated or composed by a human author.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 31#^dc-31-13|D&C 31:13]]
