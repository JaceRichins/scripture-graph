# Internal Literary Structure in D&C 122

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from social opposition to bodily danger, family rupture, death, cosmic obstruction, and hell is strong internal evidence that the passage is rhetorically shaped to imagine an increasingly comprehensive ordeal.

**Does not establish:** The progression does not establish a chiasm, a particular source, divine authorship, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The escalation could arise naturally from impassioned accumulation rather than from a formally planned design.
- Modern verse division and punctuation may make the progression appear more formally segmented than it originally was.
