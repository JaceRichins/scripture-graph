# Scriptural Intertext in D&C 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 24:13-18 contains a dense cluster of features from New Testament missionary commissions: healing and casting out devils, departure from rejecting communities with dust as testimony, and travel without purse, scrip, extra clothing, or staves. The concentration and sequence strongly support intentional engagement with the biblical mission-discourse tradition.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not by themselves establish divine origin, supernatural revelation, or independence from the King James Bible.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may consciously adapt the King James Bible as an authoritative missionary model.
- A believing interpretation may understand the parallels as a revealed restoration of the apostolic pattern.
- Some individual similarities could arise from general Christian missionary practice, although that explanation is weaker for the whole cluster.
