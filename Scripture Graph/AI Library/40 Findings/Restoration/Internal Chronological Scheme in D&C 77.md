# Internal Chronological Scheme in D&C 77

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter clearly attests that its own interpretive system divides the earth's temporal existence into seven thousand-year periods and assigns Revelation events within that scheme.

**Does not establish:** The internal scheme does not independently establish the earth's scientific age, an exact date for creation, or a calculable date for the Second Coming.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The thousand-year periods may function as schematic sacred history rather than as a calendar for computing dates.
- Readers may differ over the starting points and exact boundaries of the periods.
