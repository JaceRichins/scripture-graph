# Explicit Scriptural Reuse in D&C 101

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 101:81-84 explicitly introduces and closely reproduces the narrative sequence and distinctive language of the widow and unjust judge in Luke 18:1-5, then applies the parable to the children of Zion's repeated petitions for redress.

**Does not establish:** This intertextual relationship does not by itself establish the historical origin, antiquity, or supernatural character of the revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may reflect direct adaptation of the English Bible familiar to Joseph Smith and his audience.
- A believing interpretation may understand the passage as revelatory reuse and application of an earlier divine teaching.
