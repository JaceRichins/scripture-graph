# Literary Structure in D&C 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of the claim that God's work continues—first in the opening thesis and again after the rebuke—strongly supports reading the chapter as a unified movement from divine constancy, through human failure, to continued divine purpose.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish a particular compositional process, date, or ancient literary form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated theme may reflect ordinary thematic coherence rather than a deliberately designed formal structure.
- Later transcription or editorial shaping could contribute to the chapter's present organization.
