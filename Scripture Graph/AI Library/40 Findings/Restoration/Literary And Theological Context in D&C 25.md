# Literary And Theological Context in D&C 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of the hymn assignment immediately before the declaration that righteous song is prayer supplies strong internal evidence that hymnody is treated here as an act of worship and communion with God, rather than merely as decoration or entertainment.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not define acceptable musical styles, make singing identical to every other form of prayer, or promise any particular temporal outcome from sacred music.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two verses also serve different functions: one gives an administrative assignment, while the other offers a general theology of sacred song.
- Calling song prayer need not imply that every hymn or musical performance functions as prayer.
