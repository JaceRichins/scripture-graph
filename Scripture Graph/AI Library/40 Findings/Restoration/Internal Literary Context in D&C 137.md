# Internal Literary Context in D&C 137

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The immediate sequence from the narrator's question about unbaptized Alvin to the Lord's declaration concerning people who die without gospel knowledge is strong internal evidence that verses 7-9 answer the problem raised in verse 6.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the mechanism by which the dead receive ordinances, nor does it independently explain the later doctrine and practice of baptism for the dead.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The declaration may be a broader teaching prompted by the question rather than an explanation limited specifically to Alvin.
- The answer establishes the promised outcome but leaves the role or timing of ordinances unexplained.
