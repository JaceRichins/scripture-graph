# Immediate Literary Context in D&C 64

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The surrounding discussion of delayed fulfillment, sustained labor, Zion, and willing obedience is strong contextual evidence that the saying about great things proceeding from small things encourages persistent participation in building Zion, rather than promising that every minor undertaking will become great.

**Does not establish:** It does not promise that every small action produces a large result, nor does it identify which modern projects qualify as the 'great work.'

**Alternative explanations:**
- The maxim may intentionally invite broader application beyond the immediate work of establishing Zion.
- The 'great work' could include the wider Restoration rather than only the provisioning and settlement concerns discussed nearby.
