# Internal Textual Organization in D&C 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shift from Edward's individual commission to instructions concerning others is good internal evidence that the revelation serves both a personal and an institutional function: it authorizes a repeatable pattern of willing acceptance, ordination, and missionary sending.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish how consistently the procedure was implemented, who qualified in practice, or the complete institutional meaning of ordination.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The universal language may be primarily exhortational rather than a complete administrative policy.
- Eligibility conditions and actual historical implementation may have depended on rules not stated in this chapter.
