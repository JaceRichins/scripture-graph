# Verbal Intertextuality in D&C 66

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close verbal correspondence between D&C 66:11 and Isaiah's repeated Zion-return formula is strong evidence of biblical intertextuality in the English wording of the revelation.

**Does not establish:** The echo does not by itself establish the mode of revelation, ancient authorship, or whether Isaiah 35, Isaiah 51, or both were consciously invoked.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may reflect general familiarity with King James Bible diction rather than a deliberate citation of one Isaiah passage.
- A shared religious vocabulary could produce the resemblance without a consciously constructed allusion.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 66#^dc-66-11|D&C 66:11]], [[Isaiah 35#^isa-35-10|Isaiah 35:10]], [[Isaiah 51#^isa-51-11|Isaiah 51:11]]
