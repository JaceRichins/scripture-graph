# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 83

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit link between consecrations, a maintained storehouse, and provision for vulnerable people is strong internal evidence that this revelation understands consecration as including organized material care rather than only private generosity.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the storehouse's precise administration, the program's historical implementation or success, or the exact form present-day church welfare must take.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may prescribe a particular historical arrangement rather than a universal administrative blueprint.
- The term "storehouse" may encompass different practical arrangements rather than require one fixed institutional form.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 83#^dc-83-5|D&C 83:5]], [[D&C 83#^dc-83-6|D&C 83:6]]
