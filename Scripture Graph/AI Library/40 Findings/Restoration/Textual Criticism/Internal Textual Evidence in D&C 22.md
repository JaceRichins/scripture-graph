# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides textual support for the official heading's doctrine of authoritative baptism, but that support is indirect: the revealed text invokes God's command, covenant, church, and an ineffective prior baptism without explicitly using the terms "authority" or "priesthood."

**Does not establish:** The wording alone does not define priesthood authority, identify who performed the earlier baptisms, or specify every reason those baptisms were unacceptable.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The rejected baptisms could be deficient because they belong to superseded covenants or are classified as dead works, rather than solely because the officiants lacked authority.
- Authority may be assumed from the surrounding historical setting even though it is not articulated in these four verses.
