# Textual Teaching in D&C 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within this translation instruction, D&C 9 strongly supports a model in which disciplined mental effort and petitionary prayer work together: merely asking is criticized, while studying and then asking are commanded. Its immediate scope is sacred translation, so extending it into a universal test for every personal decision requires additional argument.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every correct decision produces warmth, that every incorrect decision produces mental stupor, or that feelings alone reliably identify divine revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The procedure may be tailored specifically to the attempted translation rather than intended as a general decision-making formula.
- The bodily and cognitive language may be phenomenological or metaphorical rather than a fixed physiological description.
- Emotional warmth, confusion, or forgetfulness can arise from ordinary psychological causes, so similar experiences outside this narrative are not self-authenticating.
