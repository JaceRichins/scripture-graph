# Intertextual Parallel in D&C 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The millennial sacrament image in D&C 27:5 ('the hour cometh that I will drink of the fruit of the vine with you on the earth') closely echoes the Synoptic Last Supper saying in which Jesus tells the apostles he will not drink 'this fruit of the vine' again until he drinks it new with them 'in my Father's kingdom' (Matthew 26:29).

**Does not establish:** Does not establish literary dependence on a specific Gospel text versus independent revelation drawing on shared biblical idiom.

**Alternative explanations:**
- 'Fruit of the vine' and future covenant-meal language were common Second Temple/early Christian liturgical idiom, so the echo may reflect shared vocabulary rather than direct dependence on Matthew's specific wording.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 27#^dc-27-5|D&C 27:5]], [[Matthew 26#^matt-26-29|Matthew 26:29]]
