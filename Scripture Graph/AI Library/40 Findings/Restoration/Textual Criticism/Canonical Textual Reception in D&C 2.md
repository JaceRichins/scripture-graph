# Canonical Textual Reception in D&C 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence and interpretation of this prophecy in Joseph Smith—History 1, D&C 110, D&C 128, and D&C 138 provide strong textual evidence that Elijah's mission and intergenerational covenant work became a coherent theme within Latter-day Saint scripture.

**Does not establish:** This textual pattern does not establish the historicity of Elijah's appearance, the antiquity of D&C 2's distinctive wording, or the truth of every later doctrinal inference.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence may reflect later authors or editors interpreting earlier wording rather than every implication being present in D&C 2 itself.
- Repeated canonical language demonstrates reception and doctrinal development but does not independently verify the narrated supernatural events.
