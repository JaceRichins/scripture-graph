# Textual Interdependence in D&C 95

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The unusually dense verbal and conceptual correspondence between D&C 95:8-9 and Luke 24:49 is strong evidence that the Jerusalem apostolic commission is being invoked deliberately as a pattern for the latter-day community.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish that the Kirtland and Jerusalem experiences were identical in ritual form, chronology, or every theological detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may primarily reflect conscious reuse of familiar biblical language rather than a claim that the two historical events were identical.
- The word endow in D&C 95 need not specify every later-developed feature of Latter-day Saint temple practice.
