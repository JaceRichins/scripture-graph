# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Emma's commissions to expound scripture, exhort the church, write, learn much, and select sacred hymns provide strong textual evidence that this revelation envisions substantial intellectual, devotional, and public religious work for her, not merely private emotional support.

**Does not establish:** These commissions do not by themselves establish equivalence with any particular priesthood office, define the institutional authority involved, or settle modern debates about ecclesiastical roles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The assignments may have been understood as exceptional and personal rather than as a general institutional model.
- The word ordained can designate appointment or setting apart without implying ordination to a priesthood office.
- The scope and setting of expounding and exhorting are not specified in the chapter.
