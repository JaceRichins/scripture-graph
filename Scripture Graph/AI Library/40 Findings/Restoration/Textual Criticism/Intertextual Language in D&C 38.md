# Intertextual Language in D&C 38

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The titles "Great I AM" and "Alpha and Omega" connect the speaker's self-presentation with biblical divine-title traditions in Exodus 3 and Revelation 22. This is strong evidence of deliberate biblical framing, but its implications for the revelation's origin depend on prior assumptions.

**Does not establish:** The parallels alone do not establish supernatural origin, historical antiquity, or independence from the English Bible.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may represent revelatory continuity with biblical divine speech.
- The language may reflect conscious composition from familiar English Bible terminology.
- Both theological continuity and literary dependence may be present.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 38#^dc-38-1|D&C 38:1]], [[Exodus 3#^ex-3-14|Exodus 3:14]], [[Revelation 22#^rev-22-13|Revelation 22:13]]
