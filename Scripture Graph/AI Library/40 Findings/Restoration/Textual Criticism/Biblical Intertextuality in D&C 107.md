# Biblical Intertextuality in D&C 107

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 107:19 and Hebrews 12:22-24 share an unusually concentrated cluster: general assembly, church of the Firstborn, God, Jesus as mediator, and the new covenant. This is strong evidence that the wording of D&C 107:19 participates in the language of Hebrews 12.

**Does not establish:** The verbal relationship alone does not establish a particular revelatory mechanism, compositional process, or ancient versus modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be a conscious scriptural quotation or adaptation.
- It may reflect scriptural language recalled from memory rather than direct copying.
- A believer may understand revelation as being expressed through familiar biblical vocabulary.
