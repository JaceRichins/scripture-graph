# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 51

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated instructions concerning portions, written security, deeds, agents, repayment, surplus storage, and administrative support provide strong internal evidence that the revelation addresses a concrete resource-governance problem rather than presenting only an abstract theology of communal life.

**Does not establish:** This evidence does not establish successful historical implementation, economic effectiveness, divine origin, or any larger authorship claim.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Detailed prescriptions can describe an intended system without showing that it was fully implemented.
- Administrative detail alone cannot establish that the arrangements were effective or durable.
- A concrete institutional setting does not by itself determine the revelation's divine or human origin.
