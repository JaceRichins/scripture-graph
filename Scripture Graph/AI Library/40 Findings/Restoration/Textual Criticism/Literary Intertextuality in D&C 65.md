# Literary Intertextuality in D&C 65

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close concentration of identifiable biblical language and imagery provides strong internal evidence that D&C 65 deliberately engages biblical texts, especially Daniel's kingdom-stone vision, Isaiah's preparation summons, and New Testament wedding imagery.

**Does not establish:** The intertextuality alone does not establish divine revelation, fulfillment of Daniel's prophecy, or the historical success of the kingdom prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The biblical language may reflect Joseph Smith's conscious familiarity with the King James Bible.
- Believers may understand the reuse as revelatory adaptation of earlier prophecy.
- Some verbal resemblance may arise from the shared religious vocabulary of early nineteenth-century English.
