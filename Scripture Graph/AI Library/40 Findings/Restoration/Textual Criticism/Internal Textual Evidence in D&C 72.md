# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 72

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated requirements for accounts, records, certificates, recommendations, and communication between local leaders and the bishop in Zion provide strong internal textual evidence that the revelation prescribes documented institutional accountability, not merely private spiritual faithfulness.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the system's historical implementation, effectiveness, or the universal applicability of every procedural detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may prescribe an intended system without demonstrating how fully or successfully it was implemented.
- Some procedures may respond specifically to migration and economic conditions associated with early Zion rather than define an unchanged system for every period.
