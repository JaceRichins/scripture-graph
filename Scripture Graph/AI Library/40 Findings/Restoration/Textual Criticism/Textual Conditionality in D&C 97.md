# Textual Conditionality in D&C 97

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated conditional statements provide strong textual evidence that the chapter presents Zion's prosperity and escape from judgment as dependent on holiness, obedience, and refraining from further sin, rather than as unconditional guarantees.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every later blessing or affliction can be assigned a simple one-to-one cause, nor does it establish when the threatened judgments were or will be fulfilled.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The conditional language may function partly as exhortation and may not provide an exhaustive explanation for every later experience of the community.
- Some promised outcomes may have collective or eschatological dimensions not exhausted by immediate historical events.
