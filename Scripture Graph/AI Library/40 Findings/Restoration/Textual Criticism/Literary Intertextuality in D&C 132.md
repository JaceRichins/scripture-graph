# Literary Intertextuality in D&C 132

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 132:24 closely reworks John 17:3 by defining eternal life through knowing God and Jesus Christ. The close wording is strong evidence of deliberate scriptural intertextuality.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish divine origin, ancient provenance, ethical legitimacy, or the truth of the surrounding doctrinal claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage could reproduce familiar biblical language from memory rather than carefully quote a written text.
- The relationship establishes literary dependence or allusion but does not determine whether the reuse is revelatory.
