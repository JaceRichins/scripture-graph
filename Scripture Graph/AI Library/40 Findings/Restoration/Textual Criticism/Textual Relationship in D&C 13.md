# Textual Relationship in D&C 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close textual identity between D&C 13:1 and Joseph Smith—History 1:69 strongly supports reading them as two canonical presentations of the same reported conferral rather than as separate priesthood declarations.

**Does not establish:** The duplicate wording does not independently establish that the reported angelic appearance or ordination occurred.

**Alternative explanations:**
- One canonical presentation may have been copied or editorially extracted from the other.
- Their textual identity alone does not independently corroborate the historical event because both presentations arise within the same Restoration record tradition.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 13#^dc-13-1|D&C 13:1]], [[Joseph Smith—History 1#^jsh-1-69|Joseph Smith—History 1:69]]
