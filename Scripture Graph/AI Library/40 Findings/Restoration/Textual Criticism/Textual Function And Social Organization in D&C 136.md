# Textual Function And Social Organization in D&C 136

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The density and specificity of the operational instructions are strong internal evidence that the chapter functions as a covenantal migration charter: it assigns authority, apportions burdens, inventories supplies, creates advance parties, and regulates conduct under stress.

**Does not establish:** The practical detail does not by itself establish divine origin, successful implementation, or the historical results of the migration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The instructions can be understood primarily as pragmatic administrative planning expressed in religious language.
- Some provisions may codify practices already developed by the migrating community rather than introduce entirely new arrangements.
