# Cumulative Textual Evidence in D&C 104

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Assigned duties, mandatory accounting, pooled receipts, consent-controlled withdrawals, and disciplinary review strongly support interpreting consecration here as an organized covenantal practice, not merely an inward attitude or occasional charitable gift.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that this precise organization is universally required, that all consecration must use pooled treasuries, or that participants implemented every command.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The chapter may regulate one historically specific organization without prescribing the same institutional form for every later community.
- Its disciplinary rhetoric may describe an ideal more fully than actual enforcement.
