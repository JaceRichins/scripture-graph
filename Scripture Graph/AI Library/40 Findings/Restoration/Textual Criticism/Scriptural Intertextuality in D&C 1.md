# Scriptural Intertextuality in D&C 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The distinctive combination of a sword bathed in heaven and judgment involving Idumea provides strong textual evidence of a literary relationship between D&C 1:13, 36 and Isaiah 34:5.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish how the language entered the revelation, prove divine origin, or show that every detail of Isaiah 34 has the same referent here.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may be a conscious reuse of familiar King James Bible language.
- A believing interpretation may understand the shared language as revelatory reuse of Isaiah's prophecy.
- The vocabulary could function as inherited apocalyptic idiom without requiring a detailed verse-by-verse correspondence.
