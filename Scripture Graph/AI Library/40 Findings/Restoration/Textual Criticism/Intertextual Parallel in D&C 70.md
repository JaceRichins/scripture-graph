# Intertextual Parallel in D&C 70

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The principle that spiritual laborers are 'worthy of their hire' echoes New Testament language (e.g., Luke 10:7; 1 Timothy 5:18) applying the same idiom to gospel ministers, suggesting D&C 70 draws on an established biblical formula for supporting those who labor in spiritual things.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish a direct textual citation of a specific biblical verse, only a shared idiom and theme.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The phrase 'worthy of his hire' is a common English idiom independent of any deliberate biblical allusion.
- The parallel may reflect Joseph Smith's general familiarity with KJV phrasing rather than an intentional citation.
