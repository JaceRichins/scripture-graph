# Internal Textual Synthesis in D&C 105

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's combination of acknowledged communal failure, acceptance of faithful participants' offering, postponement, training, temple endowment, land purchase, legal redress, and peace provides strong internal evidence that the text reframes an unrealized immediate redemption as a longer program of moral and institutional preparation.

**Does not establish:** This internal coherence does not establish divine authorship, the correctness of the chapter's causal diagnosis, or the historical accuracy of every implied circumstance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The chapter may be read primarily as a practical administrative response to changed circumstances rather than as a deliberately unified theological program.
- Believing readers may understand the postponement as revealed divine instruction, while critical readers may understand it as retrospective legitimation of an unsuccessful expedition.
- Some elements of the longer program may reflect several concerns placed together rather than one tightly integrated design.
