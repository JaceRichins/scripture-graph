# Explicit Textual Dependence in D&C 109

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The prayer explicitly introduces D&C 88:117–120 as a prior revelation and closely reproduces its commands concerning a solemn assembly, learning by study and faith, organizing a house of God, and conducting incomings and outgoings in the Lord's name. This strongly supports conscious textual dependence on D&C 88.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the divine origin of either revelation or determine the human process by which the wording was composed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may have been reproduced from memory rather than copied from a written text.
- The two sections may reflect the same author's established liturgical vocabulary.
