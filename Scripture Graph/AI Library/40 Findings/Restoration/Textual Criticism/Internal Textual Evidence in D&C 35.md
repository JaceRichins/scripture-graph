# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter, Joseph's commission concerning revealed mysteries is explicitly conditional upon his abiding in Christ, with the stated possibility that another could be planted in his stead.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Joseph was replaced, identify a possible replacement, or settle broader questions about prophetic authority or succession.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The warning may function primarily as covenantal exhortation rather than as a prediction that replacement was likely.
- Later theological systems may define the duration or scope of Joseph's commission using evidence outside this chapter.
