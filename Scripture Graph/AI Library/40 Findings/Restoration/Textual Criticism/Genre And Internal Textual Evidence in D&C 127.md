# Genre And Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 127

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The first-person references to unavailable public preaching, sending instruction by mail, lack of time, an alert enemy, and a closing subscription strongly support classifying the chapter as a situational letter rather than a context-free doctrinal treatise.

**Does not establish:** The literary form does not independently prove the truth of the persecution allegations, divine authorship, or the ordinance's heavenly efficacy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The preserved chapter may be an excerpt or edited presentation of a longer historical document.
- Epistolary form alone cannot independently establish authorship or the historical accuracy of every reported circumstance.
