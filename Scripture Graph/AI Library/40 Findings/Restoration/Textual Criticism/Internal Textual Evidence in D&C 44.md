# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 44

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chain of purpose clauses in verses 4–5 is strong internal evidence that civil organization is valued instrumentally: it is expected to restrict hostile power, preserve the community, and enable obedience to divine law.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish that every civil structure is divinely approved, that legal incorporation guarantees safety, or that the passage supplies a universal political program.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may express immediate administrative prudence rather than a developed theology of church-state relations.
- The anticipated protection may depend principally on increased membership or social standing, with legal organization only one part of the process.
