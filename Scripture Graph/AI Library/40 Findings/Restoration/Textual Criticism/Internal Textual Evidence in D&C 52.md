# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 52

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The warning that Satan is deceiving the nations, followed by an explicit pattern for knowing spirits, is strong internal textual evidence that discernment of religious manifestations is a central concern of this portion of the revelation. It provides only limited evidence about which particular experiences or controversies prompted the instruction.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the identity of any deceptive manifestation, the infallibility of human application of the pattern, or the revelation's divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may be a general pastoral warning rather than a response to identifiable incidents.
- Its language may draw chiefly on familiar biblical warnings about deception.
- The precise historical target cannot be recovered from this chapter alone.
