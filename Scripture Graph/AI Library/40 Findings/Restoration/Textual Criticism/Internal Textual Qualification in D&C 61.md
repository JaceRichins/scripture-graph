# Internal Textual Qualification in D&C 61

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter’s internal qualifications strongly support reading its water instructions as differentiated rather than as an unqualified prohibition on every journey by water: faithful persons receive a promise of preservation, some missionaries may later choose land or water, and a person empowered to command the waters is to follow the Spirit.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself determine how the instructions apply to later generations or eliminate every possible literal or supernatural reading of the dangers.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The permissions may be narrow exceptions within a generally restrictive rule.
- Different instructions may apply to different persons because of distinct callings rather than expressing a general principle about water travel.
- The future warning in verse 15 may be broader in scope than the immediate travel directions.
