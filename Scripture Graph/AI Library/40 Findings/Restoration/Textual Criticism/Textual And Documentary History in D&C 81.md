# Textual And Documentary History in D&C 81

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Joseph Smith Papers transcription of the 15 March 1832 revelation addresses Jesse Gause, whereas the present canonical text addresses Frederick G. Williams. The documentary comparison therefore supports the historical observation that the named recipient was changed in the transmitted, canonical form.

**Does not establish:** The changed name does not establish that the entire revelation was composed later, that its duties were considered person-specific, or that historical and canonical forms must serve identical editorial purposes.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The substitution can be understood as updating an office-centered revelation for Gause's successor rather than preserving the original historical addressee in the canonical text.
- The documentary evidence establishes the differing names but does not, by itself, disclose the exact date, agent, or full rationale for every stage of the change.
