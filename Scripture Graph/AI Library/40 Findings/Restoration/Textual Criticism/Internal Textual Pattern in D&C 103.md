# Internal Textual Pattern in D&C 103

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated conditional clauses provide strong internal textual evidence that participation in the promised prevailing and continued possession is not depicted as automatic: obedience is required, and renewed pollution carries renewed loss.

**Does not establish:** This textual pattern does not establish that the promised restoration occurred historically, nor does it independently establish the revelation's divine origin or a general rule that all adversity results from disobedience.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The conditions may function primarily as exhortative covenant rhetoric rather than as a precise prediction of historical outcomes.
- The repeated language of divine decree may be read as guaranteeing Zion's ultimate redemption while leaving only its timing or individual participation conditional.
