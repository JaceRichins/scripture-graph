# Textual Intertextuality in D&C 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of distinctive language from Acts 2:40 and Jude 1:23 is strong evidence that the canonical wording of D&C 36:6 is deliberately scriptural or deeply shaped by King James Bible diction.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallels do not by themselves establish a particular theory of revelation, composition, historical authenticity, or divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be a conscious quotation or synthesis of biblical passages.
- The language may arise from an early nineteenth-century religious register thoroughly saturated with the King James Bible.
- The canonical wording may reflect some combination of revelatory composition, oral phrasing, scribal transmission, and later editing.
