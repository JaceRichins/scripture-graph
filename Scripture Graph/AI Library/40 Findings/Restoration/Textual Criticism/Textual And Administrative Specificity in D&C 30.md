# Textual And Administrative Specificity in D&C 30

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Named companions, defined authority relationships, local fields of labor, and clauses such as “until I command” provide strong textual evidence that the chapter presents individualized administrative and missionary instructions rather than a single generic sermon.

**Does not establish:** Administrative specificity does not by itself establish divine origin, historical accuracy beyond the document's claims, or successful completion of the assignments.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some personal or administrative detail could reflect editorial framing or compilation.
- Specificity alone does not demonstrate that the instructions were carried out.
