# Intertextual in D&C 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The near word-for-word correspondence between D&C 20:77/79 and the Moroni 4-5 sacrament prayers is evidence of a direct literary/liturgical relationship between the Book of Mormon text and the earliest Restoration sacrament ordinance, but does not by itself establish the direction of dependence or resolve claims about the ancient origin of the underlying Nephite liturgy.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which text (Book of Mormon or D&C 20) is the literary source for the other, nor does it independently verify the antiquity of the Nephite sacrament prayer.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Consistent liturgical phrasing could reflect a single translator/revelator (Joseph Smith) producing similar English wording across two separate revelatory processes rather than one text quoting the other.
- Both texts may echo common biblical Lord's Supper language, producing convergence without direct dependence.
