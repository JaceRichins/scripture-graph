# Intertextual Verbal Resemblance in D&C 115

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The cluster of arising, shining, light, and nations in D&C 115:5 resembles Isaiah 60:1-3 strongly enough to support an Isaianic echo, but not strongly enough by itself to establish a formal quotation or a specific mode of textual dependence.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a verbatim quotation, the precise intention behind the resemblance, ancient authorship, or any larger historical claim.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may draw on common biblical imagery without deliberate quotation.
- The wording may reflect Joseph Smith's familiarity with the King James Bible rather than an explicitly signaled allusion.
- The revelation may deliberately adapt Isaiah while changing its immediate institutional application.
