# Internal Textual Distinction in D&C 113

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within this chapter, the description of the rod as a servant in Christ's hands is strong internal evidence against treating the rod as merely another designation for Christ.

**Does not establish:** It does not identify the unnamed servant, determine whether the rod and root are the same person, or prove a particular historical fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Prophetic symbols can overlap, so a reader might still argue that one person occupies more than one symbolic role.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 113#^dc-113-2|D&C 113:2]], [[D&C 113#^dc-113-4|D&C 113:4]]
