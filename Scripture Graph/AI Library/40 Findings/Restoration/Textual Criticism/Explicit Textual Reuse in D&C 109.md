# Explicit Textual Reuse in D&C 109

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 109 explicitly introduces D&C 88:117–120 as a prior revelation and incorporates its language concerning a solemn assembly, learning by study and faith, the functions of the house, and conduct when entering or leaving. The later petitions adapt that revealed program into requests for the dedicated house.

**Does not establish:** The reuse does not by itself establish the precise compositional process, editorial history, or broader claims about divine or prophetic authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may have been recited from communal memory rather than copied from a written document.
- The present textual alignment could partly reflect later editorial standardization, although D&C 109 itself explicitly calls the material a prior revelation.
