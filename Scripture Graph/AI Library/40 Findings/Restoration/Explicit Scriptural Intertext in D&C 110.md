# Explicit Scriptural Intertext in D&C 110

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit attribution to Malachi and the close reuse of Malachi 4:5-6 provide strong textual evidence that D&C 110 deliberately presents Elijah's commission as related to that prophecy.

**Does not establish:** The textual correspondence alone does not establish that the reported vision occurred historically or that Malachi's prophecy was supernaturally fulfilled in this event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The report may frame or narrate an experience through familiar biblical language.
- The correspondence could reflect conscious theological interpretation rather than independently demonstrating supernatural fulfillment.
- Some readers may understand the passage typologically rather than as a literal appearance of the ancient prophet.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 110#^dc-110-14|D&C 110:14-16]], [[Malachi 4#^mal-4-5|Malachi 4:5-6]]
