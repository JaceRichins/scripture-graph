# Paraphrastic Parallel in D&C 45

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 45:37-38 restates the Olivet Discourse's fig-tree parable ('when they begin to shoot forth, and their leaves are yet tender, that summer is now nigh at hand') in wording close to Matthew 24:32.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish an independent historical source for the parable beyond the canonical Gospel account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parable is framed in D&C 45 as Christ's own prior teaching being repeated, so the parallel wording may be presented in-narrative as self-quotation rather than literary borrowing from the Gospel text as such.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 45#^dc-45-37|D&C 45:37]], [[Matthew 24#^matt-24-32|Matthew 24:32]]
