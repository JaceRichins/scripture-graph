# Internal Literary Challenge in D&C 67

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Considered as evidence outside the revelation's own stated logic, an inability to produce a comparable commandment would offer only limited support for divine origin. The chapter provides no stable comparison criteria and does not report either the attempted composition or its evaluation.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish divine authorship, ancient provenance, historical accuracy, or the impossibility of comparable human composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Producing a text in an unfamiliar revelatory genre may be difficult without implying supernatural origin.
- The participants may have faced social or religious pressure that affected their performance or judgment.
- Standards of likeness or literary quality may be subjective.
- Failure by one selected participant would not show that no other person could produce a comparable text.
