# Book Of Mormon Intertext in D&C 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The final commission to prune the vineyard with a mighty pruning 'for the last time' closely parallels the climactic final labor in Zenos's vineyard allegory. This strongly supports a deliberate Book of Mormon intertext and frames the missionaries' work as urgent, collective, latter-stage labor.

**Does not establish:** The intertext does not establish ancient authorship of the Book of Mormon or the revelatory status of D&C 24.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Vineyard labor is a widespread biblical and Christian metaphor and need not derive exclusively from Jacob 5.
- The wording may combine Jacob 5 with biblical vineyard passages familiar from Isaiah and the New Testament.
- The connection could be deliberate literary reuse without implying anything about supernatural origin.
