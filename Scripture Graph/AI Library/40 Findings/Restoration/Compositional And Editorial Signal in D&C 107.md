# Compositional And Editorial Signal in D&C 107

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verses 58 and 77 introduce ensuing material as wording from an already identifiable revelation or commandment. Together with the chapter's shifts between institutional rules and sacred history, these formulas support reading the present chapter as an arrangement of related instructional blocks.

**Does not establish:** These internal signals do not establish the dates, original boundaries, number of source documents, or identity of an editor.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The formulas could be rhetorical reminders of prior teaching within a substantially unified discourse.
- The topic shifts may reflect progressive revelation or dictation rather than later editorial compilation.
- Related material could have been arranged at more than one stage, which the canonical wording does not date.
