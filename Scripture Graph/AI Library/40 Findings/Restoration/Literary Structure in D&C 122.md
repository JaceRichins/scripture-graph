# Literary Structure in D&C 122

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extended conditional syntax, the intimate interruption by the child's speech, the abrupt two-sentence christological pivot, the inferential “Therefore,” and the repeated assurances of permanent divine presence jointly provide strong evidence that the chapter is rhetorically shaped around endurance under escalating opposition.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a rigid formal pattern, a particular compositional process, historical authenticity, or divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition and framing may arise naturally from extemporaneous or scriptural-style consolation rather than from a consciously planned literary design.
- The divine-presence repetition may function as ordinary reassurance without constituting a formal inclusio.
