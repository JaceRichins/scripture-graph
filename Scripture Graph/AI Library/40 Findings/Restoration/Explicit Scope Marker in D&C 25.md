# Explicit Scope Marker in D&C 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The revelation is explicitly addressed to Emma and contains assignments tied to named individuals, yet its closing sentence declares that the Lord's voice in the revelation is unto all. This strongly supports broader application of its covenantal and devotional principles while leaving open whether every personal assignment is universal.

**Does not establish:** The closing statement does not establish that every reader must become a scribe, compile hymns, or fulfill Emma's marriage-specific instructions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The closing statement may universalize the revelation's moral principles without universalizing Emma's particular assignments.
- It may invite all readers to hear divine instruction while still preserving the document's original personal address.
- Different commands within the chapter may have different degrees of general applicability.
