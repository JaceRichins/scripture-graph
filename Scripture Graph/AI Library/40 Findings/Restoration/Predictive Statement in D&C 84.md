# Predictive Statement in D&C 84

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verses 4-5 express a concrete temporal expectation: a temple at the appointed place is said to be reared “in this generation,” and the generation is said not all to pass before a house is built. On the face of these verses, this is predictive language rather than merely a command to attempt construction.

**Does not establish:** The wording alone does not establish whether, when, or how the expectation was fulfilled; that judgment requires independently sourced historical and interpretive analysis.

**Alternative explanations:**
- “Generation” may be assigned a broader or nonstandard duration.
- The prediction may be interpreted as implicitly conditional on the Saints' faithfulness.
- A proposed fulfillment may identify a different house, place, or sense of construction than the most immediate reading suggests.
