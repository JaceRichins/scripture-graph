# Clustered Verbal And Imagistic Parallel in D&C 110

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of Revelation 1 imagery and titles in D&C 110:3-4 is strong evidence of a meaningful literary relationship or a deliberately shared scriptural vocabulary for portraying the glorified Christ.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not determine how the vision originated, prove supernatural causation, or by itself demonstrate ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may consciously imitate familiar biblical language.
- A visionary experience may have been remembered or narrated through scripture-saturated vocabulary.
- The similarities may derive from a shared Christian descriptive tradition rather than direct dependence on Revelation.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 110#^dc-110-3|D&C 110:3-4]], [[Revelation 1#^rev-1-14|Revelation 1:14-18]]
