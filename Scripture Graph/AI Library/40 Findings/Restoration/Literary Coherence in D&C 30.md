# Literary Coherence in D&C 30

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of fear-of-man language and commands to speak across the three addresses provides moderately strong evidence that the chapter has thematic coherence despite its changes of recipient.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence does not establish formal literary design, a particular compositional history, divine origin, or the historical fulfillment of the commissions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Fear and proclamation may simply be conventional language recurring independently in missionary revelations.
- Thematic coherence may result from editorial grouping rather than from an intentionally designed literary unit.
