# Internal Thematic Structure in D&C 133

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's internal sequence provides strong evidence that worldwide gospel proclamation is presented as part of preparation for the Lord's coming: elders are sent to the nations, the everlasting gospel goes to every people, and the servants announce approaching judgment.

**Does not establish:** This relationship does not establish a timetable for the Second Coming or prove that missionary expansion is a precise chronological indicator.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passages may be thematically associated without defining a strict chronological sequence.
- Preparation in the chapter includes sanctification, gathering, prayer, and repentance as well as missionary proclamation.
