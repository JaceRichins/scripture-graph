# Internal Personal Specificity in D&C 66

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The unusually specific reference to William's temptation is strong internal evidence that the revelation presents itself as personally tailored rather than as wholly generic missionary counsel.

**Does not establish:** The specificity alone does not establish divine origin, supernatural access to private information, historical fulfillment, or the precise source of the information.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author or recorder could have learned of William's concern through ordinary conversation or observation.
- A broadly applicable sexual warning could have been personalized rhetorically.
- The surviving wording could reflect later recollection or editorial shaping.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 66#^dc-66-10|D&C 66:10]]
