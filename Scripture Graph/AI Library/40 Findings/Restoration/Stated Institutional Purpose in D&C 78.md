# Stated Institutional Purpose in D&C 78

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 14 presents the church's ability to stand independent despite coming tribulation as an intended result of the commanded preparation and organization.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish political sovereignty, economic isolation, rejection of outside commerce, or the precise institutional mechanism by which independence would be achieved.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Independence may primarily denote spiritual fidelity rather than financial self-sufficiency.
- The unusual phrase 'above all other creatures' leaves the intended comparison less precise than a modern institutional-independence claim would suggest.
