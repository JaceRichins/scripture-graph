# Documentary Chronology in D&C 87

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Joseph Smith Papers identifies this revelation with the date 25 December 1832, well before the American Civil War. That documentary chronology is essential when assessing the passage's predictive character.

**Does not establish:** The prewar date does not by itself establish divine inspiration, exhaustive fulfillment, or a miraculous absence of relevant contemporary information.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An 1832 origin establishes prewar composition but does not show that the prediction arose supernaturally.
- The surviving record may be a copy rather than the original dictation and therefore cannot establish word-for-word fixity at the first delivery.
- The contemporary Nullification Crisis supplied an immediate political setting for anticipating conflict involving South Carolina.
