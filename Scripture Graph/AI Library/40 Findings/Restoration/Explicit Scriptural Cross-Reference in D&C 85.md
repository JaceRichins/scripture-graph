# Explicit Scriptural Cross-Reference in D&C 85

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 85:12 explicitly invokes Ezra 2:61–62, where claimants connected with priestly families cannot find their genealogical registration and are excluded from priesthood. This supplies strong evidence that D&C 85 uses recorded genealogy as an analogy for recognized eligibility and communal standing.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not establish that loss of a temporal inheritance or recognized priestly standing is necessarily equivalent to an irreversible judgment about eternal salvation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The analogy may concern only the general principle that documentary enrollment governs eligibility, rather than matching every detail of Ezra's priestly case.
- The reference may emphasize institutional order more than eternal judgment.
