# Institutional Self-Designation in D&C 115

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 4 is direct textual evidence that this revelation authorizes the institutional designation The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It is not, by itself, evidence that every historical or informal usage immediately conformed to that designation.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish how consistently the full name was used afterward or settle every modern dispute about abbreviated references.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verse may prescribe an official theological name without regulating every shortened or descriptive form of reference.
- Later conclusions about naming practice require evidence beyond this verse.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 115#^dc-115-4|D&C 115:4]]
