# Internal Thematic Evidence in D&C 105

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As internal textual evidence, the repeated causal statements strongly support the reading that Zion's redemption is delayed because the covenant community is not yet socially and spiritually prepared, rather than because territorial recovery alone is the chapter's controlling concern.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that internal failings were the only historical cause of delay or that external violence, law, logistics, and political conditions were unimportant.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The preparation language may supplement rather than replace a primarily territorial or strategic concern.
- The revelation may be interpreting a particular failed undertaking without giving a complete account of every historical cause.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 105#^dc-105-2|D&C 105:2-13]], [[D&C 105#^dc-105-31|D&C 105:31-37]]
