# Intra-Canonical Formulaic Parallel in D&C 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The first six verses of D&C 14 closely track the corresponding sequence in D&C 6: announcement of a marvelous work, the penetrating word, a harvest call, divine calling of reapers, asking and knocking, and establishing Zion. This is strong evidence of deliberate formulaic continuity within these revelations.

**Does not establish:** The repetition alone cannot determine the direction of dependence, the precise compositional process, or whether the language is revelatory, editorial, or both.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Repeated language may signify a consistent divine commission adapted to different recipients.
- It may reflect reuse of an established revelatory template.
- It may reflect common authorship or editorial standardization.
