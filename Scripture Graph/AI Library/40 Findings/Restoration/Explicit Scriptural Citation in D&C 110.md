# Explicit Scriptural Citation in D&C 110

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit naming of Malachi and close reuse of Malachi 4:5-6 are decisive evidence that the account consciously frames Elijah's commission as prophetic fulfillment. The text's fulfillment claim is not independent verification that the reported appearance occurred.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the vision's historicity, divine causation, or the correctness of later doctrinal applications.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment framing could have been composed by a narrator interpreting an experience through Malachi.
- The framing could reflect editorial arrangement rather than words independently recoverable from the reported visitor.
- A skeptical account may explain the passage as literary appropriation without a supernatural event.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 110#^dc-110-14|D&C 110:14-16]], [[Malachi 4#^mal-4-5|Malachi 4:5-6]]
