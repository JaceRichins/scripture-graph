# Identity And Agency Language in Exodus 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Angel's possession of the divine name and the demand to obey his voice support an interpretation that he represents divine authority unusually closely. The passage itself, however, does not identify the Angel as Jesus Christ or explain the metaphysics of that representation.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not establish that the Angel is the premortal Christ, a particular named angel, or God himself.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Angel may be an authorized heavenly messenger who bears God's name without sharing God's identity.
- The Angel may function as a literary expression of God's guiding presence.
- Later Jewish and Christian traditions can supply identities not specified by this chapter.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 23#^ex-23-20|Exodus 23:20]], [[Exodus 23#^ex-23-21|Exodus 23:21]], [[Exodus 23#^ex-23-22|Exodus 23:22]]
