# Recurrent Thematic Language in Ecclesiastes 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of rejoicing in present work and goods supports reading the chapter as offering a constructive response to human limitation, rather than treating it as unqualified nihilism.

**Does not establish:** The refrain does not eliminate the chapter's pessimistic observations or provide a complete doctrine of happiness, vocation, or material prosperity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The advice may express resignation to limited goods rather than a strongly affirmative theology of work.
- The refrain may be intentionally provisional within the book's continuing argument.
