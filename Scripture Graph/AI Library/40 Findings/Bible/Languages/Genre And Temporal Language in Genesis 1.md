# Genre And Temporal Language in Genesis 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated sequence of numbered days, divine speeches, fulfillment formulas, evaluations, and evening-morning closures is strong evidence of a carefully patterned account. Those literary features alone do not decide whether the days are ordinary-length days, extended periods, analogical divine workdays, or elements of a literary framework.

**Does not establish:** The literary pattern does not establish a scientific chronology, the age of the earth, or the duration and mechanism of each creative act.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The evening-morning formula and numbered days may be read as ordinary calendar days.
- The arrangement may be chronological while also being highly literary.
- The days may function primarily as a theological framework rather than a measurement of physical duration.
