# Lexical And Contextual Wordplay in Hosea 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The juxtaposition of Ishi and Baali in Hosea 2:16, immediately followed by removal of the names of Baalim in Hosea 2:17, is strong textual evidence for deliberate wordplay: the renewed relationship is described with a husband-term separated from language compromised by association with Baal.

**Does not establish:** The wordplay does not by itself establish the precise historical form of Israelite Baal worship or a universal ban on every use of baal as a common noun.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The contrast may emphasize relational intimacy over mastery without making the Baal association its sole point.
- The language may oppose a particular cultic use of Baal rather than prohibit every ordinary use of the underlying title.
