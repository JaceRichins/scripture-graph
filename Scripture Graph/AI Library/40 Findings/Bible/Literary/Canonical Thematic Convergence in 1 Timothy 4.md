# Canonical Thematic Convergence in 1 Timothy 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The thematic convergence of 1 Timothy 4:3-5 with Romans 14:2-6 and Colossians 2:20-23 shows that disputes over food restriction, piety, and human religious regulations occur elsewhere in the New Testament.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not identify the opponents in 1 Timothy or prove that every passage addresses the same movement.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passages may address distinct communities and different reasons for abstinence.
- Romans emphasizes mutual tolerance in a disputed practice, while 1 Timothy directly condemns particular teachers.
- Shared themes do not prove dependence by one writer on another.
