# Explicit Scriptural Allusion in Hebrews 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hebrews 6:13-14 explicitly attributes to God's promise to Abraham wording drawn from the oath associated with Genesis 22:16-17. This is strong evidence for a deliberate scriptural allusion and for the Abrahamic promise functioning as the chapter's model of reliable divine commitment.

**Does not establish:** The allusion does not establish the authorship of Hebrews, prove every proposed interpretation of the Abrahamic covenant, or show that Abraham received every aspect of the promise during his mortal life.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may have reached the writer through a familiar Greek scriptural form or liturgical usage rather than through independent consultation of a manuscript.
- The allusion establishes literary dependence on the Abraham tradition but does not by itself determine the date or personal identity of Hebrews' author.
