# Epistolary Self-Description in 1 Peter 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The phrase 'By Silvanus' is internal evidence that Silvanus participated in producing or transmitting the letter, but it does not specify whether he served as carrier, secretary, collaborator, or some combination of these roles.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not establish Silvanus's exact role or resolve questions about the letter's authorship and style.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Silvanus may have been only the letter carrier.
- Silvanus may have acted as an amanuensis or stylistic secretary.
- Silvanus may have contributed more substantially to composition.
