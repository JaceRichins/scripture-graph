# Inner-Biblical Reception in Isaiah 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Revelation 3:7 applies the key-of-David and irreversible opening-and-shutting language to Christ. This supplies a clear canonical basis for a Christian typological or christological reading of Eliakim's authority, while the immediate named referent in Isaiah remains Eliakim.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every detail about Eliakim or the falling nail is a direct prophecy about Jesus, nor that Eliakim is absent as the oracle's immediate historical referent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Revelation may reuse suitable royal-administrative imagery without claiming that Isaiah's oracle was originally an exclusive prediction of Jesus.
- Eliakim may function as an analogy or type rather than as a concealed direct reference to the Messiah.
