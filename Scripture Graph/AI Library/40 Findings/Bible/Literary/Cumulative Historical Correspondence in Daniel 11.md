# Cumulative Historical Correspondence in Daniel 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence in verses 5–20 is widely identified with Ptolemaic-Seleucid conflicts: the failed marriage of Berenice and Antiochus II, Ptolemy III’s retaliatory invasion, later campaigns of Antiochus III, the marriage involving Cleopatra I, and the short reign of Seleucus IV. The cumulative sequence is substantially more specific than a generic prediction of war.

**Does not establish:** The cumulative fit does not prove that every clause has been correctly mapped or, by itself, settle the date and mode of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may represent detailed revelation before the events rather than retrospective composition.
- Some individual clauses and royal identifications are uncertain even if the broad Ptolemaic-Seleucid setting is correct.
- A typological reading can treat the events as patterns that recur beyond their initial historical setting.
