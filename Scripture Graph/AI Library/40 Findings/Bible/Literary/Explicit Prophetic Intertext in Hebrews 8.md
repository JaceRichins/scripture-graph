# Explicit Prophetic Intertext in Hebrews 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah's pre-Christian promise of a new covenant provides strong textual evidence for the narrower claim made in Hebrews: Israel's own prophetic scriptures anticipated a covenantal development beyond the exodus covenant. It does not by itself settle every question about which Mosaic practices cease, continue, or are transformed.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the rejection of Israel, the abolition of all Old Testament instruction, a precise date for the epistle, or the truth of every later theological model of covenant replacement.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah's oracle may be read primarily as the renewal of Israel's covenant after national failure rather than the replacement of Israel by another people.
- Different interpreters identify differently which features of the earlier covenant are rendered obsolete.
