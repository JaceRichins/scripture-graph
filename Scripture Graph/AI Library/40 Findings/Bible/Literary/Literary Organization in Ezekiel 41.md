# Literary Organization in Ezekiel 41

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated measurements, repeated encircling language, room distinctions, and continuous decorative program strongly support reading the chapter as a deliberate literary portrayal of ordered sacred space rather than as an unstructured inventory.

**Does not establish:** Literary and numerical order does not establish that the design was physically buildable, historically constructed, or intended as a complete construction blueprint.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The ordering may primarily serve practical architectural description rather than encode a separate symbolic system.
- Some apparent symmetry may result from conventional schematic reporting.
