# Intra-Narrative Prediction And Fulfillment in John 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John's later account narrates a third denial by Peter followed immediately by the cock crowing, creating a direct literary fulfillment of Jesus' prediction in John 13:38. This is strong evidence of deliberate narrative correspondence but, because prediction and fulfillment occur in the same Gospel, only limited independent evidence for historical foreknowledge.

**Does not establish:** The internal correspondence alone does not establish when the prediction originated or independently prove supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prediction may have been formulated or sharpened in light of the known denial tradition.
- The author may have arranged inherited material to emphasize fulfillment.
- The correspondence can serve a literary and theological purpose without resolving every historical question.
