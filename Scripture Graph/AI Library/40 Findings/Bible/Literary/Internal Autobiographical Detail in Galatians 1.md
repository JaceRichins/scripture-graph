# Internal Autobiographical Detail in Galatians 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The named places, elapsed period, length of visit, and limited named contacts provide concrete internal support for the claim Paul is arguing: that his commission and gospel did not originate with the Jerusalem apostles. These details are historically useful but remain Paul's own advocacy rather than independent corroboration.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Paul had no human religious influence, that the itinerary is exhaustive, or that every chronological reconstruction based on it is certain.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be selecting details that best serve his defense rather than supplying a complete itinerary.
- The account can allow unreported human contact even though Paul denies receiving his gospel or commission from human authorities.
- The starting point and exact inclusiveness of the expression "after three years" are not specified.
