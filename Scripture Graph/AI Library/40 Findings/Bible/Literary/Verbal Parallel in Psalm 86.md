# Verbal Parallel in Psalm 86

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Psalm 86:14 and Psalm 54:3 share the sequence of enemies rising against the speaker, seeking his soul, and failing to set God before them. This is strong evidence of shared wording but only moderate evidence for direct dependence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish common authorship, which psalm came first, or the historical identity of either speaker's enemies.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both texts may draw independently on conventional lament language.
- One psalm may depend on the other, but the verbal evidence alone cannot establish direction.
- The wording may reflect later editorial or liturgical standardization.
