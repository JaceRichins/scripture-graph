# Internal Literary Structure in Matthew 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated sequence of practice, wrong public motive, received human reward, private alternative, and the Father's reward is strong internal evidence that Matthew 6:1–18 is deliberately structured around a three-part treatment of almsgiving, prayer, and fasting.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the date, authorship, compositional history, or exact historical setting of the discourse.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition could partly reflect conventional oral teaching formulas rather than a structure created at one stage of composition.
- The three practices may have circulated as separate teachings before being arranged together.
