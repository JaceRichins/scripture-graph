# Literary-Theological Pattern in 1 Kings 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's explicit appeal to the law of Moses, conditional dynastic promise, bloodguilt reasoning, and fulfillment notice strongly support the conclusion that the narrator interprets royal politics through a covenantal and prophetic framework.

**Does not establish:** This pattern does not by itself establish the date, unity, or precise authorship of Kings, nor does literary fulfillment language independently prove supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The theological notices may reflect editorial shaping of older political traditions.
- Political decisions may have had pragmatic motives even when the narrator explains them covenantally.
