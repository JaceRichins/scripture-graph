# Ordinance Sequence in Acts 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In the Samaritan episode, baptism in the name of the Lord Jesus and reception of the Holy Ghost are narrated as distinguishable events. Peter and John pray and lay hands upon people who have already been baptized, after which they receive the Holy Ghost.

**Does not establish:** This episode alone does not establish a complete universal ordinance policy, the precise office required to confer the Holy Ghost, or that every conversion must reproduce the same timing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some interpreters understand the delay as an exceptional sign authenticating the incorporation of Samaritans rather than a universal sequence.
- Other interpreters treat the episode as evidence for a regular post-baptismal laying on of hands.
- The chapter does not explain why Philip's baptisms were not accompanied immediately by reception of the Holy Ghost.
