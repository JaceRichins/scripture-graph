# Canonical Parallel Comparison in 2 Samuel 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The parallel accounts use different immediate attributions for the impulse to conduct the census: 2 Samuel says the LORD moved David, whereas 1 Chronicles says Satan provoked David. This is strong evidence of distinct narrative or theological framing, but the texts do not explain whether they intend competing or complementary levels of causation.

**Does not establish:** The wording difference alone does not establish that the accounts are logically irreconcilable or determine a complete doctrine of divine and human agency.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The accounts may describe different levels of causation, with divine permission or judgment operating through an adversarial agent.
- Chronicles may deliberately reframe the older account for theological reasons.
- Differences may reflect the development or transmission of distinct textual traditions.

**Scripture:** [[2 Samuel 24#^2sam-24-1|2 Samuel 24:1]], [[1 Chronicles 21#^1chr-21-1|1 Chronicles 21:1]]
