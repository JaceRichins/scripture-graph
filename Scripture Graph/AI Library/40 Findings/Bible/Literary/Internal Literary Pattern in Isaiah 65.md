# Internal Literary Pattern in Isaiah 65

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated repetitions of calling, answering, naming, eating, drinking, rejoicing, and crying provide strong internal evidence that the chapter's contrast between rebels and servants is a sustained literary design rather than a collection of unrelated promises and threats.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish a particular date, compositional history, author, or fulfillment scheme.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may arise naturally from recurring prophetic themes rather than from a tightly planned whole.
- The chapter's present coherence could partly reflect editorial arrangement of previously distinct materials.
