# Narrative Structure in 2 Chronicles 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the Chronicler's presentation, the chapter strongly supports an integrated model of reform: removing rival worship, restoring prescribed temple service, financing its ministers, and administering the resulting resources are treated as parts of one undertaking.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself prove the historical completeness, chronology, or institutional dating of every reported measure.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The arrangement may reflect the Chronicler's theological and literary program rather than the exact sequence of historical implementation.
- Some administrative details may summarize institutions from more than one period.
