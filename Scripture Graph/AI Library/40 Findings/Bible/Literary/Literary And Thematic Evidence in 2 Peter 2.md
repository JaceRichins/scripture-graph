# Literary And Thematic Evidence in 2 Peter 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The arrangement of punitive and rescuing examples supplies strong textual support for reading final judgment and deliverance as a central organizing theme of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a complete doctrine of the afterlife, the chronology of final judgment, or that faithful people will avoid every mortal trial.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The examples may principally serve as warnings against false teachers rather than as a comprehensive account of final judgment.
- The sequence is rhetorical and does not by itself define the timing or mechanics of judgment.
