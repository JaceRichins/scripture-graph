# External Historical Corroboration in Mark 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Josephus independently reports that Herod Antipas imprisoned and executed John the Baptist. This is substantial external corroboration for the basic association of John's death with Herod, while Josephus explains the execution through Herod's fear of John's political influence rather than Mark's banquet, oath, and Herodias narrative.

**Does not establish:** Josephus does not establish the historicity of the dance, oath, request for John's head, or every chronological and motivational detail in Mark.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Josephus and Mark may preserve distinct interpretive traditions about the same execution.
- One account may emphasize a public political rationale while the other preserves a court-level precipitating event.
- The shared core does not independently corroborate Mark's banquet details.

**Scripture:** [[Mark 6#^mark-6-14|Mark 6:14-29]]

**Sources:**
- Flavius Josephus — *Jewish Antiquities, Book 18, sections 116-119* (c. 93-94 CE)
