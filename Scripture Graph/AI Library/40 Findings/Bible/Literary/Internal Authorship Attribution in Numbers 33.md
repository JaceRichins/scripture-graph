# Internal Authorship Attribution in Numbers 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Numbers 33:2 is direct textual evidence that the chapter presents the itinerary as a record written by Moses under divine command. Because this is an internal attribution rather than independent corroboration, it is strong evidence for the text's self-presentation but weak evidence by itself for the historical authorship or date of the chapter's final form.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish that Moses composed the entire chapter, the whole book of Numbers, or the chapter's final literary form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later compiler may have incorporated an older itinerary associated with Moses.
- The statement may function as conventional attribution within the narrative.
- A Mosaic core and later editorial shaping are compatible with the wording.
