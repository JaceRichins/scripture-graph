# Internal Prophecy-Fulfillment Correspondence in 1 Kings 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The announcement against Baasha and the narrator's later fulfillment statement form a clear word-and-event correspondence within the chapter. This strongly supports deliberate prophetic framing in the received narrative, while providing much weaker evidence by itself for the oracle's date relative to the events.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish when the oracle was recorded, independent foreknowledge, or direct divine causation of Zimri's violence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment notice could reflect retrospective theological composition or editing.
- An earlier oracle may have been preserved and applied to events after they occurred.
- The correspondence may combine remembered prediction with later literary shaping.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 16#^1kgs-16-1|1 Kings 16:1-4]], [[1 Kings 16#^1kgs-16-11|1 Kings 16:11-12]]
