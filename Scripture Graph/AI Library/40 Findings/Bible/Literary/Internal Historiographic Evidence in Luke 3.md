# Internal Historiographic Evidence in Luke 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of named rulers and offices is strong internal evidence that Luke intends to place John's ministry within public history rather than present it as an undated legend. It is only moderate evidence for the accuracy of every chronological or political detail.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not by itself establish an exact modern date, eyewitness authorship, or the accuracy of every title and chronological detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Named officials can create historical verisimilitude without guaranteeing factual precision.
- Luke may have inherited some or all of the synchronism from prior tradition.
- Ancient regnal-year calculations can differ according to the starting point and counting method used.
