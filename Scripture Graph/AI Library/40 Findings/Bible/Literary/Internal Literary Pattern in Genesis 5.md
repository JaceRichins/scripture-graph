# Internal Literary Pattern in Genesis 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Enoch's entry conspicuously departs from the recurring death formula: it twice says that he walked with God and concludes that he "was not" because God took him. This is strong internal literary evidence that the narrator marks Enoch's departure as exceptional, but Genesis 5 alone does not define its mechanism.

**Does not establish:** The passage by itself does not establish where Enoch went, the physical process involved, or a general doctrine governing translation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Read in isolation, the compressed wording could be understood as an unexplained disappearance or divine removal without specifying bodily translation.
- The later interpretation that Enoch did not experience death is explicit in Hebrews 11:5, but that clarification is not stated in the same terms within Genesis 5.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 5#^gen-5-22|Genesis 5:22-24]]
