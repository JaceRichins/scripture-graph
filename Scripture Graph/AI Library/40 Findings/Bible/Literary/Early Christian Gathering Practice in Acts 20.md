# Early Christian Gathering Practice in Acts 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 20 supplies strong evidence that at least one early Christian community met on the first day of the week for teaching and breaking bread. It is weaker evidence for a universal weekly rule, a formal transfer of Sabbath status, or the exact ritual meaning of the meal.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish that all Christians had replaced seventh-day Sabbath observance with a first-day Sabbath.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The meeting may have been scheduled exceptionally because Paul was departing the next day.
- Breaking bread may denote a common meal, a sacramental observance, or both.
- The passage reports one gathering without prescribing a universal worship calendar.

**Scripture:** [[Acts 20#^acts-20-7|Acts 20:7]], [[Acts 20#^acts-20-11|Acts 20:11]]
