# Poetic Pattern in Psalm 139

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated contrasts in verses 7–12 provide strong textual evidence that the poem portrays divine presence as inescapable and divine perception as unhindered. They support a comprehensive claim within the poem, although they do not by themselves define a complete metaphysical doctrine of omnipresence.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not specify the metaphysical mechanics of God's presence or settle every later doctrinal question about divine embodiment and location.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The extremes may be poetic amplification rather than an attempt to formulate systematic theology.
- The passage may emphasize the speaker's personal inability to escape rather than making an exhaustive proposition about every possible realm.
