# Internal Theological Evidence in Romans 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 2 is strong textual evidence that deeds matter in divine judgment. By itself, however, the chapter does not define the complete relationship among deeds, grace, faith, atonement, and justification, so it cannot establish a works-only doctrine of salvation.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish salvation by unaided moral effort or settle Paul's wider account of justification.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some interpreters understand the obedient people in verses 7 and 13 as a real category enabled by grace.
- Others understand the description as a hypothetical standard that exposes universal failure.
- Another reading treats deeds as evidence disclosed at judgment rather than the independent basis of salvation.
