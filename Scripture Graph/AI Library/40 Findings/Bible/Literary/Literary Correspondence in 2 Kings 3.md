# Literary Correspondence in 2 Kings 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated correspondence between the oracle and the outcome strongly supports a deliberate oracle-fulfillment pattern within the received narrative: water arrives, Moab is defeated, and the reported devastation repeats the announced treatment of cities, land, wells, and trees.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence does not independently establish the oracle's date, the account's complete historicity, or supernatural causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A narrator or editor could have shaped the oracle and battle report into correspondence after the events.
- A historical oracle may have been preserved and understood as fulfilled.
- Some details may reflect conventional descriptions of ancient warfare rather than a uniquely precise prediction.
