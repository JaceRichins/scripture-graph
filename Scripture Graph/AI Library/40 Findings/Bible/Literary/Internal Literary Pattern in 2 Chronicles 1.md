# Internal Literary Pattern in 2 Chronicles 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clustered references to Moses, the wilderness tabernacle, Bezaleel's altar, David's transfer of the ark, and congregational worship are strong internal literary evidence that the chapter deliberately legitimates Solomon's beginning through continuity with Israel's sacred past.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the historical precision of every cultic detail, the date of composition, or the narrator's access to eyewitness records.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The details may primarily preserve inherited source material rather than form a newly designed theme.
- The narrator may be supplying antiquarian or logistical information without making every detail symbolically significant.
- The presentation may be institutional legitimation rather than neutral historical description.
