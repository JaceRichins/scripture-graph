# Literary And Narrative Parallel in 2 Kings 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of multiplying a widow's household provisions and restoring a woman's dead son closely parallels Elijah's ministry in 1 Kings 17. This supports the literary interpretation that 2 Kings presents Elisha as continuing Elijah's prophetic ministry.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the historicity of either event, the direction of literary dependence, or a broader claim about biblical authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both accounts may employ conventional miracle-story motifs without requiring a detailed authorial correspondence.
- The parallels may reflect editorial shaping of inherited traditions rather than the original circumstances of the reported events.
- Similar acts alone do not determine whether one written account directly depends upon the other.
