# Composite Scriptural Citation in Romans 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 9:33 combines the stumbling-stone language of Isaiah 8:14 with the believing-in-the-foundation-stone language of Isaiah 28:16. The verbal and thematic correspondence strongly supports identifying it as a composite scriptural citation.

**Does not establish:** The combination does not establish who first joined the passages, which textual edition was used, or every intended implication of the stone imagery.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may have received the combined wording from an earlier Jewish or Christian interpretive tradition rather than composing the combination himself.
