# Repetition And Thematic Coherence in Malachi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated accusation of dealing treacherously links betrayal among brothers, Judah's religious offense, and betrayal of wives. Together with the repeated covenant vocabulary, this supplies strong internal evidence that the priestly, communal, and marital accusations are presented under a shared logic of covenant unfaithfulness.

**Does not establish:** The coherence does not establish single authorship, the chapter's precise compositional history, or the prevalence of the condemned behavior.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler could have joined originally separate accusations by adding or preserving shared covenant language.
- The repeated vocabulary may indicate thematic editing rather than composition of the entire chapter as one oracle.
