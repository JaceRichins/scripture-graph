# Internal Narrative Rationale in Exodus 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The practical explanation for gradual displacement shows that the chapter's conquest promise attends to anticipated land-management consequences. As literary evidence, it does not verify that the predicted displacement occurred historically in the manner described.

**Does not establish:** This internal rationale does not establish the date, scale, mechanism, or historical fulfillment of the conquest.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The rationale may be a literary explanation for an already remembered or experienced gradual settlement process.
- The language may be theological or idealized rather than a chronological account.
- Different biblical passages may preserve differing portrayals of Israel's settlement.
