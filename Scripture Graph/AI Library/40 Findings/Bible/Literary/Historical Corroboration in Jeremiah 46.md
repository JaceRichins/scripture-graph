# Historical Corroboration in Jeremiah 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The historical framework in Jeremiah 46:2 accords with the Babylonian Chronicle's report that Nebuchadnezzar defeated the Egyptian army at Carchemish in 605 BCE. This is strong external corroboration for the chapter's Carchemish setting, while leaving the oracle's date of composition and predictive status unresolved.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not establish that the oracle was written before the battle, prove supernatural prediction, or determine the date of the chapter's final editorial form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The historical notice could have been composed or finalized after the battle.
- An author or editor could have relied on widely remembered political events rather than a contemporary record.
- Agreement about the event does not establish when the poetic oracle in verses 3-12 reached its present form.

**Scripture:** [[Jeremiah 46#^jer-46-2|Jeremiah 46:2]]
