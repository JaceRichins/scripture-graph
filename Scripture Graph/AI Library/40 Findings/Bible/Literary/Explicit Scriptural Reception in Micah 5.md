# Explicit Scriptural Reception in Micah 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 2 provides strong evidence that Micah 5:2 was received within early Christianity as a prophecy concerning the Messiah's association with Bethlehem.

**Does not establish:** This reception history does not by itself prove that Micah's first audience understood the passage exclusively as a prediction of Jesus or that every detail has only one fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew records an early Christian application of Micah without necessarily exhausting the prophecy's earlier historical horizon.
- Matthew's wording adapts the Micah passage and may combine it with broader Davidic shepherd traditions.
