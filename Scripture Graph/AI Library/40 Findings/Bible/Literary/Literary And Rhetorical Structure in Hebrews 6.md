# Literary And Rhetorical Structure in Hebrews 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's movement from warning, to agricultural illustration, to explicit confidence in the readers, and finally to God's oath and anchored hope is strong textual evidence that judgment and assurance are coordinated parts of one exhortation rather than unrelated teachings.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not resolve every theological question about apostasy, predestination, assurance, or whether a genuinely saved person can finally fall away.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The transitions may reflect conventional exhortational material assembled without a tightly planned literary design.
- Interpreters disagree over whether the warning describes an actual possibility for the readers, a hypothetical case, or a distinct group.
