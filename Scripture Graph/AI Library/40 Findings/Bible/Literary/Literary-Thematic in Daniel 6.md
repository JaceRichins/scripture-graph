# Literary-Thematic in Daniel 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated assertion that the king's law cannot be changed, followed by the proclamation that God's kingdom cannot be destroyed, strongly supports an intended contrast between rigid but limited imperial authority and God's enduring dominion.

**Does not establish:** The contrast does not establish the historical scope of Persian royal law or imply that every human law is opposed to God.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The legal repetition may primarily supply plot mechanics rather than establish a deliberate theological contrast.
- The language about God's kingdom may be a conventional royal praise formula appended after the rescue.
