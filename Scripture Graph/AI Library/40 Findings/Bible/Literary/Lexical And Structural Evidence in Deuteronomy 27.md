# Lexical And Structural Evidence in Deuteronomy 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of "all the words of this law" in commands to write and in the final demand to do provides strong internal evidence that the chapter presents law as both a recorded standard and an enacted obligation.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence does not identify the precise contents or extent of "this law," nor does it prove that the inscription contained the whole Pentateuch.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated wording may reflect conventional legal phrasing without a deliberately designed literary linkage.
- The passages may use the same formula while referring to differently bounded bodies of law.
