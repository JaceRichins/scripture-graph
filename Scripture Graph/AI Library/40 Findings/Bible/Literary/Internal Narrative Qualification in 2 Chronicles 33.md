# Internal Narrative Qualification in 2 Chronicles 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The survival of high-place sacrifice after Manasseh's reforms is internal evidence that the narrative does not depict his reversal as instantly eliminating every entrenched practice among the people.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Manasseh's repentance was insincere or that none of his reforms had lasting effect.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Verse 17 may distinguish unauthorized locations from outright idolatry and thus may not portray the people as rejecting the Lord.
- The notice may function primarily as an explanation for practices encountered later rather than as an assessment of Manasseh's sincerity.
