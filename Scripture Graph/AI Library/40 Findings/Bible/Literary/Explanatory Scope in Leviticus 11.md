# Explanatory Scope in Leviticus 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's rules cannot be reduced from the text alone to a simple ban on hazardous meat: they also regulate temporary status after touch, contamination of containers, flowing or abundant water, dry and wetted seed, and holiness before God.

**Does not establish:** This evidence does not establish that the laws had no health benefits or that one symbolic theory explains every prohibited animal.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some distinctions may have practical sanitation, food-preservation, or contamination-related value.
- Ritual, symbolic, social, and practical functions need not be mutually exclusive.
