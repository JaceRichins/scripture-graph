# Internal Lexical And Thematic Parallel in Amos 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The internal parallel between seeking the LORD and seeking good strongly supports reading ethical reform as an integral part of the chapter's call to seek God. Verse 15 makes the reform concrete through hating evil, loving good, and establishing judgment in the gate.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that ethical conduct is the whole of devotion to God or that salvation is earned through moral works.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two appeals may be complementary rather than strict definitions of one another.
- Seeking the LORD may also include dimensions of worship and covenant allegiance that this ethical formulation does not exhaust.
