# Literary And Thematic Correspondence in 2 Samuel 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The correspondence between the promise that the Lord would be with David wherever he went, cut off his enemies, and make him a great name and the chapter's repeated preservation formula, victories, and statement that David gained a name is strong evidence that the narrator presents these campaigns as realizing the preceding oracle.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not independently establish divine causation, exact chronology, or the historical details of every campaign.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler or editor may have placed or worded the campaign summary to echo the oracle retrospectively.
- Some expressions may be conventional royal or victory language rather than an independently intended fulfillment formula.
- The literary correspondence does not require that every campaign occurred after the oracle in the order now presented.
