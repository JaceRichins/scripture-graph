# Reception History And Prophetic Reuse in Zechariah 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts associates the promised return of Jesus with his departure from the Mount of Olives, and Doctrine and Covenants 45 explicitly places the Lord upon that mountain as it divides. These texts form a strong Christian and Latter-day Saint reception-history link to Zechariah 14.

**Does not establish:** The reception history does not by itself determine whether every geographic feature in Zechariah 14 should be interpreted literally.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Acts does not explicitly quote Zechariah 14:4 or state that the return must occur at the identical point of departure.
- Doctrine and Covenants 45 supplies a later revelatory interpretation within the Latter-day Saint canon rather than neutral evidence for the original historical meaning of Zechariah.
