# Literary Structure in Micah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's observable progression—from corruption and visitation, through confession and waiting, to deliverance and covenant mercy—strongly supports reading judgment and hope as deliberately integrated theological themes.

**Does not establish:** The thematic progression does not establish single-stage composition, an exact historical setting, or the identity of every speaker.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The progression may partly result from the arrangement of originally distinct speeches or liturgical units.
- Different readers may divide the chapter at different verses without denying its broad movement from lament to hope.
