# Explicit Doctrinal Statement in John 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 5:28-29 is strong textual evidence that this discourse teaches a future resurrection followed by differentiated outcomes associated with what people have done.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not by itself provide a complete chronology of the afterlife or define a detailed system of post-resurrection glories.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Interpreters differ over how deeds relate to faith, grace, and judgment.
- The two outcomes may summarize judgment without specifying all possible degrees or conditions of final destiny.
- The meaning and translation of the term rendered damnation can be discussed more narrowly as adverse judgment.

**Scripture:** [[John 5#^john-5-28|John 5:28]], [[John 5#^john-5-29|John 5:29]]
