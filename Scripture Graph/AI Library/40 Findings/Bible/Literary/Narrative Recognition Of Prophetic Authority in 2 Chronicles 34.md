# Narrative Recognition Of Prophetic Authority in 2 Chronicles 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The royal delegation consults Huldah the prophetess, and her answer is delivered as the word of the LORD and returned to the king. Within this narrative, a woman functions as an authoritative prophetic interpreter of the discovered law and its consequences.

**Does not establish:** This episode alone does not establish institutional parity across every religious office or reveal how common female prophetic leadership was.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage does not explain why this delegation consulted Huldah rather than another prophet.
- Recognition of Huldah's authority in this episode does not by itself define the full institutional role of female prophets in ancient Israel.
