# Internal Historical Specificity in Ezekiel 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit naming of Egypt, Pharaoh, and the king of Babylon is strong internal evidence that the oracle addresses a concrete geopolitical judgment rather than only an abstract or exclusively end-time conflict.

**Does not establish:** The named participants do not by themselves establish the oracle's exact absolute date, the date of composition, or a one-to-one historical fulfillment for every poetic image.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Later readers may legitimately apply the imagery typologically to other proud powers.
- Some details may be poetic conventions rather than predictions of discrete historical events.
