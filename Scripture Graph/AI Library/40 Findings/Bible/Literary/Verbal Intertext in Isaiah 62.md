# Verbal Intertext in Isaiah 62

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 62:11 and Isaiah 40:10 contain the same distinctive declaration that his reward is with him and his work is before him. This is strong evidence of a deliberate literary relationship or shared Isaianic formulation.

**Does not establish:** The verbal correspondence does not establish the date, direction of dependence, historical fulfillment, or single authorship of every section of Isaiah.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may draw independently on a conventional prophetic formula.
- An editor or later transmitter could have coordinated language already present in separate compositions.
- The shared wording does not by itself determine which passage is earlier.
