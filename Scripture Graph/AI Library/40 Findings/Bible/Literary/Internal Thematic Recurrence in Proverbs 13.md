# Internal Thematic Recurrence in Proverbs 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The distribution of instruction, commandment, law, knowledge, reproof, wise companionship, and chastening supplies strong internal evidence that teachability is a major concern of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence does not establish a precise macrostructure, single compositional occasion, or deliberate placement of every saying.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Instruction is pervasive throughout Proverbs, so recurrence may reflect the genre rather than a specially designed chapter theme.
- The sayings may have been collected independently without forming a deliberate continuous argument.
