# Internal Anthropological Evidence in Romans 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The discussion of Gentile conduct, conscience, and accusing or excusing thoughts supports the conclusion that Paul recognizes meaningful moral accountability apart from possessing the written Jewish law.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that conscience is infallible, that all people possess equal moral knowledge, or that Gentiles fulfill the entire law.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Gentiles may be ordinary morally responsive Gentiles whose consciences sometimes correspond to the law.
- Some interpreters identify them more specifically as Gentile Christians transformed under the new covenant.
- The passage may describe limited moral knowledge rather than a comprehensive natural-law system.
