# Scriptural Literary Parallel in Micah 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Micah's threats that the people will sow without reaping, process olives without using the oil, and prepare wine without drinking it closely parallel the frustrated agricultural labor described among the covenant curses in Deuteronomy.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish direct literary dependence, the direction of dependence, or the date of either textual formulation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently use conventional ancient descriptions of agricultural disaster.
- Micah may draw on a broadly shared covenant tradition without directly quoting the present form of Deuteronomy.
