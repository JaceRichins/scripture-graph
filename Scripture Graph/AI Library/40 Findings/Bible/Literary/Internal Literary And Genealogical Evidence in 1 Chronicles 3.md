# Internal Literary And Genealogical Evidence in 1 Chronicles 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The preservation of several generations after Jeconiah is strong internal evidence that the genealogy's interest continues beyond the sequence of named royal successors. It supports an interpretation of continuing concern for Davidic descent, but it does not by itself demonstrate the historical accuracy of every relationship or a promise of restored kingship.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historicity of every individual, an unbroken biological pedigree, the date of composition, or a prediction that Davidic monarchy would be restored.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later names may have been preserved primarily for administrative or communal identity rather than for an expectation of renewed monarchy.
- Some relationships may use legal, dynastic, or conventional genealogical categories rather than immediate biological paternity.
- The genealogy may contain editorial shaping or gaps while still expressing continuity.
