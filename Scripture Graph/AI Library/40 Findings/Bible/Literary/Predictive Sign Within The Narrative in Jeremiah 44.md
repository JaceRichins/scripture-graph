# Predictive Sign Within The Narrative in Jeremiah 44

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The text presents the downfall of Pharaoh-hophra as a sign that the LORD's threatened judgment against the Judeans in Egypt will stand, explicitly comparing Hophra's fate with Zedekiah's delivery to Nebuchadrezzar.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish when the prediction was composed, whether its present wording predates Hophra's downfall, or whether supernatural foreknowledge best explains it.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prediction could have been composed or edited after Hophra's downfall.
- Hophra's political vulnerability may have made his downfall foreseeable without supernatural knowledge.
- A general prediction of defeat may later have been associated with a particular political outcome.
