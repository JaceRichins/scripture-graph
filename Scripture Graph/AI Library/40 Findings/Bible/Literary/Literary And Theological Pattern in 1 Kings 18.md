# Literary And Theological Pattern in 1 Kings 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained contrasts between Baal's unanswered invocation and the LORD's consuming fire, between the people's silence and confession, and between famine and rain are strong textual evidence that the narrator designed the chapter as a polemic for exclusive allegiance to the LORD.

**Does not establish:** This literary pattern does not independently establish the historical occurrence of the contest or prove the chapter's supernatural claims to readers who do not already accept its testimony.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence could reflect later literary shaping of received traditions.
- A strongly polemical narrative design does not by itself show that every reported detail occurred historically.
