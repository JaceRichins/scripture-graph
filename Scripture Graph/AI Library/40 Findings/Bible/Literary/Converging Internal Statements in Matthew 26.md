# Converging Internal Statements in Matthew 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus rejects armed resistance, claims that angelic aid is available, and grounds his surrender in the fulfillment of scripture. Taken together, these statements strongly support the narrative interpretation that his arrest is accepted voluntarily rather than caused by an inability to resist.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not independently verify the historical availability of angels or identify every prophetic text Matthew believed was fulfilled.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statements may primarily express Matthew's post-Easter theological interpretation of the arrest.
- Appeal to angelic aid is a narrative assertion and is not independently testable from this passage.

**Scripture:** [[Matthew 26#^matt-26-51|Matthew 26:51-56]]
