# Literary Parallel in Obadiah 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Obadiah's language concerning a report among the nations, Edom's small and despised status, its lofty habitation, grape gatherers, hidden treasures, and Edomite wisdom has close literary parallels in Jeremiah's oracle against Edom. This strongly supports a literary or traditional relationship between the passages, although it does not by itself determine dependence, direction of borrowing, or date.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish which text is earlier, who borrowed from whom, the date of either passage, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Obadiah may have drawn on Jeremiah.
- Jeremiah may have drawn on Obadiah or an earlier form of its oracle.
- Both passages may preserve a shared prophetic source or conventional oracle against Edom.
