# Internal Literary Pattern in Exodus 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repetition and balanced contrasts—including the repeated "right hand," the enemy's boast followed by God's wind, and the two uses of the stone image—provide strong internal evidence that the song is carefully shaped poetry.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish a composition date, identify an author, prove that every correspondence was intentional, or independently establish the historicity of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetitions may arise from conventional oral-poetic formulas rather than a single author's detailed design.
- The English translation may heighten or obscure correspondences present in the underlying Hebrew.
- Careful poetic shaping is compatible with composition, transmission, or editing at more than one historical stage.
