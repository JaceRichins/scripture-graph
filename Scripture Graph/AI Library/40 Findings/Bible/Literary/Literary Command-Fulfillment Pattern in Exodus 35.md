# Literary Command-Fulfillment Pattern in Exodus 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The item-by-item correspondence between the requested materials and the reported donations is strong evidence that the chapter presents Israel's response as a deliberate fulfillment of the command. It is also evidence of careful literary organization, though not by itself evidence for a particular date or author.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish Mosaic authorship, an early date, historical precision for every listed contribution, or any broader claim about the origin of Exodus.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may primarily serve administrative completeness rather than a developed literary purpose.
- An editor could have aligned the two inventories during composition or transmission.
