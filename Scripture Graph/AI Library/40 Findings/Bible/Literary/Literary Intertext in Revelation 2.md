# Literary Intertext in Revelation 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Revelation 2:23 closely echoes Jeremiah 17:10 in combining the search of heart and reins with recompense according to conduct. The verbal and conceptual overlap strongly supports deliberate scriptural reuse or deeply scriptural diction.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself determine the author's method of composition, the date of Revelation, or every theological implication of applying divine-search language to Christ.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording could arise from a wider biblical idiom rather than a direct quotation of Jeremiah.
- A shared traditional formula could underlie both passages.
