# Internal Literary Correspondence in Jeremiah 44

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 7 and Jeremiah 44 both depict household participation in making offerings to the queen of heaven. The recurrence strongly supports thematic and literary continuity between the two passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish single authorship, the exact chronology of composition, or the historical accuracy of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later editor could have reinforced an inherited theme across the passages.
- Both passages could independently draw on a recurring Judean religious practice.
- Formulaic language could account for some of the verbal and thematic similarity.
