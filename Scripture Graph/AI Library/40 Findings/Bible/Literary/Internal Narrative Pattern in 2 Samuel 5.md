# Internal Narrative Pattern in 2 Samuel 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated inquiry-command-action pattern is strong internal literary evidence that the chapter portrays David's victories as dependent upon receiving and following situation-specific divine direction.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the historicity of every detail, supernatural causation, or a universal promise that obedience always produces military success.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may reflect deliberate theological or literary shaping rather than a complete event-by-event record.
- The account may omit ordinary intelligence, logistics, and tactical considerations that also contributed to victory.
- Readers may disagree about whether the reported divine speech describes historical revelation or the narrator's theological interpretation of events.
