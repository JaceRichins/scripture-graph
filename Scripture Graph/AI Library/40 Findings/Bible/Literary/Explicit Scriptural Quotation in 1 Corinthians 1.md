# Explicit Scriptural Quotation in 1 Corinthians 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Paul's explicit introductory formula "For it is written" and the wording about destroying the wisdom of the wise provide strong textual evidence that 1 Corinthians 1:19 invokes Isaiah 29:14.

**Does not establish:** The quotation alone does not establish that Isaiah 29 predicted the Corinthian controversy as its sole or primary historical fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be quoting a Greek textual form rather than the precise wording represented by the English Old Testament translation.
- The quotation's application in this argument does not require every detail of Isaiah 29 to refer exclusively to the Corinthian situation.

**Scripture:** [[1 Corinthians 1#^1cor-1-19|1 Corinthians 1:19]], [[Isaiah 29#^isa-29-14|Isaiah 29:14]]
