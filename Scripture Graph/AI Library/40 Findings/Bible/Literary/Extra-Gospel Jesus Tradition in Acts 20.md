# Extra-Gospel Jesus Tradition in Acts 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts preserves the saying attributed to Jesus, "It is more blessed to give than to receive," although that saying is not otherwise recorded in the four canonical Gospels. This is meaningful evidence that sayings of Jesus circulated beyond those incorporated into the Gospel narratives.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the saying's exact original-language wording, its transmission route, or that every later saying attributed to Jesus is authentic.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The saying may be a concise paraphrase of a broader theme in Jesus' teaching.
- Acts may depend on a written source that no longer survives rather than solely on oral transmission.
- The author may have formulated traditional teaching into this memorable wording.

**Scripture:** [[Acts 20#^acts-20-35|Acts 20:35]]
