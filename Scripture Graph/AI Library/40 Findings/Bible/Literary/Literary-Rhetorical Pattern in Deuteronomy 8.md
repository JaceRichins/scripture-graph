# Literary-Rhetorical Pattern in Deuteronomy 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of remembering, forgetting, humility, pride, hunger, fullness, wilderness, and land forms a coherent rhetorical network supporting the reading that the chapter treats both deprivation and abundance as tests of covenant loyalty.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish single authorship, Mosaic authorship, a composition date, or the historicity of each narrated event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some coherence may arise from conventional Deuteronomic vocabulary rather than a specially designed pattern unique to this chapter.
- Editorial shaping could account for the present literary unity without requiring that the whole discourse originated in one oral delivery.
- Readers may disagree about the exact boundaries and arrangement of the units.
