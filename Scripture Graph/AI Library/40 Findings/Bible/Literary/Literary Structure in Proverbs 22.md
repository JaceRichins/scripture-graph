# Literary Structure in Proverbs 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The change at verse 17 from largely self-contained, third-person observations to sustained second-person instruction is strong internal evidence for a literary boundary between verses 1–16 and 17–29.

**Does not establish:** The shift does not by itself establish the date, author, editorial history, or original extent of the collection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The transition may reflect ordinary variation within a collected anthology rather than a single editor's deliberately marked division.
- The exact boundaries of the larger instructional collection require evidence from the following chapters, not this chapter alone.
