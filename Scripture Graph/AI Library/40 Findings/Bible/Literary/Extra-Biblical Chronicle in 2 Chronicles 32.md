# Extra-Biblical Chronicle in 2 Chronicles 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Sennacherib's assassination by one or more of his own sons (referenced in the Babylonian Chronicle and in later Assyrian inscriptions of his successor Esarhaddon) is a well-attested historical event, though it occurred in 681 BC, roughly two decades after the 701 BC campaign against Judah — supporting the fact of the murder in verse 21 but not any implied proximity in time to the retreat from Jerusalem.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that the murder was a direct, immediate consequence of the Jerusalem campaign; the ~20-year gap is a real chronological fact the text does not flag.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The identity of the assassin(s) and precise motive vary between the biblical text (2 Kings 19:37 names two sons) and Babylonian/Assyrian sources, which are not fully consistent with each other.
