# Internal Historical-Geographic Evidence in Ezekiel 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The geographic and commodity catalogue is strong evidence that the chapter presents Tyrus as the center of a far-reaching commercial network. It is only limited evidence for reconstructing the precise routes, dates, or volume of historical trade represented by every item.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish that every named partner exchanged every listed commodity with Tyre at the same date or in the implied quantities.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The list may be stylized or arranged for rhetorical comprehensiveness rather than functioning as a commercial ledger.
- Some place names and commodity terms are textually or linguistically uncertain.
- The catalogue may combine knowledge from more than one period rather than depict a single commercial moment.
