# Specific Predictive Sign Within The Narrative in Jeremiah 44

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The text attaches the broader warning to a concrete political sign: Pharaoh-hophra will be delivered to enemies as Zedekiah was delivered to Nebuchadrezzar. This makes the narrative's prophetic claim more specific and potentially testable than a wholly indefinite prediction.

**Does not establish:** The specificity of the sign does not by itself establish when the passage was composed, that the prediction preceded the event, or that its source was divine.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prediction may have been recorded or shaped after Hophra's fate was known.
- The wording could preserve an earlier prediction while later editors supplied its present framing.
- A correct political prediction can result from informed judgment without supernatural revelation.
