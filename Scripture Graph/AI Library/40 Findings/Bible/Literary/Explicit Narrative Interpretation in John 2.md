# Explicit Narrative Interpretation in John 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 2:21-22 is strong internal evidence that the Gospel's narrator intends the temple saying to be read as a prediction of Jesus's death and resurrection, understood retrospectively by the disciples.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the resurrection as a historical event, prove the exact pre-Easter wording of the saying, or show that the original hearers understood it.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The explanation may represent the evangelist's post-resurrection theological interpretation of an earlier saying.
- The disciples' recollection may have been shaped by later resurrection belief rather than preserving their exact earlier understanding.

**Scripture:** [[John 2#^john-2-19|John 2:19]], [[John 2#^john-2-21|John 2:21]], [[John 2#^john-2-22|John 2:22]]
