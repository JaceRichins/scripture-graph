# Explicit Scriptural Quotation And Verbal Parallel in James 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
James 4:6 introduces the saying about God opposing the proud and giving grace to the humble as scriptural authority. It corresponds to Proverbs 3:34 and is repeated in nearly the same form in 1 Peter 5:5, providing strong evidence for a shared early Christian use of this wisdom saying.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish which New Testament writing came first or that either author copied directly from the other.

**Alternative explanations:**
- James and 1 Peter may independently draw on Proverbs or on a widely circulated proverbial form.
- One New Testament author may know the other's formulation, but the verbal agreement alone does not determine dependence.
