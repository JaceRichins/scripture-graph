# Internal Literary And Theological Evidence in 2 Kings 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close sequence from Hezekiah's temple prayer to the statement that his prayer was heard and then to Jerusalem's reported deliverance is strong evidence for the narrator's theological presentation of the event as an answer to prayer.

**Does not establish:** The narrative structure does not by itself independently prove the historical scale, precise mechanism, or supernatural causation of the Assyrian deaths.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may reflect theological shaping of remembered history rather than a recoverable causal demonstration.
- A literary account can attribute deliverance to prayer without independently establishing the physical mechanism of the Assyrian losses.
