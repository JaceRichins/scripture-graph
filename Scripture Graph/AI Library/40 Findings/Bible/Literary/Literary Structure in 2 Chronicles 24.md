# Literary Structure in 2 Chronicles 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated time marker connecting right conduct and continual worship with "the days of Jehoiada," followed immediately by Jehoiada's death and Joash's changed allegiance, is strong literary evidence that Jehoiada's death functions as the chapter's central hinge.

**Does not establish:** This pattern does not establish Joash's private motives or prove that he possessed no genuine faith while Jehoiada lived.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The arrangement may partly follow the chronology of events rather than a deliberately constructed literary pattern.
- Joash's later apostasy does not prove that all of his earlier devotion was insincere.
