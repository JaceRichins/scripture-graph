# Terminological And Syntactic in Titus 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The movement from appointing plural “elders” in verse 5 to explaining what “a bishop” must be in verse 7 is substantial internal evidence that the terms overlap functionally in this argument.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every use of elder and bishop elsewhere denotes an identical office, nor does it settle later questions of church polity or priesthood organization.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A bishop could be treated as a particular kind of elder rather than as an exactly interchangeable office.
- The terminology may reflect a local or early arrangement that need not govern every later ecclesiastical structure.
