# Scriptural Intertext in Philippians 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The distinctive knee-and-tongue pairing is strong textual evidence that Philippians 2:10–11 invokes Isaiah 45:23. Applying this language to Jesus is significant evidence for an elevated identification of Jesus with divine sovereignty within the passage.

**Does not establish:** The allusion alone does not settle every later metaphysical question about the relationship between Jesus and the Father, nor does it identify who first formulated verses 6–11.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul could be drawing on a broader early Jewish or Christian tradition already shaped by Isaiah rather than directly composing from Isaiah 45:23.
- The passage can distinguish Jesus from God the Father even while assigning Jesus language of universal divine sovereignty.
