# Internal Literary Pattern in Exodus 40

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The general compliance statement and seven recurring command-fulfillment formulas are strong internal evidence that Moses's obedience is a major literary emphasis of Exodus 40.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish symbolic intent for the number seven, a particular author or date, or the historicity of the narrated construction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be a conventional completion-report formula rather than a specially constructed theological pattern.
- An editor or compiler could have imposed the recurring formula on inherited material.
- The number seven may be incidental even though the broader emphasis on compliance is clear.
