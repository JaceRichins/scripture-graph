# Scriptural Quotation Typology in John 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The disciples' recollection in verse 17, 'The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up,' is a direct quotation of Psalm 69:9, applying a psalm of the suffering righteous one to Jesus's temple action; the same psalm is echoed elsewhere in the Passion narratives (e.g., Psalm 69:21 and the vinegar detail), suggesting early Christian reading of Psalm 69 as prefiguring Jesus.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish that Jesus or the disciples read all of Psalm 69 messianically at the time of the event, only that the Gospel writer applies this verse to the scene.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The quotation may function narrowly, applying only the 'zeal for the house' idea rather than signaling a full messianic reading of Psalm 69 as a whole.
