# Literary Structure in 1 Chronicles 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The exact recapitulation of the Eleazar-to-Ahimaaz sequence provides strong evidence that Aaron's branch is structurally emphasized within the chapter, although the reason for that emphasis remains interpretive.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a symbolic design, the completeness of the genealogy, or the precise compositional history of the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may result from combining overlapping genealogical and administrative source lists.
- The second occurrence may simply identify the family whose duties have just been described.
