# Narrative And Literary Evidence in Mark 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The fulfilled predictions concerning the prepared room, the disciples' scattering, and Peter's denials provide strong evidence that Mark deliberately characterizes Jesus as possessing foreknowledge and that prediction-and-fulfillment is a major device organizing the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The literary pattern alone does not establish the historical timing, exact wording, or transmission history of each prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The evangelist may have shaped inherited traditions so that predictions and outcomes correspond.
- Some predictions may have been formulated or transmitted with knowledge of the later events.
