# Literary And Thematic Pattern in Deuteronomy 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated removal formula links the unknown-homicide procedure with the rebellious-son law: one removes the guilt of innocent blood and the other removes evil. Together with the warning against defiling the land, this supports reading the chapter as concerned with communal accountability and the integrity of Israel's covenant land.

**Does not establish:** The repetition does not establish single authorship, precise compositional history, or the modern moral acceptability of every penalty and institution regulated here.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated formula may be conventional legal language rather than evidence of a carefully designed chapter-wide literary structure.
- The laws may have been grouped by a compiler because of broad thematic affinity without originally forming a single composition.
