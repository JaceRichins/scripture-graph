# Internal Literary Evidence in Exodus 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The livestock episode's sequence—conditional warning, specified time, fulfillment on the next day, and Pharaoh's investigation—provides strong internal evidence of deliberate narrative construction and emphasizes that the event occurs as announced.

**Does not establish:** This literary pattern does not establish the event's historicity, the narrative's date, or single authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The pattern may reflect conventional narrative shaping rather than an eyewitness sequence.
- An editor or later tradent could have arranged inherited material into a prediction-and-fulfillment form.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 9#^ex-9-2|Exodus 9:2-7]]
