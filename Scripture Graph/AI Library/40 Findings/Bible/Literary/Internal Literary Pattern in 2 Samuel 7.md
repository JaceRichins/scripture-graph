# Internal Literary Pattern in 2 Samuel 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated shifts in the meaning of "house" provide moderately strong internal evidence that the chapter is deliberately shaped around a reversal: David intends to build God a house, but God first promises to build David a house.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish the chapter's date, single authorship, historical accuracy, or supernatural origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated vocabulary may partly reflect the subject matter rather than an elaborate intentional wordplay.
- The pattern could have arisen through oral or editorial shaping rather than from a single original composition.
