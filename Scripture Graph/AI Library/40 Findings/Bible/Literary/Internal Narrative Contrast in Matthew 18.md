# Internal Narrative Contrast in Matthew 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between ten thousand talents and one hundred pence, reinforced by the matching pleas and opposite responses, is strong internal evidence that the parable is designed to expose the forgiven servant's gross inconsistency. It strongly supports the lesson that received mercy creates an obligation to extend mercy.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a precise monetary-to-spiritual conversion, a general abolition of civil debts, or that divine forgiveness is earned by human forgiveness.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The exaggerated contrast may serve primarily as memorable storytelling rather than invite a precise theological ratio.
- The debt imagery may also invite socioeconomic or judicial readings alongside its explicit application to interpersonal forgiveness.
