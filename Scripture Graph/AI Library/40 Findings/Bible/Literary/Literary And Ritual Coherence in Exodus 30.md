# Literary And Ritual Coherence in Exodus 30

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of spatial placement, priestly washing, exclusive substances, authorized personnel, daily service, and annual atonement strongly supports reading the chapter as a system of regulated approach to sacred presence rather than as an miscellaneous inventory of ritual objects.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the date, human authorship, compositional history, or uniquely Christian meaning of the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence may result from later collection or editorial arrangement of originally separate ritual rules.
- Some repetitions may be practical legal drafting rather than a deliberate literary theology.
