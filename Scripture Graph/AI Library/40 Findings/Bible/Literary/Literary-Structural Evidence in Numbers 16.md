# Literary-Structural Evidence in Numbers 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The paired incense scenes provide strong internal evidence of purposeful literary and theological contrast: the same ritual medium marks both fatal unauthorized approach and life-preserving authorized mediation.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish the account's date, authorship, historicity, or the direct applicability of its institutional boundaries to later religious organizations.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallel may reflect thematic editorial arrangement rather than a single author's original design.
- The contrast may arise naturally because incense is the disputed priestly act at the center of both episodes.
- Readers may disagree about how much theological meaning the parallel is intended to carry.
