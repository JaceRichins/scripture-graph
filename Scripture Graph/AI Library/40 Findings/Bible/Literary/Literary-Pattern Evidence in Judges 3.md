# Literary-Pattern Evidence in Judges 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated ordering of apostasy, oppression, appeal, deliverance, and rest is strong internal evidence that the narrator presents Israel's political fortunes through a theological framework of disobedience and deliverance.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish that every event in the period followed an identical sequence, that the accounts are exhaustive, or that the reported durations can be added into an uncontested chronology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may partly reflect recurring historical conditions rather than deliberate literary shaping alone.
- Formulaic language could have been applied during compilation to diverse earlier traditions.
