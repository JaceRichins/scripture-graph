# Repeated Literary Motif in Mark 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated commands and descriptions involving hearing tie the chapter's teaching together. The pattern strongly supports reading responsible receptivity as a central literary theme.

**Does not establish:** The repetition does not by itself establish the chapter's compositional history, date, or the precise relationship among its component traditions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition follows naturally from the subject of teaching and may not reflect an elaborate literary design.
- Individual sayings may have circulated independently before being arranged in the present sequence.
