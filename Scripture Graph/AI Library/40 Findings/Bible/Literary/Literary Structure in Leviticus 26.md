# Literary Structure in Leviticus 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated conditional statements about continued refusal to hearken, together with repeated declarations of punishment 'seven times' more, are strong internal evidence that the judgment section is arranged as an escalating sequence.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the chapter's date, single authorship, or the historical occurrence of each threatened judgment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions may partly reflect the combination or expansion of earlier sanction traditions rather than composition as a single literary unit.
- The expression 'seven times' may be an idiom of intensified or complete punishment rather than a precise sevenfold calculation.
