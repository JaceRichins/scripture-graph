# Canonical Reception And Explicit Application in Isaiah 61

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Luke reports Jesus reading language from Isaiah 61:1-2 in the Nazareth synagogue and declaring that the scripture was fulfilled in the hearers' presence. This is strong canonical evidence for an early Christian messianic reading of Isaiah's anointed herald, although it does not by itself settle every question about the passage's original historical referent.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove that every statement in Isaiah 61 refers exclusively to Jesus or that the original audience understood the speaker in precisely the same way.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Luke's application may be typological or programmatic rather than an exhaustive identification of Isaiah's original historical speaker.
- A historical reading may first locate the voice in a prophetic herald addressing a restored Zion while still recognizing later messianic application.
