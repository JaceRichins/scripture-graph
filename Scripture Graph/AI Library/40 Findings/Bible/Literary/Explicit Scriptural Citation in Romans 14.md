# Explicit Scriptural Citation in Romans 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 14:11 explicitly introduces a scriptural citation and closely corresponds to Isaiah 45:23 in its declaration that every knee will bow and every tongue will acknowledge God. The explicit formula and distinctive paired images make the citation relationship very strong.

**Does not establish:** The citation does not by itself establish that every detail of Paul's teaching about final judgment derives solely from Isaiah, nor does it settle broader questions of authorship or dating.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The precise wording may reflect a Greek textual form or traditional rendering rather than a direct reproduction of the Hebrew wording.
- Paul adapts the citation to his argument, so correspondence does not require verbatim quotation.

**Scripture:** [[Romans 14#^rom-14-11|Romans 14:11]], [[Isaiah 45#^isa-45-23|Isaiah 45:23]]
