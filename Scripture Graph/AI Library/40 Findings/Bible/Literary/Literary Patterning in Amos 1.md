# Literary Patterning in Amos 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurring messenger formula, numerical accusation, named offense, and fire-and-palaces refrain provide strong evidence that the five oracles are deliberately arranged as a literary sequence in the received text.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish a single author, one compositional stage, a precise date for every oracle, or the historical fulfillment of each prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A conventional prophetic form could account for much of the repetition.
- An editor or compiler could have arranged originally separate oracles into the present sequence.
