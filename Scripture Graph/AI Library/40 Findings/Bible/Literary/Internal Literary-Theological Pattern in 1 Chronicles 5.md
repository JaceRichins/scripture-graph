# Internal Literary-Theological Pattern in 1 Chronicles 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between divine help when the tribes trust God and Assyrian deportation after they abandon God is strong internal evidence that the narrator interprets their changing fortunes through a theological pattern of fidelity and apostasy.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently verify divine causation, establish that every military outcome follows this pattern, or make the campaign a universal moral model.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The notices may employ conventional theological formulas inherited from Israelite historiography.
- The theological framing may reflect a compiler's interpretation rather than the understanding of every participant.
- The theological explanation can coexist with ordinary military, economic, and imperial causes.
