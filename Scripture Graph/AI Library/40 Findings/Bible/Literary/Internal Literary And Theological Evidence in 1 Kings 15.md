# Internal Literary And Theological Evidence in 1 Kings 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 29 is strong internal evidence that the writer intentionally interprets Baasha's purge through an earlier oracle attributed to Ahijah. It is evidence for the narrative's fulfillment framework, but the report alone cannot independently establish when the oracle originated or whether it was a supernatural prediction.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the oracle's date, precise transmission history, or supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment notice may have been composed or incorporated after the purge.
- The author may be applying a traditional oracle retrospectively to known events.
- The notice can express a theological interpretation without supplying independent historical corroboration.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 15#^1kgs-15-29|1 Kings 15:29]], [[1 Kings 15#^1kgs-15-30|1 Kings 15:30]]
