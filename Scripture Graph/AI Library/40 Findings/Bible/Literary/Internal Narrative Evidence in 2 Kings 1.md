# Internal Narrative Evidence in 2 Kings 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Ahaziah's death is explicitly presented as fulfillment of the word of the LORD spoken through Elijah. This is strong evidence for the narrator's theology of prophetic reliability, but it is not independent evidence that the reported events occurred historically or that the death had a supernatural cause.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the episode's historicity, the precise sequence of composition, or supernatural causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may have been composed or shaped retrospectively so that the prediction and outcome correspond.
- Ahaziah may have died from the injuries or illness already reported, without requiring a supernatural causal explanation.
