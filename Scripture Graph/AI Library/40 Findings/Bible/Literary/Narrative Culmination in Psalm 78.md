# Narrative Culmination in Psalm 78

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The final movement explicitly contrasts the rejection of Joseph/Ephraim with the choice of Judah, Zion, and David. This provides strong evidence that the historical recital is shaped toward a theological explanation of the Davidic-Zion order.

**Does not establish:** The climax does not establish the psalm's exact date, authorship, political setting, or the historical correctness of every event as narrated.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The ending may primarily celebrate divine election rather than conduct a polemic against northern Israel.
- The Joseph/Ephraim contrast may reflect sanctuary history more directly than later political rivalry.
- The conclusion may legitimate an already established Davidic-Zion tradition without revealing when the psalm was composed.
