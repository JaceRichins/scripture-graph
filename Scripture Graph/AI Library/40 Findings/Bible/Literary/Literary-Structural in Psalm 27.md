# Literary-Structural in Psalm 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The transition from confident declaration to urgent petition strongly supports a reading in which trust coexists with unresolved danger. It provides little evidence by itself for assigning the sections to different authors or occasions.

**Does not establish:** The structural change does not establish separate authorship, editorial combination, or a specific historical crisis.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The shift may reflect a conventional progression within a single lament or prayer.
- The sections could have originated separately and later been joined, although the shift alone cannot demonstrate this.
- The change in tone may be rhetorical, moving worshippers from confession to petition.
