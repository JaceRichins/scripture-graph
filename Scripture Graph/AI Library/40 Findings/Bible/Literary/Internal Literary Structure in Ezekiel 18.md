# Internal Literary Structure in Ezekiel 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated conclusions that each person lives or dies in relation to that person's own conduct provide strong internal literary evidence that individual moral accountability is a deliberate organizing thesis of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that actions lack intergenerational consequences, define precisely what kind of death is intended, or provide a complete account of final salvation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions may primarily serve pastoral exhortation rather than offer a comprehensive theory of judgment.
- The three generations are stylized moral cases, not a description of every mixed or ambiguous life.
