# Doctrinal-Overlay Caution in Luke 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The afterlife imagery in vv.22-26 (Abraham's bosom, conscious torment, a fixed gulf) is drawn from a parable and should not, by itself, be treated as establishing a complete or literal cosmology of the afterlife.

**Does not establish:** That Luke 16 teaches a complete or literal geography of the afterlife; the passage is didactic and parabolic in genre.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The imagery may borrow popular period conceptions of the afterlife for a moral point about repentance in mortal life, rather than assert new revelation about the afterlife's structure.
- Later, more systematic afterlife doctrine may be read back into this parable rather than derived from it.

**Scripture:** [[Luke 16#^luke-16-22|Luke 16:22]], [[Luke 16#^luke-16-23|Luke 16:23]], [[Luke 16#^luke-16-26|Luke 16:26]]
