# Internal Linguistic Evidence in 1 Samuel 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The parenthetical explanation distinguishes an earlier designation, "Seer," from the narrator's current designation, "Prophet." This is strong internal evidence that the verse adopts a retrospective linguistic viewpoint, but it cannot by itself establish when the chapter or the explanatory gloss was composed.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a precise date, author, redactional layer, or comprehensive distinction between the offices of seer and prophet.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The explanation may be a later editorial gloss rather than part of the earliest form of the surrounding narrative.
- The terms may have overlapped in usage, with the verse simplifying a more complex terminological history.
- The retrospective wording alone does not reveal how much time separates the narrator from the events.
