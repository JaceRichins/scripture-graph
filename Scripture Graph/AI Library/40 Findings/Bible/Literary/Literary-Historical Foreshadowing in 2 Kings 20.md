# Literary-Historical Foreshadowing in 2 Kings 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the completed Kings narrative, Isaiah's announcement that royal wealth and descendants will be taken to Babylon functions as explicit foreshadowing of Judah's later Babylonian losses and deportations.

**Does not establish:** The narrative correspondence does not by itself establish supernatural foreknowledge, a precise date of composition, or that every detail was fulfilled in a single event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later compiler could have shaped or positioned the oracle with knowledge of the Babylonian exile.
- The oracle may preserve an earlier warning that was subsequently interpreted in light of later events.
- The literary relationship alone cannot determine when the prediction was first spoken or written.
