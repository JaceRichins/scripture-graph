# Intra-Chapter Verbal Repetition in Proverbs 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The exact repeated half-lines are evidence of literary linkage among sayings in the received collection. They are compatible with deliberate arrangement, reuse of stock proverbial lines, or both.

**Does not establish:** The repetitions do not establish a comprehensive structure, single authorship, or deliberate placement by the original composer.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have combined independent sayings that used familiar formulaic lines.
- The repetition may reflect conventional wisdom diction rather than a larger designed structure.
- The degree of exactness visible in English may partly reflect translation choices.
