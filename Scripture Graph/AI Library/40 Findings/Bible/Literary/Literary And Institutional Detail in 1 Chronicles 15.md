# Literary And Institutional Detail in 1 Chronicles 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive lists of Levitical families, chiefs, singers, instrumentalists, priests, and doorkeepers are strong internal evidence of the chapter's sustained interest in organized sacred service.

**Does not establish:** The presence of detailed names and offices does not by itself establish the roster's historical accuracy, its date, or eyewitness authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The detail may derive from preserved administrative traditions or lists.
- It may reflect the Chronicler's own institutional and theological interests.
- Some names or assignments could have been arranged for literary or legitimating purposes.
