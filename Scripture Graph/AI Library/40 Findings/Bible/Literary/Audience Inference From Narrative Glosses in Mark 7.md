# Audience Inference From Narrative Glosses in Mark 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The accumulation of explanations for Jewish customs and untranslated terms is evidence that Mark addresses at least some readers who could not be assumed to understand them unaided.

**Does not establish:** The glosses do not by themselves establish a specific place of composition, a wholly Gentile audience, or the precise identity of Mark's first readers.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The explanations may serve rhetorical clarity even for readers already somewhat familiar with Jewish customs.
- Some wording may reflect the narrator's general explanatory style rather than a narrowly defined audience.
