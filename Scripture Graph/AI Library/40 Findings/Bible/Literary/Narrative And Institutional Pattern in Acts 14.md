# Narrative And Institutional Pattern in Acts 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated sequence of evangelizing, revisiting converts, strengthening them, appointing elders, and reporting to the sending church strongly supports the interpretation that Acts presents mission as durable community formation rather than preaching alone.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a complete later ecclesiastical hierarchy, the precise ordination ritual, or an exact chronology for each appointment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may have arranged or compressed a more complicated historical process into a coherent missionary pattern.
- The term "elders" alone does not disclose every function or authority associated with the role.
