# Literary Structure in Deuteronomy 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated vocabulary and paired contrasts—love and obedience, seeing and teaching, rain and drought, blessing and curse—together with the commandment frame in verses 1 and 32 provide substantial evidence that the received chapter is rhetorically organized as a coherent exhortation.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not by itself establish Mosaic authorship, an early date, a single stage of composition, or the historical accuracy of every narrated event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some coherence may arise from conventional covenant formulas rather than a specially designed chapter structure.
- An editor could have arranged inherited materials into their present coherent form.
- Repetition may serve oral emphasis without implying an elaborate formal pattern.
