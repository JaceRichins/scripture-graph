# Literary Parallel in Isaiah 38

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 38:1–8 and 21–22 closely correspond in persons, sequence, promises, remedy, and sign to 2 Kings 20:1–11, while Isaiah 38:9–20 contains Hezekiah's writing absent from that portion of 2 Kings. This is strong evidence of a shared narrative tradition, but it does not by itself determine literary priority.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish which account is earlier, whether either preserves the event without editorial shaping, or who composed Hezekiah's writing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah may depend directly or indirectly on Kings.
- Kings may depend directly or indirectly on an Isaianic account.
- Both accounts may draw from a shared written or oral source.
- Some correspondence may result from later editorial harmonization.
