# Internal Biblical Consistency in Nehemiah 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Nehemiah 8:17's claim that Israel had not observed the feast of booths this way since Joshua sits in tension with other biblical references to the feast being kept in the intervening centuries (e.g., Solomon's dedication of the temple during the feast in 1 Kings 8, and the returned exiles keeping 'the feast of tabernacles, as it is written' in Ezra 3:4). This is a genuine point of internal tension worth flagging rather than smoothing over.

**Does not establish:** That the feast of booths (in any form) was literally never observed between Joshua and Nehemiah's day.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Verse 17 may refer specifically to the scale or manner of observance — the whole congregation dwelling in booths together — rather than to whether the feast's sacrifices were offered at all in earlier periods.
- The statement could be rhetorical emphasis common in biblical narrative ('nothing like this since...') rather than a strict historical claim.
