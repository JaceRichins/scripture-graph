# Scriptural Reuse And Conceptual Development in 1 Corinthians 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The use of celestial and terrestrial bodies and of sun, moon, and star glories in both 1 Corinthians 15 and D&C 76 strongly supports a literary and theological connection within Latter-day Saint scripture. In 1 Corinthians itself, however, the imagery directly serves an analogy about resurrected bodies; the chapter does not explicitly label three post-judgment kingdoms.

**Does not establish:** The connection does not establish that Paul was explicitly teaching the complete three-kingdom scheme later articulated in D&C 76.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Readers outside the Latter-day Saint canon can understand Paul's imagery solely as an illustration of diverse resurrected glory.
- Shared wording does not require every detail of D&C 76's taxonomy to have been part of Paul's intended meaning.
