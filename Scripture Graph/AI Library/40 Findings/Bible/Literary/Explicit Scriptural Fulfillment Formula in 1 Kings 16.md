# Explicit Scriptural Fulfillment Formula in 1 Kings 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The link between Joshua's warning and Hiel's rebuilding of Jericho is explicit intertextual evidence that the Kings narrator interprets a much later event through an earlier prophetic word. The abbreviated report does not independently disclose the event's mechanism or the compositional history of either passage.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish when either text was composed, the medical or historical causes of the sons' deaths, or that every detail of the event can be reconstructed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may be theologically interpreting coincidental family deaths during the construction project.
- The report may have been shaped retrospectively to correspond to Joshua 6:26.
- The compressed wording may refer to losses or circumstances not fully narrated in the chapter.

**Scripture:** [[Joshua 6#^josh-6-26|Joshua 6:26]], [[1 Kings 16#^1kgs-16-34|1 Kings 16:34]]
