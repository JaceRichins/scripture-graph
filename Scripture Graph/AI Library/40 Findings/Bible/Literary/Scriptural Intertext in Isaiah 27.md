# Scriptural Intertext in Isaiah 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 27:1 and Psalm 74:13-14 share a cluster of sea, dragon, Leviathan, and divine-defeat imagery. This is strong evidence of a shared biblical image complex, but only moderate evidence for a direct literary relationship between the passages.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish which passage depends on the other, the date of either passage, or a unique historical identity for Leviathan.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently draw on a broader Israelite or ancient Near Eastern stock image.
- The shared vocabulary may establish thematic resemblance without indicating direct dependence.
