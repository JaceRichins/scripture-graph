# Literary Intertext And Prophecy-Fulfillment in 2 Kings 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Beth-el episode is tightly coordinated with 1 Kings 13: Josiah is named in the earlier proclamation against the altar, the burning of human bones is predicted, and the earlier narrative associates the two prophets' burial with the altar and the high places of Samaria. This is strong evidence of deliberate literary linkage within Kings.

**Does not establish:** The linkage alone does not establish supernatural foreknowledge, the independence or date of either passage, or the historical accuracy of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have coordinated the two accounts retrospectively.
- The prediction may preserve an earlier tradition subsequently shaped in transmission.
- A believing reading may understand the coordination as preservation of an authentic predictive prophecy.
- Some details could reflect literary composition rather than independently recoverable events.
