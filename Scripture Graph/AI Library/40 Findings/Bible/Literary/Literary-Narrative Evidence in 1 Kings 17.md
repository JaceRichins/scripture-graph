# Literary-Narrative Evidence in 1 Kings 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the literary presentation of the chapter, the repeated correspondence between announced divine words and narrated outcomes strongly supports the conclusion that the narrator intends readers to recognize Elijah as a true bearer of the Lord's word.

**Does not establish:** The literary pattern does not by itself independently establish the historical occurrence or supernatural causation of the drought and miracles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondences may reflect the theological shaping of inherited traditions rather than a recoverable transcript of events.
- A reader may accept the characterization function of the stories without accepting every event as independently historically verified.
