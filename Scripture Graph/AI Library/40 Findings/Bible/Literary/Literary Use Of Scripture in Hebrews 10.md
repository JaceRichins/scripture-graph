# Literary Use Of Scripture in Hebrews 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of scriptural language in Hebrews 10 supports the observation that its theological argument is constructed through interpretation of Israel's sacred writings: sacrificial obedience, enthronement, the inward covenant, divine judgment, and faithful endurance are all developed through scriptural citations or allusions.

**Does not establish:** This pattern does not establish the identity, ethnicity, location, or precise date of the author, nor does it independently prove the theological conclusions drawn from the cited texts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some verbal correspondences may reflect familiar liturgical or communal language rather than fresh consultation of written scrolls.
- Identifying a citation's textual form does not by itself identify the author's personal manuscript or translation process.
