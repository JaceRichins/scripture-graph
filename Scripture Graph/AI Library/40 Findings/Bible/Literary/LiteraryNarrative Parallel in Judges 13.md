# LiteraryNarrative Parallel in Judges 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Judges 13:19-20 (a kid offered on a rock, the angel of the LORD ascending in the flame from the altar) closely parallels Judges 6:19-21 (Gideon's offering on a rock consumed by fire, after which the angel of the LORD departs), and both accounts are followed immediately by the human witness's terrified realization that they have seen a divine being (Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22-23).

**Does not establish:** This parallel does not establish direct literary dependence of one account on the other, nor common authorship; it shows shared narrative convention.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallel may reflect a shared oral-formulaic theophany 'type-scene' convention used across Judges rather than deliberate cross-referencing between the two specific accounts.
- The similarity could result from common editorial shaping of Judges narratives during compilation rather than intentional literary allusion by an author.
