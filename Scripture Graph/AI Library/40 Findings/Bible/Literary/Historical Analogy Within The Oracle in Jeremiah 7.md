# Historical Analogy Within The Oracle in Jeremiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The appeal to Shiloh supplies an internal precedent against belief in Jerusalem's inviolability: a former place bearing the divine name did not escape judgment, and the present temple is threatened with the same fate.

**Does not establish:** The analogy alone does not establish the precise archaeology, date, or mechanism of Shiloh's downfall.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The analogy's rhetorical force does not depend on reconstructing precisely when or how Shiloh was destroyed.
- The passage may invoke the loss of Shiloh's sanctuary or status rather than one specific destruction event.
