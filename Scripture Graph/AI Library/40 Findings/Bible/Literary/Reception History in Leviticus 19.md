# Reception History in Leviticus 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated New Testament use of Leviticus 19:18 is strong evidence for its importance in early Christian interpretation of neighbor-love and the law.

**Does not establish:** Later quotation does not establish the chapter's authorship, date, or the original scope of the Hebrew term translated “neighbour.”

**Alternative explanations:**
- The New Testament writers emphasize one command from the chapter and do not thereby endorse every possible later interpretation of every law in Leviticus 19.
