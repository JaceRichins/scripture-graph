# Literary Structure in Psalm 107

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurring cry, deliverance, and praise formulas are strong internal evidence that the four distress episodes function as coordinated literary units rather than as an unstructured list.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish authorship, date, historical setting, or whether every scene reports a particular historical event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler could have joined originally independent scenes by adding a conventional liturgical refrain.
- The repetition may reflect communal worship formulas rather than composition by one author at one time.
