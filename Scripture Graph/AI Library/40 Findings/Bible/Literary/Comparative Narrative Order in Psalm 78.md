# Comparative Narrative Order in Psalm 78

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The plague recital is selective and does not reproduce Exodus's sequence exactly: Psalm 78 places flies before frogs and clusters other destructive agents rhetorically. This is strong evidence that the psalm is a theological-poetic retelling rather than an attempt at a complete chronological transcript.

**Does not establish:** The altered order does not establish historical error, dependence on a particular later source, or a date for the psalm.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The poet may arrange the plagues by imagery or rhetorical effect rather than chronology.
- The list may draw on a compressed traditional summary in addition to the form of the Exodus account now preserved.
