# Source Criticism Documentary Hypothesis in Genesis 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Genesis 1 orders creation as vegetation (day 3), then animals (days 5-6), then humankind last (day 6), whereas Genesis 2:7-19 narrates man formed first, before the garden is planted and before field animals and birds are formed; source-critical scholars (documentary hypothesis) have long cited this difference as evidence of two originally distinct creation traditions, while harmonizing readings treat Genesis 2 as a topical, non-chronological close-up on the events of day six rather than a second, conflicting sequence.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish the number, identity, or date of underlying sources behind Genesis 1-2; the documentary hypothesis itself is contested, especially outside historical-critical scholarship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Genesis 2 may be read as a non-chronological, topical recapitulation focused on day six's events rather than a second sequential creation account.
- The differing order may reflect a shift in narrative emphasis (cosmic overview in chapter 1 versus a human-centered focus in chapter 2) rather than distinct authorial sources.
