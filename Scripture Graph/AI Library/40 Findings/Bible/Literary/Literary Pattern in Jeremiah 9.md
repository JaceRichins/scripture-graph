# Literary Pattern in Jeremiah 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of tears, weeping, wailing, and lamentation from the opening through verses 17–20 is strong evidence that lament is a major organizing motif, but only limited evidence for a formally designed literary inclusio.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence does not by itself establish a precise literary structure, single-stage composition, date, or authorial intention.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Repeated mourning language may arise naturally from the sustained subject of catastrophe rather than from deliberate structural framing.
- The chapter may preserve several related oracles collected together rather than a single composition organized by an inclusio.
