# Near-Contemporary Monumental Art in 2 Chronicles 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Reliefs from Sennacherib's palace depict his capture of Lachish, independently supporting the chapter's historical setting and its specific placement of Sennacherib's forces at Lachish during the campaign.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish Chronicles' account of Jerusalem's deliverance, the speeches attributed to Assyrian representatives, or supernatural intervention.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Palace reliefs are royal propaganda and may stylize the scale, sequence, or character of the victory.
- The relief confirms the Lachish campaign but cannot show that Sennacherib was physically there at the exact moment his representatives addressed Jerusalem.

**Scripture:** [[2 Chronicles 32#^2chr-32-9|2 Chronicles 32:9]]

**Sources:**
- British Museum — *Relief: Sennacherib watches the capture of Lachish, British Museum object 124911* (700-692 BCE)
- British Museum — *Assyria: Siege of Lachish*
