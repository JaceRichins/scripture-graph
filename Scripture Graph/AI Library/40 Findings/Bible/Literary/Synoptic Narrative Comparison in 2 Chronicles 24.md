# Synoptic Narrative Comparison in 2 Chronicles 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Comparison with 2 Kings 12 shows agreement on Joash's qualified righteousness, temple repairs, Syrian pressure, and assassination. Within that comparison, Chronicles supplies the extended account of Jehoiada's death, apostasy, prophetic rejection, Zechariah's killing, the small Syrian force as judgment, and Joash's dishonorable burial. This is strong evidence that the Chronicler's presentation gives special emphasis to theological causation and moral reversal.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not independently prove the historicity of every distinctive episode or identify the Chronicler's precise sources.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may preserve additional source material rather than freely composing every distinctive episode.
- Some differences may reflect abbreviation in Kings rather than expansion in Chronicles.
- The exact literary relationship and direction of dependence between the accounts require broader source-critical analysis.
