# Early Literary Attestation in Luke 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close correspondence between Luke's words over the bread and cup and 1 Corinthians 11 shows that substantially similar teachings about Jesus's final meal circulated outside Luke's narrative by the time 1 Corinthians was written.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not prove that either account is a verbatim transcript of Jesus's words or determine the precise wording and sequence used at the meal.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Luke and Paul may preserve a shared oral or liturgical tradition rather than independent eyewitness reports.
- Literary influence between Pauline and Lukan circles could contribute to the similarity.

**Scripture:** [[Luke 22#^luke-22-19|Luke 22:19-20]], [[1 Corinthians 11#^1cor-11-23|1 Corinthians 11:23-25]]
