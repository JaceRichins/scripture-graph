# Internal Literary Pattern in Genesis 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated use of failed sight, recognizable voice, deceptive touch, food, clothing, and smell is strong internal evidence that the episode is carefully constructed as a literary narrative centered on uncertain identity and misdirected recognition.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the episode's historicity, date, inspiration, single authorship, or any particular source-critical theory.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sensory detail may arise naturally from telling a plausible impersonation story rather than from an elaborate formal design.
- Some repetitions may reflect conventional oral storytelling or later editorial shaping.
