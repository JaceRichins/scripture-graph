# Prosopographic And Literary Overlap in Colossians 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Colossians and Philemon share an unusually concentrated personal network: Onesimus, Epaphras, Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, and Archippus occur in both. This is substantial internal evidence of a close literary or situational relationship between the letters.

**Does not establish:** The shared network alone does not prove common authorship, Pauline authorship, or an exact date and place of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- One letter could be literarily dependent on or deliberately imitating the other.
- An editor could have preserved or adapted an authentic personal network.
- The overlap need not mean every greeting was written at precisely the same time.
