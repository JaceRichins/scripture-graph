# Narrative Battle Report in 2 Samuel 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrator's statement that Abner and Israel were beaten, together with the reported losses of twenty on David's side and 360 on Abner's side, strongly supports the limited conclusion that David's forces won this particular engagement.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every reported number is independently verifiable, that David ordered the confrontation, or that the victors held divine or moral approval for every action.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The casualty figures may be schematic, stylized, or based on the narrator's source rather than a modern comprehensive count.
- A victory in one battle does not by itself determine the final political outcome.
