# Parallel-Account Numerical Variation in 1 Kings 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
First Kings 4:26 reports forty thousand stalls of horses, whereas the parallel notice in 2 Chronicles 9:25 reports four thousand stalls; both report twelve thousand horsemen. The tenfold difference is direct evidence of a numerical textual or traditional problem that should be acknowledged rather than silently harmonized.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy does not by itself identify the earlier reading, prove which figure is historically correct, or show that the remainder of either account is unreliable.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A copyist may have introduced a numeral variation during transmission.
- The accounts may preserve different numerical traditions.
- The writers may be counting different units, although the similar wording makes this less straightforward.
- One or both figures may be schematic rather than census-level statistics.
