# Internal Literary Evidence in Deuteronomy 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit schedule of dispossession 'by little and little,' together with the concern that wild animals could increase if the land were emptied too quickly, is strong internal evidence that verse 22 envisages a phased process. Totalizing destruction language elsewhere in the chapter remains and should not be silently dissolved into this verse.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish what historically occurred, the actual scale of violence, or that every severe command elsewhere in the chapter is merely figurative.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ancient totalizing war rhetoric may coexist with an expectation of prolonged occupation.
- The chapter may preserve or combine formulations originating in different covenant or conquest traditions.
- The gradual schedule could concern settlement and administration more than the duration of individual battles.
