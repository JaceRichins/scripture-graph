# Internal Literary Evidence in Psalm 97

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
References to the earth, the isles, the whole world, all the people, and the LORD's supremacy over all the earth provide strong internal evidence that the poem presents his reign as universal in scope. This geographical and poetic scope does not determine a specific historical or eschatological fulfillment.

**Does not establish:** A millennial chronology, the date or authorship of the psalm, or a literal future melting of geographical hills.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Universal language may function as poetic hyperbole rather than as a geographical prediction.
- The poem may celebrate the LORD's enduring kingship without locating its realization in one future event.
