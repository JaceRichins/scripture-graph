# Literary-Rhetorical Pattern in 1 Corinthians 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained contrast between the Corinthians as full, rich, kingly, wise, strong, and honorable and the apostles as condemned, foolish, weak, and despised is strong internal evidence that verses 8–10 employ irony to expose inflated Corinthian self-estimation.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish that every statement in verses 8–10 is sarcastic or that Paul denies every kind of spiritual maturity among the Corinthians.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some individual clauses may contain sincere acknowledgment or aspiration alongside the irony.
- The passage may be described more generally as hyperbolic admonition rather than assigned a narrowly defined rhetorical category.
