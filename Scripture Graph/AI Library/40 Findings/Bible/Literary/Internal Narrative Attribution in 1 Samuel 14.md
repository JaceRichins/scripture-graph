# Internal Narrative Attribution in 1 Samuel 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrator explicitly interprets the day's outcome as the LORD saving Israel. This is strong evidence for the narrative's theological framing of the victory, but it is not independent evidence for the historical mechanism of the reported events.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the account's external historicity, precise chronology, or the physical cause of the earthquake and Philistine confusion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statement may be a theological interpretation supplied by the narrator rather than a description of an independently observable cause.
- The narrative also identifies human combat, enemy confusion, defections, and reinforcements as contributing elements.
