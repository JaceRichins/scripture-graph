# Internal Narrative Sign in Mark 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative's own logic, the visible healing is offered as evidence that the Son of man has authority to perform the otherwise invisible act of forgiving sins.

**Does not establish:** By itself, the narrative does not independently establish the healing's historicity, its medical mechanism, or Jesus' divine identity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A reader may accept the episode's literary logic without accepting the healing as a recoverable historical event.
- The narrative may have been shaped to express an early Christian theological claim about Jesus' authority.

**Scripture:** [[Mark 2#^mark-2-9|Mark 2:9-12]]
