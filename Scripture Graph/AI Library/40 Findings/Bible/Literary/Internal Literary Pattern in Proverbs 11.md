# Internal Literary Pattern in Proverbs 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The density of explicit contrasts is strong internal evidence that moral comparison is a primary literary strategy in Proverbs 11. It is not sufficient evidence that every outcome described is an immediate, exceptionless prediction for every individual.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a detailed chapter outline, a single historical setting, or guaranteed immediate outcomes in every case.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may arise from collecting independent antithetical proverbs rather than designing a tightly unified chapter.
- A literary contrast can express a reliable moral norm without functioning as an exhaustive or exceptionless causal law.
