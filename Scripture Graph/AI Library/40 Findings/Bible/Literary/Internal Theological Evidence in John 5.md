# Internal Theological Evidence in John 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 5 provides strong internal textual evidence for an elevated portrayal of the Son: he gives life, receives authority to judge, and is to be honored as the Father is honored. The same discourse also distinguishes the Son from the Father and describes authority and life as given by the Father.

**Does not establish:** These verses alone do not define the later technical vocabulary of Trinitarian creeds or settle every question about the metaphysical relationship between Father and Son.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may be interpreted principally through delegated divine agency rather than as a complete statement of shared ontology.
- The Father's giving of life and authority, together with the Son's dependence, can be emphasized as functional subordination.
- Different theological traditions integrate the equality and dependence language differently.
