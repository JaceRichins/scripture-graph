# Synoptic Literary Comparison in 2 Chronicles 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The parallel account in 1 Kings moves directly from the priests leaving the sanctuary to the cloud filling the house, while Chronicles places detailed information about sanctified priests, named singer groups, instruments, unity, and thanksgiving immediately before that outcome. This comparison is strong evidence for Chronicles' distinctive emphasis on organized temple worship.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not establish whether Chronicles directly edited Kings, used a shared source, or preserved an independent tradition, and it does not prove that every reported ceremonial detail is historically exact.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may preserve independent historical or liturgical tradition rather than having composed the additional material.
- The difference could reflect access to a shared source in a fuller form rather than a deliberate expansion of Kings.
