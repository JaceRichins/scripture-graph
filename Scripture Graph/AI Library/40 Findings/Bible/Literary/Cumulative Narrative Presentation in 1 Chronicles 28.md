# Cumulative Narrative Presentation in 1 Chronicles 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Solomon's authority is reinforced by public assembly, explicit divine choice, Davidic endorsement, a revealed plan, allocated resources, organized priests and Levites, skilled workers, and support from leaders and people. This cumulative presentation strongly supports interpreting the chapter as a literary legitimation of Solomon's succession and temple commission.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently verify the historicity of the speech, the assembly, or every administrative detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The details may primarily teach an ideal model of authorized temple service rather than address disputed succession.
- The narrative can simultaneously legitimate Solomon and emphasize communal responsibility; these purposes are not mutually exclusive.
