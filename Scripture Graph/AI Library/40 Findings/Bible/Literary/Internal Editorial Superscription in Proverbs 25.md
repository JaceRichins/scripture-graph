# Internal Editorial Superscription in Proverbs 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The superscription presents this collection as Solomonic proverbs copied by officials associated with King Hezekiah. It is internal evidence for an editorial or scribal stage distinct from the sayings' attributed origin.

**Does not establish:** The heading alone does not establish that Solomon composed every saying, that the present form is unchanged from Hezekiah's reign, or that the attribution is independently verified.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The heading may preserve reliable information about an actual Hezekian compilation project.
- A later editor may have attributed the collection to Hezekiah's officials without direct contemporary documentation.
- The officials may have copied, selected, edited, or reorganized earlier material; the verb alone does not specify the full process.

**Scripture:** [[Proverbs 25#^prov-25-1|Proverbs 25:1]]
