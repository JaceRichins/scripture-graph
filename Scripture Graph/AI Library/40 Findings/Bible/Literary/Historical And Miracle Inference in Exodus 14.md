# Historical And Miracle Inference in Exodus 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The strong east wind and overnight duration supply a narrated physical process, while divine commands and the divided-water imagery frame that process as the LORD's act. These details firmly establish how the text presents the event but offer little by themselves for choosing among modern historical or physical reconstructions.

**Does not establish:** The wording alone does not verify a miracle externally, identify a reproducible natural mechanism, determine the water's depth, or settle the event's historicity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may describe an extraordinary natural event interpreted theologically.
- The wind may be part of a miraculous event that cannot be reduced to ordinary processes.
- The details may be literary imagery rather than a technically precise physical description.
- A remembered event may have been amplified or reshaped through transmission.
