# Literary Structure in Revelation 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated message form and the systematic reuse of Christ's attributes from Revelation 1 provide strong literary evidence that Revelation 1-3 functions as an integrated opening unit rather than as unrelated sayings.

**Does not establish:** Structural unity does not independently establish single-stage composition, authorship, historical accuracy, or divine inspiration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor could have imposed the shared form on previously independent messages.
- Formulaic similarity alone cannot prove that every line originated at the same compositional stage.
