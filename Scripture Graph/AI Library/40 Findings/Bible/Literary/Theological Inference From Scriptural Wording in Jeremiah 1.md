# Theological Inference From Scriptural Wording in Jeremiah 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 1:5 provides substantial textual support for divine foreknowledge and a prophetic designation preceding Jeremiah's birth. It is compatible with belief in premortal life, but the verse alone does not specify Jeremiah's conscious premortal existence or describe a general doctrine concerning all human beings.

**Does not establish:** The verse does not by itself establish the nature, duration, or universal scope of premortal human existence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may express divine foreknowledge without asserting conscious premortal existence.
- The language may be prophetic call rhetoric emphasizing that Jeremiah's vocation originated with God rather than with Jeremiah.
- The statements may concern Jeremiah's exceptional prophetic commission rather than a universal anthropology.
