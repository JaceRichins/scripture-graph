# Internal Prediction-Fulfillment Pattern in 2 Kings 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the final form of the narrative, Isaiah's prediction that Sennacherib will return to his land and fall by the sword there is answered point by point when Sennacherib returns to Nineveh and is later killed by his sons.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence alone does not prove supernatural foreknowledge, establish the oracle's date, or show that all narrated events occurred close together.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prediction may have been formulated or edited after the events.
- An editor may have selected known events to create a deliberate narrative closure.
- The chapter does not state that the assassination followed immediately after the Jerusalem campaign.
