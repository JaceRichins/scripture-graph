# Literary Structure in Genesis 39

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated combinations of divine presence, favor, comprehensive delegated authority, lack of supervision, and prosperity provide strong textual evidence that the house and prison episodes are deliberately presented as parallel stages in Joseph's rise.

**Does not establish:** The parallelism does not by itself establish a particular compositional history, date, author, or historical reconstruction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may arise partly from conventional storytelling formulas rather than a carefully engineered literary structure.
- Some similarities follow naturally from narrating the same character succeeding under two different supervisors.
