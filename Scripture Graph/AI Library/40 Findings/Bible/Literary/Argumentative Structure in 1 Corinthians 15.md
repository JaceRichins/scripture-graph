# Argumentative Structure in 1 Corinthians 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The consequences Paul derives from denying resurrection provide strong evidence that Christ's resurrection is structurally indispensable to the gospel argued in this chapter, rather than an optional supplement to it.

**Does not establish:** The logical importance of resurrection within Paul's theology does not by itself prove that the resurrection occurred historically.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage establishes centrality within Paul's argument, not necessarily within every early Christian group's theology.

**Scripture:** [[1 Corinthians 15#^1cor-15-12|1 Corinthians 15:12-20]]
