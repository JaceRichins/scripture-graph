# Internal Literary Pattern in Ezekiel 40

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The accumulation of repeated dimensions, paired architectural elements, matched gate plans, measured intervals, and a foursquare court strongly supports the limited literary conclusion that symmetry and ordered sacred space are foregrounded in the account.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish that the complex was historically constructed, that every dimension has a symbolic meaning, or that the vision was intended as a directly executable blueprint.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Repetition and symmetry may primarily serve the practical needs of a technical architectural description.
- Some regularization could reflect the transmission or editorial shaping of the account rather than a separate symbolic program.
- The layout may be simultaneously practical and symbolic; the chapter itself does not require an exclusive choice.
