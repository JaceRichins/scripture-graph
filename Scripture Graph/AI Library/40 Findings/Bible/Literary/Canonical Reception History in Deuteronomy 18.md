# Canonical Reception History in Deuteronomy 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 3 applies the promise of the prophet like Moses within its proclamation of Jesus, while Acts 7 repeats the promise in Stephen's account of Moses. This is strong evidence for early Christian messianic reception of Deuteronomy 18, though it does not by itself establish that an exclusive prediction of Jesus exhausts the passage's meaning in its Deuteronomic setting.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish from Deuteronomy 18 alone that every feature of the promised prophet refers exclusively to Jesus or that no nearer institutional fulfillment was intended.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Acts may present a christological rereading or culmination of a passage that originally concerned an ongoing prophetic office.
- A succession of prophets and a final Moses-like prophet need not be mutually exclusive interpretations.
