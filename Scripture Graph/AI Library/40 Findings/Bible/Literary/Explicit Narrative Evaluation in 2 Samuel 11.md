# Explicit Narrative Evaluation in 2 Samuel 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closing sentence is strong textual evidence that the narrator intends David's conduct to be judged rather than treated as morally neutral royal behavior. Responsibility is explicitly centered on "the thing that David had done."

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish every historical detail, Bath-sheba's interior state, or the mechanism by which the narrator knew the LORD's judgment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sentence is a theological judgment within the narrative, not independent corroboration of the event or of a divine communication.
- Readers may differ over precisely which preceding acts the singular phrase encompasses, although the plot strongly centers David's conduct.

**Scripture:** [[2 Samuel 11#^2sam-11-27|2 Samuel 11:27]]
