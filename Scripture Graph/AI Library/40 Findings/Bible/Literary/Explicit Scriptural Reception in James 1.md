# Explicit Scriptural Reception in James 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Joseph Smith—History explicitly identifies James 1:5 as the passage that impressed Joseph Smith and contributed to his decision to ask God. This establishes a direct textual and reception-history connection between James 1 and the First Vision narrative as that narrative presents it.

**Does not establish:** The intertextual connection alone does not establish the supernatural occurrence, precise dating, or theological interpretation of the First Vision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The First Vision account may be evaluated as retrospective religious narration while still demonstrating James 1:5's stated influence within the account.
- The textual dependence establishes how the narrative frames Joseph Smith's decision; it does not independently adjudicate every historical or theological claim associated with the vision.
