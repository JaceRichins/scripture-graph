# Internal Eyewitness Attestation in John 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 19:35 contains an explicit internal claim that an eyewitness truthfully testified about the pierced side so that readers might believe. This is meaningful evidence that the narrative presents the report as grounded in testimony, but internal self-attestation alone cannot identify the witness or independently verify the event.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove the reported phenomenon, the identity of the witness, or direct eyewitness authorship of the entire Gospel.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statement may be a literary authentication formula rather than evidence permitting modern identification of an eyewitness.
- A final narrator or editor could be affirming testimony received from another person.
- The unnamed witness need not be identical with the final author of the Gospel.
