# Internal Historical Attribution in Zephaniah 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The superscription is direct internal evidence that the book presents Zephaniah's ministry as occurring during Josiah's reign, but it is not independently sufficient to date every oracle or editorial layer in the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove authorship, exact year, or that the entire present chapter reached its final form during Josiah's lifetime.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The superscription may preserve reliable contemporary historical memory.
- It may have been supplied or standardized by a later editor using received tradition.
- Some individual oracles could have been revised after their initial delivery.
