# Internal Narrative And Theological Framing in Jeremiah 40

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Nebuzar-adan's speech is strong internal evidence that the narrative presents Judah's defeat as the fulfillment of divine warning rather than as an event outside the LORD's declared purposes.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish Nebuzar-adan's conversion, the precise historical wording of his speech, or external verification of divine causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may be placing the book's theology in the Babylonian official's mouth.
- The speech may be a literary summary rather than a verbatim historical transcript.
- The official may be acknowledging Jeremiah's predictions pragmatically without expressing personal covenant faith.

**Scripture:** [[Jeremiah 40#^jer-40-2|Jeremiah 40:2]], [[Jeremiah 40#^jer-40-3|Jeremiah 40:3]]
