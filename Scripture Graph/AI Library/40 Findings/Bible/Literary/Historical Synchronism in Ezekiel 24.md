# Historical Synchronism in Ezekiel 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ezekiel 24:1-2, 2 Kings 25:1, and Jeremiah 52:4 give the same ninth-year, tenth-month, tenth-day date for Babylon's move against Jerusalem. This is strong evidence that the passages refer to the same siege event, although literary dependence or use of common records means the agreement is not necessarily independent corroboration.

**Does not establish:** The synchronism does not by itself establish independent eyewitness testimony, supernatural foreknowledge, or a precise modern-calendar date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The books may draw on a common chronological record.
- One textual tradition may have influenced or harmonized another.
- Agreement about the event does not by itself establish that Ezekiel recorded the oracle before news arrived.
