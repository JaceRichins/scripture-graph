# Internal Literary And Theological Evidence in Isaiah 48

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 48 provides strong internal literary evidence that prediction and fulfillment are being used polemically to distinguish the LORD from idols. The text confidently makes that theological argument; by itself, however, it cannot independently verify when each declaration or fulfillment occurred.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish the historical date of every declaration, the mechanism of revelation, or supernatural prediction independently of external dating evidence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A reader may understand the declarations as genuine predictions made before the events.
- A reader may understand the passage as retrospective theological narration framed as prior prediction.
- The rhetoric may combine earlier prophetic material with later interpretation of its fulfillment.
