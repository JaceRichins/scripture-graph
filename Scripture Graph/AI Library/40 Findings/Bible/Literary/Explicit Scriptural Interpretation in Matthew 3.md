# Explicit Scriptural Interpretation in Matthew 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 3:3 is strong evidence that the Gospel writer interpreted John's ministry through Isaiah 40:3, but the author's identification is not independent proof that Isaiah originally predicted John specifically or that the narrated events occurred exactly as described.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the original historical referent of Isaiah 40:3, predictive fulfillment in a modern evidentiary sense, or the independent historicity of every narrative detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew may be applying an older restoration text typologically rather than claiming that John was its sole original referent.
- The identification may reflect early Christian theological interpretation of John rather than independent historical corroboration.

**Scripture:** [[Matthew 3#^matt-3-3|Matthew 3:3]], [[Isaiah 40#^isa-40-3|Isaiah 40:3]]
