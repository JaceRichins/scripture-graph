# Explicit Rhetorical Contrast in 1 Corinthians 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The movement from "such were some of you" to "washed," "sanctified," and "justified" is strong textual evidence that the warning about unrighteous conduct is paired with the possibility of a decisive change in status and life through the Lord Jesus and the Spirit.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a complete chronology of salvation, the relative timing of the three acts, or that moral transformation occurs without continuing repentance and discipleship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The three passive verbs may emphasize overlapping aspects of conversion rather than a fixed chronological sequence.
- The statement of changed status does not imply that converted believers can no longer be tempted or sin, since the chapter itself continues to admonish them.
