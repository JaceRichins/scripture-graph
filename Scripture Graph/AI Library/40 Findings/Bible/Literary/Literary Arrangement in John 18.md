# Literary Arrangement in John 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of Jesus' examination between Peter's denials is meaningful literary evidence that the reader is invited to compare their responses under pressure.

**Does not establish:** The arrangement does not establish a particular source theory, date of composition, or the precise intention of the author.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The alternation may preserve the chronology or inherited tradition without having been designed chiefly as a character contrast.
- The scenes may also emphasize simultaneous proceedings rather than only contrasting courage and failure.
