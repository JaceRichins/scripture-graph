# Internal Narrative Evidence in Ezra 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The simultaneous joy and weeping provide strong textual evidence that the foundation ceremony elicited divergent responses, especially among older participants who remembered the first house; the passage does not explicitly state every reason for their tears.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the older witnesses rejected the new temple, that the new foundation was objectively inferior, or that all older people wept.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The older witnesses may have wept from gratitude or emotional remembrance rather than disappointment.
- Their tears may have combined grief over past destruction with joy at restoration.
- The passage itself does not compare the dimensions or quality of the two buildings.
