# Repeated Geographic And Communal Framing in 2 Chronicles 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated inclusion of northern territories and the remnant of Israel is meaningful evidence that the chapter presents proper worship at Jerusalem as concerning more than Judah alone.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish the actual extent of Josiah's political control or the degree of popular participation in the former northern kingdom.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may express an idealized theological claim rather than effective royal authority over all named territories.
- Participation by a remnant does not imply participation by the whole northern population.

**Scripture:** [[2 Chronicles 34#^2chr-34-6|2 Chronicles 34:6-9]], [[2 Chronicles 34#^2chr-34-33|2 Chronicles 34:33]]
