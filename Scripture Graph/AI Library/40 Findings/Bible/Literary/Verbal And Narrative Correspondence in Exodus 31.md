# Verbal And Narrative Correspondence in Exodus 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Exodus 35:30-35 later repeats the names, tribal identities, divine wisdom, and craft abilities introduced in Exodus 31. This correspondence supplies strong internal literary evidence that the later construction narrative is presented as fulfillment of the earlier commission.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence establishes a textual instruction-and-execution relationship but does not independently establish Mosaic authorship, historical dating, or the historicity of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition could be conventional recapitulation rather than a specially crafted fulfillment device.
- An editor could have harmonized the commission and execution accounts.
- The correspondence does not by itself determine when, by whom, or through what compositional process the passages reached their present form.
