# Canonical Ritual Parallel in Genesis 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 34 describes covenant makers cutting a calf and passing between its parts. That explicit biblical parallel strongly supports reading the divided animals and passage between them in Genesis 15 as covenant-ritual imagery.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish the precise historical form, date, or complete meaning of the Genesis ceremony.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two passages may employ related imagery without describing identical ceremonies.
- Genesis 15 itself does not explain every component or state the consequence symbolized by passing between the pieces.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 15#^gen-15-9|Genesis 15:9-10]], [[Genesis 15#^gen-15-17|Genesis 15:17-18]], [[Jeremiah 34#^jer-34-18|Jeremiah 34:18-20]]
