# Narrative Continuity in 1 Kings 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The unresolved stories of Joab, Barzillai, and Shimei from Samuel are all taken up in David's final instructions, providing strong evidence of deliberate literary continuity between the Samuel narratives and the opening of Kings.

**Does not establish:** The connections do not establish single authorship, contemporaneous composition, or the historical accuracy of every narrative detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later compiler could have connected originally independent traditions.
- Literary continuity alone cannot determine whether all narrated events are historically recoverable.
