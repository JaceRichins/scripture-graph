# Internal Literary Evidence in Ezra 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of archival search, recovered memorandum, royal directive, and official compliance provides strong evidence that the narrative is constructed to legitimate the rebuilding through recognized administrative authority. This literary function does not by itself authenticate every reported decree as a preserved historical document.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove the date, archival provenance, verbatim accuracy, or historical authenticity of the decrees.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The administrative details may primarily preserve historical procedure rather than serve a deliberate literary strategy.
- A later narrator or editor could have arranged genuine or traditional materials into a legitimating sequence.
