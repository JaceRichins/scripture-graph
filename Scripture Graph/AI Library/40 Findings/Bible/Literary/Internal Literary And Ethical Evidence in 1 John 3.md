# Internal Literary And Ethical Evidence in 1 John 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from Christ's self-sacrifice, to willingness to lay down one's life, to sharing material goods with a needy brother provides strong textual evidence that the chapter understands Christian love as concrete and costly rather than merely verbal or emotional.

**Does not establish:** This passage alone does not specify a complete economic program or prove that every request for material assistance must be met in the same way.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The examples may illustrate love without exhaustively defining every form that love must take.
- Laying down one's life may function as an ideal of disposition as well as a demand for literal martyrdom.
