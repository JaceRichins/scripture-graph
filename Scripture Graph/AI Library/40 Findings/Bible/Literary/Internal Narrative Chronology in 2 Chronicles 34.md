# Internal Narrative Chronology in 2 Chronicles 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter's stated chronology, the book's discovery cannot be the initiating cause of Josiah's earlier search for God or the beginning of his purge, because those developments are dated six and ten years before the eighteenth-year discovery.

**Does not establish:** This chronology does not establish the historical circumstances behind the book's composition, deposition, or discovery.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The regnal dates may serve a literary or editorial scheme rather than preserve a complete chronology of every reform activity.
- The discovery could have intensified, redirected, or supplied textual authority for an already existing reform even though it did not initiate it.
