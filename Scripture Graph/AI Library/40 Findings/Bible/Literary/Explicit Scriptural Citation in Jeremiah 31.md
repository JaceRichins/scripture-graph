# Explicit Scriptural Citation in Jeremiah 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hebrews 8:8-12 reproduces Jeremiah 31:31-34 at length and employs the passage in a Christian argument about covenant. This is strong evidence for the passage's early Christian reception, but the citation alone does not settle every question about Jeremiah's original historical horizon.

**Does not establish:** The citation does not by itself prove that Jeremiah's original audience understood the oracle in precisely the same way as Hebrews, nor does it resolve all debates over continuity between the covenants.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Hebrews interprets Jeremiah within its own Christian covenant argument; that reception need not exhaust the oracle's earlier Judean and Israelite setting.
- Differences in wording can reflect the Greek textual tradition used by Hebrews rather than uncertainty about the literary dependence.
