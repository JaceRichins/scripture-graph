# Internal Rhetorical Context in 2 Corinthians 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence from Paul's intended itinerary, through the question whether he acted lightly, to his statement that he delayed coming in order to spare the Corinthians is strong internal evidence that he is defending a changed or postponed travel plan against an accusation of unreliability.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not independently verify Paul's motive, identify his critics, or reconstruct the complete sequence of visits and correspondence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The precise accusation may have concerned more than the itinerary, since the chapter preserves only Paul's side of the exchange.
- The statement about sparing Corinth might explain one stage of the travel history rather than every alteration to the plan.
