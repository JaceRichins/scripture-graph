# Internal Logistical Fit in Numbers 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of the tabernacle carriers before the sanctuary bearers supplies an intelligible logistical reason for the otherwise highly formal marching order: the structure can be ready to receive the sacred objects. This supports the reading that the sequence is purposefully arranged.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove eyewitness reporting, historical execution of the arrangement, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The logistical explanation may be an idealized literary rationale rather than a record of actual practice.
- The sequence could have been systematized retrospectively.
- The antecedent of the KJV phrase 'the other' is implicit rather than explicitly named in verse 21.
