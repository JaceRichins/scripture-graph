# Internal Literary Structure in Deuteronomy 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The ordered movement from remembered benefits to obligation, identification of covenant parties, sanctions, and future public witness is strong internal evidence that the chapter is deliberately organized as covenant rhetoric.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish a precise date, compositional history, or direct dependence on any particular extrabiblical treaty form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence could reflect ordinary persuasive discourse rather than a consciously formal covenant pattern.
- Some of the organization may result from literary shaping or compilation rather than a single original delivery.
