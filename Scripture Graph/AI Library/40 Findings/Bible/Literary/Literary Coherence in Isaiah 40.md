# Literary Coherence in Isaiah 40

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated vocabulary and coordinated contrasts—including grass and withering, divine blowing, the known/heard questions, and the movement from cosmic power to strength for the faint—provide moderate-to-strong internal evidence that the chapter has been deliberately shaped as a coherent literary argument.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence alone does not establish single authorship, composition in one sitting, a particular date, or supernatural origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetitions may arise naturally from a shared subject rather than a detailed compositional design.
- Editorial arrangement could produce coherence even if individual materials originated separately.
