# Lexical And Literary Pattern in 2 Kings 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The movement from the covenant requirement of wholehearted obedience to the closing evaluation that Josiah turned with all his heart, soul, and might is substantial literary evidence that the narrator portrays him as embodying the covenant ideal. The wording also closely parallels Deuteronomy 6:5.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not establish Josiah's unobservable inner state, direct literary dependence on one particular manuscript, or a precise date of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The similarity may arise from conventional covenant vocabulary rather than a deliberately constructed frame.
- The formulation may reflect an editor's standard Deuteronomic diction rather than the language of the historical event.
