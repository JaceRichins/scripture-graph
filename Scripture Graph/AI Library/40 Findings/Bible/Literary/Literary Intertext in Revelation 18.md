# Literary Intertext in Revelation 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Revelation 18:7–8 closely reworks Isaiah's oracle against Babylon: both depict a self-secure royal woman who denies widowhood or sorrow and then suffers sudden disaster in one day. The distinctive cluster makes literary reuse or deliberate allusion highly probable.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the author's identity, the date of Revelation, or a single exhaustive historical fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author could be drawing on a wider stock of prophetic judgment language rather than directly composing from Isaiah 47.
- The Isaiah language may have reached Revelation through liturgical or oral reuse.
