# Repetition And Discourse Structure in Amos 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The fivefold refrain “yet have ye not returned unto me” gives verses 6-11 a deliberate cumulative shape, and “Therefore” in verse 12 marks the consequence of that repeated refusal.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish that the whole unit arose in one compositional stage or that the listed disasters appear in chronological order.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated formula may have originated in separate prophetic sayings later collected into their present sequence.
- The structure demonstrates literary arrangement but does not by itself identify the arranger.
