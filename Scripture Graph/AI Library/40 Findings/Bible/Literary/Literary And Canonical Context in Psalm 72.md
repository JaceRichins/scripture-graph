# Literary And Canonical Context in Psalm 72

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The references to a king, a king's son, governance of God's people, and the concluding notice about David give the psalm a strong Israelite royal horizon. The parallel with Solomon's prayer makes a Solomonic application especially intelligible.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the psalm's precise author, date, original performance setting, or an exclusively Solomonic referent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may describe an ideal Davidic ruler without referring exclusively to Solomon.
- The psalm may have been reused for successive kings in royal worship.
- A later messianic application can coexist with an initial royal setting.
