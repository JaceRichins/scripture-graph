# Literary-Theological Pattern in Daniel 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Divine action structures the chapter's movement: the Lord gives Jehoiakim into Nebuchadnezzar's hand, God brings Daniel into favor with the official, and God gives the four youths knowledge and skill. This repeated attribution strongly supports the interpretation that divine sovereignty operates through both national defeat and personal preservation.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the chapter's date of composition, the historicity of every detail, or a promise that faithful people will always receive visible success.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated divine attributions may reflect conventional theological narration rather than a deliberately constructed three-part pattern.
- The pattern establishes the narrator's theology but does not independently verify the historical causes of the events.
