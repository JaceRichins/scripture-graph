# Literary And Genre Evidence in Genesis 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated combination of ancestry, peoples, languages, territories, cities, borders, and kingdoms is strong internal evidence that Genesis 10 functions as an ethnogeographic genealogy—a literary map of nations expressed through kinship—not merely as a private family pedigree.

**Does not establish:** This literary form does not by itself establish the historical completeness, precise date, or biological accuracy of every relationship in the table.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author may have understood the named peoples as biological descendants while also using them to organize geography.
- Some geographic notices may be explanatory expansions attached to an older genealogical list.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 10#^gen-10-5|Genesis 10:5]], [[Genesis 10#^gen-10-10|Genesis 10:10-12]], [[Genesis 10#^gen-10-15|Genesis 10:15-20]], [[Genesis 10#^gen-10-30|Genesis 10:30-32]]
