# Fulfillment Citation in John 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 13:18's citation ('He that eateth bread with me hath lifted up his heel against me') is presented by the text itself as scripture being fulfilled, and is a clear allusion to Psalm 41:9, applying a psalm about betrayal by a trusted friend to Judas's act.

**Does not establish:** does not establish that Psalm 41 was originally composed as predictive prophecy of Judas specifically; establishes only that the Gospel writer frames Judas's act through that psalm's language.

**Alternative explanations:**
- John's wording is a paraphrase rather than a verbatim LXX/MT quotation, so the connection is thematic/typological rather than a strict citation formula.
- Psalm 41 is a lament of an afflicted individual generally, not an explicit messianic prophecy in its original setting; the messianic reading is a Christian retrospective application.

**Scripture:** [[John 13#^john-13-18|John 13:18]], [[Psalm 41#^ps-41-9|Psalm 41:9]]
