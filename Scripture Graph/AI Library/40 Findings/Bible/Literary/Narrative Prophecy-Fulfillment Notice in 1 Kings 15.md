# Narrative Prophecy-Fulfillment Notice in 1 Kings 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrative explicitly frames Baasha's destruction of Jeroboam's house as fulfillment of the LORD's prior saying through Ahijah and as judgment for Jeroboam's sins. This is strong evidence for the narrator's fulfillment theology but, standing within the same literary history, only limited independent evidence that the prediction preceded the event.

**Does not establish:** The internal fulfillment notice alone does not independently establish the date of the oracle, the compositional history of the passages, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ahijah's prediction was preserved before the event and subsequently recognized as fulfilled.
- The prediction or fulfillment notice was shaped after the dynasty's destruction.
- A historical oracle and later editorial formulation may both contribute to the present account.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 14#^1kgs-14-10|1 Kings 14:10-11]], [[1 Kings 15#^1kgs-15-27|1 Kings 15:27-30]]
