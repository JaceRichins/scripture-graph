# Internal Literary Pattern in 1 Kings 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated declaration that victory will cause Ahab or Israel to know that the LORD is the LORD is strong internal evidence that the two campaigns have been arranged around a common theological theme.

**Does not establish:** The repetition does not establish single authorship, exact historical reportage, or supernatural causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler or editor could have supplied or emphasized the repeated formula while combining originally separate battle traditions.
- The shared wording may reflect a conventional prophetic formula rather than a deliberately designed two-part composition.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 20#^1kgs-20-13|1 Kings 20:13]], [[1 Kings 20#^1kgs-20-28|1 Kings 20:28]]
