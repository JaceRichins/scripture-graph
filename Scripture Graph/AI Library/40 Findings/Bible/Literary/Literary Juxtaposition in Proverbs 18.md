# Literary Juxtaposition in Proverbs 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The immediate juxtaposition of two fortification sayings—divine name as a strong tower and wealth as an imagined strong city—is strong internal evidence that the reader is invited to compare reliable refuge with perceived material security.

**Does not establish:** The juxtaposition does not establish that wealth is inherently evil, that material resources have no legitimate protective value, or that verse 10 promises exemption from every temporal harm.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sayings may have been placed together chiefly because they share fortification vocabulary, without requiring an absolute opposition.
- Verse 11 may warn against imagined invulnerability rather than condemn wealth itself.

**Scripture:** [[Proverbs 18#^prov-18-10|Proverbs 18:10]], [[Proverbs 18#^prov-18-11|Proverbs 18:11]]
