# Internal Literary Evidence in 1 Chronicles 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As internal literary evidence, the repeated inventories, work assignments, charge to Solomon, and mobilization of the princes strongly establish that this chapter portrays David as the temple project's principal preparer even though Solomon will be its builder.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently verify the historical quantities, labor arrangements, or exact sequence of preparations.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prominence assigned to David may reflect the theological and literary interests of Chronicles rather than a complete administrative history.
- Some preparations may be summarized or idealized instead of reported with modern documentary precision.
