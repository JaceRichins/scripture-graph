# Internal Chronological Indicator in Judges 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The expressions stating that the Jebusites remained in Jerusalem and that the second Luz retained its name 'unto this day' present the narrator as looking back from a time later than the narrated events. They provide evidence of temporal distance but no precise date.

**Does not establish:** These formulas do not establish how many years had passed, whether the entire chapter was composed simultaneously, or a precise date for Judges.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later narrator may have composed the notices.
- An editor may have added or retained inherited formulas.
- The formulas may reflect only a short interval after the events.

**Scripture:** [[Judges 1#^judg-1-21|Judges 1:21]], [[Judges 1#^judg-1-26|Judges 1:26]]
