# Verbal And Thematic Parallel in Numbers 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Numbers 10:35 and Psalm 68:1 share the distinctive sequence of divine arising, enemies scattering, and haters fleeing. The verbal correspondence strongly supports a literary or traditional relationship, while its direction and historical development remain uncertain.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish direct dependence, the direction of borrowing, a shared author, or a particular date for either text.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Psalm 68 may reuse the saying preserved in Numbers.
- Numbers may reflect language also used in the psalmic tradition.
- Both passages may draw independently on a conventional sacred-war formula.
- One or both texts may have acquired the wording through later liturgical transmission.
