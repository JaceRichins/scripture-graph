# Internal Narrative Correspondence in 2 Samuel 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Nathan's prediction of calamity arising from David's own house and of another man publicly taking David's women has a close narrative counterpart when Absalom rebels and publicly enters David's concubines.

**Does not establish:** The narrative correspondence alone does not establish supernatural foreknowledge, the precise composition history of Samuel, or that every later disaster in David's family fulfills this oracle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The final narrator or editor may have shaped the prediction and later account to correspond.
- The correspondence may express a literary theology of consequences rather than disclose the historical mechanism behind the events.
