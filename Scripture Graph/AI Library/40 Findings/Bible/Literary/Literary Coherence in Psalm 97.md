# Literary Coherence in Psalm 97

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated rejoicing and righteousness/judgment vocabulary, together with the movement from royal proclamation through manifestation to ethical exhortation, provides substantial evidence that the chapter is a thematically integrated composition.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the psalm's author, date, historical occasion, or the intentionality of every proposed structural detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence may arise from conventional royal and worship vocabulary rather than an elaborate compositional design.
- Some perceived structural boundaries may reflect modern analytical preferences rather than formally marked divisions.
