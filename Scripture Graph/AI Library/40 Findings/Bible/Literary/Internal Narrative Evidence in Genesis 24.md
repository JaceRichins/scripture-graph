# Internal Narrative Evidence in Genesis 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, the exact and immediate correspondence between the servant's prayer and Rebekah's actions functions as strong literary evidence for the narrator's claim that the LORD guided the mission.

**Does not establish:** The narrative correspondence does not independently verify a supernatural cause, the account's precise historical sequence, or a general method for obtaining divine signs.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may reflect deliberate literary construction intended to communicate providence.
- The account may preserve an event interpreted theologically by its participants and narrator.
- From the text alone, coincidence cannot be independently excluded as an explanation of the reported events.
