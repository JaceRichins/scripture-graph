# Intra-Biblical Prediction-Fulfillment Pattern in 2 Kings 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Kings, Jehu's promised four-generation succession is realized through Jehoahaz, Joash, Jeroboam II, and Zechariah; Zechariah's death is explicitly identified as fulfillment of the word concerning Jehu's fourth generation.

**Does not establish:** The literary match by itself does not establish when the prediction was composed or prove supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The promise or its wording may have been shaped by an author or editor who already knew the dynasty's history.
- The regnal notices may have been arranged to make the historical succession serve the book's theological pattern.
