# Internal Literary Evidence in Jeremiah 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter, the recurrence of fields, money, signed documents, seals, and witnesses at the end provides strong internal evidence that Jeremiah's purchase is intentionally framed as a concrete sign of return rather than an incidental biographical detail.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not by itself establish the transaction's historicity, predictive fulfillment, date of composition, or single authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor could have arranged or expanded the narrative so that the transaction and restoration oracle interpret one another.
- The transaction account could have been composed as a theological illustration rather than preserving a performed historical act.
