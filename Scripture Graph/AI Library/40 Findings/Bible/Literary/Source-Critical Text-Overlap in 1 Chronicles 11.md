# Source-Critical Text-Overlap in 1 Chronicles 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verified text-overlap data show 1 Chronicles 11 shares substantial verbatim wording with 2 Samuel 23 (19 verses) and 2 Samuel 5 (3 verses), consistent with the well-documented pattern of the Chronicler drawing on Samuel-Kings as a primary source for the united-monarchy narrative.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself prove the direction of literary dependence (Chronicles using Samuel versus both using a common source), though direct dependence on Samuel-Kings is the majority scholarly view.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both books could independently draw on a shared earlier source (e.g., royal or military archives) rather than one text directly depending on the other
