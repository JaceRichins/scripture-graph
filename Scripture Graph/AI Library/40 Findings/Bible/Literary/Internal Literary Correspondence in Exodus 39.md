# Internal Literary Correspondence in Exodus 39

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The order and wording of Exodus 39's execution report closely correspond to the garment instructions in Exodus 28: ephod, memorial stones, breastplate, robe, and inscribed gold plate occur in substantially the same sequence. This is strong internal evidence that the two chapters were composed or arranged as command-and-fulfillment counterparts.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish Mosaic authorship, the date of composition, or the historical occurrence of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor or compiler could have fashioned the execution account directly from the earlier instructions.
- Close correspondence demonstrates a deliberate textual relationship but does not identify when or by whom either account was written.
