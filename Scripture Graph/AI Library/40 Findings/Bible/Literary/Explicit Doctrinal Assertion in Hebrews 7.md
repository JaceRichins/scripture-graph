# Explicit Doctrinal Assertion in Hebrews 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hebrews 7:23–25 gives strong textual support for the chapter's doctrine that Christ's saving mediation is enduring rather than interrupted by death. The passage attributes this capacity to his continuing life and priesthood.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself settle every question about the conditions, process, or human reception of salvation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Christian traditions differ over how Christ's intercession relates to earthly priesthood, ordinances, and individual perseverance.
- The phrase “to the uttermost” may be understood as complete salvation, perpetual salvation, or both.
