# Literary Structure in Acts 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The ordered geography of Acts 1:8 is strong internal evidence that the verse functions programmatically for Acts: the mission begins at Jerusalem and subsequently reaches Judæa, Samaria, and Gentile regions.

**Does not establish:** The structural correspondence does not by itself prove the historical accuracy of each episode or that Acts 1:8 exhaustively outlines the book.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may arise partly from the natural historical expansion of the movement rather than from a rigid literary outline.
- The later narrative does not follow these geographical labels as four neatly separated sections.
