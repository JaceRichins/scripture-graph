# Canonical Verbal Allusion in Isaiah 57

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared combination of peace with those who are “far off” and “near” is strong textual evidence that Ephesians 2:17 reuses or alludes to Isaiah 57:19. Ephesians applies the wording within its argument about the reconciliation of Gentiles and Jews through Christ.

**Does not establish:** The allusion does not establish that Isaiah 57 itself explicitly identified the far and near as Gentiles and Jews, nor that every element of Ephesians' argument is already present in Isaiah.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ephesians could be drawing on a familiar scriptural or liturgical peace formula rather than quoting Isaiah 57 directly.
- The far-and-near language could have reached Ephesians through an intervening interpretive tradition.
