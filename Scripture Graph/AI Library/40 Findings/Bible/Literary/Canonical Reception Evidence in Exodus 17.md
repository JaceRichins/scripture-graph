# Canonical Reception Evidence in Exodus 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Explicit reuse of the Massah and rock traditions in Deuteronomy, Psalms, and First Corinthians is strong evidence that Exodus 17 became an enduring source for legal exhortation, worship, and Christian interpretation.

**Does not establish:** Canonical reuse does not independently establish the historical occurrence of the events, the date of Exodus, or a single line of textual dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Later authors may draw on a shared wilderness tradition rather than directly on the present wording of Exodus 17.
- Different later uses reflect each author's theological purposes and should not automatically be read back into the Exodus narrative.
