# Explicit Narrative Interpretation in Judges 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative's own theological explanation, the rupture between Abimelech and Shechem is intended to return responsibility for the brothers' blood upon both the killer and those who aided him. The closing verses interpret both parties' destruction as divine retribution.

**Does not establish:** The narrator's attribution does not independently establish how divine agency operated historically or settle the ontology of the 'evil spirit.'

**Alternative explanations:**
- A historical reconstruction could explain the rupture through unstable alliances, competing elites, and retaliatory violence without accepting supernatural causation.
- The narrator may be theologically interpreting remembered political events rather than reporting an independently testable causal mechanism.
