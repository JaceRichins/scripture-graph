# Close Scriptural Reuse in Romans 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The instruction to feed and give drink to an enemy, followed by the image of heaping coals of fire on the enemy's head, closely follows Proverbs 25:21-22. Romans omits the concluding statement in Proverbs that the Lord will reward the benefactor.

**Does not establish:** The verbal connection does not by itself settle the meaning of the coals-of-fire metaphor or identify the precise textual form Paul used.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The instruction could have circulated as a familiar Jewish ethical maxim, although the shared sequence and distinctive coal imagery strongly favor dependence on Proverbs.

**Scripture:** [[Romans 12#^rom-12-20|Romans 12:20]], [[Proverbs 25#^prov-25-21|Proverbs 25:21]], [[Proverbs 25#^prov-25-22|Proverbs 25:22]]
