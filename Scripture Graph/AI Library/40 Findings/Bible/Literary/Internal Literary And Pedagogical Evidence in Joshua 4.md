# Internal Literary And Pedagogical Evidence in Joshua 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of a durable physical sign and a repeated question-and-answer script is strong internal evidence that Joshua 4 presents remembrance as an intentional form of communal and family teaching.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish how long the monument survived, how consistently later families used it, or whether the reported dialogue preserves the exact words historically spoken.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated explanation may primarily serve the narrative's readers rather than record a continuing educational practice.
- A monument can acquire meanings different from those assigned to it in its founding account.

**Scripture:** [[Joshua 4#^josh-4-6|Joshua 4:6-7]], [[Joshua 4#^josh-4-20|Joshua 4:20-23]]
