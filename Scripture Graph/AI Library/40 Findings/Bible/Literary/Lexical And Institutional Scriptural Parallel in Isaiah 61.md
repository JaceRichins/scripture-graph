# Lexical And Institutional Scriptural Parallel in Isaiah 61

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah's paired language of proclaiming liberty and an acceptable year has a substantial thematic and lexical connection with the Jubilee legislation, which commands liberty to be proclaimed in the fiftieth year and associates it with return and release. This background illuminates Isaiah's deliverance imagery without proving that the prophecy announces a literal calendrical Jubilee.

**Does not establish:** The verbal and thematic parallels do not establish that Isaiah 61 predicts a specific historical Jubilee year or mandates one economic implementation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah may employ general emancipation language without prescribing observance of the Jubilee calendar.
- The release language may metaphorically describe restoration from exile or oppression.
- The imagery may deliberately combine several deliverance traditions rather than depend exclusively on Leviticus 25.
