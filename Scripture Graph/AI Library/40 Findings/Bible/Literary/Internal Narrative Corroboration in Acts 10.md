# Internal Narrative Corroboration in Acts 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative's own evidentiary logic, the independently reported experiences of Cornelius and Peter present the Gentile encounter as divinely initiated rather than as Peter's unilateral innovation.

**Does not establish:** This narrative coordination does not by itself establish that the visions occurred historically or that they had a supernatural cause.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coordinated revelations may reflect deliberate literary or theological shaping by the author.
- Narrative agreement between characters is not independent external corroboration of the historical events.
