# Explicit Scriptural Appeal in Luke 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus explicitly appeals to Moses' bush narrative and the divine titles God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as evidence that the dead are raised. The cited background is Exodus 3:6.

**Does not establish:** This brief argument does not specify the timing, physical character, universality, or differing outcomes of resurrection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The argument may depend primarily on the continuing covenantal identity of the patriarchs rather than solely on the tense of a verb.
- Readers may disagree about whether the inference establishes future bodily resurrection by itself or presupposes a broader theology of resurrection.

**Scripture:** [[Luke 20#^luke-20-37|Luke 20:37]], [[Luke 20#^luke-20-38|Luke 20:38]], [[Exodus 3#^ex-3-6|Exodus 3:6]]
