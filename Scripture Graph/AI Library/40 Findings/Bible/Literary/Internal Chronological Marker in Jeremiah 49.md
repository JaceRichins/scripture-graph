# Internal Chronological Marker in Jeremiah 49

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The heading in verse 34 is strong evidence that the received text intentionally assigns the Elam oracle to the beginning of Zedekiah's reign. It is only limited evidence for the oracle's actual date of first composition because a chronological heading could have been transmitted or added during later compilation.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the date of first composition, Jeremiah's personal authorship of every word, fulfillment, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The heading may preserve an authentic contemporary date.
- A later compiler may have supplied the heading from received tradition or editorial chronology.
- The heading dates only the Elam oracle, not necessarily every oracle collected in Jeremiah 49.

**Scripture:** [[Jeremiah 49#^jer-49-34|Jeremiah 49:34]]
