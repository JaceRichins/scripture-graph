# Internal Thematic Evidence in Leviticus 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of agricultural generosity, honest wages, disability protections, impartial judgment, neighbor love, stranger care, and honest measurements within a chapter framed by holiness supplies strong textual support for reading social ethics as part of the chapter's conception of holiness.

**Does not establish:** This textual pattern does not by itself determine how each ancient command applies in later religious or civil settings.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The chapter may be a collection of distinct laws whose juxtaposition does not imply a fully systematic definition of holiness.
- Some commands may be linked primarily by covenant authority rather than by a single ethical concept.
