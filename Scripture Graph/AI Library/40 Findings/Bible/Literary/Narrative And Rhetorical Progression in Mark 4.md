# Narrative And Rhetorical Progression in Mark 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrative moves from a great storm to a great calm and then to the disciples' exceeding fear. This progression supports a Christological emphasis: the story's culminating question concerns Jesus' identity and authority, not merely survival at sea.

**Does not establish:** Literary emphasis alone does not establish the event's historicity or settle later creedal formulations about Christ.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated intensity may be conventional storytelling rather than a carefully designed verbal progression.
- The scene can emphasize prophetic miracle-working authority without independently defining a complete doctrine of Jesus' divine identity.
