# Inner-Biblical Interpretation in Genesis 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hosea 12:3-4 retells this episode and adds details absent from Genesis 32 itself — explicitly calling the opponent 'the angel' and stating that Jacob 'wept, and made supplication unto him' — showing an independent prophetic tradition elaborating on the Genesis account.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that 'weeping and supplication' occurred historically as described in Genesis 32; it shows only that a later prophetic text added this detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Hosea may be drawing on a variant oral tradition about Jacob rather than directly interpreting the Genesis 32 text as we have it.
