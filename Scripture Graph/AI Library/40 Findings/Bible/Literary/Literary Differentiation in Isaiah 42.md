# Literary Differentiation in Isaiah 42

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The effective servant of verses 1-7 and the blind, unresponsive servant of verses 19-20 are sharply different rhetorical profiles. This is strong internal evidence that readers should not assume without argument that every occurrence of 'servant' functions identically.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself determine whether the chapter refers to one servant, multiple servants, an individual, collective Israel, or a deliberately layered combination.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single collective servant, Israel, may be portrayed first according to its calling and later according to its actual failure.
- The same servant may be described paradoxically rather than two distinct referents being intended.
- The term may shift between an ideal individual agent and collective Israel.
