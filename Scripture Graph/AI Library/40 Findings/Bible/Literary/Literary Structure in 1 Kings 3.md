# Literary Structure in 1 Kings 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The received chapter forms a clear request-promise-demonstration sequence: Solomon requests judicial discernment, God grants it, and the following lawsuit publicly displays it. This strongly supports deliberate literary arrangement of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the events' historicity, supernatural causation, eyewitness status, or a particular date or stage of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have arranged previously independent dream and judgment traditions to create the sequence.
- The sequence may reflect conventional royal-wisdom storytelling rather than a single eyewitness composition.
