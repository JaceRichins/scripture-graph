# Narrative Command-Fulfillment Correspondence in Genesis 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed repetition of the commanded categories—Abraham, Ishmael, those born in the house, and those bought with money—in the execution account is strong textual evidence that the narrator emphasizes comprehensive obedience. The repeated phrase “in the selfsame day” additionally emphasizes promptness.

**Does not establish:** This literary correspondence does not independently establish the event's historicity or prove that Abraham's obedience was effortless.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may reflect conventional legal or narrative style rather than a special literary design.
- The account's emphasis on obedience does not determine Abraham's unreported emotions or motives.
