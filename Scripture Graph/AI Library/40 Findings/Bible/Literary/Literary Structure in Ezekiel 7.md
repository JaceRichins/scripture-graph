# Literary Structure in Ezekiel 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated end/day announcements and the parallel judgment-recognition units provide substantial internal evidence that the chapter is rhetorically patterned rather than a random catalogue of disasters.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish a single-stage composition, a precise outline, or the identity and date of every compositional layer.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions may arise partly from conventional prophetic formulas rather than a comprehensive literary design.
- Some patterning could reflect compilation or editorial arrangement of related oracle material.
