# Genealogical Identification in 1 Chronicles 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Uri's son Bezaleel, named in 1 Chronicles 2:20 as a great-grandson of Caleb through Hur, is generally identified with the Bezaleel of Exodus 31:2 and 35:30, the Spirit-filled craftsman who built the tabernacle, since both share the same name and the same father (Uri) and grandfather (Hur).

**Does not establish:** Does not establish any additional biographical facts about Bezaleel beyond the lineage stated in both texts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Bezaleel, Uri, and Hur could theoretically be a different family that happens to share these names.
- Chronicles genealogies sometimes telescope generations, so shared names across 'begat' links do not guarantee the identical individual is meant.
