# Literary Sequence And Explicit Contrast in Amos 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence from qualified destruction, through the sieve image, to the death of the sinners supplies strong internal evidence that Amos distinguishes preservation of the house of Jacob from preservation of every individual Israelite.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish which individuals survive, that survivors are sinless, or the chronology and historical mechanism of the promised preservation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The precise mechanics of the sieve metaphor are ambiguous and should not be pressed into a detailed theory of individual selection.
- The house-level promise may concern corporate survival rather than the temporal protection of particular faithful individuals.
