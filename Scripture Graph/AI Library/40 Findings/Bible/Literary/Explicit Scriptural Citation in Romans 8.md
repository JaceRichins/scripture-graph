# Explicit Scriptural Citation in Romans 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 8:36 explicitly introduces an adapted citation of Psalms 44:22 with "As it is written." This is strong evidence that Israel's scripture supplies language through which the passage interprets faithful suffering.

**Does not establish:** The citation does not establish that every statement or historical circumstance in Psalms 44 directly predicts the Christian experience described in Romans 8.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be citing a familiar Jewish scriptural form rather than independently interpreting every feature of the psalm.
- The citation can function rhetorically without transferring the entire original context of Psalms 44 into Romans 8.

**Scripture:** [[Romans 8#^rom-8-36|Romans 8:36]], [[Psalm 44#^ps-44-22|Psalm 44:22]]
