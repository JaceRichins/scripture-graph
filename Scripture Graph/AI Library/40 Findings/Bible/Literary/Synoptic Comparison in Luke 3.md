# Synoptic Comparison in Luke 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Between David and Joseph, Luke's line (through Nathan) shares almost no names with Matthew's line (through Solomon), apart from Zorobabel and Salathiel appearing in both. This is a long-recognized synoptic discrepancy without a scholarly-consensus resolution.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which harmonization, if any, is correct, or that either genealogy is a precise biological record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Luke records Mary's ancestry despite naming Joseph (a traditional harmonization with no direct textual support in either Gospel).
- A levirate-marriage scenario gave one man two legal fathers (an explanation attributed to Julius Africanus).
- One or both lists may be constructed with theological/symbolic aims rather than as strict biological records.
