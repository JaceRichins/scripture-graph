# Explicit Composite Scriptural Citation in Luke 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus interprets his temple action by combining the prophetic descriptions of God's house as a house of prayer and of a corrupted sanctuary as a den of thieves. These correspond to Isaiah 56:7 and Jeremiah 7:11 and frame the action as prophetic criticism of temple practice.

**Does not establish:** The citation alone does not establish that Jesus rejected the temple itself, all commerce associated with pilgrimage, or the legitimacy of every temple institution.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The compressed wording may represent a remembered prophetic formulation rather than a formal literary quotation procedure.
- The text does not specify every commercial abuse or identify every individual trader as personally fraudulent.
