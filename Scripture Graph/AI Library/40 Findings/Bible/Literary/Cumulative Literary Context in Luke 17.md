# Cumulative Literary Context in Luke 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The flood, the destruction of Sodom, urgent flight, paired persons being separated, and the body-and-eagles saying collectively support a sudden-judgment reading of Luke 17:26-37. They do not unambiguously identify whether the person “taken” or the person “left” is delivered.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish a detailed end-times sequence or settle the fate of each member of the taken-and-left pairs.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some interpretations identify those taken as the saved and connect the passage with a rapture.
- Because Noah and Lot escape while others are destroyed, other interpretations identify those taken as those removed in judgment.
- The paired-person sayings may chiefly emphasize unavoidable separation rather than specify either group's destination.
