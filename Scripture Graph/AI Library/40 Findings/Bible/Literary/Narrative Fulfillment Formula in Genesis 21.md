# Narrative Fulfillment Formula in Genesis 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentrated fulfillment language is strong internal evidence that the narrative presents Isaac's birth as the deliberate completion of God's promise rather than as an unrelated family event.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historicity or miraculous character of the birth.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions may partly reflect conventional biblical fulfillment language or editorial shaping.
