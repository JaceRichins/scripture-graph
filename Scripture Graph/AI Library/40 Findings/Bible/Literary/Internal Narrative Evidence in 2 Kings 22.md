# Internal Narrative Evidence in 2 Kings 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit because-and-therefore reasoning in Huldah's oracle is strong internal evidence for the narrator's theological claim that God hears humble responses to warning. Josiah's response changes his personal experience of the announced disaster, but the communal judgment itself remains in force.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical circumstances of the oracle or a universal rule that repentance always removes temporal consequences.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The episode may be a historian's theological shaping of remembered events rather than a transcript permitting independent verification.
- Josiah's reprieve can be read as delayed judgment rather than cancellation or comprehensive forgiveness.
