# Verbal Parallel in Proverbs 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Proverbs 24:33–34 repeats the two-verse warning found at Proverbs 6:10–11 in the canonical wording. This is strong evidence of literary reuse or a shared stock saying within Proverbs, but the repetition alone cannot determine direction of borrowing, date, or individual authorship.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish the date, identity, or historical setting of either passage's original author.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently preserve an already traditional proverb.
- One passage may reuse the other, but the verbal parallel does not reveal which form came first.
- A compiler or editor may have placed the same saying in both contexts.
