# Literary-Spatial Pattern in Exodus 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's successive boundaries—people below, elders ascending, Moses drawing nearer, and Moses entering the cloud—strongly support a reading centered on mediated and graded access to divine presence.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the mountain scene was intended as a complete blueprint for later Israelite or modern temple worship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The distinctions may primarily express narrative hierarchy and leadership rather than a formal sacred-space pattern.
- Later temple interpretations may organize these details more systematically than the chapter itself does.
