# Literary-Rhetorical Structure in Deuteronomy 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from denial of Israelite merit to remembered instances of rebellion is strong evidence that the historical review has been arranged to support the chapter's anti-boasting argument.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the date, individual authorship, compositional history, or historical accuracy of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence could partly reflect inherited chronological tradition rather than deliberate rhetorical arrangement.
- The anti-merit theme may arise from editorial framing applied to older narrative materials.
