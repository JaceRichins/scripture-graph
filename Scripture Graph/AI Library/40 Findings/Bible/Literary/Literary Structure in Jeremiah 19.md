# Literary Structure in Jeremiah 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of acquiring, displaying, breaking, and interpreting the bottle provides strong evidence for deliberate rhetorical organization of the chapter around a prophetic sign-act.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not by itself prove when the episode was composed, whether every detail is a contemporaneous record, or whether the prediction was fulfilled exactly as described.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The organization could reflect later editorial shaping of a shorter sign-act tradition.
- The conventional nature of prophetic sign-acts may account for some of the formal structure.
