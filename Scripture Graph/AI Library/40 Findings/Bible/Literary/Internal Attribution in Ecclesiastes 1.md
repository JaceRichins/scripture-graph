# Internal Attribution in Ecclesiastes 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The royal and Davidic self-description is strong internal evidence that the work presents a Solomonic persona, but it is only limited evidence that Solomon personally composed the book in its final form.

**Does not establish:** These internal descriptions do not by themselves establish the historical identity of the author, the date of composition, or the history of the book's editing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later author may have adopted a Solomonic literary persona.
- “Son of David” may designate Davidic descent or royal identity without uniquely naming Solomon.
- The first-person royal material may be a literary framework rather than a direct autobiographical report.

**Scripture:** [[Ecclesiastes 1#^eccl-1-1|Ecclesiastes 1:1]], [[Ecclesiastes 1#^eccl-1-12|Ecclesiastes 1:12]], [[Ecclesiastes 1#^eccl-1-16|Ecclesiastes 1:16]]
