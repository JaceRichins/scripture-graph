# Internal Historical Memory in Zechariah 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Zechariah invokes an earthquake in Uzziah's reign as a remembered analogy for future flight, while Amos also locates his prophetic activity relative to an earthquake. Together the passages show that this earthquake functioned as a durable historical memory in biblical tradition.

**Does not establish:** These references alone do not establish the earthquake's exact date, magnitude, affected area, or the date of Zechariah 14.

**Alternative explanations:**
- One biblical author or textual tradition may depend on the other rather than preserve an independent recollection.
- A remembered disaster can acquire conventional or symbolic force without preserving precise historical details.
