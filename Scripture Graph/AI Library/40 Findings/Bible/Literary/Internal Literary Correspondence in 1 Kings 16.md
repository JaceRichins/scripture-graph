# Internal Literary Correspondence in 1 Kings 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrative explicitly presents Zimri's destruction of Baasha's house as corresponding to the earlier word delivered by Jehu. This is strong evidence for a deliberate prophecy-and-fulfillment pattern within the received narrative, but it is not by itself proof that the prediction was recorded before the event.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish when the oracle was composed, supernatural prediction, or the historical accuracy of every reported detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later narrator or editor may have shaped the oracle and fulfillment account together.
- The correspondence may express a theological interpretation of known dynastic events rather than preserve a demonstrably prior prediction.
