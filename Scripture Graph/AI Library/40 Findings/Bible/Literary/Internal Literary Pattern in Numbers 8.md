# Internal Literary Pattern in Numbers 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated command-and-performance notices provide strong internal evidence that obedience to revealed instructions is a deliberate emphasis of the chapter. They offer only weak evidence concerning the date, compositional history, or historicity of the account.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish Mosaic authorship, a particular composition date, or the historical performance of the rites.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be conventional legal or priestly narrative style rather than a distinctive theological design.
- An editor could have strengthened the command-performance correspondence during transmission or composition.
