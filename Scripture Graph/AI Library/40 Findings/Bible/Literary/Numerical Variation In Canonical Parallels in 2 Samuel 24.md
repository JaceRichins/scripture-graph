# Numerical Variation In Canonical Parallels in 2 Samuel 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The parallel accounts report different census totals: 2 Samuel gives 800,000 for Israel and 500,000 for Judah, while 1 Chronicles gives 1,100,000 for Israel and 470,000 for Judah.

**Does not establish:** The differing totals by themselves do not identify which explanation is correct or prove that either surviving figure is the original one.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The writers may use different military or administrative categories.
- One total may include groups omitted from the other accounting.
- The figures may reflect variant source or manuscript traditions.
- One or more numbers may have changed during textual transmission.

**Scripture:** [[2 Samuel 24#^2sam-24-9|2 Samuel 24:9]], [[1 Chronicles 21#^1chr-21-5|1 Chronicles 21:5]]
