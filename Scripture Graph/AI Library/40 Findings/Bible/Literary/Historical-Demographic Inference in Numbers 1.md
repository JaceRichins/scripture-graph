# Historical-Demographic Inference in Numbers 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The stated total strongly establishes that the received narrative portrays Israel as possessing a very large mobilizable force, but the figure by itself is weak evidence for reconstructing the historical population of the wilderness generation.

**Does not establish:** The total alone does not establish the historical size of Israel, the total population including uncounted groups, or the logistical feasibility of the wilderness account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The number may be intended as a literal military census.
- The figures may have been idealized, schematized, or shaped for literary and theological purposes.
- Ancient numerical terminology or the transmission of figures may complicate modern demographic reconstruction.

**Scripture:** [[Numbers 1#^num-1-45|Numbers 1:45]], [[Numbers 1#^num-1-46|Numbers 1:46]]
