# Literary-Theological in Jeremiah 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 23:5-6 is strong internal evidence that the passage anticipates an ideal future Davidic ruler and thus supports a broadly messianic reading. By itself, however, it provides only limited evidence for identifying that ruler specifically with Jesus rather than with another conception of restored Davidic kingship.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish the ruler's identity, historical date of fulfillment, or an exclusively Christian interpretation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may envision a future historical Davidic monarch without identifying him with Jesus.
- The Branch may function as an idealized representation of righteous Davidic kingship.
- Later Jewish and Christian communities may legitimately receive the oracle differently while agreeing on its Davidic royal language.
