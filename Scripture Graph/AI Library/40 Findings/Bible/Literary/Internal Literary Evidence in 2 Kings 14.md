# Internal Literary Evidence in 2 Kings 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit appeal to the law of Moses is strong internal evidence that the narrator evaluates Amaziah's exercise of royal justice against a recognized scriptural legal norm.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish when the passage was composed, when Deuteronomy reached its final form, or whether every reported detail is historically recoverable.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The legal citation could have been supplied by a later editor rather than belonging to an earlier account of Amaziah.
- The rule may also reflect a wider principle of individual liability rather than dependence on one uniquely identifiable written manuscript.
