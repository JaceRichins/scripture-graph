# Internal Narrative Qualification in Joshua 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coexistence of rest, past victories, remaining nations, and promised future expulsion is strong internal evidence that Joshua 23 presents conquest and possession as substantial but not uniformly complete.

**Does not establish:** This internal qualification does not establish the historical scale or chronology of the conquest and does not resolve archaeological debates about Israel's emergence in Canaan.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language of rest may refer to regional security rather than total territorial occupation.
- The text may distinguish nations already defeated from surviving enclaves still requiring removal.
- Different conquest formulations may preserve distinct literary perspectives later placed together.
