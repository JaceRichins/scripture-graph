# Historical Correlation in Jeremiah 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The fates assigned to Shallum and Coniah correspond in outline with the royal removals and foreign captivity narrated in Kings. This is strong evidence that the chapter is anchored in identifiable events of Judah's late monarchy.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish when each oracle was first delivered, predictive foreknowledge, independent historical corroboration, or the precise editorial history of the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracles or their present wording could have been preserved or edited after the described outcomes were known.
- The biblical narratives are related literary witnesses and therefore do not constitute fully independent corroboration.
- Identifying Shallum with the Jehoahaz of 2 Kings depends on reconciling differing royal names.
