# Narrative Emphasis in 1 Samuel 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated placement of the ark's capture at decisive moments provides strong literary evidence that the narrative presents it as the chapter's central calamity: it concludes the battle report, triggers Eli's fatal fall, and supplies the stated reason for the name I-chabod.

**Does not establish:** The literary emphasis does not establish that God was captured, defeated, or literally absent from Israel.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may arise from the reporting sequence and conventional narrative recapitulation.
- The deaths of Eli's family and Israel's casualties remain major calamities even though the ark receives special emphasis.
