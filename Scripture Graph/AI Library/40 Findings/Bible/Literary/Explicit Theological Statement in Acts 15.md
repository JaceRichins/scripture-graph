# Explicit Theological Statement in Acts 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Acts 15, the strongest explicit theological answer to the claim that circumcision is necessary for salvation is Peter's assertion that both Jewish and Gentile believers are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ.

**Does not establish:** This statement does not establish that Acts rejects obedience, moral requirements, or every form of Jewish Torah observance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Peter may be rejecting the law as a condition of Gentile salvation without addressing every possible role of Jewish law observance among Jewish believers.
- The speech is Luke's narrative presentation and cannot by itself establish Peter's exact historical wording.
