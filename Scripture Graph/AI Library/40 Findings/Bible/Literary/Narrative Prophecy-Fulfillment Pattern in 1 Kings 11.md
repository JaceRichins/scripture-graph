# Narrative Prophecy-Fulfillment Pattern in 1 Kings 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The correspondence between Ahijah's symbolic prophecy and the division narrated in 1 Kings 12 provides strong evidence for purposeful prophecy-fulfillment structure within Kings.

**Does not establish:** Narrative correspondence alone does not independently establish that the prediction was recorded before the events or that supernatural foreknowledge is the only explanation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prophecy may have been formulated or edited after the kingdom divided.
- A preexisting prophetic tradition may have been adapted to the later narrative.
