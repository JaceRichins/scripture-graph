# Literary Genre in Psalm 44

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from remembered deliverance, through communal defeat and protestation of fidelity, to direct petition strongly supports reading Psalm 44 as a communal lament.

**Does not establish:** The literary form does not identify the original historical crisis, author, date, temple setting, or precise circumstances of performance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single speaker could be voicing personal suffering through representative communal language.
- The present sequence could reflect editorial arrangement of materials that did not originate in one performance setting.
