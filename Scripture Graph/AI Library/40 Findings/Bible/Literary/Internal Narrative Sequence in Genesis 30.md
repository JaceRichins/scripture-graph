# Internal Narrative Sequence in Genesis 30

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence gives internal literary support to a theological reading in which fertility ultimately depends on God rather than on the mandrakes: Leah conceives because God hearkens to her, while Rachel receives the mandrakes but does not conceive until God remembers and opens her womb.

**Does not establish:** The sequence does not establish a medical conclusion about mandrakes, prove that the timing was miraculous, or exclude ordinary biological processes.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may simply record successive events without deliberately contrasting mandrakes and divine action.
- Ancient hearers may have regarded mandrakes as one ordinary means operating alongside divine agency rather than as a rival explanation.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 30#^gen-30-14|Genesis 30:14-17]], [[Genesis 30#^gen-30-22|Genesis 30:22-23]]
