# Internal Narrative Fulfillment in 1 Samuel 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The oracle's prediction that Hophni and Phinehas would 'die both of them' 'in one day' (2:34) is presented within the book as fulfilled at 1 Samuel 4:11, giving this chapter's prophecy an explicit, checkable fulfillment later in the same narrative.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish the historicity of the oracle as actually spoken before the events; it only shows the book's own narrative presents the prediction as fulfilled.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment could reflect deliberate narrative composition after the events rather than a prediction genuinely spoken beforehand.
- The 'sign' of same-day death may function as a literary marker of certainty rather than a claim about detailed foreknowledge.
