# Explicit Dominical Saying Within Narrative in Luke 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Luke 23:43 strongly supports the claim that Jesus promised postmortem fellowship to the criminal, but the single saying does not by itself define the location, duration, or complete doctrinal relationship of paradise to resurrection and final judgment.

**Does not establish:** This verse alone does not supply a complete map of the spirit world, settle the timing of final resurrection, or resolve every question about judgment and ordinances.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Christian traditions differ over whether paradise here denotes heaven, an intermediate state, or the blessed region of the dead.
- Interpretations may differ over the punctuation and temporal force of 'To day,' since the earliest manuscripts did not use modern punctuation.
