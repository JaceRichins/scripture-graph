# Internal Literary Pattern in Genesis 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The orderly reduction from fifty to ten, together with recurring formulas of request and response, is strong internal evidence that the intercession scene has been deliberately shaped as a sustained literary dialogue.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish whether the dialogue is a verbatim historical transcript, how it was transmitted, or what compositional history produced its present form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may preserve features of an earlier oral exchange rather than result from written composition alone.
- A historical conversation could have been orderly; literary shaping does not imply that the scene is unhistorical.
