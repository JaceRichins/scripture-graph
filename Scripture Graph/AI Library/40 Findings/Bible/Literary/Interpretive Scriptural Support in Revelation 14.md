# Interpretive Scriptural Support in Revelation 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Latter-day Saint interpretation, the angel bearing the everlasting gospel provides scriptural support for associating latter-day gospel proclamation with angelic ministry. The verse itself, however, neither identifies a particular angel nor names a specific historical restoration event.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the identity of the angel, the date of fulfillment, or the exclusive truth of one historical application.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The angel may symbolize divine authorization for the church's proclamation rather than one identifiable heavenly visitation.
- The vision may describe a future angelic announcement immediately preceding final judgment.
- The scene may function primarily as apocalyptic drama without mapping onto one modern historical event.

**Scripture:** [[Revelation 14#^rev-14-6|Revelation 14:6-7]], [[D&C 133#^dc-133-36|D&C 133:36-40]]
