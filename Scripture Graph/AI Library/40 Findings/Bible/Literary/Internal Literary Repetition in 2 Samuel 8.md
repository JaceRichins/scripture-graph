# Internal Literary Repetition in 2 Samuel 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The identical statement that the LORD preserved David appears after the Damascus and Edom accounts. This repetition strongly supports the literary conclusion that the narrator frames David's success as involving divine preservation.

**Does not establish:** The refrain does not by itself establish that God commanded or morally approved every action reported in the campaigns, nor does it independently verify the casualty totals.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated wording may be a conventional royal-theological formula used in summarizing military success.
- The formula may reflect an editor's theological framing rather than wording originating in reports of the individual campaigns.

**Scripture:** [[2 Samuel 8#^2sam-8-6|2 Samuel 8:6]], [[2 Samuel 8#^2sam-8-14|2 Samuel 8:14]]
