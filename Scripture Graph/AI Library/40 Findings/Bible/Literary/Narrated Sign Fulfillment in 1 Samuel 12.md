# Narrated Sign Fulfillment in 1 Samuel 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Samuel announces that he will call for thunder and rain, calls upon the Lord, and receives the announced sign that same day. This sequence functions as validation of his warning for the assembled people.

**Does not establish:** The narrative correspondence alone does not independently prove that the reported weather event occurred or that supernatural causation is the only explanation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A historical thunderstorm could have occurred naturally and then been interpreted providentially.
- The episode may have been shaped as a literary sign validating Samuel.
- A remembered event may have acquired theological emphasis during transmission.
