# Scriptural Intertext in Matthew 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 11:10 explicitly introduces written scripture and applies language closely corresponding to Malachi 3:1 to John. This is strong evidence that the passage presents John through Malachi's messenger motif.

**Does not establish:** The intertext alone does not establish the date or authorship of Matthew, nor does it independently prove that Malachi's original historical referent was exclusively John.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew and Malachi could reflect a shared scriptural or traditional formulation, although Matthew's introductory formula and close wording strongly favor deliberate citation.
- The wording in Matthew is adapted rather than reproduced identically, so conclusions about a particular textual recension require evidence beyond this comparison.

**Scripture:** [[Matthew 11#^matt-11-10|Matthew 11:10]], [[Malachi 3#^mal-3-1|Malachi 3:1]]
