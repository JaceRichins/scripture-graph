# Literary Structure in 2 Chronicles 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The tightly repeated strength vocabulary at the transition from success to trespass is strong internal evidence that the chapter's reversal is intentionally framed rather than being a merely accidental sequence of notices.

**Does not establish:** Literary design does not by itself establish the historical accuracy of the events, the date of composition, or the identity of the writer.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may arise from ordinary thematic vocabulary rather than a formally planned structure.
- The final literary arrangement may reflect an editor's shaping of inherited material.
