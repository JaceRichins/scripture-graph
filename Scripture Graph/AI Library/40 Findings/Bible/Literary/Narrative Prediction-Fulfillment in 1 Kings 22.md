# Narrative Prediction-Fulfillment in 1 Kings 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Ahab's failure to return in peace and the dispersal of his army confirm Micaiah's publicly stated test and adverse prediction while contradicting the promised victory of the other prophets.

**Does not establish:** This internal fulfillment does not by itself establish the account's historical independence, the precise mechanism of inspiration, or the metaphysical details of the heavenly vision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may have been shaped retrospectively so that the prediction and outcome correspond.
- A literary narrative can establish which prophet the narrator endorses without independently verifying every reported historical detail.
