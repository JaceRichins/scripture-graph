# Internal Literary And Theological Evidence in 1 Kings 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of obedience leading to dynastic stability and apostasy leading to expulsion and temple disgrace strongly supports reading the oracle as a covenantal warning rather than as an unqualified guarantee of political and institutional permanence.

**Does not establish:** This internal pattern does not by itself resolve the historical fulfillment, compositional date, authorship, or every possible meaning of the phrase 'for ever.'

**Alternative explanations:**
- The dynastic promise and the threatened national judgment may have distinct scopes, permitting continuation of a Davidic claim despite political catastrophe.
- The language of permanence may be theological or idealized rather than a prediction of uninterrupted institutional continuity.
