# Canonical Reception And Typology in Numbers 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit reuse of this episode in John and the Book of Mormon is strong evidence for its importance in later canonical interpretation as a pattern of salvation through responding to a divinely provided means.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the episode's historicity, the survival of the original object, or an explicitly christological intention in the earliest form of the narrative.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later writers may be applying a retrospective typology rather than recovering the original narrator's conscious intent.
- The theological meaning supplied by later texts is more explicit than the explanation given within Numbers itself.
