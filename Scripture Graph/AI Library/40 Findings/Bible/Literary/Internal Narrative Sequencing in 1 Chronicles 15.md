# Internal Narrative Sequencing in 1 Chronicles 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence from David's diagnosis of the earlier breach, through conformity to the prescribed carrying method, to divine help provides strong internal evidence that the narrator associates the successful procession with corrected observance.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a universal rule that every adverse event results from procedural disobedience, nor does narrative sequence independently prove historical causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The successful outcome may be attributed primarily to God's help rather than to ritual conformity considered by itself.
- The sequence expresses the narrator's theological interpretation and is not independent verification of the event's causal mechanism.
