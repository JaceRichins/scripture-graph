# Internal Prediction-Fulfillment in 2 Kings 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Elisha's announcement that Hazael will become king is followed immediately by Ben-hadad's death and Hazael's accession. This is strong evidence for a deliberate prediction-fulfillment pattern in the account.

**Does not establish:** The internal pattern does not independently establish the prediction's date, the account's historicity, or divine foreknowledge outside the narrative.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may have been composed or edited after Hazael's accession.
- The close placement may reflect literary arrangement rather than the original timing of the prediction.
- Accurate political expectation would not by itself demonstrate supernatural revelation.

**Scripture:** [[2 Kings 8#^2kgs-8-13|2 Kings 8:13]], [[2 Kings 8#^2kgs-8-15|2 Kings 8:15]]
