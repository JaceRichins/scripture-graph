# Literary-Pattern in 1 Peter 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated contrasts between what perishes and what endures create a coherent chapter-wide motif: an incorruptible inheritance, perishing gold, corruptible silver and gold, incorruptible seed, withering flesh, and the enduring word.

**Does not establish:** The motif supports thematic coherence but does not by itself establish authorship, date, or a rigid structural outline.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may arise naturally from closely related theological subjects rather than from a formally planned literary structure.
- English translation can make lexical relationships appear more or less exact than they are in the underlying Greek.
