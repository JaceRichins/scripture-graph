# Literary And Thematic Correspondence in Daniel 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The parallel grants of everlasting dominion to the one like the Son of man and to the saints strongly support a deliberate relationship between that figure and the holy people, but the text does not explicitly define the relationship.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not prove that the figure is exclusively corporate, exclusively individual, or explicitly identified as Jesus within Daniel 7 itself.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Son-of-man figure may represent the saints corporately.
- The figure may be an individual representative or ruler who shares his kingdom with the saints.
- The vision and interpretation may intentionally preserve both individual and corporate dimensions.
