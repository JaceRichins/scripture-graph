# Literary Structure in Romans 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of question, rejection, and "Know ye not" language supports reading Romans 6 as a deliberate two-panel argument: verses 1–14 develop death-and-resurrection participation, while verses 15–23 develop the analogy of competing forms of service.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallels do not establish a rigid formal structure or require every verse to have a matching counterpart.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated formulas may reflect Paul's ordinary debating style rather than a carefully balanced literary design.
- Other outlines may subdivide verses 1–14 or 15–23 differently while retaining the same general progression.
