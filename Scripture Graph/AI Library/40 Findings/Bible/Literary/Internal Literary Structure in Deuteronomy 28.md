# Internal Literary Structure in Deuteronomy 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated verbal and conceptual reversals between blessings and curses are strong internal evidence that the chapter's two halves were deliberately coordinated as covenant sanctions.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish a particular author, date, compositional history, or historical fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coordination may reflect conventional covenant or treaty formulas rather than a distinctive authorial design.
- An editor could have arranged or expanded inherited blessing and curse materials into their present correspondence.
- Some parallels arise naturally because blessings and curses are semantic opposites.
