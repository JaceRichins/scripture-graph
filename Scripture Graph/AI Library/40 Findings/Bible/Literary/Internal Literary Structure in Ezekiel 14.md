# Internal Literary Structure in Ezekiel 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The four parallel hypothetical cases followed by the words "How much more" provide strong internal evidence that verses 13–21 form a deliberate rhetorical intensification from a sinning land in general to Jerusalem in particular.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the passage's date, compositional history, source unity, or divine authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The pattern could reflect conventional prophetic judgment formulas rather than a uniquely composed literary design.
- A later editor could have arranged inherited judgment sayings into the present sequence.
