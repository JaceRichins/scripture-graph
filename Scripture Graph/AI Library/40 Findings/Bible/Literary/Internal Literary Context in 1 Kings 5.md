# Internal Literary Context in 1 Kings 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter, the statement that the LORD gave Solomon wisdom is placed beside peace and a league with Hiram, providing internal textual support for reading Solomon's wisdom here as having diplomatic and administrative expression.

**Does not establish:** The juxtaposition does not establish that every policy or labor practice described in the chapter was wise or divinely approved.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verse may simply collect separate blessings without asserting that the alliance resulted from Solomon's wisdom.
- The wisdom statement may primarily recall an earlier divine promise rather than interpret the commercial agreement.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 5#^1kgs-5-12|1 Kings 5:12]]
