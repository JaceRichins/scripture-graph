# Literary Parallel in Jeremiah 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared combination of a Davidic righteous Branch, judgment or justice, salvation, and the title 'The LORD our righteousness' is strong evidence of a literary or traditional relationship between Jeremiah 33:14-16 and Jeremiah 23:5-6. A notable variation is that Jeremiah 23 applies the title to the royal figure, whereas the wording of Jeremiah 33:16 applies it to Jerusalem.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish common authorship, the date of either passage, an exclusive identification of the Branch, or the fulfillment of the prophecy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently use a shared stock of royal and restoration language.
- One passage may adapt the other, but the parallel by itself does not establish the direction of dependence.
- An editor may have coordinated previously separate traditions.
