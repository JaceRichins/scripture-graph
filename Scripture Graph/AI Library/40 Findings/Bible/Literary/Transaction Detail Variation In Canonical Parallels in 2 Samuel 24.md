# Transaction Detail Variation In Canonical Parallels in 2 Samuel 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The accounts also give different descriptions of David's payment: 2 Samuel reports fifty shekels of silver for the threshingfloor and oxen, while 1 Chronicles reports six hundred shekels of gold for the place.

**Does not establish:** The difference does not by itself prove two separate purchases, nor does it establish that the figures are impossible to reconcile.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The smaller payment may cover the threshingfloor and oxen, while the larger payment concerns a broader property called the place.
- Chronicles may preserve or construct a different transaction tradition.
- A number, metal, or transaction detail may have changed during transmission.

**Scripture:** [[2 Samuel 24#^2sam-24-24|2 Samuel 24:24]], [[1 Chronicles 21#^1chr-21-25|1 Chronicles 21:25]]
