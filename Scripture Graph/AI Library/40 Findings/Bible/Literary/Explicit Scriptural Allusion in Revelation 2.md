# Explicit Scriptural Allusion in Revelation 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The promise of authority over the nations, rule with a rod of iron, and broken pottery unmistakably reuses the royal language of Psalm 2:8-9. Revelation extends this messianic authority to the faithful conqueror while grounding it in authority Christ received from his Father.

**Does not establish:** The allusion does not establish a detailed political timetable or specify precisely how the promised rule will be exercised.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may reach Revelation through an established early Christian messianic tradition rather than a fresh direct reading of Psalm 2.
