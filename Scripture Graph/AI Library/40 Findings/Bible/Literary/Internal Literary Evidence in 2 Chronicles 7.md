# Internal Literary Evidence in 2 Chronicles 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The juxtaposition of the temple's perpetual consecration language with the warning that God may reject the house after apostasy is strong internal evidence that verse 16 does not guarantee the physical building's survival regardless of covenant conduct.

**Does not establish:** It does not settle every theological question about divine promises, later temples, or the continuing scope of the Davidic covenant.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The perpetual language may describe God's enduring covenant purpose or the temple's ideal status rather than the uninterrupted survival of one building.
- Some readers may distinguish the continuing significance of God's name from the conditional fate of the physical structure.
