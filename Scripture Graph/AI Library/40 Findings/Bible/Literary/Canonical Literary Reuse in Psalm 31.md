# Canonical Literary Reuse in Psalm 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The reuse of Psalm 31:5 in Luke's crucifixion narrative is strong evidence that this verse was given christological significance in early Christian scripture. It is not, by itself, proof that the original psalmist consciously predicted the crucifixion or that every part of Psalm 31 speaks directly as the Messiah.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the psalm's original historical setting, conscious predictive intent by its first author, or a direct messianic referent for every verse.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jesus may be applying an established prayer of trust to his own death without implying that the entire psalm is predictive.
- Luke's presentation may emphasize scriptural pattern and theological interpretation rather than a one-to-one prediction-and-fulfillment relationship.
