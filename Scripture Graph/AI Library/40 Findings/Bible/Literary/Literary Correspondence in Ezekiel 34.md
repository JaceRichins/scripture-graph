# Literary Correspondence in Ezekiel 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The actions God promises in verse 16 deliberately answer the leaders' failures in verse 4: seeking the lost, restoring the driven away, binding the broken, and strengthening the sick. This close lexical and conceptual reversal is strong evidence for purposeful literary organization.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the chapter's date, authorship, compositional unity, or fulfillment in any particular historical figure.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence could arise from ordinary thematic repetition rather than from a formally designed structure.
- Some repetition may reflect conventional shepherd vocabulary.
