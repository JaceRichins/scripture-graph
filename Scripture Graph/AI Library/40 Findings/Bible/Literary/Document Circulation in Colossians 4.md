# Document Circulation in Colossians 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The instruction to exchange and read letters between the recipients and the Laodicean church is strong textual evidence that these writings were intended for communal reading and regional sharing, not merely private possession.

**Does not establish:** It does not identify the epistle from Laodicea, prove that it is lost, or establish that either letter was already regarded as scripture.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The instruction depicts the practice expected by the letter's final author or editor but does not independently prove how consistently the exchange occurred.
- Regional exchange does not necessarily imply universal circulation or canonical status.

**Scripture:** [[Colossians 4#^col-4-16|Colossians 4:16]]
