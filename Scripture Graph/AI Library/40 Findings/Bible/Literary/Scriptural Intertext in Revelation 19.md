# Scriptural Intertext in Revelation 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The rod-of-iron language strongly supports deliberate reuse of Psalm 2's royal and judicial imagery, although Revelation's other repetitions of the same language may mediate the immediate connection.

**Does not establish:** The verbal relationship does not by itself determine the chronology of the depicted events or require a literal iron instrument.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The immediate source may be Revelation's own earlier uses of Psalm 2 rather than a fresh direct citation.
- The phrase may have become conventional messianic language within early Christian interpretation.
