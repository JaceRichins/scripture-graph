# Reception And Communal Memory in Psalm 105

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The psalm is strong evidence that the patriarchal, Joseph, exodus, wilderness, and land traditions functioned together as a covenant-centered account of Israel's identity in the community that used this hymn. As retrospective poetry, it is not independent contemporary documentation of every narrated event.

**Does not establish:** The hymn alone does not establish the dates, precise sequence, or independent historicity of all the events it celebrates.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The poet may have assembled the recital directly from already authoritative written traditions.
- The sequence may reflect a common liturgical summary rather than an independent historical tradition.
- Its omissions and rearrangements may be governed primarily by poetic and theological aims.

**Scripture:** [[Psalm 105#^ps-105-8|Psalm 105:8-45]]
