# Genre And Documentary Features in Ezra 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrent formulas, numerous personal and place names, occupational categories, subgroup figures, genealogy registers, aggregate totals, animal counts, and contribution amounts support the interpretation that the chapter preserves or imitates administrative and archival material.

**Does not establish:** The documentary appearance does not establish contemporaneous composition, complete preservation, or numerical precision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later author could have reshaped an older list for literary or theological purposes.
- Administrative-looking detail can be reproduced or stylized without preserving a fully contemporaneous document.
- Several records from different occasions may have been combined.

**Scripture:** [[Ezra 2#^ezra-2-2|Ezra 2:2-42]], [[Ezra 2#^ezra-2-58|Ezra 2:58-69]]
