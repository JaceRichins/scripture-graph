# Internal Narrative Correspondence in Genesis 41

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The correspondence between Joseph's forecast and the chapter's subsequent fulfillment report is strong internal evidence that the narrative presents Joseph as a reliable recipient and interpreter of revelation. Because both prediction and fulfillment are reported by the same narrative, the correspondence is not independent historical corroboration.

**Does not establish:** The internal correspondence alone does not establish the account's historical date, independent historicity, or supernatural causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A narrator could shape an account retrospectively so that an earlier prediction corresponds to later events.
- The correspondence may serve a literary or theological purpose without preserving independently verifiable administrative history.
- Later composition or redaction could have arranged the prediction-and-fulfillment pattern.
