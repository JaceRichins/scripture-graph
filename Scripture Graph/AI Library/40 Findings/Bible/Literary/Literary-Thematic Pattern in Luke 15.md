# Literary-Thematic Pattern in Luke 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated movement from loss to finding and from finding to communal joy is strong internal evidence that the three stories form a coordinated response to the opening complaint about receiving sinners.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish the precise compositional history, the exact historical wording of the discourse, or a one-to-one allegorical identity for every character and object.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coordination could reflect Luke's editorial arrangement of originally separate traditional materials.
- Shared conventional motifs could account for some similarity without requiring every narrative detail to correspond allegorically.
