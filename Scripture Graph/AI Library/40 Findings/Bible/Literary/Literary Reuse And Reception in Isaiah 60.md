# Literary Reuse And Reception in Isaiah 60

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combined motifs of divine light replacing sun and moon, nations and kings bringing their glory, and gates never closing provide strong evidence that Revelation 21 draws substantially upon Isaiah 60 in its portrayal of the New Jerusalem.

**Does not establish:** This literary relationship does not establish that every detail of Isaiah 60 has only one fulfillment, nor does it identify a particular modern nation or chronology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Revelation may be composing from several prophetic texts simultaneously rather than quoting Isaiah 60 alone.
- Some shared images belong to a wider biblical vocabulary, although their concentration and sequence make mere coincidence unlikely.
