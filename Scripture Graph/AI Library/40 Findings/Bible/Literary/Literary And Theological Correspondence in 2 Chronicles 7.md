# Literary And Theological Correspondence in 2 Chronicles 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close sequence of question, covenant abandonment, worship of other gods, and resulting disaster is strong evidence that 2 Chronicles 7:19-22 frames the temple's future through Deuteronomic covenant theology.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the exact direction of literary dependence, the identity of an author or editor, or a precise date of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages could draw upon a shared covenant-warning tradition rather than one text directly borrowing from the other.
- Some verbal similarity may result from the wording of the English translation, although the thematic sequence remains substantial.
