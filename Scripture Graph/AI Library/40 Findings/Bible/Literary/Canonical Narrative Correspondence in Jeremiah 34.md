# Canonical Narrative Correspondence in Jeremiah 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the canonical narrative, Jeremiah 39 and 52 correspond closely to the earlier oracle: Zedekiah is captured, brought before the Babylonian king, and taken toward Babylon, while Jerusalem is burned. The later narrative does not explicitly record the promised lamentation and funeral honors of Jeremiah 34:5.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish the oracle's exact date, independent predictive verification, or supernatural causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may have been transmitted or edited with knowledge of the outcome.
- The oracle and fulfillment accounts may draw on the same historical memory.
- Agreement inside one literary corpus is not independent corroboration of predictive foreknowledge.
