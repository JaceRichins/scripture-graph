# Explicit Authorial Interpretation in Hebrews 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Because the chapter itself calls the earlier sanctuary a figure and the handmade holy places figures of the true, the claim that Hebrews intentionally reads Israel's cult typologically is strongly supported by explicit textual evidence.

**Does not establish:** This establishes the literary strategy of Hebrews, not a complete one-to-one symbolism for every tabernacle object or an empirically demonstrable map of heaven.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Readers may disagree about whether each furnishing has an individual symbolic counterpart.
- The typology can be understood as theological exposition without assuming that every architectural detail was designed solely to encode Christian meanings.
