# Literary Structure in Psalm 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Psalm 25 is commonly identified as an alphabetic acrostic in its Hebrew form, although its sequence is irregular. That feature strongly supports deliberate literary organization but provides little independent evidence about authorship, date, or provenance.

**Does not establish:** The form does not establish Davidic authorship, a particular date, divine inspiration, or the poem's precise compositional history.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The acrostic may reflect a conventional compositional or mnemonic technique rather than a distinctive theological design.
- Some irregularities could reflect textual transmission or editorial shaping.

**Scripture:** [[Psalm 25#^ps-25-1|Psalm 25:1]], [[Psalm 25#^ps-25-22|Psalm 25:22]]

**Sources:**

- Robert Alter — *The Book of Psalms: A Translation with Commentary* (2007)
