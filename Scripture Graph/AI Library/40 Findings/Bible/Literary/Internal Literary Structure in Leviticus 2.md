# Internal Literary Structure in Leviticus 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close repetition of the sequence memorial portion burned, sweet savour, priestly remainder, and most-holy designation supports reading the chapter as deliberately organized procedural instruction.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish a particular author, date, compositional history, or theory of inspiration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may primarily reflect ordinary legal formula rather than a separate literary design.
- The parallel wording could result from editorial standardization of related ritual cases.
- Repetition may serve oral clarity and memorization without implying an elaborate artistic structure.
