# HistoricalEpistolary Convention in Galatians 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Galatians 6:11 ('Ye see how large a letter I have written unto you with mine own hand') reflects the common ancient practice of dictating a letter to a scribe (amanuensis) and then adding a personal, often larger-lettered, closing note or signature in one's own hand — a practice attested elsewhere in the Pauline corpus.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself prove Pauline authorship of the whole letter; it only documents a known ancient letter-closing convention consistent with Paul's other epistles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The 'large letters' comment could instead refer to Paul's handwriting style or an eyesight limitation rather than functioning purely as an authentication device.
