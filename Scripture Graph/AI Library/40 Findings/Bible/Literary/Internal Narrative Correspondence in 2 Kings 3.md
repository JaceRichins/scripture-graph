# Internal Narrative Correspondence in 2 Kings 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close correspondence between Elisha's oracle and the ensuing water, victory, and devastation is strong internal evidence that the narrator intends these events to be read as fulfillment of the prophetic word.

**Does not establish:** Internal correspondence establishes the narrative's intended interpretation, not by itself the event's historicity or supernatural cause.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A narrator or editor could have shaped the oracle and battle account retrospectively to correspond.
- The water may have had an ordinary hydrological cause, such as rain outside the army's immediate field of view.
- Literary presentation and historical occurrence are separate questions.
