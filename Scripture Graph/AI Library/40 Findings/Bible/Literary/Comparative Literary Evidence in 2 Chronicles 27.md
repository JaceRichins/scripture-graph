# Comparative Literary Evidence in 2 Chronicles 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The parallel Jotham notices in 2 Kings and 2 Chronicles share the central regnal data, maternal identification, positive evaluation, high-gate construction, death, and succession. Chronicles additionally reports the Ophel and regional fortifications, Ammonite tribute, and the explicit explanation of Jotham's might in verse 6. This comparative pattern supports the conclusion that the Chronicler's presentation has a distinctive thematic emphasis on building, strength, and devotion.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not establish the historicity or non-historicity of the additional material, direct dependence between the extant books, or the precise nature of their sources.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may preserve historical material that Kings omitted for reasons of brevity or differing interests.
- The Chronicler may have expanded inherited tradition to express characteristic theological concerns.
- Kings and Chronicles may draw on partially different source traditions rather than one simply rewriting the other.
