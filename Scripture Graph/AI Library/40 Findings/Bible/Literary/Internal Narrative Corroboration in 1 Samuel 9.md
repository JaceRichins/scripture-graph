# Internal Narrative Corroboration in 1 Samuel 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Samuel's accurate statement that the animals lost three days earlier have been found confirms the servant's prior characterization that Samuel's words reliably come to pass. This functions as internal authentication of Samuel for Saul and the reader; it is not independent historical verification of supernatural foreknowledge.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence does not independently establish the historicity or supernatural origin of Samuel's knowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- As literary characterization, the correspondence may have been composed to establish Samuel's authority.
- Samuel could have acquired the information through ordinary human communication not reported by the narrator.
- Readers who accept the narrative's theological claims may understand the knowledge as revelation.
