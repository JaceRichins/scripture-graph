# Institutional Arrangement in Numbers 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The proportional allocation rule supports the interpretation that maintaining Levitical communities was a responsibility distributed across Israel rather than assigned uniformly without regard to tribal holdings.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a general economic system, equal tribal wealth, or how consistently the arrangement was implemented.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The rule may primarily be a practical land-allocation formula rather than a broader principle of proportional religious obligation.

**Scripture:** [[Numbers 35#^num-35-8|Numbers 35:8]]
