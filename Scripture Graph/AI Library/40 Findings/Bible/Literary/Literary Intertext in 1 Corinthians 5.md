# Literary Intertext in 1 Corinthians 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of Passover, sacrifice, leaven removal, feast, and unleavened-bread language provides strong evidence that verses 6–8 deliberately frame communal holiness through Passover imagery rather than using leaven as an isolated proverb.

**Does not establish:** The Passover pattern does not by itself determine the precise scope of exclusion, the meaning of delivering someone to Satan, or the mechanism of the flesh's destruction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The imagery may have been prompted by the season of Passover rather than by a comprehensive theory of church discipline.
- Some details may function as flexible rhetoric without requiring a one-to-one correspondence between the feast and every disciplinary action.
