# Verbal And Narrative Correspondence in Matthew 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of being 'offended' links the sower's rootless hearer with the final hometown rejection: the first falls away under pressure because of the word, while Jesus' neighbors are offended in him despite recognizing his wisdom and mighty works.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence does not prove that every hometown hearer corresponds specifically to the rocky soil or that Matthew deliberately designed the episode as a formal parable fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated word may be ordinary vocabulary rather than an intentional cross-reference.
- The rocky-soil hearer and the hometown audience reject for different immediate reasons.
- The hometown episode may principally conclude the preceding ministry section rather than function as an enacted commentary on the parable.
