# Historical Difficulty in Daniel 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's historical setting also contains unresolved difficulties: extant evidence identifies Nabonidus, not Nebuchadnezzar, as Belshazzar's father, and no proposed identification of Darius the Median has achieved broad scholarly acceptance. These are material limitations on claims of straightforward historical precision.

**Does not establish:** These difficulties do not by themselves prove that the entire account is fictional, but they prevent treating every royal and genealogical designation as independently verified.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Father and son terminology can sometimes express broader ancestry, succession, or dynastic relationship rather than immediate biological descent.
- Darius the Median has been identified by some interpreters with Gubaru, Ugbaru, Cyrus, or another official, but every proposal faces textual or historical objections.
- The narrative may compress a more complex transfer of imperial authority into a concise succession notice.
