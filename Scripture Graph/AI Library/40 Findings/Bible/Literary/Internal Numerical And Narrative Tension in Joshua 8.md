# Internal Numerical And Narrative Tension in Joshua 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter first reports thirty thousand men sent by night and later reports about five thousand men placed in ambush in the same general area west of Ai. The text does not explain whether the five thousand are a subset, a second detachment, or a separate narrative description.

**Does not establish:** The unexplained figures do not by themselves establish a contradiction, a scribal error, or the existence of multiple sources.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The five thousand may be a subset of the previously selected thirty thousand.
- Joshua may have established two distinct ambush forces.
- One number may reflect a textual transmission problem.
- The account may combine descriptions from different narrative traditions or editorial stages.
