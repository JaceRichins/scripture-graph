# Same-Book Structural Correspondence in Revelation 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The first four vials follow the same broad environmental order as the first four trumpets: earth, sea, inland waters, and the heavenly lights or sun. This ordered correspondence strongly supports a deliberate literary relationship between the two judgment cycles.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish whether the trumpets and vials describe separate chronological events or repeated perspectives on judgment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both cycles could independently use a conventional catalogue of cosmic domains.
- Even if the parallel is deliberate, interpreters may understand the cycles as chronological stages, recapitulations, or thematic variations.
