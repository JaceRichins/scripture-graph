# Literary-Scriptural Framing in Matthew 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated fulfillment formulas strongly establish that Matthew frames Jesus's early life through Israel's scriptures. They do not, by themselves, establish external historicity, a single uniform method of fulfillment, or that every cited passage was originally a direct prediction of the narrated event.

**Does not establish:** The formulas alone do not independently verify the narrated events or prove that each source passage originally referred exclusively or directly to Jesus.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The formulas may employ several interpretive methods, including direct messianic reading, typology, analogy, and thematic synthesis.
- Some narrative details may have been selected or shaped to emphasize scriptural correspondences.
- Readers may disagree over whether the correspondences reflect prediction, providential recapitulation, authorial composition, or a combination.
