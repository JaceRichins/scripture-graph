# Internal Literary Evidence in Jeremiah 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The oracle's explicit comparison provides very strong internal literary evidence that the wine episode was included to expose Judah's failure to respond to divine instruction.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the episode's historicity, a universal ban on wine, or the moral authority of every ancestral custom.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The scene may preserve a historical encounter that was subsequently shaped into its present rhetorical form.
- The episode can simultaneously be a remembered event and a deliberately arranged prophetic sign.
