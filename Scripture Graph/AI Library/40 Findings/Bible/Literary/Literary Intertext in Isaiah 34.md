# Literary Intertext in Isaiah 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 34:4 and Revelation 6:14 share the distinctive image of the heavens being rolled or departing like a scroll. This is strong evidence that Revelation's judgment vision echoes or reuses Isaianic imagery.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not by itself establish a single fulfillment, a precise chronology, or that every feature of Isaiah 34 must be interpreted literally.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both texts could employ a wider stock of ancient cosmic-judgment imagery, although the shared scroll simile makes direct or mediated reuse plausible.
- Revelation may reuse Isaiah's language without claiming that every detail in both passages describes one identical historical event.
