# Internal Numerical Coherence in Numbers 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The exact agreement between the tribal subtotals and the aggregate of 601,730 is strong evidence that the numbers are deliberately coordinated in the chapter's present literary form.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historical accuracy, antiquity, or method of collection of the population figures.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor or copyist could have coordinated the aggregate with inherited subtotals.
- Deliberate literary construction can produce numerical consistency without the figures deriving from a historical field count.
