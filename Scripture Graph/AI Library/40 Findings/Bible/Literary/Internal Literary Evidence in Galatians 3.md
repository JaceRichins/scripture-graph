# Internal Literary Evidence in Galatians 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The density of explicit scriptural appeals is strong internal evidence that Paul's case is framed as an interpretation of Israel's scriptures: Abraham's belief, Abraham's blessing, the law's curse, life by faith, life through doing the law, and the curse associated with hanging on a tree are all invoked in sequence.

**Does not establish:** The presence of scriptural quotations does not by itself establish that Paul's interpretations are the only possible readings of the cited passages or that his historical chronology is independently verified.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A reader may regard some individual applications as selective proof-texting rather than as the original meanings of the cited passages.
- The sequence demonstrates Paul's interpretive method but does not show that every implied audience member accepted his premises.
