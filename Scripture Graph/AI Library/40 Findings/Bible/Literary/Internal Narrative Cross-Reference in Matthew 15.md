# Internal Narrative Cross-Reference in Matthew 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew later has Jesus separately recall the feeding of five thousand with twelve baskets and the feeding of four thousand with seven baskets. This is strong internal evidence that Matthew's final narrative intends readers to distinguish the two feeding accounts.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish that two separate miraculous feedings occurred historically.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew may have transformed variant versions of one inherited feeding tradition into two deliberately distinguished narrative episodes.
- The literary distinction in Matthew's final form does not by itself resolve the historical relationship between the traditions.
