# Scriptural Intertext in Hebrews 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The description of Moses as faithful in all God's house closely recalls Numbers 12:7. This is strong textual evidence that the Moses-Christ comparison draws upon an established scriptural characterization of Moses rather than diminishing him.

**Does not establish:** The verbal connection does not by itself determine the author's identity, date, or complete theology of Moses.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may have become a familiar traditional description of Moses rather than being independently retrieved from Numbers for this passage.

**Scripture:** [[Hebrews 3#^heb-3-2|Hebrews 3:2]], [[Hebrews 3#^heb-3-5|Hebrews 3:5]], [[Numbers 12#^num-12-7|Numbers 12:7]]
