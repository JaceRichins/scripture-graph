# Internal Narrative And Ritual Evidence in Numbers 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The ordered transfer of Aaron's garments to Eleazar is strong internal narrative evidence of an authorized succession of Aaron's priestly role rather than an unstructured replacement after his death.

**Does not establish:** This episode alone does not establish a universal procedure for every later priesthood succession or settle claims about succession in other religious institutions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The garments could be read chiefly as symbols of status, although their divinely commanded transfer still indicates continuity of role.
- Numbers 20 alone does not describe every duty or rite associated with Eleazar's succession.
