# Internal Narrative Qualification in Joshua 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit statement that Judah could not drive the Jebusites from Jerusalem supports a distinction between territory assigned to Judah and territory fully controlled by Judah.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the precise historical extent of Judah's control, the date of the lists, or whether every named settlement was occupied simultaneously.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The city list may express an ideal or legal territorial claim rather than an assertion that every site was already occupied.
- Verse 63 may describe only one stage in a longer process of settlement and conquest.
- The concluding notice may be an editorial qualification attached to an older allotment list.
