# Literary-Structural in 1 Kings 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated establishment statements provide strong internal evidence that consolidation of Solomon's rule is a principal organizing theme of the chapter. They do not, by themselves, indicate that every consolidating action receives divine or ethical approval.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the legality, morality, divine inspiration, or historical necessity of Solomon's executions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated wording may be a conventional regnal formula rather than a deliberately constructed inclusio.
- The framing may emphasize political effectiveness without endorsing the means used.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 2#^1kgs-2-12|1 Kings 2:12]], [[1 Kings 2#^1kgs-2-46|1 Kings 2:46]]
