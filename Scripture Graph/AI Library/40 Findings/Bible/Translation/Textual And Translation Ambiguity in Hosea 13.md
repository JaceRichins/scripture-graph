# Textual And Translation Ambiguity in Hosea 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hosea 13:14 is genuinely disputed at the translation and interpretation level. The KJV reads its opening clauses as promises to ransom and redeem, but interrogative construals of the Hebrew and the surrounding return to judgment in verses 15-16 permit the contrary reading that deliverance is being withheld and Death is being summoned. The verse should not be presented as an uncomplicated resurrection promise without acknowledging this ambiguity.

**Does not establish:** The ambiguity does not establish that the verse lacks hope, nor that its original immediate referent was individual bodily resurrection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The affirmative KJV reading may correctly preserve an abrupt promise of divine victory over death.
- The verse may intentionally juxtapose a remembered promise of redemption with a declaration that God will not relent from present judgment.
- The questions may be rhetorical invitations to Death and the grave, making the entire verse a judgment oracle.
- Later resurrection-oriented reception may legitimately develop the verse beyond its immediate historical sense.
