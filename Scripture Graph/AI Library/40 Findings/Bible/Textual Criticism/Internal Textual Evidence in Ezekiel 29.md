# Internal Textual Evidence in Ezekiel 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter supplies strong textual evidence that this oracle presents Nebuchadrezzar's campaign as service used and recompensed by God, even though the military action is carried out by Babylon.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every act of Babylon was morally approved, nor does it by itself prove a comprehensive theory of divine causation in warfare.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may be prophetic rhetoric explaining international events rather than a general doctrine about every imperial war.
- Calling Babylon an instrument of judgment need not imply divine approval of Babylon's motives or conduct.
