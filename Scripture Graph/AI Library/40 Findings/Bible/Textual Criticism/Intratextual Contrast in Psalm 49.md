# Intratextual Contrast in Psalm 49

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The intrachapter contrast between wealth's inability to ransom life and God's promised redemption strongly supports reading divine rather than economic power as the psalm's answer to death.

**Does not establish:** The contrast does not by itself establish a detailed doctrine of resurrection, the intermediate state, or the precise meaning of God receiving the speaker.

**Alternative explanations:**
- God's redemption in verse 15 may refer to rescue from premature death rather than a fully articulated doctrine of resurrection.
- The language may express poetic trust without specifying the mechanism or timing of deliverance.
