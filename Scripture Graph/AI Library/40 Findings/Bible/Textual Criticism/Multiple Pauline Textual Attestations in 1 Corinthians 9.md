# Multiple Pauline Textual Attestations in 1 Corinthians 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Taken together, 1 Corinthians 9 and Paul's later discussion in 2 Corinthians provide strong textual evidence that he deliberately declined ordinary material support from the Corinthian congregation, at least during significant portions of his ministry among them.

**Does not establish:** This evidence does not establish that Paul rejected all financial support everywhere, nor does it require unpaid ministry as a universal rule.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later references may reuse the same rhetorical presentation and are not fully independent testimony.
- Paul may have declined support from Corinth while accepting assistance from other congregations.
- The statements do not specify every financial arrangement or every phase of his Corinthian ministry.
