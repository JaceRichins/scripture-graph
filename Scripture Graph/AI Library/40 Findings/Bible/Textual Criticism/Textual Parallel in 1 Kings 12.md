# Textual Parallel in 1 Kings 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive agreement between 1 Kings 12 and 2 Chronicles 10–11 is strong evidence that the accounts belong to a shared textual tradition. Agreement between literarily related accounts is not equivalent to multiple independent historical attestation.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish independent corroboration, the historicity of every detail, or which precise source relationship produced the agreement.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may depend directly or indirectly upon Kings rather than representing an independent witness.
- Both works may draw upon a shared earlier written source.
