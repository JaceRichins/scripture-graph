# Intertextual Verbal Correspondence in 1 Kings 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeroboam's declaration over the calves closely reproduces the golden-calf declaration in Exodus 32:4 and 32:8. The shared wording and calf setting strongly support a deliberate literary association with the Exodus apostasy, while leaving the direction, date, and compositional stage of the relationship unresolved.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not establish the historical details of Jeroboam's cult, the date of either passage, or a particular theory of authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may employ an inherited traditional formula rather than one directly quoting the other.
- An editor may have shaped one or both accounts to create the correspondence.
- The parallel by itself cannot identify the chronological direction of literary dependence.
