# Textual Interdependence in Isaiah 55

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained verbal correspondence between Isaiah 55:1-2 and 2 Nephi 9:50-51 is strong textual evidence that the latter passage deliberately invokes or reproduces the invitation in Isaiah 55.

**Does not establish:** The verbal relationship alone does not establish the historical date, authorship, or translation mechanism of 2 Nephi.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may reflect direct quotation by the speaker within the narrative.
- The shared English wording may partly reflect a translator's or editor's use of familiar King James diction.
- Both passages could be mediated through a common scriptural tradition rather than one simple compositional mechanism.
