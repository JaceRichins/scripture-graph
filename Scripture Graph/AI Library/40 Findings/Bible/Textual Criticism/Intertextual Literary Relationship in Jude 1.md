# Intertextual Literary Relationship in Jude 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jude and 2 Peter share a dense sequence of unusual themes and images: covert teachers who deny the Lord, sinful angels, Sodom and Gomorrha, contempt for authority, irrational animals, Balaam, waterless clouds or wells, boastful speech, and predicted last-days mockers. The cumulative correspondence strongly supports literary dependence in one direction or use of substantial common material.

**Does not establish:** The parallels alone do not determine whether Jude used 2 Peter, 2 Peter used Jude, or both used another source; nor do they establish the dates or traditional authorship of either letter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both authors may have independently drawn upon a shared Jewish-Christian source or established teaching tradition.
- Some individual parallels may reflect common early Christian vocabulary, though this is less persuasive as an explanation of the whole sequence.
