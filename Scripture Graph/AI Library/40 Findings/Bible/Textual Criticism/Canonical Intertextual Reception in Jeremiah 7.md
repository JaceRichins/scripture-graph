# Canonical Intertextual Reception in Jeremiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Synoptic accounts of Jesus' temple action reuse Jeremiah's distinctive den-of-robbers image, providing strong evidence that Jeremiah 7 became an interpretive script for later criticism of corrupt confidence or conduct associated with the temple.

**Does not establish:** This reuse does not by itself prove that Jeremiah directly predicted Jesus' temple action, that the historical circumstances were identical, or that every detail of the Gospel narratives derives from Jeremiah.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Gospel writers may have shaped their accounts with scriptural language when narrating Jesus' action.
- The Gospel saying combines Jeremiah's den image with the house-of-prayer language of Isaiah 56:7, so Jeremiah is not its only scriptural context.
