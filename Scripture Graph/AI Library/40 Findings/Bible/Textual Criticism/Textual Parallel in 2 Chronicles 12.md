# Textual Parallel in 2 Chronicles 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed agreement between 2 Chronicles 12:9-11 and 1 Kings 14:25-28 is strong evidence of a shared literary tradition, but it is weak as independent corroboration because one account may depend on the other or both may use a common source.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish independent attestation, the precise historical details of the invasion, or a supernatural explanation for it.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Chronicler directly adapted the account in Kings.
- Kings and Chronicles drew upon a shared written source.
- Both accounts reflect a circulating tradition that was not independent by the time either text reached its present form.
