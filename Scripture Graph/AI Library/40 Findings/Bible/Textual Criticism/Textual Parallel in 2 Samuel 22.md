# Textual Parallel in 2 Samuel 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The verified index identifies extensive verbal overlap between 2 Samuel 22 and forty-two verses of Psalm 18, showing that substantially the same song is preserved in both the historical books and the Psalter.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not establish which version is earlier, the precise date of composition, the direction of literary dependence, or that every line originated with David.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Psalm 18 may derive from the Samuel form.
- The Samuel form may derive from a form preserved in the Psalter.
- Both versions may descend from an earlier shared form and have undergone separate transmission.
