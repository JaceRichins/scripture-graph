# Textual Ambiguity Interpretive Dispute in Psalm 74

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The phrases 'divide the sea' (v.13) and 'cleave the fountain and the flood... driedst up mighty rivers' (v.15) are worded ambiguously enough to evoke either the primordial ordering of chaotic waters at creation or the historical Exodus/Reed Sea and Jordan crossings; the psalm itself does not specify which is meant.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that the psalmist intends a reference to the historical Exodus rather than (or in addition to) the creation account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A purely cosmogonic reading (God subduing primordial chaos-waters at creation, with no Exodus reference intended).
- A purely historical reading (allusion to the Red Sea and Jordan crossings of the Exodus/conquest narratives).
- A deliberate double entendre merging creation and Exodus imagery, a known feature of biblical poetry elsewhere.
