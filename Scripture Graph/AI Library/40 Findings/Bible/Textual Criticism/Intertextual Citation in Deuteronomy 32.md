# Intertextual Citation in Deuteronomy 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 35's phrase 'To me belongeth vengeance, and recompence' is quoted directly in the New Testament as apostolic teaching that personal vengeance belongs to God alone, showing the verse was treated as authoritative ethical instruction beyond its original judgment-oracle context.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Paul intended readers to recall the full judgment-oracle context of Deuteronomy 32 rather than just the isolated maxim.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be drawing on a common proverbial tradition about divine vengeance rather than directly citing Deuteronomy 32 specifically, since similar language also appears in Deuteronomy elsewhere and in Psalms.
