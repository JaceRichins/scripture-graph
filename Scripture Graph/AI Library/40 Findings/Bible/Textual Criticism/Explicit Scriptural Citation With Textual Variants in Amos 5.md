# Explicit Scriptural Citation With Textual Variants in Amos 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 7:42–43 cites the closing wilderness-idolatry passage through wording associated with the Greek textual tradition. Compared with the KJV text of Amos, Acts has “Remphan” rather than “Chiun” and “beyond Babylon” rather than “beyond Damascus.” The differences are strong evidence for a significant textual and reception history but do not alone identify the earliest wording.

**Does not establish:** The differences do not by themselves establish which wording is original, prove simple scribal corruption, or show that Acts misunderstood Amos.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Acts may reproduce or adapt a Greek version whose underlying Hebrew differed from the later Masoretic form.
- The geographical change may reflect interpretive updating or rhetorical application.
- Some variation may arise through translation and transmission rather than a single act of corruption.
