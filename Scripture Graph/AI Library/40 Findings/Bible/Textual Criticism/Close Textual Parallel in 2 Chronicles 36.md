# Close Textual Parallel in 2 Chronicles 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closing proclamation in 2 Chronicles 36:22–23 has a close verbal parallel in Ezra 1:1–3, strongly supporting a deliberate literary or compositional relationship between the ending of Chronicles and the opening of Ezra.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not establish common authorship, the direction of literary dependence, or that Chronicles and Ezra originally formed one work.

**Alternative explanations:**
- One book may quote or adapt the other.
- Both passages may draw from a shared decree tradition or editorial source.
- A later editor may have used the overlap to connect two previously distinct works.
