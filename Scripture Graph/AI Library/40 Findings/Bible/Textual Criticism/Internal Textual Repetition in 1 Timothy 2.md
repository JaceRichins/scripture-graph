# Internal Textual Repetition in 1 Timothy 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repetition of universal language—prayer for all, God's saving will toward all, and Christ's ransom for all—is strong internal evidence that the chapter deliberately broadens its horizon beyond a narrow social or ethnic group.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every person will finally be saved, nor does it by itself resolve debates about divine will, human response, judgment, or universal salvation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated word "all" may mean all kinds or classes of people, especially rulers and Gentiles, rather than making a precise statement about every individual.
- The universal phrasing may be pastoral and rhetorical rather than a technical definition of the eventual extent of salvation.
