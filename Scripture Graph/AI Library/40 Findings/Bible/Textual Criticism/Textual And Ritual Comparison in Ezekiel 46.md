# Textual And Ritual Comparison in Ezekiel 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared daily, Sabbath, and new-moon framework is strong textual evidence that Ezekiel's temple vision engages Israel's established sacrificial tradition; the differences in quantities show that this engagement is not simple verbatim repetition.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not establish direct literary dependence, the date or direction of dependence, or that Ezekiel's program was historically implemented.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ezekiel and Numbers may draw independently on a broader shared priestly tradition.
- The differences may represent an idealized program rather than a historically enacted revision.
- Some differences could reflect distinct textual or ritual stages rather than deliberate correction of Numbers.
