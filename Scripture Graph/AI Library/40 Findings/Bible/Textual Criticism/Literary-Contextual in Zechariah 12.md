# Literary-Contextual in Zechariah 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of an outpoured spirit of grace and supplications with mourning supports a penitential reading of the response, and the immediately following promise of a fountain for sin and uncleanness strengthens that reading. Nevertheless, Zechariah 12 never explicitly calls the mourning repentance.

**Does not establish:** The sequence does not establish that repentance is the sole meaning of the mourning or conclusively identify the pierced figure.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The mourning may primarily express bereavement for a slain leader rather than repentance.
- Zechariah 13:1 may begin a related but distinct oracle rather than define the emotional meaning of Zechariah 12:10-14.
- The supplications may be pleas for divine aid without implying confession of culpability.
