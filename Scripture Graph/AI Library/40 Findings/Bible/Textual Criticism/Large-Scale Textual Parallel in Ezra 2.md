# Large-Scale Textual Parallel in Ezra 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ezra 2 has an extensive verbal and structural parallel in Nehemiah 7, including the return setting, leaders, family and service groups, genealogical dispute, grand total, possessions, offerings, and settlement. The verified overlap strongly supports a shared textual tradition or source relationship.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish which version is earlier, the direction of dependence, or which differing readings preserve the original figures.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both books may independently preserve a common administrative list.
- One version may have been adapted from the other.
- A later editor may have inserted or harmonized a received list in one or both settings.

**Scripture:** [[Ezra 2#^ezra-2-1|Ezra 2:1-70]], [[Nehemiah 7#^neh-7-6|Nehemiah 7:6-73]]
