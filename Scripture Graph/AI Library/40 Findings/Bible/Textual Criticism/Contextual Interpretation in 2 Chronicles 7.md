# Contextual Interpretation in 2 Chronicles 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The immediate context strongly supports reading 2 Chronicles 7:14 first as a promise to the covenant people associated with Solomon's temple, in response to specified covenant calamities. It may supply a broader pattern of humility, prayer, repentance, and forgiveness, but the text does not directly promise that every modern nation will receive national healing through the same formula.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the principles have no application beyond ancient Israel; it limits what can be claimed as the verse's direct historical promise.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Readers may treat Israel's experience as a typological or analogical pattern for other communities without claiming that those communities are the passage's original addressees.
- Some theological traditions understand covenant promises to Israel as directly extensible to a modern church or nation.
