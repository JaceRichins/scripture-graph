# Internal Textual Evidence in Deuteronomy 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The warning against withholding aid because the release year is near is strong internal evidence that the law recognizes a foreseeable economic incentive to evade its demand for generosity.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish how often Israelites observed the release, how loans were administered, or whether the anticipated refusal was historically widespread.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The warning may address a hypothetical temptation rather than document an abuse known to have occurred.
- As legal exhortation, the passage describes prescribed conduct and does not by itself show ordinary economic practice.
