# Intertextual Architectural Correspondence in Ezekiel 41

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The cluster of architectural and decorative correspondences with 1 Kings 6 is substantial evidence that Ezekiel's temple vision is framed in continuity with earlier Israelite sanctuary traditions.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish direct literary dependence, exact historical reconstruction, or that Ezekiel's measurements were intended as construction instructions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both accounts may independently employ architectural conventions common to temples in the region.
- A later writer or editor could have shaped one account using established scriptural temple language.
- The correspondences may express symbolic continuity without preserving a directly buildable architectural plan.
