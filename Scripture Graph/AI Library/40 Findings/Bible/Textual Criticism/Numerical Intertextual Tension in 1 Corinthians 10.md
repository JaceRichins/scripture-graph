# Numerical Intertextual Tension in 1 Corinthians 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The KJV texts preserve a surface numerical difference: Paul reports that twenty-three thousand fell in one day, while Numbers reports twenty-four thousand deaths in the plague. The texts themselves do not explain the difference.

**Does not establish:** The numerical difference does not by itself disprove the underlying event, Paul’s ethical argument, or the broader historical reliability of either book.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul’s number may cover those who fell within a single day, while Numbers gives the plague’s total.
- One or both numbers may reflect rounding or a different way of counting the deaths.
- The difference may have arisen during textual transmission.
- Paul may be combining or adapting traditional descriptions for rhetorical purposes.

**Scripture:** [[Numbers 25#^num-25-9|Numbers 25:9]], [[1 Corinthians 10#^1cor-10-8|1 Corinthians 10:8]]
