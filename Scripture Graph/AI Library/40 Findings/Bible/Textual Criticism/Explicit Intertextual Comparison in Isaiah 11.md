# Explicit Intertextual Comparison in Isaiah 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit comparison with Israel's departure from Egypt makes a new-exodus reading of the remnant's passage in verses 15-16 strongly grounded in the text.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not establish the identity of the river, the mechanics of the deliverance, its date, or whether its geographical imagery must be fulfilled literally.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The imagery may chiefly describe a concrete return route while invoking the earlier exodus as an analogy.
- The language may poetically portray political deliverance rather than predict specific hydrological events.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 11#^isa-11-15|Isaiah 11:15-16]], [[Exodus 14#^ex-14-21|Exodus 14:21-29]]
