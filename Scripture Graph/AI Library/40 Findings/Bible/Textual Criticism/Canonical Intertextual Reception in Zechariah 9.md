# Canonical Intertextual Reception in Zechariah 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 21 explicitly presents Jesus's entry into Jerusalem as fulfillment and applies to it the daughter-of-Zion and ass-and-colt language of Zechariah 9:9. This is strong evidence for Matthew's messianic interpretation of the passage.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the date of Zechariah 9, exclusive original reference to Jesus, or independence between the narrated event and the scriptural wording.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew may be making a typological or theological application rather than documenting the only possible original referent of Zechariah's oracle.
- The entry may have been deliberately enacted or narrated in language shaped by Zechariah 9:9.
