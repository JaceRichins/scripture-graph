# Textual Reuse And Reception in Isaiah 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The numerous close correspondences between Isaiah 29 and 2 Nephi 27 strongly establish deliberate textual reuse in the English Book of Mormon, with 2 Nephi expanding the sealed-book imagery into a more detailed revelatory narrative.

**Does not establish:** Textual overlap by itself does not establish ancient authorship, the historical direction or mechanism of dependence, or an exclusive latter-day fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Believing translation models understand 2 Nephi as an ancient prophetic expansion rendered in KJV-like English.
- A modern-composition model can explain the English overlap as direct literary dependence on the KJV.
- A theological reception model can focus on the deliberate canonical reapplication without resolving the text's historical origin.
