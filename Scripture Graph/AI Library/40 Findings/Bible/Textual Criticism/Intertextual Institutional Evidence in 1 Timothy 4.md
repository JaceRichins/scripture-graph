# Intertextual Institutional Evidence in 1 Timothy 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Read together, 1 Timothy 4:14 and 2 Timothy 1:6 provide substantial textual evidence for an early Christian commissioning or gift-conferral setting involving prophecy and the laying on of hands.

**Does not establish:** These verses alone do not establish the complete form, offices, succession, or theological meaning of any later ordination system.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two passages may describe different occasions rather than a single commissioning event.
- The laying on of hands may recognize or activate a gift without defining a formal office.
- The precise relationship between Paul's hands and the presbytery's hands is not explained.

**Scripture:** [[1 Timothy 4#^1tim-4-14|1 Timothy 4:14]], [[2 Timothy 1#^2tim-1-6|2 Timothy 1:6]]
