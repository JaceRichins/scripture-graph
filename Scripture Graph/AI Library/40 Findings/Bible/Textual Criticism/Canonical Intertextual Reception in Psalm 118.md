# Canonical Intertextual Reception in Psalm 118

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
New Testament writers apply Psalm 118:22’s rejected-stone image to Jesus. This is strong evidence for an early Christian messianic reading of the verse, although it does not by itself settle the metaphor’s original historical referent.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every line of Psalm 118 originally referred exclusively or directly to Jesus, nor does it identify the psalm’s original speaker.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The New Testament may be giving a typological or christological rereading rather than identifying the psalm’s sole original referent.
- In its initial setting, the stone could have represented an individual ruler, Israel, or another vindicated figure.
