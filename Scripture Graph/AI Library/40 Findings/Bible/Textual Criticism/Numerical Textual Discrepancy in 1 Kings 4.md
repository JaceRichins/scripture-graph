# Numerical Textual Discrepancy in 1 Kings 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The forty thousand stalls reported in 1 Kings 4:26 differ from the four thousand stalls reported in 2 Chronicles 9:25, while both passages give twelve thousand horsemen. This is clear evidence of a numerical textual or source-tradition problem, but the comparison by itself does not identify the earlier figure.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy alone does not establish whether forty thousand or four thousand represents the earliest recoverable reading.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A copyist may have altered one numeral.
- Kings and Chronicles may depend on differing source traditions.
- The accounts may count different kinds of stalls or facilities, though neither verse explicitly explains such a distinction.
