# Canonical Intertextual Parallel in Micah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Micah's description of conflict between parents, children, in-laws, and members of one household has a strong canonical literary parallel in Jesus's warnings about household division in Matthew 10 and Luke 12.

**Does not establish:** The parallel by itself does not establish that every detail of Micah 7:6 is a direct prediction of Jesus's ministry or that Micah's original historical subject was the later gospel mission.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passages could draw independently upon a familiar description of family conflict.
- Similar wording in the English KJV may make the degree of verbal dependence appear stronger than comparison of the underlying languages alone would establish.
