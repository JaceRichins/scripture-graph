# Scope Established By Repeated Textual Qualifiers in 1 Corinthians 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated descriptions "another," "brother," and "brethren" provide strong textual evidence that the immediate instruction concerns civil disputes within the Christian community. The passage therefore should not automatically be treated as a comprehensive prohibition on every possible appeal to public courts.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that believers must keep crimes, abuse, threats, or matters requiring public protection away from lawful authorities.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may expect the principle of accepting loss and avoiding public scandal to influence disputes beyond the precise situation described.
- The immediate intra-church setting does not by itself identify every circumstance in which secular adjudication would be permissible or necessary.
