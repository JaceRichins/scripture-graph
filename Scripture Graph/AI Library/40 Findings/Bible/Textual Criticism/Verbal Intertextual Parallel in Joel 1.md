# Verbal Intertextual Parallel in Joel 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Joel 1:15 and Isaiah 13:6 contain a near-verbatim sequence about howling because the day of the LORD is at hand and will come as destruction from the Almighty. This strongly supports shared prophetic wording or literary relationship, but direction of dependence cannot be determined from the parallel alone.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish which text is earlier, direct dependence, the date of Joel, or common authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Joel directly reused Isaiah.
- Isaiah directly reused Joel.
- Both writers employed an already circulating prophetic formula.
- Later transmission or editorial activity brought the wording into closer agreement.
