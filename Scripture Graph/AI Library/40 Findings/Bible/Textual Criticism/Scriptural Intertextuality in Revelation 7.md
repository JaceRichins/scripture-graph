# Scriptural Intertextuality in Revelation 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clustered correspondences with Isaiah—no hunger, no thirst, protection from heat, guidance to water, and the removal of tears—provide strong evidence that Revelation 7 frames final salvation through Israel's prophetic restoration language.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish a date for fulfillment, a unique identification of the multitude, or the correctness of one detailed eschatological system.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some correspondences could arise from a shared stock of biblical deliverance imagery.
- The passage may combine several scriptural traditions rather than consciously quote one continuous source.
- Recognition of the allusions does not settle whether their fulfillment is historical, eschatological, symbolic, or layered.

**Scripture:** [[Revelation 7#^rev-7-16|Revelation 7:16-17]], [[Isaiah 49#^isa-49-10|Isaiah 49:10]], [[Isaiah 25#^isa-25-8|Isaiah 25:8]]
