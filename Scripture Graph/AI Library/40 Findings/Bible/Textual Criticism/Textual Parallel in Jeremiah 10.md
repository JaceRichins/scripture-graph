# Textual Parallel in Jeremiah 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 10:12–16 and Jeremiah 51:15–19 form a close literary parallel, preserving the same sequence of creation, weather, human foolishness before images, the images' lack of breath, their coming destruction, and the distinct identity of Jacob's portion.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of borrowing, the date of either form, or whether every line came from one author.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah 51 may reuse material from Jeremiah 10.
- Jeremiah 10 may preserve material also used in Jeremiah 51.
- Both passages may draw on a shared hymn, prophetic tradition, or later editorial arrangement.
