# Parallel Textual Tradition in 1 Chronicles 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Agreement between the city lists in 1 Chronicles 6 and Joshua 21 supports their participation in a shared administrative or literary tradition, but their extensive overlap limits their value as independent corroboration.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself prove that every named allocation was implemented historically or that either surviving list is textually complete.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may depend directly or indirectly on a form of the Joshua list.
- Both texts may draw independently from a shared archival tradition.
- Later transmission may have increased or reduced agreement between the lists.
