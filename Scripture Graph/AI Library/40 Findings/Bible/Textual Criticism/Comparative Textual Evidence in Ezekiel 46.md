# Comparative Textual Evidence in Ezekiel 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ezekiel's Sabbath and new-moon quantities differ materially from those in Numbers 28: Ezekiel prescribes six lambs and a ram for the Sabbath and one bullock, six lambs, and a ram for the new moon, whereas Numbers prescribes two lambs for the Sabbath and two bullocks, one ram, and seven lambs for the new moon. This is strong evidence that Ezekiel 46 is not merely a verbatim repetition of that Pentateuchal schedule.

**Does not establish:** The numerical differences do not establish why the schedule changed, whether it was enacted, or whether the vision should be interpreted as a literal future program.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The visionary legislation may deliberately adapt an earlier sacrificial system.
- The differences may reflect another legal or textual tradition.
- The quantities may have symbolic or literary functions rather than documenting an intended historical reform.
