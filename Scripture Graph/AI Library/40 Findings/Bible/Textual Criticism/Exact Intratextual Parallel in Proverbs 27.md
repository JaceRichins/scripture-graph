# Exact Intratextual Parallel in Proverbs 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Proverbs 27:12 repeats Proverbs 22:3 verbatim in the KJV. This strong intrabook parallel shows that the same maxim is preserved in more than one location and supports studying Proverbs as a collection in which sayings could be repeated or reused.

**Does not establish:** The repetition alone does not identify the original author, date either occurrence, or prove a particular theory of how Proverbs was compiled.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may reflect deliberate editorial reuse.
- Both locations may derive from a shared earlier stock of sayings.
- Some textual alignment could have arisen during transmission or translation.
