# Intratextual Argument in Romans 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Paul's explicit denial that the law is sin, together with his positive appraisal of the law and commandment, is strong internal evidence that his argument assigns the lethal agency primarily to sin rather than to God's commandment.

**Does not establish:** This does not determine whether every occurrence of 'law' in the chapter denotes the Mosaic law or settle the law's continuing covenantal status.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Although the law is not evil, it can still be described as the occasion or instrument through which sin produces death.
- Some readers understand Paul's defense as applying specifically to the Mosaic law, while others extend it to divine commandment more generally.
