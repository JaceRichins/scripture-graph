# Explicit Textual Principle in Jeremiah 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 18 is strong textual evidence that at least some biblical announcements of national judgment or benefit are conditional rather than unconditional forecasts. It therefore cautions against treating every unfulfilled warning as necessarily a failed prediction.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every biblical prophecy is conditional or provide a universal means of classifying all prophetic statements.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The principle may apply primarily to corporate or national oracles rather than to every form of prophecy.
- Some prophetic declarations may be presented elsewhere as unconditional.
