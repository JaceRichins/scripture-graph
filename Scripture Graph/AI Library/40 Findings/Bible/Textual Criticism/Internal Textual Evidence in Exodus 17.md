# Internal Textual Evidence in Exodus 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Exodus 17 portrays communal memory as both written and oral: Moses must write the event as a memorial and rehearse it to Joshua. This is strong textual evidence for the chapter's concern with preserving the event and preparing Joshua to remember it.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the date, authorship, compositional history, or historicity of the present form of Exodus.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The command may function primarily as a literary authorization of memory rather than preserving details about the production history of Exodus.
- The referenced book need not be identical with the present canonical book of Exodus.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 17#^ex-17-14|Exodus 17:14]]
