# Intertextual Citation in Deuteronomy 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Paul explicitly quotes the 'cursed' language of Deuteronomy 21:23 in Galatians 3:13 and applies it to Christ's crucifixion, making this one of the clearest direct New Testament citations of a Deuteronomic law.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Deuteronomy's author anticipated or intended a messianic reading; that connection is made by Paul, not by the Deuteronomic text itself.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fact of citation is not disputed, but Paul's theological move—that Christ 'became' the curse for others—is a New Testament application layered onto a law that, in its original context, addressed the burial of executed Israelite criminals, not a vicarious-atonement mechanism.
