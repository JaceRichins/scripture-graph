# Literary And Intertextual Pattern in Psalm 74

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The literary integration of sea division, monster defeat, dried waters, and cosmic ordering is meaningful evidence that the speaker invokes a broad biblical repertoire of divine victory and creation. It is not, by itself, evidence for one specific source, borrowing relationship, date, or theory of authorship.

**Does not establish:** The imagery does not establish direct literary dependence on a particular external text, nor does it prove the historical date or origin of the psalm.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may primarily recall Israel's historical deliverance rather than creation.
- The imagery may primarily describe creation through conventional poetic language.
- The poet may combine several inherited traditions without intending readers to identify a single narrative referent.
