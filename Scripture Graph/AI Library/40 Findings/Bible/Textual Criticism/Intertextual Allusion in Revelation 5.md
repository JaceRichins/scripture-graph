# Intertextual Allusion in Revelation 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The numeric formula for the countless heavenly host, 'ten thousand times ten thousand,' matches the same phrase used in Daniel's throne-room vision of the Ancient of Days, suggesting the scene in Revelation 5 is patterned on that Danielic court scene.

**Does not establish:** That Revelation 5 is a direct literary quotation of Daniel 7 rather than a shared use of conventional apocalyptic court imagery.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The phrase could be a stock idiom for an uncountable multitude common to Jewish apocalyptic writing generally, not a deliberate citation of Daniel specifically.
- John may be drawing on a broader throne-room/court topos (also found in 1 Enoch and other Second Temple texts) rather than Daniel alone.

**Scripture:** [[Revelation 5#^rev-5-11|Revelation 5:11]], [[Daniel 7#^dan-7-10|Daniel 7:10]]
