# Textual Criticism in Luke 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Luke 22:43-44 has a major ancient textual variant: the verses are absent from important early witnesses, including Papyrus 75 and Codex Vaticanus, but present in many other manuscripts. Their place in the manuscript tradition is therefore genuinely disputed even though they belong to the canonical text supplied for this study.

**Does not establish:** The variant does not by itself determine whether the described angel and agony are historical, nor does it undermine the textual stability of the remainder of the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A copyist may have added the verses from an early independent tradition to emphasize Jesus's suffering.
- A copyist may have omitted authentic verses because their portrayal of Jesus's anguish was considered theologically difficult.
- Accidental omission or early liturgical handling could have contributed to the divided transmission.
