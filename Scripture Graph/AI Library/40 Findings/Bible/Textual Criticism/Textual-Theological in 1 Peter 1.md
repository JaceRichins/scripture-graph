# Textual-Theological in 1 Peter 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The statement that Christ was foreordained before the foundation of the world is strong textual evidence that the chapter understands his redemptive role as established before its historical manifestation.

**Does not establish:** This verse alone does not define the nature of premortal existence, settle debates about predestination, or provide a complete doctrine of the Godhead.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Foreordination may refer specifically to God's prior redemptive purpose rather than functioning as a complete statement about the mode of Christ's premortal existence.
- Different theological traditions construe divine foreknowledge and foreordination differently.

**Scripture:** [[1 Peter 1#^1pet-1-19|1 Peter 1:19]], [[1 Peter 1#^1pet-1-20|1 Peter 1:20]]
