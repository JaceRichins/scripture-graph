# Internal TextualAuthorship Claim in Proverbs 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Proverbs 25:1's claim that Hezekiah's men copied out this collection is internal textual evidence for a stage of editorial compilation in the book's literary history, but does not by itself establish the historical accuracy of that attribution.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Solomon authored the underlying proverbs or that the compilation occurred exactly as described; it only shows the text itself makes this claim.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The superscription may be a later editorial framing device lending royal authority to the collection rather than a literal historical record of an 8th-century scribal project.
- Even if historical, the attribution to Hezekiah's court says nothing about whether the underlying sayings originated with Solomon himself.
