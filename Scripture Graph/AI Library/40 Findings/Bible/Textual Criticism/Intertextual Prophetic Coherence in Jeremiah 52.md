# Intertextual Prophetic Coherence in Jeremiah 52

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 52 reports the unusual conjunction that Zedekiah personally faced the king of Babylon and was then blinded before being taken to Babylon. This coheres with Jeremiah's statement that Zedekiah would see the Babylonian king and go to Babylon, and with Ezekiel's statement that he would be brought to Babylon without seeing it.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish when the prophecies were composed, supernatural prediction, or the direction of literary influence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prophetic texts may have been edited or shaped after Zedekiah's fate was known.
- The narrative may have been formulated to correspond with existing prophetic language.
- Blinding and deportation were historically plausible imperial punishments, so the conjunction need not require predictive prophecy.
