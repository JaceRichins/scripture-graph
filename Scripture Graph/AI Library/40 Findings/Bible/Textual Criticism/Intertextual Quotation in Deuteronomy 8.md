# Intertextual Quotation in Deuteronomy 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Deuteronomy 8:3 is directly quoted by Jesus in Matthew 4:4 during his own wilderness temptation, applying Israel's lesson about dependence on God's word to himself.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish anything about the historicity of Jesus' wilderness experience; it only establishes a documented literary/theological dependence of the Gospel text on the Deuteronomy passage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The near-verbatim overlap could reflect common liturgical/proverbial phrasing rather than a deliberate literary allusion, though the shared wilderness-temptation setting makes deliberate quotation the standard reading.
