# Textual Eschatological Indicators in Ezekiel 38

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of explicit latter-time language, a regathered Israel, an international invasion, and a public manifestation of divine judgment provides substantial textual support for an eschatological reading and for relating the chapter to the Second Coming. The chapter itself, however, never explicitly names Jesus Christ or uses the expression Second Coming.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a date, a specific modern coalition, or an exact synchronization with every later apocalyptic passage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may describe an idealized final enemy using conventional prophetic imagery rather than one chronologically identifiable battle.
- Some elements may reflect expectations tied to Israel's restoration after exile while also permitting later typological application.
- The language may telescope several stages of judgment rather than describe a single event.
