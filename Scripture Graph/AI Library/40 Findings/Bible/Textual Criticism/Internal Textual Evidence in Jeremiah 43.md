# Internal Textual Evidence in Jeremiah 43

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated statement that the group did not obey the voice of the LORD is strong internal evidence for the narrator's theological framing of the migration as disobedience rather than successful discernment.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the leaders privately recognized the message as divine, nor does it independently prove the historical or divine truth of the narrative's evaluation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated judgment expresses the narrator's theological perspective and is not independent historical verification that the message came from God.
- The leaders may have sincerely, though within the narrative mistakenly, believed that Jeremiah was being manipulated by Baruch.

**Scripture:** [[Jeremiah 43#^jer-43-4|Jeremiah 43:4]], [[Jeremiah 43#^jer-43-7|Jeremiah 43:7]]
