# Textual Parallel in 2 Kings 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive agreement in sequence and detail between 2 Kings 25 and Jeremiah 52 is strong evidence of a literary relationship or shared source tradition.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of borrowing, a single author, eyewitness composition, or the historical accuracy of every reported detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah 52 may depend on material also preserved in Kings.
- Kings may depend on a source also used by Jeremiah 52.
- Both passages may independently draw on a shared court or archival account.
- Later scribal transmission may have increased the passages' similarity.
