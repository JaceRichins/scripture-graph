# Textual Parallel in Psalm 105

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Psalm 105:1-15 has a substantial close parallel in 1 Chronicles 16:8-22. This is strong evidence of a literary or liturgical relationship, although the parallel alone cannot determine direction of borrowing, precise date, or authorship.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish which passage came first, the date of either composition, a particular author, or the historicity of the events recited.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Chronicler may have drawn from a form of the psalm.
- The psalm may have drawn from the liturgical composition preserved in Chronicles.
- Both passages may derive from a shared worship tradition or earlier source.
