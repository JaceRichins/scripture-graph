# Intertextual Narrative Continuity in Genesis 50

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Exodus 13:19 presents Moses's removal of Joseph's bones as the fulfillment of the oath recorded in Genesis 50:25. This is strong evidence of explicit narrative continuity between Genesis and Exodus.

**Does not establish:** The textual correspondence does not by itself establish the historicity, date, or single authorship of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later writer or editor could have composed one passage with the other already in view.
- The continuity may reflect a shared literary tradition rather than direct composition by a single author.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 50#^gen-50-25|Genesis 50:25]], [[Exodus 13#^ex-13-19|Exodus 13:19]]
