# Textual Parallel in Luke 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Luke 12:22–34 has a substantial verbal and thematic parallel in Matthew 6:19–34 and 3 Nephi 13:19–34, including heavenly treasure, anxiety about food and clothing, birds, lilies, and seeking God's kingdom.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of literary dependence, the original arrangement of the sayings, or independent historical corroboration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The evangelists may draw on shared oral or written traditions.
- One textual tradition may depend directly or indirectly on another.
- Individual sayings may originally have circulated separately before being grouped differently.
