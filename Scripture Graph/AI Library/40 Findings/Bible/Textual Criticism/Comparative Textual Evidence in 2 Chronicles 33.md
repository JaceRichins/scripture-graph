# Comparative Textual Evidence in 2 Chronicles 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extant accounts make materially different narrative selections. Second Chronicles records Manasseh's captivity, humility, prayer, restoration, and reform, while 2 Kings 21:1-18 proceeds from his sins and prophetic condemnation to the notice of his death without narrating that rehabilitation.

**Does not establish:** The literary difference alone does not determine the historical origin, date, or reliability of the additional material in Chronicles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may preserve historical information omitted by Kings.
- The Chronicler may have drawn on a later tradition or interpreted available traditions according to the work's theology of humility and restoration.
- Kings may have omitted the episode because its account emphasizes Manasseh's enduring responsibility for Judah's judgment.
