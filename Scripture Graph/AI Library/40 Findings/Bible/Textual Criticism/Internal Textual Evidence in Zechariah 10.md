# Internal Textual Evidence in Zechariah 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The deliberate pairing of Judah with Joseph/Ephraim is good internal evidence that the oracle envisions restoration extending beyond Judah alone, although it does not specify the later historical identity of every group included.

**Does not establish:** This wording does not establish a particular modern identification of the tribes, the date of fulfillment, or a single exclusive fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The paired names may be conventional language for an idealized national reunification rather than a detailed prediction about identifiable later populations.
- Joseph and Ephraim may represent northern Israel collectively without implying the separate return of every tribe.
