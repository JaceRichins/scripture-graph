# Intertextual Citation in Micah 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Micah 3:12 is quoted essentially verbatim roughly a century later in Jeremiah 26, where elders invoke 'Micah the Morasthite' and this specific oracle against Zion as precedent that a prophet who pronounces coming judgment on Jerusalem should not be put to death for it. This is one of the few places in the Hebrew Bible where one named prophet's oracle is explicitly cited by a later generation as authoritative precedent.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish the exact composition date or original wording of the book of Micah, nor prove a specific direction of literary dependence beyond the citation Jeremiah 26 itself claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The overlap could reflect shared prophetic/liturgical stock phrasing about Zion's judgment rather than a direct literary quotation of Micah specifically.
- Jeremiah 26's narrator may be summarizing a broader oral tradition attributed to Micah rather than quoting a fixed written text identical to the Micah scroll we have.
