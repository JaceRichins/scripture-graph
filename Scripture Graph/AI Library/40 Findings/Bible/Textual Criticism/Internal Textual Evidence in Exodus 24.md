# Internal Textual Evidence in Exodus 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The twelve tribal pillars, repeated collective responses, public reading, sacrifices, and application of covenant blood provide strong internal evidence that the chapter presents Israel's covenant as public, corporate, verbal, and ritualized.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the event's historicity, the precise mechanics of sprinkling a large population, or a modern political model of consent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The ceremony may be a literary-theological presentation rather than an independently verifiable record of a historical event.
- The people's collective response and their representation by leaders do not necessarily correspond to modern concepts of individual informed consent.
