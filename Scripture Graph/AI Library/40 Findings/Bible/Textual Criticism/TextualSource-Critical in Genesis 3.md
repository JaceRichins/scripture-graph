# TextualSource-Critical in Genesis 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The near-complete verse-level overlap between Genesis 3 and Moses 4 is evidence of a close literary dependency between the two texts, but it does not by itself establish whether Moses 4 represents restored ancient content independent of the King James Version or an inspired revision/expansion built on the existing KJV wording.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish the historical/textual origin of the Book of Moses; overlap percentage alone cannot distinguish 'restoration of ancient text' from 'revision of the KJV.'

**Alternative explanations:**
- The JST/Book of Moses process is understood by many LDS scholars as inspired revision and expansion of the existing KJV base text, which would produce exactly this pattern of high verbal overlap with added material, independent of any separate ancient manuscript.
- Alternatively, believers may hold the overlap reflects substantial preservation of an underlying ancient text that the KJV translators rendered accurately in the overlapping portions.
