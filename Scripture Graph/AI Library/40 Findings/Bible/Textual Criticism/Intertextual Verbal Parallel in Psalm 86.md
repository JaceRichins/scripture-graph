# Intertextual Verbal Parallel in Psalm 86

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Psalm 86:9's hope that 'all nations... shall come and worship before thee' is echoed almost verbatim in Revelation 15:4's eschatological vision, where the redeemed sing that 'all nations shall come and worship before thee.'

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that the author of Revelation intended readers to recall Psalm 86's specific individual-lament setting, only that closely matching wording appears in both texts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The shared phrasing ('come and worship before thee,' 'glorify thy name') may reflect common liturgical/hymnic stock language about eschatological nations-worship (cf. Psalm 22:27) rather than a deliberate citation of Psalm 86 specifically.
- John of Patmos may be drawing on a broader stream of psalmic and prophetic universal-worship language rather than this psalm in particular.
