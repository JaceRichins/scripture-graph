# Textual-Comparison in Exodus 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Exodus 20:11 grounds the sabbath commandment in the Creation week (God rested the seventh day), whereas the parallel Decalogue in Deuteronomy 5:15 grounds the same commandment in the memory of deliverance from Egyptian bondage. The two accounts give different theological warrants for the identical command.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish separate authorship or dates for Exodus and Deuteronomy; it only documents a real difference in the stated rationale for the same command.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both rationales may be viewed as complementary rather than competing (creation rest and redemption both model rest), a harmonizing reading common in devotional commentary.
- The variation could reflect two originally distinct recitation traditions later preserved in different books rather than a single author choosing among motives.
