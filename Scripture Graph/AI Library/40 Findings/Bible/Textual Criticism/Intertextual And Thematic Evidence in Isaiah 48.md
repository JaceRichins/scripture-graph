# Intertextual And Thematic Evidence in Isaiah 48

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The consecutive references to departure from Babylon, divine leading through deserts, thirstlessness, and water from the rock constitute strong internal evidence for a deliberate new-exodus portrayal of deliverance.

**Does not establish:** The imagery does not establish that the departure from Babylon followed every detail of the Mosaic exodus literally or that verse 21 is a separate historical report of the Babylonian return.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may simply reuse familiar wilderness traditions without constructing a comprehensive new-exodus pattern.
- Verse 21 may recall the original exodus as evidence of divine power rather than predict that every feature will recur literally.
