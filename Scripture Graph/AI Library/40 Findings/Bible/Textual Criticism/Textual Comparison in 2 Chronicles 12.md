# Textual Comparison in 2 Chronicles 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared sequence in 1 Kings 14 and 2 Chronicles 12—Shishak's attack, removal of temple and palace treasures, loss of Solomon's gold shields, brass replacements, and the guard procedure—is strong evidence of a literary or traditional relationship between the accounts. Because Chronicles may have used Kings or a shared source, the agreement is not automatically independent historical corroboration.

**Does not establish:** The verbal and narrative agreement does not establish that the two accounts are independent witnesses or prove every reported historical detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may directly adapt the account in Kings.
- Both works may draw on a shared court or historical tradition.
