# Literary And Intertextual Evidence in John 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus' declaration "Before Abraham was, I am" clearly asserts priority to Abraham within the narrative. Its unusual wording, the absolute "I am," and the ensuing attempt to stone Jesus strongly support a divine-identity reading and a possible allusion to biblical divine self-identification. The exact dependence on Exodus 3:14, however, is debated.

**Does not establish:** The wording alone does not establish every later metaphysical formulation about Christ's nature or prove a direct verbal quotation of Exodus 3:14.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The saying may assert personal preexistence without functioning as a direct quotation of the divine name in Exodus 3:14.
- The attempted stoning may respond to the total perceived claim and confrontation rather than to one identifiable divine-name formula.
- The Greek expression translated "I am" can occur in non-divine contexts, so the phrase alone is not decisive.
