# Internal Textual Coherence in Joshua 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The borders are presented as an interlocking territorial system: Asher's boundary reaches Zebulun, while Naphtali reaches Zebulun on the south and Asher on the west. This internal coordination supports reading the chapter as more than a collection of wholly isolated town lists.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every boundary was surveyed at one time, continuously occupied, or historically implemented exactly as described.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor could have harmonized separate lists into a coherent territorial scheme.
- The borders may preserve an idealized administrative geography rather than boundaries consistently controlled in practice.
- Some place names and directional expressions remain too uncertain for complete modern reconstruction.

**Scripture:** [[Joshua 19#^josh-19-27|Joshua 19:27]], [[Joshua 19#^josh-19-34|Joshua 19:34]]
