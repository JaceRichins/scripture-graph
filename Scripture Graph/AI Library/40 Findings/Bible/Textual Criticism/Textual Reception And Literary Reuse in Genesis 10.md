# Textual Reception And Literary Reuse in Genesis 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
First Chronicles 1:5-23 preserves much of the same sequence of names found in Genesis 10. This is strong evidence for the list's later canonical reuse and continuing importance as an account of national ancestry, but it is not independent corroboration of every historical identification.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not provide two independent witnesses proving that all the relationships are historically or biologically exact.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may depend directly on Genesis or on a common received genealogical tradition.
- Differences between the lists may reflect textual variants, scribal transmission, or deliberate abbreviation.
