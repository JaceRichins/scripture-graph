# Intertextual And Titular in Revelation 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of Judah-lion and Davidic-root titles, followed by the appearance of the Lamb, provides strong textual evidence that the Lamb is being presented as the promised Davidic Messiah.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish direct quotation from one source, a complete theory of Messiahship, or the date and provenance of the composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author may be using inherited Christian titles without intending direct literary dependence on particular passages.
- Lion, root, and lamb imagery may draw on a wider network of Jewish and early Christian traditions rather than a single identifiable source.
