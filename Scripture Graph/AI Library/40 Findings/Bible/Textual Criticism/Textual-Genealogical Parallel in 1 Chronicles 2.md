# Textual-Genealogical Parallel in 1 Chronicles 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence from Ram to David closely parallels Ruth 4:19-22, with the minor name-form difference Salma/Salmon. This strongly supports a shared genealogical tradition or literary relationship between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish which text is earlier, the independence of either witness, or the historical completeness of the genealogy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may depend directly or indirectly on Ruth.
- Both passages may draw upon a common genealogical source.
- The sequence may have circulated as a conventional Davidic lineage before either surviving text.
