# Literary Intertextuality in Ephesians 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The paired correspondences of righteousness with a breastplate and salvation with a helmet provide strong textual evidence that Ephesians 6 participates in the prophetic armor imagery of Isaiah 59:17; the association of feet, good news, and peace also recalls Isaiah 52:7.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish that every item derives from Isaiah, that only one background influenced the metaphor, or any larger conclusion about the epistle's authorship or date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Armor was a broadly intelligible metaphor, so some correspondences could arise from conventional imagery.
- A contemporary military setting may also have shaped the presentation.
- The link to Isaiah 52:7 is less verbally exact than the link to Isaiah 59:17.
