# Textual And Narrative Correspondence in Deuteronomy 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The numerous specific correspondences between Deuteronomy 9 and Exodus 32 strongly support a literary or shared-traditional relationship between the accounts.

**Does not establish:** The correspondences alone do not determine the direction of dependence, the exact compositional process, Mosaic authorship, or the historicity of every detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Deuteronomy may adapt a form of the tradition preserved in Exodus.
- Exodus and Deuteronomy may draw on a shared earlier tradition.
- The surviving texts may reflect a more complex history of mutual influence and editorial development.
