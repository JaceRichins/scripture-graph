# Contextual Literary Evidence in Isaiah 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Isaiah 28, the repeated "precept upon precept" and "line upon line" formula has a judicial edge. The people refuse the offered rest, and the formula recurs immediately before their falling, breaking, ensnaring, and capture.

**Does not establish:** This context does not establish that every later positive use of "line upon line" is mistaken or that progressive revelation is an invalid principle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Verses 9-10 may preserve the prophet's own description of elementary, incremental instruction rather than the leaders' mocking imitation of it.
- The formula can express a valid principle of progressive instruction even if its effect on these particular hearers is judicial.
