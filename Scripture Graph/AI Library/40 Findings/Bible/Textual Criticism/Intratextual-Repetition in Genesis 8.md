# Intratextual-Repetition in Genesis 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The near repetition of the evil-heart motif before and after the Flood strongly supports reading Genesis 8:21 as a deliberate reassessment of how God will govern a still-corrupt humanity, rather than as evidence that the Flood eradicated sin.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not establish a complete doctrine of inherited sin, human moral incapacity, or the precise scope of the Flood.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may primarily explain why periodic destruction would be futile rather than articulate a developed theology of mercy.
- Different models of Genesis's composition may attribute the related statements to editorial arrangement rather than a single author's design.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 6#^gen-6-5|Genesis 6:5-7]], [[Genesis 8#^gen-8-21|Genesis 8:21]]
