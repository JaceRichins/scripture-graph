# Ecclesiological Textual Evidence in Acts 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 6 describes the seven as appointed over a particular business but never calls them deacons. The episode can inform later discussions of church offices, yet identifying it as the formal institution of the diaconate goes beyond what this chapter explicitly states.

**Does not establish:** The absence of the title does not prove that the seven had no relationship to the later diaconate; it only prevents that identification from being claimed as explicit.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The functions and commissioning of the seven may represent an early form of an office later known as deacon.
- Luke may assume terminology or institutional knowledge that he does not explicitly supply here.

**Scripture:** [[Acts 6#^acts-6-2|Acts 6:2-6]]
