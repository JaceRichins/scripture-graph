# Intratextual Narrative Recall in Genesis 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Later Genesis passages explicitly recall Jacob's Bethel pillar and vow and narrate his return there, providing strong evidence that Genesis 28 functions as a deliberate foundation for later episodes in Jacob's story.

**Does not establish:** These narrative callbacks do not establish single authorship, eyewitness transmission, or the historicity of the events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The cross-references may reflect editorial coordination of originally distinct traditions.
- Later passages may adapt Genesis 28 rather than preserve independent memories of the same event.
