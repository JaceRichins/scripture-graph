# Literary And Intertextual Evidence in 2 Chronicles 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of temple-prayer language, exodus-deliverance language, Deuteronomy's territorial commands, and a conventional worship refrain is strong evidence that the account frames the crisis through established Israelite scriptural and liturgical traditions.

**Does not establish:** These parallels do not independently establish the battle's historical details, the mechanism of the attackers' defeat, or a precise date for the account's composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some similarities may reflect common Israelite religious vocabulary rather than conscious citation of particular written passages.
- A later narrator or editor could have shaped an older account with familiar scriptural language.
- The parallels demonstrate literary relationship or shared tradition more readily than they identify when each formulation arose.
