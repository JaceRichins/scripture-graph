# Internal Textual Evidence in Jeremiah 44

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As literary evidence, the reported reply strongly supports the conclusion that the chapter intentionally presents a contested interpretation of recent history: the Judeans appeal to remembered material outcomes, and Jeremiah directly reverses their causal explanation.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not independently establish the historical accuracy of either causal account, the precise words spoken, or a general rule that temporal prosperity reliably identifies true worship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The reported speech may be shaped by the narrator for a theological or rhetorical purpose rather than preserving the speakers' words verbatim.
- The people's inference may reflect selective memory or a post hoc association rather than a demonstrated causal relationship.
- Material conditions could have had political, military, and economic causes that neither side's theological argument attempts to describe exhaustively.
