# Internal Textual Parallel in Proverbs 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Proverbs 18:8 and Proverbs 26:22 contain the same KJV wording, providing strong internal evidence that a single saying appears in two locations within Proverbs.

**Does not establish:** The repetition does not establish which occurrence is earlier, who composed the saying, or whether one biblical location directly copied the other.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have placed the same traditional saying in two collections.
- The repetition may reflect independent preservation of a commonly circulated proverb.
- Identical English wording can obscure any minor distinctions in the underlying textual history.
