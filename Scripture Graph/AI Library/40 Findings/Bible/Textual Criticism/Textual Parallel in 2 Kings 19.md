# Textual Parallel in 2 Kings 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 37 preserves an extensive parallel to the entire thirty-seven-verse narrative of 2 Kings 19. The close correspondence is strong evidence of literary dependence or a shared textual tradition, but the two versions should not be counted as fully independent witnesses to the events.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of dependence, the date of the shared material, or independent historical corroboration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah may depend on Kings.
- Kings may depend on an Isaianic narrative.
- Both books may draw on an earlier common source that was subsequently edited.

**Scripture:** [[2 Kings 19]], [[Isaiah 37]]
