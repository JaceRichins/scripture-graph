# Textual Overlap in 1 John 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moroni 7:48 closely parallels 1 John 3:2-3 by linking Christ's future appearance, becoming like him, seeing him as he is, hope, and purification. This is a strong and study-relevant English textual parallel, although the parallel by itself cannot determine the direction or historical mechanism of the relationship.

**Does not establish:** The overlap does not by itself establish ancient authorship, independence from the King James Bible, direct dependence in either direction, or a specific common ancient source.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Book of Mormon English may deliberately echo the King James wording of 1 John.
- The texts may express a shared Christian hope through familiar biblical language.
- A believer may understand the parallel as reflecting a common revealed doctrine rather than direct literary dependence.
