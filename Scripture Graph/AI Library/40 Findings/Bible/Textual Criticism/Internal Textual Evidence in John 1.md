# Internal Textual Evidence in John 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 1 provides strong internal textual evidence that the Gospel portrays Jesus as preexistent and divine: the Word is placed before creation and credited with creating all things, becomes flesh, and is identified in the ensuing exposition with Jesus Christ.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove the metaphysical claims it proclaims, establish the historical circumstances of composition, or resolve later debates about the precise relationship between the Father and the Son.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may be read as theological proclamation rather than independently corroborated metaphysical history.
- John's statement that Jesus was 'before' him could denote rank if considered alone, although the creation language of the prologue strongly favors preexistence within the Gospel's presentation.

**Scripture:** [[John 1#^john-1-1|John 1:1-3]], [[John 1#^john-1-14|John 1:14-18]], [[John 1#^john-1-30|John 1:30]]
