# Scriptural Reception And Intertextuality in Isaiah 65

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Explicit attribution in Romans and distinctive new-creation parallels in later Christian scripture provide strong evidence that Isaiah 65 became an important source for Christian reflection on Gentile inclusion, divine responsiveness, and eschatological renewal.

**Does not establish:** Literary reception does not by itself establish that any one later application exhausts Isaiah's meaning or proves a specific fulfillment chronology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- For 2 Peter and Revelation, some wording may derive from broader Isaianic tradition rather than exclusive dependence on Isaiah 65.
- Later authors can adapt an earlier passage to new settings rather than reproduce its original meaning in full.
