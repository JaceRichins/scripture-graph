# Textual Criticism in Matthew 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 17:21 ('Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting') is widely treated by textual critics as absent from the earliest extant Greek manuscript tradition of Matthew, likely a scribal harmonization drawn from the near-identical statement at Mark 9:29; many modern critical editions and translations bracket, footnote, or omit the verse.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish that the teaching about prayer and fasting is doctrinally false — only that this particular verse's presence in Matthew's earliest text is textually uncertain; the same content is attested at Mark 9:29 in a form with its own similar textual questions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verse could be an authentic saying independently omitted by some early copyists for unrelated reasons (e.g., eye-skip, deliberate abridgement).
- The KJV and Textus Receptus tradition include it, so its 'disputed' status is itself a product of which manuscript family a given translation follows.
