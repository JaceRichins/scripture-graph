# Intertextual Reception in Micah 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 26:18 preserves a close verbal parallel to Micah 3:12 and explicitly attributes the prophecy to Micah the Morasthite. This is strong evidence of the warning's reception as a prophecy of Micah within the canonical Jeremiah narrative.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the precise date or original wording of Micah 3, nor does it independently verify every historical detail reported in Jeremiah 26.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The literary form of Jeremiah 26 may reflect later editorial shaping even if it preserves an older recollection.
- The parallel establishes literary or traditional dependence but does not by itself determine every stage of textual transmission.
