# TextualLinguistic Scholarship in Daniel 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrator marks a language shift at Daniel 2:4, noting the Chaldeans spoke 'in Syriack' (the KJV's rendering of Aramaic); in the underlying Hebrew Bible text, Daniel 2:4b through the end of Daniel 7 is actually composed in biblical Aramaic rather than Hebrew, making this one of the longest sustained Aramaic sections in the Old Testament. The KJV's English translation obscures this shift for modern readers even though it explicitly labels the language.

**Does not establish:** Why the author switched languages at this point (proposed reasons include source-compilation history, audience targeting, or literary framing of 'foreign' court speech), which remains debated.
