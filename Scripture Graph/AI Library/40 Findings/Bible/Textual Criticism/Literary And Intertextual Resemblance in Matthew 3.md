# Literary And Intertextual Resemblance in Matthew 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clothing correspondence between John and Elijah is meaningful literary evidence for Elijah typology, but clothing alone is not decisive proof of conscious imitation or authorial intent.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that John deliberately copied Elijah's clothing, that Matthew 3:4 was composed solely to evoke Elijah, or that every aspect of John's ministry fulfills an Elijah expectation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The clothing may represent austere prophetic life generally rather than Elijah specifically.
- The resemblance may reflect conventional wilderness attire.
- Readers may perceive a stronger allusion than the author intended.

**Scripture:** [[Matthew 3#^matt-3-4|Matthew 3:4]], [[2 Kings 1#^2kgs-1-8|2 Kings 1:8]]
