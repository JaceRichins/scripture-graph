# Intertextual List Parallel in 1 Chronicles 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clan-by-clan city distribution in 1 Chronicles 6 closely parallels Joshua 21. This strongly supports a literary or traditional relationship between the lists, but the parallel cannot function as independent corroboration unless their source relationship is first established.

**Does not establish:** The overlap does not by itself prove independent historical confirmation, determine which text is earlier, or show that every listed settlement was occupied simultaneously.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may depend directly or indirectly on Joshua.
- Both passages may draw on a shared administrative or literary source.
- Later editorial harmonization may have increased their similarity.

**Scripture:** [[1 Chronicles 6#^1chr-6-54|1 Chronicles 6:54-81]], [[Joshua 21#^josh-21-1|Joshua 21:1-42]]
