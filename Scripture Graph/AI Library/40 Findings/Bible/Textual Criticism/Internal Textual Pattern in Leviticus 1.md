# Internal Textual Pattern in Leviticus 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The availability of herd, flock, and bird offerings is strong internal evidence that the burnt-offering rite could be performed with more than one class of animal. It provides only limited evidence for the further claim that the alternatives constitute a deliberate economic accommodation, because this chapter never states why the options are offered.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the worshippers' relative wealth, the prices of the animals, or an explicit policy of economic inclusion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The alternatives may reflect differences in animal availability rather than an explicit poverty scale.
- The sequence may primarily organize ritual rules by animal type.
- Different species may have carried ritual meanings not explained in this chapter.
