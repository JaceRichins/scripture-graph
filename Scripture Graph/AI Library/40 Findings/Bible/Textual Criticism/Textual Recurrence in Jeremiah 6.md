# Textual Recurrence in Jeremiah 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 6:13-15 and Jeremiah 8:10-12 contain a closely repeated sequence about universal covetousness, false prophets and priests, superficial healing, false peace, absence of shame, and an appointed fall. The extensive recurrence is strong evidence of a direct literary relationship between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence alone does not establish which passage came first, whether Jeremiah or an editor repeated it, or the date and compositional history of either context.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah may have deliberately reused an established prophetic indictment in more than one setting.
- An editor or compiler may have repeated the material while arranging separate collections.
- Both passages may preserve adaptations of a shared earlier oracle.
