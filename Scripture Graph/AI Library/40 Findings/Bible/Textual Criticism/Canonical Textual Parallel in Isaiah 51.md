# Canonical Textual Parallel in Isaiah 51

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The text of Isaiah 51:1-23 has a close chapter-length parallel in 2 Nephi 8:1-23. This is strong evidence that 2 Nephi 8 presents and reuses this Isaianic discourse.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the historical direction or mechanism of textual transmission, the antiquity of the Book of Mormon translation, or the originality of particular variants.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Competing historical models explain the English-language correspondence differently, including ancient-scriptural transmission and modern dependence on the King James Bible.
- Individual wording differences require separate analysis and should not be treated automatically as ancient textual variants.
