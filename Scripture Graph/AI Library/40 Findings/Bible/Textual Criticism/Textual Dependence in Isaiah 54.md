# Textual Dependence in Isaiah 54

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter-wide verbal correspondence between Isaiah 54 and 3 Nephi 22 is strong evidence of a deliberate literary relationship: the entire seventeen-verse chapter has a corresponding seventeen-verse presentation in 3 Nephi 22.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not establish the historical authenticity, date, or transmission history of the Book of Mormon account; those larger conclusions require independent evidence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The relationship may be described as direct quotation, adaptation, or dependence on a shared scriptural textual tradition.
- Differences between the chapters may reflect transmission, translation, or purposeful adaptation rather than independent composition.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 54#^isa-54-1|Isaiah 54:1-17]], [[3 Nephi 22#^3ne-22-1|3 Nephi 22:1-17]]
