# Intratextual Narrative Evidence in Numbers 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, the announced condition and its immediate fulfillment function as evidence for Moses's divine commission. This is strong evidence for what the narrator intends readers to conclude, but the narrative report alone cannot independently verify the event historically.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historicity, supernatural causation, date of composition, or Mosaic authorship of the account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The episode may be a literary authentication story shaped to defend Mosaic authority.
- A historical disaster could have been interpreted and narrated as divine judgment.
- Later transmission or editing could have strengthened the prediction-fulfillment form.

**Scripture:** [[Numbers 16#^num-16-28|Numbers 16:28-33]]
