# Internal Textual Structure in Jeremiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's explicit if-then formulation strongly supports reading continued residence in the land as conditional on repentance, just judgment, protection of vulnerable people, avoidance of bloodshed, and rejection of other gods.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that repentance mechanically guarantees temporal safety to every individual or that ritual worship has no legitimate place.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The promise may function primarily as prophetic exhortation rather than as a comprehensive statement of covenant theology.
- The phrase about dwelling in the land may refer to national continuity, not a guarantee that every repentant individual would escape hardship.
