# Repeated Textual Formulation in Genesis 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated references to every living creature and all flesh provide strong textual evidence that the flood promise has a creature-inclusive scope, not merely a human one.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish animal salvation, a comprehensive environmental ethic, or that every biblical covenant has the same scope.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The creature language may define the beneficiaries of protection from another worldwide flood without assigning animals an independent covenantal role.
- The universal wording may function rhetorically within the narrative rather than as a complete theology of humanity's relationship with the natural world.
