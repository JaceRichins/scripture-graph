# Synoptic Textual Parallel in 2 Chronicles 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The substantial overlap with 2 Kings 8 supports the conclusion that the two accounts preserve a shared regnal tradition, but because literary dependence or use of common sources is likely, the overlap should not be treated as fully independent corroboration of every event.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish complete historical independence, the direction of literary dependence, or the historicity of details unique to Chronicles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may depend directly on Kings.
- Both books may draw upon one or more common royal records.
- The shared material may combine written sources with established historical tradition.
