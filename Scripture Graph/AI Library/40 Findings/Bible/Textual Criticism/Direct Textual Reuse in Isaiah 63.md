# Direct Textual Reuse in Isaiah 63

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 133:46-51 consciously reuses and expands the wording and sequence of Isaiah 63:1-6, placing the passage in an explicit latter-day scene of the Lord's glorious return.

**Does not establish:** The reuse does not by itself establish the historical setting, original date, or exclusive original meaning of Isaiah 63.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The revelation may be applying Isaiah typologically rather than asserting that every detail had only one original referent.
