# Textual Intertext in Psalm 86

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Psalm 86:15 closely parallels the divine-attributes formula in Exodus 34:6-7. The cluster of compassion, graciousness, longsuffering, mercy, and truth strongly supports a literary or traditional relationship, while the differences caution against calling Psalm 86:15 an exact quotation.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish direct quotation, the date of composition, authorship, or a unique historical setting.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may employ a widely circulated liturgical formula without direct borrowing by the author of Psalm 86.
- A later editor or transmitter may have shaped the wording.
- Shared vocabulary alone cannot determine the direction of dependence.
