# Textual Underdetermination in 2 Corinthians 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Paul's repeated refusal to specify whether the ascent was bodily or out of the body is strong textual evidence that the passage leaves the mode of the experience unresolved.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not establish a general doctrine about disembodied travel, the constitution of the soul, or the physical geography of heaven.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be using conventional language of visionary uncertainty rather than reporting literal uncertainty about the experience.
- The repeated qualification may chiefly serve his rhetoric of modesty.

**Scripture:** [[2 Corinthians 12#^2cor-12-2|2 Corinthians 12:2]], [[2 Corinthians 12#^2cor-12-3|2 Corinthians 12:3]]
