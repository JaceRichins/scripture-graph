# Intertextual Convergence in Revelation 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of Edenic tree-and-curse imagery with Ezekiel's river-and-healing-tree imagery is strong evidence that Revelation 22 presents the final city as the literary and theological consummation of earlier biblical hopes.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the vision's historical fulfillment, a literal geography or botany, the date of composition, or divine authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author may be reusing familiar scriptural symbols without intending a comprehensive Eden-restoration scheme.
- Some details may derive primarily from Ezekiel 47 rather than independently from Genesis.
- The convergence demonstrates literary composition and scriptural reuse, which can be explained under differing views of inspiration and authorship.
