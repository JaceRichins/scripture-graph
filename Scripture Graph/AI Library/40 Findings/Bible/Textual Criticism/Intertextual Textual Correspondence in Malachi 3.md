# Intertextual Textual Correspondence in Malachi 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The text of 3 Nephi 24 substantially reproduces Malachi 3 and explicitly presents the material as words given by the Father to Malachi. This is strong evidence of deliberate literary reuse within the Book of Mormon text, but textual correspondence by itself cannot determine the historical mechanism or direction of dependence.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish Book of Mormon historicity, the date of the underlying Nephite record, or whether the shared English wording reproduces ancient textual form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Within the Book of Mormon narrative, Jesus supplies Malachi's words because the Nephite records lacked them.
- A nineteenth-century-origin model explains the correspondence as direct dependence on the King James Bible.
- The English wording could reflect translation into familiar biblical idiom rather than the precise wording of an underlying ancient-language text.
