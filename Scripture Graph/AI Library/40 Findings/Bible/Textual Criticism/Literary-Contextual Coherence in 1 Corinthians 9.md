# Literary-Contextual Coherence in 1 Corinthians 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's sustained movement from legitimate rights to voluntary restraint strongly supports reading it as part of the broader argument in 1 Corinthians 8–10 about limiting freedom when its exercise may obstruct another person's spiritual welfare.

**Does not establish:** The thematic coherence does not by itself settle every question about the letter's composition or identify the precise historical circumstances behind each objection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chapter 9 also independently answers challenges to Paul's apostleship and financial practice.
- Some details of the apostolic defense may reflect a distinct controversy even though they serve the surrounding argument.
