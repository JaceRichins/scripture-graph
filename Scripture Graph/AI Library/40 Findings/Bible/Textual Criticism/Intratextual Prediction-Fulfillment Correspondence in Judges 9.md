# Intratextual Prediction-Fulfillment Correspondence in Judges 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jotham predicts reciprocal destruction between Abimelech and Shechem. Abimelech later burns the tower of Shechem, producing a concrete echo of the predicted fire, and the narrator declares that Jotham's curse came upon the Shechemites. Abimelech's own death, however, is not a literal death by fire or directly at the hands of the men of Shechem.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish predictive supernatural foreknowledge or an exact clause-by-clause fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may result from retrospective literary shaping of the narrative.
- Fire is a natural siege method, so its occurrence alone would not demonstrate supernatural prediction.
- The second half of the curse may be understood broadly through the rebellion's consequences rather than as a precise literal fulfillment.
