# Textual Categorization in Leviticus 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of the bat in the fowl list and the description of flying creeping things as going upon all four indicate that the chapter's categories and body-part terminology should not be assumed to correspond directly to modern zoological classification.

**Does not establish:** This observation does not by itself resolve debates about translation, ancient natural knowledge, or theories of scriptural inerrancy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some apparent classification differences may arise from the English translation rather than from the underlying Hebrew terms.
- The expressions may describe ordinary appearance and locomotion without intending a comprehensive taxonomy.
