# Intertextual Verbal And Thematic Parallel in Jeremiah 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 5:15 closely parallels the covenant-curse language of Deuteronomy 28:49: both describe a nation brought from far away whose language Judah or Israel cannot understand. This strongly supports reading the announced invasion through a Deuteronomic covenant-curse framework.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the direction of literary dependence, the date of composition, or the precise historical identity of the invading nation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may draw independently on conventional ancient invasion imagery.
- Shared wording can demonstrate literary relationship or common tradition without determining which text influenced the other.
- English translation choices may make the verbal similarity appear closer than it is in the underlying Hebrew.
