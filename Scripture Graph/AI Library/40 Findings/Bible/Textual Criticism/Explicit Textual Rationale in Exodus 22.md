# Explicit Textual Rationale in Exodus 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit reasons given for protecting strangers and poor debtors—Israel's experience in Egypt, God's attention to cries, and God's gracious character—are strong evidence that the text grounds care for vulnerable people in theology as well as in social regulation.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish Israelite compliance, the historical frequency of enforcement, or that every vulnerable group received equal protection in practice.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The stated rationales may function rhetorically to authorize laws without describing actual social practice.
- The protections remain situated within an ancient hierarchical society and do not articulate modern legal equality.
