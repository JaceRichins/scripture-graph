# Intra-Textual Repetition in 1 Chronicles 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Eleazar-to-Ahimaaz segment of Aaron's genealogy appears both near the beginning and again immediately after the description of Aaronic duties. This repetition is strong evidence of compositional emphasis on Aaronic descent, but its precise source or editorial purpose cannot be determined from the chapter alone.

**Does not establish:** Repetition does not establish that the genealogy is complete, biologically immediate at every link, or independently corroborated by two separate witnesses.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may deliberately frame or recap the section on priestly duties.
- A compiler may have preserved overlapping material from separate genealogical sources.
- The repeated list may provide a transition into the allocation of Aaronic cities.

**Scripture:** [[1 Chronicles 6#^1chr-6-4|1 Chronicles 6:4-8]], [[1 Chronicles 6#^1chr-6-49|1 Chronicles 6:49-53]]
