# Direct Textual Statement in Genesis 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Genesis 1:27 explicitly places the creation of male and female within its statement that humankind was created in God's image. This strongly supports the textual conclusion that the divine-image designation includes both, while leaving the precise meaning of that image undefined.

**Does not establish:** The verse does not define the metaphysical nature of God or provide a complete doctrine of gender and social roles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Interpretations differ over whether the image refers chiefly to human nature, vocation, relationship, representation, or some combination.
- The verse does not settle every question about distinctions, social roles, or embodiment.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 1#^gen-1-27|Genesis 1:27]]
