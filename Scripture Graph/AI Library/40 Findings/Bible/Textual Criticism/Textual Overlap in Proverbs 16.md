# Textual Overlap in Proverbs 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Proverbs 16:25 closely parallels Proverbs 14:12, supporting the conclusion that the book preserves repeated or shared proverbial material. The parallel alone cannot determine whether one placement copied the other or whether both derive from an earlier common tradition.

**Does not establish:** The overlap does not establish direction of dependence, a particular author, a date of composition, or the reason for both placements.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler may have intentionally repeated the saying in two collections.
- Both occurrences may independently preserve a familiar oral proverb.
- The repetition may reflect overlapping source collections incorporated into Proverbs.
