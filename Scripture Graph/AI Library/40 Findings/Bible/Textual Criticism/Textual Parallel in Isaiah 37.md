# Textual Parallel in Isaiah 37

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The verified overlap between Isaiah 37 and 2 Kings 19 establishes an extensive literary relationship between the two chapter accounts.

**Does not establish:** The overlap by itself does not determine the direction of borrowing, identify the earliest form of the account, or prove the historicity of every reported detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah may depend literarily on Kings.
- Kings may depend literarily on an Isaianic account.
- Both may draw on a shared written or oral source.
- Later editorial work may have brought the accounts into close agreement.
