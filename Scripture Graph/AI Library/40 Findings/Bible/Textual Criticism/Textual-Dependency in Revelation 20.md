# Textual-Dependency in Revelation 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 128 (Joseph Smith's 1842 letter on baptism for the dead) draws directly on Revelation 20:12's language of "books" and "the book of life" being opened and the dead judged "according to their works" to argue for the necessity of earthly ordinance records; the verified index shows a genuine 2-verse text overlap between the two documents, indicating direct literary dependence rather than mere topical similarity.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that D&C 128's doctrinal application (vicarious ordinance records) reflects John's original intent in Revelation 20:12, only that Joseph Smith directly drew on this verse's wording.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Joseph Smith may be alluding to common biblical judgment imagery generally rather than this specific verse, though the phrase-level match is close.
