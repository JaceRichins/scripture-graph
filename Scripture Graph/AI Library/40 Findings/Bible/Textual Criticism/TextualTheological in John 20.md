# TextualTheological in John 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Thomas responds to Jesus directly with 'My Lord and my God' (John 20:28), a second-person address ascribing the title 'God' to Jesus, and the text records no rebuke or correction from Jesus for this address.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish a fully developed doctrine of the Trinity or of the Godhead's nature; it establishes only that Thomas addressed Jesus as 'God' and was not corrected.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some read the exclamation as spontaneous astonishment rather than a formal theological confession
- Traditions differ on what doctrine of God the statement is best read to support (e.g., Trinitarian formulations develop later than the first century; a Latter-day Saint reading of 'Godhead' as distinct personages differs from later creedal formulations) — the verse itself does not adjudicate between these frameworks
