# Textual-Comparative in Mark 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mark's version of the cup-saying ('shed for many') omits the phrase 'for the remission of sins' found in Matthew's parallel account of the same words, a checkable textual difference between the two Gospels.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which wording is closer to what was historically said at the meal; it only documents a verifiable difference between the two Gospel texts as we have them.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Mark may simply summarize more tersely, assuming the remission-of-sins connection is understood from wider gospel teaching rather than intentionally omitting it
- Matthew may be the one who expanded a shorter core saying for catechetical clarity, rather than Mark abbreviating a longer original

**Scripture:** [[Mark 14#^mark-14-24|Mark 14:24]], [[Matthew 26#^matt-26-28|Matthew 26:28]]
