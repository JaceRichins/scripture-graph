# Lexical And Contextual Evidence in Nehemiah 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Nehemiah 8:7–8 clearly depicts mediated instruction, not bare recitation alone. The wording establishes that the Levites helped the people understand, but it does not determine whether they translated the Hebrew into Aramaic, explained its meaning, divided the reading into units, or combined these activities.

**Does not establish:** The verse does not establish one exclusive teaching or translation procedure.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Levites may have translated Hebrew into the population's spoken Aramaic.
- They may have explained or expounded the law without translating it.
- The description may refer principally to clear, segmented oral reading.
- Translation, exposition, and careful reading may all have been involved.
