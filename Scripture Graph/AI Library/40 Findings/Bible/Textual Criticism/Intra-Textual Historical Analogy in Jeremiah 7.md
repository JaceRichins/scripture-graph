# Intra-Textual Historical Analogy in Jeremiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the oracle, the fate of Shiloh is offered as evidence that a place where God had set his name was not immune from judgment; the same outcome is threatened against the Jerusalem temple and Judah.

**Does not establish:** It does not identify the exact historical event at Shiloh, prove that every sanctuary will be destroyed, or reject temples as such.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The analogy may concern loss of divine protection without specifying the precise event by which Shiloh was judged.
- The passage may invoke a remembered theological lesson about Shiloh rather than provide a complete historical account of its destruction.
