# Intertextual And Narrative-Pattern Evidence in Acts 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Stephen's hearing parallels the trial tradition concerning Jesus: hostile witnesses present a charge involving the destruction of the holy place or temple. Together with the narrator's designation of Stephen's accusers as false witnesses, this supports a literary presentation of Stephen as following the pattern of Jesus' rejection.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not prove that every detail of Stephen's hearing was composed to imitate Jesus' trial or that the two cases were historically identical.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Temple-related accusations could arise independently because disputes about the temple and law were inherently serious.
- Some similarity may derive from recurring judicial procedures rather than deliberate literary imitation.
