# Extended Textual Parallel in Jeremiah 52

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 52 and the conclusion of 2 Kings share the same ordered account—from Zedekiah's reign and Jerusalem's fall through Jehoiachin's release—providing strong evidence of direct literary dependence or use of a common written source.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish which version came first, who composed the shared material, or when Jeremiah 52 was attached to the book.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah's editor may have copied a form of Kings.
- Kings may preserve material taken from a Jeremianic historical source.
- Both books may derive from a shared archival or historical source.
- A later editor may have supplied related versions to both books.
