# Textual Interconnection in Exodus 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
First Peter applies closely corresponding identity language—royal priesthood, holy nation, and peculiar people—to its Christian audience. This is strong evidence that Exodus 19:5–6 became a reusable biblical formulation of communal covenant vocation.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish that the church replaces Israel, nor does it settle how Israel's continuing covenant identity should be understood.

**Alternative explanations:**
- First Peter may draw on a broader scriptural complex that includes similar language in Deuteronomy rather than on Exodus 19 alone.
- The reuse does not require every aspect of Israel's Sinai vocation to be transferred unchanged to the later audience.
