# Textual And Narrative Complexity in Genesis 37

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The account refers to an Ishmeelite caravan, Midianite merchantmen at the pit, a sale to the Ishmeelites, and a final sale by the Midianites. This variation is genuine evidence of textual complexity, but it is not sufficient on its own to establish one particular source-critical reconstruction.

**Does not establish:** The variation does not by itself prove a contradiction, identify the sellers with certainty, or validate a specific documentary-source division.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ishmeelite and Midianite may function as overlapping or loosely applied group designations in this account.
- Midianite merchants may have belonged to, accompanied, or interacted with the larger Ishmeelite caravan.
- The variation may preserve the joining of distinct narrative traditions.
- Some ambiguity may result from the shifting antecedents of the pronoun translated as 'they' in verse 28.
