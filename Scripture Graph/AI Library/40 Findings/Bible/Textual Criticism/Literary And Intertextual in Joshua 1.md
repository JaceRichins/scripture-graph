# Literary And Intertextual in Joshua 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Joshua 1 closely parallels Moses's earlier public commissioning of Joshua: both associate courage with entering and inheriting the land, and both ground Joshua's confidence in the Lord's unfailing presence. This is strong internal evidence that the narrative deliberately presents Joshua as Moses's authorized successor.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the date, authorship, or historical circumstances of either passage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The similarities may partly reflect conventional commissioning language rather than a uniquely designed succession pattern.
- A compiler or editor could have shaped the passages to emphasize continuity between Moses and Joshua.
