# Comparative Textual Evidence in Deuteronomy 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The comparison with Exodus 20 provides strong textual evidence that Deuteronomy gives Sabbath observance a distinctive liberation-centered and socially protective emphasis.

**Does not establish:** The variation by itself does not establish the date, compositional history, authorship, or relative priority of Exodus and Deuteronomy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two rationales may be complementary applications rather than competing explanations.
- The variation may reflect adaptation to Deuteronomy's immediate sermonic context without indicating a different historical source.
