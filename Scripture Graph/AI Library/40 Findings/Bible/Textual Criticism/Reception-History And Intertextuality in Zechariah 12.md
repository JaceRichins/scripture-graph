# Reception-History And Intertextuality in Zechariah 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 19:37 and Revelation 1:7 provide strong evidence that early Christians read Zechariah's pierced figure christologically: John connects the wording with Jesus' death, while Revelation joins piercing, seeing, and worldwide mourning to Christ's appearing.

**Does not establish:** The reception establishes an early Christian interpretation, not universal agreement, the exact compositional intention of Zechariah, or proof of Jesus' identity independent of broader historical and theological arguments.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The New Testament writers may be giving a typological or theological application rather than identifying Zechariah's sole original historical referent.
- The pronoun shift and textual variants permit more than one account of the pierced figure within Zechariah itself.
- Jewish interpretations commonly identify the pierced or mourned figure without reference to Jesus.
