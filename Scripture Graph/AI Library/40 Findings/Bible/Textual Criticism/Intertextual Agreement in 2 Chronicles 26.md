# Intertextual Agreement in 2 Chronicles 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The agreements between 2 Chronicles 26 and 2 Kings 15 support the existence of a stable shared regnal tradition about Uzziah, especially concerning his long reign, affliction, Jotham's administrative role, and succession.

**Does not establish:** The overlap does not establish independent attestation, the precise historical cause of the affliction, or the historicity of every detail unique to Chronicles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may depend directly or indirectly on Kings rather than preserve an independent witness.
- Both accounts may draw on a common royal or literary source.
- Later scribal transmission may have reinforced agreement between the accounts.
