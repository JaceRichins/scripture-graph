# Textual And Contextual in Ezekiel 37

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter supports an interpretation of the two sticks as symbols of Judah and Joseph/Ephraim becoming one people. Although both sticks are written upon, the chapter itself never identifies them as the Bible and the Book of Mormon. That identification is a later theological interpretation rather than an explicit statement of Ezekiel 37.

**Does not establish:** It does not disprove a typological or revelatory application to the Bible and Book of Mormon; it establishes only that this application is not the explicit interpretation given within the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The required inscriptions may permit a secondary typological application to written records.
- Latter-day Saint interpretation can treat the political reunion and the joining of scriptural witnesses as complementary fulfillments.
