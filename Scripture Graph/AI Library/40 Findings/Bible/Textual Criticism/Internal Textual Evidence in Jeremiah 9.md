# Internal Textual Evidence in Jeremiah 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Judah's inclusion in the punishment oracle and Israel's description as uncircumcised in heart provide strong textual evidence that outward covenant status does not, in this passage, guarantee exemption from judgment when inward fidelity is absent.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Jeremiah abolishes circumcision, rejects Israel's covenant, or treats Israel and the surrounding nations as identical in every respect.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may chiefly emphasize universal exposure to judgment rather than present a developed doctrine of inward covenant identity.
- The contrast does not require the conclusion that physical circumcision itself is rejected or regarded as meaningless.

**Scripture:** [[Jeremiah 9#^jer-9-25|Jeremiah 9:25]], [[Jeremiah 9#^jer-9-26|Jeremiah 9:26]]
