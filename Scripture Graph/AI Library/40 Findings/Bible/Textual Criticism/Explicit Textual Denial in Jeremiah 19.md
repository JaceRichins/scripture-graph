# Explicit Textual Denial in Jeremiah 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The three-part denial in verse 5 is strong textual evidence that the chapter categorically rejects child-burning as something authorized by the Lord.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish a comprehensive philosophical position about divine foreknowledge, possibility, or omniscience.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be emphatic or anthropomorphic rhetoric rather than a technical description of divine cognition.
