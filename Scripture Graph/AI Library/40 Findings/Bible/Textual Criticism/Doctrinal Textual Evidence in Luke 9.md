# Doctrinal Textual Evidence in Luke 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Luke 9 provides strong textual evidence for the narrower proposition that Jesus delegated religious authority to the Twelve for a particular mission. It does not by itself identify later priesthood offices, rules of succession, or a modern restoration of authority.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish priesthood succession, the form of later ecclesiastical offices, or claims concerning a latter-day restoration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The commission may be read as authority limited to this journey rather than a complete model for later church offices.
- Different traditions understand the relationship between apostolic authority and later priesthood structures differently.

**Scripture:** [[Luke 9#^luke-9-1|Luke 9:1]], [[Luke 9#^luke-9-2|Luke 9:2]]
