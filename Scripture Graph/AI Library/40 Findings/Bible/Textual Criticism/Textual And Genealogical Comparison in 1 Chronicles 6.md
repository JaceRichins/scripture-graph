# Textual And Genealogical Comparison in 1 Chronicles 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
A textual naming difficulty is visible when the passages are compared: 1 Chronicles 6:28 names Samuel's sons as the firstborn Vashni and Abiah, whereas 1 Samuel 8:2 names Joel as the firstborn and Abiah as the second; the Heman genealogy in 1 Chronicles itself includes Joel as the son of Shemuel. The data strongly justify investigating textual or translation history rather than forcing an effortless harmonization.

**Does not establish:** The comparison alone does not identify the precise stage at which the variation arose or prove that the chapter's larger genealogy is unreliable.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Vashni could be understood as a personal name in the textual form represented by the KJV.
- The difference may reflect copying, translation, or segmentation history.
- The genealogical notices may preserve variant source traditions.
