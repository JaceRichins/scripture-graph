# Direct Textual Correspondence in Isaiah 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained correspondence between Isaiah 6 and 2 Nephi 16 is strong textual evidence that 2 Nephi's Isaiah material incorporates or transmits this chapter.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence by itself does not determine the historical source, translation process, date of composition, or authenticity of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A believing account attributes the correspondence to Isaiah material preserved on the brass plates and translated by Joseph Smith.
- A naturalistic account attributes the English correspondence substantially or entirely to direct dependence on the King James Bible.
- Translation-language explanations may combine an underlying ancient source claim with deliberate use of familiar King James diction.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 6#^isa-6-1|Isaiah 6:1-13]], [[2 Nephi 16#^2ne-16-1|2 Nephi 16:1-13]]
