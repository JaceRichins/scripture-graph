# Literary-Contextual Identification in Isaiah 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the surrounding literary context, Cyrus is the leading identification of the unnamed eastern agent in Isaiah 46:11: the preceding chapter names Cyrus as the Lord's anointed and says he will be raised up in righteousness, while Isaiah 46 describes a man from the east who executes God's counsel. Isaiah 46 itself, however, does not name him.

**Does not establish:** The identification does not by itself establish when the passage was composed, whether it predates Cyrus's career, or a particular theory of Isaiah's authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The description could intentionally leave the agent unnamed and emphasize his function rather than his identity.
- Read independently of the surrounding Cyrus material, the wording could describe another eastern conqueror or a generalized instrument of judgment.
- Later readers may apply the imagery typologically beyond its most likely historical referent.
