# Multiple Textual Attestation in 2 Chronicles 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The core sequence of Babylonian intervention, royal deportation, Jerusalem's destruction, and exile is also narrated in Kings and Jeremiah, giving the chapter substantial canonical corroboration for the broad event sequence.

**Does not establish:** The convergence does not independently prove every reported detail, the accounts' full independence, or the chapter's theological explanation of why the events occurred.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The biblical accounts may share sources or depend literarily upon one another and therefore are not fully independent witnesses.
- Each account selects and shapes details according to its own theological and literary purposes.
