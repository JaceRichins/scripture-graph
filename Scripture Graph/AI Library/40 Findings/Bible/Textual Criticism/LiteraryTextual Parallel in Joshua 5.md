# LiteraryTextual Parallel in Joshua 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The captain's instruction, 'Loose thy shoe from off thy foot; for the place whereon thou standest is holy,' closely parallels the LORD's words to Moses at the burning bush, 'put off thy shoes from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground' (Exodus 3:5), a wording pattern this study did not find repeated elsewhere in the text, which supports (without proving) a deliberate literary echo casting Joshua's commission in parallel to Moses's.

**Does not establish:** Does not prove literary dependence or a conscious 'new Moses' typology on the part of the author; the shared wording could arise independently from shared cultural convention.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Removing footwear on sacred ground may reflect a broader ancient Near Eastern convention for approaching holy space rather than a deliberate quotation of the Exodus scene.
