# Intertextual Ritual Correspondence in Leviticus 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close sequence-level correspondence between Exodus 29 and Leviticus 8 strongly supports reading Leviticus 8, in the canonical narrative, as the enactment of the priestly installation instructions in Exodus 29.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not establish when either passage was composed, the direction of literary dependence at every compositional stage, or that the narrated ceremony occurred historically exactly as described.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages could have been shaped from a common ritual tradition.
- Editorial coordination could account for the correspondence without establishing the historical order in which the texts were composed.
