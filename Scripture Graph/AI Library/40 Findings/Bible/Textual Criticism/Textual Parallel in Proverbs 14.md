# Textual Parallel in Proverbs 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Proverbs 14:12 and Proverbs 16:25 contain the same warning that a way which seems right can end in death. This repetition supports the observation that sayings were reused or reiterated within Proverbs, but it does not identify when, by whom, or through what editorial process the repetition arose.

**Does not establish:** The duplicate does not establish a particular source theory, chronology, named author, or direction of dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler may have preserved two independently circulating forms of the same maxim.
- The repetition may be intentional reinforcement rather than evidence of separate sources.
- The small wording difference may reflect ordinary textual or editorial variation.
