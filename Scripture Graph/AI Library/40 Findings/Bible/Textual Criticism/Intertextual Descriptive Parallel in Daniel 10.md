# Intertextual Descriptive Parallel in Daniel 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The figure in Daniel 10:5-6 and the figure in Revelation 1:13-16 share a golden girdle, fiery eyes, brasslike limbs or feet, and an immense voice; both visions also include the seer's collapse, a strengthening touch, and 'fear not.' These correspondences strongly support a literary relationship or shared visionary idiom and help explain a Christological reading of Daniel's figure, but they do not settle his identity within Daniel 10.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish that the linen-clothed man, every speaker in Daniel 10, and the risen Christ in Revelation 1 are necessarily the same person.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Revelation may adapt Daniel's imagery without asserting that both passages depict the same individual.
- Glorious angelic and divine figures may share conventional apocalyptic imagery.
- Daniel 10 may shift between more than one heavenly figure, making identification from pronouns and actions uncertain.
