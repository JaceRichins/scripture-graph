# Scriptural Intertextuality in Matthew 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The identifiable appeals to Exodus 3:6, Deuteronomy 6:5, Leviticus 19:18, and Psalm 110:1 are strong textual evidence that Matthew frames these disputes as interpretation of Israel's scriptures.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the exact wording of the historical conversations, the evangelist's sources, or a particular theory of Gospel composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The evangelist may have shaped inherited sayings or controversy traditions around these texts.
- Some wording may reflect scriptural translations or textual forms different from the KJV Old Testament wording.
