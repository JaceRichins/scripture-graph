# Internal Textual Juxtaposition in 2 Chronicles 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter, the temple's chosen and sanctified status does not function as an unconditional guarantee that the building will remain accepted: the declaration of enduring divine attention is followed by an explicit warning that apostasy can result in the temple being cast out of God's sight.

**Does not establish:** This juxtaposition does not by itself establish a general lexical rule for every biblical use of 'for ever' or 'perpetually.'

**Alternative explanations:**
- The perpetual language may refer to God's enduring choice of the place or name rather than the uninterrupted survival of one building.
- The language may employ conventional covenant rhetoric whose precise temporal force cannot be settled from this chapter alone.
