# Intratextual Identification in Daniel 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter, Nebuchadnezzar first describes the fourth figure as resembling “the Son of God” and later says that the men's God sent “his angel.” This supports an angelic interpretation of the figure more directly than a specifically Christological identification, although it does not settle the figure's precise identity.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not conclusively identify the figure as Jesus Christ, determine the figure's ontology, or show that Nebuchadnezzar possessed a developed Christian understanding.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A Christian reading may identify the fourth figure as the premortal Jesus Christ.
- Nebuchadnezzar's first description may reflect his own limited or polytheistic conceptual vocabulary.
- An angelic designation need not, within every theological system, exclude a divine manifestation.

**Scripture:** [[Daniel 3#^dan-3-25|Daniel 3:25]], [[Daniel 3#^dan-3-28|Daniel 3:28]]
