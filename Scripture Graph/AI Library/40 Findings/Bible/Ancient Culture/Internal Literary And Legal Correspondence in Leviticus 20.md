# Internal Literary And Legal Correspondence in Leviticus 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive overlap in subject matter and land-expulsion language provides strong internal evidence that Leviticus 18 and 20 should be studied together as prohibition-focused and sanction-focused treatments of related material.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not establish a single author, one date of composition, or the precise stages by which the chapters reached their present form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The chapters may independently draw upon a shared body of traditional legal material rather than having been deliberately designed as a complementary pair.
- Their present correspondence may partly reflect editorial arrangement.
