# Legal And Intertextual Alignment in 1 Chronicles 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's stated procedure corresponds to Pentateuchal instructions that Levi was set apart to bear the ark, that designated bearers should not touch holy objects, and that the ark was to be moved by staves. This strongly supports a deliberate narrative emphasis on conformity to Mosaic instruction.

**Does not establish:** The textual alignment does not independently prove the event's historicity, a particular date of authorship, or that the cited legal details were applied identically in every period.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The alignment may reflect the narrator's theological presentation without independently verifying how the historical procession occurred.
- Different Pentateuchal passages address related but not entirely identical circumstances and groups of bearers.
- The Chronicler may be harmonizing inherited tradition with the Torah as understood in the writer's own period.
