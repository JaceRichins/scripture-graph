# Intratextual Legal-Rhetorical Evidence in Matthew 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between the Pharisees' word "command" and Jesus's word "suffered" is strong textual evidence that Matthew presents the divorce certificate as a regulated concession rather than the governing ideal for marriage.

**Does not establish:** This distinction does not by itself resolve every question about lawful divorce, remarriage, abuse, abandonment, or how the teaching should be applied in later legal and pastoral settings.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Pharisees may use "command" loosely rather than offering a precise characterization of Deuteronomy.
- A concession can still possess binding regulatory force once divorce has occurred.
