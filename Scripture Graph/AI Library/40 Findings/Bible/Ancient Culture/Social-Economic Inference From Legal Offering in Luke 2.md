# Social-Economic Inference From Legal Offering in Luke 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The offering of turtledoves or pigeons corresponds to the alternative permitted in Leviticus 12:8 when a mother could not afford a lamb. It is therefore meaningful evidence consistent with limited means, although it does not establish destitution.

**Does not establish:** The offering does not prove extreme poverty, permanent poverty, or every proposed theological interpretation of the family's economic status.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Luke may mention the offering chiefly to emphasize conformity to scripture rather than to characterize the family's social class.
- A single offering does not reveal the family's financial circumstances at every period.
- The narrative does not explain how the offering was acquired or financed.

**Scripture:** [[Luke 2#^luke-2-24|Luke 2:24]], [[Leviticus 12#^lev-12-8|Leviticus 12:8]]
