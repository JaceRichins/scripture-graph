# Legal-Text And Fulfillment Correspondence in Joshua 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The forty-eight-city total and the provision of surrounding lands closely correspond to the command in Numbers 35, strongly supporting the literary claim that Joshua 21 presents the allocation as fulfillment of Mosaic instruction.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence does not by itself establish the allocation's historical implementation, the date of either passage, or a particular theory of authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may reflect a shared legal tradition without establishing the chronological direction of dependence.
- An editor could have shaped the allocation account to correspond numerically and verbally with the legal command.
- The correspondence may describe an administrative ideal rather than independently verify that every settlement was occupied as listed.
