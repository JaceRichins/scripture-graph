# Literary And Covenantal Correspondence in 1 Kings 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of multiplied wives, foreign marriages, a turned heart, other gods, and covenant sanctions is strong evidence that the chapter presents Solomon's reign through Deuteronomic covenant categories.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the chapter's author, exact composition date, or the historicity of every reported detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The texts may draw independently on a broadly shared Israelite covenant tradition.
- A later editor may have framed older royal traditions in Deuteronomic language.
- The parallels alone cannot determine the direction of literary dependence.
