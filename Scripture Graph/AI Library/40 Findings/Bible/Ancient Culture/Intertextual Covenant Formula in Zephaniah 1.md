# Intertextual Covenant Formula in Zephaniah 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The paired loss of newly built houses and planted vineyards is strong evidence that Zephaniah 1 participates in Israel's covenant-curse prophetic tradition.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish which written text influenced which, nor the precise date or editorial history of the oracle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Zephaniah and Amos may independently describe ordinary consequences of conquest.
- The wording may derive from a broadly shared curse tradition rather than directly from Deuteronomy.
- Later textual shaping could have strengthened the verbal resemblance.

**Scripture:** [[Zephaniah 1#^zeph-1-13|Zephaniah 1:13]], [[Deuteronomy 28#^deut-28-30|Deuteronomy 28:30]], [[Amos 5#^amos-5-11|Amos 5:11]]
