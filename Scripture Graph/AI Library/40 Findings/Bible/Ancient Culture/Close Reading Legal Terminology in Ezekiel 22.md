# Close Reading Legal Terminology in Ezekiel 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 26's charge against the priests — that they 'put no difference between the holy and profane... between the unclean and the clean' and 'hid their eyes from my sabbaths' — uses the specific technical language of the priestly office's core duty to discern holy from common and clean from unclean (cf. Leviticus 10:10), framing the priests' failure as dereliction of their defining function rather than generic misconduct.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish literary dependence on a fixed written text of Leviticus as opposed to shared legal-cultic tradition or vocabulary.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The 'holy/profane, clean/unclean' pairing may be stock priestly vocabulary broadly attested rather than a pointed citation of the specific verse Leviticus 10:10.
