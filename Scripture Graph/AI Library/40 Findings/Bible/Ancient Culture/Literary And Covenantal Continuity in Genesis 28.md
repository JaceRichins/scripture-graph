# Literary And Covenantal Continuity in Genesis 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of land, seed, worldwide blessing, and divine presence in Genesis 28 strongly supports reading Jacob's experience as a continuation of the promises previously given to Abraham and Isaac.

**Does not establish:** The literary continuity does not by itself establish the historical date, authorship, or independent historical accuracy of the narratives.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later author or editor could have deliberately aligned Jacob's story with earlier patriarchal promises.
- Shared formulas may reflect a common covenant tradition rather than direct dependence between individual passages.
