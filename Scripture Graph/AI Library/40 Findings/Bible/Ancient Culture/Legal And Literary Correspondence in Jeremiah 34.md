# Legal And Literary Correspondence in Jeremiah 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared six-year service and subsequent release pattern is strong evidence that Jeremiah 34:14 and the laws in Deuteronomy 15:12 and Exodus 21:2 belong to a common legal tradition. The correspondence alone cannot determine whether Jeremiah directly quotes a written text, draws on an orally transmitted law, or reflects editorial formulation.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the direction of literary dependence, the date of composition or editing, or the historical degree of compliance with the law.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah and the legal texts may independently preserve a shared customary rule.
- An editor may have shaped Jeremiah's wording to resemble an existing legal text.
- The correspondence may reflect oral transmission rather than direct use of a written document.
