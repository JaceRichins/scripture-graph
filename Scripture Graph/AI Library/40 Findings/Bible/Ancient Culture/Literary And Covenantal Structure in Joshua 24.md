# Literary And Covenantal Structure in Joshua 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Joshua 24 presents a coherent covenant-renewal sequence: a representative assembly before God, a recital of the LORD's prior acts, a demand for exclusive loyalty, repeated public declarations, self-witness, formal covenant and ordinance, a written record, and a monumental witness. This strongly supports reading the chapter as deliberately shaped covenant-renewal literature.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the event's date, single authorship, precise compositional history, correspondence to one ancient treaty form, or the historicity of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may reflect broad biblical legal and liturgical conventions rather than one identifiable treaty template.
- A later writer or editor could have arranged inherited traditions into this covenantal form.
- Some apparent structural elements may arise naturally from narrating a public commitment ceremony.
