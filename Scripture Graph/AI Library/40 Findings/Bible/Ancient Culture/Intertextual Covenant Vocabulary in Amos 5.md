# Intertextual Covenant Vocabulary in Amos 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The loss of newly built houses and planted vineyards in Amos 5:11 closely parallels covenant-curse outcomes in Deuteronomy 28:30 and 28:39. This substantially supports reading Amos's threatened judgment within Israel's covenantal warning tradition, while direct literary dependence remains unproven.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish direct quotation, the direction of dependence, an exact composition date, or the identity of an editor.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently employ common ancient agrarian judgment formulas.
- Amos and Deuteronomy may draw on a shared covenant tradition without either text directly quoting the other.
- The resemblance could have been strengthened during later transmission or editorial shaping.
