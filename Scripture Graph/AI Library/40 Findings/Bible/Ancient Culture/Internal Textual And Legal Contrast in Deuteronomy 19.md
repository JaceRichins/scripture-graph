# Internal Textual And Legal Contrast in Deuteronomy 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between the axe accident and the ambush provides strong internal textual evidence that this legal presentation treats intention and premeditation as relevant to culpability.

**Does not establish:** This contrast does not establish the complete historical procedure used in every Israelite homicide case or demonstrate how consistently the law was implemented.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two cases may be representative illustrations rather than an exhaustive classification of every degree of homicide.
- Prior hatred is one indicator of intent in the chapter, but the text does not say that it is the only legally relevant indicator.
