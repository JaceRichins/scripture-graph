# Explicit Intertextual Comparison in Moroni 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Jaredite comparison is strong internal evidence that the Book of Mormon's final Nephite conflict is deliberately interpreted through an earlier destruction narrative.

**Does not establish:** The analogy does not establish that every feature of the Jaredite and Nephite collapses was identical, nor does it independently prove the historicity of either account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallel may function primarily as theological or literary patterning rather than as an explanation of historical causation.
- Similar end-stage violence can produce similar descriptions without requiring close correspondence in every detail.

**Scripture:** [[Moroni 9#^moro-9-23|Moroni 9:23]], [[Ether 15#^ether-15-19|Ether 15:19]], [[Ether 15#^ether-15-22|Ether 15:22]]
