# Internal Textual Consistency in 3 Nephi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chronological notices in 3 Nephi 2:6–8 yield 609 minus 9, or 600 years, matching the interval stated in 1 Nephi 10:4 and 1 Nephi 19:8. The calculation joins the earlier prediction, its narrated fulfillment, and the new reckoning of years into one coherent final-form timeline.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Third Nephi 2:6 dates the narrative to 609 years after Lehi's departure, while verses 7–8 place it nine years after the sign associated with Christ's coming. Subtracting nine from 609 yields the 600-year interval stated in 1 Nephi 10:4 and 1 Nephi 19:8.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The final text deliberately coordinates the earlier 600-year prediction, the narrated sign of Christ's coming, and the new chronological era.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

## Historical Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
The calculation reveals how the Book of Mormon's final-form chronology links the Lehite departure, the sign of Christ's coming, and the subsequent calendrical transition.
<!-- SG:END historical-significance -->

## How This Fits
<!-- SG:BEGIN how-it-fits -->
On the narrative's chronological reconstruction, the sign inaugurating the new era occurred 600 years after Lehi left Jerusalem, and the notice in 3 Nephi 2 falls nine years later. The 609-year total and nine-year new-era count therefore describe the same moment from two temporal starting points, showing how the old and new calendars interlock.
