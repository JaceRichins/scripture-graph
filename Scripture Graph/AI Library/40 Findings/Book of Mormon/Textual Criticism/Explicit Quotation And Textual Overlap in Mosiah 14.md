# Explicit Quotation And Textual Overlap in Mosiah 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 14 reproduces the twelve-verse sequence of Isaiah 53 in closely corresponding English and explicitly introduces it as Isaiah's words. This is conclusive evidence of an intended literary relationship between the two chapters.

**Does not establish:** The textual relationship does not by itself establish a particular account of Book of Mormon historicity, textual transmission, or translation method.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Different models may explain how the Isaian passage entered the Book of Mormon record or its English translation.
- Close English correspondence alone cannot distinguish among proposed ancient transmission and modern translation mechanisms.

**Scripture:** [[Mosiah 14#^mosiah-14-1|Mosiah 14:1-12]], [[Isaiah 53#^isa-53-1|Isaiah 53:1-12]]
