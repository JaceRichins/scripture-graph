# Literary Coherence And Intertextuality in Mosiah 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 15 forms a sustained exposition of adjacent Isaianic servant and herald texts: it applies the silent-sheep image, answers the questions about generation and seed, interprets the publisher of peace, and concludes with Isaiah's watchmen and universal vision of salvation. This is strong internal evidence of deliberate literary coherence across Mosiah 14–15.

**Does not establish:** The coherence does not by itself establish the date, ancient provenance, translation method, or historical setting of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler or editor could have produced the coherence by arranging and expanding inherited materials.
- A modern author familiar with the King James Isaiah text could also construct this kind of sustained exposition.
- Some correspondences follow naturally from Abinadi commenting on a passage just quoted and need not imply an elaborate literary design.
