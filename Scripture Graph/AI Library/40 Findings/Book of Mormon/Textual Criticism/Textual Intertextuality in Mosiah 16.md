# Textual Intertextuality in Mosiah 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 16:7-10 contains a concentrated textual cluster parallel to 1 Corinthians 15:53-55: corruption and incorruption, mortality and immortality, death swallowed up, the sting of death, and the grave's victory. Deliberate scriptural intertextuality in the English wording is highly likely, although the direction and mechanism of dependence remain unresolved.

**Does not establish:** The parallel establishes a close literary relationship in English, not whether that relationship arose in an ancient source, during translation, or through modern composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A translation of an ancient record may intentionally render related concepts in familiar King James Bible language.
- A modern composer could have drawn directly from the King James New Testament.
- Both passages could draw on a broader scriptural tradition represented in Isaiah 25:8 and Hosea 13:14.
