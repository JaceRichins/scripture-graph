# Literary And Thematic Pattern in Mosiah 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The movement from bondage through a nighttime departure into the wilderness and toward a new political home strongly supports classifying this chapter under the theme of Exodus and Deliverance.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish conscious literary dependence on Exodus, ancient authorship, supernatural deliverance, or the historicity of either account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Bondage, escape, wilderness travel, and resettlement are also ordinary elements of escape narratives.
- The similarities may arise from the immediate plot rather than a deliberate allusion to the biblical Exodus.
