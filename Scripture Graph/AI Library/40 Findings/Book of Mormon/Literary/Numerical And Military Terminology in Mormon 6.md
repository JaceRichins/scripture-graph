# Numerical And Military Terminology in Mormon 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated expression 'ten thousand' supports the conclusion that the text portrays casualties on an enormous scale. It is less certain whether every occurrence is intended as an exact headcount, a rounded number, or the designation of a military body.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish an exact total population or exact casualty count.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Each expression may be intended as a literal and exact count.
- The numbers may be rounded estimates.
- A 'ten thousand' may function as the conventional name of a military formation whose actual strength varied.
- The repeated number may serve a literary function in the death roll.
