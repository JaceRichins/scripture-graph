# Internal Narrative Evidence in Moroni 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter's own portrayal, the parenthetical recollection that the people had been civil and delightsome only a few years earlier supports a reading of rapid social and moral disintegration.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical pace, causes, or full geographical extent of the reported collapse.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The phrase "only a few years" may be a rounded or rhetorical contrast rather than a precise chronology.
- The portrayal may compress a longer deterioration to heighten the letter's warning.
- The passage provides the writer's assessment rather than independent social data.
