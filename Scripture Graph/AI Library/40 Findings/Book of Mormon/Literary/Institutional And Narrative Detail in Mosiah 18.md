# Institutional And Narrative Detail in Mosiah 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Concrete details such as the baptismal formula, approximate membership, priest-to-member ratio, weekly assembly, priestly labor, and proportional relief give the account institutional specificity. These details support the interpretation that the narrator intends to portray a functioning community rather than baptism as an isolated ceremony.

**Does not establish:** Specificity alone does not prove historicity, eyewitness authorship, or that every practice described originated in the narrated period.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Institutional detail can be produced by literary construction or later community experience as well as by eyewitness memory.
- Some details may serve didactic purposes more than documentary ones.
