# Behavior Under Lethal Coercion in Mosiah 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As narrated, Abinadi's refusal to recant under threat and execution strongly supports the sincerity of his commitment to his message. Sincerity is distinct from the truth of the message.

**Does not establish:** His death does not prove the theological truth of his teachings or independently verify the narrative's historical details.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A person may sincerely die for a mistaken belief.
- The account may employ conventional martyrdom narration to portray exemplary fidelity.
- Once condemned, Abinadi may have perceived no realistic opportunity to escape by recanting.
