# Internal Chronological Evidence in Mormon 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of named years, cities, retreats, renewed assaults, and record retrieval supplies a coherent internal chronology for the collapse narrated in Mormon 4.

**Does not establish:** Internal chronological coherence does not independently verify the events, identify the cities archaeologically, or prove the proposed modern A.D. dating.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A coherent internal chronology can be produced in either historical narrative or literary composition.
- The text's year-counting system cannot by itself be securely synchronized with an external calendar.
