# Literary Coherence in Alma 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated language of wish, desire, allotment, calling, glory, remembrance, and joy organizes [[Alma 29#^alma-29-1|Alma 29:1]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-3|Alma 29:3]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-4|Alma 29:4]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-6|Alma 29:6]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-7|Alma 29:7]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-9|Alma 29:9]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-10|Alma 29:10]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-13|Alma 29:13]], [[Alma 29#^alma-29-14|Alma 29:14]], and [[Alma 29#^alma-29-16|Alma 29:16]]. The chapter moves from a wish for universal influence through acceptance of a particular calling to grateful remembrance and joy in the success of fellow laborers.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Across Alma 29, recurring language of wish, desire, allotment, calling, glory, remembrance, and joy traces a progression from Alma's wish for universal influence to acceptance of his allotted calling and joy in both his own ministry and the success of others.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The repeated vocabulary organizes the chapter as a reflective movement: expansive desire is corrected by recognition of divine allotment, then redirected into grateful service, remembrance, and shared joy.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

## Historical Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
The chapter exemplifies sustained religious self-examination in which vocation and personal limitation are reconciled through remembered experience. Its coherence can belong to composed writing, shaped oral discourse, or later editorial presentation.
<!-- SG:END historical-significance -->

## How This Fits
<!-- SG:BEGIN how-it-fits -->
Read as a meditation on vocation, the opening angelic wish supplies the tension, the language of allotment and calling supplies the correction, and the closing language of remembrance and joy supplies the resolution. Alma's contentment is not withdrawal from missionary desire; it is the relocation of that desire within a particular calling and a willingness to rejoice in others' success.
<!-- SG:END how-it-fits -->
