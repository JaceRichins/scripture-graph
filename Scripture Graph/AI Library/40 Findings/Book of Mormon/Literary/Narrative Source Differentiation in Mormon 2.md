# Narrative Source Differentiation in Mormon 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mormon 2:17–18 explicitly contrasts a fuller account on the plates of Nephi with a selective account on the present plates. This moderately supports deliberate source-conscious editorial characterization at 0.58, matching the registry's comparable partial cluster in Helaman 3, while leaving the records' external existence and antiquity unverified.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Mormon 2:17 says that Mormon retrieved the plates of Nephi and made a record as Ammaron instructed. Verse 18 contrasts a "full account" on those plates with "these plates," on which Mormon "did forbear to make a full account."
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The passage explicitly presents omission as a conscious editorial decision and differentiates two records by their scope.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

## Historical Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
The final text's internal record system permits fuller and more selective accounts to coexist, with the narrator explaining why the present account is compressed.
<!-- SG:END historical-significance -->

## How This Fits
<!-- SG:BEGIN how-it-fits -->
Under an ancient-record model, Mormon maintains a fuller chronicle while preparing a selective account for another audience or purpose. Under redactional and literary-composition models, the same contrast supplies documentary depth and explains narrative omission. In each reconstruction, the notice functions as deliberate editorial self-presentation.
<!-- SG:END how-it-fits -->

## Apologetic Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN apologetic-significance -->
The explicit source-and-selection contrast supports deliberate construction of a source-conscious editor. Because ancient record keeping, later redaction, and literary simulation all predict such a notice, it provides no independent support for the plates' external existence or ancient provenance.

**Inspiration:** Whether the record distinction is historical or literary does not by itself determine whether the text is inspired.
<!-- SG:END apologetic-significance -->

## What It Does Not Establish
<!-- SG:BEGIN does-not-establish -->
It does not establish the physical existence, contents, date, or provenance of either record, or verify that the stated motive for compression was historical.
