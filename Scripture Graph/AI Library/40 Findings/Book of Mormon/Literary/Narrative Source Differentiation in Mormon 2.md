# Narrative Source Differentiation in Mormon 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The distinction between a fuller record on the plates of Nephi and a selective account on the present plates is evidence that the narrator presents his work as conscious historiographical selection rather than exhaustive reporting.

**Does not establish:** The passage establishes the narrator's source claim, not the external existence, date, or provenance of the plates.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Source differentiation can be a literary device and need not correspond to physically distinct ancient artifacts.
- The exact relationship among the records called plates is not fully explained in this chapter.

**Scripture:** [[Mormon 2#^morm-2-17|Mormon 2:17]], [[Mormon 2#^morm-2-18|Mormon 2:18]]
