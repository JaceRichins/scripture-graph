# Literary Continuity in 2 Nephi 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
[[2 Nephi 31#^2ne-31-17|2 Nephi 31:17]], [[2 Nephi 31#^2ne-31-18|31:18]], and [[2 Nephi 31#^2ne-31-21|31:21]] introduce entering the way, receiving the Holy Ghost, and the doctrine of Christ; [[2 Nephi 32#^2ne-32-1|2 Nephi 32:1]], [[2 Nephi 32#^2ne-32-5|32:5]], and [[2 Nephi 32#^2ne-32-6|32:6]] resume those terms while explaining what disciples do afterward. The two chapters form successive stages of one discipleship argument.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Second Nephi 31 describes entering by repentance and baptism, receiving the Holy Ghost, proceeding in the way, and recognizing the doctrine of Christ. Second Nephi 32 opens by asking what hearers should do after entering that way and repeats receiving the Holy Ghost and the doctrine of Christ.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
Second Nephi 32 directly continues the discipleship instruction of the preceding chapter, moving from entry into the way to conduct after entry.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

## Historical Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
The continuity shows that the present chapter boundary divides a sustained discourse rather than separating unrelated treatments. The recurring vocabulary also clarifies the instructional progression from baptism and reception of the Holy Ghost to continuing divine guidance.
<!-- SG:END historical-significance -->

## How This Fits
<!-- SG:BEGIN how-it-fits -->
Read as one discourse, [[2 Nephi 31#^2ne-31-17|2 Nephi 31:17]], [[2 Nephi 31#^2ne-31-18|31:18]], and [[2 Nephi 31#^2ne-31-21|31:21]] define the gate, way, and doctrine; [[2 Nephi 32#^2ne-32-1|2 Nephi 32:1]], [[2 Nephi 32#^2ne-32-5|32:5]], and [[2 Nephi 32#^2ne-32-6|32:6]] answer the resulting question about what follows. The second section thus functions as the practical continuation of the first.
<!-- SG:END how-it-fits -->
