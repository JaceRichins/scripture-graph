# Internal Literary Structure in Words of Mormon 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter explicitly explains the joining of records and then resumes the historical narrative with King Benjamin. This strongly supports reading the chapter as a deliberate literary bridge between the account ending with Amaleki and the succeeding Benjamin narrative.

**Does not establish:** The editorial bridge does not by itself establish the date, authorship, historicity, or divine inspiration of the record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The bridge could have been composed at any stage of the work's production.
- Its literary coherence alone cannot distinguish ancient compilation from modern composition or later redaction.
