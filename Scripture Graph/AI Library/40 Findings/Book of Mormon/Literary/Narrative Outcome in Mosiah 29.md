# Narrative Outcome in Mosiah 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrator reports righteous judgment and continual peace under Alma, supporting the limited conclusion that the new order is presented as initially successful within the narrative.

**Does not establish:** The report does not establish the long-term stability, fairness, representativeness, or historical existence of the system.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The peace report may be a compressed narrative formula rather than a comprehensive assessment of political conditions.
- Peace could reflect Alma's personal conduct or other circumstances rather than the institutional design itself.
