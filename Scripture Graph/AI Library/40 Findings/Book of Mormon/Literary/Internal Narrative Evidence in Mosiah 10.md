# Internal Narrative Evidence in Mosiah 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As internal narrative evidence, Mosiah 10 strongly supports the narrow conclusion that this account represents inherited grievance, parental teaching, and royal persuasion as mechanisms by which group conflict is perpetuated and mobilized.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historical accuracy of Zeniff's reconstruction, the uniform beliefs of all Lamanites, or the absence of legitimate Lamanite grievances.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Zeniff may simplify a complex conflict by attributing it primarily to inherited falsehoods.
- The account may employ conventional wartime polemic to delegitimize the enemy.
- Material interests, political power, and territorial pressures may have contributed even though Zeniff emphasizes tradition.
