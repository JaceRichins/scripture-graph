# Internal Literary Evidence in Mormon 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mormon 5:8-14 clusters selection/abridgment, audience, preservation, an explicit divine command, and stated purpose -- matching the fullest instances of this evidence class already registered elsewhere in the corpus (1 Nephi 6, 3 Nephi 5). This strongly supports deliberate construction of a self-conscious editorial persona for Mormon, at a weight (0.82) consistent with those siblings rather than demoted, since the registry already treats this exact evidence class as a live contested question about ancient versus modern authorship. It gives no independent discrimination between an authentic ancient record-keeper and literary characterization, and does not establish Mormon's historicity, the plates, or the record's antiquity.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Verses 8-14 cluster five elements in close sequence: an abridgment/selection notice ('I write a small abridgment' -- a selection statement, not identification of an underlying source document), a stated audience (the remnant of Jacob's house and the Gentiles), a preservation plan ('hid up unto the Lord'), an explicit divine command ('this is the commandment which I have received'), and a stated theological purpose (persuading the Jews that Jesus is the Christ).
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
This is a full, five-element editorial-persona cluster comparable to the fullest registered instances of this class (1 Nephi 6, 3 Nephi 5), not a partial notice like Helaman 3's source/selection-only cluster.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
