# Internal Narrative Continuity in Mormon 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The agreement among Ammaron's earlier concealment of the records, his detailed instructions here, and Mormon's later retrievals is strong evidence of deliberate narrative and editorial continuity across these chapters.

**Does not establish:** This internal continuity does not independently establish the narrative's historicity, the antiquity of the records, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single modern author could deliberately create the same cross-chapter continuity.
- Later editorial planning or revision could harmonize the archival episodes.
- Internal consistency can characterize either historical narrative or carefully constructed fiction.
