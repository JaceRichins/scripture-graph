# Reported Miracle And Theological Attribution in Alma 57

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Helaman explicitly interprets the survival of all 2,060 wounded young soldiers as miraculous and connects it with their faith. The text is strong evidence for the narrator's theological interpretation, but by itself is limited evidence for supernatural causation because no independent casualty record or comparison controlling for tactical factors is supplied.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove supernatural causation or establish a universal promise that faithful people will survive battle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The survival pattern could involve chance or unreported tactical and medical factors.
- The account may have been selected or shaped to emphasize exemplary faith.
- The casualty numbers and outcomes lack independent corroboration within the supplied evidence.
