# English Verbal And Sequential Correspondence in Moroni 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive ordered correspondence between Moroni 6:1–3 and Doctrine and Covenants 20:37 is strong evidence of a direct textual relationship or deliberate reuse in their English forms. The observation itself is secure, while conclusions about the theological or historical mechanism remain disputed.

**Does not establish:** The parallel by itself does not establish the direction of dependence, the ultimate origin of the wording, or either an ancient or modern authorship theory.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Doctrine and Covenants 20 may deliberately reuse Moroni's wording as an authoritative ecclesiastical pattern.
- Both passages may express the same revealed requirements through a shared religious vocabulary.
- Common biblical and revival-era Christian language can explain part of the overlap, though it less readily explains the whole ordered cluster.
