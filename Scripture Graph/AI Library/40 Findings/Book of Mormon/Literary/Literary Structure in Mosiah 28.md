# Literary Structure in Mosiah 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrator twice postpones fuller accounts—first the sons' missionary proceedings and then the translated record—creating a deliberate roadmap to later narrative units.

**Does not establish:** This organization does not by itself establish ancient authorship, historical accuracy, or the precise process by which the text was composed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The roadmap could arise during a single author's ordinary planning rather than from a complex editorial process.
- The cross-references may reflect later compilation or revision rather than composition from an already fixed master plan.
