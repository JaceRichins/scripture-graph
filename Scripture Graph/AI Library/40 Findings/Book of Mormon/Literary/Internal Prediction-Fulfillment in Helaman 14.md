# Internal Prediction-Fulfillment in Helaman 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Samuel's predicted five-year interval, night without darkness, and new star are matched in 3 Nephi 1's report that no darkness fell and the new star appeared. This reuses the registry's existing strong (0.65) assessment of deliberate prophecy-fulfillment design; the chapter's separate death-sign prophecy and its 3 Nephi 8 fulfillment are tracked under a different, already-registered issue and are not added here.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Helaman 14:2-5 predicts Christ's birth within five years, a night without darkness, and the rising of a new star. 3 Nephi 1:15 and 1:19 report that no darkness fell on the appointed night, and 3 Nephi 1:21 reports the appearance of the new star, matching Samuel's stated birth signs. Helaman 14:20-22 separately predicts darkness, seismic disturbance, and rent rocks as death signs, whose fulfillment is narrated later in 3 Nephi 8 and is tracked under the separate bom-3nephi8-death-signs-fulfillment issue.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The birth-sign portion of Samuel's prophecy is matched, including the new star, against its narrated fulfillment three chapters later. This is the same evidentiary pattern already registered under bom-3nephi1-samuel-signs-correspondence and should not be re-scored as a fresh, independent finding.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->
