# Internal Narrative Coherence in Helaman 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Helaman 10 grants Nephi authority that expressly includes famine, and Helaman 11 narrates a famine following his prayer with the formula that it occurred 'according to the words of Nephi.' This deliberate linkage gives the shift from warfare to famine a clear prophetic and theological frame, illuminating how Mormon's abridgment builds cause and consequence into the record rather than reporting events as an undifferentiated chronicle.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Helaman 10:5-6 grants Nephi authority to act according to his word, including power to smite the land with famine. In Helaman 11:3-6 Nephi prays that warfare be replaced by famine, the narrator states it was done 'according to the words of Nephi,' and a famine follows that continues into the seventy-fifth year.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The famine episode is the narrative activation of the authority granted in the preceding chapter. The explicit fulfillment formula ('according to the words of Nephi') makes the connection an authored textual link rather than a reader-inferred resemblance.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

## Historical Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
The sequence follows a recognizable historiographic shape in which a granted authority or commission is immediately followed by a scene narrating its exercise, giving the transition from war to famine an explicit theological cause.
<!-- SG:END historical-significance -->

## How This Fits
<!-- SG:BEGIN how-it-fits -->
On Mormon's own account of his editorial method, he arranges his abridged material so that Nephi's commission (10:5-6) directly precedes and explains the famine that follows (11:3-6); whether the underlying events are an accurately preserved ancient memory or a later editorial arrangement, the authorization-and-enactment shape is what carries the chapter transition and gives the famine its meaning within the narrative.
<!-- SG:END how-it-fits -->
