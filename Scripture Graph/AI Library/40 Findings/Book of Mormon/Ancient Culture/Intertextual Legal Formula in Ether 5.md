# Intertextual Legal Formula in Ether 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close verbal and conceptual relationship between Ether 5:4 and biblical witness formulas is strong evidence that the passage deliberately frames the plates and their witnesses in a scriptural legal-testimony tradition.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not by itself establish ancient authorship, the historical occurrence of the predicted manifestation, or the reliability of any later witness testimony.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A modern author or translator familiar with the King James Bible could intentionally reuse the same formula.
- The resemblance may reflect conventional scriptural diction rather than a precise allusion to one particular biblical verse.
