### Good Memorial

Biography
From the "YELL COUNTY HERITAGE" book. ROBERT GRAY As early as 1841, Robert Gray is listed on the Yell Co.,AR. tax rolls along with his sons, William and Joseph. Robert was born about 1795 in Tennessee and his wife, Tabitha Jane Cooper, was born in Alabama about 1798-1802. She was believed to be part Cherokee Indian. They settled in the Spring Creek Township and raised several children. Their first child, Sarah, was born 1819 in Alabama and married Obediah Wood and settled in Yell Co. Arkansas. William, was born 1820 in Jackson County, Alabama, married Matilda Monk in Yell Co. Arkansas. Joseph was born 1821 in Tennessee and married Nancy Lorena McBride in Yell Co. Arkansas. Betsy was born 1822 in Tennessee. James M., born 1825 in Tennessee. Pheldery, born 1832 in Tennessee, married James McBride in Yell Co. Kiziah, born 1837 in Tennessee, married William T. Wilson. Martin Van Buren, born 1840 in Marion Co.,AR., married Harriett Caroline Hodges in 1859 at Havana, Arkansas. Jane was born 1842 in Yell Co. and Lucinda was born 1844 in Yell Co. AR. Robert and Tabitha farmed for a living and Tabitha is listed as head of house on 1850 Yell County census. Surviving court house records show she died between 1850 and 1858, leaving her son, Martin Van Buren, under the guardianship of his brother, Joseph.
