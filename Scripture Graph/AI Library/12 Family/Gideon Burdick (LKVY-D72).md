## Life
- **Birth**: 6 November 1762 · Hopkinton, Kings, Rhode Island, British America
- **Death**: 5 April 1846 · Quincy, Adams County, Illinois, United States
- **MilitaryService**: from 1775 to 1783 · New York
- **MilitaryService**: 1781 · New York, United States

- **MilitaryService**: 4 March 1831 · Albany, Albany, New York, United States
