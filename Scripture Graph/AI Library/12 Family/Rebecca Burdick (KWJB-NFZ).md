![[AI Library/12 Family/_media/KWJB-NFZ/4115944.jpg|Christening Record - St. Paul's Lutheran Church]]
_The christening record of Rebecca Burdick as it appears in the book entitled "Records of the Lutheran St. Paul’s Church in the town of Minden, otherwise known as the Geisenberg Church, formerly at Hallsville, in the town of Miden, Montgomery county, N.Y." (published by the New York Genealogical and Biographical Society in 1914).

The record, written in German, contains Rebecca's birth and christening dates, along with the names of her parents and of the witnesses.

The book was microfilmed and is available on film no. 000,534,218. Along with the transcription of church records, it includes a history of the church.

According to the book, the current location of the original handwritten records of the church are unknown. This transcription was created using the original records, or possibly another transcription of the records, by the New York Genealogical and Biographical Society in 1914._
