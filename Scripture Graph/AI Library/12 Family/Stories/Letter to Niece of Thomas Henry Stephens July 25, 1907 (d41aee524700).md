# Letter to Niece of Thomas Henry Stephens July 25, 1907

_A story of [[Thomas Henry Stephens (KWNL-JWK)|Thomas Henry Stephens]]._

## The story as it was written

My Dear Niece:
I received your most ever kind and welcome letter this morning, and I was pleased to hear from you, but am sorry to hear that William Henry had passed away, as I don't remember as having heard of his death before and I see from the Memorial Card that he has been dead over a year. I received a letter saying that my Brother Henry had been dead, very near a year ago, but never remember hearing of William been gone. There is one thing I would like to speak of and that is the name of my brother on the Memorial Card, his name is Henry Thomas, and not Thomas Henry, for that is my name and our parents never put in two names alike, and I think it should be corrected. I would like to know how Henrys family is getting along now, those that are left, and I would also like to know how John and his family are getting along, as John is used about 72 years of age, and whether he is still at Woosinger, as I would like to write to him, so the next time you write be sure and send me his address. I would like to know how George and his family is, and am glad to hear his eldest son is married and has two children. I would also like to know how Brother Richard and family all are, and how your Mother and family are, and I want you to give my best wishes and respects to all and used you speak about your Grand Pas farm and the bad weather you are having, we have also had a very backward and cold spring in this country, infact I do not remember having so much rain in the spring of the year, but the weather has been very fine for the last two months, and things are growing and looking good. I have not very much gain in this year. I have only about 10 acres of wheat planted this season, but I have about 5 acres of sugar beets, which are looking fine and growing well. I have not as many onions in this year as I had last. Last year I growed about 16 ton, but I could not sell all of them, so this year I did not plant as many, but still I have a good bed of them and of what was left over from last year. I had to dump 6 wagon loads in the river. You can imagine how hot it is here now as it is about 92 degrees every day. And speaking about the grand children your Grand Pa has got, I have 25 grand children 23 of these are Stephens and 2 of them are named Fowler, so you See we are not going to let the Stephens family die out in this Country, and these grand children are all living. Nearly all my farm his hay, and we have now just got nicely started haying, and will be very busy from now up till harvest. I have not rented out my farm this season and am running it myself, and I here what help I will need, so you see I will be quite busy from now on. I would like for you to ask your uncle Thomas, if he ever found a white spotted pig yet. I also see in your letter that your Grand Ma asks if Mr. Jones is alive and well, and you can tell her that Mr. Jones is a neighbor of mine, that his is living here and is doing well and since Mr. Jones returned from from his mission, he has had one son fill a mission to England, he has also one son gone to Holland on a Mission this season, Mr. Jones family is all well. Yesterday was Pioneer Day in Utah, and we had a big celebration all day. In the morning from 10 am to 12 oclock there was a dance for the children. In the afternoon there was sports and games of all kinds and a horse race and at night we had a big dance, at which we had 90 couple. Now my dear Niece I hope to hear from you soon again, and to hear all the news from Old England as your letters are always welcome and it is a pleasure to receive them. This time I answered your welcome letter on the same day I received it and I did not lay it by. I wish to be remembered to all, and hope all are enjoying good health, as this leaves me at present. Hopeing to hear from you soon again I remain
Your Loving Uncle Thomas H Stephens.
Henefer
Summit Co Utah
P. S.
I would like you tell my brothers to write to me, and all my priends, I will be glad to hear from them and will always answer there welcome letters, as I would like to hear from them all, as all of us are now getting along in years. I will be 69 on 20th day January next, but I am enjoying good health and feeling well. I would like to see all again, but I am afraid I will not on this side of the Grave-
Your Uncle
Thomas
