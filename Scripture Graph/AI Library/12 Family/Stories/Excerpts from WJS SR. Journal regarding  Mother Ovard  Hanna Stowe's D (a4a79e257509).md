## The story as it was written

This is a letter from Mary Ann Ovard to her husband William Joseph Smith, located in his Journal Transcripts, unedited.
 [ October 1888]
5.2. THE THIRD JOURNAL
 5.2.75-76 page 76-77
"The Death of Mother Ovard; My Dear Friend, America Fork, Oct. 11, 1888
 I received your kind and welcome letter, but have not had time to answer before as mother kept me by her side. She was consious to hear your letter read. It plesed her. This has been the hardest thing I have passed through. If I had not felt that you was praying for me all the time. Her suffering was so great. She called us all one by one and blessed us and told us she was ready to go when she was called. This was Friday morning. At 11 o'clock she stratend herself and to all apperance she was dead. Whe laid her on the bed. She laid there til half past 3 o'clock Saturday morning. She then opend her eyes and called us all again and blessed and counseled us to walk in the strait and narrow path that leads to life eternal. She then kissd me, askd wat time. It was, I told her, half past 3. She ask if it was morning or afternoon. I told her morning. She ask me what time she died. I told her she was not dead. She said no, not dead, but sleeping. She then kissed us all goodby and hutterd a prayer in our behalf and then to all appearance she was breathing her last, but she gasped very hard and then turnd almost back then her bodie was racked almost to pieces sometimes. I could not stay in the room. She lay in that state until Tuseday morning at 11 o'clock. At 10 minutes to 11 she lookd lovely and passed away without a strugle. She was buried yesterday at 2 o'clock. I shall come home on Saturday if all is well. I want to make father comfortable before I leave him. I feel completley worn out, but I want to get my spirit before I leave. The children are well. Father feels better than I expected he would. He sends his best respects as so does all of my brothers and sister Ester. I must close for this time. Be sure and take care of your health. From your true friend.--- Mary Ann Ovard Smith at the death of her mother Hannah Stoe Ovard, A good Saint. Amen I received a letter from Rashoe Scovile and Ruber C. Fuller."
