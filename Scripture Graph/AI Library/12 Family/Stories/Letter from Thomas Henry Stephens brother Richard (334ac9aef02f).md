# Letter from Thomas Henry Stephens brother Richard

_A story of [[Thomas Henry Stephens (KWNL-JWK)|Thomas Henry Stephens]]._

## The story as it was written

Tunley Farm, Sapperton, Old England, Cirencester dated May 21 1906
Dear Brother
I am very sorry to have to write to say that our Brother Henry passed away at 9 oclock this morning after a few hours illness he was taken at 3 oclock on Sunday morning. John and Gor (George) and myself are keeping fairly well. I have been waiting for an answer of my last letter but I have begun to think it must have been on the mail that was lost just after it was wrote. I trust you are well. How are things looking over the seas. Things are in a very poor state over here in Old England there are great many coming over this spring to try what can be done out under your sunny skys. I shall be very glad to hear from you to know how you all are. All my family are fairly well just now. I should very much one of your papers now and again. I will send you the one which have it in about our dear Brother. I cant tell you more about him. Now trust this will find you all well. I remain your lovingly Brother Richard.
With love from us all here.
