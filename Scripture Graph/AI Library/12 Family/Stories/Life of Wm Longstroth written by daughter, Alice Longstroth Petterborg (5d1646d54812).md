# Life of Wm Longstroth written by daughter, Alice Longstroth Petterborg

_A story of [[Stephen Longstroth (KWJY-1YP)|Stephen Longstroth]], [[Ann Gill (LZ8S-S5B)|Ann Gill]]._

## The story as it was written

The following is the handwritten history for William Longstroth by his daughter, Alice Longstroth Petterborg. Alice only went to the 8th grade in school and her spelling is sometimes very “cute”. The following life sketch is transcribed with her original spelling and punctuation. I have added the scans of the pages, under documents for William, so that her grandchildren can see a sample of her handwriting.
Life of Wm Longstroth.
Wm Longstroth was born in Clifteroe Lankshire Eng. June 15, 1840. Mother Ann Gill Longstroth, & father Stephen Longstroth. Born June 30-1789 and he son of John Longstroth mother or wife of Agnese Sheckelton.. They all reside at Langcliff’s Hamlet.
While in England there home was spelled Langcliffe and while in Eng. They spelled there name Langstroth. When Stephen Longstroth was 25 years of age is when he married Ann Gill. She was christened Aug 8, 1795 at Arncliffe Parish.
They Stephen Ann made there home 19 years during that time they had 9 children. Four live to accompany them to America. There were Alice married to Moses Whitaker. He died in 1851 & she married Geo D Watt who was the first person baptized by the Mormon Elders. Sarah later married Willard Richards.
Sarah and Nanny being Polygamous wives of Willard Richards, councler to the Prophet Joseph Smith. Nannie had 3 children by Willard Richards.
One of whitch was Stephen L. Richards the father of Stephen Longstroth Richards of the first Presidency.
After his death she married. ? the death of Willard Richards she married his nephew, Franklin D. Richards. one of there children were President George F. Richards and a quorm of the 12. Geo who died in SLC 1849
A year later after they arrived 1849, age 19. A daughter Ann married John F Whitney and Willie my father married Charlott Baker Longstroth We all have the births & death & baptism of all the family.
They were converted baptized & confermend on Nov 10 1839. By Orsen Hide & Heber C. Kimble It note each year he was ordained to teacher, Preast while he was ordained to the higher Preasthood he Baptized Margrett & Mary Chipendale, Rich & Rolafe Doughelas and David Nuttle.
On Feb. 5 1842 all set sail to America from Liverpool Eng. Leaving all person belonging behind. how ever it remarks they carried a feather Bed for Nannie which was an invelad at at the time as they left there hearts were sad leaving 7 children buried in England.
The ship was a weeklate in arriving in New Orleans. from there they traveled up the Missipy River to St Louis Missouria where they made the first home in Zion
The Longstroth at the time is noted very poor at the completion of there journey they lived in St Louis 42-44
Nannie was 17 she went to Nauvoo 1844 & married Willard Richards, by Prophet Joseph Smith
? Martyrdos of Pro Joheph Joseph Smith June 27 1844.
