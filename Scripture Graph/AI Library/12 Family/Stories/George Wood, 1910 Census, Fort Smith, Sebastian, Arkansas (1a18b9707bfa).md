## The telling

This record comes from the 1910 federal census, taken in Fort Smith Ward 1, Sebastian County, Arkansas. It shows George Wood, age 31, born about 1879 in Arkansas to Arkansas-born parents, living on North D Street with his wife Flora, age 21, whom he had married six years earlier. George worked as a driver for a transfer company, earning wages, and the family rented the house they lived in. Also in the household were their sons John D. Wood, age 2, and Eugene Wood, an infant under one year old. Sharing the home were Wilfred Dora, 22, Bertha Dora, 21, and a one-year-old named Ruth Dora, likely Flora's relatives. Both George and Flora could read and write, and George had not been out of work at all in the past year. The record notes the family as white, English-speaking, and settled in a working-class household in early twentieth-century Fort Smith.
