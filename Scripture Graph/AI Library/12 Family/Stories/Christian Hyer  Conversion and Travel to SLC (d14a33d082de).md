# Christian Hyer: Conversion and Travel to SLC

_A story of [[Christian Larsen Hyer (KWJH-WBR)|Christian Larsen Hyer]]._

## The story as it was written

Christian Hayer, another son of Lars Hayer tells his own story: "We located ourselves in Wisconsin Territory in the spring of 1843 when an elder of Israle by the name of Ole Olsen Hayer came in the neighborhood, preaching the gospel of the son of God. I was convinced of the truth of the principles he taught and was baptized June 23, same year. As there was no branch of the church there, I moved to LaSalle County, Illinois where there was a branch. In February of 1846, I was called to Nauvoo, Illinois where in the company of others, we received our endowments, and in the Seventies Hall, I was ordained a Seventy. In the spring of 1849, I left my father's family in LaSalle County and came to Salt Lake City, Utah." It was there he dropped the "A" from the family name, making it Hyer and was married to Caroline Hogan. In 1950, their descendants were listed as 38 grandchildren, 231 great grandchildren, and 356 great great grandchildren. Christian was the only one of Lars Hayer's children to unite with the Utah church. Thus we have a testimony of his being a member of the Old Mission Branch for almost six years, probably a very active worker, belonging to the branch priesthood.
(Taken from The History of Mission Branch of the Reorganized Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints, April 1961, by Charles A Reedy)
