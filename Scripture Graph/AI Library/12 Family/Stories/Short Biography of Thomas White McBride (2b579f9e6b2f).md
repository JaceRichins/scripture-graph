# Short Biography of Thomas White McBride

_A story of [[Thomas White McBride (KWJR-J45)|Thomas White McBride]], [[Catherine Ruth John (LLQX-D8P)|Catherine Ruth John]]._

## The story as it was written

Biography
12 Mar. 17761–30 Oct. 1838.2 Farmer.3 Born in Virginia.4 Son of James McBride and Mary White.5 Married Catharine John, 28 Sept. 1797, in Berkeley Co., Virginia (later in Jefferson Co., West Virginia).6 Moved to Lancaster, Fairfield Co., Ohio, 1810.7 Moved to Amanda, Fairfield Co., by 1820.8 Settled on branch of Mohican River, Wayne Co., Ohio, 1820.9 Served as justice of the peace in Wayne Co.10 Resided in Jackson, Wayne Co., 1830.11 Baptized into LDS church by Harvey Green, ca. 1831, in Wayne Co.12 Moved to Richland Co., Ohio, fall 1833; to Bowling Green, Pike Co., Missouri, June 1834; to Ray Co., Missouri, spring 1836; and near Hawn’s Mill settlement on Shoal Creek (later in Caldwell Co.), Missouri, Sept. 1836.13 Served as justice of the peace in Grand River Township (later subdivided into several townships), Caldwell Co.14 Killed during attack at Hawn’s Mill.15
