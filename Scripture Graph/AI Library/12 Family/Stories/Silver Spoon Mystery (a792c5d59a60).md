# Silver Spoon Mystery

_A story of [[Ann Olsdatter (L5XC-4J5)|Ann Olsdatter]]._

## The story as it was written

"A hand-made silver spoon has been handed down to the youngest daughter in each Hayer family since 1705. It was brought to this country by Ann Olson Hayer. It is now (1953) in the possession of Pearl V. Anderson, daughter of Louisa Elefson Anderson. These initials are on the back of the spoon, but the meaning is not known: O.M.E.L. D.P.D 1905 C.B."
Hayer, Oliver. What We Know About the Hayer Family, page10. Logan, UT: Published 1954.
