# Alice's Bio

_A story of [[Stephen Longstroth (KWJY-1YP)|Stephen Longstroth]]._

## The story as it was written

Alice Longstroth Watt, George F. Richards’s aunt, was born on 28 January 1824 to Ann Gill and Stephen Longstroth in Arncliffe, Yorkshire, England.
While living in Navoo, Illinois Alice met and married Moses Whitaker. The couple soon after completed the trek to Utah and took up residence in Salt Lake City where her husband served as a member of the seventy in the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and in the militia until his death in 1852 at the age of 33 while Alice was the age of 28. The couple having no children.
On Dec 11, 1853 Alice became the fourth wife of six in a plural marriage situation to George Darling Watt who died in October of 1881 after 28 years of marriage. The couple had no children.
 Alice remained a widow a further 18 years until her death on 21 November 1909 in Layton, Utah.[1]
