# Historical Sketch of James John McBride

_A story of [[James John McBride (KWJ5-DHB)|James John McBride]]._

## The story as it was written

MCBRIDE, JAMES (son of Thomas McBride and Catherine John of Iowa). Born May 15, 1818, in Ohio. Came to Utah in October, 1850. Married Olive M. Cheney (daughter of Aaron Cheney and Mahetable Wells, pioneers 1850). She was born May 16, 1817. Only child:
Amos O., m. Mary E. Jenson.
High priest. Farmer. Died Jan. 6, 1881, Grantsville, Utah.
Source: Esshom, Frank Elwood. Pioneers and Prominent Men of Utah. Salt Lake City, UT: Utah Pioneers Book Publishing Co., 1913. LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.
