# James Lythgoe Probably around 1870's

_A story of [[Esther Wilcock (LB89-VQR)|Esther Wilcock]]._

## The story as it was written

James Lythgoe born 15 March 1842 Pendlebury, Lancashire, Cheshire, England.
Brother of Rachel Florence Capson Peck's grandfather, John Lythgoe. John was killed in a mine in Coalsville, UT.
THey are the sons of Thomas Lythgoe, a green grocer who remained in England and died there in 1887.
His wife (THomas'), the mother of James and John Lythgoe came to Utah- Esther Wilcocks Lythgoe- died 1885- Hennifer, Utah.
