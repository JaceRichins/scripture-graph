# Aaron Cheny, Father of Elam Cheney

_A story of [[Aaron Cheney (KNX4-J2Z)|Aaron Cheney]]._

## The story as it was written

On July14,1787, Aaron Cheney was born in Berwich, Providence County Maine. When but a small boy his father moved to Grandby, Vermont. When he was 26 or 27 he met and married Mehitable Wells, Aaron was a propsperous farmer, a factory owner, and a good business manager. About the time of his marriage he moved to the town of Marshfield, Vermont and in 1812 he sold some land in Grandby, Vermont to his younger brother Hurd. Twelve years later he sold two remaining pieces of property in Grandby. He moved to Freedom, Chattaragus County, New York, 1817, where his father's family was located. Where he gained several lots of land. While in Freedom, Aaron built and operated an Iron Foundry with a large crew of men. He produced great quantities of cast iron.
Aaron and his wife were religious people, being faithful members of the Methodist Church. He was chosen Trustee in 18227 of the First Methodist Episcopal Church of Sandusky. In 1831 they joined the Mormon Church. In 1835 Aaron and family moved to Kirtland Ohio. He purchased a farm which is located two miles southeast of where the Kirtland temple was built. After building a house, he saw possibilities of an Iron Foundry in the new country so he built and operated one. In 1836 the Kirtland camp organized by the Seventies of the L>D. S. Church for the purpose of going to Missouri and building up Zion, was established, Aaron Cheney, Amos Cheney, Orin Cheney, Nathan Cheney and Elijah Cheney with their families were among those who enlisted in the camp. Just what relation Nathan and Elijah Cheney were to Aaron and his son Orin is not known. Elijah Cheney and Zera Pulsipher baptized President Wilford Woodruff while on a mission to the east ;(31st of Dec 1833).
Owing to sickness, Aaron was forced to stop temporarily at Decatur, Mason Count Illinois on the 19th o Sept. 1838. While delayed he fenced a piece of ground, built a small house and raised a crop. The Prophet Joseph Smith stayed on night at his house when he was on a memorable trip to Washington DC for the purpose of laying the grievances of the Saints before the President of the United States.
The next spring the Cheney's continued their journey to Nauvoo, where Aaron help build the temple. On Dec 22, 1845 Aaron and wife did their own temple work. Later they came west with the Saints in 1850.
