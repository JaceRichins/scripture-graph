# Emma Louise Turner Stayner Pioneer, Wife, Mother

_A story of [[Emma Irene Turner (KWJ6-XX3)|Emma Irene Turner]]._

## The story as it was written

Emma Louise Turner Stayner was born November 21, 1834 in Great Malvern Worcester, England to Henry and Anna Steed Turner, as the eldest of their ten children.
Emma Louise attended an all-girls school when she was young, and learned art, music, sewing, embroidery, and propriety in addition to the normal academic subjects. Emma crossed the plains in 1856, a year before her family.
Emma Louise married Arthur Stayner and had nine children. At the young age of 41, she contracted Mountain Fever and died in 1875. Emma Louise is buried beside Arthur in the Salt Lake City Cemetery.
