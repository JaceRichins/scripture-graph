## The story as it was written

Letter to James Macefield Fowler
From Parents: George and Ellen Fowler
Dated: May 14, 1863
82 Duke Street
Wolverhampton
Dear Son James
 I now take up my pen to write to you hoping this will fiend sic you in good health as am glat to day this leves us all at this time. Thanks be to our heavenly father for all his good ness to us. We have received a few lines from you through Bro & Sister Poultons Cotten this morning and we was very glad to here that you are well, but we was very much seprised sic to fiend sic that you had wrot sic to us foure sic letters, and we have not received one line from you sence sic you was at florance ?. We received a letter from Eliza about las sic Christmas and it said that you and Loranzo had arived sic safe there, but we have not had any letter since, and me and your mother began to think that we was soon forgot, but it aperes sic that your letters have been detained somewhere as we never have had one. I hope you have received the few lines which sent you through Bro. Sis. Poultons letter. You will see in there that we have not had any word from you. We have herde sic through Charlotte Pattin's letter that you had writen sic to her in New York. We thought it very strenge sic that you could not fiend sic time to write to us, but we understand now how it is. Well James we hope to have a letter from you and tell us all the news, and how you are prospering in the things of the Kingdom of God, becase sic we know if you are going on well in these matters we are sure you are doing well in every othere sic thing. I dohope sic you have herd sic of your Brother George beening sic dead? but if you have not herd sic the particlers sic about it, I will tell you a little about it allthough sic the Subject is still painfull sic one. you and Lorenzo Left one the Saterday sic and George give up work on monday middel sic of the Day and he

Did not work after, he was ill 12 weeks when he died. You must Excuse me for not Entering in to more Perticlers sic of this painfull sic a feare sic . We had grette sic trouble of mind and of Body for many a night and day. Well Blessed be god for his goodness to us in giving us Strength to up hold us in all our Difficlty sic . These things all most sic brought your other to Death Doore sic , but thank God she is got pretty well againe sic . James you have been gone just turned twelve months, but I do asure sic you many are the ups and downs since you left . . . very unplesent sic one to. I cane sic say and timely that the times are geting sic worse & worse and the Peopel sic are geting sic more wicked Every Day. Murders and Sucide sic are the order of the day. Very much sickness as Proved and is still Proveling sic amungst sic all classes of seciety sic . Old Mr. Charles Clark our master shot himself at his home residence last Febuery sic . No case we cannot asine sic for it, and many have Died in this nabourhood sic since you left. Well James in the midest of all these things we do not feel to be discuridgesic yet but feel to go on and do the best we cane sic and keep the Commandments of God, and then we know all will be well as you know we are still in old babeland sic battling a gains sic the meny sic difficultys sic , and we very oftimes Pray that oure sic Deliverenc sic was comsic , but still we are here and God know how long we shall remain here. but there is one satisfaction to us we have delevered sic some of oure sic children, and we Do hope that you will all of you Do what you cane sic for us. Evans nor Hurst as not sent us even a letter yet mor sic I do not know there address or I wold sic write to them. if you cane sic get to know there whereabouts Plese sic let me know. Brother Probert wife and family are getting ready to com sic out. I Expect she will sail about the 24 of this month. I pray God that she may have a prosprus sic journey to Zion.

There is no one eles sic coming from Wolverhampton this year. I have wnoton sic to say that Chapels is coming out this season but I beleve sic the have softened there mindes a gaine sic . There is a gret sic many going to Emigrate sic this season. We are going on at the work about as usual. Only the masters wants more work and to give less money. They are never satisfied do what you will. You are watched. Do what you will. Brother Adkins works in your old jobe sic and is geting long prettey well. Many of the old hands at the works are Dead sence sic you left, whose names I do not now . . . . Old John Jones is among the namber sic . We are geting along as regards the Church Prettey sic well. We are Batizing sic a few and theSaints are feeling well. That is those that are living there sic religion, but you know that there is those that do notlive as they should do, but I belivesic it is all right. It is nedfull sic to have such characters to keep us a weak or we Pospelsic should go to sleep. You will be no doubt bne seprised sic when I tell you that President W. G. Mills as was is Cut off from the Church about 10 of April last for adultry and whont sic is so strong in the afear sic is that he as been carring sic it on so long, for this last 15 months, not only with one but with several of the sisters in Birmingham and eles where sic . There is no wonder at others carring sic it on, when such men as him that holds such a Pession sic in the Church and Kingdom of God. It us cased sic a gret stire sic in this conference. The same spirit as that of adultry as more or les spred sic through the conference. There is scenstey sic a Branch In the Conference but what as been practizing the same it as been carred sic on in Wolverhampton Prettey well. There is Alfred Turner and Hunah Smith, as Done Very well for a big man like him, for she as brought him two a boy & a girl, but the are now married. The sic was married about last Christmas, and she was confiend sic in about 3 months afterward. So you see they was not long about it. There are others but I forbere sic mentioning names. The thing will come out in there time and in there season. These . . . are a disgrace to same.

Now James all these things should be a leasson sic for every one both man and woman, and the Evil Consquences leding sic to those and every other evil. Such things are but little thought of in the world, but such thing should nnot be named among Latter Day Saints. Let a lone Practized sic . Mills and his wife have left Birmingham. I here sic that they are somewhere yet in England. I should think that he never will show is face againe sic in the Valley of the mounting or any where else where there is Latter Day Saints. The trend in England still continus sic to be very bad, meny sic thousands are out of work, nor mon nor food nor home things never looked so bad as the Do at the present time, if a man gets out of work it is of no use of him traveling to look for work, for he may well Set down as to travel. Poverty and Ditness sic is on the incress sic , and there is no likeyhood sic of it been sic any better. There as been a gret sic deal of robres sic this last winter. Murders not a few. Now James a word or two about Emigration , as you and Eliza are belessed sic with the Priveleg sic of been geathered sic to the valley of the mountains. We that . . . left beind sic in old babeland sic should be glad with same privelg sic , for our harts sic are sick all the Day long to have been the . . . of oppression, and the meny sic wrongs that we have to put up with. It Dose sic apeare sic some times that all hell is lose a gainist sic us and that our dome sic is to lay our Bones Down here as we have hard work to get along, as there is no one but myself to work. We think it very unkind of James Evans and David Hinst of not writing to us to give us some incuregment sic when they wold sic send us . . . many if we had that L. G. 10-0 - it wold sic be agnes sic help to us . . . . We could do but that is very little. We should be glad you could get a leaf out of Bother Probents Book to get to . . . how he as done. It to get is wife and family out this season. I have since I begine sic this letter received Sister Probents and family notification from Liverpool for them to sail on the 24 of this month May. They will be over at our house on the 23 to be ready to go to Liverpool on the 26. I must now conclud sic for this time Praying god to bless and Prase sic you and Eliza and Lorenza and all that Desires to Do right, with our love and affection. George & Ellen Fowler your father and mother.
Children sends there kind love to you.
