# RLDS Connection

_A story of [[Lars Andersen Heyer (LKK2-XKN)|Lars Andersen Heyer]]._

## The story as it was written

Mamie Elfreda Johnson, daughter of Oscar H. and Bertha Hayer Johnson, was baptized August 27, 1911, at Plano, Illinois, by Elder Lester O. Wildermuth. She was the fifth generation of the Hayer clan in the Reorganized Church, tracing her lineage down through her grandfather, Goodman L. Hayer, through his father, Austin back to the first Lars Hayer. She never married. She has been a member of Mission Branch for 50 years.
(Excerpt is taken from the History of Mission Branch of the Reorganized Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints, April 1961, by Charles A. Reedy)
