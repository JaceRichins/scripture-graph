# Wm J. Smith, Son in Law Recounts a Blessing

_A story of [[Hannah Stowe (KWJD-SCL)|Hannah Stowe]]._

## The story as it was written

"I went on the 13th to my Wifes Mother, Hannah Ovard, and found her very ill. She
said if I would go and minister to her she would get better. I went 35 miles and found
her very ill. Cold Swets, Terrible Cough. I ministered to her and then ministered and I
wrote what the Spirit Indited. In two days [s]he was much better. I returned home on
15th to City" See Transcribed Wm J Smith Journal entry page 39 (3.2.30)
