# History of Robert Chapel and Prudence Golding Shill

_A story of [[Robert Chapel Shill (KWJ7-586)|Robert Chapel Shill]], [[Prudence Golding (LZPH-MQM)|Prudence Golding]]._

## The story as it was written

Robert Chapel Shill was born on June 7, 1789, in Colesborne, England. His parents were Robert and Mary Shill Chappel. Robert’s father may have been a sailor on a ship out of London. Robert was three years old when his mother married Thomas Lawrence. Robert John and Joan Mary Perry Golding lived in Shipton, England, when their daughter, Prudence Golding, was born on October 14, 1787. She was the seventh of ten children.
 Robert and Prudence were married on May 30, 1809, in Syde, England. They had twelve children—Richard Golding (born August 12, 1810, Shipton, England), Robert (born December 11, 1811, Syde, England), Mary (born December 12, 1812, Syde, England), Elizabeth (born July 31, 1814, Syde, England), Susanna (born May 12, 1816, Syde, England), Rachel (born May 12, 1817, Syde, England), John (born December 18, 1818, Syde, England), Charles Golding (born February 12, 1821, Syde, England), William (born April 10, 1822, Syde, England), Ann (born January 21, 1823, Syde, England), George (born June 1, 1826, Syde, England), and Louisa (born June 29, 1829, Syde, England). Robert earned a living as a farmer. He worked hard to support his large family.
 Missionaries from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints were in the Gloucestershire area and taught members of the Shill family. Charles Golding, a son, was first to join the Church on May 12, 1846. Prudence joined the Church, along with some of her children. This took place in July 11, 1846. Robert was baptized on February 2, 1847. Both Prudence and Robert were baptized by their son, Charles Golding. The family attended the Caudle Green Branch of the Church. Prudence later wrote a letter to her son, Charles Golding Shill, who was at the time serving as a missionary in London. She was a woman of great faith and reported to him that she continued to attend Church meetings in Sheepscomb and that they had a full house every Sunday.
 Prudence wanted to go to the Salt Lake Valley to be with other members of the Church, but died on September 30, 1851, in Syde, England. She was able to read and write, evidenced by eloquent letters to her children.
 Robert did travel to the United States with Charles Golding and his family. They sailed on the ship, “Chimboraizo” and landed in Philadelphia. They traveled by rail and by boat to St. Louis. They stayed in St. Louis, Missouri, while preparing for the journey to Salt Lake City.
 Robert was a hard worker. He accepted the beliefs of the restored Church of Jesus Christ and tried to follow the Savior’s teachings. While in St. Louis, son, Charles Golding Shill, worked as a carpenter and was away from the family all week. Before leaving on one occasion, Robert said to Charles, “Goodbye Charles, I shall never see thee again.” When Charles returned at the end of the week, he found that his father had passed away. Robert died on October 6, 1856, in St. Louis, Missouri.
