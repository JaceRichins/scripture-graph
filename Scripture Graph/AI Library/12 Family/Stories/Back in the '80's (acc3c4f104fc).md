# Back in the '80's

_A story of [[Søren Christensen (KWJ5-DCW)|Søren Christensen]]._

## The story as it was written

I went looking for grandpa Soren Christensen's Patriarchal Blessing. I found his blessing on an index at the library and was told the blessings were in the church office building only. So I went there and the matron tells me it does not exist. She also states no one else but her is allowed to go looking in the film room. But after she realizes I am not leaving without it and I promise I will take only 5 minutes to grab it, she lets me proceed: a miracle. I kept my promise by finding it in less than five minutes. So please copy this blessing and treasure it. Acquisition of it did not come easy. For one thing, although his name is Soren Christensen from birth, he goes by Soren Sorensen in the USA since he wants his name to be the same as his daughter, Caroline's. This unknown truth may have blocked some in their searches, for awhile. Soren and Caroline were in the Willie handcart company with a wagon. The rest of the family had perished by the time they arrived in Missouri.
