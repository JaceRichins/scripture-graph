# The Longstroth Seal of the Langstroth Coat of Arms

_A story of [[Stephen Longstroth (KWJY-1YP)|Stephen Longstroth]]._

## The story as it was written

My father, Jay William Wright born 1909 had in his possession the Longstroth seal. It is now in possession of his son, Timothy Clawson Wright. Using the small seal, Jay had a gold impression made and then mounted it onto a powder compact for his mother, Sarah Longstroth Wright. The compact is in the possession of his daughter, Lucy Charlotte Wright Openshaw
