# The scar on her Arm

_A story of [[Mary Ann Stearns (KWJC-2J2)|Mary Ann Stearns]]._

## The story as it was written

While her mother was visiting Parley P. Pratt in jail, Mary (jr.) broke her arm when the hatch door closed. In the commotion her mother, who had hidden papers from the prophet, was not searched. See her mothers obituary attached.
Mary would roll up her sleeve exposing her scar proudly and tell the story of her bravery in helping the prophet to her grandchildren.
