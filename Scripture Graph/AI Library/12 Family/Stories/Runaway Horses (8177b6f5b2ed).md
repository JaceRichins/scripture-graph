# Runaway Horses

_A story of [[Newel Kimball Whitney (KWVM-M24)|Newel Kimball Whitney]]._

## The story as it was written

Grandpa and the Prophet Joseph went on many trips together. They went to New York to try to obtain funds to help further the work. One time they went to Missouri together, to see how the Saints were doing there. They went down the Ohio River and from there they took a carriage to St. Louis and on to Independence. On their way back, something scared the horses and they ran away. The Prophet jumped from the carriage and cleared the wheel, but when Grandpa jumped he caught his foot in the wheel and broke his leg in several places. So they had to stay in this little town for four weeks while Grandpa's leg healed.
Every day they were together, and on this one particular day the Prophet came to the bedroom where Grandpa lay and he said, "Newel, we've got to get out of here. Someone has poisoned my food. They do not like the Mormons here and they have tried to kill me. I have been so sick and vomited so hard that I have thrown my jaw out of place and I've had to put it back myself. And I would like you to give me a blessing." Which Grandpa did.
But then the Prophet said, "We must move out of here early in the morning. We can't stay here any longer."
Grandpa said, "Oh, Prophet Joseph, I can't move. I can't walk. I can't go now."
The Prophet said, "We will go in the morning. I am going to give you a blessing." And at that time, he laid his hands on Grandpa's head and gave him a blessing that he would be able to move in the morning and walk and be able to catch a carriage for them to return to Kirtland.
This was so. It all happened and they returned to Kirtland in safety.
(As told by Newel K. Whitney's great grandson, Norman K Whitney)
