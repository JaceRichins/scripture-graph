# HOW TO FIND "WESTSIDE FARM" (USE THE POSTAL CODE BELOW IN A GPS)

_A story of [[Stephen Longstroth (KWJY-1YP)|Stephen Longstroth]]._

## The story as it was written

From the Journal of Rex Hansen Petterborg, 15 September 2016:
On Thursday morning we went into Langcliffe and Pam (my wife) was able to go to the quilt shop and store at the Watershed Mill in Langcliffe. While she was there, I went over to Giggleswick and saw the Church of England there. My family had used that church, as well as the ones in Kirkby Malham and Arncliffe, before moving to Clitheroe. Upon my return, I spoke to the lady in the quilt shop about my ancestors and learned she had worked at or near Malham Tarn for 14 years and she thought she remembered Westside House. She told me of a farm on Henside Road owned by Alan and Sue Lodge. She said if I would inquire of them they would be able to tell me which one it was. As we are getting near the location I saw a man and his son driving some sheep up the road in a two seater ATV. As I was waiting for him, I saw another farm with a man standing outside so I stopped and asked him. He said that the man I was following pushing the sheep was Alan Lodge and that his home was Westside House or Farm and was the next one up the road. So we got back in the car and caught up to Alan Lodge and his son. The son got out of the ATV at one point and I confirmed he was a Lodge. When they stopped to get the sheep into a pasture, I spoke to Alan Lodge, the father, and told him who I was and asked if he would visit with me when done with the sheep. He agreed and we followed him to the farm. I asked him questions about the farm and took pictures. I learned that the house had been built in 1702, it is two stories with coal fireplaces on the first and second floor (he said the second floor one had been sealed off and they now primarily heat the house with oil). His parents had originally rented the farm in 1890 and then purchased it in 1946. The house is three bedrooms and one bath and the farm includes 310 acres and he has 1300 sheep and 70 cows. He uses them to raise calves. The focus is no longer on milking although they apparently did that in the past. I also learned that the house has a small cellar that you can enter from inside the home. There was a barn and a couple other out buildings already on the property when his parents rented it. They have since added a couple other barn type buildings and a home that his son lives in. He said the formal address is:
“Westside Farm” (also called Westside House), Henside Road, Lancliffe, Settle, North Yorkshire, BD24 9PR.
Note: These immediately preceding numbers are the Postal Code you can use in a GPS to find the approximate location of the original Longstroth Farm near Lancliffe, Yorkshire, England.
