# Budge Family Reunion

_A story of [[Robert Sutton Budge (KWZM-X9J)|Robert Sutton Budge]], [[June Stayner (KWZM-X9K)|June Stayner]]._

## The story as it was written

At the end of every summer we would go to the Robert Budge family reunion. Grandpa Budge volunteered to help out with the Cache Valley Council for the Boys Scouts. His involvement allowed him contact with various persons who managed the scout camps for the area. Every year Grandpa would reserve a week in August for his family to go to Camp Hunt on the shores of Bear Lake. Every year we would go and stay in the cabins. There was one cabin we always stayed on up close to the gate on the left hand side. I don’t remember the name of it today. We would go down to the beach every day and play in the water or in the sand. As we got older we would take out the canoes, row boats or kayaks. Sometimes they had a motor that could be attached to the row boat. They would tow us in tubes. For various years Gandpa had a motor boat as well that we’d all get to ride in. The older people would get to ski. I was never old enough to ski, but I remember many rides on the boat as well as launching it from the marina. Grandpa also had a four wheeler for various years. He would hitch a trailer on it and give us rides to the beach. Grandma would dress up in her swimsuit when she was younger. Sometimes I remember her wearing a yellow wig or fuzzy hat to the beach. There were many water fights. My dad was especially known for getting buckets of water and drenching the adults on the shore who were not getting into the water. Many of the adults did not like this act. These water fights are always a fun recollection among those adults today. We would have breakfast at the mess hall. My uncle Allen Palmer served in the Navy as a cook. He made excellent breakfasts. There would also be cooking of other meals in the mess hall. One year there was an ice cream machine. My aunt Nancy went and purchased some supplies so we kids could have all the ice cream we wanted. In the evening we had dinner and then a program of some nature. Grandma always had crafts of gluing sea shells to wood or something like that. Sometimes we would have camp fires by the beach and stores about the bear lake monster would be told. Other times at night we would have night games around the totem pole. We’d play kick the can and other games that would be so much fun. In the early morning grandpa would play taps to wake everyone up. Grandma and Grandpa would stay in the cabin where the camp director would stay. While staying there Grandpa would trim trees and clean various areas up to help maintain the camp for others who came along after. For we kids it was an adventure every time we went to see what would be there, what buildings would be open, etc.
