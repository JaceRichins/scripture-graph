# Sacrifice brings forth the blessings of heaven

_A story of [[John Allen Sutton (KWZ8-RFV)|John Allen Sutton]]._

## The story as it was written

In England, November 1849, John A. Sutton defied threats of violence in order to be baptized. On the way to the stream, Elder Henry Sanders and John were followed by dozens of people who said they would drown the Elder if he baptized John. The missionary and the new convert sang a hymn and prayed. The ordinance was performed; afterward, "not a word was said by anyone."
John and his father were Wheelwrights, and the persecution extended to threats of taking away work from them. John, not yet of age, left home, a refugee of religious persecution. He later returned home and married, still under age, before deciding to gather with the Saints in America. Without support from family, he left his homeland and his young wife, never to see her again in this life.
He made these sacrifices willingly and gratefully, for the truth.
