Mehitable Wells was born on 31 January 1787 to Selah Wells and Mehitable Tuthill, they had 13 children, Mehitable was their 4th child. Selah Wells is a descendent of King Edward I.
Mehitable was among the loyalist refugees who came with her family from Long Island, New York to Guilford, Maine after the Revolutionary War.
She married Aaron Cheney on 14 August 1807 in Marshfield, Washington County, Vermont, they had 13 children. She and Aaron were religious people and faithful members of the Methodist Church.
In 1817, Mehitable and Aaron moved to Freedom, Cattaraugus County, New York to be closer to Aaron’s parents. Their families owned and operated an iron foundry that produced great quantities of cast iron.
In 1834, the Prophet Joseph Smith and Orson Pratt taught the Cheney family the gospel. Aaron and his sons, Elam and Orin, joined the Restored Church first and Mehitable joined a short time later. In 1835, the family moved to Kirtland, Ohio to gather with the main body of the Saints. Aaron built an iron foundry and participated in the construction of the Kirtland Temple.
The Cheney family attended the dedication of the Kirtland Temple and Mehitable’s son, Elam, age 11, noticed there were six personages, 3 on each side of the room. He described them as being dressed in white robes with white caps on their heads and wearing white moccasins on their feet. He asked his mother, Mehitable, who they were, and she replied she could not see them. Elam realized later in life that they were wearing temple clothing.
The Cheney family volunteered to move to Missouri to help build Zion but in route, Aaron got sick and the family decided to temporarily stay in Decatur, Illinois until he was strong enough to finish the trip. In the meantime, the Saints were driven from Missouri to Nauvoo, so the Cheney family moved to Nauvoo in 1840.
The Cheney family were present when the cornerstone of the Nauvoo Temple was laid on April 6, 1841. Aaron worked on the Temple.
Aaron saw the Prophet Joseph Smith leave for Carthage and heard him remark, “I am going like a lamb to the slaughter.” Aaron participated in retrieving the bodies of the martyred brothers. He was present at their funeral and attended the meeting when President Brigham Young stood and declared that leadership for the Church rested with the 12 Apostles.
Since Mehitable’s husband was an experienced blacksmith and carpenter, the Cheney family constructed their own wagon for the journey westward. They joined the George A. Smith/Dan Jones company in 1849.
The family eventually settled in Tooele County. Mehitable exercised strong faith and made sacrifices to move her family multiple times to unknown places. She died 30 November 1869 at the age of 82 and is buried in Grantsville.
