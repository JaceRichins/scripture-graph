# 1868 Letter to James from his mother, Eleanor Fowler

_A story of [[James Macefield Fowler (KWNL-J7K)|James Macefield Fowler]]._

## The story as it was written

Letter to James Macefield Fowler
From Ellen Fowler
Date: 23 August 1868
Dear Son and Daughter
 I am very sorrow to take my pen in hand at this time to wright the painful intlenges of the sickens and death of your father. He took sick on the 15 and died on the 20th of inflamation of the kidnes his sicknes was short but very sever but we did all we could for him but it did no good the reson of sickness he lost his crop by grasshopers and he had to do something for a liven so he went to herd sheep down to the lake so he took cold with bean out in the rain and drinking the lake water. O' James he wished so much to see you and I feell so lonesome without him the partner of my youth and the father of my children and I fell wore out both in body and mind James but my trust is in God for he says he will be a father to the fatherless and a husband to the widdow so my trust is in him and it was your fathers last wish that I should be near you and I have a good many
things to se to and I would like if you could come down to help me to fix up things for I am not able to look after them my self and we would like to come there if we had things fixed up for we have nothing to live on here but bread and water and hardly that it has bean very hard for us this summer and I fell to be away from here as quick as poseble so James wright what I had better... and wether you could come or not for I would like to sell our house and land so use your jugment what to do.
The nebuars and William has done there best for me and him Brigham has bean very sick but is a good deall better Sariah is porly she is very sickly to Eliza is prety well William has bean very sick they send there love to you P hely Roberts send her love to you
and Rachal so I will conclude my letter so no more at present from sorrowfull mother and sister
 Ellen Fowler
 an Sarrah Fowler
ps.
 We telegraphed to you from Payson to come when your father took sick for you to come and see him but it sems you never got the news.
 E F
