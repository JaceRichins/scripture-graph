# Sarah Elizabeth Ovard and Joseph Thomas Jordan

_A story of [[Thomas Ovard (KWJD-SCG)|Thomas Ovard]], [[Hannah Stowe (KWJD-SCL)|Hannah Stowe]]._

## The story as it was written

Sarah Elizabeth Ovard was born on the 22nd of September 1839 in Swerford, Oxfordshire, England, a daughter of Thomas and Hannah Stowe Ovard. She was the
Not much is know about her life as a child, but when she was a young woman, she sailed to America from England with her parents and siblings.
In 1860, she shows up as coming to Utah with the Eardley Family in the William Budge Company, who left on the 20th of July 1860 and arrived in the Salt Lake Valley on the 5th of October 1860. This was the same year her parents came to Utah with the Jesse Murphy Company who left on the 19th of June 1860 and arrived in the Salt Lake Valley on the 30th of August 1860.
Also in the William Budge company was a man by the name of Thomas J. Jordan who was 30 years old. Could this be her future husband, Joseph Thomas Jordan who was born 16th of April 1830, in Whixley, Yorkshire West Riding, England?
Sarah Elizabeth Ovard married Joseph Thomas Jordan, in the Endowment House in Salt Lake City, Utah on the 20th of July 1861. It is a sad state of affairs that Sarah Elizabeth Ovard died on the 20th of October 1861, just three months after their marriage.
Joseph Thomas Jordan was born on the 16th of April 1830 in Whixley, Yorkshire West Riding, England. At the time of this marriage to Sarah Elizabeth Ovard in Salt Lake City, he was a widower. His first wife's name was Margaret Richley.
Joseph Thomas Jordan and Margaret Richley were married on the 28th of April 1855 in Newcastle Upon Tyne, Northumberland, England. Their first child, a son, Joseph Thomas Jordan, was born on the 29th of April 1859 in St. Louis, Missouri. He died on the 20th of July 1857. Margaret gave birth to another child, Joseph Alma Jordan, the 24th of December, 1858. Margaret died on the 17th of September 1859, less than a year later.
After his mother died, the baby Joseph Alma Jordan was adopted by William and Sarah A. Haddon. He went by the name of Alma Haddon throughout his life and the name of Haddon is on his death record along with the name of William and Sarah A Haddon as his parents.
Sarah Elizabeth Ovard and Joseph Thomas Jordan only spent a short time together as husband and wife. She was only 21 years old when she died.
Joseph Thomas Jordan married Jane Murdoch on the 11th of November 1862 in Logan, Cache County, Utah. They had 9 children.
