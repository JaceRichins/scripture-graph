# William Budge was a Polygamist

_A story of [[William Budge (KWC8-8X9)|William Budge]]._

## The story as it was written

William Budge had three wives, totaling up to 36 kids. According to Grant Budge Wiser, he was called upon by the Lord along with others to, if they were worthy, have multiple spouses. He had three separate homes with a little hiding place in each just in case the authorities came to take him away for his polygamy. He was never caught!
