# Daniel Miley Smith, Jr.

_A story of [[Daniel Miley Smith Sr (KWJC-G74)|Daniel Miley Smith Sr]], [[Emma Hannah Sinfield (KWJC-G7W)|Emma Hannah Sinfield]]._

## The story as it was written

Daniel was the second of 9 children born to Daniel and Emma Smith. He was born in 1866 in Pleasant Grove, Utah. Daniel was baptized at the age of 11.
He married Margaret Emeretta White in 1885 in Pleasant Grove when he was 18 and she was 20. They had 9 children, one of whom was Jenav who married Albert John Olsen. Daniel died in Pleasant Grove in 1934 at the age of 68 of chronic cystitis and was buried in Pleasant Grove.
