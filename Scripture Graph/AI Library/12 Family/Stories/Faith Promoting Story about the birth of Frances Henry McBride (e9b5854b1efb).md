# Faith Promoting Story about the birth of Frances Henry McBride

_A story of [[Henry Francis McBride (KW8L-S3Z)|Henry Francis McBride]], [[Caroline Sørensen (KWNW-G5G)|Caroline Sørensen]], [[Thomas Aaron McBride (KWNW-G5L)|Thomas Aaron McBride]]._

## The story as it was written

FAITH PROMOTING STORY
AS TOLD AT GRANDFATHER MC BRIDE’S FUNERAL [Thomas Aaron McBride]
[TOLD TO WYLA TAYSOM AMES ABOUT 1925]
It happened one year when the McBride familywere living up in the hills. Caroline gave birth to a baby boy (Elam). Her life was despaired of. For nearly three days she lay nearly dead.
At that time one of the Twelve Apostles of the Church was in Oakley [Idaho] for a visit. He was called with the President of the Stake to go to administer to Sister McBride. In sealing the anointing, he blessed her saying “…and you shall give birth to another son this day.”
That evening another baby boy was born and Sister McBride immediately began to recover.
[Elam Leroy, was the first twin to be born and did not live. The second twin, Francis Henry, lived to 1954]
