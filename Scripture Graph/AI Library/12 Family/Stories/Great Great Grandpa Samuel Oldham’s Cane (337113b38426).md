# Great Great Grandpa Samuel Oldham’s Cane

_A story of [[Samuel Oldham (KWZ1-FBS)|Samuel Oldham]]._

## The story as it was written

The cane in these pictures belonged to Samuel Oldham(my great-great grandfather). My grandpa (Reed) Oldham son of William B. Oldham remembered his grandpa visiting when he was a little boy in Hibbard near Rexburg on the farm and his grandpa using this cane. The cane was passed to William, then my grandfather Reed, then my father Lyle and now myself.
