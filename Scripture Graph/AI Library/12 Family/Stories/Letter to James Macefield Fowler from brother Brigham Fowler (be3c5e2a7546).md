# Letter to James Macefield Fowler from brother Brigham Fowler

_A story of [[James Macefield Fowler (KWNL-J7K)|James Macefield Fowler]]._

## The story as it was written

(This transcription is corrected from the original. The original image is below.)
Dear Brother James I write these few lines hoping to find in good health, as it leaves me at present thanking God for it. I was glad to hear from you for we thought you was lost. James I am glad to hear that you have got your cattle and you are doing so well. Mother says you must do all you can to get us out of this land, as we are about tired of being here. I should like to see you James and Eliza and Lorenzo and I hope it will not be long. First you must get us a small little pony to ride up on and one for Dorcas. I will now conclude for this time praying God to bless you until, see Brigham Fowler.
Your brother
Dorcas has sent you some kisses and sings a good night James, warmest first going. XXXXXXXXXXXX
