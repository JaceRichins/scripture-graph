# Life Story of Aaron Cheney

_A story of [[Aaron Cheney (KNX4-J2Z)|Aaron Cheney]]._

## The story as it was written

Aaron Cheney
Aaron Cheney was born in Berwick Maine. When but a small boy his father moved to Grandby, Vermont. When he was 26 or 27 he met and married Mehitable Wells. Aaron was a prosperous farmer, a factory owner, and a good business manager. About the time of his marriage he moved to the town of Marshfield, Vermont. In 1812 he sold two remaining pieces of property in Grandby. He moved to Freedom, N.Y. (Chataragus County) in 1817 where his father’s family was located and where he gained possession of several lots of land. While in Freedom Aaron built and operated an Iron Foundry with a large crew of men. He produced great quanities of cast iron.
Aaron and his wife were religious people, being faithful members of the Methodist Church. He was chosen trustee in 1827 of the first Methodist Episcople Church of Sanducky. In 1851 they joined the Mormon Church. In 1835 Aaron and family moved to Kertland, Ohio. He purchased a farm which was located two miles south-east of Where the Kirtland Temple was built. After building a house, he saw the possibilities of an iron foundry in this new country so he built and operated one. In 1838 the Kirtland Camp., organized by the Seventies of the LDS Church for thre purpose of going to Missouri and building up Zion, was established. Aaron Cheney, Amos Cheney, Orin Cheney, Nathan Cheney, and Elijah Cheney with their families were amoung those who enlisted in the camp. Just what relation Nathan and Elijah Cheney were to Aaron and his son Orin is not known. It is safe to surmise that they were cousins (Elijah Cheney and Zian Pulsfer baptized president Wilford Woodruff while on a mission in the east Dec. 31, 1833), owing to sickness, Aaron was forced to stop temporaily at Decanter, Mason Co. Ill. Sept. 10, 1838. While delayed he fenced a piece of ground, built a small house, raised a crop. The Prophet Joseph Smith stayed with then one night at his house when he was on his memorable rip to Washington DC for the purpose of laying he grievances of the Saints before the President of the U.S.. The next spring the Cheney’s continued their journey to Nauvoo where Aaron helped build the Temple. On Dec. 22, 1845, Aaron and his wife Mehitable did their own Temple Work. Later they came west with the Saints in 1850.
