# Thoughts on Great-Great Grandparents

_A story of [[Thomas Henry Stephens (KWNL-JWK)|Thomas Henry Stephens]]._

## The story as it was written

Written by Ronald Stephens. Great-Great-Grandson of Thomas Henry Stephens.
In late October 2021 I took a copy of a family history record for my great-grandpa, Thomas Henry Stephens and my great-grandmother, Mary Jones with me to what we refer to as “the ranch” located between Henefer and Echo, Utah. The ranch includes the privately owned ground in what is referred to as Leonard’s Canyon. It as a beautiful day and I remember parking just inside the front gate. I took out the record and started reading out loud about the marriage of these two courageous people. The record indicated that the marriage took place at the residence of Edward Richins which was located at “Leonard’s Point” between Henefer and Echo. It has been confirmed to me that where I was sitting at that point was within not too many yards of where that marriage was performed by Charles Richins, Presiding Elder of Henefer. I had some very sacred feelings about how special it was that we now own the property and have owned it for about forty years. The record goes on to state that following their marriage they lived in a rock dugout that Thomas built on the property of Edward Richins.
The last couple of months I have been working to clean up the property by removing dead sage brush as well as trees that have either died or have been blown over. Knowing how hard it has been for me to do this work and realizing that they lived in a dugout possibly quite close by, it deepened my respect for and gratitude of the character and work ethic of these wonderful pioneers.
