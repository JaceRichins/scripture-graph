_A story of [[Caroline Hogan (KWJX-KS2)|Caroline Hogan]]._

## The story as it was written

"Caroline was a good cook and housekeeper. She was noted for being the first to have new straw hats. She braided the straw and made it into hats for all the family and in the fall she made the men their caps. She always prided herself on having the whitest front porch, which could be done by scrubbing the native lumber flooring with lye, which made the boards very white."
Kemp, Annie Hyer. What We Know About the Hayer Family, page15. Logan, UT: Published 1954.
