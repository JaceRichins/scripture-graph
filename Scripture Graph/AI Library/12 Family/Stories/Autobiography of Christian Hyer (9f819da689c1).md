# Autobiography of Christian Hyer

_A story of [[Christian Larsen Hyer (KWJH-WBR)|Christian Larsen Hyer]]._

## The story as it was written

"I, Christian Hyer, son of Larse Hyer and Ann Olsen was born in Christiania, Norway, September 17, 1817. Left Norway for America in the spring of 1842. I landed in Milwaukee, Wisconsin; settled with my parents and their family which consisted of 8 children--six boys and 2 girls--namely:"
"We located ourselves in Misquite County, Wisconsin Territory, in the spring of 1843, when an elder of Israel by the name of Ole Olsen Hayer came into the neighborhood, preaching the gospel of the Son of God; and as I was convinced of the truth of the principles that he taught, I immediately embraced it, and was baptized the 23rd of June 1843 the same year. As there was no branch of the church there, I moved to La Salle County, Illinois, where there was a branch."
In the fall of '44, for the first time I beheld President Brigham Young, Heber C Kimball, and Parley Pratt. In the month of February 1846, I, in connection with others of the branch were called upon to come to Nauvoo, which we did, and there received our endowments in the temple, and in the Seventies Hall I was ordained a seventy.
In the spring of '49 I left my father's family in La Salle County, Illinois, and came to Salt Lake City. We were 7 wagons in co. from the settlement, crossed the plains in co. with Ezra T Benson and arrived in Salt Lake City, October 25, 1849. Lived in the 6th Ward in the old fort until the spring of '50 when I moved to North Canyon Ward, now called Bountiful.
November 23, 1850, I took to myself for wife, Caroline Hogan. On the 1st of September 1855 I was called to take a mission in the U.S. which I did, leaving home on the 9th of September in co. with several others, Andrew Cunningham being the captain.
