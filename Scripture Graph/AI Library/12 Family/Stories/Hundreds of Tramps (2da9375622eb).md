# Hundreds of Tramps

_A story of [[Samuel Oldham (KWZ1-FBS)|Samuel Oldham]]._

## The story as it was written

It was 1898, and the Mutual Improvement Associations of the LDS Church was undergoing a reformation. Local service missionaries had been called within heir ranks to search out the young people who had fallen away from the Church.
Bishop Samuel Oldham of Paradise stood to give the missionaries and their officers instructions.
“When the boys say they do not care to attend meetings say to him at once, ‘you must go.’ We have hundreds of tramps in our country, and they are tramps because they lack regularity in their daily habits.”
Source: Minutes, Feb 20, 1898, YMMIA district conf, Cache Stake, YMMIA board minutes, LR 1280/16 v 2, Church History Library, Salt Lake City.
