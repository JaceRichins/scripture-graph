# Obituary

_A story of [[June Stayner (KWZM-X9K)|June Stayner]]._

## The story as it was written

June Stayner Budge, 90, cherished mother, wife, devoted church worker and popular teacher, passed away Thursday, June 7, 2007, in Logan, Utah, from natural causes.
She was born June 2, 1917, in Price, Utah, to Ralph James Stevenson Stayner and Nina Pearl Smith Winters. She married Dr. Robert Sutton Budge in the Salt Lake LDS Temple on Sept. 8, 1941. She has lived in Price and Garland, Utah; Mesa, Ariz.; St. Louis, Mo.; and Salt Lake City, Smithfield and Logan, Utah.
June graduated from Utah State University in 1939 with a bachelor's of arts degree in physiology and physical education. She taught physical education and art at Bear River High School.
In her school years, she excelled in sports and dance. At Utah State, she was an R.O.T.C Army Sponsor Spur (service organization), president of Chi Omega Sorority, Phi Delta Pi Honorary Greek Fraternity, school newspaper, and many other activities. She organized and taught dancing on Saturday in Garland to help financially with her education.
While in Smithfield, she was president of the Junior Civic Club, Fine Arts, Library Board, Logan and Smithfield. She was a member of the Protection and White House Committee on Youth, University Women's Club, member of the Disabled Children's "Day Care," started "Friends of the Library" in Smithfield, was a member of Cache Valley Committee on Obscene Literature - giving lectures throughout the valley on this subject.
She was a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, serving since she was in her early teens in many different callings, which she loved. Stake dance director for Bear River, Ensign and Smithfield Stake Speech, theology, mini art classes, visiting teacher for 51 years, choir and singing mothers, Primary president and sister. The friendly Primary called her Grandmother Budge. Art merit badge instructor for Boy Scouts, extraction (missionary genealogy) in Latin and German, and missionary at the Logan Family History Library.
June is survived by her eight children, June Ann Oldham (Whit) of Hoytsville, Utah, Robert Cort (Marcia) of LaVerne, Calif., Bonnie Palmer (Allen) and Mike (Cynthia) of Smithfield, Nina Hatch (Blaine) of Fairview, Idaho, Nancy Thomas (Lynn) and Keith (Bonnie) of Hyde Park, and Margaret of Smithfield. She was a loving grandmother of 30 grandchildren and 45 great-grandchildren.
She was preceded in death by her husband on July 31, 1995; mother and father; one brother, Herbert Winters Stayner; and his wife, Ruth Barrus Clark Stayner.
Funeral services will be held at noon Tuesday, June 12, at the Smithfield 7th Ward LDS chapel, 76 E. 200 South. Family and friends may call from 6 to 8 p.m. on Monday at Nelson Funeral Home, 85 S. Main, Smithfield, and also from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m. Tuesday at the church.
Her family wishes to thank Terrace Grove Assisted Living Center and IHC Hospice, both of Logan, for their exceptional care during her illness.
