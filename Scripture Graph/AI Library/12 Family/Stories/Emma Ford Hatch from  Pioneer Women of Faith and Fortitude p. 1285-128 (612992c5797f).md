# Emma Ford Hatch from: Pioneer Women of Faith and Fortitude p. 1285-1286

_A story of [[Aaron Cheney (KNX4-J2Z)|Aaron Cheney]]._

## The story as it was written

Birthdate: 17 April 1842 Chesterfield, England
Death: 22 June 1926 Woods Cross, Davis Co., Utah
Parents: William Ford and Mary Neal
Pioneer: 11 May 1862 Handcart Company
Spouse: Ransom Osborn Hatch
Married: 15 May 1863
Death Sp: 30 March 1895 Woods Cross, Davis Co., Utah
Children: John Arthur, 24 Feb 1864; Mary, 19 Jan 1866; Charles William, 13 Oct 1867; Sarah Elizabeth, 24 Jan 1869; Richard Ford, 13 Mar 1875; Samuel, 26 Oct 1877.
 Emma was born in Chersterfield, Derbyshire, England, in 1842, being the youngest child in a family of seven. She was baptized into the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints when she was eleven years old. She often told the story of her family entertaining Joseph F. Smith in their home in Chesterfield, England. He was a young missionary at the time.
 When Emma was twenty years old, she and her sister, Sarah Elizabeth, left England togehter and sailed on the "William Tappscott," to America. Due to a bad storm Sarah Elizabeth was lost at sea. Emma entered America alone.
 She joined wiht some other Mormon converts to journey to Utah. They traveled with the John Murdock Ox-Train Company. Emma pushed a handcart across the Plains, arriving in the Salt Lake Valley on May 11, 1862. President Brigham Young met them at the tithing yard and saw that they were comfortable with food and lodging.
 Emma was the only member of her family in America at this time. Other family members came later. She immediately sought employment but having no special training she did housework. President Young arranged for her to work in the home of Ransom Hatch in Woods Cross. Ransom's parents were the first settlers in Woods Cross.
 Emma was a very alert and likeable person and soon won admiration in the Hatch home. One year later Emma married Ransom Hatch, becoming his third wife in polygamy. They had six children.
 Emma and her children had their own home in Woods Cross. She found much pleasure in her family and never forgot to acknowledge the hand of the Lord in her blessings. She was endowed and sealed at the Logan Temple, May 23, 1863,
 After thirty-two years of marriage her husband passed away, leaving her a widow at the age of fifty-three. for the next thirty-one years, she cared for all of her family, until she passed away on June 22, 1929, in Woods Cross, Utah,
