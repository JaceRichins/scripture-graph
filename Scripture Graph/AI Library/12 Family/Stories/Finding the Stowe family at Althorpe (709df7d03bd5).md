# Finding the Stowe family at Althorpe

_A story of [[Hannah Stowe (KWJD-SCL)|Hannah Stowe]]._

## The story as it was written

One day I was watching a program on PBS about the "Secrets of Althorpe - The Spencer Family. (It can also be found on YouTube.) The person taking the tour was Charles Spencer, the younger brother of Lady Diana Spencer, who married Prince Charles. Diana is buried on a little island in a pond at the Althorpe Estate.
It was a very interesting program and Charles was showing the gardens that surround the estate when he came upon a garden wall that his grandfather had the engraved names of the gardeners at the estate who were killed in World War I. As the camera panned the wall, a name on the wall "jumped out" at me. It was the name of Ernest James Stowe, a family name I had been working on for a while.
My great great grandmother, Hannah Stowe was his grandfather's sister.
Because of this experience, I was able to find a whole branch of the Stowe family that had gone to Northamptonshire and settled there.
