# Daniel Miley Smith

_A story of [[Daniel Miley Smith Sr (KWJC-G74)|Daniel Miley Smith Sr]]._

## The story as it was written

Daniel Miley Smith's Family Lived in Pennsylvania.His ancestry is German. His relatives helped feed the troops during the civil war. His uncle was a weaver and one of his carriage blankets is on display in a Pennsylvania museum. Daniel Miley Smith joined the Mormon Church and immigrated to Pleasant grove , Utah. There he had a profession of Nurseryman. The trees surrounding the park in downtown Pleasant grove were planted by Daniel Miley Smith. His home still stands and has a big black walnut tree near the sidewalk.
