# Idaho’s First “Dentist”

_A story of [[John Allen Sutton (KWZ8-RFV)|John Allen Sutton]]._

## The story as it was written

Taken from pages 795 and 796 of "History of Bear Lake Pioneers" book by Dorothy Hardy Matthews and Edith Parker Haddock published by Daughters of Utah Pioneers Bear Lake County Idaho 1968 copied at City of Paris Historical Museum on July 22, 2013.
John Allen Sutton was born the 16th of November 1834 or 1835 in Leire, England. When he was 15 years old, he joined The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, on November 11, 1849. His father was a blacksmith and taught him the trade.
When their [John and Margaret's] last son, Robert, was just a few months old, John A. was called on a mission to England. When John A. returned, he continued his trade of blacksmith. He assisted the doctors whenever they needed help. If someone had a tooth ache they came to him to have it pulled and he always kept his forceps handy in his blacksmith shop. Even the Indians came to him to get a tooth pulled.
