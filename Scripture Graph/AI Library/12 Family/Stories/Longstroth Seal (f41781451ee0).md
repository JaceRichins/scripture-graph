# Longstroth Seal

_A story of [[Stephen Longstroth (KWJY-1YP)|Stephen Longstroth]]._

## The story as it was written

This seal is now in the possession of Timothy Clawson Wright. Stephen Longstroth is his 3rd great grandfather. These portable/small seals replaced the need to carry the very large wood seals that were used to seal and contract or covenant. Seals usually passed from eldest son down. Stephen was the eldest living son when he left England for Zion in Utah. The seal was removed from England and ended in Nauvoo and then Salt Lake City until Stephen's death.
