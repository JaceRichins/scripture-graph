# 2 Page History Compilation - Aaron Cheney (and Wife Mehitable Wells)

_A story of [[Mehitable Wells (KN6W-2R1)|Mehitable Wells]], [[Aaron Cheney (KNX4-J2Z)|Aaron Cheney]]._

## The story as it was written

Aaron Cheney (and Wife Mehitable Wells)
Aaron Cheney was born July 14, 1787 in Berwick, Maine. His parents were Benjamin Cheney and Eunice Hubbard. When Aaron was a small boy, his father moved to Granby, Vermont. When he was twenty-six or twenty-seven he met and married Mehitable Wells.
Mehitable Wells was born January 31, 1787. She was among the refugees who came from Long Island, New York to Guilford, Maine with her parents during the Revolutionary War. She was frail but an industrious woman.
Aaron was a prosperous farmer, a factory owner, and a good business manager. About the time of his marriage he moved to the town of Marshfield, Vermont, and in 1812 he sold some land in Granby, Vermont to his younger brother Hurd.
Twelve years later he sold two remaining pieces of property in Granby. He moved to Freedom, Cattaragus County, New York in 1817, where his father's family was located, where he gained possession of several lots of land. While in Freedom, Aaron built and operated an iron foundry with a large crew of men. He produced great quantities of cast iron.
Aaron and his wife were religious people, being faithful members of the Methodist Church of that town. He was chosen trustee in 1827 of the First Methodist Episcopal Church of Sandusky. In 1831 they were visited by the Prophet Joseph Smith and Elder Orson Pratt.
They received the Gospel and were baptized members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
Aaron L Cheney records have the baptism of Aaron April 5, 1834, which is consistent with other family members' baptisms in April 1834. The Wells Cheney Family record (Rexburg, Idaho, 1957) states, “Aaron Cheney moved to Freedom, N.Y. and he and his family belonged to the Methodist Church until they were visited by the Prophet Joseph Smith and Orson Pratt.
They received the gospel and were baptized in the Mormon Church in 1831.” Orson Pratt's records reveal that it was 1834, not 1831 (Orson Pratt Journals, Watson, Comp. p. 38-39, Pioneer Heritage Library, L.D.S. Family History Suite CD-Rom): Year 1834, April 2nd Preached at Fish Lake School House, After meeting there were four baptized: Joseph Fountain, Caroline Fountain, Amasa Cheney, Olive Cheney. In the evening we held a confirmation meeting and confirmed those mentioned above. April 3rd we baptized and confirmed two: Hannah Cheney and Polly Lawson, April 5th baptized and confirmed two: Aaron Cheney and Lucy.
In 1838 the Kirtland Camp, organized by the Seventies of the LDS Church for the purpose of going to Missouri and building up Zion, was established. Aaron Cheney, Amos Cheney, Orin Cheney, Nathan Cheney, and Elijah Cheney with their families were among those who enlisted in the camp.
Just what relation Nathan and Elijah Cheney were to Aaron and his son Orin is not known. It is safe to surmise that they were cousins (Elijah Cheney and Zian Pulsfer baptized president Wilford Woodruff while on a mission in the east Dec. 31, 1833), owing to sickness, Aaron was forced to stop temporarily at Decanter, Mason County Illinois Sept. 10, 1838.
While delayed he fenced a piece of ground, built a small house, raised a crop. The Prophet Joseph Smith stayed with them one night at his house when he was on his memorable trip to Washington DC for the purpose of laying the grievances of the Saints before the President of the U.S.
The next spring the Cheney’s continued their journey to Nauvoo where Aaron helped build the Temple. On Dec. 22, 1845, Aaron and his wife Mehitable did their own Temple Work. Later they came west with the Saints in 1850.
