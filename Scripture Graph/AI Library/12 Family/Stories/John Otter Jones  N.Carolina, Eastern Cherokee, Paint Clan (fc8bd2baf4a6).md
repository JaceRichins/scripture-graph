# John Otter Jones: N.Carolina, Eastern Cherokee, Paint Clan

_A story of [[John Otter Jones (LZNN-5JJ)|John Otter Jones]]._

## The story as it was written

This is a compilation of information obtained from various family trees (thanks to the research of many!) along with Cherokee information that probably applied to John Otter Jones:
"Sarah Patton's grandfather was Otter Jones. He was a full-blood Cherokee and the family story is he could run so fast that he could catch and tackle a deer to the ground" per Flaherty familiy history.
A bit of Native American history: the Cherokee were known as the “mountaineers of
the south,” once occupying the Allegheny country of southwestern Virginia, western
N. Carolina and Alabama. The Cherokee can trace their history, in N.Carolina; back more than a thousand years. According to the old stories, the people arrived in this territory from somewhere to the northeast.
John “Otter” Jones’ Native American heritage is said to be of the Cherokee Paint Clan. Historically, the Paint or Ani'-Wa'di Clan had a settlement on lower Socco creek, within the reservation in Jackson and Swain counties of N. Carolina.
Either John and/or his Native American ancestors gave up their Cherokee name/s for 'Christian' ones thus the name ‘Jones,” but John Jones did maintain the middle name of Otter in reference to his ancestry.
Interestingly, note the Census docs that locate John, his wife Sarah (Benton) and their kids in Alabama along with some other Jones relatives. Maybe John Otter moved his family to Alabama to live with some of his relatives? Also note that Census docs state John's state of birth as N.Carolina, which supports the Native American ancestry, as opposed to other ancestry info that listed Virginia for his state of birth.
