## The story as it was written

Mehitable Wells Cheney
Mehitable Wells, wife of Aaron Cheney was born Jan. 31, 1787. She was among the refugees who came from Long Island, NY to Cailford, Maine with her parents during the Revolutionary War. She was a frail but an industrious woman.
 (Aaron Cheney Had a patricartical blessing given by John Smith My 5, 1855 in Nauvoo, Ill. His wife Mehitable received one the same day also.
