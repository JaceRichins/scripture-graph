# Mary Jones Stephens

_A story of [[Mary Jones (KWNL-JWJ)|Mary Jones]]._

## The story as it was written

Mary Jones
Most of Mary Jones Stephens’ life is shared in the combined biography of Thomas and Mary-this is just a taste!
 Mary Jones was born at Brimpsfield, England, June 16, 1842. Her father’s name was William Jones, and he was born at Brimpsfield, June 7, 1805.He came to Utah August 18, 1868 with the John Murdock Company. He was a road and ditch builder. Her mother’s name was Mary Shill, who died in England from scarlet fever. Two of Mary’s older sisters, Lucy and Ann also died. Mary Jones’ father was left with a family of 5 girls under 15 to raise. Mary’s older sister Susannah, who was 15, took care of them. Her older brother Robert was 19. Mary grew up to be an unselfish, kind, helpful, intelligent, thoughtful girl who was loved by everyone. She was also attractive!
 Mary met Thomas Henry Stephens in England and introduced him to her brother Robert who was a missionary for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Thomas came to America in 1864. He sent Mary the money to immigrate. So Mary was able to come to Utah in the next year, 1865. She crossed the ocean on the ship Belle Wood, that sailed from Liverpool on Saturday April 29, 1865 with 636 saints. The ship landed in New York City. From there she traveled to Utah. She was met by Thomas Henry Stephens who was already in Salt Lake City but had traveled to Heneferville (as it was called back then) to met her and they went to Edward and Ellen Richins’ place that was up toward Echo. Mary and the Richins’ became good friends.
 Mary married Thomas Henry Stephens the 25th of Dec 1865, she was 23 at the time. They were married by Charles Richins, the Prsiding Elder of Henefer. The marriage took place at the residence of Edward Richins, located at Lenord’s Point between Henefer and Echo, Utah. Mary and Thomas had 6 boys and 3 girls. They lived and raised their family in Henefer, Utah.
 One of Mary’s daughters described her as kind and humble and always ready to help anyone in sickness or any other way. She was great to go and see the sick and help those in need. She was the first President of the Henefer Ward Relief Society. She was put in the 24th of August 1878 and was released 10 years later on August 30, 1885. She had seven children when she accepted the call and gave birth to two more while she was President. She was a block teacher up until the time of her death.
 Mary and Thomas were sealed in the Endowment house on June 16, 1873 which was her 31st birthday. Her sister Prudence and Prudence’s husband Joseph Edgeworth were also sealed at the same time. Allen Jones was married at the same time and all 3 couples traveled by teams to Salt Lake City.
 Mary’s daughter stated that she was a good mother and a hard worker. She was a lover of lfowers and had pretty flower beds. She always swept her door yard. She would help milk. One time she was helping milk and she stepped on a rusty nail. Joe Dearden who was working for my father, Thomas, at the time pulled it out. Mother suffered from the rusty nail for the rest of her life. Lady Thackery at Croydon took care of her foot. She was a very kind woman, she knew what to do to help mother. At another time mother had a blood vessel break in her leg and she was very patient with it. She exercised her faith. She would have to bandage her leg twice a day.
 Her Uncle, Robert Jones, gave her a Patriarchal blessing on the 12 of July 1905, the day she died.
 Patriarchal Blessing given by Patriarch Robert Jones upon the head of Mary Jones Stephens, daughter of William and Mary Shill Jones. Born at Brimpsfield Common, England.
 Dear Sister Mary Jones Stephens, In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, and by virtue of the Holy Priesthood, I lay my hands upon thy head, to give thee a blessing at this time, Lift up thy heart before the Lord, So that the vision of thy mind may be opened to see things as they are. I Seal upon thee at this time the blessing of thy Mother, Thou art of the Seed of Ephraim, and have a right to all the blessings pertaining to that Lineage, Thou has been faithful in the cause of truth, here upon the earth. Thou art numbered with the Daughters of Zion and thy name is written in the Lambs Book of Life. Thou hast been held in reserve to come forth upon the earth to help to replenish it in these the Later day's. The Lord has been Mindful of all thy Labors. He has a work for thee to do in the Spirit World, Therefore be faithful and true to thy integrity weather in life or in death, for the Almighty will let his power rest upon thee. Thou hast filled up the day's and measure of thy creation here upon the earth, and the blessing of God Shall rest upon thee, bear testimony of the work in which thou doest know and fully understand, Mary is awaiting thy arrival in the Spirit World, who will hail thee with great delight. I seal upon thee every blessing that will promote thy happiness in this World, and the one to come, and I seal thee up unto Eternal Life to come forth in the Morning of the first resurrection with many of thy Kindred and Friends.
 Even so, Amen.
