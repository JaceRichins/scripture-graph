# Budge Cabin up Logan Canyon

_A story of [[Dr Oliver Hyer Budge (KWCQ-H83)|Dr Oliver Hyer Budge]], [[Robert Sutton Budge (KWZM-X9J)|Robert Sutton Budge]], [[June Stayner (KWZM-X9K)|June Stayner]]._

## The story as it was written

My great grandfather Oliver Hyer Budge and his sons built a log cabin up Logan Canyon off of Browns Roll off. The cabin is several cabins past the bridge that goes over the Logan River. It is immediately west of a creek that flows into the river. In fact the creek was harnessed to provide a small pool for watermelon, soda and other things that needed cooling off in the summer. The cabin had a kitchen on the south end of it and a large single room just to the north. There was a large open face fire place in the main room with a bathroom off to the side. There were two lofts up stairs. An open loft was above the kitchen with a large log rail around it. It had 2 beds in this loft. The other loft went over a patio that went to the opposite direction of the other loft. This loft was enclosed with two beds. I always slept in this loft when I stayed overnight at the cabin. I slept there only a few times. I don’t remember the situations. I do remember though going up more often for the 4th of July or summer activities. We would eat lunch on the front patio with Grandma and Grandpa. We’d play in the water, take walks and hike up the mountain. I also remember as a teen when the river was not as swift, wading in the river and playing in the cool of the water. We would also have cook outs and grill hot dogs. I also remember taking dates to the cabin and playing cards in the parlor.
