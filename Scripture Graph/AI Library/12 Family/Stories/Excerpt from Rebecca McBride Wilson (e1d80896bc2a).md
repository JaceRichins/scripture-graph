## The story as it was written

Rebecca McBride Wilson 1826-1902
Contributed By jlwilson • 2013-06
SKETCH OF THE LIFE OF REBECCA MCBRIDE WILSON (This sketch of the life of Rebecca McBride Wilson was written October 6, 1958 by her daughter Rebecca Wilson and granddaughters Minnie Hubbard Dixon of Pima Arizona and Eunice Cope of Richfield, Utah.)
“Rebecca’s early childhood was spent with her paternal grandmother Catherine John McBride. She loved her grandmother very much and describes her as being a very neat lady, always wearing a white “kerchief” about her neck. She always remembered and talked about her grandmother’s big Bible with its many pictures and bible stores told her by her grandmother. One of the high lights of her entire life was these days with this grandmother.”
