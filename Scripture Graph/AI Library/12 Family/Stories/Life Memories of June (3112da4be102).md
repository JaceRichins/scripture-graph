# Life Memories of June

_A story of [[June Stayner (KWZM-X9K)|June Stayner]]._

## The story as it was written

On June 2, 2020, Margaret Budge, June’s daughter asked the following question.
“What do you remember wonderful about June Budge on her birthday.”
The following were responses from children, in-laws, grand children, cousins and friends.
• Cynthia Budge (Daughter-in-law) I have been thinking of June today. She always treated me like I was one of her daughters. I always felt like we were kindred souls. I miss her.
• Jason Hatch (Grandson) Grandma had an exuberance and excitement to share life with us. She wanted us to know or see something she found as neat or something she just learned.
• Rebecca Hatch (Jason’s Wife) I loved sitting in the backyard watching birds with her.
• Lisa Budge Cordova (Granddaughter) Loved doing ceramics with her, looking through photo books and watching birds.
• June Ann Oldham (Daughter) I loved taking her for rides around the valley and listen to all the old stories.
• Anne Marie Budge Hatch (Granddaughter) Grandma June Budge always had fun activities at our Budge reunions.
• Amalia Johnson (Friend) I love her pictures she painted and drew. She was such a wonderful person to me.
• Meg (Margaret Ann) Blanchard (Granddaughter) Fish sandwiches at Arctic Circle.
o When I used to say “I don’t care” when she would ask if I wanted something to eat or a treat she would quickly say” that means yes💛”
o Swinging in the couples swing in the back yard holding hands and talking
• Becky Profaizer (Granddaughter) She would always wait by the side door to wave goodbye to us. Even if it took us a few minutes to get buckled in the car and get going, she was always there waiting.
• June Ann Oldham (Daughter) Her kitchen table was always decorated for the season, I also loved her colorful clothes. She loved her bed of pink poppies.
• Amber Budge Nelson (Granddaughter) While she was at Terrace Grove she always told stories about grandpa Bob. Snowmobile rides was one my favorite reoccurring ones. She seemed to see the beauty and brightness in each day. I loved that she always had a candy dish filled for us.
• Bracken Berger (Grandson) I remember breaking my back emptying one of her shed dusting off all the stuff and putting it all back in the shed then my reward for that hard days work was half a banana popsickle I miss her...
• Kellie Budget (Ryan Budge’s wife, Ryan is a Grandson) She constantly talked about how clever we all were, shared her love for life and art with all around, managed to charm all of the old folks in the care home when she moved in, and sat with us and chatted in her back yard. She was something special and I’m so grateful I knew her.
• Kathryn Thomas Whatson (Granddaughter) It’s been fun to read all of these memories and how unique each of them are. I remember grandma often showing me how poppy seeds came out of the seed head like a shaker. She loved to share nature with us. I also remember playing with the bucket stilts she had. Grandma taught me how to fry chicken and can fresh peaches. I always think of her each year when I can peaches.❤️ I miss her.
• Janny Richins (Granddaughter) Taught me a song on the piano when I was little. I can still play that song.

She gave me a strand of reindeer bells to ring at every doorstep before I left the oranges. I love how excited she would get whenever we would visit like she had been waiting for us all day and couldn’t wait to hear about our day. She always went over and beyond to make sure everything was more special. I miss her so much 💕
