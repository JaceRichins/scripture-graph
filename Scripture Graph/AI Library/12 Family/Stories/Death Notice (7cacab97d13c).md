# Death Notice

_A story of [[Robert Jones (KWJ7-5Q6)|Robert Jones]]._

## The story as it was written

The Coalville Times, April 27, 1906, page 1.
GONE TO HIS FINAL REST
Word reached Coalville this (Friday) morning that Patriarch Robert Jones of Henefer, died at about 6 o'clock this morning. The immediate cause of death was pneumonia and general debility, being in the neighborhood of 77 years of age when the end came. He was an old resident of this county and crossed the plains with the ox train of 1866. Funeral services will be held from the Henefer Meeting house Sunday afternoon at 2 P.M.
