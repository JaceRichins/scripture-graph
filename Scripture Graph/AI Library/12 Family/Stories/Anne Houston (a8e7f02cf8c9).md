# Anne Houston

_A story of [[Newel Kimball Whitney (KWVM-M24)|Newel Kimball Whitney]]._

## The story as it was written

I was born 18 May 1821 in Ohio, dying in Winter Quarters, Douglas, Nebraska, 23 September 1848, age 27. My remains were the first to be buried in the Whitney & Kimball Cemetery at State Street and North Temple in Salt Lake City. My mother Mary Ettleman Houston also died in Winter Quarters. My father is James Houston.
I married Newell Kimball Whitney 7 Jan 1846 in Nauvoo, Illinois. Our first son was Jethro Houston Whitney born 8 May 1848 in Winter Quarters. My son survived, although I died when he was 4-1/2 months old. Jethro died in Salt Lake City in 1922, the last living child of Presiding Bishop Newell Kimball Whitney.
