# Aaron Cheney, conversion and migration

_A story of [[Aaron Cheney (KNX4-J2Z)|Aaron Cheney]]._

## The story as it was written

Aaron Cheney, son of Benjamin C. Cheney and Eunice Hubbard, was born in 1787 in Berwick, Maine. He married Mehitable Wells in 1807 in Marshfield, Washington, Vermont. They were both age 20. There are 14 children listed in AF 1808-1832, four born in Marshfield 1808-1813 and the rest born in Freedom, Cattaraugus, New York: 1813-1832. Most of the family converted to the LDS Church. Four of the children were baptized in April 1834. Two other children were baptized in 1836 and 1837. Aaron married a second wife, Sarah Griffiths, in Jan 1846. Sarah had been previously married to John Pisel, and they are listed in nFS with 10 children, born 1811-1832, mostly born in Pennsylvania. It looks as if Sarah had converted to the LDS Church and left her family to move to Nauvoo. Aaron and his two wives, and a son and his wife and infant child came across the plains in the George A. Smith/Dan Jones Company (1849). Another Cheney was in the same company: Elijah (63) and his wife: Sarah Harmon Cheney (62). Aaron and Elijah were not close relatives, but they did have a common ancestor in England 8 generations back: Frances Cheney, born 1542. Full birth and death dates are listed on the trail website for all of these Cheneys except for Sarah Griffith Cheney, who is listed with an Unknown death date.
John Pisel divorced Sarah in Oct 1849 for abandonment. John died in Sep 1850 and left Sarah out of his will.
One of the daughters of John Pisel and Sarah Griffiths, Margaret Pisel, married Thomas Kington in Nauvoo in 1842. Margaret was baptized in Nauvoo in 1845, and the Kington family of four came across the plains in 1850. They are listed on the trail with full birth and death dates.
For the family of Aaron and Mehitable,the parents and three children were endowed while living: 1845-1856. Two of the children died in Iowa, and the parents and four children died in Idaho, Utah or Arizona. There are many problems with the data. Some of the children are duplicated in AF. Six of the children, four males and two 'U' are listed with the name of 'Infant', with DNS for their SP. There is much ordinance duplication. Aaron is listed in nFS with three wives. One of them is questionable: Louisa Pizel.
George A. Smith/Dan Jones Company (1849). Departure: 14 July 1849. Arrival: 26-30 October 1849
Company Information: 370 or 447 individuals and 120 wagons were in the company when it began its journey from the outfitting post at Kanesville, Iowa (present day Council Bluffs). This company combined with the Ezra T. Benson company as they traveled close together.
