![[AI Library/12 Family/_media/KWHC-V4C/149177985.jpg|My last goodbye and picture.]]
_I was about 9 months into my missionary service in Michigan Lansing, in a small area called Harrison. My companion and I had just finished singing “Peace in Christ” while waiting to have a meeting with our branch when I received a text from my parents telling me to call them immediately. I hurried into the other room to call them, and that's when I heard the news about my grandma Emma's passing. I wasn't able to fly back home for her funeral, but I'm grateful for technology that allowed me to watch as others talked about how amazing she was. Grandma Emma would always call me "Beautiful" and tell me I was wonderful, just like she did with all her grandkids. She always made sure we knew how much she loved us. She and I used to watch Hallmark movies together during the summer while eating overly buttered popcorn or brownies. She always made sure there was nacho cheese in her pantry for me. My grandparents were my biggest supporters. I can't think of a time when they missed one of my sports events - they were always there for all of us kids. Grandma Emma was one of my biggest role models, and I love and miss her so much. I think she was pretty wonderful herself._

_This will forever be my favorite vacation with my grandma. A couple weeks before my mission we went to Arkansas. I’ve always wanted to go and see where my grandma grew up. On this trip we had walkie talkies for all of the cars. My grandma would tell us stories about all of the streets and her cousins. It was amazing to hear more about my grandma and her childhood. This was my last trip with her. I am grateful for the stories I was able to listen to before she passed._

Around Christmas, this picture was taken in 2019. Grandma would also read the Christmas story with Santa for the kids and Grandpa would read in Bible about Christ. The Christmas tree was always real until recently with little glass angels for our sweet Kimberly._
