### GREAT MEMORIAL

Biography
From the "YELL COUNTY HERITAGE" book. ROBERT GRAY As early as 1841, Robert Gray is listed on the Yell County, Arkansas. tax rolls along with his sons, William and Joseph. Robert was born about 1795 in Tennessee and his wife, Tabitha Jane Cooper, was born in Alabama about 1798-1802. She was believed to be part Cherokee Indian. They settled in the Spring Creek Township and raised several children. Their first child, Sarah, was born 1819 in Alabama and married Obediah Wood and settled in Yell Co. Arkansas. William, was born 1820 in Jackson County, Alabama, married Matilda Monk in Yell Co. Arkansas. Joseph was born 1821 in Tennessee and married Nancy Lorena McBride in Yell Co. Arkansas. Betsy was born 1822 in Tennessee. James M., born 1825 in Tennessee. Pheldery, born 1832 in Tennessee, married James McBride in Yell Co. Kiziah, born 1837 in Tennessee, married William T. Wilson. Martin Van Buren, born 1840 in Marion County, Arkansas, married Harriett Caroline Hodges in 1859 at Havana, Arkansas. Jane was born 1842 in Yell Co. and Lucinda was born 1844 in Yell Co. AR. Robert and Tabitha farmed for a living and Tabitha is listed as head of house on 1850 Yell County census. Surviving court house records show she died between 1850 and 1858, leaving her son, Martin Van Buren, under the guardianship of his brother, Joseph.
Research Notes
Possibly the sister of John Cooper.
In 1896 the wife of Tabitha's grandson, John Henry Wood, made a Dawes Roll application, based on John Henry Wood being the grandson of the Cherokee named Tabitha Gray. This application was overturned, but the application stated Tabitha Gray's maiden name was Davis. More details on the overturned application at Arminta Wood.
No source found to support her parents are Isaac Cooper and his Cherokee wife: Some believe Tabitha's father is Isaac Cooper, a well researched early American who married a Full Blood Cherokee, Nancy Black Fox. While many of their children are listed on their Find A Grave memorials, the list does not include a daughter named Tabitha in the lengthy biography on Isaac Cooper's Find A Grave: Memorial #94345115. The list mentions an unnamed daughter born after 1810. Their children listed were born in Tennessee (or maybe NC) and Kentucky between 1795 and 1811. Tabitha is believed to have been born in Alabama from 1798-1802. Her two oldest children were born in Alabama (perhaps Jackson County) circa 1820. While it's not impossible she's Isaac and Nancy's daughter, the relationship is not sourced and seems unlikely considering the depth of research on their children and Tabitha's origins in Alabama.
