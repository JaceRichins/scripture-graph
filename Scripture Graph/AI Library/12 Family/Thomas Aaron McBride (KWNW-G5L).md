_This large stone bowl belonged to Thomas Aaron McBride.  He used it as a basin to cool the horseshoes  after he would shape them.  It is my understanding that he made this basin. There is an inscription, a name perhaps, in the basin but the years of wear and weathering have worn most of it down. It is made entirely from a large stone and has a drainage hole drilled through the bottom. It is in the possession of Thomas Aaron McBride's great grandson._
