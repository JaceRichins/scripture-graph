# Part 1: My Servant Joseph, April 1815–April 1830

*Saints V1* · [source](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/part-1?lang=eng)

April 1815–April 1830

I the Lord, knowing the calamity which should come upon the inhabitants of the earth, called upon my servant Joseph Smith, Jun., and spake unto him from heaven … that mine everlasting covenant might be established.
