# Part 4: Fullness of Times, April 1839–February 1846

*Saints V1* · [source](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/part-4?lang=eng)

April 1839–February 1846

Let this house be built unto my name, that I may reveal mine ordinances therein unto my people; for I deign to reveal unto my church things which have been kept hid from before the foundation of the world, things that pertain to the dispensation of the fulness of times.
