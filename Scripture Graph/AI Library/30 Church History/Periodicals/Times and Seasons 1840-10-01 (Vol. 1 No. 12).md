## Excerpt

> The death of br. Edward Partridge (in that paper) struck me with deep solemnity. Since 1831 we had pass- ed through many trying scenes, and he ever proved himself a faithful friend. His private and official duties were performed with an eye single to the glory of God. He was a faithful stew- and the church had unlimited confi dence in bis integrity. He lived Gadlly in Christ Jesus, and suffered persecu- tion: As a Bishop he was one of the Lords great men, and few will be able to wear his mantle with such simple dignity. He was an honest man, and I loved him.
