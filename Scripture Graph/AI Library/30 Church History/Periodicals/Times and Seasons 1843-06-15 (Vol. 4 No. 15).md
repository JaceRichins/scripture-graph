## Excerpt

> For prophecy concerning the Jewe, I would refer to Moses, Ezekiel, Isaiah, Jeremiah and Amos, all of which predictions are delivered with the clearness of history, and the confi- dence of truth. After the Jews had been des- troyed, as above described, when their num- bers had again increased, they combined to- gether to make a desperate attempt to regain Jerusalem, when they fell by the edge of the sword, in such numbers that few escaped.— They were then banished from’Judea, and by an imperial edict, it was death for a Jew to set his foot in Jerusalem, and from that time they have been scattered among the nations, among the heathen, among the people, even from one end of the earth unto the other. They have been removed into all the kingdoms of the earth; they have been scattered unto all the - winde, and dispersed throughout all countries, among nations which neither they nor their fa- thers had known. |
