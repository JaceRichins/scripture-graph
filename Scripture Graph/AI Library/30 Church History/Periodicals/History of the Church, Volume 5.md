> The greatest, the best, and the most useful gifts would be known nothing about by an observer. It is true that a man might prophesy, which is a great gift, and one that Paul told the people — the Church — to seek after and to covet, rather than to speak in tongues; but what does the world know about prophesying? Paul says that it "serveth only to those that believe.'' But does not the Scriptures say that they spake in tongues and prophesied? Yes; but who is it that writes these Scriptures?
