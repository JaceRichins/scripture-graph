> | least for the present. I have been forth through the United States and Europe, in fact, I have spent ‘my whole time at it, since I came into the Church. It) is now all council for me.— We haveva greet many things to say to day and I suppose we shall always have plenty to io, I presume many have got out of business; but we will now have work enough, to get ready to go to some other country; to get there, and to. plough our fields when we get there. I have seen people crying, and weeping,and mourning, because they bad nothing to do; but when we leaye this place; you will never have cause to weep, for not having anything to do, fromithis time forth. and forever more, if you are faithful . to your calling.
