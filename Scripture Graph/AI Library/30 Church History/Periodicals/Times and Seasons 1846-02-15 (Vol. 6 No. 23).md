## Excerpt

> It would be a work of supererogation to la- bor to shew the truth of the gathering of the children of Israel in these last days; ‘for the proph et told us long ago, That it should ne more be said, the Lord liveth that brought the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt, bat the Lord liveth thet brought up the children of Israel from the land of the North, and from the lands whither he had driven them; and so it must be for the honor and glory of God.
