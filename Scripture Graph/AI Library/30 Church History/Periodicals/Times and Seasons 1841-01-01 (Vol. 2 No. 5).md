## Excerpt

> Brother’s Robinson & Smith had a letter from Elders Kimball, Smith and Woodruff, in London, which gave us information of the commence- ment of ‘the work of the Lord in the city of London, which I was glad to hear 1 am likewise informed that Elders have gone to Australia and fo the East Indias, I feel desirous that every providential opening 'of the kind should be filled, and that you should prior to your leaving England, send the gospel into as many parts as y possibly can.
