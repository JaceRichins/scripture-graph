## Excerpt

> „Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault. ye which are spiritual restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself lest thou also be tempted. Bear ve one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ. For if a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself. But let eve- ry man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself, alone, and not in another.“
