> in building up of the: Zion of our God; for it.is after the wd. has;built up Zion, that he will appear in his glory.— Psalms 102:16. We all look for the appearing of the great God, and our Savior Jesus Christ; but we shall look in vain, until Zion is built; for Zi- on is to be the dwelling: place of our God when he comes.— Joel 3:21. Any one who will read this chapter with atten- tion, will see that it treats of the last days, and of the Zion of the last days.
