## Excerpt

> Let us here enumerate the signs spoken of which are to precede the Messiah’s sec- ond coming, and the setting up of his kingdom. ‘There shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars, and upon the earth; distress of nations, with perplexity—the sea and the waves roaring, men’s hearts failing them for fear, and for looking after those things which are coming on the earth: for the powers of heaven shall be shaken, and then shall they see the Son of Man com- ing in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.”
