## Excerpt

> Now have we ever had any such prophet: that people have all been des- truyed that did not believe in him?— No, never, some will pretend to say that it had reference to Christ, but were all the people destroyed that did not be- lieve in him? On the other hand, a great part of those that did believe in him suffered martyrdom. And that main of sin will never triumph over the true saint of God again as he did then. I mean the Roman Catholic church.
