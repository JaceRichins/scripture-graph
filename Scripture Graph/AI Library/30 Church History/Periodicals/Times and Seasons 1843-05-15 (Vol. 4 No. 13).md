> Brother: Is this truth? But we believe, now our Great Father knows our condition, he will listen to us. We are as mourning“orphans in our country; but our father will take us by the hand. When he fulfils his promise, we will an- wer his talk. He means well. We know is But we cannot think now. Grief has made children of us. When our business is settled we shall be men again, and talk to our Great Father about what he has promised.
