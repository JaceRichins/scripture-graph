> We might continue this subject with great effect among the children of Israel, for so soon as they began to be puffed up with self-suffi- ciency, they too, like tie ancients, honored the old revelations in word, or profession, but they stoned the prophets which came with new ones; not because God had ever said that he had ceased to give line upon line; precept upon precept, here a little, and there a little; but because they chose darkness rather than light because their deeds were evil.
