# Proverbs 11

**1** A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight. ^prov-11-1

**2** When pride cometh, then cometh shame: but with the lowly is wisdom. ^prov-11-2

**3** The integrity of the upright shall guide them: but the perverseness of transgressors shall destroy them. ^prov-11-3

**4** Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death. ^prov-11-4

**5** The righteousness of the perfect shall direct his way: but the wicked shall fall by his own wickedness. ^prov-11-5

**6** The righteousness of the upright shall deliver them: but transgressors shall be taken in their own naughtiness. ^prov-11-6

**7** When a wicked man dieth, his expectation shall perish: and the hope of unjust men perisheth. ^prov-11-7

**8** The righteous is delivered out of trouble, and the wicked cometh in his stead. ^prov-11-8

**9** An hypocrite with his mouth destroyeth his neighbour: but through knowledge shall the just be delivered. ^prov-11-9

**10** When it goeth well with the righteous, the city rejoiceth: and when the wicked perish, there is shouting. ^prov-11-10

**11** By the blessing of the upright the city is exalted: but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked. ^prov-11-11

**12** He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour: but a man of understanding holdeth his peace. ^prov-11-12

**13** A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter. ^prov-11-13

**14** Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety. ^prov-11-14

**15** He that is surety for a stranger shall smart for it: and he that hateth suretiship is sure. ^prov-11-15

**16** A gracious woman retaineth honour: and strong men retain riches. ^prov-11-16

**17** The merciful man doeth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel troubleth his own flesh. ^prov-11-17

**18** The wicked worketh a deceitful work: but to him that soweth righteousness shall be a sure reward. ^prov-11-18

**19** As righteousness tendeth to life: so he that pursueth evil pursueth it to his own death. ^prov-11-19

**20** They that are of a froward heart are abomination to the LORD: but such as are upright in their way are his delight. ^prov-11-20

**21** Though hand join in hand, the wicked shall not be unpunished: but the seed of the righteous shall be delivered. ^prov-11-21

**22** As a jewel of gold in a swine's snout, so is a fair woman which is without discretion. ^prov-11-22

**23** The desire of the righteous is only good: but the expectation of the wicked is wrath. ^prov-11-23

**24** There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; and there is that withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty. ^prov-11-24

**25** The liberal soul shall be made fat: and he that watereth shall be watered also himself. ^prov-11-25

**26** He that withholdeth corn, the people shall curse him: but blessing shall be upon the head of him that selleth it. ^prov-11-26

**27** He that diligently seeketh good procureth favour: but he that seeketh mischief, it shall come unto him. ^prov-11-27

**28** He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the righteous shall flourish as a branch. ^prov-11-28

**29** He that troubleth his own house shall inherit the wind: and the fool shall be servant to the wise of heart. ^prov-11-29

**30** The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and he that winneth souls is wise. ^prov-11-30

**31** Behold, the righteous shall be recompensed in the earth: much more the wicked and the sinner. ^prov-11-31
