# Leviticus 19

**1** And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying, ^lev-19-1

**2** Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall be holy: for I the LORD your God am holy. ^lev-19-2

**3** Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father, and keep my sabbaths: I am the LORD your God. ^lev-19-3

**4** Turn ye not unto idols, nor make to yourselves molten gods: I am the LORD your God. ^lev-19-4

**5** And if ye offer a sacrifice of peace offerings unto the LORD, ye shall offer it at your own will. ^lev-19-5

**6** It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the morrow: and if ought remain until the third day, it shall be burnt in the fire. ^lev-19-6

**7** And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it is abominable; it shall not be accepted. ^lev-19-7

**8** Therefore every one that eateth it shall bear his iniquity, because he hath profaned the hallowed thing of the LORD: and that soul shall be cut off from among his people. ^lev-19-8

**9** And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest. ^lev-19-9

**10** And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger: I am the LORD your God. ^lev-19-10

**11** Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another. ^lev-19-11

**12** And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-12

**13** Thou shalt not defraud thy neighbour, neither rob him: the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning. ^lev-19-13

**14** Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind, but shalt fear thy God: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-14

**15** Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour. ^lev-19-15

**16** Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-16

**17** Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him. ^lev-19-17

**18** Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-18

**19** Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee. ^lev-19-19

**20** And whosoever lieth carnally with a woman, that is a bondmaid, betrothed to an husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her; she shall be scourged; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free. ^lev-19-20

**21** And he shall bring his trespass offering unto the LORD, unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, even a ram for a trespass offering. ^lev-19-21

**22** And the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering before the LORD for his sin which he hath done: and the sin which he hath done shall be forgiven him. ^lev-19-22

**23** And when ye shall come into the land, and shall have planted all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count the fruit thereof as uncircumcised: three years shall it be as uncircumcised unto you: it shall not be eaten of. ^lev-19-23

**24** But in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy to praise the LORD withal. ^lev-19-24

**25** And in the fifth year shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the increase thereof: I am the LORD your God. ^lev-19-25

**26** Ye shall not eat any thing with the blood: neither shall ye use enchantment, nor observe times. ^lev-19-26

**27** Ye shall not round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard. ^lev-19-27

**28** Ye shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor print any marks upon you: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-28

**29** Do not prostitute thy daughter, to cause her to be a whore; lest the land fall to whoredom, and the land become full of wickedness. ^lev-19-29

**30** Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-30

**31** Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards, to be defiled by them: I am the LORD your God. ^lev-19-31

**32** Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man, and fear thy God: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-32

**33** And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him. ^lev-19-33

**34** But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God. ^lev-19-34

**35** Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure. ^lev-19-35

**36** Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am the LORD your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt. ^lev-19-36

**37** Therefore shall ye observe all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: I am the LORD. ^lev-19-37
