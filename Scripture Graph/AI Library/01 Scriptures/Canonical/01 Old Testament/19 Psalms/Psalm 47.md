# Psalm 47

**1** O clap your hands, all ye people; shout unto God with the voice of triumph. ^ps-47-1

**2** For the LORD most high is terrible; he is a great King over all the earth. ^ps-47-2

**3** He shall subdue the people under us, and the nations under our feet. ^ps-47-3

**4** He shall choose our inheritance for us, the excellency of Jacob whom he loved. Selah. ^ps-47-4

**5** God is gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of a trumpet. ^ps-47-5

**6** Sing praises to God, sing praises: sing praises unto our King, sing praises. ^ps-47-6

**7** For God is the King of all the earth: sing ye praises with understanding. ^ps-47-7

**8** God reigneth over the heathen: God sitteth upon the throne of his holiness. ^ps-47-8

**9** The princes of the people are gathered together, even the people of the God of Abraham: for the shields of the earth belong unto God: he is greatly exalted. ^ps-47-9
