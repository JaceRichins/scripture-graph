# Psalm 123

**1** Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens. ^ps-123-1

**2** Behold, as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress; so our eyes wait upon the LORD our God, until that he have mercy upon us. ^ps-123-2

**3** Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt. ^ps-123-3

**4** Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, and with the contempt of the proud. ^ps-123-4
