# Psalm 54

**1** Save me, O God, by thy name, and judge me by thy strength. ^ps-54-1

**2** Hear my prayer, O God; give ear to the words of my mouth. ^ps-54-2

**3** For strangers are risen up against me, and oppressors seek after my soul: they have not set God before them. Selah. ^ps-54-3

**4** Behold, God is mine helper: the Lord is with them that uphold my soul. ^ps-54-4

**5** He shall reward evil unto mine enemies: cut them off in thy truth. ^ps-54-5

**6** I will freely sacrifice unto thee: I will praise thy name, O LORD; for it is good. ^ps-54-6

**7** For he hath delivered me out of all trouble: and mine eye hath seen his desire upon mine enemies. ^ps-54-7
