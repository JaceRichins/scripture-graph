## Evidence & Easter Eggs
<!-- SG:BEGIN evidence -->
> [!evidence] Archaeological/Epigraphic *(tentative)*
> A clay seal impression (bulla) reading 'belonging to Gemaryahu [son of] Shaphan' was recovered in a documented, provenanced excavation context (Yigal Shiloh's 'House of Bullae' archive in the City of David, Area G, dated to the final years before Jerusalem's fall). The name and patronymic match the 'Gemariah the son of Shaphan the scribe' whose chamber is used for the scroll reading in this chapter, showing that officials bearing exactly this name and lineage are attested in the correct social class and narrow time window.
