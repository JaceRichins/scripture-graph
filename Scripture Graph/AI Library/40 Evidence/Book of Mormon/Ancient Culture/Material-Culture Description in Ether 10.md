# Material-Culture Description in Ether 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The economic catalog is strong evidence for the kind of complex, specialized society portrayed by the narrative, but only weak evidence for identifying a particular archaeological culture or assigning modern technical meanings to every material term.

**Does not establish:** The catalog alone does not establish the historical identity, date, location, or archaeological confirmation of the Jaredite society.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The list may use broad translation vocabulary rather than modern technical classifications.
- Some items may be literary idealization or a conventional prosperity catalog.
- Words such as silks, brass, and iron do not by themselves identify production methods, species, alloys, or archaeological sites.
