# Intertextual Language in Jacob 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jacob 6 concentrates recognizable biblical formulations concerning a second recovery, outstretched hands, hearing today, hardened hearts, the narrow gate, judgment, and fire. This strongly supports reading the chapter as consciously scriptural discourse, but the parallels have limited value for determining provenance.

**Does not establish:** The parallels alone establish neither ancient authorship nor modern composition, and they do not determine the direction of literary dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallels may represent ancient Israelite scriptural inheritance.
- They may arise from a modern English translation deliberately using King James Bible diction.
- Some expressions may be common religious formulas rather than specific quotations.
- Different parallels may have different explanations rather than one common source.
