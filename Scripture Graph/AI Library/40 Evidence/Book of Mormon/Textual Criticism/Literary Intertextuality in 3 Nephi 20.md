# Literary Intertextuality in 3 Nephi 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit instruction to search Isaiah, followed by a sustained and thematically integrated reuse of Isaiah 52, is strong evidence that the chapter deliberately interprets Isaiah through the themes of covenant fulfillment, gathering, and redemption rather than merely reproducing an isolated verbal parallel.

**Does not establish:** The intertextual pattern does not by itself establish ancient authorship, the historical setting of the discourse, or the fulfillment of its prophecies.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler, editor, or translator could have created the integration rather than the historical speaker.
- The arrangement may primarily reflect the compositional aims of the English text rather than a recoverable ancient-language structure.
- Readers may disagree about the degree to which the Isaiah units have been reinterpreted rather than simply applied.
