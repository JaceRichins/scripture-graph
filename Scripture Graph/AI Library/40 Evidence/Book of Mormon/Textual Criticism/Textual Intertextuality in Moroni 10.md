# Textual Intertextuality in Moroni 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moroni 10:8-17 closely parallels the organization and several items of the spiritual-gift catalog in 1 Corinthians 12:4-11: diverse gifts arise from the same Spirit and include wisdom, knowledge, faith, healing, miracles, prophecy, tongues, and interpretation.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of dependence, the language of an underlying source text, or ancient versus modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Moroni and Paul may describe a shared religious understanding without direct literary dependence.
- The English wording may reflect biblical language familiar to the translator.
- The catalog may have been deliberately modeled on the King James rendering of 1 Corinthians.
