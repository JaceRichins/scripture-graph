# Scriptural Intertextuality in 2 Nephi 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of siege, being brought low, speech from the ground, whispering from the dust, chaff, and sudden destruction closely follows Isaiah 29:3-5. This is strong evidence of a literary relationship between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the date, direction, mechanism, or ancient authorship of the Book of Mormon passage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Nephi may be deliberately adapting Isaiah's language to a new people and record.
- A modern composition or English translation process could have drawn directly on the King James wording of Isaiah.
- Some shared imagery could reflect a common prophetic idiom, although the ordered cluster of parallels makes coincidence alone unlikely.
