# Literary Intertextuality in 3 Nephi 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained and ordered reuse of Isaiah 52 and Micah 5 is strong evidence that the chapter intentionally interprets its restoration and gathering scenario through earlier prophetic texts.

**Does not establish:** Intentional intertextuality by itself does not establish when the chapter was composed, its original language, or the historical antiquity of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The English parallels could have been introduced through conscious modern composition or editing rather than through an ancient source text.
- A translation could deliberately render ancient allusions in familiar King James language, increasing the visible verbal overlap.
