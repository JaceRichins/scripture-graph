# Literary Intertextuality in 2 Nephi 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of dust, rising, bonds, and captivity language is good evidence of a literary relationship between Lehi's exhortation and Isaiah 52:2. It is less certain whether the passage presents a conscious quotation, an allusive reuse by the narrator, or biblical language supplied through translation.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish ancient authorship, a particular original language, or direct quotation by the historical Lehi.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The shared expressions may arise from broad biblical diction rather than a deliberate reference to one verse.
- The relationship may belong to the English translation rather than to Lehi's original wording.
- A modern author familiar with Isaiah could deliberately construct the same verbal cluster.
