# Doctrinal Textual Inference in Alma 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma 13:3-5 provides genuine but not conclusive textual support for a premortal-foreordination reading. Phrases such as "from the foundation of the world," "foreknowledge of God," and "in the first place" permit that interpretation, but the syntax does not unambiguously locate the described faith, works, and choices in premortal life.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the faith, good works, or rejection described in these verses necessarily occurred before mortal birth, nor that foreknowledge removes meaningful agency.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The calling may have been prepared eternally while the qualifying faith and works occurred in mortality.
- The passage may move between divine preparation and mortal response without marking the temporal transitions explicitly.
- The language may emphasize foreknowledge rather than a prior ordination of particular individuals.
