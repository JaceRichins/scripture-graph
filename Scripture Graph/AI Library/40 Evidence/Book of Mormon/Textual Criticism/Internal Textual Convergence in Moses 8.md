# Internal Textual Convergence in Moses 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated references to teaching as "in the beginning," continuity with Enoch's Gospel, and named Christian ordinances strongly support the literary conclusion that Moses 8 deliberately frames Noah's ministry as a continuation of the Gospel of Jesus Christ rather than only as a generic warning of disaster.

**Does not establish:** This literary pattern does not by itself establish when the wording originated, whether Noah used these exact terms, or the historical antiquity of the account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Christian terminology may be understood as revelatory expansion that renders an earlier message in later theological language.
- A historical-critical reading may understand the terminology as nineteenth-century theological recasting rather than verbatim antediluvian speech.
