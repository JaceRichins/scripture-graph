# Textual Dependence And Intertextuality in 3 Nephi 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained agreement with Matthew 5 in sequence, imagery, and English wording is strong evidence of a direct literary relationship between the two received texts. It is not, by itself, evidence that can determine whether the relationship arose through divine translation into familiar biblical language, deliberate modern composition, or another process.

**Does not establish:** The English correspondence does not establish the date, language, or authorship of the underlying discourse, nor does it independently prove or disprove ancient historicity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A revealed or ancient parallel sermon may have been translated into familiar King James English.
- Joseph Smith may have consciously adapted Matthew 5 while composing or dictating the passage.
- A combination of an underlying parallel discourse and translator-level biblical phrasing may account for the text.
