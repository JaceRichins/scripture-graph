# English Textual Dependence in Mosiah 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 23:13 closely parallels the English wording of Galatians 5:1. This is strong evidence that the Book of Mormon's English text participates in King James biblical diction, but the observation by itself cannot determine whether that diction arose through translation, conscious literary imitation, or another mode of composition.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not independently establish the chapter's historical setting, date of composition, or whether its underlying narrative is ancient or modern.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A translator could have rendered an underlying idea in familiar biblical English.
- A modern author could have consciously adapted Galatians 5:1.
- Pervasive familiarity with the King James Bible could have produced an unconscious verbal echo.
