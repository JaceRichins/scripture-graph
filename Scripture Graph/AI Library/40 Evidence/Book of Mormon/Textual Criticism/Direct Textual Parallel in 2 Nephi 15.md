# Direct Textual Parallel in 2 Nephi 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive verse-level correspondence between 2 Nephi 15 and Isaiah 5 is strong textual evidence that 2 Nephi 15 presents Isaiah 5 material.

**Does not establish:** Textual correspondence by itself does not establish the date of composition, the direction or mechanism of transmission, or the larger historical claims of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence alone does not determine the precise translation or textual-transmission process.
- Shared wording may reflect quotation, dependence on a common textual tradition, or later translation conventions.
