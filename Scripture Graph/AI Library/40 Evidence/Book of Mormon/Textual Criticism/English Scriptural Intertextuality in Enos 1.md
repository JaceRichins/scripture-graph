# English Scriptural Intertextuality in Enos 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The English expressions about faith making a person whole and about the blessed entering a prepared place in the Father's mansions closely parallel New Testament language. This is strong evidence that the English text participates in a King James biblical register.

**Does not establish:** The English parallels alone do not determine the wording of an underlying ancient text, the historical direction of dependence, or the truth or falsity of the narrative.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallels may reflect Joseph Smith's familiar biblical diction.
- Believers may understand the wording as a revealed translation accommodated to the scriptural language of its intended audience.
- Some phrases may be conventional Christian expressions rather than direct borrowing from one specific verse.
