# Literary Dependence And Intertextuality in 2 Nephi 30

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained verbal and sequential correspondence with Isaiah 11 is strong evidence that 2 Nephi 30:9, 11–15 intentionally invokes the Isaianic vision and uses it as a framework for Nephi's prophecy.

**Does not establish:** The parallel by itself does not establish the date, translator, production mechanism, or ancient historicity of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence could be described as quotation, adaptation, or reuse of a shared scriptural source; the text alone does not settle the best technical label.
- A modern-composition model can explain the Isaiah language through direct use of the King James Bible, while a traditional model understands Nephi as drawing on Isaiah available on the brass plates.
