# Intra-Textual Ethical Contrast in Alma 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between wealth obtained by industry, prideful treatment of others, and voluntary aid to the needy supports the interpretation that the chapter's ethical critique centers strongly on pride and the social use of possessions, not simply on productive labor itself.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that wealth is morally neutral in every circumstance or that industry guarantees righteous prosperity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrative may also regard riches themselves as a recurring spiritual danger, even when honestly acquired.
- The text does not disclose whether those serving the poor possessed wealth comparable to the proud group.
- The passage does not provide a general economic theory distinguishing acceptable from excessive accumulation.
