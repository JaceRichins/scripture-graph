# Textual Parallel in Helaman 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Helaman 10:7 and Matthew 18:18 share a close earth-and-heaven ratification formula and the language of loosing. This strongly supports a literary or verbal relationship in their English forms, although it does not determine the relationship's direction, date, or historical mechanism.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish ancient authorship, direct dependence on Matthew, or that sealing and binding denote identical offices in every context.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The English wording may intentionally invoke familiar King James Bible language during translation.
- A modern-composition model can explain the parallel as direct or indirect borrowing from Matthew.
- A believing model can explain it as related divine commissions expressed through a shared scriptural idiom.
