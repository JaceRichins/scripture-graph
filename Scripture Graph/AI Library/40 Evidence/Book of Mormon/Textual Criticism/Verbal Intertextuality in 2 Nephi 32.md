# Verbal Intertextuality in 2 Nephi 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The English wording about asking and knocking and about praying always without fainting closely parallels New Testament phrasing, providing strong evidence of Christian scriptural intertextuality at the level of the received English text.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallels alone do not establish the direction of dependence, the underlying source language, or ancient versus modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallels may represent direct literary dependence on familiar biblical language.
- They may reflect a translation rendered in the idiom of the King James Bible.
- They may arise from teachings understood by believers as deriving from the same divine source.
