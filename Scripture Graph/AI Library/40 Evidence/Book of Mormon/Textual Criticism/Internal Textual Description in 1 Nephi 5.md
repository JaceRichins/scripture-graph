# Internal Textual Description in 1 Nephi 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The inventory of the brass plates is strong internal evidence that the narrative portrays them as a broad scriptural, historical, prophetic, and genealogical archive rather than as only the five books of Moses.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish that physical plates existed, determine their exact textual contents, or prove that their collection matched any later biblical canon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The inventory may summarize several sections of one collection without specifying their exact extent or arrangement.
- Later readers may import assumptions about the contents and boundaries of the later Hebrew Bible that the chapter itself does not state.
