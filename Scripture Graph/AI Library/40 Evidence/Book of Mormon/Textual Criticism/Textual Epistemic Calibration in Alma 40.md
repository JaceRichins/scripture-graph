# Textual Epistemic Calibration in Alma 40

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The discourse carefully separates what Alma says he knows from unresolved chronology: he reports diligent inquiry and angelic disclosure, calls some mysteries known only to God, treats the number of resurrection times as immaterial, and labels the timing of the righteous resurrection as his opinion.

**Does not establish:** This textual care does not independently establish that an angel appeared, that the disclosed doctrine is historically true, or that the work has ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The qualifications may function rhetorically to make the speaker appear modest and trustworthy.
- A later composer could intentionally construct the same distinction between revelation, inference, and uncertainty.
