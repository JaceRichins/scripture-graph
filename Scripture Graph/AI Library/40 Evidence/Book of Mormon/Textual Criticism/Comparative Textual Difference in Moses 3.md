# Comparative Textual Difference in Moses 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moses 3 contains explicit material not present in the corresponding Genesis verses, notably the explanation of spiritual creation and the statement that Adam may choose for himself. This establishes that Moses 3 is not merely an exact reproduction of Genesis 2 at these points, while leaving the origin and authority of the expansions open.

**Does not establish:** The differences alone do not establish antiquity, translation from an ancient manuscript, historical accuracy, or revelatory origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The differences may be modern interpretive expansions.
- They may represent revelatory clarification or restoration.
- They may adapt ideas available from other religious or intellectual sources.
