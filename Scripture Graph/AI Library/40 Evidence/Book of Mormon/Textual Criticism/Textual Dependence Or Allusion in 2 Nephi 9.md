# Textual Dependence Or Allusion in 2 Nephi 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close verbal correspondence between 2 Nephi 9:50-51 and Isaiah 55:1-2 is strong evidence of literary reuse or deliberate allusion at the level of the received English texts.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish when the dependence arose, its original language, a particular translation process, or ancient authorship of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence could have been produced or sharpened through the English translation's reliance on familiar biblical diction.
- A later editor or narrator could have formulated the invitation with Isaianic language.
