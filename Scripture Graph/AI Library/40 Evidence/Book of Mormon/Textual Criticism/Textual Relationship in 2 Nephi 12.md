# Textual Relationship in 2 Nephi 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's attribution to Isaiah and its extensive verbal correspondence with Isaiah 2 provide very strong evidence of a direct literary relationship between the two chapter texts.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish the date or direction of dependence, the antiquity of the Book of Mormon text, or a particular translation mechanism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence can be explained within a traditional account by Nephi copying an Isaiah text available on the brass plates.
- A modern-origin model can explain the correspondence through dependence on the King James Bible.
- Some differences may reflect a distinct underlying textual form, translation choices, deliberate adaptation, or later transmission.
