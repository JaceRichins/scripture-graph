# VerbalTextual Parallel in Jacob 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jacob 1:7's phrase 'as in the provocation in the days of temptation while the children of Israel were in the wilderness' closely echoes the King James wording of Psalm 95:8 and its quotation in Hebrews 3:8 ('as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the wilderness'), raising a question about the relationship between Book of Mormon phrasing and specific KJV biblical texts.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish direct literary dependence in either direction, nor does it bear on Book of Mormon historicity by itself; it is primarily a translation-language observation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The overlap may reflect Joseph Smith's translation rendering the underlying text into familiar King James idiom, a translation-process phenomenon that would not indicate the ancient source text directly quoted this specific psalm.
- Both texts may independently reference the well-known wilderness-rebellion tradition (also recounted in Numbers 14) without any direct literary dependence between them.
