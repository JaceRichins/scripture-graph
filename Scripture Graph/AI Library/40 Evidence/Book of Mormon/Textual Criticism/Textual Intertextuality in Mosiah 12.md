# Textual Intertextuality in Mosiah 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 12:21–24 reproduces Isaiah 52:7–10 in closely corresponding sequence and wording. This is strong evidence of a deliberate textual relationship and makes Isaiah's proclamation of peace, Zion's restoration, and God's salvation central to the ensuing dispute.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not establish the direction, date, or historical circumstances of textual dependence, nor does it independently establish ancient or modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may depend on a shared scriptural source or translation tradition.
- The English correspondence may partly reflect the language chosen by a translator rather than the precise wording of an underlying source text.
