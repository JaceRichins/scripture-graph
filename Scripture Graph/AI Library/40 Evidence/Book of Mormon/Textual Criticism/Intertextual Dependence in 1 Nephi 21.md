# Intertextual Dependence in 1 Nephi 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained sequence and close wording across all twenty-six verses constitute very strong evidence that 1 Nephi 21 is a conscious quotation or adaptation of Isaiah 49.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence by itself does not establish when, how, or by whom the Book of Mormon passage was composed or translated.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The close wording could be described as translation through shared English biblical diction, but that still presupposes deliberate dependence on Isaiah 49.
- Different theories of Book of Mormon composition can agree on the textual dependence while explaining its origin differently.
