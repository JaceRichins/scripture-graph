# Internal Textual Transmission in Mosiah 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Alma's written record supplies an explicit transmission mechanism by which Abinadi's teachings could be preserved after his death. Because this mechanism is reported by the same narrative whose transmission it helps explain, it is internal evidence rather than independent corroboration.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historicity, completeness, or verbatim accuracy of the preserved speeches.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may function literarily to explain how Abinadi's discourse entered the record.
- The phrase "all the words" may be conventional or comprehensive language rather than a claim of verbatim transcription.
- Alma may have reconstructed the teachings from memory rather than recording them contemporaneously.
