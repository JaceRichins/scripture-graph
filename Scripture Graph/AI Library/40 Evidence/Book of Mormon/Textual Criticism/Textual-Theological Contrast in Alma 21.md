# Textual-Theological Contrast in Alma 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter's theological argument, the contrast between the Amalekite's appeal to sanctuaries and worship and Aaron's call to repentance supports the limited conclusion that religious participation is not presented as sufficient evidence of righteousness or redemption.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that sanctuaries, assemblies, or communal worship are intrinsically insincere, nor does it independently document the complete theology of the historical Amalekites.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may principally contrast competing doctrines of salvation rather than criticize outward religious practice in general.
- The narrator may be presenting a selective or polemically sharpened account of Amalekite beliefs.
