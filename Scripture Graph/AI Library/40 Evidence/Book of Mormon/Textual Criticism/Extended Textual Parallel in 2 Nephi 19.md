# Extended Textual Parallel in 2 Nephi 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained, sequential correspondence between 2 Nephi 19 and Isaiah 9 is strong evidence of a direct literary relationship or shared textual tradition.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not determine the direction, date, or precise mechanism of dependence, nor does it establish ancient Book of Mormon authorship, historicity, or divine inspiration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Nephi directly quoted or adapted an available Isaiah text.
- A modern translator rendered the passage through familiar English Isaiah wording.
- A modern author could have composed the chapter using the King James Bible.
- Both passages could represent transmission from a common antecedent text.
