# Intertextual New Testament Citation in 3 Nephi 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The New Testament Gospels apply verse 1's 'messenger' who 'shall prepare the way before me' to John the Baptist preparing for Christ's ministry (Matthew 11:10; Mark 1:2; Luke 7:27 each quote this verse in that context), while 'the messenger of the covenant' who 'shall suddenly come to his temple' is read as Christ himself — a reading already implicit in Jesus quoting this text to the Nephites about his own mission.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish that the original Malachi oracle had a single specific individual in view; NT usage may be typological application rather than fulfillment of a strictly predictive prophecy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some scholars read the Malachi 'messenger' as a rhetorical or composite figure rather than a specific predicted individual, with New Testament authors applying it typologically rather than citing a narrowly predictive prophecy with one intended referent.
