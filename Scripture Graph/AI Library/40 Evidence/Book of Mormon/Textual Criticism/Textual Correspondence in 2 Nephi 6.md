# Textual Correspondence in 2 Nephi 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained correspondence with Isaiah 49 is strong evidence of direct literary reuse or dependence at the textual level. It does not, by itself, identify when or by what historical mechanism that reuse occurred.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish ancient authorship, a particular source manuscript, a translation method, or the historicity of Jacob's discourse.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jacob could be quoting an ancient Israelite textual source within the narrative.
- The English text could have been produced through conscious or unconscious dependence on the King James Bible.
- The correspondence could reflect a combination of an underlying source and later English biblical phrasing.
