# Intertextual Verbal Parallel in 3 Nephi 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The English wording of 3 Nephi 7:8 closely parallels the paired dog-and-sow proverb in 2 Peter 2:22; its dog-and-vomit image also appears in Proverbs 26:11. This is strong evidence of biblical intertextuality in the English Book of Mormon wording.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the language of an ancient source, the direction of dependence, or either ancient or modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A translation could deliberately render an older proverbial idea in familiar King James biblical language.
- Joseph Smith or another modern participant could have supplied the wording during composition or dictation.
- The passages may draw on a broadly shared proverbial tradition rather than direct literary dependence.
