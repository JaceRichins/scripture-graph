# Doctrinal-Textual in Ether 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In Ether 4:12, Christ declares 'I am the Father, I am the light, and the life, and the truth of the world.' Read in isolation, this self-identification as 'the Father' raises a genuine interpretive question about the Godhead that this verse alone does not resolve.

**Does not establish:** This single verse does not by itself establish or disprove any particular model of the Godhead; it must be read alongside other Book of Mormon passages (e.g., Mosiah 15:1-4) that address the Father/Son relationship more directly.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Read as an ancient spokesman idiom (a messenger speaking fully in the name/authority of the one who sent him), not a claim of numerical identity between Father and Son.
- Could be read by outside critics as evidence of ambiguity or tension with a model of three wholly distinct, separately embodied personages.
- Could simply emphasize unity of purpose and will between Father and Son rather than making any claim about personhood.
