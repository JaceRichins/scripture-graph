# TextualLinguistic Parallel in 3 Nephi 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
3 Nephi 26:5's wording describing the righteous going 'to the resurrection of everlasting life' and the wicked 'to the resurrection of damnation' closely parallels John 5:29 KJV, which uses the same 'resurrection of life... resurrection of damnation' contrast for the same doctrinal point.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish literary dependence, translation method, or anything about the antiquity of the underlying record; it establishes only a strong verbal similarity between the two English texts as we have them.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The life/damnation judgment dichotomy is common biblical theology and could be independently expressed rather than textually borrowed.
- Many Latter-day Saint readers hold that the translation process rendered ancient concepts into familiar King James idiom, which would produce this kind of verbal overlap regardless of any literal dependence on the biblical text.
