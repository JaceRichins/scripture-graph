# Explicit Intratextual Interpretation in 1 Nephi 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Later visions and explanations within 1 Nephi explicitly identify the tree with the love of God, the rod with the word of God, the mist with the temptations of the devil, and the building with the vain imaginations and pride of humanity; they also interpret the filthy water and gulf. This strongly supports reading 1 Nephi 8 symbolically within the book's own narrative framework.

**Does not establish:** These interpretations do not establish that every detail has exactly one symbolic referent, nor do they independently establish the narrative's external historicity or date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later explanations may develop or systematize the vision rather than exhaust every possible meaning of its imagery.
- Readers may distinguish the final narrator's canonical interpretation from hypotheses about earlier stages of composition.
