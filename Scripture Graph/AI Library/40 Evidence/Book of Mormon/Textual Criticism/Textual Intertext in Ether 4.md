# Textual Intertext in Ether 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ether 4:18 closely parallels Mark 16:16–17 in sequence and wording: belief and baptism lead to salvation, unbelief leads to condemnation, and signs follow believers. The overlap securely establishes an English-text intertext, although it does not by itself determine how or when the relationship arose.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of dependence, the date of the underlying discourse, or the historical authenticity of either narrative setting.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The translation may intentionally render an ancient discourse in familiar King James biblical language.
- The wording may reflect direct literary dependence on the King James New Testament.
- Both passages may express a shared Christian commission formula, with the English similarity intensified in translation.
