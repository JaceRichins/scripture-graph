# Literary Intertextuality in 1 Nephi 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentrated parallels with Revelation 17 provide strong evidence of a deliberate literary or revelatory relationship between the two presentations. They do not by themselves determine the historical direction or mechanism of that relationship.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish ancient authorship, the direction of dependence, or which account is historically prior.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Believing readers may understand both visions as independent revelations of the same future realities.
- The Book of Mormon translation may intentionally express Nephi's vision through the familiar language of the King James Bible.
- A modern-composition hypothesis can explain the parallels as direct literary dependence on Revelation.
- Some images may derive from a broader stock of biblical apocalyptic symbolism rather than direct dependence on one passage.
