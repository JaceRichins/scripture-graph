# Textual Correspondence in 2 Nephi 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter corresponds closely and continuously to Isaiah 10, establishing a direct literary relationship between the two chapter texts.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not determine the direction or historical mechanism of dependence, the date of the underlying material, or the Book of Mormon's antiquity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The English correspondence could reflect direct use of King James phrasing during translation.
- Both texts could represent the same inherited Isaianic source transmitted through different textual histories.

**Scripture:** [[2 Nephi 20#^2ne-20-1|2 Nephi 20:1-34]], [[Isaiah 10#^isa-10-1|Isaiah 10:1-34]]
