# Textual Characterization in Alma 55

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit counterfactual in Alma 55:18–19 is strong textual evidence that the narrative's ethical ideal for military leadership includes restraint when killing is unnecessary and prioritizes deliverance over bloodshed. It is not independent evidence of Moroni's historical motives.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historicity of the episode, prove that no one was harmed, or show that every tactic used in the operation was ethically justified.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may idealize Moroni rather than preserve his actual motives.
- Avoiding an attack may also be explained by tactical risk and the desire to take prisoners.
- The narrator's approval of restraint does not settle the ethics of the preceding deception or the subsequent forced labor.
