# Textual Relationship in Moses 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared genealogical order, ages, and wording provide strong evidence of a literary relationship between Moses 6 and Genesis 5. The additional material in Moses 6 makes the genealogy a history of transmitted revelation as well as descent.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not establish the date, historical accuracy, ancient provenance, or revelatory mechanism of the additional material.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Moses 6 may directly expand the biblical text of Genesis 5.
- Both accounts may be understood within the faith tradition as deriving from older revealed material.
- The relationship could combine inherited biblical wording with newly composed interpretive expansion.
