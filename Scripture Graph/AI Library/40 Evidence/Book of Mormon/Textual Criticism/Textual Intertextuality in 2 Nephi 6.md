# Textual Intertextuality in 2 Nephi 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The material in 2 Nephi 6:6-7 and 6:16-18 closely corresponds to Isaiah 49:22-26, corroborating the chapter's explicit presentation of Isaiah as its scriptural source. The final portion includes explanatory or expanded wording, so the relationship should not be described as mechanically verbatim throughout.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the direction or historical mechanism of dependence, the language of composition, or the Book of Mormon's ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- English correspondence may reflect direct literary reuse, a shared source, translation conventions, or editorial shaping.
- Jacob's explanatory wording may be interwoven with the cited prophetic language.
