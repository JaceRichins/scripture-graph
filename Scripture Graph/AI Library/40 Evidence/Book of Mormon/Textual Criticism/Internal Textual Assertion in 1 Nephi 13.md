# Internal Textual Assertion in 1 Nephi 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter clearly witnesses to the narrator's claim that plain and precious teachings and covenants were withheld or removed. By itself, that assertion is not independent historical evidence identifying particular lost passages, responsible institutions, dates, or manuscript changes.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish which passages were removed, that all losses occurred through one organization, or that extant manuscript evidence confirms the complete historical scenario.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may refer broadly to lost teachings or covenants rather than to identifiable deletions from surviving biblical manuscripts.
- Some losses could be understood as interpretive suppression, mistranslation, selective transmission, or doctrinal obscuring rather than physical excision.
- The passage may express a theological account of apostasy without supplying a reconstructable history of the biblical text.
