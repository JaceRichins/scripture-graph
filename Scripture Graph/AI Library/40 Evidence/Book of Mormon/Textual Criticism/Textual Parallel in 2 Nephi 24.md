# Textual Parallel in 2 Nephi 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Corresponding passages at the beginning, center, and end of 2 Nephi 24 parallel the same verse positions in Isaiah 14, supporting a sustained and direct literary relationship between the chapters.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the date, original language, or historical transmission mechanism of the Book of Mormon text.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The relationship may be explained through quotation from or dependence on an Isaiah textual tradition.
- The English correspondence may partly reflect the language of the translation rather than the precise wording of an ancient Nephite record.
