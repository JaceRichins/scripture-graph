# Intertextual Reuse in 2 Nephi 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The description of Gentile kings and queens as nursing fathers and mothers closely reuses Isaiah 49:23 language already quoted in Jacob's discourse, providing strong evidence of deliberate literary continuity with the surrounding Isaiah exposition.

**Does not establish:** The verbal correspondence does not establish ancient authorship, access to a particular Hebrew manuscript, or the historical fulfillment of the prophecy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording could have been introduced or standardized during English translation.
- A modern author familiar with the King James Bible could also reproduce Isaiah's language.
- The connection demonstrates literary dependence but does not by itself identify when or how that dependence arose.
