# Textual Parallel in 3 Nephi 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The six verses of 3 Nephi 25 correspond in sequence and substance to the six verses of Malachi 4. This is strong evidence of literary dependence or a shared textual source at the level of the English texts, but it does not by itself determine the historical route or direction of that dependence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the Book of Mormon's antiquity, modern composition, translation method, or historical authenticity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrative represents Jesus as transmitting Malachi's prophecy to the Nephites.
- A translator may have rendered an ancient quotation in familiar King James Bible language.
- A modern author or editor may have adapted Malachi 4 directly.
- Both passages may represent a shared source expressed through the English wording of Malachi.
