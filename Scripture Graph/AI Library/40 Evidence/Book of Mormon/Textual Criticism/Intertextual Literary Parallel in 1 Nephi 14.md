# Intertextual Literary Parallel in 1 Nephi 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
1 Nephi 14:11's description of a 'whore' who 'sat upon many waters' echoes Revelation 17:1's description of 'the great whore that sitteth upon many waters,' suggesting the two passages draw on related apocalyptic imagery of a corrupt world-power personified as a woman enthroned on waters.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish literary dependence of Nephi's vision on the book of Revelation, only that both employ related apocalyptic imagery of a woman/whore enthroned on many waters.

**Alternative explanations:**
- 'Many waters' applied to a corrupt power is a stock biblical idiom that predates Revelation (e.g., Jeremiah 51:13 addresses Babylon as one that 'dwellest upon many waters'), so the echo may reflect shared biblical idiom rather than a deliberate link specifically to John's Revelation.
- The Book of Mormon's English rendering is saturated with KJV phrasing generally, which can produce verbal overlaps without the underlying vision itself being literarily dependent on Revelation.
