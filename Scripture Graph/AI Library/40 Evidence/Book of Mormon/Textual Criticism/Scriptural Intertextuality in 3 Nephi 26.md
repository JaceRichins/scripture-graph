# Scriptural Intertextuality in 3 Nephi 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The pairing in 3 Nephi 26:5 of good works with a resurrection of life and evil works with a resurrection of damnation closely parallels John 5:28–29. The distinctive correspondence strongly supports an intertextual relationship, although it does not determine the direction or historical mechanism of dependence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish ancient authorship, an independent ancient textual tradition, or whether John or 3 Nephi is historically prior.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jesus could be presented as repeating substantially the same teaching in two settings.
- A translator could have rendered related teachings with familiar Johannine or King James wording.
- A modern author could have directly adapted the language of John.
