# Textual Interdependence in 3 Nephi 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter-length correspondence with Malachi 3, reinforced by the explicit attribution to Malachi, is strong evidence of a direct literary relationship. The parallel alone cannot determine whether that relationship arose through an ancient citation rendered in familiar biblical language, modern dependence on the King James text, or another transmission process.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence by itself establishes neither ancient Book of Mormon authorship nor modern fabrication, and it does not identify the precise translation or transmission mechanism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An ancient Malachi citation was translated using familiar King James phrasing.
- A modern author or translator directly adapted the King James text.
- Both texts reflect a shared intermediary textual tradition.
