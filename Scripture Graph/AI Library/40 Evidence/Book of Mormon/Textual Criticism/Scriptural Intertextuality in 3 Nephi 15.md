# Scriptural Intertextuality in 3 Nephi 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repetition of the other-sheep saying, followed by an explicit identification and an explanation of its former misunderstanding, provides very strong textual evidence that 3 Nephi 15 deliberately engages John 10:16 as an interpretive problem.

**Does not establish:** The verbal relationship alone does not establish the direction or historical mechanism of dependence, the historicity of the reported discourse, or ancient Book of Mormon authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The relationship may represent Jesus historically explaining an earlier saying.
- The English wording may reflect an inspired translation expressed through familiar King James Bible language.
- The relationship may reflect Joseph Smith's familiarity with John 10 or deliberate modern literary composition.
