# Textual Parallel in 3 Nephi 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive sequence and wording shared by 3 Nephi 12 and Matthew 5 provide strong evidence of a literary or traditional relationship between the two chapters. That observation is much stronger than any conclusion about the direction or mechanism of dependence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel by itself does not establish ancient American provenance, direct access to Matthew's written Gospel, the historical setting of the sermon, or a particular theory of Book of Mormon translation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both texts may represent versions of a shared sermon or teaching tradition.
- The Book of Mormon text may deliberately adapt the familiar language of the King James Bible.
- A believing account may understand the correspondence as the risen Jesus repeating and adapting teachings delivered in the Old World.
- The English translator or translation process may have supplied biblical phrasing for conceptually corresponding material.
