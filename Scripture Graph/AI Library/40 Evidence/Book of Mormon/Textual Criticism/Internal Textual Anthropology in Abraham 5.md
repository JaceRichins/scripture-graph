# Internal Textual Anthropology in Abraham 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Abraham 5:7 is strong internal textual evidence that this account portrays mortal human life as involving both a dust-formed body and a distinguishable spirit. It supports, but does not by itself fully define, a doctrine of premortal human existence.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish when or how the spirit originated, nor a complete metaphysical account of the relationship between spirit, body, breath, and soul.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may be visionary or narrative imagery rather than a technical account of the origin and embodiment of spirits.
- The wording may expand the biblical breath-of-life motif without intending to settle every question about premortal existence.
