# Verbatim Textual Parallel in 3 Nephi 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
3 Nephi 10:5 reproduces, almost verbatim, Jesus' lament over Jerusalem recorded in Matthew 23:37, including the closing clause 'and ye would not.' The wording match is close enough to be a direct textual echo rather than a general thematic similarity.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish whether the repetition reflects an actual historical utterance repeated in two hemispheres versus a translation artifact drawing on familiar biblical phrasing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verbatim overlap may reflect the translation process rendering an underlying revelation in familiar King James cadence, rather than proof that Christ spoke identical words on two continents.
- If read as historical, Christ could plausibly have reused a favorite teaching image in more than one setting during the same year, since Matthew 23 predates the crucifixion and 3 Nephi 10 follows it by only months.
- The exact match could also indicate later editorial harmonization in how the text was recorded or transmitted.
