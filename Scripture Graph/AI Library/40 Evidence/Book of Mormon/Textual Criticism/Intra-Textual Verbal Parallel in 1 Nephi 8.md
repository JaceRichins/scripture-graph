# Intra-Textual Verbal Parallel in 1 Nephi 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma's seed discourse culminates in fruit described through the same unusual cluster of superlative sweetness, whiteness, and preciousness found in Lehi's vision. The close overlap is strong evidence of an intra-Book-of-Mormon literary and theological relationship between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the direction of literary dependence, the historical setting of either discourse, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently draw on shared scriptural tree-and-fruit imagery.
- The overlap may partly reflect a common English translation register rather than direct dependence by one depicted speaker upon the other.

**Scripture:** [[1 Nephi 8#^1ne-8-10|1 Nephi 8:10-12]], [[Alma 32#^alma-32-40|Alma 32:40-42]]
