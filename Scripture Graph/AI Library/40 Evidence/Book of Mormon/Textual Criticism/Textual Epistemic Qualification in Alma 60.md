# Textual Epistemic Qualification in Alma 60

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter strongly establishes that Moroni suspects the government of seeking power or committing treason, but his own wording marks this as an inference rather than confirmed knowledge.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish either the guilt or the innocence of Pahoran and the government officials.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated qualification may be a rhetorical device intended to pressure the recipients rather than a careful statement of evidentiary limits.
- Moroni may possess information not reproduced in the chapter, although the text does not identify such information.

**Scripture:** [[Alma 60#^alma-60-18|Alma 60:18]], [[Alma 60#^alma-60-19|Alma 60:19]]
