# TextualLiterary Parallel in 3 Nephi 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's near-total wording match with the King James rendering of Isaiah 54 is real and mechanically checkable evidence of a direct textual relationship between the Book of Mormon text and the Isaiah tradition preserved in the KJV. This textual dependency is consistent with more than one explanatory model of the Book of Mormon's origin and translation, and the wording match alone does not settle the historical question between them.

**Does not establish:** The presence of KJV-parallel wording, by itself, does not establish either ancient Nephite authorship or 19th-century composition; it establishes only that this chapter and Isaiah 54 (KJV) are textually related.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Believing model: an ancient Nephite record quoted Isaiah from the brass plates, and Joseph Smith's translation drew on familiar KJV idiom to render the underlying text into English.
- Critical model: the wording match reflects direct literary dependence on the KJV during the Book of Mormon's 19th-century composition/translation process, used as a base text for these passages.
