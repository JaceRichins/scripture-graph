# Textual And Literary Dependence in 3 Nephi 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive agreement in content, order, and distinctive English expressions between 3 Nephi 14 and Matthew 7 is strong evidence of a literary relationship or shared English textual form. It is not merely a similarity of general doctrine: the correspondence extends through nearly the whole chapter in the same sequence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not determine the translation process, identify the historical source behind the Nephite sermon, or prove either ancient authenticity or modern fabrication.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A translator may have rendered an ancient parallel sermon through familiar biblical English.
- The English text may directly adapt the King James Version of Matthew.
- The parallel language may reflect deliberate divine accommodation to the audience of the English translation.
- Some shared material may derive from common Jesus tradition, although the sustained English wording and order require additional explanation.
