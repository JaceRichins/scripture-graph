# Internal Textual Consistency in 3 Nephi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The 609-minus-9 calculation genuinely coordinates 3 Nephi 2 with the earlier 600-year prophecy, but ancient editing, translation harmonization, and nineteenth-century construction predict that coordination about as well as continuously preserved chronology. It therefore provides none-band support for a historical Nephite chronological system.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
3 Nephi 2:6 dates the narrative to 609 years after Lehi's departure, while verses 7–8 place it nine years after the sign associated with Christ's coming. Subtracting nine from 609 yields the 600-year interval stated in 1 Nephi 10:4 and 1 Nephi 19:8.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The final text deliberately coordinates the earlier prediction, its narrated fulfillment, and the new chronological era.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

## Historical Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
A preserved ancient chronology could produce the correspondence, but internal coordination alone cannot distinguish continuous record-keeping from retrospective construction.
<!-- SG:END historical-significance -->

## How This Fits
<!-- SG:BEGIN how-it-fits -->
On the narrative's chronological reconstruction, the sign occurred 600 years after Lehi's departure and the present notice falls nine years later. This joins prediction, narrated fulfillment, and calendrical transition in one coherent timeline.
<!-- SG:END how-it-fits -->

## Apologetic Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN apologetic-significance -->
The exact arithmetic is sometimes offered as evidence of authentic record-keeping. Its evidentiary force is minimal because ancient editing, translation harmonization, and nineteenth-century composition working from the earlier prophecy predict the same result about as well.

**Inspiration:** The arithmetic bears on final-form textual coordination, not on whether the text or its underlying message is divinely inspired.
<!-- SG:END apologetic-significance -->

## What It Does Not Establish
<!-- SG:BEGIN does-not-establish -->
It does not establish a continuously tracked ancient year-count, the historicity of the narrated events, or a pre-nineteenth-century origin.
<!-- SG:END does-not-establish -->

## Models On The Table
<!-- SG:BEGIN models -->
- **Authentic ancient record** — predicts: A coherent count if the chronology was continuously preserved. Fit with this evidence: *consistent but not uniquely favored*.
- **Editorial systematization** — predicts: Numbers coordinated with the earlier prophecy in the final narrative. Fit with this evidence: *equally consistent*.
- **Translation-level harmonization** — predicts: A clarified or coordinated English chronology. Fit with this evidence: *consistent*.
- **Nineteenth-century composition** — predicts: Numbers constructed from the already-stated 600-year interval. Fit with this evidence: *equally consistent*.
<!-- SG:END models -->

## Alternative Explanations
<!-- SG:BEGIN alternatives -->
- Deliberate editorial coordination with the earlier prophecy — *plausible*: It directly explains why the two chronological eras meet at exactly the previously specified interval.
- Nineteenth-century construction working backward from the earlier prophecy — *plausible*: The necessary information appears within the same final text and requires no external chronological knowledge.
- A genuinely tracked ancient count — *possible*: The text permits this reconstruction, but the arithmetic supplies no independent support over the rival models.
<!-- SG:END alternatives -->

## Comparative Check
<!-- SG:BEGIN symmetry -->
An equivalent numerical correspondence in another religious corpus would establish internal chronological coordination but would not, without independent anchoring, be treated as evidence of an unbroken historical count.
<!-- SG:END symmetry -->
