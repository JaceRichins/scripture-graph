# Internal Textual Consistency in 3 Nephi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Subtracting the 9 years since the sign of Christ's coming (3 Nephi 2:7-8) from the 609 years since Lehi left Jerusalem (3 Nephi 2:6) yields exactly 600 years from Lehi's departure to Christ's coming, matching the earlier prophecy (1 Nephi 10:4; 1 Nephi 19:8) that this interval would be 600 years.

**Does not establish:** This internal arithmetic consistency does not establish the historicity of the events or an ancient (non-19th-century) origin for the text; it only shows the received text is internally self-consistent on this point.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The arithmetic could reflect deliberate literary/editorial shaping by Mormon (compiling centuries after the fact) to make the earlier prophecy's fulfillment explicit, rather than an independently preserved year-count.
- Minor internal chronological notices elsewhere in the Nephite record are occasionally imprecise, so precise agreement here could be coincidental rounding rather than a tracked count.
