# VerbalTextual Echo in Moroni 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 19's description of Christ as 'the same yesterday, today, and forever' verbally matches the formula in Hebrews 13:8 ('Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever'), an identical three-part temporal formula applied to Christ's unchanging nature.

**Does not establish:** The verbal match does not by itself establish direct literary dependence on Hebrews or settle questions of composition or translation method.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The phrase is a widely used devotional formula that could be independently applied without direct literary borrowing.
- If composed in a King James idiom, the echo may reflect stylistic imitation of biblical English rather than intentional citation.

**Scripture:** [[Moroni 10#^moro-10-19|Moroni 10:19]], [[Hebrews 13#^heb-13-8|Hebrews 13:8]]
