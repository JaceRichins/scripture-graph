# Textual Interrelationship in 3 Nephi 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus' prayer for the immediate disciples and those who will believe through their words, together with its language of mutual indwelling and becoming one, has a close literary and theological affinity with Jesus' prayer in John 17. The affinity strongly supports intertextual relationship but does not determine its historical direction or mechanism.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish direct quotation in an ancient source, textual independence from the New Testament, ancient authorship, or any particular theory of translation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may intentionally recall John 17 as part of the English translation's biblical register.
- A modern author familiar with the King James Bible could have composed the parallel.
- Some similarities could arise from shared Christian themes, although the combined sequence and phrasing make purely generic resemblance less adequate.
