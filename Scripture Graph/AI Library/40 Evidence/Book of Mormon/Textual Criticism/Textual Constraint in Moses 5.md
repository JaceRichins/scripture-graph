# Textual Constraint in Moses 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The only purpose explicitly assigned to the mark upon Cain in this chapter is protection from being killed. The text does not identify the mark's physical form, call it a skin color, or state that it passes to Cain's descendants.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish what the mark looked like, whether it was hereditary, or how it should be correlated with any later population.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The unspecified mark could have been physical, social, symbolic, or otherwise recognizable.
- Other interpreters may combine this passage with other texts or traditions, but those additions are not stated in Moses 5:40.

**Scripture:** [[Moses 5#^moses-5-39|Moses 5:39]], [[Moses 5#^moses-5-40|Moses 5:40]]
