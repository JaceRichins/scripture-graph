# Internal Textual Argument in 2 Nephi 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Nephi's argument, Christ's baptism supplies an example-based, a fortiori reason for human baptism: if the holy Lamb was baptized, unholy people have still greater need to be baptized and follow him.

**Does not establish:** This textual argument does not independently establish the historicity of the baptism, Nephi's prophetic access to it, or every later doctrinal claim about baptism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The reasoning is theological and rhetorical; a reader who rejects its premises need not accept its conclusion.
- Christ's baptism may also have meanings unique to his mission, so not every aspect of it necessarily transfers to disciples.
