# English Textual Parallel in 3 Nephi 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sayings about asking and knocking, followed by the contrast between the strait and wide gates, closely parallel Matthew 7 and their earlier presentation in 3 Nephi 14. This is strong evidence of an intertextual relationship in the received English wording.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not by themselves establish the historical direction of dependence, the language of an underlying source, or either ancient or modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be an intentional reprise of teachings Jesus had already delivered in 3 Nephi 14.
- The Matthew-like wording may reflect the language chosen by the English translation rather than identical wording in an ancient source.
- Some elements may derive from shared proverbial or traditional teachings rather than direct literary copying.
