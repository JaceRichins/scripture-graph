# Internal Textual Coherence in Mosiah 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit connection between retaining remission from day to day and relieving the poor is strong internal textual evidence that material and spiritual care for those in need is integral to Benjamin's account of persevering discipleship, not a detached social appendix.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that charitable gifts purchase forgiveness, nor does it settle broader debates over grace, works, justification, or sacramental conditions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Care for the poor may be read primarily as evidence that remission is being retained rather than as an instrumental condition for retaining it.
- The language may function as covenant exhortation without defining a systematic doctrine of the causal relation between grace and works.
