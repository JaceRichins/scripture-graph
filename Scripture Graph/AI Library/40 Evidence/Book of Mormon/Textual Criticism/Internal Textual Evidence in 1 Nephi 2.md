# Internal Textual Evidence in 1 Nephi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated conditional clauses are strong internal evidence that prosperity, leadership, separation from God's presence, and power between the descendants are narrated as consequences contingent on obedience or rebellion, rather than as unconditional privileges attached permanently to one lineage.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every prosperous community is righteous, that every suffering community is rebellious, or that later ethnic or racial categories inherit an unconditional curse.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The conditional formula may serve the narrator's theological explanation of later history rather than provide a complete account of every historical outcome.
- Readers may differ over whether the promised prosperity is primarily spiritual, material, communal, or some combination.
