# Textual Correspondence in 2 Nephi 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The verified chapter-wide textual correspondence with Isaiah 13 is strong evidence that 2 Nephi 23 presents that Isaianic oracle. The correspondence establishes a literary relationship much more securely than it establishes any particular account of authorship or translation.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not prove ancient Book of Mormon authorship, identify the underlying-language text, or determine the mechanism by which the English wording arose.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may reflect Isaiah text transmitted on the plates and rendered through translation.
- The English rendering may have been consciously adapted to familiar King James wording.
- A modern-composition explanation can also account for extensive dependence on the King James Isaiah text.
