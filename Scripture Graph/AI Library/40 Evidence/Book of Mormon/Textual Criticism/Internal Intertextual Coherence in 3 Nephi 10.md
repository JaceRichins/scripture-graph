# Internal Intertextual Coherence in 3 Nephi 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The appeal to Zenos and Zenock as prophets of events associated with Christ's coming forms a substantial internal connection with 1 Nephi 19, which also attributes signs surrounding Christ's death—including three days of darkness and disruptions of the earth—to these prophetic traditions. This is strong evidence of deliberate intertextual coherence within the Book of Mormon.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the antiquity, independent transmission, or historical fulfillment of the attributed prophecies.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single author or editor could intentionally create the correspondence.
- Later editorial harmonization could connect the destruction narrative to earlier prophecy.
- Both passages could independently draw upon the same narrative or prophetic tradition.
