# Internal Textual Evidence in Alma 39

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma 39 supplies direct textual evidence that its speaker considers knowledge of Christ and the plan of redemption necessary before Christ's coming. The argument appeals to the equal preciousness of present and future souls and to God's ability to send an angel in either period.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the historical antiquity of the discourse, the occurrence of the angelic revelations mentioned, or a detailed theory of how redemption operates across time.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage establishes what the narrative's speaker teaches; by itself it cannot verify when or under what historical circumstances the discourse originated.
- The argument may explain the availability of pre-advent revelation without specifying the complete mechanism by which Christ's future atonement applies to earlier generations.
