# Intertextual Resonance in Mormon 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mormon 7:5 combines the biblical images of death being swallowed up and death's sting with victory over the grave. The correspondence strongly supports deliberate participation, at least in the English wording, in the scriptural language found in Isaiah 25:8 and 1 Corinthians 15:54-57. It does not determine the historical direction or mechanism of that relationship.

**Does not establish:** The verbal relationship does not by itself establish ancient authorship, modern authorship, a direct quotation from one particular passage, or a specific translation process.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may represent ancient authors drawing on a shared scriptural tradition.
- A translator may have rendered related ideas through familiar King James Bible language.
- A modern author could have composed the passage from biblical language.
