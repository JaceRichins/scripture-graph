# Textual Intertextuality in 2 Nephi 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained textual correspondence between 2 Nephi 13 and Isaiah 3 is strong evidence that 2 Nephi 13 deliberately incorporates the Isaiah passage.

**Does not establish:** Textual correspondence alone does not determine the date or provenance of the Book of Mormon record, the source route by which Isaiah entered it, or the mechanics of translation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The relationship may reflect translation of an ancient record containing Isaiah.
- The wording may have been mediated through the King James Bible during a modern translation process.
- A modern-composition hypothesis can also explain direct dependence on the King James Isaiah text.

**Scripture:** [[2 Nephi 13#^2ne-13-1|2 Nephi 13:1-26]], [[Isaiah 3#^isa-3-1|Isaiah 3:1-26]]
