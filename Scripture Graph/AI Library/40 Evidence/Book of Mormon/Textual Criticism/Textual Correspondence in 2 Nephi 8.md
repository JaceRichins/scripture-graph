# Textual Correspondence in 2 Nephi 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close, sustained correspondence of 2 Nephi 8:1–23 with Isaiah 51:1–23 and of 2 Nephi 8:24–25 with Isaiah 52:1–2 is strong evidence of a direct literary relationship with Isaiah.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish the direction or historical mechanism of dependence, the antiquity of a particular source manuscript, the translation method, or the Book of Mormon's larger historical claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jacob may be quoting an inherited Isaiah text, as the Book of Mormon narrative presents the passage.
- The English wording may reflect deliberate use of, or harmonization with, the King James Version during translation or later textual preparation.
- Some differences may represent translation choices, textual variants, dictation or copying effects, or editorial changes.
