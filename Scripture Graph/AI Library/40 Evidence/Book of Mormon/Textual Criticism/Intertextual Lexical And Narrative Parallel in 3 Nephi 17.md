# Intertextual Lexical And Narrative Parallel in 3 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clustered lists of disabilities and the mass-healing setting in 3 Nephi 17 and Matthew 15 provide substantial evidence of a literary or traditional relationship between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish the direction of dependence, the date of composition, or the historicity of either reported healing event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may employ a conventional catalogue of illnesses associated with Jesus's healing ministry.
- The resemblance may reflect deliberate biblical allusion or English translation into biblical diction.
- A shared type of remembered healing event could produce similar lists without direct textual dependence.
