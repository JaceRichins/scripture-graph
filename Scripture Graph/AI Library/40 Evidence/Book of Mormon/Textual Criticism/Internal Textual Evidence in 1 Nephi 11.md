# Internal Textual Evidence in 1 Nephi 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Because the narrative supplies explicit interpretations for the tree, waters, rod, and building, those identifications are strong evidence for the symbols' intended functions within this chapter. The same internal evidence does not independently verify the vision as a historical event or prove that each symbol has only one possible application.

**Does not establish:** The historicity of the vision, ancient authorship of the account, or an exhaustive and exclusive meaning for every symbol.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The explicit glosses may represent the narrator's or final editor's literary construction rather than a recoverable historical vision.
- A symbol can retain additional associations even when the narrative gives it a primary interpretation.
