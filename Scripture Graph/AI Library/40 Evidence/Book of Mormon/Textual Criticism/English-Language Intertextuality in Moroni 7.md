# English-Language Intertextuality in Moroni 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moroni 7:45-46 has a close verbal and sequential correspondence with the description of charity in 1 Corinthians 13:4-8. This strongly supports a literary relationship at the level of the English texts, while the direction and historical mechanism of that relationship remain disputed.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence by itself does not establish who composed the underlying discourse, when it originated, or whether ancient Nephites possessed 1 Corinthians.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Joseph Smith may have rendered an ancient discourse through familiar King James biblical language.
- The correspondence may reflect modern composition or conscious reuse of 1 Corinthians.
- The two passages could be understood within a faith framework as separate revelations expressing the same divine teaching.
- Some individual phrases are conventional, although the accumulated sequence makes wholly accidental resemblance unlikely.
