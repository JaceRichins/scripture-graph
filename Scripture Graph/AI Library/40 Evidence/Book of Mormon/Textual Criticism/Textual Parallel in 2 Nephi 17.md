# Textual Parallel in 2 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive verbal overlap between 2 Nephi 17 and Isaiah 7 strongly establishes a direct literary relationship between the chapters. The overlap alone does not determine a complete transmission history or independently establish the antiquity of the Book of Mormon account.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the direction, date, or precise mechanism of textual transmission, nor does it prove either ancient or modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Book of Mormon presents Nephi's record as reproducing Isaiah from an ancient scriptural source.
- A naturalistic account can attribute the English wording to direct dependence on the King James Bible.
- The shared wording by itself cannot decide between competing accounts of composition and translation.
