# Textual Parallel in 2 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
2 Nephi 17's chapter-length correspondence with Isaiah 7 strongly supports textual dependence on the Isaiah/KJV tradition, exactly as with every other Book of Mormon Isaiah block. This note's weight is corrected from 0.99 down to the registry's canonical 0.84 for this shared issue, since scoring one instance of a registered pattern near-certain while the pattern itself is rated strong is an internal inconsistency, not new evidence. It does not determine the direction, date, or mechanism of textual transmission, nor does it prove ancient or modern authorship. ## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
2 Nephi 17 reproduces Isaiah 7 almost verbatim across its full length, including the Ahaz narrative frame (v.1), the Immanuel sign (v.14), and the closing agricultural imagery (v.25), with only minor orthographic/punctuation differences from the KJV.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The chapter-length, near-total verbal identity is best read as direct textual dependence on the King James rendering of Isaiah 7, the same relationship already established for the corpus's other Isaiah blocks.
