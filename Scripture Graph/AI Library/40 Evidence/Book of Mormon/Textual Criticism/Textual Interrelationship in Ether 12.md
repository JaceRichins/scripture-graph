# Textual Interrelationship in Ether 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of New Testament-like language is strong evidence of textual interrelationship at the level of the English wording, but it is weak evidence for choosing among historical explanations for that relationship.

**Does not establish:** These verbal and conceptual parallels do not independently establish ancient authorship, modern composition, or a particular theory of translation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Joseph Smith's translation language may consciously or unconsciously draw on the King James Bible.
- A divine translation process may render earlier ideas through familiar biblical language.
- Some similarities may arise from shared religious themes rather than direct dependence.
