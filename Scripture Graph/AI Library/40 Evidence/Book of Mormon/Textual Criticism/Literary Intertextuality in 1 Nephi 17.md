# Literary Intertextuality in 1 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained concentration of Exodus motifs is strong evidence that the chapter deliberately frames the Lehite journey as analogous to Israel's deliverance. It is literary evidence for purposeful scriptural interpretation within the narrative, not independent proof that the narrated events occurred.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the text's date, historical authenticity, or uniquely ancient origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallels may reflect a modern author's deliberate reuse of biblical narratives rather than an ancient writer's typology.
- Some similarities arise naturally because both accounts describe migration through a wilderness under claimed divine guidance.
