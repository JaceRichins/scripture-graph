# Internal Textual Claim About Ancient Practice in Moroni 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter asserts, as an internal historical claim, that a real doctrinal dispute over infant baptism existed among Mormon's people; this is an in-text assertion only and is not independently corroborated by any external record of ancient American religious practice.

**Does not establish:** That infant baptism was actually practiced by any historical pre-Columbian American population; only that the text asserts internal disputation about it.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The dispute could function as a literary/rhetorical device addressing a live 19th-century American controversy over infant baptism rather than reporting an actual 5th-century Nephite practice.
- The 'disputation' may reflect generalized doctrinal drift described in broad terms rather than a specific, datable practice.
