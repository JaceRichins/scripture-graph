# Textual Parallel in 3 Nephi 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of taking, breaking, blessing, giving, eating, drinking, and remembering Jesus has a strong thematic and verbal relationship to New Testament accounts of the Last Supper and Paul's discussion of the Lord's Supper.

**Does not establish:** The parallel by itself does not establish the direction of literary dependence, the historical setting of the narrative, or the antiquity of the English wording.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallel may reflect Jesus instituting the same ordinance in two settings.
- The English wording may reflect biblical language familiar to the translator or the nineteenth-century translation environment.
- Shared early Christian liturgical language could account for recurring expressions.
