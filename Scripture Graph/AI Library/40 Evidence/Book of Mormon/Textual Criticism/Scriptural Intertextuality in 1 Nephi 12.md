# Scriptural Intertextuality in 1 Nephi 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The judgment of Israel by twelve and the making-white-in-the-Lamb's-blood formula demonstrate close Christian scriptural intertextuality. These correspondences strongly support literary dependence, shared tradition, or deliberate allusion, but the parallels by themselves cannot determine the direction or historical setting of that relationship.

**Does not establish:** The verbal and conceptual parallels do not by themselves establish ancient provenance, modern composition, or a specific translation mechanism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Book of Mormon text may directly reuse language from the King James Bible.
- The correspondences may reflect revelation expressed through familiar biblical language.
- The texts may share broader Christian traditions rather than a direct literary relationship in every instance.
