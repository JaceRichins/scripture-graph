# Literary Intertextuality in 2 Nephi 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's recognizable links to the raised serpent, the Lord setting his hand a second time, a marvelous work and wonder, healing in the Messiah's wings, and salvation through no other name provide strong evidence that its discourse is deliberately composed within a biblical intertextual world.

**Does not establish:** These literary correspondences do not by themselves establish ancient authorship, prophetic foreknowledge, or a particular translation mechanism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some correspondences may arise from shared scriptural vocabulary rather than a distinct intended allusion.
- The intertexts are compatible with more than one account of the Book of Mormon's composition and translation.
