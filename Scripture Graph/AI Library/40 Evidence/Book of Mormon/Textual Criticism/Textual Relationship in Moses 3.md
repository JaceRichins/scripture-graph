# Textual Relationship in Moses 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Close correspondences in the formation of man, planting of Eden, garden vocation, command concerning the tree, creation of the woman, and one-flesh saying provide strong evidence of a direct literary relationship between Moses 3 and Genesis 2. The correspondences alone do not determine the direction or mechanism of that relationship.

**Does not establish:** The overlap does not by itself establish direction of dependence, ancient provenance, modern composition, or divine revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Moses 3 may consciously revise or expand the received Genesis text.
- Both accounts may reflect a shared source or narrative tradition.
- A believing interpretation may understand the relationship as revelatory restoration or clarification.
