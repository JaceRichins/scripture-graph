# Textual Dependence in 2 Nephi 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequential, chapter-length correspondence between 2 Nephi 18 and Isaiah 8 is very strong evidence of a direct textual relationship. It establishes dependence upon or transmission of the Isaiah passage, but by itself cannot determine when, where, or by what translation process that relationship arose.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the Book of Mormon's antiquity, the historical possession of Isaiah by Nephi, or a particular translation mechanism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A believing account attributes the parallel to Isaiah material preserved on the brass plates and translated by divine means.
- A naturalistic account attributes the English wording to direct or mediated use of the King James Isaiah text.
- Later editorial or translation-stage harmonization could also increase the English-level similarity.

**Scripture:** [[2 Nephi 18]], [[Isaiah 8]]
