# Repeated Textual Motif in Abraham 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurring statements that created domains “were obeyed,” creatures “would be obeyed,” and the completed plan would be “very obedient” provide strong textual evidence that responsiveness to divine ordering is a deliberate motif in Abraham 4.

**Does not establish:** The motif does not establish that matter, plants, animals, or natural forces possess moral agency equivalent to human agency.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions may chiefly be a stylistic feature used to mark successful completion of each command.
- “Obeyed” may describe reliable natural processes rather than moral agency in nonhuman creation.
