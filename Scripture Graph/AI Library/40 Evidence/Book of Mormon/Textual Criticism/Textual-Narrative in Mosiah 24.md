# Textual-Narrative in Mosiah 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As primary textual evidence for the chapter's own theology, the sequence from inward prayer to sustaining strength and eventual escape strongly supports the interpretation that divine visitation may include both endurance within affliction and release from it.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical occurrence or mechanism of the reported miracles, promise that every affliction will end similarly, or show that suffering itself indicates divine approval.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may be a literary-theological shaping of remembered events rather than a transparent account of their historical mechanism.
- The people's improved ability to bear labor could be interpreted psychologically or communally rather than as a physically measurable change.
- An escape later understood providentially could be narrated through direct divine causation.
