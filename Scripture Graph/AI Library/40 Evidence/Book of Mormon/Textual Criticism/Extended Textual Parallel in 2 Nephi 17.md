# Extended Textual Parallel in 2 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Second Nephi 17 and Isaiah 7 share the same sustained narrative, sequence, characters, oracles, and close wording. This establishes a direct literary relationship or shared textual ancestry with very high confidence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not decide between ancient-source, translation, or modern-compositional explanations and therefore does not independently prove either ancient or modern authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An ancient source text may have contained Isaiah material that was rendered in familiar biblical English.
- The English text may directly use or closely imitate the King James Version.
- The relationship may reflect a combination of source inheritance, translation choices, and later biblical phrasing.
