# Extensive Textual And Narrative Parallel in Abraham 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Abraham 2 has a strong literary relationship with Genesis 12: both narrate the call from kindred, departure from Haran with Sarai and Lot, arrival at Moreh, a land promise, altar building near Bethel and Ai, southward travel, famine-driven movement to Egypt, and the sister strategy concerning Sarai.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of dependence, the date of the expansions, or the ancient or modern origin of Abraham 2.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both texts may preserve versions of a shared earlier tradition.
- Abraham 2 may be an inspired expansion or reinterpretation of the Genesis account.
- The relationship may reflect modern composition or revision using the King James Genesis text.
