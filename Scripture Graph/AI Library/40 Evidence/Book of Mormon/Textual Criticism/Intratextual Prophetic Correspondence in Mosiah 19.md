# Intratextual Prophetic Correspondence in Mosiah 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The earlier warning and the later account of Noah's death provide strong evidence of deliberate intratextual correspondence, but their force as evidence for supernatural foreknowledge or independent historicity is limited without evidence external to the narrative.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish supernatural foreknowledge, the historical independence of the prediction and fulfillment, or the historicity of either event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later episode may have been composed or edited to correspond to the earlier prediction.
- The prediction may have been narrated retrospectively after the reported death.
- The correspondence may function primarily as literary retribution within the story.

**Scripture:** [[Mosiah 12#^mosiah-12-3|Mosiah 12:3]], [[Mosiah 19#^mosiah-19-20|Mosiah 19:20]]
