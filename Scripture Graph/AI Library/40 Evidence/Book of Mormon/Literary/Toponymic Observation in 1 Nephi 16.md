# Toponymic Observation in 1 Nephi 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter supplies the internally specific claim that Ishmael was buried at a place called Nahom, but the chapter by itself cannot identify that place with any modern archaeological or geographic site.

**Does not establish:** The occurrence of the name alone does not establish a particular Arabian route, an archaeological match, or ancient authorship of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Nahom could reflect an ancient regional name.
- The name could be literary, coincidental, adapted from a known name, or transmitted through a modern linguistic environment.
- Proposed external matches may refer to a tribe or broad region rather than the precise burial location described.

**Scripture:** [[1 Nephi 16#^1ne-16-34|1 Nephi 16:34]]
