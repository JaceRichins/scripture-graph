# Narrative Structure in 3 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from limited understanding, to compassionate healing, to ineffable prayer, to individualized blessing, and finally to an angelic manifestation supports reading the chapter as a deliberately shaped ascent in intimacy and wonder.

**Does not establish:** Narrative coherence does not establish a particular author, ancient provenance, or historical accuracy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The progression may simply follow the remembered chronology of events rather than a conscious literary design.
- Readers may perceive escalation because the final supernatural scene is intrinsically more dramatic.
