# Biblical Intertext in Jacob 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jacob 1:7 closely invokes the scriptural complex found in Psalms 95 and Hebrews 3: provocation, temptation in the wilderness, divine wrath, and failure to enter God's rest. The clustered vocabulary and sequence make a literary relationship highly probable, while leaving its precise mechanism unresolved.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not determine whether Psalms, Hebrews, an older shared tradition, translation choices, or modern composition produced the English wording.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jacob may be invoking an Israelite wilderness tradition represented by Psalms 95.
- The English translator may have rendered an underlying idea in familiar biblical language.
- A modern author familiar with the King James Bible could have composed the passage from Psalms 95 or Hebrews 3.
