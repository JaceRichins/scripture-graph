# Literary Organization in Ether 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The alternating summaries of rulers, prophetic warnings, responses, and consequences provide strong textual evidence that this chapter is organized as moral historiography rather than as a comprehensive political chronicle.

**Does not establish:** The organization does not by itself establish the historical accuracy, supernatural causation, date, or ancient provenance of the account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may result from ordinary annalistic compression rather than an elaborate literary plan.
- The moral pattern may reflect a later narrator's selection and arrangement of inherited material.
- Omitted political, environmental, and economic causes could have been important in the underlying events.
