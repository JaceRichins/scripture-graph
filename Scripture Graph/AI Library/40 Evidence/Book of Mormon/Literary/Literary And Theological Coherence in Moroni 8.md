# Literary And Theological Coherence in Moroni 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained progression from accountability to repentance, baptism, remission, the Holy Ghost, hope, love, endurance, and salvation is evidence of a carefully integrated theological argument rather than a bare prohibition of infant baptism. This is meaningful literary evidence of coherence but weak evidence for any particular theory of origin.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not establish ancient authorship, historicity, or a particular process of composition or translation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may reflect deliberate authorial composition.
- It may reflect editorial organization of inherited teachings.
- It may arise from fluent extemporaneous religious discourse.
- Some perceived order may result from readers imposing a system on related statements.
