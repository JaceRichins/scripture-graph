# Internal Literary Structure in Joseph Smith—Matthew 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The localized references in verses 12–20 and the explicit transition in verse 21 provide strong internal evidence that the opening tribulation is not presented solely as an undifferentiated description of the final coming.

**Does not establish:** This structure does not establish a complete prophetic timetable, the date of any fulfillment, or that every image has only one historical referent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Jerusalem material may also be treated typologically as a pattern for later events.
- Repeated language may intentionally blend historical and eschatological horizons rather than separating them completely.
- Readers may disagree about where individual predictions within the second section belong chronologically.
