# Internal Narrative Causation in Alma 56

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter gives a multi-causal account of the Nephite success: it invokes divine favor and extraordinary strength while also describing reinforcements, provisioning, deception, forced marches, surprise, enemy reorientation, regrouping, and encirclement. This is strong evidence that the narrative presents providence and human action together rather than treating tactics as irrelevant.

**Does not establish:** It does not measure the contribution of each cause, disprove providence, or independently verify that supernatural intervention occurred.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The tactical details may function as literary realism without preserving a complete historical explanation.
- A providential reading can regard the ordinary military causes themselves as instruments of divine action.
- The account may selectively emphasize causes favorable to Helaman's interpretation.
