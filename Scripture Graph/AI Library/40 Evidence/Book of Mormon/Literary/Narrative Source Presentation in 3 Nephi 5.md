# Narrative Source Presentation in 3 Nephi 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combined notices about severe compression, an identified intermediary record, personally manufactured plates, inherited versus eyewitness material, and linguistic limitation strongly establish that the chapter presents itself as self-conscious abridged historiography.

**Does not establish:** This literary evidence does not independently verify Mormon's historical existence, the physical plates, the accuracy of the omitted history, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The notices can be read as features of a deliberately constructed literary persona rather than descriptions of actual record production.
- Comparable source claims can occur in both genuine historiography and pseudohistorical literature.
