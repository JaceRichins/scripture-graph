# Narrative And Sensory Pattern in 3 Nephi 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated references to hearing, seeing, touching, knowing, and bearing record provide strong internal literary evidence that personal witness is a deliberate narrative emphasis in this chapter.

**Does not establish:** Narrative emphasis on eyewitness experience does not independently verify that the narrated event occurred historically.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sensory sequence may primarily serve dramatic narration rather than a formal theology of testimony.
- Repetition may reflect conventional scriptural storytelling rather than a tightly designed literary pattern.
