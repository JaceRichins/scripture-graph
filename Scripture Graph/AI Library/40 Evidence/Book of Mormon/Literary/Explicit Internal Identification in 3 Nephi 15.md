# Explicit Internal Identification in 3 Nephi 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative's own argument, the case that the present hearers belong to the 'other sheep' is explicit rather than merely inferred: Jesus directly identifies them and points to their sensory encounter with him.

**Does not establish:** This internal identification does not independently establish the historical existence, location, or ancestry of the people addressed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Readers may dispute the historical reality of the narrated encounter while still recognizing what the text explicitly claims.
- The statement identifies the immediate hearers but does not by itself identify every possible group included among the other sheep.

**Scripture:** [[3 Nephi 15#^3ne-15-21|3 Nephi 15:21]], [[3 Nephi 15#^3ne-15-24|3 Nephi 15:24]]
