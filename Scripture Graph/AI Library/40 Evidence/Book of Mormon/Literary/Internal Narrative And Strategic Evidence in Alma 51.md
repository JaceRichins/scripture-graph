# Internal Narrative And Strategic Evidence in Alma 51

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, the concurrence of internal dissent, refusal of military service, and Moroni's diversion against the king-men provides substantial evidence that political division impaired Nephite readiness during this invasion. The text explicitly treats dissension as destructive, while the Lamanites advance and capture the inadequately defended city of Moroni.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that internal dissent was the sole cause of the losses, that every dissenting position was illegitimate, or that the same political response is justified in other circumstances.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The city of Moroni may have fallen principally because its local force was insufficient, regardless of the domestic campaign.
- The Lamanites' numerical strength and Amalickiah's strategy are independently identified as causes of their success.
- The narrative's causal emphasis may reflect the narrator's political and moral interpretation rather than a complete military analysis.
