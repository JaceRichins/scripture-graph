# Narrative Countervoice in 1 Nephi 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The preservation of Laman's accusation is strong textual evidence that Nephi's authority is contested within the narrative. Because the accusation is reported by Nephi, it is not independent evidence establishing either Laman's account of Nephi's motives or Nephi's account of Laman's motives.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish that Nephi sought political power, that Laman accurately described events, or that the narrator gives a neutral account of the dispute.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The speech may be narratively shaped or compressed by Nephi rather than preserving Laman's exact words.
- Its inclusion may function primarily to legitimate Nephi by portraying opposition as murderous and faithless.

**Scripture:** [[1 Nephi 16#^1ne-16-37|1 Nephi 16:37]], [[1 Nephi 16#^1ne-16-38|1 Nephi 16:38]]
