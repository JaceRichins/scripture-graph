# Internal Prediction-Fulfillment in 3 Nephi 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed correspondence between Samuel's prophecy and 3 Nephi 8 strongly supports deliberate prediction-and-fulfillment composition within the Book of Mormon narrative. Because the prophecy and fulfillment are preserved in the same literary work, this correspondence alone is not independent evidence that the events occurred historically or that the prophecy predates them.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish independent prediction, ancient authorship, or the historical occurrence of the reported disasters.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment account may have been composed or edited with the earlier prophecy in view.
- Both passages may participate in a shared literary description of divine judgment rather than preserve independently reported details.
