# Explicit Source Attribution in 2 Nephi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Lehi explicitly introduces his account of the angel's fall as a supposition based on things he has read and on what is written. This is strong evidence for the discourse's presented posture as interpretation of prior writings, although it does not identify those writings.

**Does not establish:** The statement does not identify a specific source, prove dependence on any surviving textual form, or verify the historical circumstances of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be a rhetorical appeal to scriptural authority rather than a description of Lehi's actual reasoning process.
- The implied written source could be one text, several texts, or a synthesized tradition.

**Scripture:** [[2 Nephi 2#^2ne-2-17|2 Nephi 2:17]]
