# Narrative And Literary Structure in Mosiah 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter presents a coherent progression from private preaching to covenant initiation, church organization, communal discipline, economic care, discovery, and flight. This strongly supports reading Mosiah 18 as a deliberately structured account of community formation under persecution.

**Does not establish:** The narrative's coherence does not by itself establish the account's historical accuracy, date of composition, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The progression may partly reflect ordinary chronological narration rather than an elaborate literary design.
- The final form could result from editorial arrangement of inherited episodes.
