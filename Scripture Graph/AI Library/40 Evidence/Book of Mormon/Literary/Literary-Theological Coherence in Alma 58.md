# Literary-Theological Coherence in Alma 58

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from extremity through prayer, assurance, courage, disciplined action, and deliverance provides substantial textual evidence for a coherent theology of providence in the chapter: trust in God energizes prudent action rather than replacing it.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove divine intervention, supernatural communication, ancient authorship, or the historical accuracy of the narrated campaign.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence can be read as conventional religious interpretation imposed on an otherwise explicable military success.
- The successful strategy could be attributed entirely to human planning and Lamanite mistakes, with theological attribution reflecting Helaman's worldview.
