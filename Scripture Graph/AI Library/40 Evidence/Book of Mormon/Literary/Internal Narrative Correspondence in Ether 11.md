# Internal Narrative Correspondence in Ether 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative's own presentation, the correspondence between the announced curse and destruction in verse 6 and the wars, famines, pestilences, and destruction in verse 7 functions as strong literary evidence that the narrator regards the warning as fulfilled. This is evidence about the text's causal and theological framing, not independent verification of the events or their causes.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historicity of the calamities, supernatural prediction, divine causation, or the identity and date of the narrator.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator or an editor may have selected and arranged events to conform to a theological warning-and-fulfillment pattern.
- Wars, famine, and pestilence can arise through political, environmental, and epidemiological processes without requiring direct supernatural causation.
