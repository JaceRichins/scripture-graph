# Internal Literary Evidence in Helaman 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Helaman 3:13-17 is strong internal evidence that the work presents itself as a selective record compiled from a much larger documentary tradition. As internal literary evidence, however, it cannot independently verify that the claimed source records existed outside the narrative.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historical existence, date, contents, or transmission of records beyond the text's own claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The record notices could function as a literary historiographic frame rather than document a recoverable archive.
- Statements about omitted sources are not independently corroborated within this chapter.
