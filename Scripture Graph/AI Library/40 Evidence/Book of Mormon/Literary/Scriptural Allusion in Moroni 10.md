# Scriptural Allusion in Moroni 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moroni 10:31 combines recognizable language from Isaiah's calls for Jerusalem or Zion to awake, arise from dust, put on beautiful garments, enlarge her borders, and strengthen her stakes.

**Does not establish:** The allusion does not by itself establish when the chapter was composed, the form of its source language, or a particular theory of authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Moroni may intentionally combine several Isaianic restoration images.
- The English formulation may reflect the translator's biblical idiom.
- The wording may derive from a broader scriptural tradition shared with Isaiah.
