# Narrative Characterization in 2 Nephi 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Lehi's defense supplies direct narrative evidence that authority, motive, and manner of speech were contested within the family. It supports the interpretation that the later division was preceded by a genuine conflict over leadership, but Lehi's testimony is partisan evidence within the narrative and cannot independently settle every accusation.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not independently prove Nephi's historical motives, validate every exercise of his authority, or provide the brothers' full account of the conflict.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The accusations may function chiefly as literary preparation for the later division rather than as a balanced record of the dispute.
- Lehi may sincerely defend Nephi while still lacking access to every motive or interaction.
- Readers may regard the account as theological characterization rather than independently recoverable family history.
