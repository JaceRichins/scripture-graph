# Narrative Temporal Association in Alma 43

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrative associates united prayer for freedom with an immediate reversal in battle, stating that the Lamanites began to flee in the same hour that the Nephites cried to the Lord.

**Does not establish:** The sequence alone does not empirically establish that prayer was the sole or mechanically sufficient cause of the Lamanite retreat.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The military reversal can also be explained within the narrative by armor, morale, terrain, surprise, and encirclement.
- The temporal association may express the narrator's theology without isolating prayer as the sole military cause.

**Scripture:** [[Alma 43#^alma-43-49|Alma 43:49]], [[Alma 43#^alma-43-50|Alma 43:50]]
