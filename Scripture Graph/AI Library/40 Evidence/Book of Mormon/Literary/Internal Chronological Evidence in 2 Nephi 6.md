# Internal Chronological Evidence in 2 Nephi 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the Book of Mormon's narrative chronology, Jacob's statement about the Messiah's scourging and crucifixion is presented as a prophecy before those events. Its force as external evidence of predictive prophecy depends on independently establishing the text's date and history of composition.

**Does not establish:** The narrative placement alone does not establish a pre-Christian composition date or supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may preserve genuine pre-Christian prophecy.
- The passage may have been composed or shaped with knowledge of Christian accounts.
- The narrative setting and the historical date of composition may differ.
