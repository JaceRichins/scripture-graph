# Literary Continuity in 2 Nephi 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of entering the way, receiving the Holy Ghost, and the doctrine of Christ provides strong internal evidence that 2 Nephi 32 continues the discipleship argument of 2 Nephi 31.

**Does not establish:** This continuity does not establish a particular date, ancient authorship, or the antiquity of the chapter divisions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The continuity may reflect ordinary development of the same subject rather than a deliberately patterned literary design.
- Modern chapter boundaries can make the transition appear more pronounced than it was in the longer original textual unit.
