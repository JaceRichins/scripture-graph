# Messianic And Typological Interpretation in 2 Nephi 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
A Christian messianic reading is meaningfully supported by the servant's willing suffering, rejection, confidence in divine help, and anticipated vindication. The official chapter heading adopts this reading. Nevertheless, the chapter itself never explicitly calls the speaker the Messiah or Jesus Christ, so the evidence does not establish an exclusive original referent.

**Does not establish:** These correspondences do not prove that Jesus was the passage's sole original referent or that every detail is a direct prediction of his passion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The speaker may first portray Isaiah or another prophetic disciple.
- The servant may represent faithful Israel or an idealized faithful remnant.
- The passage may be typological, applying to Jesus without making him its sole initial referent.
- The Christian identification may arise primarily through retrospective reading of Jesus' passion.
