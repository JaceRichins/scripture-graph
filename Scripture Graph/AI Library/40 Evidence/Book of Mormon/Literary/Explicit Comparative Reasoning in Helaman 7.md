# Explicit Comparative Reasoning in Helaman 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Helaman 7 strongly supports a principle of responsibility proportionate to received knowledge, but its comparison is contextual and does not warrant a timeless claim that every Lamanite is more righteous than every Nephite.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a permanent moral ranking of ethnic or political groups.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The comparison may function primarily as prophetic shock rhetoric directed at a complacent audience.
- The statement may evaluate the two populations collectively rather than specify an abstract doctrine of individual accountability.

**Scripture:** [[Helaman 7#^hel-7-23|Helaman 7:23]], [[Helaman 7#^hel-7-24|Helaman 7:24]]
