# Doctrinal And Literary Integration in Moses 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moses 6:52–68 presents an internally integrated sequence of faith, repentance, baptism, reception of the Holy Ghost, justification, sanctification, and divine sonship. This is strong evidence that the passage intentionally presents a coherent plan of salvation.

**Does not establish:** The doctrinal coherence does not by itself establish when the discourse originated, whether Adam spoke these exact words, or which historical process produced the text.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The synthesis may represent restored primordial teaching, as the narrative claims.
- It may reflect a later Christian theological expansion placed in Adam's setting.
- Its coherence may result from editorial arrangement of several inherited scriptural themes.
