# Cross-Text Chronological Tension in Abraham 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Abraham 2 reports that Abraham was sixty-two when he departed Haran, whereas Genesis 12 reports that Abram was seventy-five when he departed Haran. On their most straightforward readings, the chronological notices differ.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy by itself does not determine which chronology is earlier or historically correct, nor does it settle the authorship of either account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The accounts may refer to separate departures from Haran.
- One age may derive from a different chronological tradition.
- A transmission or compositional error may account for the difference.
- A proposed harmonization may assign the notices to distinct stages of migration.

**Scripture:** [[Abraham 2#^abr-2-14|Abraham 2:14]], [[Genesis 12#^gen-12-4|Genesis 12:4]]
