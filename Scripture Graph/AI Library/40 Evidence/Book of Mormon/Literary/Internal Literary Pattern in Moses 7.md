# Internal Literary Pattern in Moses 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated weeping of God, the heavens, Enoch, and the personified earth is strong internal evidence that compassionate grief over sin and suffering is a central literary theme of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish a complete metaphysical account of whether or how God experiences changing emotions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may primarily heighten the vision's emotional force rather than define a general doctrine of divine emotion.
- Some readers may treat references to divine weeping as anthropomorphic or accommodated language.
