# Literary Structure in Abraham 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated comparative formula involving two things, followed by a greater third, is strong textual evidence that the celestial and spiritual discussions form a deliberate literary analogy. It is not evidence by itself that the described cosmology corresponds to modern astronomical categories.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the scientific accuracy of the cosmology, the physical location of God's throne, or the ancient provenance of the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition could be a loose rhetorical habit rather than a formally designed analogy.
- The celestial hierarchy may function only as an illustration suited to Abraham's vision, not as a technical account of astronomy.
