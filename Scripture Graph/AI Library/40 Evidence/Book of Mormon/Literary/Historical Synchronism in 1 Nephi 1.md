# Historical Synchronism in 1 Nephi 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The reference to Zedekiah and the anticipated Babylonian captivity is consistent with the late-Judah political crisis summarized in 2 Kings 24:10-17. This constitutes a coherent historical synchronism within the narrative, but only modest evidence beyond internal plausibility because the same setting is readily available in the Bible.

**Does not establish:** The synchronism does not independently establish the historicity of Lehi, the precise year of the vision, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A modern writer could construct the setting from the biblical account.
- The dating formula may be a broad narrative approximation rather than evidence of precise regnal chronology.
- Internal consistency can reflect literary research or imitation as well as historical memory.
