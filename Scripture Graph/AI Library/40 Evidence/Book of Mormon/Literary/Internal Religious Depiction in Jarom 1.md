# Internal Religious Depiction in Jarom 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jarom supplies direct internal evidence that the Book of Mormon depicts this Nephite community as combining Mosaic practice with explicit expectation of a coming Messiah.

**Does not establish:** The passage establishes what the text depicts; by itself it does not establish when that theology originated or whether the described community historically practiced it.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Believing readers may understand the passage as preserving pre-Christian revelation about Christ.
- Critical readers may understand the explicit Christian interpretation of Mosaic law as retrospective Christian composition or editorial framing.

**Scripture:** [[Jarom 1#^jarom-1-5|Jarom 1:5]], [[Jarom 1#^jarom-1-11|Jarom 1:11]]
