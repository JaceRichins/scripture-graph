# Internal Narrative Evidence in Mosiah 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Alma writes the revealed instructions down and subsequently judges according to them. This is strong internal evidence that the chapter portrays written revelation as a continuing standard for church administration.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historicity, date, or ancient authorship of the narrated institution.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recordkeeping detail may chiefly serve a literary or theological purpose rather than preserve an independently verifiable administrative history.
- The passage does not specify the document's later transmission, exact contents, or relationship to other records.
