# Narrative Coherence in Helaman 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The tower, garden gate, highway, market, passersby, and gathering crowd form a coherent spatial and social explanation for how Nephi's prayer becomes a public sermon.

**Does not establish:** Narrative coherence does not establish historicity, ancient authorship, or the identification of an archaeological site.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A skilled author can construct coherent spatial details without drawing on a historical event.
- The setting may primarily serve a literary need by supplying Nephi with an audience.
