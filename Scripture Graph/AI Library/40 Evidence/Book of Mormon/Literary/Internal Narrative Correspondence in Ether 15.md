# Internal Narrative Correspondence in Ether 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the Book of Ether's own narrative framework, the destruction of the rival forces, Coriantumr's survival of the final duel, and the narrator's explicit fulfillment statement strongly support the intended reading that Ether's warning in Ether 13 is being fulfilled.

**Does not establish:** This internal correspondence does not independently establish supernatural prediction, the historical scale of the war, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment correspondence may reflect deliberate literary or editorial construction rather than a historically predictive event.
- Ether 15 alone does not narrate every element of the earlier prophecy, including Coriantumr's eventual burial by another people.
