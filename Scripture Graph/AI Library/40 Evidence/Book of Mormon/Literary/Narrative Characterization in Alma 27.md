# Narrative Characterization in Alma 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, the converts' former murders, present repentance, refusal to kill, honesty, faithfulness, and lack of terror before death form a coherent portrait of moral transformation.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the narrative's historicity, the precise behavior of every community member, or that conversion always produces the same outward choices.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The portrait may be intentionally idealized for theological or moral instruction.
- Narrative coherence demonstrates literary design but does not independently verify the events historically.
- The narrator summarizes the whole community in general terms that need not describe every individual without exception.
