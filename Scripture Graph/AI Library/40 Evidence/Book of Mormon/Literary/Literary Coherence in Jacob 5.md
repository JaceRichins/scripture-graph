# Literary Coherence in Jacob 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The allegory sustains changing tree states across several cycles: initial decay, successful grafting, general corruption, reciprocal regrafting, gradual recovery, and final sorting. Recurrent roots, branches, fruit, nourishment, fire, and storage remain functionally connected throughout. This supports deliberate and comparatively sophisticated literary construction.

**Does not establish:** Literary complexity does not establish a particular date, mode of composition, historical author, or miraculous origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A capable modern author could construct a long, coherent allegory.
- Editing or revision could produce consistency.
- Some perceived structure may result from readers grouping naturally recurring narrative episodes.
