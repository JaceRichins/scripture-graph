# Compositional Coherence in Alma 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter coherently integrates the hearers' questions about worship and planting the word with a scriptural argument about prayer, a typology of looking and believing, and a final return to the seed-and-tree imagery. This strongly supports purposeful literary unity across Alma 32–33.

**Does not establish:** The coherence does not by itself determine authorship, date, historical setting, or whether the reported discourse is verbatim.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Literary unity can result from modern composition or editorial shaping as readily as from ancient authorship.
- Readers may perceive greater structural closure because the repeated seed vocabulary makes the links conspicuous.
