# Literary Dependence And Interpretation in 2 Nephi 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The density, wording, imagery, and ordered progression of correspondences constitute strong evidence that 2 Nephi 27 is literarily dependent on, or deliberately presented as an interpretation of, Isaiah 29 rather than merely sharing several general religious themes.

**Does not establish:** The literary relationship alone does not establish when, by whom, or through what revelatory or compositional process the expansion was produced.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A believer may understand the expansion as Nephi's inspired application of Isaiah to a future record.
- A historical-critical reader may attribute the expansion to the Book of Mormon's nineteenth-century production or translation context.
- Some correspondences reflect the shared English wording of the King James tradition and therefore cannot independently identify the expansion's historical author.
