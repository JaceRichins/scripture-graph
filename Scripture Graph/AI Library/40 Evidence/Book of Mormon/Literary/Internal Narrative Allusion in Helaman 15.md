# Internal Narrative Allusion in Helaman 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The uncommon combination of buried weapons, refusal to use swords, fear of renewed sin, and willingness to be slain is strong textual evidence that Helaman 15:9 recalls the Anti-Nephi-Lehi narrative in Alma 24.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish that every converted Lamanite in Samuel's day had personally participated in the events of Alma 24, nor does it independently establish the narrative's historicity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verse could summarize a recurring Lamanite conversion practice modeled on the earlier Anti-Nephi-Lehies rather than identify precisely the same community.
- The narrator may be invoking a familiar communal memory without intending a narrowly defined historical identification.
