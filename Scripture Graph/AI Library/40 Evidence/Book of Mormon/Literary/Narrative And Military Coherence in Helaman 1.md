# Narrative And Military Coherence in Helaman 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of surprise penetration, overextension, interception, rear attack, encirclement, and surrender provides strong evidence of deliberate strategic cause-and-effect within the narrative.

**Does not establish:** Narrative and strategic coherence do not establish that the battle occurred historically or that the account derives from an ancient military record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A skilled modern narrator could construct the same strategically coherent sequence.
- The account may simplify a more complicated conflict to emphasize a moral-political lesson.
- Plausible military narration can arise from literary convention or general knowledge rather than eyewitness reporting.
