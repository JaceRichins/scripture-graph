# Literary Characterization in Alma 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The crowd's several competing explanations are strong evidence of deliberate narrative polyphony: the text represents belief, hostility, political blame, traditional religious categories, and uncertainty rather than giving every observer immediate understanding.

**Does not establish:** Narrative complexity does not establish the account's historical origin, date, or factual accuracy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The alternatives may primarily heighten dramatic tension.
- The range of reactions can be explained by skillful storytelling without implying eyewitness preservation.
