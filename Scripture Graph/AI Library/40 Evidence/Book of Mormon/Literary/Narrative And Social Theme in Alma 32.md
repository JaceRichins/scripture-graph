# Narrative And Social Theme in Alma 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated references to clothing, poverty, social contempt, and denied synagogue access are strong internal evidence that economic exclusion from worship is a deliberate concern of this chapter, not incidental scenery.

**Does not establish:** The narrative emphasis does not independently establish that the described institution or exclusion existed historically outside the account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The exclusion may principally function as a literary setting for the sermon.
- The passage may portray a local abuse rather than a general feature of Zoramite or ancient worship.
