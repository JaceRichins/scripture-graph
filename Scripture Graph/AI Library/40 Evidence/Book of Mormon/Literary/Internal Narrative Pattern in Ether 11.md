# Internal Narrative Pattern in Ether 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated association of combinations with refusal, power-seeking rebellion, and prophetic rejection is strong internal evidence that the chapter deliberately presents organized secrecy as a systemic cause of Jaredite instability.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not independently establish the historical existence, structure, continuity, or exclusive causal responsibility of any secret organization.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may be imposing a theological explanatory framework on more complicated political events.
- Succession rivalry, economic distress, or other unreported causes could also have produced rebellion and war.
- The three expressions may summarize diverse groups rather than one continuous organization.
