# Internal Theological Presentation in Moses 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moses 5 presents the gospel as operating from humanity's beginning through angels, God's voice, the Holy Ghost, sacrifice, repentance, belief in the Son, and an ordinance. This strongly supports a literary-theological claim of continuity within the book's own narrative, but by itself provides little independent evidence for the historical antiquity of the described institutions.

**Does not establish:** The internal narrative does not independently establish the historical date, wording, or institutional form of primordial gospel practice.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A believing interpretation may understand the material as restored revelation about primordial religion.
- A historical-critical interpretation may understand the Christian terminology as a later theological recasting of Genesis.
- The text may be read primarily as typological theology rather than as a detailed institutional chronology.
