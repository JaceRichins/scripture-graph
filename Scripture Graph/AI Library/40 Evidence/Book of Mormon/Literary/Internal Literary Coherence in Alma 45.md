# Internal Literary Coherence in Alma 45

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The correspondence between Alma's prediction and the narrative in Mormon provides strong evidence of deliberate long-range literary coherence within the Book of Mormon. Its force as independent evidence for supernatural prediction or ancient authorship is much weaker because the final narrative and prophecy belong to the same transmitted work.

**Does not establish:** The internal correspondence does not by itself establish supernatural revelation, independent historical corroboration, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single author or editor could construct the later account to fulfill the earlier prediction.
- The prophecy could have been composed retrospectively or shaped during transmission.
- Both passages could reflect a shared narrative plan without demonstrating supernatural foresight.
