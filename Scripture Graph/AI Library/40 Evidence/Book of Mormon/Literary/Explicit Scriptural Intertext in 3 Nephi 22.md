# Explicit Scriptural Intertext in 3 Nephi 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The verse-for-verse correspondence between 3 Nephi 22 and Isaiah 54, together with the introductory reference to what is written, is very strong evidence of a deliberate literary relationship with Isaiah 54.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish the historical date of the discourse, the direction or mechanism of transmission, or the Book of Mormon's ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrative may preserve an ancient speaker's direct use of Isaiah.
- The English translation may deliberately render the passage in familiar King James Bible language.
- A modern-origin hypothesis can explain the correspondence as direct literary reuse of the King James Bible.

**Scripture:** [[3 Nephi 22#^3ne-22-1|3 Nephi 22:1-17]], [[Isaiah 54#^isa-54-1|Isaiah 54:1-17]]
