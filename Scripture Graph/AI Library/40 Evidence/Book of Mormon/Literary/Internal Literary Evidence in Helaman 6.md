# Internal Literary Evidence in Helaman 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The movement from peaceful prosperity in verses 7-14 to status-seeking, secret crime, and political capture in verses 15-40 is strong internal evidence for a deliberate literary contrast. The narrator's diagnosis focuses not on wealth alone but on setting hearts upon riches and seeking gain in order to rise above others.

**Does not establish:** The sequence does not establish as a historical or universal rule that material prosperity necessarily produces corruption.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may reflect annalistic chronology rather than a tightly designed literary contrast.
- The causal framing may be retrospective theological interpretation by the narrator.
- Status competition, organized crime, and institutional weakness may better explain the collapse than prosperity itself.

**Scripture:** [[Helaman 6#^hel-6-7|Helaman 6:7-17]], [[Helaman 6#^hel-6-31|Helaman 6:31-40]]
