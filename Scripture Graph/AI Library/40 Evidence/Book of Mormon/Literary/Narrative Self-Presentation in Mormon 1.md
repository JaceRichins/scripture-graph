# Narrative Self-Presentation in Mormon 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The first-person seen-and-heard formula, the age notices, and the observe-remember-engrave commission support the narrower conclusion that the text deliberately presents Mormon as an eyewitness recorder trained from childhood.

**Does not establish:** These literary features establish the text's claimed narrator and perspective, not that every reported event occurred or that Mormon has been externally verified as a historical person.

**Alternative explanations:**
- First-person memoir conventions can be used by a fictional or pseudonymous narrator.
- Specific ages and personal details can create verisimilitude without supplying external corroboration.
- The presentation could reflect the choices of a later composer or editor.
