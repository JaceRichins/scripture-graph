# Literary And Thematic Coherence in Alma 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from atonement, to faith and repentance, to prayer, charity, urgency, and endurance provides strong internal evidence that the discourse integrates doctrine with demanded conduct.

**Does not establish:** This coherence does not establish a particular compositional process, date, historical setting, or theory of authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A coherent progression can arise naturally in effective preaching without elaborate literary design.
- Narrative editing or abridgment could have strengthened connections among originally separate themes.
- Readers may perceive a more exact structure than the text requires.
