# Narrative Framing in 3 Nephi 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The command to write the passage and the statement that Jesus expounded it provide strong evidence that the narrative deliberately presents Malachi's words as authoritative and relevant to the assembled people. This literary function can be identified without deciding whether the narrated event is historical.

**Does not establish:** Narrative framing alone does not verify the historicity of the discourse or explain how the wording reached its present English form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The framing reports an actual command and exposition by the risen Jesus.
- The framing is a literary device used by an author to authorize the inserted scripture.
- The framing may serve both historical and literary functions.

**Scripture:** [[3 Nephi 24#^3ne-24-1|3 Nephi 24:1]]
