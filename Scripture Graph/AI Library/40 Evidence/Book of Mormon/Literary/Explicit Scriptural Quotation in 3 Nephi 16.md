# Explicit Scriptural Quotation in 3 Nephi 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrator explicitly introduces Isaiah before reproducing Isaiah 52:8-10 in 3 Nephi 16:18-20. This establishes an intentional scriptural quotation relationship, although it does not by itself establish when the Book of Mormon text was composed or when the prophecy was fulfilled.

**Does not establish:** The quotation alone does not prove ancient authorship, a particular fulfillment date, or a single exhaustive interpretation of Isaiah 52.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The quotation may function as literary reuse without claiming that every detail has already been historically fulfilled.
- Different theories of Book of Mormon composition can all account for the presence of recognizable biblical wording.
