# Historical And Chronological Specificity in 2 Nephi 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The oracle is historically particular rather than generic: it names Damascus, Samaria, Assyria, Judah, Rezin, and Remaliah's son and attaches the anticipated plunder to a child's early development. This strongly supports reading the source passage against a concrete regional crisis, although the chapter alone does not prove when the prediction was composed or fulfilled.

**Does not establish:** Specific details do not independently prove predictive revelation, pre-event composition, fulfillment, or the historicity of the Book of Mormon framing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle could have been recorded before the events as a genuine prediction.
- It could have been written or edited after the relevant events and presented as prediction.
- A real earlier oracle could have received later literary shaping while retaining its historical names.
