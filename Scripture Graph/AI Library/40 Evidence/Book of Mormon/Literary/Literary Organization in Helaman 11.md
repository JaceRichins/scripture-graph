# Literary Organization in Helaman 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated dated transitions and paired language of remembering, forgetting, and being stirred up constitute strong textual evidence that the narrator deliberately frames these years as a recurring moral cycle.

**Does not establish:** This literary pattern does not establish the historical accuracy of every reported event, a supernatural causal mechanism, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The pattern may partly result from compressing complex events into a moralizing historical summary.
- Repeated annalistic date formulas can create a cyclical appearance even without a formally designed literary structure.
