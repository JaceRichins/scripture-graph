# Source Date And Proximity in Joseph Smith—History 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Because the chapter identifies itself as an 1838 history while recounting experiences beginning decades earlier, it is strong primary evidence for Joseph Smith's public account and self-understanding in 1838, but it is not a contemporaneous record of the earliest narrated events.

**Does not establish:** Its first-person status does not independently establish the historical occurrence or supernatural interpretation of the visions it reports.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Later memory, subsequent experiences, and the stated apologetic purpose may have shaped the selection and presentation of earlier events.
- A retrospective account can preserve genuine memories without preserving every date, phrase, or sequence exactly.
