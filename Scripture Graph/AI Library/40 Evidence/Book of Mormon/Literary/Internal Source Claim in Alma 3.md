# Internal Source Claim in Alma 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma 3:12 is evidence that the narrator presents Nephite record keepers as custodians of true information about both Nephites and Lamanites. As self-attestation within the record, however, it is not independent corroboration of that claimed accuracy.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical accuracy or impartiality of the Nephite account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The assertion may function rhetorically to authorize the Nephite version of disputed group history.
- Sincere record keeping does not guarantee neutrality, completeness, or factual accuracy.
- The account preserves the perspective of one side in the conflict rather than independent testimony from both groups.

**Scripture:** [[Alma 3#^alma-3-11|Alma 3:11]], [[Alma 3#^alma-3-12|Alma 3:12]]
