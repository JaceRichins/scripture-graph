# Limits Of Internal Technical Evidence in 1 Nephi 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Although the narrator attributes the ship's design to revelation and calls its workmanship exceedingly fine, the account gives no dimensions, hull form, rigging plan, construction sequence, or sailing specifications. It therefore cannot independently support a technical reconstruction of the vessel.

**Does not establish:** The description does not establish what kind of ship was built, whether it matched or departed from any identifiable historical shipbuilding tradition, or whether it was seaworthy by independently testable criteria.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Technical information may have been omitted because the narrator's purpose was theological rather than instructional.
- Details may have been known to the participants but not preserved in the abridged narrative.
