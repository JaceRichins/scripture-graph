# Internal Literary Correspondence in 1 Nephi 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close fit between this vision's sequence and later Book of Mormon narratives is strong evidence of deliberate internal narrative design and thematic continuity. As historical evidence for a pre-event prophecy, however, the correspondence is limited because prediction and narrated fulfillment occur within the same literary collection.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish the vision's date, supernatural origin, ancient authorship, or the historicity of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single author or editorial tradition could construct later episodes to correspond with the earlier vision.
- The vision could have been composed or revised retrospectively after the events it portrays.
- Both passages could draw on a shared Christian apocalyptic pattern without preserving independent historical prediction.
