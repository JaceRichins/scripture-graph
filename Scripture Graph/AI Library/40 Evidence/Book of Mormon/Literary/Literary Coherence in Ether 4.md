# Literary Coherence in Ether 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence and development of the veil motif across Ether 3–4 is good evidence of literary coherence between the vision narrative and its interpretation: the barrier crossed by the brother of Jared becomes a figure for the unbelief later audiences must overcome.

**Does not establish:** This literary coherence does not establish ancient authorship, the historicity of the vision, or a particular theory of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Veil imagery is common religious language and need not represent a deliberately constructed motif.
- The coherence could result from editorial composition or revision rather than from the historical circumstances portrayed by the narrative.
