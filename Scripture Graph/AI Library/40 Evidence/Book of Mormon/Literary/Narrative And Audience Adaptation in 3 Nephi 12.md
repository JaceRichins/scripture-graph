# Narrative And Audience Adaptation in 3 Nephi 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated additions and substitutions—coming to Christ, filling with the Holy Ghost, persecution for Christ's name, light of this people, senine, and Jesus alongside the Father as perfect—support the claim that the Matthew-like material has been deliberately adapted to this narrative audience and setting.

**Does not establish:** Deliberate adaptation does not identify the adapter, the historical stage at which adaptation occurred, or the antiquity of the underlying material.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The adaptations may represent Jesus tailoring a related sermon to a different audience.
- The adaptations may be translator or editor choices intended to integrate Matthew's sermon with Book of Mormon theology.
- The adaptations may be compositional changes made by a modern author using Matthew 5 as a base text.
