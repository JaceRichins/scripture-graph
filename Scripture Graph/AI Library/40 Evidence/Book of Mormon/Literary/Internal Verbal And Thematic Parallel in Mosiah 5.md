# Internal Verbal And Thematic Parallel in Mosiah 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 5:8 closely reprises the exclusive-saving-name teaching of Mosiah 3:17. This strongly supports reading the covenant scene as a response to and continuation of Benjamin's preceding sermon, although it does not identify the history of composition.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish ancient authorship, a particular source history, or dependence on a specific ancient covenant ceremony.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be ordinary restatement of a central teaching rather than evidence of elaborate literary design.
- Editorial shaping could strengthen the continuity between the sermon and the people's covenant response.
