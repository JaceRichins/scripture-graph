# Literary And Editorial Pattern in 1 Nephi 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated distinction among record types across 1 Nephi, Jacob, and Words of Mormon is strong evidence that the Book of Mormon's final text contains a sustained and self-conscious recordkeeping framework.

**Does not establish:** The framework's coherence does not by itself determine when, where, or by whom the text was composed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The framework could arise from ancient recordkeeping practices.
- The framework could be a coherent feature of modern composition.
- Some repetition may serve to help readers navigate deliberately complex plate terminology.
