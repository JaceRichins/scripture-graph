# Narrative Political Theology in Alma 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma 31:4-5 is strong evidence for the narrative's preference for moral persuasion over coercive force in this crisis. It is not, by itself, empirical proof that preaching is always more effective than military or political action.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish a universal rule against all defensive force or prove the historical effectiveness of this mission independently of the narrative.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The stated comparison may function as theological narration rather than a universal policy prescription.
- The mission also serves a strategic Nephite interest in preventing an alliance with the Lamanites.

**Scripture:** [[Alma 31#^alma-31-4|Alma 31:4]], [[Alma 31#^alma-31-5|Alma 31:5]]
