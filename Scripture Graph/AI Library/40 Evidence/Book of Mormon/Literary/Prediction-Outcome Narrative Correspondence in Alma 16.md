# Prediction-Outcome Narrative Correspondence in Alma 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma 16:6–8 strongly coordinates Alma's specified interception route with the army's narrated movement and encounter, deliberately presenting the rescue as vindication of his prophetic guidance. Because prediction and outcome come through one continuous narrative source, the correspondence supplies only none-band support for genuine pre-event revelation, external historicity, or a particular identification of Sidon and Manti.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
Alma 16:6 specifies crossing Sidon, traveling beyond Manti into the south wilderness, and meeting the Lamanites east of Sidon; verses 7–8 narrate the army following that route, encountering the Lamanites, and recovering the captives.

The ordered geographic repetition deliberately links Alma's direction to the narrated outcome and presents the rescue as vindication of his prophetic role.
