# Prediction-Outcome Narrative Correspondence in Alma 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma's specified interception route in verse 6 is followed closely by the army's route and encounter in verses 7–8. This is strong evidence of deliberate internal narrative correspondence between revelation and outcome.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not independently establish supernatural revelation, external historicity, or a specific real-world identification of Sidon and Manti.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator or editor could have shaped the prediction and outcome into matching accounts.
- The correspondence may function primarily as a conventional literary demonstration of prophetic authority.
- A coherent route within a narrative does not independently verify the proposed geography.
