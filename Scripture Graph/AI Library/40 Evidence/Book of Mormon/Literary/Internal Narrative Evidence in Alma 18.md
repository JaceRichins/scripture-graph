# Internal Narrative Evidence in Alma 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The question-and-answer progression from 'Great Spirit' to God, creation, heaven, and revelation is strong internal evidence that the narrative portrays Ammon as adapting instruction to Lamoni's present vocabulary and understanding.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Lamoni's Great Spirit concept was identical to Ammon's theology or that every missionary setting should use the same instructional sequence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The progression may primarily serve the narrator's exposition for readers rather than preserve a deliberate missionary method.
- Identifying the Great Spirit with God may simplify or replace Lamoni's prior concept rather than merely translate equivalent terminology.
