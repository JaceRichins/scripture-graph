# Literary Typology in 2 Nephi 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit comparison with Moses, followed by shared motifs of speech limitation, writing, deliverance, and a spokesman, is strong internal evidence that the chapter deliberately constructs the future seer as a Moses-like deliverer.

**Does not establish:** The Moses pattern does not establish the prophecy's date, historical transmission, divine origin, or the identity of the later seer.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondences may be conventional reuse of familiar biblical deliverance language rather than a tightly designed type.
- Deliberate typology is compatible with ancient composition, later redaction, or modern composition.
