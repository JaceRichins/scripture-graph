# Narratorial And Geographic Framing in Ether 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The references to the later Nephite destruction site and to a land "called by the Nephites Zarahemla" are strong internal evidence that the surviving account presents Jaredite geography through a later narratorial frame.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish modern locations for Shim, Ablom, Zarahemla, or the Jaredite route, nor does it prove that Jaredite inhabitants used these later names.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some labels could derive from an intermediate editor or source rather than Moroni personally.
- A translation may substitute reader-familiar names for older place-names.

**Scripture:** [[Ether 9#^ether-9-1|Ether 9:1]], [[Ether 9#^ether-9-3|Ether 9:3]], [[Ether 9#^ether-9-31|Ether 9:31]]
