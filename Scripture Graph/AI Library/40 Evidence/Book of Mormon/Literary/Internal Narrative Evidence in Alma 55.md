# Internal Narrative Evidence in Alma 55

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence provides strong internal textual evidence that the narrative evaluates Moroni positively for choosing capture and liberation when he possessed an opportunity to kill incapacitated enemies. This establishes the narrator's ethical framing of this episode, not the historical accuracy of the account or the conformity of every action to modern laws of war.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish historicity, universal pacifism, compliance with modern humanitarian law, or divine approval of every tactic described.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may be idealizing Moroni or selectively reporting events.
- Taking the guards prisoner may also have offered a practical military advantage.
- Avoiding an immediate killing does not resolve the ethics of deception, intoxication, child participation, or subsequent prisoner labor.
