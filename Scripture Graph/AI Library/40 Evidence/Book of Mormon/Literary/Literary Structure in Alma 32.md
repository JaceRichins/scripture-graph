# Literary Structure in Alma 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The ordered recurrence of planting, swelling, sprouting, rooting, nourishing, maturing, and harvesting is strong internal evidence that the seed passage is a deliberately sustained metaphor rather than a collection of unrelated agricultural images.

**Does not establish:** The literary coherence does not establish the discourse's date, mode of composition, historicity, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A sustained metaphor could arise through oral sermonic elaboration rather than prior literary composition.
- The organization could reflect editorial shaping of an earlier discourse.
