# Literary Organization in Mosiah 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence and reversal of warning, liberty, trust, bondage, and deliverance provide moderately strong internal evidence that the chapter is deliberately organized as a theological narrative rather than a mere chronological list of events.

**Does not establish:** Literary cohesion does not establish historicity, ancient authorship, or a particular compositional process.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some coherence may arise naturally from recounting a flight, capture, and eventual deliverance.
- The organization could reflect an editor's shaping rather than the original participants' understanding.
- Deliberate organization is compatible with either ancient or modern composition.
