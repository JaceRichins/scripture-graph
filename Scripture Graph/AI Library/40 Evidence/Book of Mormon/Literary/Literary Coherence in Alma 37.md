# Literary Coherence in Alma 37

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of small means, preservation, guidance, diligence, and salvation across the record instructions and Liahona discourse is meaningful evidence that Alma 37 functions as an integrated didactic composition rather than as a collection of unrelated sayings.

**Does not establish:** This coherence does not establish the text's historical date, ancient provenance, single-stage composition, or divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence could arise from ordinary thematic repetition rather than elaborate literary design.
- An editor could have arranged or reshaped originally separate materials.
- Literary integration is compatible with either ancient or modern composition.
