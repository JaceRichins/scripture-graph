# Internal First-Person Narrative Testimony in Alma 56

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma 56 is strong evidence that its narrator reports the preservation and exceptional fighting of the two thousand as miraculous, but the chapter by itself is limited evidence for establishing supernatural causation outside the narrative because it supplies no independent corroboration or comparative casualty data.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove miraculous protection, guarantee that comparable faith always prevents death, or show that the two thousand escaped all injury.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An unusually favorable but natural survival outcome is possible.
- The report may have undergone selection, compression, exaggeration, or theological shaping.
- The tactical arrival in the Lamanite rear and the renewed attack by Antipus's men may explain part of the outcome.
- The absence of comparative information about wounds, positioning, weapons, or total force sizes limits causal analysis.
