# Internal Narrative Coherence in Jacob 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jacob's distinction between sacred small-plate material and fuller history coheres with earlier explanations of the two records and with the later account of preserving the small plates. This is substantial evidence of internal literary continuity across the Book of Mormon's record narrative.

**Does not establish:** The coherence does not by itself establish multiple ancient plates, historical authenticity, or the identity of the author or translator.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single author could plan and maintain the record system consistently.
- A later editor could harmonize separate passages.
- Internal coherence can arise in either historical or fictional narratives.
