# Internal Literary Structure in 2 Nephi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained sequence from law and punishment through opposition, creation, enticement, the Fall, redemption, and choice is strong internal evidence that these themes function as one integrated argument rather than as unrelated aphorisms.

**Does not establish:** The coherence of the discourse does not by itself establish the truth of its metaphysical premises, intentional formal philosophy, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some transitions may reflect associative preaching rather than a tightly formal philosophical system.
- Later readers may systematize terms that Lehi uses with some flexibility.
