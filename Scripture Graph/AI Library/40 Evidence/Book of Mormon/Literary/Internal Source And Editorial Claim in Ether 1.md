# Internal Source And Editorial Claim in Ether 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides strong internal evidence for the limited claim that its narrator presents the work as a selective abridgment: Moroni identifies twenty-four source plates, attributes the underlying record to Ether, and explicitly says he supplies only part of the account.

**Does not establish:** The internal source description does not independently establish the plates' historical existence, date, authorship, or preservation history.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The archival and editorial framing could function as a literary device rather than describe an actual transmission history.
