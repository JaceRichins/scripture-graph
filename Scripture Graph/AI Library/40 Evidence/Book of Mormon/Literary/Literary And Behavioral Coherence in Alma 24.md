# Literary And Behavioral Coherence in Alma 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from confession of murder, to public weapon burial, to willingness to die supplies strong internal evidence that this chapter portrays repentance as a costly and durable change of conduct rather than merely an emotion.

**Does not establish:** The coherence does not establish the episode's historicity, ancient authorship, or a universal command that all believers renounce every use of force.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A deliberately constructed moral narrative could exhibit the same thematic coherence.
- The account presents group behavior and may not describe every individual's motives or actions.
