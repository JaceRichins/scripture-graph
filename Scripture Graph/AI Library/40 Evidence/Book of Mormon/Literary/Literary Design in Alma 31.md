# Literary Design in Alma 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained oppositions between the Zoramite prayer and Alma's prayer are strong evidence that the chapter has been composed or edited to invite comparison between two models of worship.

**Does not establish:** The literary contrast does not establish the episode's historicity, a specific source language, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The contrast could arise from the events as remembered rather than from elaborate authorial planning.
- A narrator or later editor could have sharpened an underlying historical contrast.
- Some verbal symmetry may reflect English translation or dictation choices.
