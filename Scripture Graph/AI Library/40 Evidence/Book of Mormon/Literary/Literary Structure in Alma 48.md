# Literary Structure in Alma 48

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit transition from Amalickiah's deceptive acquisition of power to Moroni's preparations is strong evidence that the chapter's final form deliberately contrasts their motives and methods.

**Does not establish:** The literary contrast does not establish the narrative's historical accuracy, date of composition, or the precise compositional stage at which the contrast arose.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The contrast could reflect final editorial shaping rather than the structure of an earlier source record.
- Readers may disagree about how sharply the contrast should govern interpretation of every subsequent detail.
