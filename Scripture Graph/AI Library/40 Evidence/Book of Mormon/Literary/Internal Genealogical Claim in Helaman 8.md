# Internal Genealogical Claim in Helaman 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Helaman 8:21 is a key internal Book of Mormon text used to identify Mulek as a surviving son of King Zedekiah and to link the Mulekite/Zarahemla population to the Davidic royal line.

**Does not establish:** Does not independently establish a historical Mulekite migration outside of the Book of Mormon's own testimony; it is uncorroborated by outside sources.

**Alternative explanations:**
- There is no extrabiblical or biblical corroboration that any son of Zedekiah named Mulek survived the fall of Jerusalem; 2 Kings 25 and Jeremiah 39/52 describe Zedekiah's sons being slain without naming a survivor.
- The claim could be a later Nephite tradition rather than a preserved historical fact.
