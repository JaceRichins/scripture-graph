# Narrative Conduct In War in Alma 52

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Moroni's conditional offer and the resulting mass surrender are strong evidence that this episode distinguishes armed resistance from disarmed captivity and portrays surrender as a means of limiting further deaths. The episode is not sufficient evidence for a comprehensive Nephite law of war, especially because the seizure of Mulek includes killing defenders who would not yield their weapons.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish compliance with modern laws of armed conflict, universal Nephite practice, or an unqualified condemnation of wartime killing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Moroni may be acting from immediate tactical advantage rather than from a general humanitarian principle.
- Preserving prisoners may serve exchange, labor, intelligence, or political purposes as well as limiting bloodshed.
- The narrator may selectively summarize conduct and does not provide a complete legal or ethical code.
