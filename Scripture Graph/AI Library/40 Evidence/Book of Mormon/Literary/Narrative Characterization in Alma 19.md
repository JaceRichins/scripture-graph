# Narrative Characterization in Alma 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Abish's name, prior undisclosed conversion, independent interpretation, emotional reaction, and repeated actions provide substantial evidence that she is a developed causal character rather than incidental scenery.

**Does not establish:** The specificity of Abish's portrayal does not by itself establish historicity or prove broader social conclusions about Lamanite women.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A fictional or heavily edited narrative can also create a specific and effective secondary character.
- Her prominence may reflect literary selection rather than the full historical role of women in the society.
