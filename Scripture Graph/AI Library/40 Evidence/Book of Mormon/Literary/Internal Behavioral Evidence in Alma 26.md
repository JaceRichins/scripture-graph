# Internal Behavioral Evidence in Alma 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Ammon's argument, costly love and refusal to kill function as evidence of the converts' sincerity: he points to their love, burial of weapons, and willingness to die rather than take an enemy's life.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historicity of the events, the motives of every convert, or the universal effectiveness of missionary work.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The speech is an interested participant's retrospective account rather than independent testimony.
- Public nonviolence could demonstrate covenant loyalty or group solidarity without proving every individual's inward sincerity.
- The account may select exemplary conduct while omitting less favorable cases.
