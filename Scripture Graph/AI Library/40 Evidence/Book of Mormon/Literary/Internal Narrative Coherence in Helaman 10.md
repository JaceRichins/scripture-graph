# Internal Narrative Coherence in Helaman 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The authorization to smite the earth with famine in Helaman 10:6 is narratively activated in Helaman 11:3-6, where Nephi asks the Lord to replace warfare with famine and the famine follows. This is strong evidence of deliberate internal narrative continuity.

**Does not establish:** Narrative continuity establishes neither the historicity of the famine nor the antiquity of the record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An author or editor can deliberately construct this continuity without the narrated events being historical.
- The famine episode may be a conventional prophetic narrative patterned partly on earlier scripture.
