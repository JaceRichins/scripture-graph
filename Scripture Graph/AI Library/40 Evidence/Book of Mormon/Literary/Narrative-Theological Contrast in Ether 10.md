# Narrative-Theological Contrast in Ether 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Morianton's case is strong internal evidence that Ether 10 does not treat prosperity or popular legitimacy as sufficient proof of divine approval: economic recovery follows his reforms even while the narrator says he is cut off from the Lord's presence.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a universal rule about the relationship between morality, political success, and material prosperity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The juxtaposition may simply report separate political, economic, and moral facts rather than deliberately correct a simplistic prosperity doctrine.
- The narrator may regard prosperity as a communal blessing while judging the ruler separately.
