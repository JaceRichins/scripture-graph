# English Biblical Intertext in 3 Nephi 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The uncommon English expression “breathing out threatenings” in 3 Nephi 5:5 is a direct verbal parallel to Acts 9:1. It is strong evidence of an English-level biblical intertext or biblical register, while its implications for translation method and composition history remain disputed.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel alone does not establish whether the Book of Mormon is ancient or modern, nor does it determine a precise translation mechanism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The translator may have deliberately rendered an underlying idea in familiar King James biblical language.
- A modern author may have consciously or unconsciously borrowed the expression from Acts.
- The phrase may function primarily as conventional scriptural diction without identifying a specific production process.
