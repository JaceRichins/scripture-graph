# English Phraseological Intertext in Helaman 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Helaman 15:1-2 has conspicuous English phraseological parallels with two warnings in Matthew: the declaration of a house left desolate in Matthew 23:38 and the woe concerning pregnant and nursing women during flight in Matthew 24:19. The verbal correspondence is strong, while its explanation remains open.

**Does not establish:** The parallels alone do not establish whether the relationship arose in an ancient source, during translation, through direct modern literary dependence, or through revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A translator may have rendered analogous prophetic ideas in familiar King James biblical diction.
- The wording may be a deliberate literary reuse of Matthew's English phrasing.
- Within a revelatory model, the similarity could reflect a shared prophetic warning pattern expressed through the language of the translation.
- Some correspondence could arise independently from the similar circumstance of flight during destruction, though the combined parallels make pure coincidence less persuasive.
