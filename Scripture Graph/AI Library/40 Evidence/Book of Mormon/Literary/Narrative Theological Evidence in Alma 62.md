# Narrative Theological Evidence in Alma 62

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Alma 62:41 is strong textual evidence that the chapter treats adversity as spiritually non-deterministic: suffering may accompany either hardening or humility. It therefore resists a simple claim that affliction automatically produces righteousness.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that suffering is divinely caused in every case, that suffering is necessary for humility, or that every response to trauma can be reduced to hardening or repentance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verse may describe differing prior dispositions rather than agency exercised during suffering.
- As a theological narrative summary, it does not identify all psychological or social causes of the differing responses.
