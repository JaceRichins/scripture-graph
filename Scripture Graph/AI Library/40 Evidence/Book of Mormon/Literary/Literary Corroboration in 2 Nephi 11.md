# Literary Corroboration in 2 Nephi 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The three-witness arrangement is strong evidence of deliberate literary and theological corroboration within the chapter, but it provides only limited independent historical corroboration because the reader receives all three reported testimonies through Nephi's record.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish that each reported vision occurred or resolve questions about the history and authorship of the record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The three testimonies may be arranged chiefly for rhetorical or theological purposes rather than to satisfy modern standards of independent historical attestation.
- The statement may invoke a familiar scriptural witness formula without claiming that the witnesses provide mutually independent documentary accounts.

**Scripture:** [[2 Nephi 11#^2ne-11-2|2 Nephi 11:2]], [[2 Nephi 11#^2ne-11-3|2 Nephi 11:3]]
