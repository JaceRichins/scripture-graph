# Internal Attribution in 1 Nephi 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Nephi's identification of John supplies an explicit internal explanation for the overlap between his vision and a Johannine apocalypse. Because the identification is made within the Book of Mormon account itself, it is not independent historical corroboration of either authorship claim.

**Does not establish:** The internal attribution does not independently establish that the historical apostle John wrote Revelation or verify the antiquity of Nephi's account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Traditional interpretation identifies the apostle John with John of Patmos and the author of Revelation.
- Many scholars distinguish John of Patmos from John the son of Zebedee.
- A modern-composition model can account for the identification through familiarity with traditional Christian authorship.
