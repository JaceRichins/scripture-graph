# Literary Structure in Enos 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The ordered expansion from Enos's soul to the Nephites and then to the Lamanites is substantial textual evidence that the prayer account has been shaped to portray forgiveness producing widening concern for others.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish a particular date, source language, compositional process, or historical setting.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may reflect the natural chronology of Enos's concerns rather than deliberate literary architecture.
- Later abridgment or editorial shaping could account for the compact progression.

**Scripture:** [[Enos 1#^enos-1-4|Enos 1:4]], [[Enos 1#^enos-1-9|Enos 1:9]], [[Enos 1#^enos-1-11|Enos 1:11-13]]
