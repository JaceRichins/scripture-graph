# Internal Narrative Evidence in Alma 54

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The separate statements by Moroni and Ammoron that an exchange would conserve food for their own forces are strong internal textual evidence that military logistics drive the negotiation alongside ideological and familial concerns.

**Does not establish:** This internal correspondence does not independently establish the historicity of the exchange, the authenticity of the letters, or the actual severity of wartime supply constraints.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallel logistical motives could be a literary construction used to balance the two letters.
- Food conservation may be only one motive and need not be the commanders' dominant concern.
- The narrative supplies both statements, so they are not independent historical corroboration.

**Scripture:** [[Alma 54#^alma-54-2|Alma 54:2]], [[Alma 54#^alma-54-20|Alma 54:20]]
