# Literary And Internal Predictive Sequence in Helaman 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, the sequence of prior announcement, later signs, and explicit recognition of fulfillment supplies evidence for the reliability of the prophets and creates continuity between Helaman 14, Helaman 16, and 3 Nephi 1.

**Does not establish:** The narrative sequence alone does not establish external historicity, supernatural foreknowledge, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A unified author or editor could construct prediction and fulfillment as part of a coherent narrative.
- The account could have been composed or shaped retrospectively after the narrated events.
- Broad predictions and selective reporting can produce an appearance of predictive success.
