# Authorial Purpose And Audience in Mormon 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mormon's repeated 'I write unto you' statements are strong textual evidence that this passage is consciously composed for future audiences and organized around persuasion, witness, repentance, and judgment rather than functioning as a bare military chronicle.

**Does not establish:** Self-conscious address to future readers does not by itself establish ancient authorship, prophetic foresight, or the historicity of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The addresses may reflect a final editor's framing rather than words composed at the time of the reported battles.
- A consciously theological literary purpose does not determine whether the underlying historical narrative is accurate.
