# Narrative And Thematic Context in 1 Nephi 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's opening address to scattered Israel, its return imagery, and Nephi's immediate exposition provide strong evidence that the quotation functions in 1 Nephi as a prophecy of covenant scattering and gathering.

**Does not establish:** This framing does not identify one exclusive fulfillment, a complete timetable, or every group represented by Israel and the Gentiles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The gathering language can encompass spiritual reconciliation, communal renewal, and physical return rather than only one geopolitical event.
- Later readers may legitimately reapply the imagery without exhausting its earlier historical settings.
