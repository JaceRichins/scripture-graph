# Internal Literary Evidence in 1 Nephi 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides strong internal evidence that its narrator is portrayed as a self-conscious editor: he identifies a source he will not duplicate, explains a material constraint, states a theological objective, and prescribes a policy for for later custodians.

**Does not establish:** This internal coherence does not independently establish the plates' physical existence, the narrative's antiquity, or the historicity of Nephi.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A modern author could construct the same editorial persona and internal source distinctions.
- Statements about limited plate space may function literarily even if they do not describe an actual artifact.
