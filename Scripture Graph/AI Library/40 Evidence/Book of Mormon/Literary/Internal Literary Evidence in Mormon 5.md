# Internal Literary Evidence in Mormon 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verses 8–14 provide strong internal evidence that the chapter presents itself as consciously shaped sacred history: the narrator identifies selection, audience, preservation, divine command, and theological purpose.

**Does not establish:** This internal record-making framework does not independently prove the historicity, antiquity, or divine origin of the record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A fictional or pseudonymous narrator can also be written with explicit editorial self-consciousness.
- The editorial explanation may reflect more than one stage of composition or translation.
