# Literary Pattern in Alma 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The passage develops a sustained garment pattern: Moroni rends his coat, the people rend theirs, the garments symbolize a covenant curse, and Moroni interprets the action through Joseph's rent coat and a preserved remnant. The recurrence strongly supports deliberate literary shaping of this episode, while the source or language level at which the pattern arose remains uncertain.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish Semitic composition, ancient authorship, or the historicity of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The pattern could reflect Mormon's editorial arrangement of inherited material.
- Some verbal cohesion may arise from the English translation.
- A modern composer could also create a sustained symbolic pattern.
