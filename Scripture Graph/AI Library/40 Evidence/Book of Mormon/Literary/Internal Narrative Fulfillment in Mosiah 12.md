# Internal Narrative Fulfillment in Mosiah 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Later portions of Mosiah narratively correspond to two prominent warnings in this chapter: Noah is put to death by fire, and his people are subjected to heavy burdens. Mosiah 21 explicitly presents the oppression as fulfillment of the Lord's word. These are strong internal narrative correspondences.

**Does not establish:** Internal correspondence does not independently prove supernatural prediction, the external historicity of the events, or the date at which the prophecy was composed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A unified author or redactor could deliberately compose later events to correspond with earlier predictions.
- The predictions could have been recorded or shaped retrospectively after the narrated events.
- Bondage and violent royal death are narratively plausible outcomes of the political conflict without requiring supernatural foreknowledge.
