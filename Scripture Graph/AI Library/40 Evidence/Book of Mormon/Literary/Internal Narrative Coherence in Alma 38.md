# Internal Narrative Coherence in Alma 38

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Shiblon's remembered Zoramite service, persecution, and exposure to ostentatious prayer create strong internal narrative continuity between Alma 31 and Alma 38. This supports the literary coherence of the record but is only modest evidence regarding the historical origin of the narrative.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish ancient authorship, the historicity of Shiblon's mission, or supernatural inspiration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A skilled modern author could deliberately maintain continuity between related episodes.
- Editorial revision could harmonize later counsel with the earlier Zoramite narrative.
- Literary coherence can arise within either historical narrative or composed fiction.
