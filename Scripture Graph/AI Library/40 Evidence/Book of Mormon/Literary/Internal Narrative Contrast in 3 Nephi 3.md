# Internal Narrative Contrast in 3 Nephi 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The letter and the narrator provide sharply opposed accounts of the robbers' grievances: Giddianhi claims that dissenters were deprived of governmental rights, while the narrator says they had received no wrong except that caused by their own dissent. This is strong evidence for an intentional clash of moral-political narratives within the chapter, but the text supplies no independent adjudication beyond its narrator's judgment.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish whether the dissenters possessed any historically legitimate grievance or whether either account is politically complete.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The contrast may function chiefly as moral characterization rather than as a balanced record of an actual political dispute.
- Giddianhi's grievance language may be propaganda, while the narrator's rebuttal also reflects the narrator's partisan perspective.

**Scripture:** [[3 Nephi 3#^3ne-3-10|3 Nephi 3:10]], [[3 Nephi 3#^3ne-3-11|3 Nephi 3:11]]
