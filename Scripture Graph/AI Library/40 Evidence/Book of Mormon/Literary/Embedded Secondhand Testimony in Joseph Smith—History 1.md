# Embedded Secondhand Testimony in Joseph Smith—History 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Anthon episode is explicitly embedded secondhand testimony: Joseph introduces it as Martin Harris's later account, and Harris's speech supplies Anthon's alleged statements and actions. The chapter therefore directly establishes what Joseph says Harris reported, not by itself what Anthon actually said.

**Does not establish:** Without independent evidence, this passage does not establish Anthon's exact words, his evaluation of the characters, or the accuracy of Harris's interpretation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Harris may have accurately remembered and reported the conversation.
- Harris may have misunderstood, compressed, or reshaped Anthon's comments.
- Joseph's later written presentation may not reproduce Harris's exact language.
