# Literary Coherence in Moses 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The book of remembrance introduced near the beginning returns as the stated basis for Enoch's knowledge of Adam. This recurrence gives the chapter a coherent explanation for how primordial teachings were transmitted.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence does not independently authenticate an ancient book of remembrance or determine who composed the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence may simply repeat a traditional record motif.
- A later author or editor could have coordinated the passages.
- Internal narrative coherence does not demonstrate that the described record existed historically.
