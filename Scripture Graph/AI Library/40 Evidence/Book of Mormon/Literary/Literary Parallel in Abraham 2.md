# Literary Parallel in Abraham 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Abraham 2 and Genesis 12 share a closely ordered narrative sequence: a command to leave, migration toward Canaan, a land promise, altar building near Bethel, famine-driven travel to Egypt, and the sister strategy concerning Sarai. This is strong evidence of literary relationship or shared narrative tradition, but it does not by itself determine the direction, date, or mechanism of dependence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish ancient composition, independent antiquity, historical accuracy, or a particular theory of translation or revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Abraham 2 may directly adapt the Genesis narrative.
- Both texts may derive from a shared earlier tradition.
- The relationship may include expansion, harmonization, or later editorial shaping rather than simple one-directional copying.
