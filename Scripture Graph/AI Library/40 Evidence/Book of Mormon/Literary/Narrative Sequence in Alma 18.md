# Narrative Sequence in Alma 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter's narrative, Lamoni's movement from inherited assumptions through fear, questioning, belief, and prayer supports reading his conversion as a process rather than a single unprepared reaction.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the depth, permanence, or completeness of Lamoni's conversion within this chapter alone; the subsequent narrative in Alma 19 is needed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may compress a more complicated experience into a didactic sequence.
- Fear of punishment and astonishment at Ammon's power may initially motivate Lamoni as much as theological conviction does.
