# Internal Literary Evidence in Mosiah 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed sequence in verses 5-10 is strong internal evidence that the passage was deliberately constructed as a coherent prophecy of Jesus Christ. It is weak as independent evidence for the prophecy's historical antiquity because the text alone cannot adjudicate between supernatural foreknowledge, use of earlier Christian traditions, or composition informed by the New Testament.

**Does not establish:** The passage's detail does not independently establish supernatural prediction, ancient authorship, or the historical occurrence of the angelic visitation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Believers may understand the details as revelation of future events.
- The passage may draw upon Christian traditions or scriptural language already known to its composer or translator.
- The narrative setting and the historical date of the text's composition may be evaluated differently.
