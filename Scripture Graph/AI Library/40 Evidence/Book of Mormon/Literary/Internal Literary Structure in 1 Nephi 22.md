# Internal Literary Structure in 1 Nephi 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of "according to the flesh" near both the opening answer and the conclusion provides strong internal literary evidence that verses 2-27 form a sustained response to the brothers' question in verse 1.

**Does not establish:** This literary coherence does not establish a particular date, authorial history, or ancient provenance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be ordinary thematic restatement rather than a deliberately designed inclusio.
- The framing could reflect later editorial organization rather than the initial composition of every intervening unit.
