# Internal Compositional Statement in 2 Nephi 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
2 Nephi 11:1 presents Nephi as selecting only part of Jacob's teaching because that portion serves his purpose. The notice illuminates the chapter's transition from Jacob's discourse to Nephi's extended selection from Isaiah: the narrator presents these materials as deliberately chosen witnesses rather than an exhaustive transcript.
<!-- SG:END summary -->

## Observation
<!-- SG:BEGIN observation -->
In 2 Nephi 11:1, the first-person narrator says that Jacob spoke more than the record contains and that only part of the material suffices for his present purpose.
<!-- SG:END observation -->

## Interpretation
<!-- SG:BEGIN interpretation -->
The verse functions as an editorial aside explaining that the ensuing record is selective rather than exhaustive.
<!-- SG:END interpretation -->

## Historical Significance
<!-- SG:BEGIN historical-significance -->
Selective-record notices are ordinary features of historical, religious, and literary narration. Here the notice establishes how the final text presents its narrator's work: Jacob's discourse is represented as longer than the excerpt Nephi chooses to preserve.
<!-- SG:END historical-significance -->
## How This Fits
<!-- SG:BEGIN how-it-fits -->
The chapter immediately joins Jacob's testimony to Nephi's testimony and then introduces substantial Isaiah material. On the proposed literary reconstruction, the selection notice explains this arrangement: Nephi retains the part of Jacob's teaching that advances his project of assembling mutually reinforcing witnesses concerning Christ and Israel.
<!-- SG:END how-it-fits -->
