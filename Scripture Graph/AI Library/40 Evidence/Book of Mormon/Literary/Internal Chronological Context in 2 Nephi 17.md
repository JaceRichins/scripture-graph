# Internal Chronological Context in 2 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The child's development supplies a deadline for the abandonment of the lands ruled by the two hostile kings. This is strong internal evidence that the Immanuel oracle had meaningful relevance to Ahaz's contemporary crisis, even if the passage can also receive a later messianic application.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the child's historical identity, exclude a Christological fulfillment, or determine exactly how the near and later horizons relate.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The child's development may function symbolically rather than as a precise chronological measurement.
- The prophecy may intentionally combine an immediate sign with a later or fuller fulfillment.
- Interpreters disagree about whether Immanuel is a contemporary child, a royal child, the Messiah, or a sign operating on more than one horizon.
