# Internal Narrative Evidence in Mormon 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The account adopts a strongly situated survivor perspective: Mormon reports being wounded and passed over, names Moroni among the twenty-four survivors, and describes viewing the dead from Cumorah on the following day. This is strong evidence for deliberate eyewitness-style narration but only weak evidence by itself for the account's external historicity.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish that the battle occurred or that every reported detail is historically exact.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A literary narrative can simulate an eyewitness viewpoint.
- The details may reflect retrospective narrative shaping.
- Internal specificity cannot independently verify an event.
