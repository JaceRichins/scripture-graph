# Canonical Doctrinal Statement in Abraham 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Abraham 3:18–23 is direct and unusually explicit textual evidence for premortal existence within Latter-day Saint scripture: spirits are said to have existed before, and Abraham is said to have been chosen before birth.

**Does not establish:** The passage establishes what this canonical text teaches; by itself it does not independently prove premortal existence as a historical or metaphysical fact.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Readers outside the Latter-day Saint tradition may treat the passage as a nineteenth-century theological text rather than as revealed history.
- Some readers may distinguish uncreated intelligence from an organized premortal spirit, although this chapter does not explain that distinction precisely.
