# Literary Coherence in 1 Nephi 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained question-and-answer interpretation of Lehi's dream is strong evidence that 1 Nephi 15 is literarily integrated with the earlier dream narrative: multiple symbols are revisited in sequence and incorporated into a single teaching about revelation, discipleship, judgment, and the tree of life.

**Does not establish:** Literary integration does not by itself establish ancient authorship, historicity, or supernatural origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single modern author could deliberately construct the correspondences.
- A later editor or narrator could integrate separate materials retrospectively.
- Recurring religious symbols can produce coherence without requiring a particular historical provenance.
