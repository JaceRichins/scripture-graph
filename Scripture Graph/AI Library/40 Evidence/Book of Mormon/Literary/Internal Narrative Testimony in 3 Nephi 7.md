# Internal Narrative Testimony in 3 Nephi 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter constructs an internal chain of testimony: Nephi is called an eye-witness, the people see and witness miracles, converts publicly signify their experience of divine power, healed people manifest what occurred, and baptism serves as a witness and testimony.

**Does not establish:** Internal witness language does not independently establish the historicity or supernatural cause of the reported miracles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The witness language may be a literary or theological strategy rather than an independently corroborated report.
- Several reported witnesses appear only through the narrator's account and are not separately identifiable.
- Public reaction can function narratively without demonstrating that the reported events occurred historically.
