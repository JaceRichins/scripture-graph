# Internal Prediction-Fulfillment in Helaman 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed correspondences between Samuel's signs and the later narratives provide strong evidence that the Book of Mormon's present literary form intentionally links prophecy and fulfillment.

**Does not establish:** The internal correspondence does not by itself establish that the events occurred historically, that the prediction preceded the events independently, or that the prophecy had supernatural origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single author or editor could deliberately compose the fulfillment narrative to correspond with the prophecy.
- The prophecy and fulfillment could derive from a shared literary or religious tradition.
- Later editorial shaping could have strengthened correspondences between earlier and later accounts.
