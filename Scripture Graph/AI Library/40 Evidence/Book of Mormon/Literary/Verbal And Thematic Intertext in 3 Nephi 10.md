# Verbal And Thematic Intertext in 3 Nephi 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The lament over Jerusalem and the image of gathering children as a hen gathers her chickens closely parallel Matthew 23:37. The relationship is especially clear in the shared combination of Jerusalem, repeated desire to gather, the hen image, and human refusal; 3 Nephi expands the saying into past, counterfactual, and future gathering statements.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not determine literary direction, translation mechanics, historical setting, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Book of Mormon wording may depend directly on the King James rendering of Matthew.
- Both texts may preserve or represent the same saying of Jesus.
- A revelatory translation model may account for familiar biblical wording without requiring an ancient English source.
