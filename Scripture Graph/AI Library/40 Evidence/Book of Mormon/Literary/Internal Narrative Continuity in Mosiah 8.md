# Internal Narrative Continuity in Mosiah 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 8 raises the question of the identity and destruction of the people represented by the twenty-four plates; Mosiah 28 later reports their translation, and Ether 1 identifies its account with the twenty-four plates found by Limhi's people. This is strong evidence of internal narrative integration across the present Book of Mormon text.

**Does not establish:** This internal continuity does not by itself establish the plates' historicity, an ancient date of composition, or the absence of later editorial shaping.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single modern author could create the same question-and-answer continuity.
- An editor could connect originally separate narrative materials.
- Retrospective composition could introduce the earlier reference after the later account was conceived.
