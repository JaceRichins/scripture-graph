# Narrative Parallelism in Alma 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The corresponding conversion experiences of Lamoni and his father provide strong evidence of intentional literary parallelism within the narrative, but only weak independent evidence for the historicity or supernatural cause of the reported events.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish historical independence, the medical nature of the collapses, or divine causation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be a deliberate literary pattern rather than a record of two independently corroborating events.
- Similar physical reactions could be conventional features of conversion narratives.
- The text does not supply an external witness or independent source for either episode.
