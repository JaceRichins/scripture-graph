# Extended Literary Dependence in 1 Nephi 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained correspondence with Isaiah 48 strongly supports the conclusion that this chapter deliberately incorporates and applies an Isaianic oracle. The correspondence alone cannot determine the historical mechanism by which the wording entered the Book of Mormon.

**Does not establish:** Literary dependence on Isaiah 48 does not by itself adjudicate between ancient-record, revelatory-translation, and modern-composition models.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A historical Nephi could have copied and interpreted an Isaianic text available on the brass plates.
- A modern production model could account for the wording through direct dependence on the King James Bible.
- A translation model could render an ancient quotation in familiar King James-style English.

**Scripture:** [[1 Nephi 20#^1ne-20-1|1 Nephi 20:1-22]], [[Isaiah 48#^isa-48-1|Isaiah 48:1-22]]
