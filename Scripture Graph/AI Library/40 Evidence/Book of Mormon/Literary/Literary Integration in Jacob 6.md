# Literary Integration in Jacob 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained reuse of vineyard, nourishment, fruit, pruning, casting out, and burning makes Jacob 6 a tightly integrated interpretive conclusion to Jacob 5. This is strong evidence of deliberate literary continuity between the chapters.

**Does not establish:** The literary integration does not establish the historicity of Zenos, the antiquity of the allegory, or a particular authorship theory.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later editor or translator could have supplied the transition and repeated vocabulary.
- Continuity demonstrates composition and arrangement but does not identify when or by whom either chapter was composed.
