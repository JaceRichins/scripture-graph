# Historical Corroboration in Joseph Smith—Matthew 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Roman destruction of the Second Temple in Jerusalem in AD 70 is a well-attested historical event, corroborated by independent ancient historical sources, consistent with the temple-destruction prophecy in vv.2-3 and v.12.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish supernatural foreknowledge on Jesus's part; it establishes only that the predicted event (the temple's physical destruction) is historically attested. The direction of composition (prophecy-before-event vs. narrative-after-event) is a separate, contested question.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Critical NT scholarship often reads the specificity of Matthew 24's temple-destruction language as evidence the Gospel reached final form after AD 70 (vaticinium ex eventu), rather than as demonstrating advance predictive knowledge.
