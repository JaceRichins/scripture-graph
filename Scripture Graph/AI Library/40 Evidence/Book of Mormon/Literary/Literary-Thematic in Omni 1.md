# Literary-Thematic in Omni 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated association among records, genealogy, language, religious memory, interpretation, and political continuity provides moderately strong internal evidence that record preservation is a governing literary theme of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historicity of the migrations, a universal causal law about literacy and belief, or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence may arise naturally from a chapter summarizing custody of plates and encounters with other peoples.
- Some thematic coherence may reflect later editorial arrangement rather than each narrator's intention.
- The text does not prove that absence of records caused every social or religious problem attributed to Zarahemla's people.
