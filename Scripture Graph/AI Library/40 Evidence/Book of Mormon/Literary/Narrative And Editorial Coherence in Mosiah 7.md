# Narrative And Editorial Coherence in Mosiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mosiah 7 functions coherently as a narrative frame for the ensuing retrospective history: its compressed references to Zeniff's settlement, Lamanite deception, the half tribute, prophetic preaching, and the prophet's death correspond to episodes narrated in Mosiah 9–17 and Mosiah 19.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence supports deliberate literary organization but does not establish ancient authorship, historicity, or a particular model of textual composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single modern author could construct the frame and backstory coherently.
- An editor could harmonize previously separate narrative materials.
- Final-form coherence does not by itself reveal when or by whom the material was composed.
