# Internal Literary Coherence in Alma 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed correspondence between Alma 25's retrospective explanation and Abinadi's predictions in Mosiah 17 is strong evidence of purposeful internal narrative integration across the Book of Mormon account.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not independently establish that Abinadi made a historical prediction, that the reported fulfillment occurred, or that the record has ancient provenance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single later author could construct both prediction and fulfillment as parts of one planned narrative.
- An editor could shape the later account to echo an inherited earlier passage.
- The retrospective description could have been formulated from the wording of the earlier prophecy rather than from independent historical memory.
