# Literary Coherence in Alma 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated vocabulary concerning wish, desire, allotment, calling, glory, remembrance, and joy produces a coherent progression from imagined universal influence to contentment in a particular calling and shared joy in others' success. This supports purposeful literary organization.

**Does not establish:** Purposeful organization does not establish ancient authorship, historicity, or a single stage of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A coherent progression can arise in skilled oral or extemporaneous discourse.
- The pattern could reflect modern composition.
- Editorial selection or revision could account for some of the coherence.
