# Internal Rhetoric And Group Identity in 2 Nephi 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verses 21-24 are strong textual evidence that this account constructs separation from the Lamanites through moralized physical, marital, and social contrasts. They are not evidence that modern racial hierarchies are divinely authorized, nor do they independently verify the narrator's generalizations about the opposing population.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the nature, mechanism, permanence, or historical appearance of the reported change, and it does not justify applying the passage to modern racial categories.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The skin language may describe a literal physical change.
- The language may function symbolically or metaphorically within the narrative.
- The passage may preserve ancient boundary-making rhetoric without providing a neutral ethnographic description.
- The hostile characterizations may reflect Nephite-Lamanite conflict and the narrator's partisan perspective.
