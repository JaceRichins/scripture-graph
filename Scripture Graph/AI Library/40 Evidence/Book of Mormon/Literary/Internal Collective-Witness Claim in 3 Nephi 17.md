# Internal Collective-Witness Claim in 3 Nephi 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As an internal evidentiary feature, the narrative's specification of approximately 2,500 men, women, and children who individually saw and heard is a strong textual claim to public rather than private revelation. Its value as independent historical corroboration is limited because the witnesses are reported within the same narrative rather than preserved in separately identifiable accounts.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not independently establish that the event occurred historically or that 2,500 separate testimonies were collected.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The witness language may function as literary authentication rather than preserve independently recoverable testimony.
- The large and inclusive group may express the communal scope of revelation through rhetorical universalization.
