# Internal Literary Structure in Abraham 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated comparative pattern—two unequal entities followed by another above them—moves from planets and stars to spirits and intelligence. This is strong internal evidence that verses 16–19 form a deliberate literary hinge between the astronomical vision and the premortal scene.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish the chapter's date, authorship, original language, historicity, or the scientific accuracy of its cosmology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be primarily pedagogical rather than evidence of a formally designed literary structure.
- The celestial comparison may illustrate only one feature of spirits—relative intelligence—rather than a comprehensive analogy between both orders.
