# Internal Narrative Coherence in Mosiah 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The angel's appeal to the earlier bondage narratives supplies strong evidence of deliberate internal narrative coherence: past communal deliverance becomes the interpretive model for Alma's personal deliverance.

**Does not establish:** Internal coherence does not independently establish historical authenticity or ancient authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A modern author or editor could also create coherent cross-references to earlier episodes.
- The relationship may be primarily theological and literary rather than evidence about historical events.
