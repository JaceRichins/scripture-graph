# Narratorial Causal Interpretation in 3 Nephi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit connection between communal iniquity, internal dissension, and military decline is strong evidence for the narrator's theological interpretation of history. It does not demonstrate that every military defeat is divine punishment; internal factionalism also has an ordinary strategic capacity to weaken a society.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a universal rule that suffering or military defeat proves the sufferers' moral guilt.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Internal contention may have weakened coordination, defense, and public trust through ordinary political and military mechanisms.
- The editor may impose a theological pattern on events whose full historical causes were more complex.

**Scripture:** [[3 Nephi 2#^3ne-2-18|3 Nephi 2:18]], [[3 Nephi 2#^3ne-2-19|3 Nephi 2:19]]
