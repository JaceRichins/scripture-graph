# Internal Geographic Description in Ether 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrow-neck passage provides a meaningful constraint for reconstructing the narrative's internal geography: it places a city near a constricted land passage associated with divided seas and distinguishes northern settlement from a southern game reserve.

**Does not establish:** It does not identify a modern location for Jaredite lands or prove any proposed Book of Mormon geography.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The scale and orientation of the geographic terms are not specified.
- Several real-world locations could satisfy a general narrow-land description.
- The language may be schematic rather than cartographically precise.
