# Literary And Narrative Evidence in Mormon 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close juxtaposition of theological judgment with concrete battlefield causes is strong evidence that Mormon 4 is written as theological historiography: its narrator selects and interprets military events to teach moral meaning while retaining practical explanations for outcomes.

**Does not establish:** This literary pattern does not independently establish that the narrator's claim of divine judgment is historically or metaphysically correct.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A reader may regard the theological comments as later editorial interpretation rather than the meaning inherent in the military events.
- The practical details can be read simply as narrative realism without implying a deliberate synthesis of causal levels.
