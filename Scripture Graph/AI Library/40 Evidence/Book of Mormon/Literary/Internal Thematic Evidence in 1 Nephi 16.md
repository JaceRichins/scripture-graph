# Internal Thematic Evidence in 1 Nephi 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Nephi's theological presentation, the pointer and changing writing provide strong internal evidence for a model of revelation that requires continuing faith, diligence, and attention rather than passive possession of a sacred object.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the object's historical existence, physical mechanism, or the general rule that every failure to receive guidance results from deficient faith.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The conditions may describe the users' ability to benefit from the object rather than a change in its physical operation.
- The account may be primarily didactic, with the artifact serving the narrator's theology of responsive guidance.
