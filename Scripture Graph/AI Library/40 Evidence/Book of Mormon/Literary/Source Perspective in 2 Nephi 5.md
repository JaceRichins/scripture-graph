# Source Perspective in 2 Nephi 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter is a first-person account by Nephi. This provides direct textual evidence for how Nephi understood and represented the separation, but it supplies no independent voice from the brothers or the people later called Lamanites.

**Does not establish:** First-person narration does not establish that every motive, divine explanation, or group characterization in the account is independently corroborated.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A first-person narrator may report events accurately despite being an interested participant.
- Later editorial transmission may also shape the form in which the account is received.
