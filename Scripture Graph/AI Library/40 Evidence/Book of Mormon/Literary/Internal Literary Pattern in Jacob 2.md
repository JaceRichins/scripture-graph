# Internal Literary Pattern in Jacob 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated imagery of wounded, pierced, broken, and dying hearts links the opening description of the sermon audience with the concluding account of injured wives and children. This provides substantial internal evidence that the chapter's ethical focus includes the damage wrongdoing and its public exposure inflict upon vulnerable hearers.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish ancient authorship, a specific formal literary genre, or that every recurrence was consciously arranged.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence may reflect Jacob's habitual emotional vocabulary rather than a deliberately constructed literary frame.
- The imagery may chiefly emphasize the force of God's word, with audience vulnerability as a secondary concern.
