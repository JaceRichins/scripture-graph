# Hebrew Literary Wordplay in Amos 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The vision depends on a Hebrew sound-play between qayits, meaning summer fruit, and qets, meaning end. The verbal resemblance connects the ordinary object Amos sees with the judgment the Lord announces, strengthening the literary coherence of the vision.

**Does not establish:** The wordplay does not establish a precise date, supernatural prediction, fulfillment of the judgment, or sole authorship of the final form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The similar words could have suggested the vision's literary formulation without determining when or how the underlying experience occurred.
- The wordplay demonstrates careful Hebrew composition but does not by itself identify whether Amos, a disciple, or an editor fixed the final wording.
