# Textual And Translation Uncertainty in Malachi 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In the supplied KJV text, the LORD says that he hates 'putting away' and associates the offense with violence. The underlying Hebrew of verse 16 has difficult syntax and uncertain pronoun reference, producing materially different translations; therefore the verse strongly condemns marital treachery and violence in context, but its exact formulation about divorce should not be built upon the KJV wording alone.

**Does not establish:** The translation difficulty does not make marital treachery morally neutral, establish that the KJV is wholly mistaken, or settle every ethical question concerning divorce.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The traditional reading may substantially preserve the intended sense despite the difficult syntax.
- Some translations construe the subject as the divorcing husband rather than God, yielding a warning about a man who hates and divorces his wife.
