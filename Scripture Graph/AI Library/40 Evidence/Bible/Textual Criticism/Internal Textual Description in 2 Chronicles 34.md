# Internal Textual Description in 2 Chronicles 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The converging designations strongly support the conclusion that the narrative presents the discovered book as authoritative Mosaic covenant law. They provide much weaker evidence for identifying its precise contents, textual edition, date, or physical extent.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the object was the complete Pentateuch, all of Deuteronomy, a particular edition of Deuteronomy, or a newly composed text.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The several expressions may be overlapping general descriptions rather than titles identifying one known canonical work.
- The narrator may describe the book by its claimed authority and function without cataloguing all of its contents.
- The discovered object could have contained part of a larger body of Mosaic law rather than a complete later canonical book.
