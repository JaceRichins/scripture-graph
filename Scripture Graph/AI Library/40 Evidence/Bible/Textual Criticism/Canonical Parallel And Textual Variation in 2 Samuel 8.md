# Canonical Parallel And Textual Variation in 2 Samuel 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The received parallel accounts are not numerically or narratively identical: 2 Samuel 8:4 reports seven hundred horsemen while 1 Chronicles 18:4 reports seven thousand, and 2 Samuel 8:13 associates eighteen thousand Syrians with the Valley of Salt while 1 Chronicles 18:12 attributes the killing of eighteen thousand Edomites to Abishai. These differences are significant evidence of complexity in the textual transmission or source presentation.

**Does not establish:** The variants alone do not identify the original wording, prove deliberate alteration, or determine whether the underlying campaigns occurred.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The horsemen figures may reflect a copyist's numerical error or different counting conventions.
- Samuel and Chronicles may preserve different source traditions or combine related episodes differently.
- The Syrian-Edomite difference may be a transmission problem, while the surrounding reference to Edom in both accounts may preserve the intended setting.
- Abishai may have commanded forces operating under David, permitting different levels of attribution without requiring wholly different events.
