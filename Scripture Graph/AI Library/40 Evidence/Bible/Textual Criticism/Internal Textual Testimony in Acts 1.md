# Internal Textual Testimony in Acts 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 1:3 is explicit narrative testimony that Jesus appeared alive after his suffering. It is strong evidence for what the author and represented community proclaimed, but as an internal narrative assertion it is not independently sufficient to establish every reported appearance historically.

**Does not establish:** By itself, the verse does not establish the historical details, number, or nature of the appearances independently of the narrative's testimony.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statement may summarize inherited Christian testimony rather than the author's direct observation.
- The phrase translated "infallible proofs" expresses the narrator's evaluation and is not itself independent verification.
