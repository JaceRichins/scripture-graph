# Textual Interrelationship in Micah 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Micah 4:1–3, Isaiah 2:2–4, and 2 Nephi 12:2–4 share a sustained textual parallel. This is strong evidence of a literary relationship or shared prophetic tradition, but the parallel alone cannot determine the direction of dependence or the history of composition.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish which passage came first, whether either named prophet composed the shared oracle, or a precise date for its composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Micah may preserve wording derived from Isaiah.
- Isaiah may preserve wording derived from Micah.
- Both books may draw on a shared earlier oracle or liturgical tradition.
- Later transmission or editorial activity may account for the shared wording.
