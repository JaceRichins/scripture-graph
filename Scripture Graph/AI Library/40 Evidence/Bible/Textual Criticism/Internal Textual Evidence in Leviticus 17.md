# Internal Textual Evidence in Leviticus 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated linkage of blood with life, divine gift, altar, and atonement is strong internal evidence that the blood prohibition belongs to the chapter's sacrificial theology and is not presented merely as an unexplained dietary restriction.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a modern physiological theory of life, an exhaustive doctrine of atonement, or the historical origin of every biblical blood prohibition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prohibition may simultaneously express reverence for animal life, ritual boundary maintenance, and dietary identity.
- The chapter may combine rationales or traditions that had distinct earlier histories.
