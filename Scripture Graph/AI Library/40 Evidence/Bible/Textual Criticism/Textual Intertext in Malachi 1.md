# Textual Intertext in Malachi 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Malachi 1:11 and Psalm 113:3 share the east-to-west formula associated with the greatness or praise of the LORD's name. The overlap is good evidence of a common biblical idiom of universal praise, but only limited evidence that either passage directly depends on the other.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not establish the direction of dependence, a shared author, or a precise date for either passage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently use a conventional east-to-west expression.
- Both may draw upon shared liturgical language without direct literary borrowing.
- A later editorial or translation tradition could have increased the English verbal resemblance.
