# Intertextual New Testament Reception in Isaiah 45

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 45:23's oath — "unto me every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear" — is directly quoted by Paul in Romans 14:11 ("every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall confess to God") and echoed in Philippians 2:10-11, where the same universal-submission language originally spoken by/about the LORD (YHWH) is applied to Jesus Christ.

**Does not establish:** This does not by itself prove a doctrine of the Trinity or full identity of Jesus with Yahweh in the mind of Isaiah's original audience; it documents how later apostolic writers read and applied the text.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be applying general Yahweh-language typologically to Christ as part of broader early Christian practice of transferring OT God-language to Jesus, rather than making a precise one-to-one identity claim in every instance.
- The Philippians 2 allusion is verbally looser than the Romans 14 quotation and could reflect independent liturgical/hymnic language rather than a direct citation of Isaiah 45 specifically.
