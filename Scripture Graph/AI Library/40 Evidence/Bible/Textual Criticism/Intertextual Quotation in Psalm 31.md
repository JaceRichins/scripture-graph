# Intertextual Quotation in Psalm 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Luke 23:46 has Jesus, dying on the cross, say 'Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit' - a close paraphrase of Psalm 31:5, making this one of the clearest instances of a Gospel passion narrative directly echoing the wording of a lament psalm.

**Does not establish:** That the human author of Psalm 31 intended the line as messianic prophecy; the connection is demonstrated by New Testament usage, not by evidence of authorial intent within the psalm itself.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The overlap could reflect a common Jewish deathbed/commendation-of-spirit prayer formula rather than a deliberate citation of this specific psalm.
- Luke may be drawing on an independently attested liturgical tradition of committing one's spirit to God rather than quoting Psalm 31 as text.
