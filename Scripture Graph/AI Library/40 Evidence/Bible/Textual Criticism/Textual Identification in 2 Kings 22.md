# Textual Identification in 2 Kings 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The designations 'book of the law,' the claim that its words concern Judah, and the prophetic confirmation of its announced judgment provide strong internal evidence that the narrative presents the book as an authoritative covenantal legal text. The chapter itself does not identify its precise contents, edition, date, or modern canonical boundaries.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the object was identical to the complete modern Pentateuch, nor does it settle when the document was composed or deposited.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The expression may designate Deuteronomy or an earlier form of part of Deuteronomy.
- It may refer to a larger Torah collection or another covenantal document.
- The account may reflect later historiographic presentation of the book and its authority.
