# Intratextual Prediction-Fulfillment in 2 Kings 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated number three provides strong internal literary evidence that the narrator presents Joash's later victories as fulfillment of Elisha's prediction. This is strong evidence for a deliberate prediction-fulfillment correspondence in the chapter's final form, but it is not by itself proof of supernatural foreknowledge or independent historical accuracy.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish supernatural prediction, the date or authorship of the account, or the independent historical accuracy of the reported events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor could have shaped or selected the account so that the three victories matched the earlier symbolic action.
- The prediction may have been narrated retrospectively after the victories were known.
- The numerical correspondence alone cannot independently establish the historicity of either episode.
