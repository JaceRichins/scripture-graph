# Textual And Contextual in Isaiah 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of descent from Jesse, divine-spirit endowment, and judicial kingship strongly supports reading Isaiah 11:1-5 as a portrait of an ideal Davidic ruler. Identifying that ruler specifically with Jesus is a major Christian interpretation, but it requires canonical reception and theological context beyond these verses alone.

**Does not establish:** The internal wording alone does not uniquely identify the ruler as Jesus or determine a single historical or eschatological fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may originally have expressed hope for a near-term Davidic king such as Hezekiah or Josiah.
- The figure may be an idealized future Davidic monarch without an originally specified individual.
- The plant imagery could refer to renewal of the Davidic dynasty collectively rather than only one person.
