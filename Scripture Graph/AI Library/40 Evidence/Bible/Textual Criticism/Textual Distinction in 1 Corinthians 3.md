# Textual Distinction in 1 Corinthians 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The passage textually distinguishes the tested work from the worker: a work may burn and its builder suffer loss while the builder is nevertheless saved 'so as by fire.' This is strong evidence that reward, loss, and salvation are not treated as identical categories in this particular illustration.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish purgatory, universal salvation, a detailed theology of degrees of reward, or the precise identity of the fire.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may be a rhetorical construction metaphor rather than a technical classification of judgment outcomes.
- Different traditions identify the builder, the work, the fire, and the resulting salvation in materially different ways.
