# Textual Interdependence in 1 Chronicles 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The thanksgiving song has sustained, sequential correspondence with Psalm 105, Psalm 96, and Psalm 106. This is strong evidence of a literary or shared-liturgical relationship among these texts.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish the direction of borrowing, the date of a shared source, or that David personally composed every passage in its present form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Chronicler may have excerpted forms of psalms already in circulation.
- The psalms and Chronicles may draw independently from shared liturgical material.
- Some material may have passed from a chronicle tradition into later forms of the Psalter.
