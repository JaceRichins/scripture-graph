# IntertextualTypological in Luke 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
All three of Jesus' scriptural responses to the devil are drawn from Deuteronomy's account of Israel's wilderness testing (Deuteronomy 8:3, 6:13, 6:16), suggesting an intentional typological contrast in which Jesus, tested forty days as Israel was tested forty years, succeeds where Israel failed.

**Does not establish:** This does not prove the historicity of the wilderness dialogue's exact wording, only that the account as narrated draws a structured parallel to Israel's wilderness experience.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The consistent use of Deuteronomy may simply reflect that this section of Deuteronomy was a common source of memorized proof-texts in Second Temple Judaism, without a deliberate Israel/Jesus typology intended by the author.
- The forty days/forty years correspondence may be a natural narrative echo rather than a constructed literary typology.
