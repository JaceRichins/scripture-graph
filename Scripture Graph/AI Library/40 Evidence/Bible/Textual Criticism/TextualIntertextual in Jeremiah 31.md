# TextualIntertextual in Jeremiah 31

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 31:31-34 is quoted at length in Hebrews 8:8-12 (echoed again in Hebrews 10:16-17) — the single longest continuous Old Testament quotation in the New Testament — where it is applied to Christ's mediation of a new covenant.

**Does not establish:** That Jeremiah authored this oracle with Jesus Christ specifically in view, or that the New Testament application exhausts the passage's original meaning for the house of Israel and house of Judah

**Alternative explanations:**
- Hebrews may be applying the oracle typologically to the church rather than treating it as the exclusive or exhaustive fulfillment Jeremiah envisioned for national Israel and Judah
- Historical-critical readers note Jeremiah's original audience likely expected a renewed national covenant tied to return from Babylonian exile, a horizon distinct from (though not necessarily contradictory to) the individual, universalized covenant Hebrews describes
