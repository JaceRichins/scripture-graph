# New Testament Textual Criticism in Mark 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The supplied KJV text includes a final saying in Mark 6:11 about Sodom and Gomorrha and the day of judgment. Modern critical editions generally omit this sentence from Mark because it is absent from important early witnesses and is commonly judged to have entered the passage through influence from the parallel saying in Matthew 10:15.

**Does not establish:** The variant does not decide the truth of the saying, since the saying is independently present in Matthew 10:15, nor does textual priority alone determine a faith community's canonical use of the KJV form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The longer reading could theoretically preserve words lost independently from several textual lines, though the parallel in Matthew makes scribal addition more plausible.
- The transmission history of individual manuscripts can be more complex than a simple early-versus-late distinction.

**Scripture:** [[Mark 6#^mark-6-11|Mark 6:11]], [[Matthew 10#^matt-10-15|Matthew 10:15]]
