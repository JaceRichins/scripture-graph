# Extensive Textual Parallel in Matthew 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The supplied deterministic index identifies textual overlap between Matthew 5 and 43 verses of 3 Nephi 12. This is strong evidence of an extensive literary relationship between the two chapters, although the overlap alone cannot determine the historical mechanism or direction of dependence.

**Does not establish:** Textual overlap by itself does not establish the historicity of the Book of Mormon setting, the date of composition, or the precise direction and mechanism of literary dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Book of Mormon narrative presents the parallel as Jesus deliberately teaching closely related material in another setting.
- A literary-critical account may attribute the English-language correspondence to direct adaptation of the King James Matthew text.
- Some similarities could reflect a shared sayings tradition, though that explanation is less sufficient for sustained close English overlap.

**Scripture:** [[Matthew 5]], [[3 Nephi 12]]
