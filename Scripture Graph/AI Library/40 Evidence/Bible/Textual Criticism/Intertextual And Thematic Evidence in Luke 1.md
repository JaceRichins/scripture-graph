# Intertextual And Thematic Evidence in Luke 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mary's and Zacharias's speeches repeatedly name Israel, Abraham, the fathers, David, covenant, oath, mercy, prophets, and promised deliverance. This concentration is strong textual evidence that Luke frames the two births as continuation and fulfillment of Israel's covenant story rather than as isolated wonders.

**Does not establish:** The covenant framing does not independently prove that every promise has been exhaustively fulfilled, nor does it determine the historical origin of the speeches.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may reflect inherited Jewish hymns or traditions incorporated by Luke rather than Luke's own composition.
- Some phrases may be general scriptural diction rather than deliberate references to individual passages.
