# TextualNumerical Discrepancy in 1 Chronicles 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
1 Chronicles 2:13-15 names Jesse's sons as Eliab, Abinadab, Shimma, Nethaneel, Raddai, Ozem, and David the seventh - seven sons total - while 1 Samuel 16:10-11 and 17:12-14 describe Jesse as having eight sons with David the youngest, a numerical discrepancy between the two accounts.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which number is historically original or that either account contains an error.

**Alternative explanations:**
- One of Jesse's eight sons may have died without descendants and so was dropped from Chronicles' throne-focused genealogy.
- Ancient genealogies were sometimes schematic rather than exhaustive, and 'seven' may carry symbolic weight as a number of completeness.
- A transmission difference between the Chronicler's source material and the Samuel narrative could account for the gap without either being simply 'wrong.'
