# Literary Intertextuality in Revelation 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of sores, waters turned to blood, frog imagery, darkness, and hail provides strong evidence that Revelation 16 evokes the Exodus plague tradition. Revelation adapts and intensifies these motifs rather than reproducing the Exodus sequence exactly.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish a one-to-one allegorical key, a precise chronology, or that every image predicts a physically literal repetition of an Exodus plague.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some individual correspondences could arise from conventional disaster or apocalyptic imagery.
- The author may be drawing on an inherited tradition already shaped by Exodus rather than directly constructing every detail from the Exodus account.
