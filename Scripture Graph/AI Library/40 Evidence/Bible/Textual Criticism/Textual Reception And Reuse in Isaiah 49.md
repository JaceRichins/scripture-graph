# Textual Reception And Reuse in Isaiah 49

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
First Nephi 21 substantially reproduces Isaiah 49 across the full chapter, with some textual differences. This is strong evidence that Isaiah 49 is intentionally embedded in Nephi's argument, but the parallel by itself cannot determine the historical origin or independence of the Book of Mormon text.

**Does not establish:** The textual parallel does not by itself establish Book of Mormon antiquity, the age of its variant readings, or the historical circumstances in which either text was produced.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The relationship may reflect direct use of the King James Bible rather than an independent ancient textual witness.
- Individual wording differences may arise from translation, revision, textual variants, or modern compositional choices; the parallel alone does not adjudicate among them.
