# Repeated Textual Formula in Deuteronomy 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated chosen-place formula is strong internal evidence that authorized, centralized worship is a major concern of this chapter. Because the location remains unnamed here, the formula by itself does not identify the sanctuary or date the legislation.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish that the unnamed place is Jerusalem, determine when the law was written, or prove how consistently the law was practiced.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The formula may preserve an ideal requirement without referring to one fixed sanctuary throughout Israelite history.
- The repetition may reflect inherited legal language or editorial organization rather than a single compositional occasion.
