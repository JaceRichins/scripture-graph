# Intertextual Quotation in Proverbs 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hebrews 12:5-6 draws on Proverbs 3:11-12 (via the Greek Septuagint wording) as an authoritative 'exhortation' addressed to believers enduring hardship, showing that a New Testament author treated this specific passage as scriptural counsel to be personally applied.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Proverbs 3:11-12 was written with the specific New Testament application (perseverance under persecution) in mind; that is a later, contextually distinct use of the proverb.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording Hebrews quotes follows the Septuagint's Greek rendering rather than the Hebrew text underlying the KJV, so it is a citation of that Greek tradition rather than a verbatim quotation of the KJV Old Testament wording given here.
