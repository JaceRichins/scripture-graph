# Internal Textual Evidence in Ezekiel 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The inheritance rules provide strong internal textual evidence that the envisioned prince's authority is constrained by the people's hereditary possession and by a stated concern that they not be scattered from it.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that these rules were historically enforced, identify the prince, or prove that every form of property transfer outside the stated cases was prohibited.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The restrictions may describe an ideal visionary constitution rather than a historically implemented law.
- The concern for preserving allotments may be primarily theological or cultic rather than a comprehensive theory of government.

**Scripture:** [[Ezekiel 46#^ezek-46-16|Ezekiel 46:16]], [[Ezekiel 46#^ezek-46-17|Ezekiel 46:17]], [[Ezekiel 46#^ezek-46-18|Ezekiel 46:18]]
