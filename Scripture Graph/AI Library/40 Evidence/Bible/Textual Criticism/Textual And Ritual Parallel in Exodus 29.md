# Textual And Ritual Parallel in Exodus 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Leviticus 8 narrates a priestly consecration sequence closely corresponding to the instructions in Exodus 29. The parallel strongly supports a shared literary and ritual relationship between the chapters.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of literary dependence, a date of composition, Mosaic authorship, or the historicity of a particular performance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Leviticus 8 may have been composed as a narrative enactment of the instructions in Exodus 29.
- Both chapters may draw on a common ritual tradition or source.
- Editorial harmonization could account for some of the close correspondence.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 29]], [[Leviticus 8]]
