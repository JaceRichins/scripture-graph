# Canonical Reception And Intertextual Quotation in Isaiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 1:22-23 expressly applies Isaiah 7:14 to the conception and birth of Jesus. This is strong evidence for a canonical Christian messianic reading, but it is not by itself proof that Isaiah's sign had no nearer relevance or referent in the crisis of Ahaz.

**Does not establish:** Matthew's application does not, without further argument, establish the identity of the child in Isaiah's immediate setting or prove that the passage permits no additional referent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew may present a direct predictive fulfillment.
- Matthew may present a typological fulfillment in which Jesus brings an earlier Immanuel pattern to completion.
- The passage may have both an immediate historical application and a later messianic fulfillment.
