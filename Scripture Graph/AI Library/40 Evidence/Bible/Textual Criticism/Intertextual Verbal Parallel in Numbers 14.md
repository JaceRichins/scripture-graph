# Intertextual Verbal Parallel in Numbers 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The description of the Lord in Numbers 14:18 closely parallels the divine-character formula in Exodus 34:6–7. The verbal and thematic correspondence is strong evidence that the passages share a literary or traditional relationship.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish compositional direction, date, Mosaic authorship, the historicity of the narrated dialogue, or any larger claim about the Bible's origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Numbers may directly reuse the formulation in Exodus.
- Both passages may draw from a shared Israelite confessional tradition.
- A later editor may have harmonized independently transmitted materials.
- The verbal relationship alone cannot determine which passage has literary priority.
