# Negative Textual Evidence in Genesis 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Genesis 9 does not mention skin color, race, or a physical mark in connection with Noah's curse. Its wording therefore does not textually support describing skin color as the curse pronounced here.

**Does not establish:** The absence of racial language does not identify the historical circumstances behind the oracle or explain why Canaan is named.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Later interpreters have combined this passage with assumptions or traditions not stated in Genesis 9.
- Some interpreters treat Canaan as representing later populations, but that still does not insert a skin-color statement into this chapter.
