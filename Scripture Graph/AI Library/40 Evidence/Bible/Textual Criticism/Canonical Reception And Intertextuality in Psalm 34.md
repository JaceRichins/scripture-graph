# Canonical Reception And Intertextuality in Psalm 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 19:36 applies a bone-not-broken scriptural formula to Jesus' crucifixion. Psalm 34:20 is a plausible source, but Exodus 12:46 and Numbers 9:12 concerning the Passover victim are also plausible, and John may evoke more than one text. This is strong evidence for early Christian reception of the motif but not proof that Psalm 34 was originally composed as an exclusively messianic prediction.

**Does not establish:** The Gospel's application does not establish that the original psalmist consciously predicted crucifixion or that Psalm 34:20 is John's sole textual source.

**Alternative explanations:**
- John may primarily cite the Passover legislation in Exodus 12:46.
- John may primarily cite Numbers 9:12.
- The wording may intentionally combine the righteous-sufferer and Passover traditions.
