# Intertextual Historical Coherence in Romans 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans' report that Macedonia and Achaia contributed for poor saints at Jerusalem coheres closely with Paul's discussions elsewhere of a Jerusalem collection, Macedonian generosity, and Achaian readiness. This is substantial evidence of a shared concrete setting across the letters.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself prove the historicity of every detail, the exact date of Romans, or the absence of later editorial activity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The agreement may arise because the same author refers repeatedly to his own project, rather than from independent corroboration.
- An editor familiar with the Pauline collection tradition could preserve or construct consistent details.
- Shared names and locations establish coherence more readily than they establish every reported circumstance.
