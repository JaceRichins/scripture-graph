# Historical-Textual Correspondence in Ezekiel 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's explanation closely corresponds to the historical sequence reported in Kings and Chronicles: Babylon removes Jehoiachin and Jerusalem's elite, installs Mattaniah/Zedekiah, and Zedekiah later rebels after having been bound by an oath. This is strong evidence for identifying the unnamed royal figures in Ezekiel 17 with Jehoiachin and Zedekiah.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not establish supernatural foreknowledge, the precise date of composition, or the independence of the biblical accounts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ezekiel and the historical books may preserve a shared Israelite account of the events.
- Some details could reflect later editorial shaping informed by the outcome.
- Correspondence with known events does not by itself determine whether every element was written before or after Jerusalem's fall.
