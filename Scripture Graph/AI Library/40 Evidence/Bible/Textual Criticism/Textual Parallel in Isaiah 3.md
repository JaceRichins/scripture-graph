# Textual Parallel in Isaiah 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 3 has an extensive textual parallel in 2 Nephi 13; the supplied deterministic index identifies overlap across twenty-four verses. This securely supports a direct literary relationship between the chapters.

**Does not establish:** The overlap alone does not establish independent transmission, the antiquity of the Book of Mormon text, or which textual differences preserve an earlier reading.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The relationship can be explained as quotation or translation dependence.
- Differences between the chapters may reflect translation, textual variation, or editorial adaptation rather than an independent ancient witness.
