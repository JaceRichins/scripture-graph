# Textual-Parallel in Isaiah 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 3 (KJV) has a verified near-total textual overlap (24 of 26 verses) with 2 Nephi 13, consistent with the well-documented pattern that Book of Mormon quotations of Isaiah closely follow the King James Version's wording.

**Does not establish:** The verified text overlap establishes only that the two chapters share nearly identical wording; it does not by itself adjudicate between translation-method explanations and claims about an ancient underlying text, nor does it speak to the antiquity or historicity of the Nephite record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The translator (Joseph Smith) drew on the KJV text directly during the translation/dictation process rather than producing an independent rendering of an underlying record.
- An authentic ancient source (brass-plate Isaiah) existed and, through the translation process, was rendered in wording that closely tracks the KJV, a text already familiar to the translator and scribes.
- Later editorial or printing-stage harmonization with the KJV occurred, independent of the original dictation.
