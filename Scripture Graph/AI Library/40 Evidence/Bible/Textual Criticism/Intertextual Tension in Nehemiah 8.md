# Intertextual Tension in Nehemiah 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Nehemiah 8:17's statement that Israel had not “done so” since Joshua stands in prima facie tension with Ezra 3:4, which reports that the earlier returned exiles kept the Feast of Tabernacles. Plausible harmonizations understand “done so” as referring to the manner, comprehensiveness, booth-building, or exceptional communal character of the observance, but the text does not specify which qualification is intended.

**Does not establish:** The tension does not by itself establish either an irreconcilable contradiction or a particular harmonization.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ezra 3 records the feast, while Nehemiah 8 emphasizes a feature or degree of observance absent from the earlier report.
- “Done so” may be conventional hyperbole stressing the present celebration's extraordinary scale or fidelity.
- The two statements may preserve differing historical assessments that are not fully harmonized.
- Ezra 3 does not explicitly describe the construction and occupation of booths.
