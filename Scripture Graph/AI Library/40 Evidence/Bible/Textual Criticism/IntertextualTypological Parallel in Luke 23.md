# IntertextualTypological Parallel in Luke 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detail that the soldiers "parted his raiment, and cast lots" (Luke 23:34) verbally echoes Psalm 22:18, but unlike John (19:24), Luke does not explicitly present the act as fulfillment of that psalm.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Luke intended readers to see conscious prophetic fulfillment here.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Casting lots for a condemned man's clothing was ordinary Roman execution practice, so the detail may simply reflect custom rather than deliberate allusion.
- Luke's silence about fulfillment, in contrast to John's explicit citation, may indicate this author did not intend the parallel as a prophecy-fulfillment claim.
