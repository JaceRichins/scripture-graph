# Textual-Version Correspondence in Hebrews 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
At the end of the covenant-breaking clause, Hebrews 8:9 says that God "regarded them not," whereas the KJV rendering of the Hebrew form of Jeremiah 31:32 says that God was "an husband unto them." The wording in Hebrews corresponds to the ancient Greek form of Jeremiah, supplying strong evidence that this portion of the citation derives from a Greek scriptural textual tradition.

**Does not establish:** This correspondence does not identify the author, establish the original language of every source used in Hebrews, or prove any larger claim about inspiration or historical inerrancy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author may have used a Greek Jewish textual form related to, but not identical with, surviving Septuagint manuscripts.
- The wording could have circulated through an established Greek quotation tradition rather than being copied directly from a manuscript.
