# Internal Textual Repetition in Numbers 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated statements of one ordinance or one law provide strong textual evidence that resident strangers share the stated sacrificial rules and remedies for inadvertent sin on the same terms as native Israelites. The scope of the evidence is limited to the legal matters addressed here.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish complete social, political, or economic equality between strangers and native Israelites in every domain.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The equality language may be formulaic legal drafting rather than a statement about broader communal identity.
- The stranger's equal treatment in these provisions can coexist with distinctions elsewhere in Israelite law.
