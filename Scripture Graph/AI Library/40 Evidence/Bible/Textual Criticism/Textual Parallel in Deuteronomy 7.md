# Textual Parallel in Deuteronomy 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Deuteronomy 7:20 and 7:22 closely correspond in subject and sequence to Exodus 23:28-30: both mention the hornet, gradual expulsion, and the danger of wild beasts increasing. This is strong evidence of a shared covenant-conquest tradition or direct literary relationship.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the direction of dependence, the date of either passage, Mosaic authorship, or the historicity of the described events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages could independently draw from an earlier shared oral or written tradition.
- The motifs may have circulated as conventional covenant language before their present literary forms.
- Later editorial activity could account for some of the verbal and thematic alignment.
