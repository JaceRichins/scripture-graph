# Textual-Parallel in 2 Kings 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extensive close correspondence between 2 Kings 18:13-37 and Isaiah 36 is strong evidence of a direct literary relationship or substantial dependence on shared narrative material.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not determine the direction of dependence, the original source, the date of either version, or the historical verbatim accuracy of the speeches.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah may depend on Kings.
- Kings may depend on an Isaianic form of the account.
- Both books may draw on a shared court, prophetic, or archival narrative.
- The relationship may include more than one stage of transmission or editing.
