# TypologicalIntertextual in Exodus 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Paul retrospectively interprets Israel's passage under the cloud and through the sea as a type of baptism, an interpretation applied outside Exodus 14 itself rather than stated within the chapter.

**Does not establish:** does not establish that baptism symbolism was understood or intended within Exodus 14's own narrative context

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul's typology is a later apostolic theological application and may not reflect the original narrative's intended meaning to its ancient audience
- Water-crossing as divine-deliverance imagery is a common ancient Near Eastern motif independent of any baptismal association
