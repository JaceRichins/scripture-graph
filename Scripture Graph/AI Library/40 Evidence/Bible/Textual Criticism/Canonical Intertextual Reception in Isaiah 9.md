# Canonical Intertextual Reception in Isaiah 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 4:12-16 expressly presents Jesus's Galilean ministry as fulfillment of Isaiah 9:1-2, while Luke 1:32-33 associates Jesus with David's throne and an unending kingdom in language closely parallel to Isaiah 9:7. This provides strong evidence for early Christian messianic reception of the passage.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Isaiah's original audience understood the passage solely as a prediction of Jesus or that every title must be read as a direct metaphysical definition of the child.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew may be presenting typological fulfillment rather than asserting that Isaiah's only intended referent was Jesus.
- Luke's Davidic language may also derive from broader Davidic covenant traditions rather than Isaiah 9 alone.
- The oracle may have had an immediate royal horizon and later received an additional messianic application.
