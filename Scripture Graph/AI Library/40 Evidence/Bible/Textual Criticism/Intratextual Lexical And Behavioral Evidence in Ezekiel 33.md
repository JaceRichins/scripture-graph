# Intratextual Lexical And Behavioral Evidence in Ezekiel 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ezekiel 33 provides strong textual evidence that 'turning' is more than a verbal profession: the description includes doing what is lawful and right, restoring a pledge, returning stolen property, and walking without further iniquity.

**Does not establish:** This passage alone does not provide a complete account of repentance, grace, atonement, ritual obligations, or final salvation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The listed actions may be representative examples rather than an exhaustive definition of repentance.
- In context, 'live' may include survival from temporal judgment and should not automatically be restricted to, or equated with, a complete doctrine of eternal salvation.
