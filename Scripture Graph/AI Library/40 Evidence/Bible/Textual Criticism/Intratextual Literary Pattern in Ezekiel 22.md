# Intratextual Literary Pattern in Ezekiel 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The successive accusations against prophets, priests, princes, and the people of the land are strong intratextual evidence that the oracle portrays corruption as societal and institutional rather than restricted to a single office.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish how common each offense was historically or prove the guilt of every individual in the named groups.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The comprehensive list may use prophetic totalization and need not mean that every member of every group was guilty.
- The categories may organize the rhetoric of the indictment more than quantify the prevalence of each abuse.
