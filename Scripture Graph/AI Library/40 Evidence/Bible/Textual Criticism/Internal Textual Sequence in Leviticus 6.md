# Internal Textual Sequence in Leviticus 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of restitution, added compensation, sacrifice, priestly atonement, and forgiveness is strong textual evidence that, for these offenses, ritual atonement does not replace material repair to the person harmed.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every wrong can be repaired by adding twenty percent, that sacrifice mechanically produces forgiveness, or that these civil-ritual penalties transfer unchanged into modern legal systems.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence is legislation for specified property offenses and should not automatically be converted into a universal procedure for every kind of sin.
- The wording may describe legally required acts without fully stating the offender's inward disposition.
