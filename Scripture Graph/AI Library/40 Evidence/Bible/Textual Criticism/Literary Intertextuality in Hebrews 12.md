# Literary Intertextuality in Hebrews 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of scriptural citations and echoes is strong evidence that the author deliberately constructs the exhortation through Israel's scriptures: parental discipline, strengthened limbs and straight paths, the dangerous root, Sinai, cosmic shaking, and consuming fire.

**Does not establish:** The intertexts do not identify the anonymous author, determine the exact date of composition, or prove that every verbal resemblance was intended as a formal quotation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some shorter correspondences could reflect widely shared biblical diction rather than a conscious citation of one particular passage.
- English wording can obscure or exaggerate relationships because Hebrews often reflects Greek forms of Israel's scriptures.
