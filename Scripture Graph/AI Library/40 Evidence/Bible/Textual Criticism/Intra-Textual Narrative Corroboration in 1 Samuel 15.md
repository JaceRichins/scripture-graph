# Intra-Textual Narrative Corroboration in 1 Samuel 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The later Samuel narrative again names Saul's failure concerning Amalek when explaining the transfer of the kingdom. Within the final form of 1 Samuel, this is strong evidence that the Amalek episode is intended as a central explanation of Saul's loss of kingship.

**Does not establish:** This literary recurrence does not independently establish the episode's historicity, its precise chronology, or the independence of the two accounts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later passage may intentionally reuse the theology or language of chapter 15 rather than preserve independent historical corroboration.
- An editor may have linked originally separate traditions into a unified explanation of Saul's rejection.
