# Intertextual Reuse in Romans 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 9:25-26 reuses Hosea's "not my people" restoration language after explicitly describing God's call as reaching both Jews and Gentiles. This is strong evidence that Paul incorporates Hosea into his Jew-Gentile argument, although Hosea's original immediate context concerns estranged Israel.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Hosea's original audience would have understood these sayings as a direct prediction of later Gentile inclusion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be extending Hosea typologically rather than claiming that Hosea's immediate historical referent was the Gentiles.
- The sayings may describe a general pattern of God restoring excluded people, which Paul then applies to the mixed called community.
