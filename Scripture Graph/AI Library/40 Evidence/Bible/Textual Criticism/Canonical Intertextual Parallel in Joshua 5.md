# Canonical Intertextual Parallel in Joshua 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The command for Joshua to remove his shoe because he stands on holy ground closely parallels the command to Moses at the burning bush. This is strong evidence of a literary association between Joshua's encounter and Moses's commissioning encounter, although claims about the author's precise intention remain interpretive.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish the historical circumstances of composition or identify the captain of the LORD's host as God, Christ, or an angel.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages could employ a conventional protocol for entering sacred space without requiring a detailed Moses–Joshua typology.
- The correspondence may establish continuity of divine commissioning generally rather than equating Joshua's role or authority with Moses's in every respect.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 3#^ex-3-5|Exodus 3:5]], [[Joshua 5#^josh-5-15|Joshua 5:15]]
