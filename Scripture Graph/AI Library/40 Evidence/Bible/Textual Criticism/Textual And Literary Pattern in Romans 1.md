# Textual And Literary Pattern in Romans 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated language of exchange and the three statements that God gave people up or over provide strong internal evidence that Romans 1:23-28 is arranged as a connected theological sequence: rejection of God is followed by divine handover to distorted desires and conduct.

**Does not establish:** This pattern does not establish a complete doctrine of divine causation, human psychology, or the temporal consequences of every individual sin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be emphatic rhetoric without constituting a rigid formal structure.
- The sequence may portray judgment, consequence, or both; the wording alone does not fully resolve that distinction.
