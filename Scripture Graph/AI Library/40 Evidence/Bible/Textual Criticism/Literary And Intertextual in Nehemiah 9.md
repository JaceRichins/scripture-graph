# Literary And Intertextual in Nehemiah 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The prayer's accumulation of identifiable Pentateuchal persons, episodes, institutions, and language provides strong evidence that its theological argument is deliberately framed through Israel's scriptural traditions.

**Does not establish:** The parallels alone do not establish the prayer's precise date, author, compositional history, or dependence on a particular manuscript form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some shared details may derive from common communal memory or liturgical tradition rather than direct consultation of particular written passages.
- The prayer may combine multiple inherited summaries and formulas rather than depend uniformly on a single form of the Pentateuch.
