# Scriptural Reception And Textual Variation in Amos 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 7:42-43 explicitly presents material corresponding to Amos 5:25-27 as prophetic scripture, but its wording has "Remphan" and exile "beyond Babylon" where the KJV text of Amos has "Chiun" and captivity "beyond Damascus." This is strong evidence for an early differing reception or textual form of the passage, but the two passages alone cannot reconstruct the original wording.

**Does not establish:** The variation does not by itself identify the original reading, demonstrate corruption, or invalidate either passage's theological use.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Acts may preserve a source-text tradition different from the Hebrew form underlying the KJV Amos.
- Stephen or Luke may have paraphrased or interpretively adapted the destination to Babylon.
- The differences may combine textual transmission with application to later Israelite exile history.
