# Internal Textual Pattern in Leviticus 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The descending sequence from livestock to birds to flour is strong internal textual evidence that the prescribed rite accommodated worshippers with differing material means.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every Israelite experienced an equal economic burden, nor does it by itself establish a general welfare or equality program.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The alternatives may address availability of sacrificial animals as well as poverty.
- The scale may be a narrow ritual accommodation rather than an expression of a comprehensive principle of social equality.
