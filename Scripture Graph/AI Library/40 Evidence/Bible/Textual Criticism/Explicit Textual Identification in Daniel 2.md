# Explicit Textual Identification in Daniel 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides direct textual evidence for identifying the head of gold with Nebuchadnezzar and the dominion granted to him; this is the only kingdom in the sequence explicitly identified by name or ruler within the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the identities of the second, third, or fourth kingdoms.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The address may blend the individual king with the Babylonian kingdom rather than sharply distinguishing them.

**Scripture:** [[Daniel 2#^dan-2-37|Daniel 2:37]], [[Daniel 2#^dan-2-38|Daniel 2:38]], [[Daniel 2#^dan-2-39|Daniel 2:39]]
