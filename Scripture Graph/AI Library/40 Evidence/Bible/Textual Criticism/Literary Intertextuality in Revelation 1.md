# Literary Intertextuality in Revelation 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The visionary figure's white hair, golden girdle, fiery or penetrating appearance, brass-like feet, and overwhelming voice substantially overlap imagery in Daniel 7 and Daniel 10. This is strong evidence that Revelation 1 deliberately participates in the symbolic language of earlier biblical visions, though identifying every individual detail with a single source is less certain.

**Does not establish:** The imagery alone does not establish the date, identity, or historical circumstances of the author, nor does it prove a single-source compositional scheme.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some similarities may arise from a shared stock of Jewish apocalyptic imagery rather than direct dependence on a particular passage.
- Individual details may echo more than one scriptural source, making one-to-one source assignments uncertain.
