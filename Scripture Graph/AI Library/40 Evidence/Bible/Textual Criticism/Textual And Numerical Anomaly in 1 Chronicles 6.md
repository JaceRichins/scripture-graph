# Textual And Numerical Anomaly in 1 Chronicles 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The two-city shortfall in three separate family lists is strong internal evidence that the received detailed enumeration is abbreviated or has suffered omissions relative to its preserved subtotals.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy alone does not identify when omissions occurred, which names are absent, or whether the subtotals or the detailed lists preserve the earlier form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The subtotals may include cities intentionally left unnamed in the detailed account.
- A counting convention may differ from a simple count of surviving place names.
- The detailed list may have been abbreviated by the compiler rather than damaged during transmission.
