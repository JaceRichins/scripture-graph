# Textual Correspondence in Isaiah 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 2 and 2 Nephi 12 exhibit extensive chapter-level correspondence. This securely establishes a textual relationship at the level of the preserved English texts, but that correspondence alone cannot adjudicate competing explanations of the Book of Mormon's origin or translation.

**Does not establish:** Textual correspondence by itself does not establish the date, historical provenance, or translation mechanism of 2 Nephi.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An ancient Nephite record quoted an Israelite form of Isaiah.
- A revelatory translation was expressed substantially in King James English.
- The English passage was composed through direct modern reuse of the King James Bible.
- The correspondence reflects more than one of these processes.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 2#^isa-2-1|Isaiah 2:1-22]], [[2 Nephi 12#^2ne-12-1|2 Nephi 12:1-22]]
