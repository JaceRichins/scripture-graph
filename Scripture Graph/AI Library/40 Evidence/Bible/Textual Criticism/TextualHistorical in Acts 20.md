# TextualHistorical in Acts 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
This unrecorded saying is one of the NT's clearest 'agrapha' (sayings of Jesus preserved outside the Gospels), suggesting that early Christian communities retained oral traditions of Jesus' teaching beyond what the Gospel writers chose to include.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish the exact original wording, setting, or extent of a larger body of lost oral Jesus-tradition; this is a single, isolated instance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be paraphrasing or loosely summarizing a known Gospel teaching (e.g., Luke 6:38) rather than transmitting a verbatim, separately preserved saying.
- Luke, as author, could be compressing a broader teaching theme into a single memorable line attributed to Jesus.
