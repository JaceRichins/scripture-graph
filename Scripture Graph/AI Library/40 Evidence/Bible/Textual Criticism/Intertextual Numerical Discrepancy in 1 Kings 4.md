# Intertextual Numerical Discrepancy in 1 Kings 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The KJV text of 1 Kings 4:26 reports forty thousand stalls of horses, whereas 2 Chronicles 9:25 reports four thousand stalls and the same total of twelve thousand horsemen. This is firm evidence of a numerical difference between the canonical passages, but it does not determine which number, if either, reflects the earliest recoverable wording or historical total.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy alone does not identify the original number, prove deliberate deception, or settle the historical size of Solomon's cavalry establishment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A copying error may have altered one of the numbers during textual transmission.
- The two writers may have counted different kinds of stalls, horses, or administrative units.
- One figure may be a deliberate approximation or rhetorical expansion.
- The passages may preserve different source traditions.
