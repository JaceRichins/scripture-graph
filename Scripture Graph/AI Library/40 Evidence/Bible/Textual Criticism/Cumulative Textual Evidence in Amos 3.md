# Cumulative Textual Evidence in Amos 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of oppression, violence, robbery, palaces, ivory houses, and great houses provides strong internal evidence that systemic social injustice is central to the chapter's indictment, alongside its condemnation of the Beth-el altar.

**Does not establish:** The internal evidence does not identify every victim or offender, quantify the injustice, or independently verify the exact historical conditions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may be prophetic generalization rather than a description of every wealthy household.
- The text associates particular forms of wealth with injustice but does not say that every possession or large house is inherently sinful.
