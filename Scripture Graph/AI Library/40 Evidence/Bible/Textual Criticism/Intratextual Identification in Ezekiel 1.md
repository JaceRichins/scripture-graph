# Intratextual Identification in Ezekiel 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ezekiel 10 explicitly identifies the living creatures seen by the river Chebar as cherubim. Within the book's final form, this is strong intratextual evidence that the four living creatures of Ezekiel 1 are intended to be understood as cherubim.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the creatures' exact physical form, the historical circumstances of composition, or the objective mechanism of the vision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compositional-history analysis could propose that the explicit identification in Ezekiel 10 belongs to a later editorial layer.
- The identification does not require every visual feature to be a literal description of cherubic anatomy.
