# Textual Ecclesiology in Ephesians 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ephesians 2 explicitly assigns apostles and prophets a foundational place in its building metaphor while reserving the decisive cornerstone role for Jesus Christ. This strongly supports the limited claim that these witnesses and Christ are constitutive of the community portrayed here.

**Does not establish:** The metaphor alone does not establish the identity of the prophets, a full model of church government, apostolic succession, or the structure of any modern denomination.

**Alternative explanations:**
- “Prophets” may denote early Christian prophets rather than the prophets of the Hebrew Bible.
- The metaphor may emphasize foundational testimony or revelation rather than prescribing a complete, permanent institutional structure.
- Some interpreters understand apostles and prophets as a single closely associated foundational group.

**Scripture:** [[Ephesians 2#^eph-2-20|Ephesians 2:20]]
