# Intertextual Numerical Discrepancy in 1 Chronicles 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Chronicles lists seven sons of Jesse and calls David the seventh, whereas 1 Samuel 16 presents seven sons before David and 1 Samuel 17 calls Jesse the father of eight sons. The passages therefore preserve a real numerical tension in Jesse's family list.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy does not identify which explanation is correct and does not by itself establish that the wider narratives are historically unreliable.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may omit a son who died without descendants or later genealogical importance.
- The authors may have drawn upon differing family traditions.
- A textual variant or scribal change may underlie one of the numbers.
- The number seven may have been favored for literary or symbolic reasons.
