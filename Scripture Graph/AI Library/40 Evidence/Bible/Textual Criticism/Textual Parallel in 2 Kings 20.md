# Textual Parallel in 2 Kings 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The verified verbal and narrative parallels between 2 Kings 20 and Isaiah 38–39 provide strong evidence of a literary relationship among the accounts.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not by themselves establish which text is earlier, the identity of an author or editor, the precise source history, or the historical accuracy of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author of Kings may have adapted material also preserved in Isaiah.
- The author or compiler of Isaiah may have adapted material from Kings.
- Both accounts may depend on a shared earlier court, prophetic, or archival source.
- Later scribal harmonization may account for some of the close agreement.
