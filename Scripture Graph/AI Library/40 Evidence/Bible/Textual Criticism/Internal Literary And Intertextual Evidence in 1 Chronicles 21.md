# Internal Literary And Intertextual Evidence in 1 Chronicles 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit sequence from divinely designated threshing floor, to accepted sacrifice, to declaration of the future sanctuary site is strong literary evidence that the Chronicler uses this episode to legitimate the temple's location and sacrificial function.

**Does not establish:** The literary connection does not independently prove the site's archaeological history or every historical detail of the angelic appearance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may preserve remembered site tradition rather than having been created primarily to legitimate the temple.
- Literary legitimation and historical recollection are not mutually exclusive.
