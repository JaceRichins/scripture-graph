# Internal Textual Evidence in John 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 17 supplies strong internal textual evidence that the Gospel portrays Jesus as having a relationship with the Father before creation: Jesus speaks of glory he had with the Father before the world existed and of the Father's love before the foundation of the world.

**Does not establish:** These verses alone do not settle the precise metaphysics of Christ's preexistence, the nature of divine embodiment, or the historical reliability of every reported saying.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language can be read as the Gospel's theological or literary presentation rather than independently verified autobiographical recollection.
- Some interpreters understand the glory as eternally intended or proleptically possessed, although the wording that Jesus had it with the Father favors actual preexistence.

**Scripture:** [[John 17#^john-17-5|John 17:5]], [[John 17#^john-17-24|John 17:24]]
