# Historical Contextual Fit in 2 Kings 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The deportation of Israelites and resettlement of foreign populations described in the chapter is broadly consistent with the documented Neo-Assyrian use of mass deportation and population transfer as imperial policy.

**Does not establish:** This contextual agreement does not verify every named destination, identify the monarch responsible for every stage, prove the chapter's theological explanation, or establish complete narrative precision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later writer familiar with Assyrian policy could have composed a historically plausible account.
- The account may preserve genuine events while compressing several stages of deportation and resettlement.
- Assyrian royal inscriptions and biblical historiography each shape events for their own rhetorical purposes.
