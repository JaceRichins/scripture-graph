# Internal Textual Argument in Jeremiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The appeal to Shiloh supplies strong internal evidence that Jeremiah 7 explicitly rejects the doctrine of an inviolable sanctuary: a former place bearing God's name did not escape judgment, and Jerusalem's temple is threatened with the same fate.

**Does not establish:** The analogy does not establish the precise historical date of the sermon, the exact circumstances of Shiloh's destruction, or that every biblical promise concerning Jerusalem was understood as unconditional.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The comparison is a prophetic threat and does not by itself identify every historical event Jeremiah believed had occurred at Shiloh.
- Some readers may emphasize the people's abuse of the temple rather than infer that any general belief in sanctuary protection is being disputed.
