# Intertextual Narrative Continuity in Joshua 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The correspondence among the wilderness promise, Caleb's recollection, and the Hebron grant creates strong literary continuity across Numbers, Deuteronomy, and Joshua. This supports the conclusion that Joshua 14 intentionally presents Hebron as fulfillment of an earlier promise.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence by itself does not establish the events' historicity, the independence of the accounts, or a particular theory of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later author or editor could have coordinated the accounts into a coherent literary sequence.
- The repeated wording may reflect shared tradition rather than independent historical corroboration.
