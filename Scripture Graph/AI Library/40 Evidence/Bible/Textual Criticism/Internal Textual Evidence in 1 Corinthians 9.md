# Internal Textual Evidence in 1 Corinthians 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The juxtaposition of an expressly affirmed right to material support with Paul's voluntary nonuse of that right is strong internal textual evidence that this chapter does not impose unpaid ministry as a universal rule.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish which financial arrangement every modern church or minister should use, nor does it validate every appeal for religious compensation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul's refusal may address circumstances peculiar to Corinth rather than supply a general model.
- The chapter may defend a legitimate right while leaving communities free to adopt different support arrangements.
- Paul's statement about preaching without charge applies directly to his own conduct and should not automatically be transferred to every minister.
