# Sustained Textual Parallel in 2 Kings 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The embassy narrative in 2 Kings 20:12-19 has a close, sustained textual parallel in Isaiah 39:1-8, including the visitors from Babylon, display of treasures, prophetic interrogation, prediction of removal to Babylon, and Hezekiah's response. This is strong evidence of a literary relationship between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself determine the direction of dependence, the identity of an author or editor, the date of either form, or the historicity of every reported detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah may depend directly or indirectly on Kings.
- Kings may depend on an Isaianic account.
- Both books may draw on a shared earlier source or tradition.
