# Textual Historical Anchoring in 1 Chronicles 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The dating of genealogical registration by royal reigns, together with named Assyrian rulers and deportation destinations, is evidence that the chapter presents genealogy as historical and national memory rather than as an isolated list of ancestors.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself prove the accuracy or completeness of every name, number, territorial claim, or chronological relationship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later compiler could have added chronological anchors to inherited lists.
- Historical framing alone does not show that every genealogical link was preserved without compression or error.
- Some names and notices may serve literary or identity-forming purposes as well as archival ones.
