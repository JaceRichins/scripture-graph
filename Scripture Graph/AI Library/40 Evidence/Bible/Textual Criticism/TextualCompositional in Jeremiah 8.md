# TextualCompositional in Jeremiah 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The near-verbatim duplication of an oracle unit between Jeremiah 6:13-15 and Jeremiah 8:10-12 is evidence that the book of Jeremiah contains recurring oracle material rather than a single, non-repeating sequential composition.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish a specific redaction-critical theory (e.g., multiple authors, editorial layers, or which passage is 'original'); it only establishes that overlapping oracle material appears in both chapters.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah himself may have deliberately reused a memorable refrain across two distinct sermons for rhetorical effect, rather than the duplication reflecting later editorial combination of sources.
- Scribal transmission could have harmonized one passage toward the wording of the other over time, meaning the exact original wording of one location is not fully certain.
