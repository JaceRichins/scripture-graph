# Internal Textual Evidence in 2 Kings 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter strongly supports the limited conclusion that the discovered book was treated as an authoritative law text whose requirements and warnings applied to Judah. Its wording alone does not securely identify the book's complete contents, date of composition, or relationship to a particular modern reconstruction of the Pentateuch.

**Does not establish:** The chapter alone does not establish that the book was identical to the present book of Deuteronomy, that it was newly composed during Josiah's reign, or that it contained the entire present Pentateuch.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The book may have contained a form of Deuteronomy.
- It may have represented a broader Torah collection or another legal corpus.
- The account may preserve a discovery tradition while also reflecting later editorial framing.
