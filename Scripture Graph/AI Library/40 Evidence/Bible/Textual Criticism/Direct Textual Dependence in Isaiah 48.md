# Direct Textual Dependence in Isaiah 48

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The verified full-chapter textual relationship between Isaiah 48 and 1 Nephi 20 is strong evidence that 1 Nephi 20 deliberately transmits or adapts this Isaianic chapter rather than merely sharing a general theme with it.

**Does not establish:** The textual relationship alone does not establish the date, language, physical source, or historical mechanism by which the Isaiah material entered 1 Nephi.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Within the Book of Mormon narrative, the relationship is explained as Nephi quoting Isaiah from the brass plates.
- A modern-origin explanation attributes the relationship to direct dependence on the King James Bible.
- A translation-based explanation may distinguish an ancient underlying quotation from the English wording used to render it.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 48]], [[1 Nephi 20]]
