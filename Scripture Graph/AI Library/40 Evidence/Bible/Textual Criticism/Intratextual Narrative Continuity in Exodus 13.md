# Intratextual Narrative Continuity in Exodus 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close correspondence between Joseph's charge in Genesis 50 and its explicit execution in Exodus 13 is strong internal evidence that the canonical narrative intentionally connects the patriarchal promises with the exodus generation.

**Does not establish:** This literary fulfillment does not by itself establish the historical occurrence, date, or authorship of either account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later author or editor could have shaped Exodus 13 to complete the expectation in Genesis 50.
- The correspondence may demonstrate literary dependence without independently corroborating the reported events.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 50#^gen-50-24|Genesis 50:24]], [[Genesis 50#^gen-50-25|Genesis 50:25]], [[Exodus 13#^ex-13-19|Exodus 13:19]]
