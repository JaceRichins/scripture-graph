# Textual Theological Evidence in Exodus 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter's theology, Bezaleel and Aholiab provide strong textual evidence that divinely enabled service may include design, manual craft, and teaching rather than only speech or ritual performance.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every talent, artistic success, occupational role, or technical innovation is direct revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may chiefly legitimate the sanctuary's authorized artisans rather than define spiritual gifts generally.
- The passage may describe an exceptional commission rather than a rule about all human skill.
