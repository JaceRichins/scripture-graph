# TextualSocial-Historical in Exodus 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Exodus 35:25-26 explicitly designates women as 'wise hearted' practitioners of a specialized craft (spinning blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, and goats' hair), naming their skill in terms parallel to those used for other artisans in the chapter.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that women held religious authority, leadership, or standing equal to male artisans/priests in this context; the text confines women's named contributions to textile production and material offerings.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Gendered division of textile labor was standard across the ancient Near East; naming women spinners may reflect ordinary description rather than a pointed statement about status or dignity of women's work.
- The parallel 'wise/willing hearted' phrasing may simply be the chapter's general stylistic refrain applied evenly to all contributors, without intending a special claim about women specifically.
