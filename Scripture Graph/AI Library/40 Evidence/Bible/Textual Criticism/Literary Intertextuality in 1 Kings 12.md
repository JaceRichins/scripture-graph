# Literary Intertextuality in 1 Kings 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of golden calves and the exodus proclamation is strong textual evidence that the account intentionally associates Jeroboam's shrines with Exodus 32. It strongly supports an intertextual connection, but claims about the precise date, author, or compositional process remain substantially less certain.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the historicity of every reported detail, direct dependence on one specific manuscript form of Exodus, or the date of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author may be applying a familiar apostasy formula to Jeroboam rather than preserving Jeroboam's exact historical words.
- Both accounts could employ conventional Israelite cultic language without direct literary dependence, though the combined verbal and thematic similarities make coincidence less likely.
