# Cumulative Textual Pattern in Deuteronomy 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentration of commands to internalize, speak, teach, bind, and write the words strongly supports reading Deuteronomy 6:6-9 as an intentional strategy for embedding covenant memory throughout ordinary life.

**Does not establish:** The passage by itself does not establish the precise form or historical date of later Jewish practices involving tefillin or mezuzot.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some or all of the bodily and architectural language may be rhetorical imagery for constant remembrance.
- The commands may also authorize concrete material practices; the figurative and literal readings need not be mutually exclusive.
