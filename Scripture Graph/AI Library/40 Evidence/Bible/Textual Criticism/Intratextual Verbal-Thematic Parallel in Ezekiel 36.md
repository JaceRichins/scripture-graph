# Intratextual Verbal-Thematic Parallel in Ezekiel 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentrated recurrence of the heart, spirit, obedience, gathering, and covenant motifs in Ezekiel 11:17–20 and Ezekiel 36:24–28 is strong evidence that the two passages belong to a deliberately sustained theological pattern within the book.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish single authorship, precise compositional history, a date for either passage, or the supernatural origin of the prophecy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later editor could have deliberately reused and coordinated earlier material.
- Both passages could independently draw upon a shared prophetic or liturgical tradition.
- Some repetition may reflect Ezekiel's conventional vocabulary rather than a tightly designed literary scheme.
