# Intertextual Scriptural Argument in Hebrews 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated appeal to Psalm 110:4 is strong textual evidence that Hebrews constructs its argument from an earlier scriptural oracle about an enduring priest. It does not, without additional argument, prove that every reader of Psalm 110 before Hebrews understood the oracle in precisely the same christological way.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the original historical referent of Psalm 110 or demonstrate that all ancient interpreters read it messianically.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Psalm 110 may originally have been understood in relation to an Israelite king before its application to Jesus.
- The christological application may reflect the interpretive method of Hebrews rather than the only possible historical reading of the psalm.
