# Intertextual Correspondence in 1 Kings 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close correspondence between Solomon's many wives turning his heart away and Deuteronomy's specific warning about a king multiplying wives is strong textual evidence that this account engages the Torah's model of kingship.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the date, direction of literary dependence, historical accuracy, or identity of the author or editor.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may reflect a shared conventional warning about dynastic marriage rather than direct literary dependence.
- A later editor may have shaped the Solomon account in Deuteronomic language.
