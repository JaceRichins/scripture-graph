# Intratextual Verbal Parallel in Jeremiah 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 8:10-12 closely parallels Jeremiah 6:13-15 in sequence and wording: comprehensive covetousness and religious falsehood, superficial healing with a false announcement of peace, lack of shame, and an announced fall at visitation. This is strong evidence of direct literary reuse or shared textual tradition within Jeremiah.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not determine which passage came first, whether Jeremiah or an editor repeated it, or the date and stages of the book's composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah may have repeated an earlier oracle in a new context.
- A compiler or editor may have reused a traditional Jeremiah passage.
- Both passages may preserve a common prophetic formulation.
