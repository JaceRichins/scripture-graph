# Cumulative Textual Evidence in Deuteronomy 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combined references to absent participants, the coming generation, and children who possess the revealed law provide strong textual evidence that covenant memory and responsibility are being projected beyond the immediate assembly.

**Does not establish:** The wording does not by itself settle how later generations enter, renew, or personally consent to the covenant.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The person not present in verse 15 could refer to absent contemporaries rather than unborn descendants.
- Corporate continuity may be emphasized more than the independent consent of each later individual.
