# Textual Ambiguity in Joshua 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Whether Achan's sons and daughters were executed along with him is genuinely ambiguous in the received text. Verse 24 lists his sons, daughters, animals, and tent among what was 'brought... unto the valley of Achor,' but verse 25 shifts pronoun number — 'stoned him' (singular) followed by 'burned them with fire' (plural) — leaving it unclear whether the plural refers to Achan plus his possessions/animals, or to Achan's family as well. This sits in tension with the principle later stated in Deuteronomy 24:16 that children should not be put to death for a parent's sin.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish which reading is correct; it establishes only that the grammar of the received Hebrew/KJV text permits both readings, which is itself a meaningful and often under-acknowledged interpretive fact.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The plural pronouns in v.25 may refer back only to the previously listed inanimate objects and animals, with the children present merely as witnesses to the execution, consistent with Deuteronomy 24:16.
- Ancient Israelite/broader ANE legal custom could treat a household as a single corporate legal unit under its patriarch, making the family liable together for his covenant violation, consistent with a plain reading that 'them' includes the family.
