# Historical-Contextual Correspondence in Hosea 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hosea 13:16 threatens Samaria with desolation and military defeat; 2 Kings 17 later narrates the Assyrian siege and capture of Samaria and the exile of Israel. This is a strong historical-contextual correspondence at the level of the city's defeat, though 2 Kings 17:5-6 does not verify every atrocity described in Hosea 13:16.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the precise date of the oracle, predictive fulfillment, or the historicity of every violent detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle could have been composed or edited with knowledge of Samaria's fall.
- Military destruction was a conventional prophetic warning and need not refer exclusively to the final Assyrian siege.
- The violent details may be conventional ancient war rhetoric rather than a report of individually documented acts.
