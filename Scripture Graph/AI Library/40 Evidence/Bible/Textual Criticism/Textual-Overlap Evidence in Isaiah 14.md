# Textual-Overlap Evidence in Isaiah 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 14 has a complete chapter-level English textual parallel in 2 Nephi 24. This is strong evidence of a direct textual relationship at the English-text level and makes the two chapters suitable for close comparison.

**Does not establish:** The English overlap alone does not establish when the underlying Book of Mormon passage originated, whether it is textually independent, or which translation mechanism produced it.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallel may reflect direct use of the King James Bible in producing the Book of Mormon's English text.
- A translation model may intentionally render an ancient Isaiah quotation in familiar King James diction.
- The relationship may combine a claimed ancient quotation with modern English biblical phrasing.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 14]], [[2 Nephi 24]]
