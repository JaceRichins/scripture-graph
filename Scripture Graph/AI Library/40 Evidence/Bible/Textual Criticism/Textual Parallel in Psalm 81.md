# Textual Parallel in Psalm 81

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verses 9-10 closely echo the opening of the Decalogue: 'There shall no strange god be in thee...I am the LORD thy God, which brought thee out of the land of Egypt' parallels the preamble and first commandment as given in Exodus 20:2-3 and Deuteronomy 5:6-7 (confirmed by the mechanically verified text-overlap parallels for this chapter).

**Does not establish:** Does not establish literary priority (whether the psalm quotes a written Exodus/Deuteronomy text or vice versa), nor the psalm's date relative to the Pentateuch's final form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The shared wording may reflect a common ancient Israelite covenant/treaty formula circulating in oral or liturgical form, rather than direct citation of a fixed written Decalogue text.
- The overlap could arise because both the Decalogue and this psalm independently draw on a stock exodus-deliverance formula used across multiple genres.
