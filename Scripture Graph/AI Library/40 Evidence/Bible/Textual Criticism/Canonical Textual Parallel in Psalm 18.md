# Canonical Textual Parallel in Psalm 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Psalm 18 has an extensive textual parallel in 2 Samuel 22; the verified overlap index identifies forty-two parallel verses, including corresponding declarations that the LORD is the speaker's rock and corresponding vows of thanksgiving among the heathen.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish the original form, precise date of composition, direction of literary dependence, or whether every difference is intentional.

**Alternative explanations:**
- One canonical form may have been adapted from the other.
- Both forms may derive from a shared earlier song.
- Differences may reflect scribal transmission, editorial adaptation, or differing liturgical settings.
