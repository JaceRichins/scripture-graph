# Intratextual Narrative Evidence in Luke 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Luke's presentation, the combination of the healing and the animal-rescue analogy strongly supports reading this episode as a defense of merciful action on the Sabbath.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Jesus abolished Sabbath observance, that every form of work is permitted on the Sabbath, or that every historical detail can be independently verified.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The exchange may address a disputed boundary within Sabbath observance rather than contrasting mercy with Sabbath law itself.
- The analogy directly justifies this compassionate intervention but does not specify rules for every kind of Sabbath labor.
