# Explicit Textual Argument in Romans 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit enumeration of commandments followed by the neighbor-love command provides strong evidence that Paul interprets love as the ethical unity and fulfillment of the law's duties toward other people.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that love is merely a feeling, that concrete commandments are unnecessary, or that Romans 13 gives a complete theory of the law.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Fulfillment may mean obeying the law's intended purpose rather than summarizing every possible dimension of the law.
- The immediate argument may concern specifically neighbor-directed commandments rather than supplying an exhaustive account of all religious obligations.
