# Internal Textual Sequence in Leviticus 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated sequence in which a plague is first described as healed and its subject is afterward ritually cleansed is strong evidence for distinguishing physical resolution from priestly purification and communal restoration within this chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not identify the underlying disease, prove that the rite had no perceived therapeutic dimension, or establish a modern separation between medicine and religion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The vocabulary may mark successive degrees of one purity process rather than wholly independent medical and ritual categories.
- The English terms in the KJV may not preserve every nuance of the underlying terminology.

**Scripture:** [[Leviticus 14#^lev-14-3|Leviticus 14:3-8]], [[Leviticus 14#^lev-14-48|Leviticus 14:48-53]]
