# Internal Textual Evidence in 1 Corinthians 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The paired language of receiving and delivering, together with the compact sequence in verses 3-7, supports the interpretation that Paul is transmitting an established resurrection tradition rather than presenting the message as his own invention.

**Does not establish:** This wording does not establish the precise age, source, original form, or historical accuracy of every reported appearance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Receiving and delivering can describe teaching generally and do not by themselves prove that the entire passage was a fixed creed.
- Paul may have shaped inherited material substantially for his argument.
- An inherited tradition is not necessarily independent of the people whose claims it reports.

**Scripture:** [[1 Corinthians 15#^1cor-15-3|1 Corinthians 15:3-7]]
