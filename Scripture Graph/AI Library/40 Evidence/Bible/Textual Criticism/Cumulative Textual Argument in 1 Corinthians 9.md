# Cumulative Textual Argument in 1 Corinthians 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of occupational analogies, Mosaic law, spiritual sowing, temple practice, and an ordinance attributed to the Lord provides strong textual evidence that Paul regards material support for gospel workers as legitimate.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish one mandatory financial system for all churches, ministers, or historical circumstances.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The exact form, amount, and institutional arrangement of support may vary by setting.
- Paul's argument establishes a legitimate entitlement without requiring every gospel worker to exercise it.
