# Textual-Scope in Deuteronomy 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In the supplied canonical wording, verses 8–9 connect the division and boundaries of nations with the children of Israel and identify Jacob as the LORD's portion. These verses do not explicitly state that individual Israelites were known in a premortal existence.

**Does not establish:** This observation does not disprove premortal life; it only shows that the doctrine is not stated explicitly by the supplied wording of Deuteronomy 32:8–9.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A premortal interpretation may arise from reading these verses through other scriptures or a broader theological framework.
- Interpretations of verse 8 can also depend on textual variants and translation choices not displayed in the supplied KJV text.
