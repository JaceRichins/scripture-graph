# Canonical Intertextual Reuse in Psalm 109

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 1:20 explicitly presents wording from the Psalms and applies Psalm 109:8's instruction that another take the condemned person's office to the replacement of Judas. This is strong evidence for early Christian reuse of the verse in interpreting Judas and apostolic succession, while leaving the original historical referent of Psalm 109 unresolved.

**Does not establish:** This reuse does not establish that every curse in Psalm 109 directly predicts Judas, nor does it independently establish the psalm's authorship or date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Acts may be making a typological or analogical application rather than claiming that Judas was the sole person originally intended by Psalm 109.
- The application may depend on a broad pattern of rejected officeholders rather than a one-to-one predictive oracle.
