# Literary Intertextuality in Jeremiah 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentrated correspondences between Jeremiah 4:23-26 and the creation account provide strong internal evidence that the devastation is depicted as a rhetorical de-creation, not merely as ordinary battlefield damage.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish a precise compositional date, a complete verse-by-verse reversal of Genesis 1, or any conclusion about authorship beyond the literary relationship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The images may arise from conventional descriptions of wartime devastation rather than a deliberate, detailed allusion to Genesis.
- Some correspondences may reflect shared Hebrew cosmological vocabulary without requiring a formal reversal of the creation sequence.
