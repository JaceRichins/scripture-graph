# Literary Intertextuality in Revelation 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of multiple specific correspondences with Isaiah and Ezekiel is strong evidence that Revelation 21 deliberately reworks earlier prophetic visions of Zion, restoration, and divine presence, rather than merely sharing one isolated image with them.

**Does not establish:** These correspondences do not establish whether the city and its dimensions are literal, the precise mode of prophetic fulfillment, or a date for that fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author and earlier prophets may draw on a common stock of ancient temple and royal-city imagery.
- Some individual parallels could be coincidental even if the cumulative pattern is deliberate.
- The author may work from remembered liturgical or oral forms rather than directly from written copies of Isaiah and Ezekiel.
