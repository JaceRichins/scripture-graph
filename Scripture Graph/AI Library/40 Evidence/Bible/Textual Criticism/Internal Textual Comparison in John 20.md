# Internal Textual Comparison in John 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus tells Mary 'Touch me not; for I am not yet ascended to my Father' (John 20:17), yet only about a week later, before any ascension has occurred in the narrative, he explicitly invites Thomas to 'reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side' (John 20:27). Read together, these two statements are in tension if v17 is understood as a general, standing prohibition on touching the resurrected body.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish the precise force of the Greek behind 'touch me not,' which this analysis has not independently verified; it only shows that a flat universal reading creates friction with v27.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The instruction to Mary may target a specific act (e.g., clinging to or detaining him) rather than touch as such, a nuance debated among translators of the underlying wording
- The two episodes may simply reflect different purposes (Mary urged to go tell the brethren without delay vs. Thomas specifically needing tactile confirmation) rather than a genuine contradiction
