# Intratextual Narrative Evidence in John 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within John's narrative, Jesus' foreknowledge and initiative are strong textual evidence that his surrender is presented as voluntary obedience rather than merely helpless capture.

**Does not establish:** This narrative pattern does not by itself establish the historical details of the arrest or prove metaphysical claims about omniscience.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The presentation may primarily express the evangelist's theology and literary design rather than provide independent historical evidence about Jesus' inner state.
- Foreknowledge and initiative do not mean that the arrest involved no real coercion or suffering.
