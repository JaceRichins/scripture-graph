# Canonical Textual Parallel in Matthew 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 6 and 3 Nephi 13 have extensive verified verbal overlap, strongly supporting a close literary relationship between the two canonical chapters.

**Does not establish:** Verbal overlap alone does not determine the historical direction, mechanism, or date of transmission, nor does it independently establish the historicity or origin of 3 Nephi.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Within the Book of Mormon narrative, the similarity represents Jesus teaching closely related material to another audience.
- A literary-critical explanation may attribute the wording to direct dependence on the King James form of Matthew.
- A shared-source model could explain some common material, although the breadth and wording of the overlap require fuller comparison.

**Scripture:** [[Matthew 6]], [[3 Nephi 13]]
