# Intratextual Command-Fulfillment Correspondence in Exodus 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed correspondence between these commands and the construction account in Exodus 37 provides strong evidence that the book deliberately pairs divine instruction with reported execution. It supports literary coherence and the theme of obedient implementation, while not independently proving the account's date, authorship, or historicity.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish Mosaic authorship, a second-millennium BCE date, or that every described object was historically constructed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An author or editor could have composed the execution account from the instructions.
- The instructions could have been shaped retrospectively to match a known sanctuary tradition.
- The correspondence may reflect literary organization rather than an independently preserved construction record.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 25#^ex-25-10|Exodus 25:10-40]], [[Exodus 37#^ex-37-1|Exodus 37:1-24]]
