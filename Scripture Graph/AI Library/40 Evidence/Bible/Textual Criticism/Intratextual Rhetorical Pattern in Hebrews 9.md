# Intratextual Rhetorical Pattern in Hebrews 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated contrast between annual or repeated offering and the word 'once' provides strong internal literary evidence that the finality and non-repeatability of Christ's self-offering are central to the chapter's argument.

**Does not establish:** The verbal pattern alone does not independently establish the historical or metaphysical efficacy of Christ's sacrifice.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetitions may arise naturally from the comparison with an annual ritual rather than from a formally designed literary pattern.
- The repetition establishes the author's emphasis but does not by itself prove the theological claims attached to it.
