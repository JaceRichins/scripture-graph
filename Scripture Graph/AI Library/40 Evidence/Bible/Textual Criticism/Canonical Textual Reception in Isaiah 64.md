# Canonical Textual Reception in Isaiah 64

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 133:40–42 closely reproduces the opening petition and imagery of Isaiah 64:1–2 within an explicitly latter-day scene of the Lord's coming. This is strong evidence for Isaiah 64's canonical reception as Second Coming language in Latter-day Saint scripture, but it does not establish that the historical sense of Isaiah 64 was exclusively eschatological.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the date, authorship, or exclusive historical referent of Isaiah 64, nor that every detail describes only the Second Coming.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later revelation may be reapplying familiar scriptural language typologically rather than identifying Isaiah's sole original referent.
- Some shared wording reflects direct use of the King James English text and therefore does not independently establish a prediction-fulfillment relationship.
