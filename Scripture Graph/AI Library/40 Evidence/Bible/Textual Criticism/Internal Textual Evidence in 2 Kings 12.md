# Internal Textual Evidence in 2 Kings 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The collection chest, joint royal-priestly counting, designated overseers, specified expenditures, and separate priestly revenues provide strong internal textual evidence that the account depicts differentiated financial roles and categories. They moderately support reading stewardship and institutional accountability as important narrative concerns.

**Does not establish:** These details do not independently establish the account's date, archaeological historicity, or the effectiveness of comparable institutions outside this episode.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The administrative detail may reflect literary shaping rather than a complete record of actual procedure.
- Some details could reflect the practices or terminology of an editor's period rather than precisely those of Jehoash's reign.
- The faithful conduct of the fund handlers does not explain or exonerate the earlier failure to repair the temple.
