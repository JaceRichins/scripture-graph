# Internal Textual Juxtaposition in Luke 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Luke 17 supplies strong internal evidence for a kingdom discourse containing both present and future dimensions: the kingdom cannot be located by ordinary public observation and is described as “within you,” while the Son of man will later be revealed in an unmistakable day.

**Does not establish:** This juxtaposition does not by itself establish a complete chronology or systematic doctrine of the kingdom.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The saying translated “within you” may be understood corporately or as the kingdom being among the hearers rather than inside each individual.
- The present and future sayings may describe different aspects of one kingdom without implying a formal theological scheme.
- Some readers understand nearly the entire passage eschatologically.
