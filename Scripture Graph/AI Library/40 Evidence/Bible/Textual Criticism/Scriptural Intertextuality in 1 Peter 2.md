# Scriptural Intertextuality in 1 Peter 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of Exodus- and Hosea-like language is strong evidence that the author intentionally frames the Christian audience with scriptural language associated with God's covenant people.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish a comprehensive doctrine of Israel's replacement, nor does it settle every question about the identity or ethnicity of the recipients.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author may be drawing on established early Christian liturgical or catechetical language rather than independently composing the scriptural synthesis.
- The language can express inclusion in covenant identity without implying the complete displacement of ethnic Israel.
