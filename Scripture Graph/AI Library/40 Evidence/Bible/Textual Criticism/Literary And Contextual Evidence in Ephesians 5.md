# Literary And Contextual Evidence in Ephesians 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of mutual submission immediately before the instructions to wives and husbands is substantial textual evidence that the household instructions should be read within a wider ethic of deference to others under God.

**Does not establish:** The placement alone does not settle modern debates over egalitarian and hierarchical models of marriage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Verse 21 may conclude the preceding description of Spirit-filled congregational life rather than govern every following household instruction.
- Mutual submission may describe differentiated service within an asymmetrical order rather than identical obligations or authority.

**Scripture:** [[Ephesians 5#^eph-5-21|Ephesians 5:21]], [[Ephesians 5#^eph-5-22|Ephesians 5:22]], [[Ephesians 5#^eph-5-25|Ephesians 5:25]]
