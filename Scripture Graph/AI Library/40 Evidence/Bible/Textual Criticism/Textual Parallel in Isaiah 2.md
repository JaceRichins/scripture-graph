# Textual Parallel in Isaiah 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close verbal and thematic correspondence between Isaiah's vision and Micah's parallel is strong evidence that Isaiah 2:2–4 and Micah 4:1–3 share a literary tradition. It is not sufficient evidence for determining which passage is earlier or whether either author borrowed directly from the other.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish priority, the identity of the original speaker, or a precise date for the shared material.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Micah borrowed from Isaiah.
- Isaiah borrowed from Micah.
- Both passages drew upon an earlier shared oracle or liturgical tradition.
- Later transmission or redaction brought the passages into closer alignment.
