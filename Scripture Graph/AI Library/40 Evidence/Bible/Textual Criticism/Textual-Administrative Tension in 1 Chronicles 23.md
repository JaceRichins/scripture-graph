# Textual-Administrative Tension in 1 Chronicles 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 3 numbers Levites from age thirty, whereas verses 24 and 27 describe eligibility or numbering from age twenty. The juxtaposition is strong evidence that the chapter preserves two differently framed counts or service thresholds, but the text does not fully identify their relationship.

**Does not establish:** The difference does not by itself establish a contradiction, a copying error, or a particular theory of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The thirty-year count and twenty-year rule may concern different grades or kinds of work.
- David may have lowered the eligibility age when transport duties ended.
- Verse 3 may preserve an earlier census while verses 24 and 27 report a later policy.
- The differing thresholds may reflect distinct source or editorial traditions.
