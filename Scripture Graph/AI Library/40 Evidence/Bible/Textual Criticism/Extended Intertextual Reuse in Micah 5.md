# Extended Intertextual Reuse in Micah 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained verbal correspondence establishes that 3 Nephi 20-21 presents Micah 5's remnant and judgment language within Jesus's covenant discourse concerning the remnant of Jacob and the Gentiles.

**Does not establish:** Verbal dependence alone does not independently establish a particular modern fulfillment, the identity of every remnant group, or the historical provenance of the Book of Mormon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence demonstrates literary reuse but does not by itself settle how or when that textual relationship arose.
- The 3 Nephi setting interprets and adapts Micah rather than necessarily preserving every element of Micah's original historical application.
