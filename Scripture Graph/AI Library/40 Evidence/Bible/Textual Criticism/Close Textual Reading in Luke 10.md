# Close Textual Reading in Luke 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The wording supports a cautious reading that Jesus addresses Martha's distracted, anxious condition and her demand concerning Mary, rather than issuing a general condemnation of service itself.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish that practical service is spiritually inferior in every setting, nor does it fully explain what the one needful thing denotes.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jesus may be asserting the priority of hearing his word over hospitality work in this particular moment.
- The phrase about one needful thing may contrast a simple provision with Martha's elaborate preparations.
- Some readers understand the episode as a broader contrast between contemplative attention and active service.
