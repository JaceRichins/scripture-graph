# New Testament Textual Criticism in Acts 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 8:37, including the Ethiopian's confession that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, is present in the canonical text supplied here but absent from several of the earliest and strongest Greek witnesses. Textual critics generally regard it as a later expansion reflecting an early baptismal confession; interpretation of the chapter should therefore avoid depending on this verse alone.

**Does not establish:** Its probable secondary origin does not establish that the confession is theologically false, that it arose very late, or that belief in Jesus was unconnected with early Christian baptism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A minority position could attribute the verse's absence to accidental omission or deliberate shortening in an early textual stream.
- Even if secondary to Acts, the confession may preserve an early Christian baptismal tradition.
