# Explicit Contextual Identification in Hebrews 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from “our Lord” of Judah, to the enduring priest, to the named Jesus provides strong internal evidence that the chapter's new Melchisedec-order priest is Jesus Christ.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the historical Melchisedec and Jesus are the same individual; verse 3 instead states that Melchisedec was made like the Son.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A reader might distinguish some descriptions of Melchisedec from their ultimate application to Jesus, but the named identification in verse 22 remains explicit.
