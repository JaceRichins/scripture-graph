# Canonical Intertextual Reuse in Proverbs 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 12:20 reuses the commands to feed and give drink to an enemy together with the image of heaping coals of fire upon the enemy's head. This is strong evidence that Proverbs 25:21-22 became part of early Christian ethical instruction.

**Does not establish:** The reuse does not establish a single uncontested meaning for the coals of fire, nor does it settle questions about the authorship or compilation history of Proverbs 25.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Romans may quote a Greek textual form of Proverbs rather than the later wording represented by the King James Version.
- Paul could employ a recognized scriptural saying without committing to only one interpretation of the coals metaphor.
