# Canonical Intertextual Reception in Isaiah 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
First Peter explicitly quotes Isaiah 28:16 and applies the chosen, precious cornerstone to Christ; Romans also incorporates Isaiah 28:16 into a composite stone quotation. This is strong canonical evidence for the Christian interpretation of Isaiah's stone.

**Does not establish:** The New Testament application does not by itself establish that Isaiah's first audience understood the stone exclusively as a conscious prediction of Jesus Christ.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The New Testament authors may be making a typological or christological rereading rather than identifying the sole historical referent intended in Isaiah's immediate setting.
- In its original horizon, the foundation stone may symbolize God's reliable work in Zion, a faithful community, a ruler, or an institution rather than an exclusively personal messianic figure.
