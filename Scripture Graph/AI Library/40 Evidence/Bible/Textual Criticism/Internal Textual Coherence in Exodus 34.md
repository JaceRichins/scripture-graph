# Internal Textual Coherence in Exodus 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit continuity between the first and replacement tablets, together with renewed covenant language, is strong internal textual evidence that Exodus 34 portrays restoration of a covenant relationship after the rupture associated with the broken tablets.

**Does not establish:** This internal coherence does not establish the event's external historicity, a particular date or theory of composition, or exact identity between every law in Exodus 34 and the wording of Exodus 20.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The present narrative may integrate covenant materials with distinct compositional histories.
- The phrase 'I make a covenant' could emphasize a fresh covenant act rather than restoration alone.
