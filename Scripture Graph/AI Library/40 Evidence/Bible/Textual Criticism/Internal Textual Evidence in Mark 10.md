# Internal Textual Evidence in Mark 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mark 10:45 is strong internal textual evidence that the narrative presents Jesus's impending death as purposeful service benefiting others through a ransom metaphor, rather than merely as an unforeseen victimization. The precise mechanism implied by the metaphor remains open to interpretation.

**Does not establish:** This verse alone does not establish every element of penal substitution, satisfaction, Christus Victor, or another comprehensive theory of atonement.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The ransom language may emphasize liberation at great cost without specifying a fully developed theory of atonement.
- Interpreters disagree about whether the saying directly evokes Isaiah 53 or draws more generally on deliverance and servitude imagery.
- The scope and nuance of the expression translated 'for many' are debated.
