# TextualNumeric Variant Between Parallel Accounts in 1 Kings 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
1 Kings 4:26 states Solomon had 'forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots,' a figure ten times larger than the parallel notice of four thousand stalls given for the same king in the Chronicler's account (2 Chronicles 9:25), a well-known numeric discrepancy between the two administrative summaries of Solomon's reign.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself determine which number is more original or historically accurate; establishes only that the two parallel notices disagree numerically.

**Alternative explanations:**
- One number could be a scribal/copying error (e.g., confusion of numeral notation) rather than reflecting two genuinely different traditions.
- The two books could be counting different things (e.g., stalls versus horses, or different points in the reign) rather than disagreeing about the same quantity.
