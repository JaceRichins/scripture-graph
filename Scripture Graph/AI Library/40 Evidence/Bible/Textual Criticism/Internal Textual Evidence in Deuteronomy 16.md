# Internal Textual Evidence in Deuteronomy 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The prohibition on sacrificing Passover within local gates, combined with the repeated chosen-place formula, is strong textual evidence for a program of centralized festival sacrifice.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the sanctuary's identity, the legislation's date, its relationship to particular reforms, or the extent of actual observance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The legislation may describe an ideal program without demonstrating uniform historical compliance.
- The unnamed chosen place need not, from this chapter alone, be identified with Jerusalem.
- A centralized sanctuary requirement does not by itself determine when the text was composed.
