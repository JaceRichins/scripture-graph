# Doctrinal-Textual in Hebrews 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hebrews 7:25 states directly that Christ 'is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them,' making this verse a primary New Testament textual basis for the doctrine of Christ's ongoing intercessory role within the Atonement.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself define the mechanics of intercession or settle disputes about the timing/nature of Christ's ongoing mediatorial work.

**Alternative explanations:**
- 'To the uttermost' could be read temporally ('completely, forever') rather than as a statement about the scope of persons saved; both readings are attested among commentators and do not change the intercession claim itself.
