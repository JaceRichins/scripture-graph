# Intratextual Temporal And Geographic Evidence in Mark 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The opening temple prediction, the disciples' question about when these things will occur, the instructions directed to people in Judæa, and the saying about this generation together provide substantial intratextual evidence that at least part of the discourse addresses a crisis within a comparatively near historical horizon.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every element in Mark 13 was exhausted by one ancient event, nor does it identify a precise fulfillment date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The localized language may describe a future Judean crisis rather than a first-century event.
- The discourse may telescope a near crisis and a final coming without sharply separating them.
- The phrase this generation may be interpreted as the generation that sees the signs, as a people or lineage, or in another non-immediate sense.
