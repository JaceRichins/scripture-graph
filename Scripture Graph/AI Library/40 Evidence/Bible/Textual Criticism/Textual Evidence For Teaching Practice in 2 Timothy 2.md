# Textual Evidence For Teaching Practice in 2 Timothy 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit sequence from received teaching to faithful teachers and then to others is strong evidence that the chapter envisions deliberate, reproducible transmission of instruction across more than one generation of teachers.

**Does not establish:** By itself, the verse does not establish a particular later system of ordination, institutional succession, or church government.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verse may describe a practical teaching strategy without defining a formal ecclesiastical office.
- The sequence can support several models of ministerial training and authority.

**Scripture:** [[2 Timothy 2#^2tim-2-2|2 Timothy 2:2]]
