# Canonical Intertextual Reception in Exodus 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The reuse of covenant-blood language in Matthew 26:28, Luke 22:20, and Hebrews 9:18–22 is strong evidence that later Christian authors received and reapplied the language and ritual of Exodus 24.

**Does not establish:** This reception does not establish that every detail of Exodus 24 was consciously composed as a prediction of the Last Supper or that the original audience understood later Christian applications.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later authors may be drawing on a wider scriptural and Jewish covenant vocabulary rather than only on Exodus 24.
- The later texts reinterpret inherited language within new settings; verbal reuse does not require identical ritual meaning.
