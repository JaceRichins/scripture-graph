# Internal Textual Evidence in Colossians 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Colossians 1:16-17 is strong internal textual evidence that this chapter assigns the Son a cosmic role encompassing the origin, purpose, priority, and continuing coherence of creation.

**Does not establish:** These verses alone do not establish every element of later creedal formulations, settle the precise metaphysics of the Godhead, or prove a particular account of the passage's compositional history.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may be poetic or hymnic and therefore should not automatically be converted into a complete metaphysical system.
- Some interpreters understand 'all things' chiefly in relation to cosmic powers or the renewed order, although the explicit references to heaven, earth, visible, and invisible favor a comprehensive scope.
- Different theological traditions integrate this language with their doctrines of divine identity and premortal existence in different ways.
