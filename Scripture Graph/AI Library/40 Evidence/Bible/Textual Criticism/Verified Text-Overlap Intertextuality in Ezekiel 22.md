# Verified Text-Overlap Intertextuality in Ezekiel 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 28's charge that the prophets 'have daubed them with untempered mortar, seeing vanity, and divining lies' unto the people 'when the LORD hath not spoken' verbally overlaps the sustained condemnation of false prophets in Ezekiel 13, where prophets are likewise accused of daubing a flimsy wall with 'untempered morter.' The verified text-overlap index flags a shared-verse relationship between these two chapters.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which chapter was composed first or that either passage is quoting the other in a literary sense; only that the same author uses matching imagery for the same charge (false, self-generated prophecy).

**Alternative explanations:**
- The shared 'daubing with untempered mortar' idiom could be Ezekiel's recurring personal idiolect for false prophecy rather than a deliberate cross-reference meant to be noticed by a reader.
