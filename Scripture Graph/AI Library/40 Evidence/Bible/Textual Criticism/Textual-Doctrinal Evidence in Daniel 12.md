# Textual-Doctrinal Evidence in Daniel 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The imagery of people sleeping in the dust and then awakening, followed by differentiated destinies, is strong textual evidence that Daniel 12 presents an individual postmortem resurrection and judgment rather than merely national restoration.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish the complete sequence, universality, or temporal separation of resurrections.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Resurrection language elsewhere can function metaphorically for collective restoration, although the opposed individual outcomes here make a solely national metaphor less persuasive.
- The word "many" leaves the intended scope of those raised open to debate.

**Scripture:** [[Daniel 12#^dan-12-2|Daniel 12:2]], [[Daniel 12#^dan-12-13|Daniel 12:13]]
