# Textual Quotation And Reception in Isaiah 52

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Third Nephi 20 deliberately reuses a long sequence from Isaiah 52, including the watchmen, Jerusalem's rejoicing, the revealed holy arm, Zion's awakening, departure under divine protection, and the servant's exaltation and suffering. This is strong evidence of intentional canonical reuse of Isaiah 52 in the Book of Mormon discourse.

**Does not establish:** The overlap does not by itself establish the Book of Mormon's date, ancient provenance, or an Isaiah textual form independent of the King James Bible.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The shared wording may reflect both texts' dependence on the English wording of the King James Version rather than an independently transmitted ancient textual form.
- The reuse demonstrates literary dependence or quotation but does not by itself determine the historical route by which the wording entered the Book of Mormon.
