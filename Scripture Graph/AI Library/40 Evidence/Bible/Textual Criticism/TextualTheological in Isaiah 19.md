# TextualTheological in Isaiah 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 25 applies covenant terminology normally reserved for Israel ('my people,' 'mine inheritance') to Egypt and Assyria as well, making this one of the most explicitly universalistic statements in the Hebrew prophets.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish that Israel's distinct covenant/election role is abolished or superseded; the verse still distinguishes Israel's title ('inheritance') from Egypt's ('people') and Assyria's ('work of my hands').

**Alternative explanations:**
- The threefold blessing could be read as rhetorical elevation of Egypt and Assyria to honor Israel's God rather than a literal claim of equal covenant status.
- Israel alone retains the distinct title 'mine inheritance,' suggesting a graded rather than flattened equality among the three nations.
