# Literary Intertextuality in Revelation 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of distinctive correspondences with Zechariah, Moses, Elijah, Ezekiel, and Daniel strongly supports deliberate reuse of Israel's prophetic imagery in the portrayal of the witnesses.

**Does not establish:** These correspondences do not establish the personal identities of the witnesses, the date of fulfillment, or whether the account should be read primarily as literal prediction or symbolic apocalypse.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some correspondences may arise from a shared stock of Jewish apocalyptic imagery rather than direct dependence on each cited passage.
- The imagery may characterize prophetic roles without identifying the witnesses personally as Moses, Elijah, or any other earlier prophets.
- Later readers may perceive a more unified network of allusions than the author consciously designed.
