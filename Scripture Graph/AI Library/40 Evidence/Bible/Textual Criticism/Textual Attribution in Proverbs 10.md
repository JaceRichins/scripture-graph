# Textual Attribution in Proverbs 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The superscription is strong evidence that the received text presents this collection under Solomon's name, but it is only limited evidence that Solomon personally composed every saying in the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish personal authorship, a date of composition, or the editorial history of the collection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The superscription could associate the collection with a Solomonic wisdom tradition rather than identify the author of every maxim.
- An editor could have placed older or later sayings beneath an inherited attribution.

**Scripture:** [[Proverbs 10#^prov-10-1|Proverbs 10:1]]
