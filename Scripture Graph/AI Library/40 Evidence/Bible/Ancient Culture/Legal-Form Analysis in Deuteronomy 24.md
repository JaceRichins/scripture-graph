# Legal-Form Analysis in Deuteronomy 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The conditional sequence in verses 1–4 is strong textual evidence that this passage regulates a remarriage case rather than presenting divorce itself as an unrestricted moral ideal. The law nevertheless presupposes a social system in which a husband can initiate divorce.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that divorce is morally approved in every circumstance, nor that the law gave husband and wife equal legal power.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The KJV wording of verse 1 can be read as an instruction to write a divorce document, although the complete case reaches its operative prohibition in verse 4.
- Regulating a practice can confer legal recognition on it even when the passage does not present it as an ethical ideal.
