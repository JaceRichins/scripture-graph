# Legal-Idiom Intertext in 2 Kings 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Elisha's request for a double portion plausibly uses the language of the firstborn heir's share in Deuteronomy 21:17. In this reading, he asks to be recognized as Elijah's principal prophetic heir, not necessarily to possess exactly twice Elijah's miraculous power.

**Does not establish:** The idiom does not establish that Elisha expected mathematically twice as many miracles, nor does it by itself define a formal ordination procedure.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Elisha may be requesting an unusually abundant measure of prophetic power without invoking inheritance law.
- The expression may deliberately combine inheritance and empowerment imagery.
