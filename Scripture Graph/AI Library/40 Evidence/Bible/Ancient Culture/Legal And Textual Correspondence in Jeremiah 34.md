# Legal And Textual Correspondence in Jeremiah 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah's six-year service and release rule closely aligns with Deuteronomy 15:12–18, particularly in applying the rule to both an Hebrew man and an Hebrew woman. This strongly supports reading the episode as enforcement of Israel's manumission law, although it does not by itself establish direct literary dependence in either direction.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the direction or date of literary dependence, nor prove that the release law had been regularly observed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah and Deuteronomy may independently reflect a shared legal tradition.
- The close wording could reflect later editorial shaping rather than direct use by the historical Jeremiah.
