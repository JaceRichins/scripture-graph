# Legal Intertext in 2 Kings 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit attribution of the individual-liability rule to the written law of Moses is strong evidence that the received narrative consciously frames Amaziah's conduct through Deuteronomy 24:16 or a closely aligned legal tradition.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the date or authorship of either book, nor independently prove that this was Amaziah's historical motive.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Kings and Deuteronomy may both preserve an older legal principle rather than one text directly quoting the other.
- A later compiler may have aligned the account with Deuteronomic wording.

**Scripture:** [[2 Kings 14#^2kgs-14-6|2 Kings 14:6]], [[Deuteronomy 24#^deut-24-16|Deuteronomy 24:16]]
