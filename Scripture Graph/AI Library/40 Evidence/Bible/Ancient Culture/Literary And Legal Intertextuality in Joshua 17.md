# Literary And Legal Intertextuality in Joshua 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit appeal to a command given through Moses is strong internal evidence that the narrator intends the conquest allotments to be read as continuous with the legal decisions of the wilderness narratives.

**Does not establish:** This internal cross-reference does not independently establish the historical date, authorship, or precise compositional sequence of Joshua and Numbers.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later writer or editor could have deliberately harmonized Joshua's allotment account with the legal tradition in Numbers.
- Literary continuity does not by itself show that the connected narratives were composed at the same time.
