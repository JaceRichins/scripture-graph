# Intertextual Covenant-Curse Correspondence in Lamentations 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Lamentations 2:17 says the Lord fulfilled an old warning, while verse 20 invokes mothers eating their children. The latter closely parallels the siege curse in Deuteronomy 28:53–57 and supports reading the chapter as interpreting Jerusalem's fall through covenant-warning traditions.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not establish a direct quotation, the identity of the author, or that every detail of Deuteronomy's curse was understood as fulfilled.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Cannibalism is a recurrent horror of ancient siege literature and need not be a deliberate citation of Deuteronomy.
- Leviticus 26:29 provides another covenant-curse background for the same image.
- Verse 17 may refer generally to inherited prophetic warnings rather than to one identifiable passage.
