# Legal Inclusion in Numbers 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 15 strongly supports equal access across the named status groups to this particular protection for unintentional killing.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish equal treatment in every area of Israelite law or erase distinctions among Israelites, strangers, and sojourners.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The provision may reflect the need for a workable homicide system for everyone living in the land rather than a general declaration of social equality.
