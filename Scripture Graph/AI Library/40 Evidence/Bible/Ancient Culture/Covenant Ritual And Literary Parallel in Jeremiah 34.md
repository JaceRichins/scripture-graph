# Covenant Ritual And Literary Parallel in Jeremiah 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The covenant-makers cut a calf and walk between its pieces. In its judgment context, the enacted rite is strong evidence for a self-maledictory covenant ceremony: those who violate the oath accept a fate analogous to that of the divided animal. Genesis 15 contains a structurally related passage-between-pieces covenant scene.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish direct dependence on Genesis 15 or determine the rite's complete historical form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage between the pieces may primarily ratify the covenant without every participant consciously articulating a self-curse.
- Jeremiah 34 and Genesis 15 may reflect a shared ritual convention rather than direct literary dependence.
