# Legal And Intertextual Context in Judges 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Numbers 6 supplies the clearest legal context for the word 'Nazarite': both passages associate consecration with avoiding products of the vine and leaving the hair uncut. Judges 13 nevertheless adapts the pattern by making the consecration prenatal and divinely announced and by extending restrictions to the mother.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish that every rule in Numbers 6 applied identically to Samson or his mother, nor do they establish the date or direction of literary dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Judges 13 may reflect a customary form of lifelong consecration that was not identical in every detail to the temporary vow regulated in Numbers 6.
- The narrative may selectively mention only the restrictions important to Samson's story rather than offering a complete legal specification.
