# Explicit Textual Legal Principle in Leviticus 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Leviticus 24 provides strong textual evidence that, for the offenses governed by this passage, resident outsiders and native Israelites were formally subject to the same stated law.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish complete social or religious equality between Israelites and every category of foreigner, nor does it demonstrate historical enforcement.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The declaration may concern the laws immediately under discussion rather than every aspect of Israelite civil, social, and ritual status.
- Formal equality in a legal formulation does not establish how consistently the principle was enforced in practice.

**Scripture:** [[Leviticus 24#^lev-24-16|Leviticus 24:16]], [[Leviticus 24#^lev-24-22|Leviticus 24:22]]
