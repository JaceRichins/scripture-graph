# Legal Procedure And Classification in Numbers 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed classification of killings and the congregation's judgment provide strong textual evidence that the law seeks to restrain retaliatory killing by requiring a distinction between murder and accidental homicide.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a modern presumption of innocence, a modern court system, or fairness in every historical application.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The institution regulates blood vengeance rather than attempting to abolish it.
- The refuge system may protect the wider community from an escalating feud as much as it protects the individual slayer.
- The surviving text does not show whether actual hearings consistently achieved fair outcomes.
