# Internal Theological And Legal Contrast in Numbers 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated sequence of ignorance, prescribed offering, priestly atonement, and forgiveness strongly supports the conclusion that this chapter presents divine provision for unintended covenant violations. The absence of such a provision in verses 30–31 marks a serious contrast with deliberate defiance.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every intentional sinner is eternally unforgivable or that repentance can never follow deliberate wrongdoing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The distinction may primarily classify ritual and communal legal remedies rather than provide a complete theology of forgiveness.
- Other biblical passages may address repentance after deliberate sins, so silence here should not be universalized.
