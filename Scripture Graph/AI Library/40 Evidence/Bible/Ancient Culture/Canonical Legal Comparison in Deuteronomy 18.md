# Canonical Legal Comparison in Deuteronomy 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Read with Deuteronomy 13, Deuteronomy's prophetic discernment requires more than successful prediction: nonfulfillment disqualifies a claimed word, while even a realized sign cannot legitimate teaching allegiance to other gods.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not supply a complete timeless procedure for evaluating every kind of prophetic utterance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Deuteronomy 13 addresses apostasy, whereas Deuteronomy 18 addresses presumptuous prediction; the laws may govern distinct cases rather than form a single formal protocol.
- Some prophetic warnings are conditional, so apparent nonfulfillment may require attention to the stated or implied conditions.
