# Literary-Legal Structure in Numbers 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The agreement contains a stated obligation, a condition for receiving land, a consequence for default, an explicit assent, and transmission of the terms to Eleazar, Joshua, and the tribal leaders. This is strong textual evidence that the chapter presents the grant as a public and enforceable intertribal commitment rather than an informal private concession.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not establish the episode's historicity, the date of composition, Mosaic authorship, or the divine authorization of the territorial conquest.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The legal form may be a literary presentation rather than a transcript of an actual agreement.
- Some or all of the administrative detail could reflect later editorial shaping.
- Public witnesses and sanctions are common narrative devices and do not by themselves determine the text's date.
