# Intertextual Legal Convergence in Ezekiel 44

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Ezekiel 44 shares multiple legal themes with Pentateuchal priestly material: abstention from wine during ministry, instruction concerning clean and unclean, restrictions involving marriage and the dead, priestly lack of territorial inheritance, and support from offerings. This is substantial evidence of participation in a shared Israelite priestly legal tradition.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not by themselves establish Mosaic authorship, Ezekiel's dependence on a completed Pentateuch, the direction of literary borrowing, or the precise date of either legal collection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ezekiel and the Pentateuchal laws may independently reflect a common priestly tradition.
- One body of law may have influenced the other, but thematic parallels alone do not establish the direction of dependence.
- Later editorial activity could have increased the verbal or conceptual alignment among these texts.
