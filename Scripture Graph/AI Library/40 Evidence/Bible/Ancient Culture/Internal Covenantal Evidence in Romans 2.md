# Internal Covenantal Evidence in Romans 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 2 strongly supports the claim that covenant signs and religious instruction do not shield transgressors from judgment. It qualifies rather than simply dismisses circumcision, saying that it profits when accompanied by keeping the law.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself abolish Jewish identity, deny the historical value of circumcision, or prove that the church has displaced Israel.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be advancing a temporary argumentative stage whose full conclusion comes later in Romans.
- The passage can be read as attacking false confidence in covenant markers rather than redefining covenant membership.
