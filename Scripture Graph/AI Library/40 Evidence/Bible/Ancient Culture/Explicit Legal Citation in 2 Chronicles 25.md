# Explicit Legal Citation in 2 Chronicles 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit appeal to a command written in the book of Moses is strong internal evidence that the narrative evaluates royal conduct by an authoritative written legal tradition rather than by royal convenience alone.

**Does not establish:** The citation alone does not prove when the episode was composed, that Amaziah personally read the legal text, or that the reported executions can be independently corroborated.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The legal explanation could have been supplied by a narrator or editor rather than spoken or consulted by Amaziah himself.
- The citation demonstrates the narrative's legal framework but cannot by itself date when that framework entered the account.

**Scripture:** [[2 Chronicles 25#^2chr-25-3|2 Chronicles 25:3]], [[2 Chronicles 25#^2chr-25-4|2 Chronicles 25:4]]
