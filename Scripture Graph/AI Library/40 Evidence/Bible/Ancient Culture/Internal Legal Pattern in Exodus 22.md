# Internal Legal Pattern in Exodus 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated distinctions among deliberate theft, accidental or negligent damage, unexplained loss, animal attack, borrowing, hiring, and an owner's presence provide strong internal evidence that this legal collection calibrates material responsibility rather than assigning one uniform consequence to every loss.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the system matches modern theories of proportional justice, nor does it establish the collection's date, authorship, or compositional unity.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some distinctions may preserve inherited case laws created at different times rather than a single systematic theory of liability.
- Practical custom and economic valuation may explain the variations without requiring a comprehensive theological principle.
