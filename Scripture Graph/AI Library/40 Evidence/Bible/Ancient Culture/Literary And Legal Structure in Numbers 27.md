# Literary And Legal Structure in Numbers 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from a named dispute, to referral for divine judgment, to a ruling, and then to a generalized inheritance statute is strong internal evidence that the episode functions literarily as a legal precedent.

**Does not establish:** This structure does not by itself establish the historical circumstances, date of composition, divine origin, or verbatim preservation of the proceeding.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The dispute narrative may be a later literary framework composed to explain an already existing inheritance rule.
- The episode may preserve a stylized or idealized adjudication rather than a verbatim account of a historical hearing.
