# Intertextual Legal Correspondence in Leviticus 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The description of a man having his father's wife in 1 Corinthians 5:1 closely corresponds to the relationship forbidden in Leviticus 18:8. This is strong evidence that the Corinthian case falls within a category recognized by the Levitical kinship law, though it is not by itself proof that Paul was directly quoting this verse.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every sanction in Leviticus 18 or 20 was transferred unchanged into the Christian congregation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may have appealed to a broadly recognized incest prohibition rather than consciously invoking Leviticus 18:8.
- The correspondence may reflect shared Jewish and wider Mediterranean kinship norms without constituting a quotation.
