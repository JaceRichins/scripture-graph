# TextualLegal-Structural in Leviticus 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit three-tier sliding scale (vv6-11) is offered as evidence that the Mosaic sacrificial system was deliberately structured with economic accommodation for the poor rather than a uniform cost that would exclude the destitute from atonement.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish that the sliding scale was framed by ancient Israelites themselves as a statement about social justice or equity; that framing is a modern interpretive lens applied to the observed structure.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The scaled offering could reflect general ancient Near Eastern ritual pragmatism (matching cost to what is available) rather than a deliberately articulated ethic of economic equity.
- The graduated scale may primarily serve priestly/administrative interests (ensuring some offering is always rendered) rather than a compassion-motivated policy toward the poor.

**Scripture:** [[Leviticus 5#^lev-5-7|Leviticus 5:7]], [[Leviticus 5#^lev-5-11|Leviticus 5:11]]
