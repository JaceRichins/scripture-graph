# Intratextual Legal Pattern in Numbers 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides strong intratextual evidence that its legal vision couples sacred privilege with duty rather than presenting priestly support as an isolated entitlement. Restricted access is paired with liability, offerings with prescribed purity, tithes with tabernacle labor, and the Levites' income with a further offering to the priests.

**Does not establish:** This textual coherence does not establish when the laws were composed, whether they were fully practiced, their divine origin, or their direct applicability to modern religious institutions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The arrangement may reflect an idealized or redactionally organized legal program rather than consistently implemented historical practice.
- The duties and dangers may legitimate priestly privilege as well as constrain it.
- Readers may disagree about whether the economic exchange was equitable in particular historical settings.
