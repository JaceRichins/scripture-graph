# Synoptic Literary Convergence in Luke 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Substantial agreement among Luke, Mark, and Matthew on the ruler episode, passion prediction, and Jericho healing supports the existence of stable shared narrative traditions or literary sources behind these passages. Because direct or indirect literary dependence can explain agreement, this convergence alone is only limited evidence for independent historical corroboration.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not by themselves prove eyewitness independence, the exact historical sequence, or harmonization of every differing detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew and Luke may derive much of the shared material directly or indirectly from Mark rather than from independent witnesses.
- Oral tradition could preserve a common core while permitting differing narrative details.
- Evangelists may have arranged or adapted inherited material for distinct theological and literary purposes.
