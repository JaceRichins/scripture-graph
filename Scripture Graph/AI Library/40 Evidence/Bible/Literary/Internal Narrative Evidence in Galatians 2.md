# Internal Narrative Evidence in Galatians 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Paul's argument, Titus's noncircumcision is direct narrative evidence that the Jerusalem encounter he describes did not make circumcision a requirement for this Greek believer.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish every detail of the Jerusalem leaders' position or settle how every early Christian community treated circumcision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Titus may have been treated as an individual test case rather than as the basis of a fully articulated universal policy.
- The account is Paul's compressed and rhetorically purposeful presentation of the meeting.

**Scripture:** [[Galatians 2#^gal-2-1|Galatians 2:1]], [[Galatians 2#^gal-2-3|Galatians 2:3]]
