# Internal Narrative Causation in 2 Chronicles 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 6 is direct evidence that the narrator interprets Jotham's might as resulting from his having prepared or ordered his ways before the LORD. It strongly establishes the narrator's stated theological explanation, but not the complete historical causation of Jotham's power.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that righteousness always produces political or military success, or that every aspect of Jotham's power can be historically attributed to this single cause.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statement may be a conventional theological summary characteristic of the Chronicler.
- Military organization, fortification, agricultural resources, diplomacy, or regional political conditions may also have contributed to Jotham's power.
- The narrator's causal interpretation need not be an exhaustive account of historical causes.
