# Literary Allusion in 2 Corinthians 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The description of God commanding light to shine out of darkness is strong internal textual evidence that the illumination of human hearts is being portrayed through creation imagery from Genesis 1:3.

**Does not establish:** The resemblance does not by itself establish a formal quotation, a particular compositional process, or any larger conclusion about authorship or date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording could employ broadly familiar Jewish creation imagery without intending a precise quotation.
- Light and darkness are common metaphors for knowledge and ignorance, although the reference to God's command makes the Genesis connection especially likely.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 1#^gen-1-3|Genesis 1:3]], [[2 Corinthians 4#^2cor-4-6|2 Corinthians 4:6]]
