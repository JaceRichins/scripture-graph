# Internal Narrative Juxtaposition in Matthew 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The immediate sequence is strong internal textual evidence that Matthew presents Peter in a deliberately or functionally nuanced way: he receives revelation and promises, yet shortly afterward opposes Jesus's announced path and is sharply corrected.

**Does not establish:** This sequence does not by itself resolve later disputes about Peter's office, institutional succession, or the fallibility of church leaders.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The juxtaposition may partly reflect Matthew's arrangement of inherited traditions rather than a consciously designed character contrast.
- The rebuke may target Peter's particular objection rather than characterize his discipleship generally.
