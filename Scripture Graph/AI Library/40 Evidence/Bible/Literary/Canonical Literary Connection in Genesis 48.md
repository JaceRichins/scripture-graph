# Canonical Literary Connection in Genesis 48

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jacob's adoption of Ephraim and Manasseh supplies an internal narrative explanation for their later treatment as two tribes descended from Joseph.

**Does not establish:** The connection does not establish when the adoption tradition originated, the historicity of the ceremony, or direct dependence between the passages.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The adoption account may be an etiological story composed or shaped to explain an already-existing tribal arrangement.
- The texts may preserve distinct traditions later organized into a continuous ancestral history.
