# Literary Structure And Symbolic Action in 1 Kings 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated repetition of 'rend,' the twelve-piece garment, and the assignment of ten pieces provides strong evidence that Ahijah's action is an enacted prophecy designed to interpret the coming political division.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not establish when the episode was composed or whether every narrative detail can be independently reconstructed historically.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account could preserve an originally independent prophetic sign later integrated into the Solomon narrative.
- The symbolism identifies division without explaining every detail of the later tribal configuration.
