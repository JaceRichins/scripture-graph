# Literary Structure in Ezekiel 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated nautical vocabulary, the concentration of the trade catalogue before the shipwreck, and the subsequent shore-side mourning strongly support reading the chapter as a deliberately organized movement from construction and abundance to collapse and lament.

**Does not establish:** The literary coherence does not by itself determine the chapter's date, compositional history, or the historical accuracy of every commercial detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The precise boundaries assigned to the sections can vary even if the general progression is clear.
- Some repetition may arise from traditional lament or trading-list material incorporated into the chapter.
