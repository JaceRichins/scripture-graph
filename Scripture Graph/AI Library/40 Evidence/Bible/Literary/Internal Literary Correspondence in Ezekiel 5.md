# Internal Literary Correspondence in Ezekiel 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The correspondence between the three portions of hair in the sign-act and the three portions of Jerusalem's population in the explanation is strong internal evidence that the hair represents people facing distinct modes of judgment.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish exact casualty percentages, the precise historical fulfillment of each portion, or that every detail of the enacted sign has an independent symbolic referent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The thirds may be schematic or rhetorical divisions rather than predictions of mathematically exact population proportions.
- The actions may classify forms of destruction without assigning every individual to only one historical fate.

**Scripture:** [[Ezekiel 5#^ezek-5-2|Ezekiel 5:2]], [[Ezekiel 5#^ezek-5-12|Ezekiel 5:12]]
