# Narrative Emphasis And Evaluative Formula in 1 Kings 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The worsening formulas applied to Omri and Ahab, followed by details of Ahab's Baal worship, support the interpretation that the narrator evaluates kings principally by loyalty to the LORD rather than by military strength, building activity, or political durability.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Omri and Ahab lacked political accomplishments or that the narrator supplies a complete history of their reigns.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may presuppose political information known to its audience and therefore need not imply that political accomplishments were considered unimportant.
- The compressed notices may reflect the narrator's available sources as well as deliberate theological priorities.
