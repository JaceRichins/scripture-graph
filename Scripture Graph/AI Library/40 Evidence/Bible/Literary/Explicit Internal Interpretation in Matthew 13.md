# Explicit Internal Interpretation in Matthew 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus' explicit identification of the field as the world strongly constrains interpretations that treat the wheat and tares solely as a direct rule for administering membership discipline within a church.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that communities should ignore every form of wrongdoing, abandon moral discernment, or reject discipline taught in other contexts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The worldwide image may still have secondary implications for patience within religious communities.
- Readers may understand the kingdom's presence in the world as including the visible community of disciples.
- Other passages may authorize forms of correction or discipline even if this parable does not directly address them.
