# Internal Narrative Contrast in 1 Kings 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The reported numbers and the image of Israel as two little flocks against a country-filling army provide strong evidence for an intended narrative contrast between Israel's apparent weakness and Syria's scale. They are not, by themselves, independent verification of the historical troop totals.

**Does not establish:** The figures do not establish the exact historical size of either army or casualty count.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The large totals may be conventional military hyperbole.
- The figures may have been altered during textual transmission.
- They may preserve genuine administrative totals whose modern interpretation remains uncertain.
