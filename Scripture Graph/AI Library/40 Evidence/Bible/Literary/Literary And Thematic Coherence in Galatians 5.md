# Literary And Thematic Coherence in Galatians 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of love, mutual relations, and the Spirit across both the doctrinal and ethical portions is strong textual evidence that Paul's discussion of faith and freedom is integrated with his account of communal conduct: faith works through love, liberty serves through love, and walking in the Spirit produces love while resisting rivalry.

**Does not establish:** This coherence does not by itself establish authorship, date, historical circumstances, or a single intended outline.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetitions may arise from conventional early Christian exhortation rather than a deliberately patterned composition.
- The modern chapter boundary may make the unit appear more self-contained than it was within the whole letter.
