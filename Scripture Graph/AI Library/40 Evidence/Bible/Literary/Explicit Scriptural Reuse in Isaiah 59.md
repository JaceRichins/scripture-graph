# Explicit Scriptural Reuse in Isaiah 59

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 3:15–17 incorporates the language and sequence of Isaiah 59:7–8 into its scriptural indictment of universal human sin. This is strong evidence that an early Christian author read Isaiah's description as applicable beyond its immediate historical setting.

**Does not establish:** This reuse does not establish that Isaiah 59 originally referred only to universal human depravity or that its historical setting is irrelevant.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Romans presents a composite chain of scriptural texts, so its broader theological argument should not automatically be imposed on every detail of Isaiah 59.
- The reuse applies Isaiah's language to Paul's argument but does not erase the chapter's original concern with communal injustice.
