# Historical Correspondence in Matthew 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Roman destruction of Jerusalem's temple in CE 70 provides a strong historical referent for Matthew 24:2 and supports placing at least part of the discourse against a first-century Judean horizon.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not establish when the saying was composed, prove supernatural prediction, or show that every event in Matthew 24 was fulfilled in CE 70.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Gospel's final form may postdate CE 70, allowing the prediction to have been shaped in light of the event.
- Temple-destruction language belongs to an established prophetic tradition and need not depend exclusively on this one event.
- The surviving platform and retaining structures caution against treating "not one stone upon another" as an architectural inventory rather than forceful prophetic language.
