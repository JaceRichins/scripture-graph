# Internal Literary Correspondence in Genesis 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Genesis 25:16’s report of twelve princes corresponds directly to the earlier promise in Genesis 17:20 that Ishmael would father twelve princes. This is strong evidence of deliberate narrative continuity between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the genealogy’s historical accuracy, its date of composition, or a particular theory of authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An author or editor could have shaped the genealogy to correspond with the earlier promise.
- The number twelve may function schematically as a conventional representation of a complete tribal organization.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 17#^gen-17-20|Genesis 17:20]], [[Genesis 25#^gen-25-16|Genesis 25:16]]
