# Internal Chronological Marker in Isaiah 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The child-development deadline in Isaiah 7:16 is strong internal evidence that the sign has relevance to the political crisis narrated in the chapter: before the child reaches the stated level of discernment, the land associated with the two hostile kings will be forsaken. This does not, by itself, identify the child or exclude a later typological or messianic application.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the identity of the mother or child, the exact duration of the deadline, or whether the prophecy has only one fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The child's development may function symbolically rather than as a precise chronological calculation.
- The sign may address the house of David across a broader horizon while still using the contemporary crisis as its immediate setting.
- A later messianic fulfillment may be understood typologically rather than as the only referent.
