# Historical Identification in Daniel 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence from Greece through divided successor kingdoms to a ruler who persecutes the holy people and profanes the sanctuary strongly supports Antiochus IV Epiphanes as the principal historical referent of the little-horn figure. This identification is historically strong but remains an interpretation because Daniel 8 does not name Antiochus.

**Does not establish:** This identification does not establish that every detail is exhausted by Antiochus, determine the date or mode of the prophecy, or disprove possible typological applications.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some interpreters identify the horn chiefly with a still-future ruler because Gabriel associates the vision with the time of the end.
- A typological reading treats Antiochus as the immediate referent and also as a pattern of later anti-divine rulers.
- Some traditional interpretations apply parts of the vision to Rome or to a broader sequence of oppressive powers.
