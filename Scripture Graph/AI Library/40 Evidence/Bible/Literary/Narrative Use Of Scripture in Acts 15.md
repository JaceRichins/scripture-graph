# Narrative Use Of Scripture in Acts 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
James's appeal to the prophets provides strong internal evidence that Acts presents Gentile inclusion not merely as administrative accommodation but as consistent with God's scripturally disclosed purposes.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself prove a particular theory of predictive prophecy, textual transmission, or the historicity of every speech detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The quotation may primarily function as Luke's literary-theological framing rather than preserve James's speech verbatim.
- Readers differ over how precisely the prophecy's original setting maps onto the church's Gentile mission.
