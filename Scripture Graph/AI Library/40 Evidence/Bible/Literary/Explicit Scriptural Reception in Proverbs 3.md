# Explicit Scriptural Reception in Proverbs 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hebrews 12:5-6 reuses Proverbs 3:11-12 when explaining divine chastening, providing strong evidence that an early Christian author read this paternal correction as applicable to a community of believers.

**Does not establish:** This reception does not establish that every hardship is divine punishment or disclose the complete purpose behind any particular person's suffering.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording in Hebrews reflects a Greek textual form and should not be treated as a verbatim quotation of the later English KJV wording.
- Hebrews applies the proverb pastorally; that application does not settle every question about the proverb's original historical setting.
