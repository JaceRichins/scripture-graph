# Internal Numerical Coherence in 1 Chronicles 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Because the four occupational totals sum precisely to 38,000, the arithmetic strongly supports the conclusion that the categories were presented as an exhaustive, deliberately organized distribution of the census total.

**Does not establish:** The arithmetic does not establish that an actual census produced these exact numbers, that every Levite had only one assignment, or that the scheme originated in David's reign.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The totals may be schematic or idealized rather than precise historical census figures.
- A later writer or editor could have arranged inherited figures into a coherent administrative scheme.
- The figures may be rounded while still being designed to produce the stated total.
