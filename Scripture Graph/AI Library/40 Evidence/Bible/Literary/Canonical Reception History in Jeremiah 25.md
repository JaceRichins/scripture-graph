# Canonical Reception History in Jeremiah 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Daniel 9 and the closing narrative of 2 Chronicles explicitly use Jeremiah's seventy-year word to interpret Jerusalem's desolations, exile, and restoration; Ezra likewise associates Cyrus's decree with fulfillment of the word spoken by Jeremiah. This is strong evidence for the oracle's importance in later biblical interpretation.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the original date of composition, a single exact chronology for the seventy years, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Later authors or editors may have retrospectively organized remembered events around Jeremiah's seventy-year formulation.
- The passages may apply a shared prophetic tradition without requiring one universally agreed chronological starting point.
