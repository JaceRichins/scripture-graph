# Hebrew Poetic Form in Lamentations 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In Hebrew, Lamentations 2 is an alphabetic acrostic comprising twenty-two verse-stanzas. The expected order of the letters ayin and pe is reversed, with pe beginning verse 16 and ayin beginning verse 17. This formal organization is strong evidence of deliberate literary composition even though its symbolic meaning is uncertain.

**Does not establish:** The acrostic does not establish a particular author, date, ancient provenance by itself, or a single agreed symbolism for the alphabetic form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The acrostic may serve primarily as a mnemonic or conventional poetic framework.
- The reversed letter order may reflect an alternative alphabetic convention rather than symbolic disruption.
- Formal organization does not imply emotional distance or eyewitness authorship.
