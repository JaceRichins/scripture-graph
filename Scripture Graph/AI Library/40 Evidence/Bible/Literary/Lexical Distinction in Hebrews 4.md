# Lexical Distinction in Hebrews 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The underlying Greek uses sabbatismos in Hebrews 4:9, while the surrounding references to rest predominantly use katapausis or related forms. This lexical choice gives the remaining rest a genuine Sabbath resonance, although its precise implications are debated.

**Does not establish:** The word alone does not establish that Hebrews 4 commands seventh-day observance, abolishes such observance, or limits the promised rest to a weekly Sabbath.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Sabbatismos may evoke Sabbath celebration while principally describing eschatological participation in God's rest.
- The lexical variation may enrich the rest motif without prescribing a particular weekly observance.
- Creation rest, covenant worship, present salvation, and future consummation may overlap rather than function as mutually exclusive meanings.
