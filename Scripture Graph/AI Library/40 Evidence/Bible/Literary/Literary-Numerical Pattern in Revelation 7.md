# Literary-Numerical Pattern in Revelation 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated allocation of exactly twelve thousand to each of twelve named tribal groups is strong evidence of deliberate numerical design and moderate evidence that the total carries symbolic significance.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish that the number is nonliteral, identify the historical individuals involved, or prove a particular end-time chronology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The figures may describe a literal, divinely ordered census.
- A literal group could also be presented through stylized numbers.
- The pattern may primarily express military or cultic organization rather than the totality of all the saved.

**Scripture:** [[Revelation 7#^rev-7-4|Revelation 7:4-8]]
