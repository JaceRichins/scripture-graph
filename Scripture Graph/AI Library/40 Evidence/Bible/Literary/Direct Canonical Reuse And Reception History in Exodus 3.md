# Direct Canonical Reuse And Reception History in Exodus 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 22 and Mark 12 explicitly reuse God's identification as the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob from the bush episode in an argument about resurrection. This is strong evidence for the passage's reception in early Christian scripture, but not proof that resurrection was the primary subject intended by the Exodus narrator.

**Does not establish:** The later citation does not establish that the original Exodus passage was composed chiefly as a resurrection proof-text.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Synoptic argument may be a later interpretive extension of Exodus rather than its original literary purpose.
- Its force depends on Jewish interpretive assumptions about covenant relationship and the living God.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 3#^ex-3-6|Exodus 3:6]], [[Matthew 22#^matt-22-31|Matthew 22:31-32]], [[Mark 12#^mark-12-26|Mark 12:26-27]]
