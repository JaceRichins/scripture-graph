# Sustained Scriptural Allusion in 1 Peter 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of innocence, silence under mistreatment, bearing sins, healing by stripes, and wandering sheep closely parallels Isaiah 53 and provides strong evidence of sustained engagement with that chapter rather than a single isolated phrase.

**Does not establish:** The literary dependence does not independently prove the historical events described, the letter's traditional authorship, or a single exclusive model of atonement.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some wording may have reached the author through an established early Christian interpretation of Isaiah 53 rather than solely through direct literary composition.
- Recognizing the allusion does not determine one exhaustive theory of how Christ's suffering saves.
