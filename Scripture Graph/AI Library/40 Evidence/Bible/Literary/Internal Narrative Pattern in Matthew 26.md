# Internal Narrative Pattern in Matthew 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter, Judas's betrayal, the disciples' flight, and Peter's three denials correspond to Jesus's preceding predictions. This is strong internal evidence for a deliberately constructed prediction-fulfillment pattern and for Matthew's portrayal of Jesus as possessing foreknowledge.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence alone does not establish when the predictions originated or independently prove supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The evangelist could have shaped the predictions and their fulfillment retrospectively.
- Received oral or written tradition may already have contained the pattern before Matthew composed the chapter.
