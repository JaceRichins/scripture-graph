# Theological Pattern In The Text in John 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The prayer portrays the Father and Son as relationally distinguishable while profoundly united: one sends and gives to the other, they address one another, share glory and possessions, and are described through mutual indwelling.

**Does not establish:** The language does not by itself adjudicate every later creedal or non-creedal account of the Godhead.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Trinitarian readings understand the distinction as distinction of persons within one divine being.
- Other Christian readings emphasize a unity of love, purpose, glory, or divine agency while construing the Father's and Son's ontological relationship differently.
- Because believers are also invited into oneness, some interpreters give relational and participatory unity greater weight than metaphysical identity.
