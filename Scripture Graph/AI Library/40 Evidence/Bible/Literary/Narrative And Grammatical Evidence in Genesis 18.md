# Narrative And Grammatical Evidence in Genesis 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's singular-plural shifts support identifying at least one principal speaker with the LORD, but they do not securely determine the identity or nature of all three visitors.

**Does not establish:** These grammatical features do not by themselves establish a Trinitarian appearance, the LORD's corporeality, or the precise angelic or divine identity of each visitor.

**Alternative explanations:**
- All three may be heavenly messengers, with one speaking as the LORD's representative.
- The LORD may be distinguished from two accompanying messengers.
- The narrative may shift between divine presence and human appearance without intending a precise ontological explanation.
