# Internal Literary Evidence in Exodus 38

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated construction report and the summary that Bezaleel made all the Lord commanded provide strong internal textual evidence that the narrative presents the work as obedient fulfillment of revealed instructions.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish when the account was composed, that every reported action occurred historically, or that the measurements have archaeological confirmation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The compliance formula may be a conventional literary device used to close a construction account.
- The correspondence may reflect composition or editing that deliberately aligned command and fulfillment sections.
