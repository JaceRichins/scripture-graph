# Narrative And Role Evidence in 2 Kings 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Huldah's title, the royal delegation's consultation of her, and her use of the prophetic messenger formula provide strong textual evidence that this narrative recognizes a woman as an authoritative prophetic speaker.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish a complete doctrine of ecclesiastical office, ordination, or gender equality in every religious role.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Huldah may represent an exceptional case rather than a general institutional pattern.
- The title "prophetess" and this consultation do not by themselves define her office, training, or relationship to priests and other prophets.
- Recognition within this scene does not settle later theological disputes about authority or ordination.
