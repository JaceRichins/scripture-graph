# Internal Narrative And Christological Evidence in Luke 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus tells the restored man to report what God has done for him, and the narrator immediately says that the man reports what Jesus has done for him. This verbal juxtaposition is internal evidence that Luke closely associates Jesus' saving action with God's action.

**Does not establish:** The juxtaposition alone does not establish a complete doctrine of Jesus' divine identity or settle how Luke distinguishes Jesus from God.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jesus may be portrayed as God's authorized agent, allowing an act performed through him to be attributed to God without identifying Jesus ontologically with God.
- The wording may primarily serve narrative economy by connecting the commission with the man's report.

**Scripture:** [[Luke 8#^luke-8-39|Luke 8:39]]
