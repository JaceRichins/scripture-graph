# Literary Pattern in Ephesians 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated collective terms—“together,” “both,” “one,” and “builded together”—give the chapter a coherent movement from shared life in Christ to a reconciled people and shared divine habitation. This repetition is substantial evidence for deliberate thematic unity within the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish authorship, date, historical provenance, or a formal literary structure such as chiasmus.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may arise naturally from the subject matter rather than from a tightly designed literary scheme.
- Modern chapter boundaries need not correspond exactly to the original composition’s rhetorical divisions.
