# Internal Narrative Evidence in Genesis 48

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides strong internal evidence that Jacob's placement of Ephraim before Manasseh is deliberate rather than an error caused by poor eyesight: the narrator says he guided his hands knowingly, Joseph objects, and Jacob affirms that he understands which son is firstborn.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the prediction was supernaturally revealed or validate later claims made about Ephraim beyond what the passage says.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The precise symbolic significance of the crossed hands may be interpreted differently, but the narrator and dialogue make accidental placement an unlikely reading.
