# Literary-Theological Evidence in Romans 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between flesh and heart, letter and spirit, and human and divine praise is strong evidence that Paul prioritizes inward fidelity evaluated by God over merely visible religious status.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that outward ordinances are inherently worthless or that ethnic Israel has lost every covenantal role.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may define the faithful Jew rather than transfer the designation 'Jew' to all Christians.
- The contrast may concern divine transformation rather than an opposition between all inward and outward religious practice.

**Scripture:** [[Romans 2#^rom-2-28|Romans 2:28]], [[Romans 2#^rom-2-29|Romans 2:29]]
