# Internal Chronological Synchronism in 1 Kings 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The 480-year notice is strong evidence for how the narrator chronologically relates the temple to the Exodus, but it is limited evidence for determining the exact historical date of the Exodus without additional chronological assumptions.

**Does not establish:** The verse alone does not establish an uncontested absolute date for the Exodus or demonstrate that every component of the 480 years was counted by a uniform modern chronological method.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The figure may represent a literal elapsed chronology.
- The figure may be schematic, commonly understood as twelve generations of forty years.
- It may combine schematic reckoning with preserved chronological tradition.
- Different reconstructions of Israelite and Egyptian chronology can produce different absolute dates.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 6#^1kgs-6-1|1 Kings 6:1]]
