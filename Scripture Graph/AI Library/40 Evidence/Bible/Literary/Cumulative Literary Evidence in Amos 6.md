# Cumulative Literary Evidence in Amos 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence from complacency and violence to conspicuous luxury, indifference to Joseph, self-congratulation, and threatened exile provides strong internal textual evidence that Amos is condemning an elite culture of privilege insulated from communal suffering, not merely isolated acts of personal comfort.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish that all wealth, music, feasting, wine, or physical comfort is inherently condemned.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The primary target may be complacent political leadership rather than affluent people as a general class.
- Some luxury items may function rhetorically as symbols of indifference without implying that possession of such items is intrinsically sinful.
