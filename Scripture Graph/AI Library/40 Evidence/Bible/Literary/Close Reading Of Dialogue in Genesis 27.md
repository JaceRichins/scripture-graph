# Close Reading Of Dialogue in Genesis 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jacob directly claims a false identity ('I am Esau thy firstborn') and invokes God's name to explain his suspiciously quick return ('Because the LORD thy God brought it to me'), meaning the text has Jacob using God's name in direct service of the lie.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that the text endorses or excuses the deception; it only establishes what Jacob says, not how the narrator evaluates it.

**Alternative explanations:**
- later interpretive traditions (rabbinic and LDS) often read the outcome as divinely intended per the Genesis 25:23 oracle, treating the flawed means as separate from the sanctioned end
- some readers minimize the moral weight of the statement as a formulaic or perfunctory response rather than a calculated theological lie

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 27#^gen-27-19|Genesis 27:19]], [[Genesis 27#^gen-27-20|Genesis 27:20]]
