# Explicit Scriptural Citation in Isaiah 59

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 11:26–27 introduces with 'as it is written' a formulation closely dependent on Isaiah 59:20–21, applying the Redeemer and covenant language to the salvation and removal of sins associated with Israel.

**Does not establish:** The citation does not by itself settle the timing, manner, or population encompassed by Paul's expectation for Israel.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul's wording also draws on other scriptural material, especially language concerning the removal of sin, rather than reproducing Isaiah 59 alone.
- Competing interpretations understand the scope and timing of 'all Israel' differently.
