# Internal Chronology in Joshua 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Caleb's ages provide a coherent internal interval of forty-five years between the scouting mission and the Hebron petition.

**Does not establish:** The arithmetic does not independently establish an absolute date, Caleb's historical age, or the historicity of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The numbers may be schematic or literary rather than derived from contemporary chronological records.
- Internal consistency can result from deliberate narrative composition.
