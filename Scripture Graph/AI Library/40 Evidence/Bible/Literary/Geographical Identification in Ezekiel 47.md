# Geographical Identification in Ezekiel 47

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The eastward course into the desert, the reference to En-gedi, and the contrast between healed water and salt marshes strongly support identifying the unnamed sea as the Dead Sea within the vision's geography.

**Does not establish:** This identification does not establish that the passage predicts a literal modern engineering or hydrological event, nor does it determine when or how the vision is fulfilled.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sea may function primarily as a symbolic image of a death-like realm being renewed.
- The idealized geography need not describe an ordinary or presently observable watercourse.
