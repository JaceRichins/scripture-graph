# Literary Quotation And Rhetorical Adaptation in Titus 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The letter quotes a voice characterized as one of the Cretans' own prophets and employs that local cultural testimony within its rebuke.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the truth of a universal claim about Cretans, identify the unnamed prophet from the text alone, or independently prove authorship and date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may have circulated as a proverb rather than being taken directly from a written work.
- The quotation may function as conventional polemic or ethnic stereotyping rather than as a measured description of all Cretans.
- The writer's use of a saying does not require detailed knowledge of its original literary setting.
