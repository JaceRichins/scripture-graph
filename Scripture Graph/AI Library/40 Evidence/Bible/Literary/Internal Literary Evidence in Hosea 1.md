# Internal Literary Evidence in Hosea 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit analogy in verse 2 and the repeated explanations of the children's names strongly support reading Hosea's household as a prophetic sign within the narrative.

**Does not establish:** The literary function does not by itself establish whether every narrated family event occurred literally, how Gomer should be characterized historically, or when the account reached its final form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some readers may treat portions of the family account primarily as literary allegory rather than biographical reporting.
- The names could preserve historical family traditions that were subsequently arranged or interpreted for prophetic purposes.
