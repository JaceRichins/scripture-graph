# Narrative Characterization in Exodus 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter gives strong textual support for a favorable portrayal of Jethro, a priest of Midian: he celebrates the LORD's deliverance, blesses the LORD, sacrifices before Israel's leaders, diagnoses a genuine leadership problem, and offers counsel that Moses implements.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish Jethro's complete conversion, his covenant status, or the nature and authority of his Midianite priesthood.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jethro may function primarily as a literary outsider whose response highlights the greatness of the LORD.
- His sacrifices and confession may express reverence or recognition without implying abandonment of every prior belief.
- His administrative wisdom does not by itself identify the religious source of that wisdom.
