# Literary And Procedural Coherence in Numbers 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The departure narrative closely implements earlier regulations concerning the cloud, tribal order, and Levitical transport duties. This is meaningful evidence of deliberate legal-narrative coherence within Numbers.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish single authorship, a particular date of composition, Mosaic authorship, or the historicity of the journey.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A compiler or editor could have shaped the departure account to match earlier regulations.
- The correspondence may reflect a shared traditional scheme rather than composition by a single author.
- Literary consistency does not by itself demonstrate that every narrated event occurred historically.
