# Extra-Biblical Royal Inscription in 2 Chronicles 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Sennacherib's own annals (Taylor/Sennacherib Prism) describe a campaign against Judah, the siege of Judean cities, and shutting Hezekiah up in Jerusalem 'like a caged bird,' plus receipt of tribute — corroborating the broad outline of an Assyrian invasion of Judah under Hezekiah as reported in the opening of this chapter.

**Does not establish:** Does not corroborate the miraculous destruction of the Assyrian army (32:21) or the specific numbers/details found only in the biblical accounts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Royal Assyrian annals are propagandistic and never admit defeat; the Prism's silence about any catastrophe to the army is expected regardless of what actually happened, so it cannot corroborate or refute the angel/plague narrative of verse 21.

**Scripture:** [[2 Chronicles 32#^2chr-32-1|2 Chronicles 32:1]], [[2 Chronicles 32#^2chr-32-9|2 Chronicles 32:9]]
