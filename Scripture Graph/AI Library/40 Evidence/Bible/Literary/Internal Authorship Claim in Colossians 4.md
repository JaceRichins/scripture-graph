# Internal Authorship Claim in Colossians 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closing claims that the final salutation is written by Paul's own hand, functioning within the letter as a personal authentication marker.

**Does not establish:** Internal self-identification is relevant evidence but does not independently settle the disputed historical authorship of the whole letter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A secretary may have composed the main text while Paul supplied only the closing.
- A pseudonymous writer could imitate an authenticating convention.
- An authentic closing could have been incorporated into a later composition or edition.

**Scripture:** [[Colossians 4#^col-4-18|Colossians 4:18]]
