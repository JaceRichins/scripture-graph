# Literary Dependence Or Shared Source in 2 Chronicles 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close verbal correspondence between the end of Chronicles and the beginning of Ezra is strong evidence of deliberate literary continuity between the books.

**Does not establish:** The overlap does not by itself establish that Chronicles and Ezra had the same author or were originally one continuous book.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have copied the passage from one book into the other.
- Both books may independently preserve wording from a common source or established tradition.
- The overlap may intentionally link separately authored works rather than demonstrate common authorship.

**Scripture:** [[2 Chronicles 36#^2chr-36-22|2 Chronicles 36:22-23]], [[Ezra 1#^ezra-1-1|Ezra 1:1-3]]
