# Internal Evidence Of Controversy in Matthew 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 28 provides evidence that a stolen-body explanation was known to the author or intended audience, since the narrative explicitly explains and labels that report as current in the author's day.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not independently prove either the resurrection or every reported detail concerning the guards, bribery, and priestly consultation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may preserve an actual counterclaim circulated by opponents of early Christians.
- The evangelist may have constructed or stylized the counter-story as an apologetic response to objections.
- Even if the counterclaim circulated, Matthew alone does not establish when it originated or how widely it was believed.
