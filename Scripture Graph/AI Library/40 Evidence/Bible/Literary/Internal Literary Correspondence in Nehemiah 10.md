# Internal Literary Correspondence in Nehemiah 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence in Nehemiah 13 of neglected temple support, Sabbath trade, and intermarriage strongly supports reading the promises in Nehemiah 10 as a thematic agenda for the book. It also shows that recording a covenant does not demonstrate sustained compliance.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not establish how many people violated the covenant, how long observance lasted, or the precise editorial history of the book.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have deliberately arranged Nehemiah 13 to correspond with the covenant program.
- The later episodes may represent failures by only part of the community rather than wholesale abandonment of the covenant.
