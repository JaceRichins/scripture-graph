# Canonical Literary Connection in 2 Kings 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit identification of the preserved grave and the narrator's appeal to the proclaimed word provide strong internal literary evidence that 2 Kings presents Josiah's action at Beth-el as fulfillment of 1 Kings 13.

**Does not establish:** The literary connection alone does not independently establish the historical accuracy, date, or supernatural origin of the prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A shared author or redactor could have coordinated the earlier prophecy and later fulfillment account.
- The later account may have shaped the remembered details of the earlier tradition.
