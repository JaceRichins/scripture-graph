# Narrative And Literary Structure in Matthew 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew marks a clear narrative pivot when Jesus dismisses the multitude, enters the house, and explains the tares to his disciples. This supports reading the chapter as deliberately alternating public parable and private interpretation.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not establish the precise historical sequence of every saying or prove that the disciples understood the parables fully.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The arrangement may preserve remembered changes of setting without every transition serving a symbolic literary purpose.
- The disciples' earlier question in verse 10 complicates any rigid division in which everything before verse 36 is addressed only to the crowd.
