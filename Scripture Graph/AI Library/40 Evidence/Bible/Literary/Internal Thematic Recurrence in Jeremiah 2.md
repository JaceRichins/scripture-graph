# Internal Thematic Recurrence in Jeremiah 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated language of things that do not profit, together with the inability of Judah's gods and political confidences to save or prosper her, strongly supports the reading that failed substitutes are a controlling theme of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The recurrence does not independently prove the historical circumstances, date, or compositional unity of the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some occurrences may be conventional prophetic vocabulary rather than a consciously constructed refrain.
- The text is polemical and theological, not a neutral empirical comparison of religious or political systems.
