# Text-Versus-Heading Distinction in 1 Kings 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The canonical verse text calls the object a molten sea and describes its construction, supports, and capacity; it does not call it a baptismal font or describe a baptism performed in it. Identifying it as a baptismal font is therefore an interpretive or typological identification supplied by the chapter heading, not an explicit statement of verses 23–26.

**Does not establish:** This textual distinction does not disprove a legitimate typological relationship between the molten sea and later baptismal practice.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A faith tradition may reasonably use typology to connect the sea with later baptismal fonts without claiming that the ancient narrator used that terminology.
- Other biblical passages may clarify the sea's ritual washing function, but that function is not stated in this chapter.
