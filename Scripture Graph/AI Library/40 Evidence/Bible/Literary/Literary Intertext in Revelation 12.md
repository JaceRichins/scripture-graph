# Literary Intertext in Revelation 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The royal-child language in Revelation 12:5 strongly echoes Psalms 2:9 and is repeated in Revelation 19:15. This intertextual pattern is strong evidence for a messianic identification of the child.

**Does not establish:** The allusion does not by itself prove a single exhaustive interpretation of the woman, the birth, or the chapter's chronology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author could be applying royal Psalm imagery to a collective people rather than only to an individual Messiah.
- The wording identifies the child's royal function more securely than it identifies every other detail of the surrounding symbolism.
