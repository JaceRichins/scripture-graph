# Cumulative Narrative Evidence in Acts 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Acts 11 constructs a cumulative internal case that Peter's conduct and Gentile inclusion were divinely authorized: a repeated vision, the Spirit's command, six companions, the host's angelic message, the Gentiles' reception of the Holy Ghost, and Peter's recollection of Jesus's teaching converge in his defense.

**Does not establish:** The narrative convergence does not independently establish that the visions, angelic appearance, or divine causation occurred historically.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The convergence may reflect deliberate literary shaping by the author.
- The narrative may preserve a community's theological interpretation of remembered events.
- Acceptance of the narrative presentation does not require accepting every reported supernatural cause as independently verified.
