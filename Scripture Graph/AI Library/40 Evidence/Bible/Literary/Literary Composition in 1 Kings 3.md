# Literary Composition in 1 Kings 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close request-promise-demonstration sequence is strong internal evidence that the dream and lawsuit have been deliberately arranged as a unified literary account of God-given judicial wisdom.

**Does not establish:** This literary unity does not by itself establish the account's date, authorship, eyewitness status, or historical accuracy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have placed an independently circulating judgment story after the dream because their themes matched.
- Literary coherence by itself does not determine whether every reported event occurred exactly as narrated.
