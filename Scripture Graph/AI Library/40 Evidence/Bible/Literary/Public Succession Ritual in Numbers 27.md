# Public Succession Ritual in Numbers 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated emphasis on selection, public presentation, witnessed charging, transferred honor, and laying on of hands is strong textual evidence that the commissioning scene is designed to legitimate Joshua's leadership and provide visible continuity before Israel.

**Does not establish:** The scene does not independently prove the event's historicity, the passage's date, or that every later laying-on-of-hands practice derives directly from this commissioning.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The details may reflect a conventional investiture motif used to shape the narrative.
- The scene may idealize or retrospectively formalize Joshua's succession.
- The actions establish Joshua in this narrative but do not necessarily prescribe an identical succession procedure for every later community.
