# Literary And Administrative Structure in Joshua 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated opening and closing formulas, boundary descriptions, and town lists provide strong internal evidence that Joshua 13:15-31 is arranged as a deliberate territorial register rather than as an unstructured collection of place-names.

**Does not establish:** The organized form does not by itself establish the lists' date, archival origin, territorial accuracy, or the historicity of every detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The present structure may have been imposed while incorporating lists from different periods.
- Formulaic organization can be literary composition rather than a transcript of an official land record.
