# Internal Narrative Testimony in Matthew 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Jesus' innocence is asserted by parties with different relationships to the proceedings: Judas calls his blood innocent, Pilate's wife calls him just, and Pilate publicly calls him just. This is strong evidence for Matthew's deliberate innocence motif, but only internal narrative evidence concerning the historical verdict.

**Does not establish:** The repeated declarations do not independently prove every historical detail of the trial or absolve the officials who participate in the execution.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The declarations may primarily serve the evangelist's theological and rhetorical presentation.
- Pilate's declaration may function as self-exculpation rather than an impartial legal judgment.
- Repeated innocence language can derive from developed passion tradition rather than independent testimony.
