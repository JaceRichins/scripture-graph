# Explicit Scriptural Quotation in Luke 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus explicitly introduces the rejected-stone saying as something written, and Luke 20:17 closely corresponds to Psalms 118:22. This is strong evidence of deliberate scriptural quotation or adaptation within the discourse.

**Does not establish:** The quotation alone does not establish a complete historical scheme for the vineyard, builders, stone, or transfer to others.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may have reached Luke through an earlier Jesus tradition or another literary source rather than through Luke's independent comparison of texts.
- The citation's application can be read at several levels and does not uniquely determine every symbol in the vineyard parable.

**Scripture:** [[Luke 20#^luke-20-17|Luke 20:17]], [[Psalm 118#^ps-118-22|Psalm 118:22]]
