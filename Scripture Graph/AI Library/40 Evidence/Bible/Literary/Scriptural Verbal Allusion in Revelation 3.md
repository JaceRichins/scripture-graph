# Scriptural Verbal Allusion in Revelation 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The description of the one holding the key of David, whose opening and shutting cannot be reversed, closely parallels Isaiah 22:22 and is strong textual evidence for an allusion to imagery of authorized access and royal administration.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not establish a complete theory of apostolic succession, priesthood offices, or the historical identity of the angel addressed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Revelation may employ royal-key imagery that had become conventional rather than making a narrowly defined claim about Isaiah's original historical figure.
- The Isaiah language may be adapted typologically without asserting that every detail of Isaiah 22 is fulfilled here.

**Scripture:** [[Revelation 3#^rev-3-7|Revelation 3:7]], [[Isaiah 22#^isa-22-22|Isaiah 22:22]]
