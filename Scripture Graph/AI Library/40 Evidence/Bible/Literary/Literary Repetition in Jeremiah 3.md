# Literary Repetition in Jeremiah 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated vocabulary of turning and returning supplies a strong thematic spine for the chapter, linking accusation, invitation, promised healing, and the people's response.

**Does not establish:** The repetition does not by itself establish a detailed chiastic structure, single-stage composition, date of composition, or specific authorship history.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Turn and return are common prophetic terms and need not indicate an elaborate formal design.
- Some clustering may reflect editorial arrangement or the wording of the English translation.
