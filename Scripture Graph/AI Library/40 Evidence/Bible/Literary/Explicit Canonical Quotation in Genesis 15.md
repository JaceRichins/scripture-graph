# Explicit Canonical Quotation in Genesis 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans, Galatians, and James explicitly reuse Genesis 15:6 when discussing Abraham's faith and righteousness, making this verse a major later canonical foundation for reflection on faith, works, and justification.

**Does not establish:** The shared quotation does not eliminate interpretive debates about justification or reduce Genesis 15:6 to only one later doctrinal use.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later writers employ the verse in different arguments and should not be assumed to make identical points.
- Later canonical interpretation does not by itself settle every question about the verse's original literary setting.
