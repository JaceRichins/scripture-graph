# Terminological Distinction in Leviticus 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Leviticus 1 explicitly connects the burnt offering with atonement, but it does not mention a particular sin or call the sacrifice a sin offering. Accordingly, the chapter alone does not establish the stronger summary that these animals are specifically sacrificed as atonement for sins.

**Does not establish:** The absence of the word sin does not prove that forgiveness or expiation is unrelated to the offering; it limits what can be claimed from Leviticus 1 by itself.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Atonement may implicitly include sin even when no particular offense is named.
- A chapter heading may summarize the broader sacrificial system rather than reproduce only the vocabulary of this chapter.
- The burnt offering may have several functions, including but not limited to expiation.

**Scripture:** [[Leviticus 1#^lev-1-4|Leviticus 1:4]]
