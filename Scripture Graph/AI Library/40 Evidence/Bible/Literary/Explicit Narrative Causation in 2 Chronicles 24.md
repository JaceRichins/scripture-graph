# Explicit Narrative Causation in 2 Chronicles 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter explicitly interprets the defeat of Joash's much larger host by a small Syrian force as judgment for Judah's abandonment of the LORD. This is strong evidence for the narrator's theological interpretation of the event, but only limited evidence for establishing its historical cause.

**Does not establish:** The narrative's stated theological cause does not independently demonstrate the mechanism of divine action or exclude ordinary military causes.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A historically small Syrian force might have prevailed because of strategy, surprise, leadership failure, internal instability, or other circumstances not described here.
- The account may interpret an independently occurring military defeat through the Chronicler's covenantal theology.
