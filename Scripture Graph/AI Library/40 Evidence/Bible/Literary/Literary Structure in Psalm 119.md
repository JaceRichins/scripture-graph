# Literary Structure in Psalm 119

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The twenty-two-stanza acrostic, consistent eight-verse units, and sustained concentration on divine instruction provide strong evidence of deliberate literary construction. They support reading repetition as structured meditation rather than accidental redundancy.

**Does not establish:** The acrostic does not by itself establish the psalm's author, date, historical setting, or a specific symbolic meaning for every letter or stanza.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The alphabetic form may function primarily as a mnemonic or conventional poetic display rather than encoding a detailed symbolic theology of completeness.
- Some thematic patterns perceived across stanzas may arise from repeated vocabulary rather than from a tightly sequential argument.

**Scripture:** [[Psalm 119#^ps-119-1|Psalm 119:1-176]]
