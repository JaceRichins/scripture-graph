# Internal Temporal Marker in Joshua 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The phrase "until this day" presents the continued presence of the Geshurites and Maachathites from a narrative standpoint later than the initial settlement event. It is genuine evidence of temporal distance or later editorial perspective, but it cannot by itself determine how much time had elapsed.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a late date for the entire book, identify an author or editor, or determine the interval between the event and the wording.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The expression could have been written only a short time after the settlement.
- The wording may be a later editorial update embedded in older material.
- The phrase may function as a conventional narrative formula without identifying a precise date.
