# Literary-Linguistic in John 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Greek text makes a verbal connection between the Father's pruning and the disciples' cleanness: kathairei in John 15:2 and katharoi in John 15:3 are cognate forms. The KJV partially obscures the echo by translating them as “purgeth” and “clean.” This strongly supports a deliberate transition from the vine metaphor to Jesus' declaration that his word has cleansed the disciples.

**Does not establish:** The Greek wordplay does not establish which language Jesus originally used, the precise history of the discourse's composition, or the historicity of every reported detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The cognate words may have arisen naturally from the subject matter without being intended as elaborate wordplay.
- The wordplay could belong to the evangelist's Greek composition rather than to the language of Jesus' original oral discourse.
