# Literary Structure in Genesis 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The controlled repetition of the commission, prayer, encounter, and interpretation supports reading Genesis 24 as a deliberately shaped narrative whose central concern is providential guidance and covenant continuity.

**Does not establish:** Literary craftsmanship does not by itself establish the date, authorship, historical accuracy, or supernatural inspiration of the account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Repetition can arise from oral storytelling conventions as well as from written composition.
- The retelling may principally imitate a messenger's full report rather than encode an elaborate literary structure.
- Some differences between the two tellings may be incidental compression rather than strategic alteration.
