# Historical-Source Limitation in Isaiah 37

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 37:36 is clear evidence for what the biblical narrative claims, but the supplied material provides no independent evidence with which to verify the reported cause or number of Assyrian deaths.

**Does not establish:** The narrative report alone does not establish the exact casualty count, the physical mechanism of death, or independent historical corroboration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The report may preserve a historical military catastrophe interpreted theologically.
- The number may be stylized, amplified, or transmitted within a literary tradition.
- The account may function principally as a theological narrative of deliverance.
