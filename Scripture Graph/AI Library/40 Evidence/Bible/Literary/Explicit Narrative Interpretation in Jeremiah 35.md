# Explicit Narrative Interpretation in Jeremiah 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit explanation in verses 13-18 strongly establishes the wine episode's literary function as an object lesson contrasting Rechabite obedience with Judah's refusal to hear God; this function does not depend merely on inferred symbolism.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every ancestral rule is divinely binding or that abstinence from wine is the chapter's universal command.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The episode can carry secondary themes about communal identity and displacement in addition to its explicitly stated contrast.
- The narrative's final form may have shaped an older Rechabite tradition for this didactic purpose.
