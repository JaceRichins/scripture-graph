# Literary-Thematic in 2 Kings 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrator's initial praise of Hezekiah's trust, followed by the Assyrian speech's repeated assault on every possible object of trust, is strong internal evidence that the chapter has been arranged as a theological contest over confidence and allegiance, not merely as a sequence of military facts.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the historical wording of the speeches, the chapter's date of composition, or that every repetition is intentionally patterned.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Trust language could recur naturally in a siege negotiation without being part of an elaborate literary design.
- Some repetitions may derive from inherited source material rather than the final narrator's composition.
