# Literary Coherence in Numbers 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated reversals—death in the wilderness answering the adults' wish, preservation of the children answering the fear that they would be prey, and forty years answering forty days—provide strong evidence of deliberate literary organization around measure-for-measure judgment.

**Does not establish:** The literary pattern does not by itself establish the historical details, antiquity, unity of authorship, or supernatural origin of the account.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Measure-for-measure correspondence is a common narrative technique and need not indicate an elaborate formal design.
- The correspondences may reflect later literary shaping of inherited traditions.
- A theological reading can explain the pattern without resolving questions of historical composition.
