# Explicit Theological Causation In Prophetic Speech in 2 Chronicles 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The oracle explicitly connects Josiah's tender heart, humility, torn clothing, and weeping with the LORD hearing him, supporting the chapter's teaching that a humble response to divine warning matters even when larger consequences are not canceled.

**Does not establish:** The episode does not establish that repentance always removes temporal consequences or that every humble person will receive Josiah's particular form of reprieve.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may primarily explain the timing of national judgment rather than formulate a general doctrine of individual repentance.
- Josiah's favorable outcome is narrated within his particular royal and covenantal setting and should not be universalized mechanically.
