# Verbal And Thematic Parallel in 2 Kings 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The evaluation of Josiah with heart, soul, and might closely parallels the covenantal formula of Deuteronomy 6:5. This strongly supports the presence of shared Deuteronomic covenant diction in the narrator's characterization of Josiah.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not by itself identify the complete contents of the found book, date that book, or settle the direction and mechanism of literary dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The formula may have circulated as conventional covenant language rather than being copied directly from Deuteronomy 6.
- The parallel could result from editorial characterization rather than Josiah's own historical wording.
- Both passages may draw upon a broader shared tradition.
