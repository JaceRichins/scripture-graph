# Explicit Scriptural Allusion Or Quotation in 1 Corinthians 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closing formula about glorying in the Lord closely invokes Jeremiah's contrast between boasting in human wisdom, might, or riches and rightly glorying in the Lord; this reinforces Paul's preceding rejection of status-based boasting.

**Does not establish:** The connection does not by itself determine every theological implication of boasting or prove that Paul is offering a complete interpretation of Jeremiah 9.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The short wording could circulate as a conventional scriptural maxim, although Paul's "as it is written" still marks it as scriptural.
- Differences in wording may reflect a Greek textual form, abbreviation, or Paul's adaptation to the argument.
