# Literary Structure in Jeremiah 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The balanced good-fig and evil-fig interpretations, their contrasting outcomes, and the repeated language of good and evil provide strong evidence of deliberate literary organization.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence alone does not establish the passage's date, authorship, historicity, or divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The organization may reflect later editorial shaping of earlier prophetic material.
- A simple two-symbol vision naturally produces some degree of parallel structure.
