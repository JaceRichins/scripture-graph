# Grammar And Literary Context in 1 Corinthians 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The plural address 'ye' and the sustained building imagery provide strong textual evidence that the immediate temple referent is the Corinthian community collectively. The warning presents factional damage to a holy community as a serious offense against God.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that temple imagery is exclusively corporate in every New Testament passage or identify every act that constitutes defiling the temple.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A plural address can sometimes be applied distributively to each member.
- Other passages use temple language in relation to believers' bodies, so the corporate reading here need not exclude valid individual application elsewhere.
- The immediate threat may encompass misconduct beyond factionalism even though divisions dominate the surrounding context.
