# Literary Intertext in Revelation 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clustered correspondences with Daniel 7 are strong evidence that Revelation 20 presents final judgment through Danielic courtroom imagery.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not by themselves establish the date of composition, the precise literary process, or a single interpretation of the judgment's chronology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some similarities could arise from shared apocalyptic conventions rather than direct reuse of Daniel.
- The author may be creatively adapting remembered scriptural language rather than making a formal quotation.
