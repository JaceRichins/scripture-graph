# Synoptic Narrative Comparison in 2 Chronicles 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The differences between the Chronicles and Kings accounts are good evidence of differing selection and emphasis in their extant presentations, but the differences alone cannot determine whether either writer used additional sources, omitted known material, or independently preserved historical information.

**Does not establish:** The comparison by itself does not prove literary dependence, independence, historical accuracy, historical inaccuracy, or the identity and date of any underlying source.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may have drawn on information unavailable to the author of Kings.
- The author of Kings may have known but omitted details preserved in Chronicles.
- Some distinctive material could reflect later literary or theological development.
- Both accounts may selectively use sources that are no longer extant.
