# Explicit Scriptural Framing in Matthew 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew's explicit fulfillment formula and prophetic citation provide strong evidence that the narrator intends the entry to be read as the arrival of a meek king in fulfillment of scripture.

**Does not establish:** The fulfillment formula by itself does not independently establish the event's historicity, the mechanism of prophecy, or every later theological claim about Jesus' kingship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The cited scripture may have influenced how the event was narrated or remembered.
- The wording combines or echoes more than one prophetic passage, so its compositional history is more complex than a single direct quotation.

**Scripture:** [[Matthew 21#^matt-21-4|Matthew 21:4]], [[Matthew 21#^matt-21-5|Matthew 21:5]], [[Zechariah 9#^zech-9-9|Zechariah 9:9]]
