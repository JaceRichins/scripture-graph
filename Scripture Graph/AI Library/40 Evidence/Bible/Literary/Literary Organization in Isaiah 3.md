# Literary Organization in Isaiah 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The ordered progression from removal of social supports, through disorder and judicial indictment, to stripping and final desolation is moderate literary evidence that the material has been arranged as a coherent judgment oracle.

**Does not establish:** This organization does not establish a particular date, a single stage of composition, or the historical identity of the predicted crisis.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The progression may arise naturally from accumulating prophetic images rather than from a tightly planned structure.
- The present arrangement may reflect editorial shaping of originally distinct oracles.
- Modern chapter boundaries may obscure a larger unit extending into Isaiah 4.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 3#^isa-3-1|Isaiah 3:1-26]]
