# Literary Pattern in 2 Samuel 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained movement from David's proposed house for God to God's promised house for David, followed by repeated use of the latter sense in David's prayer, strongly supports deliberate literary organization around the double meaning of “house.”

**Does not establish:** The wordplay alone does not establish the oracle's date, single authorship, supernatural origin, or the full scope of its future fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence could partly arise from the ordinary use of one term for both a building and a household or dynasty.
- Some of the pattern's prominence may reflect editorial shaping rather than the wording of a single original speech.
