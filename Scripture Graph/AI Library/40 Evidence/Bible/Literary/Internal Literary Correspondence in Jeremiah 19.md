# Internal Literary Correspondence in Jeremiah 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The uncommon cluster shared by Jeremiah 7 and Jeremiah 19—Tophet, child-burning, denial of divine command, the name "valley of slaughter," exposed corpses, and insufficient burial space—provides strong internal evidence that the passages are intentionally or editorially linked.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not establish the passages' precise chronology, a single historical occasion, single authorship, or the historical fulfillment of every prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passages may independently draw upon established prophetic formulas or a common tradition.
- An editor may have deliberately harmonized material originating in different settings.
