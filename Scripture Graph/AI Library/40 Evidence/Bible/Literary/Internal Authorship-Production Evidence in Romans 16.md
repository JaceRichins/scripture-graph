# Internal Authorship-Production Evidence in Romans 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Tertius's first-person statement is strong internal textual evidence that a named scribe participated in producing the epistle. It does not specify whether he took dictation, prepared a final copy, or exercised any compositional discretion.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the exact division of compositional responsibility between Paul and Tertius.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Tertius may have taken dictation.
- Tertius may have copied or prepared a final form from prior material.
- The statement does not disclose how much freedom, if any, the scribe had in wording.

**Scripture:** [[Romans 16#^rom-16-22|Romans 16:22]]
