# Internal Literary Coherence in Numbers 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The agreement between the eastern-land exception, the composition of the representative roster, and the later allocation led by Eleazar and Joshua is meaningful evidence of literary and administrative coherence across the wilderness and conquest narratives.

**Does not establish:** This coherence does not by itself establish when the passages were composed, whether every named official is historically recoverable, or whether the allotment occurred exactly as narrated.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later compiler or editor could have harmonized the personnel and tribal arrangements across the narratives.
- The correspondences may reflect shared literary tradition rather than contemporaneous administrative records.
