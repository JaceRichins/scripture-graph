# Ordinance Symbolism in Romans 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit association of baptism with burial, death, resurrection, and new life strongly supports baptismal symbolism centered on Christ's death and resurrection.

**Does not establish:** The burial image alone does not settle every question about baptismal mode, recipient, timing, or the precise relationship between the ordinance and salvation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Burial may function primarily as a theological metaphor of participation in Christ rather than as a description of baptismal mechanics.
- Readers differ on whether the passage refers chiefly to the ordinance, spiritual incorporation into Christ, or both.

**Scripture:** [[Romans 6#^rom-6-3|Romans 6:3]], [[Romans 6#^rom-6-4|Romans 6:4]], [[Romans 6#^rom-6-5|Romans 6:5]]
