# Literary Repetition in Numbers 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated attribution of both camping and traveling to the Lord's command is strong internal evidence that dependence on divine direction is a principal literary emphasis of the cloud passage.

**Does not establish:** The repetition does not by itself establish the passage's historical accuracy, date, authorship, or the physical mechanism of the cloud.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may partly reflect formulaic priestly prose rather than a specially constructed emphasis.
- The formula may serve mnemonic or summarizing purposes in addition to expressing a theology of obedience.
