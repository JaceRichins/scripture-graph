# Explicit Canonical Reuse in Zechariah 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 26:31 and Mark 14:27 explicitly apply the struck-shepherd saying of Zechariah 13:7 to Jesus and the impending scattering of his disciples. This is strong evidence for a Christian canonical reading of the shepherd as Christ.

**Does not establish:** The Gospel citations do not by themselves settle every question about the oracle’s original setting or make every figure in Zechariah 13 a reference to Jesus.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Gospel application may be typological or a fresh application rather than an exhaustive statement of the oracle’s original historical referent.
- The original shepherd may have had an additional historical or corporate significance within Zechariah’s setting.

**Scripture:** [[Zechariah 13#^zech-13-7|Zechariah 13:7]], [[Matthew 26#^matt-26-31|Matthew 26:31]], [[Mark 14#^mark-14-27|Mark 14:27]]
