# Internal Literary And Thematic Pattern in Colossians 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated claims of wisdom, fulness, completeness, resurrection life, forgiveness, victory, and bodily growth being located in or mediated through Christ support a reading in which Christ's sufficiency is the chapter's organizing answer to rival claims and regulations.

**Does not establish:** This thematic coherence does not establish the exact identity of the opponents, the detailed content of their teaching, or any conclusion about authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions may reflect several loosely connected pastoral concerns rather than one tightly planned argument.
- The chapter may answer multiple rival practices whose precise relationship to one another cannot be reconstructed.
