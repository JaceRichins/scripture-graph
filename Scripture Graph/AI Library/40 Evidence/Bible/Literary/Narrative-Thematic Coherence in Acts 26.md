# Narrative-Thematic Coherence in Acts 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Paul's summary that the Christ would suffer, rise, and proclaim light to Jews and Gentiles substantially parallels the risen Jesus' summary that the Christ would suffer, rise, and have repentance and remission preached among all nations. The convergence supports a coherent fulfillment-and-mission theme across Luke-Acts.

**Does not establish:** The thematic correspondence does not establish verbatim reporting or independently prove the resurrection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may independently reproduce a widespread early Christian summary of scripture and mission.
- Narrative coherence may reflect the author's literary arrangement without proving the historical wording of either speech.
