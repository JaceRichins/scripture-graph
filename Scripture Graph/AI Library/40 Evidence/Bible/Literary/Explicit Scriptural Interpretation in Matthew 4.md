# Explicit Scriptural Interpretation in Matthew 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit fulfillment formula and Isaiah quotation strongly establish that Matthew presents the location of Jesus' Galilean ministry as theologically significant: his arrival is portrayed as light reaching a region associated with darkness and death.

**Does not establish:** The formula does not by itself prove that Isaiah originally referred exclusively to Jesus' move to Capernaum or that the move occurred for the purpose of satisfying a prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The citation may chiefly be Matthew's retrospective theological framing rather than the historical reason Jesus moved to Capernaum.
- Readers differ over whether fulfillment here means direct prediction, typological correspondence, or the filling out of an earlier scriptural pattern.
