# Literary Structure in Malachi 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of assertion, objection, and rebuttal is strong internal evidence that the chapter uses deliberate dialogic rhetoric. The questions expose the audience's resistance or professed incomprehension and allow the accusations to be developed through concrete examples.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the historical setting, date, authorship, or whether the quoted objections were spoken verbatim.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated questions may preserve a conventional prophetic teaching form rather than the wording of an actual exchange.
- Some repetitions could arise from editorial shaping, although the chapter itself does not identify an editor.
