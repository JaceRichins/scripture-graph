# Internal Literary And Semantic Evidence in Exodus 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The same chapter says that the LORD spoke with Moses "face to face" and later says Moses cannot see the LORD's face and live. Their proximity is evidence that "face to face" in verse 11 may describe direct, personal communication rather than an unrestricted visual sight of the divine face.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself settle the nature or embodiment of God, prove that every biblical use of "face" is figurative, or eliminate source-critical explanations.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statements may refer to different degrees or modes of divine manifestation.
- Verse 11 may preserve a conventional expression without intending a literal description of sight.
- Some source-critical readings may attribute the tension to distinct traditions or editorial layers rather than intentional semantic differentiation.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 33#^ex-33-11|Exodus 33:11]], [[Exodus 33#^ex-33-20|Exodus 33:20-23]]
