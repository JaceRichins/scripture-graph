# Direct Narrative Evidence in Acts 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrative is direct textual evidence that Priscilla participated with Aquila in further instruction of Apollos: both are named as hearing him, taking him to themselves, and expounding the way of God more perfectly. The plural wording does not specify each spouse's individual contribution.

**Does not establish:** This incident alone does not establish that Priscilla held an ordained office, presided over Apollos, or exemplified every congregation's leadership structure.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The plural description may summarize the couple's joint action without identifying how much either spouse said.
- The episode may describe informal instruction rather than an exercise of ecclesiastical office.
