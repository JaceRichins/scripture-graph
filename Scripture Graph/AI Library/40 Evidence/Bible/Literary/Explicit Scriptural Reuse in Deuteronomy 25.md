# Explicit Scriptural Reuse in Deuteronomy 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
First Corinthians 9 and 1 Timothy 5 reuse Deuteronomy 25:4 in arguments about supporting human laborers. This strongly establishes that these New Testament texts read the command analogically; it does not by itself settle whether human compensation was the command's original or exclusive concern.

**Does not establish:** The reuse does not establish that concern for animals is absent from Deuteronomy 25:4, nor does it independently define every circumstance in which religious workers must receive material support.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be extending an animal-welfare law by analogy rather than declaring its original subject to be human wages.
- The law may intentionally embody a broad labor principle that includes both animals and people.
