# Narrative Character Testimony in 1 Samuel 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The people's formal attestation functions as strong evidence within the narrative for Samuel's integrity and for the legitimacy of his ensuing rebuke. As historical evidence about Samuel's entire career, however, it remains testimony preserved by a text sympathetic to him.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical completeness of the hearing or prove that Samuel never committed any wrongdoing.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The scene may be a stylized vindication of Samuel rather than a transcript of a public hearing.
- The people's answer establishes that no accusation was voiced in this scene, not that every possible abuse had been independently investigated.
