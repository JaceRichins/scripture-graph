# Literary And Traditional-History Evidence in Psalm 68

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of the Numbers departure formula, Sinai theophany, and motifs shared with the Song of Deborah is good evidence that Psalm 68 preserves or reworks established Israelite wilderness and victory traditions.

**Does not establish:** These affinities do not establish an early date for the completed psalm, Davidic authorship, or a single direction of literary dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later poet could deliberately imitate or combine older scriptural language.
- The similarities may arise from a shared liturgical repertoire rather than direct dependence on Numbers or Judges.
- Different portions of the psalm may have originated in different settings before being combined.
