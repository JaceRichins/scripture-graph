# Internal Literary Evidence in Deuteronomy 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The matched statements that Israel has avouched the LORD and that the LORD has avouched Israel are strong internal literary evidence that Deuteronomy 26:17–19 presents a reciprocal covenant affirmation.

**Does not establish:** The verbal symmetry does not by itself establish the passage's date, authorship, historical setting, or how often such declarations were actually performed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The mirrored wording could function as a conventional legal or liturgical formula without describing a separate covenant-making ceremony.
- The passage may summarize an existing covenant relationship rather than inaugurate a new one.
