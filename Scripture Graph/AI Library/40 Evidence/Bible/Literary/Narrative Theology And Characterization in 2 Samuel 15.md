# Narrative Theology And Characterization in 2 Samuel 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
David orders the ark returned to Jerusalem and makes his restoration conditional on divine favor. His willingness to accept rejection portrays submission to God's judgment rather than an attempt to use the ark as a guarantee of political or military success.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish David's general righteousness, divine approval of all his conduct, or that his restoration is guaranteed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Returning the ark also has a practical motive because the priests can gather intelligence in Jerusalem.
- The speech may represent the narrator's theological characterization of David rather than unrestricted access to David's private disposition.
- David's refusal may reflect uncertainty about the revolt's outcome as well as religious submission.
