# Scriptural Intertext in John 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The wording “They hated me without a cause” has close antecedents in Psalm 35:19 and Psalm 69:4. This is strong evidence that John frames opposition to Jesus through scriptural patterns of the unjustly hated righteous sufferer, although the precise Psalm intended is uncertain.

**Does not establish:** The verbal correspondence does not by itself prove that either Psalm was a one-to-one prediction of a particular historical event, nor does it identify the exact source beyond dispute.

**Alternative explanations:**
- John may allude primarily to Psalm 35:19.
- John may allude primarily to Psalm 69:4.
- The wording may deliberately evoke a broader scriptural motif rather than quote one uniquely identifiable verse.
