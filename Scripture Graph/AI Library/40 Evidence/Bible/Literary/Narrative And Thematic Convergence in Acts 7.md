# Narrative And Thematic Convergence in Acts 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
God appears or acts in Mesopotamia, Egypt, Sinai, and the wilderness before Stephen states that the Most High does not dwell in human-made temples. This geographical pattern strongly supports reading the speech as opposing the confinement of God's presence to one building.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Stephen regarded the temple as illegitimate or denied that particular places can be holy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The locations may arise naturally from the historical narrative without every location serving a deliberate rhetorical design.
- Stephen may be criticizing an excessive view of the temple rather than developing a general theology of divine omnipresence.
