# Literary And Thematic Intertext in Revelation 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The merchandise catalogue and maritime lament closely parallel Ezekiel 27's lament over Tyre, including varied international cargo, ship personnel, merchants enriched by trade, cries over a fallen city, and dust cast upon mourners' heads. Revelation thus appears to combine Babylon traditions with imagery originally directed at Tyre's commercial empire.

**Does not establish:** The Tyre imagery does not identify Babylon with literal Tyre or prove that every listed commodity corresponds to one known historical shipment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some similarities could reflect common features of ancient port-city laments.
- The author may be using a traditional adaptation of Ezekiel rather than the book directly.
