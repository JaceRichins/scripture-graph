# Explicit Scriptural Citation in Romans 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Romans 12:19 explicitly introduces written scriptural authority and gives a formulation of divine vengeance and repayment corresponding to Deuteronomy 32:35. This is strong evidence of scriptural reuse, although the wording is not identical to the KJV wording of Deuteronomy.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish which Hebrew or Greek textual form Paul consulted, nor that Romans reproduces the KJV wording of Deuteronomy verbatim.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul may be using a textual form or traditional formulation differing from the wording represented by the KJV Old Testament.
- The wording could reflect remembered or mediated scriptural tradition rather than direct copying from a manuscript.

**Scripture:** [[Romans 12#^rom-12-19|Romans 12:19]], [[Deuteronomy 32#^deut-32-35|Deuteronomy 32:35]]
