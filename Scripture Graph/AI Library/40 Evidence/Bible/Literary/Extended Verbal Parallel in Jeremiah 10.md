# Extended Verbal Parallel in Jeremiah 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 10:12-16 and Jeremiah 51:15-19 contain the same extended creation-and-idol polemic with only minor wording differences. The long sequence is strong evidence of literary reuse or shared traditional material within the book.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not determine which passage is earlier, whether Jeremiah composed the unit, or at what stage it entered either context.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jeremiah 10 may have supplied material to Jeremiah 51.
- Jeremiah 51 may have supplied material to Jeremiah 10.
- Both passages may draw upon a pre-existing hymn or liturgical polemic.
- An editor may have inserted the traditional unit into one or both contexts.
