# Narrative And Theological Sequence in Deuteronomy 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrative order in the parental answer supports interpreting obedience as a response within a relationship already defined by divine deliverance: the LORD brings Israel out and in before the answer explains the statutes.

**Does not establish:** The sequence does not by itself provide a complete doctrine of salvation or settle later debates about grace, works, justification, or human merit.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may partly reflect ordinary chronological narration rather than a deliberately formulated doctrine of grace and obedience.
- Verse 25 still assigns positive significance to observing the commandments and should not be minimized.
