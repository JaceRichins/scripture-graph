# Dating And Authorship in Isaiah 45

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit naming of Cyrus and description of his role are genuine evidence relevant to debates about the oracle's date and authorship. Under an early-Isaiah dating, the details function as a specific prediction; composition or editing during or after Cyrus's rise provides a historical alternative that accounts for the same textual datum.

**Does not establish:** The naming of Cyrus alone does not establish an eighth-century date, a sixth-century date, supernatural prediction, or a particular compositional history.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage preserves a prediction made before Cyrus's rise.
- The oracle was composed in or near Cyrus's period.
- The Cyrus identification entered the text through later editorial activity.
- A prior oracle may have been adapted to a later historical setting.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 45#^isa-45-1|Isaiah 45:1]], [[Isaiah 45#^isa-45-13|Isaiah 45:13]]
