# External Ancient Inscriptional Corroboration in 2 Kings 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Sennacherib's royal annals independently attest his campaign against Hezekiah and claim that Hezekiah was confined in Jerusalem and rendered tribute, but they do not claim that Jerusalem was captured. This provides meaningful convergence on the campaign and Jerusalem's noncapture, while leaving the cause of Assyria's withdrawal unresolved.

**Does not establish:** The annals do not establish the death of 185,000 soldiers, an angelic cause, or every detail and sequence in 2 Kings 19.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Assyria may have withdrawn after receiving tribute rather than because of a catastrophic loss.
- Disease, strategic pressures, the approach of another army, or multiple combined factors could explain withdrawal.
- Assyrian royal inscriptions are propagandistic and may omit reverses; the biblical account is also theologically shaped.
- Jerusalem's noncapture does not independently confirm the reported angelic action or casualty total.
