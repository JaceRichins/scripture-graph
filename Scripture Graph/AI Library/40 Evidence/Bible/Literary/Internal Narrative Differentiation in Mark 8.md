# Internal Narrative Differentiation in Mark 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Mark's narrative, Jesus explicitly distinguishes the feeding of five thousand with five loaves and twelve baskets from the feeding of four thousand with seven loaves and seven baskets. This is strong evidence that the final narrative presents two separate episodes, although it does not by itself prove that two historically separate miracles occurred.

**Does not establish:** The internal distinction does not independently establish the historical occurrence of two separate miraculous feedings.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The two accounts may preserve variant traditions about one underlying event that Mark treated as separate.
- The repetition may have been retained primarily for theological or literary purposes.
