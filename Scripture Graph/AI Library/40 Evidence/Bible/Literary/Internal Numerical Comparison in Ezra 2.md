# Internal Numerical Comparison in Ezra 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Adding every subgroup total explicitly supplied before the grand total—verses 3-42, 58, and 60—produces 29,818, whereas verse 64 reports 42,360 for the whole congregation, a difference of 12,542. This is strong evidence that the grand total cannot be reconstructed simply by summing the surviving displayed subtotals.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy does not by itself identify its cause, prove wholesale corruption, or show that the census lacks historical value.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The grand total may include people whose individual groups are omitted from the surviving list.
- The grand total and the displayed subtotals may represent differently defined populations.
- Some numerals or entries may have changed during textual transmission.
- A compiler may have preserved a received grand total without recalculating it from the accompanying list.
