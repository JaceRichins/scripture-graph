# Testimonial And Tradition Evidence in 1 Corinthians 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Paul's argument, the ordered appearance list functions as testimonial support for the claim that Christ rose, especially because Paul names individuals and says many members of one large group remained alive. As a single textual witness, however, the list is not independent verification of every appearance.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not independently verify the identities of all witnesses, the nature of each experience, the size of the group, or the historical truth of the resurrection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Recipients may have understood some appearances as visionary experiences rather than encounters of the same kind.
- Paul may be transmitting communal tradition without personally verifying every named appearance.
- The list's rhetorical purpose may encourage selection and compression of traditions.
- Different explanations can be proposed for the origin and development of collective resurrection belief.
