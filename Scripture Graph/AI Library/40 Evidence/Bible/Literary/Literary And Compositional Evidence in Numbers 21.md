# Literary And Compositional Evidence in Numbers 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The named book and the separately introduced songs are substantial internal evidence that the chapter's narrator presents material from identifiable traditional or written sources. They are not, by themselves, reliable evidence for a precise date or author of the chapter's final form.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish Mosaic authorship, the date of the cited book, the original setting of every poem, or the historical accuracy of the narrated campaigns.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some quoted material may have circulated orally rather than in a stable written source.
- The literary introductions could be editorial framing added considerably later than the events described.
- Ancient-style poetry or source attribution could be preserved, adapted, or imitated by a later writer.
