# Scriptural And Narrative Convergence in John 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John's repeated Passover-preparation setting and his fulfillment statement about unbroken bones strongly support reading the death scene through Passover imagery. This is a substantial literary-theological connection, though the precise scriptural source of John 19:36 and the intended extent of the lamb typology remain debated.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish every proposed detail of Passover typology, the exact historical calendar, or that narrative symbolism caused the soldiers' actions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- John 19:36 may primarily invoke Psalm 34:20 and the protection of a righteous sufferer.
- The unbroken legs have an immediate narrative cause: Jesus was already dead.
- The preparation notices may principally explain the urgency of removing and burying the body.
