# Extrabiblical Historical Corroboration in Jeremiah 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Battle of Carchemish (605 BC) described in Jeremiah 46:2-12 is independently attested outside the Bible in the Babylonian Chronicle series (the tablet often called the 'Nebuchadnezzar Chronicle,' BM 21946), which records Nebuchadnezzar (then crown prince) defeating an Egyptian army at Carchemish on the Euphrates.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish that the poem in verses 3-12 was written or delivered before the battle's outcome was known, only that the battle itself, as historically dated and located, is corroborated by a non-biblical source.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Independent attestation confirms the battle occurred but does not by itself confirm that Jeremiah 46:2's heading was composed before the outcome was known; the verse could equally be an after-the-fact editorial dateline rather than a predictive element.
- Chronicle accounts and prophetic poetry describe events from different genres and purposes, so close correspondence in basic facts (place, combatants, outcome) does not establish anything about the poem's date of composition relative to the battle.

**Scripture:** [[Jeremiah 46#^jer-46-2|Jeremiah 46:2]]
