# Literary-Cultic Pattern in Zechariah 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated references to horse equipment, temple pots, altar bowls, and every pot in Jerusalem and Judah provide strong literary evidence that pervasive holiness is a principal endpoint of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish when or how this ideal will be historically realized.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may describe ideal temple purity through rhetorical hyperbole rather than the literal consecration of every vessel.
- The closing verses may primarily remove distinctions between ordinary and cultic utensils rather than teach a general doctrine of everyday sanctification.
