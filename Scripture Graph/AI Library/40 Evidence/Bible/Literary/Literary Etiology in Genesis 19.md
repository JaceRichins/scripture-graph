# Literary Etiology in Genesis 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The naming notices for Moab and Ben-ammi give the cave episode an etiological function: the story explains, from the narrator's perspective, the origins of the neighboring Moabite and Ammonite peoples.

**Does not establish:** The etiological function does not by itself establish or disprove the episode's historicity, nor does it independently date the final form of the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The notices may preserve claimed genealogy rather than functioning only as literary explanations.
- A narrative can be understood as historical by its transmitters while also serving an etiological purpose.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 19#^gen-19-37|Genesis 19:37-38]]
