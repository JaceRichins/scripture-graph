# Internal Literary Evidence in 1 Corinthians 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The statement that Paul had already written 'in an epistle,' followed by a clarification of what that instruction meant, is strong internal evidence for correspondence with Corinth preceding this passage.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the earlier communication was regarded as scripture, nor does it permit reconstruction of that letter beyond the instruction summarized here.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording has sometimes been read as referring back to material within the present letter rather than to a separate earlier letter.
- Even if a prior letter existed, the passage alone does not disclose its full contents or preservation history.
