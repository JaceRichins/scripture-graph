# Synoptic Sequence Parallel in Luke 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of the grain-field controversy beside the healing of the withered hand closely parallels Mark and Matthew. The shared pairing strongly supports a common narrative tradition, though it does not by itself prove direct dependence on a particular written Gospel.

**Does not establish:** The sequence alone does not establish the direction of literary dependence, the exact wording of the earliest account, or that both events occurred on consecutive Sabbaths.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The evangelists may share oral tradition rather than direct written dependence.
- The episodes may have circulated as an established paired controversy collection.
- A later common source could account for the sequence.
