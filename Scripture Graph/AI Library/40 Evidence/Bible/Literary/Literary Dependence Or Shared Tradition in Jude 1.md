# Literary Dependence Or Shared Tradition in Jude 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jude 1:14–15 corresponds closely to the judgment oracle preserved in 1 Enoch 1:9. This is strong evidence that Jude knew the Enochic prophecy through a written form or a closely related stream of Jewish tradition.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Jude endorsed every part of 1 Enoch, that 1 Enoch belongs in the canon, or that Jude used precisely the manuscript form now preserved.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jude could have received the prophecy through oral tradition rather than directly reading an extant form of 1 Enoch.
- Jude and the surviving Enochic text could preserve a shared earlier form of the saying.
