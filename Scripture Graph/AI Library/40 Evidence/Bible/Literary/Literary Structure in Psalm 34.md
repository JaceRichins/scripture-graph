# Literary Structure in Psalm 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Hebrew text shows deliberate alphabetic construction: Psalm 34:1-21 begins successively with letters from aleph through tav, except that waw is omitted; Psalm 34:22 then adds a second pe-initial line as a concluding redemption statement. This is strong evidence of conscious literary shaping, although the pattern is largely invisible in English translation.

**Does not establish:** The acrostic does not by itself establish the psalm's author, date, historical setting, or supernatural origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The omitted waw may reflect textual history rather than the poet's original design.
- The final pe line may be a later addition, though it also functions coherently as a redemption coda.
