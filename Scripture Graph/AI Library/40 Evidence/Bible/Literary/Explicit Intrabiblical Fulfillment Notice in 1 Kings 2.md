# Explicit Intrabiblical Fulfillment Notice in 1 Kings 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit fulfillment notice strongly establishes that the narrator wants Abiathar's deposition read in continuity with the judgment on Eli's house. It also allows political and prophetic explanations to coexist: Solomon removes an opponent while the narrator assigns the event a place in a longer theological history.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the date of composition, the complete historical process behind Abiathar's removal, or that political motives were absent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment notice may be a later literary-theological interpretation of a political change.
- Abiathar's support for Adonijah provides an immediate political explanation that need not be displaced by the prophetic explanation.

**Scripture:** [[1 Kings 2#^1kgs-2-26|1 Kings 2:26-27]], [[1 Samuel 2#^1sam-2-27|1 Samuel 2:27-36]]
