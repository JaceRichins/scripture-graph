# Internal Narrative Evaluation in 2 Kings 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter gives Jehu explicit approval for carrying out judgment upon Ahab's house, but it does not give him unqualified approval: the narrator immediately identifies his continued adherence to Jeroboam's calves and his failure to walk in the law with all his heart. This is strong internal evidence that success in a particular commission is distinguished from comprehensive covenant faithfulness.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the moral status of every killing, the Baal stratagem, or Jehu's private motives.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Verse 30 may be read as approving the entire campaign against Ahab's house more broadly than the immediate wording specifies.
- The criticism of Jehu's calf worship need not imply disapproval of every violent or deceptive action reported earlier.
