# Explicit Prophetic Statement in Isaiah 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Isaiah 27:12-13 is direct textual evidence for a promised gathering of dispersed Israelites that culminates in worship of the LORD. The text clearly supports the gathering theme, while the historical scale, date, and mechanism of fulfillment remain interpretive questions.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish a date, a modern institutional fulfillment, a single end-time event, or the complete geographical scope of the gathering.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may primarily anticipate a historical return from Assyria and Egypt.
- The named regions may function as representative endpoints for a broader gathering.
- Religious traditions may apply the passage typologically to later or future gatherings.
