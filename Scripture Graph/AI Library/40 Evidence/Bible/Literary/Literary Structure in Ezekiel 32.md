# Literary Structure in Ezekiel 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated references to graves, the pit, uncircumcised status, and death by the sword organize the second oracle into a deliberate catalogue of formerly terrifying peoples who now share one humiliating fate.

**Does not establish:** The literary pattern does not establish a literal map of the spirit world or identify the precise compositional history of the oracle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may arise from conventional ancient lament and burial language rather than from an elaborate formal design.
- The catalogue may have undergone editorial shaping while still functioning coherently in its canonical form.
