# Literary Numerology in Revelation 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The number 144,000 equals twelve times twelve times one thousand and recurs in Revelation 7. That patterned form supports reading the number as a symbolically ordered covenant community, but it does not exclude an intended literal enumeration.

**Does not establish:** The arithmetic pattern alone does not determine who belongs to the group or prove that the number cannot be literal.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The number may describe an exact, limited group.
- It may be simultaneously symbolic and referential rather than exclusively one or the other.
- Its primary function may be to connect Revelation 14 with the tribal enumeration in Revelation 7.

**Scripture:** [[Revelation 14#^rev-14-1|Revelation 14:1]], [[Revelation 14#^rev-14-3|Revelation 14:3]], [[Revelation 7#^rev-7-4|Revelation 7:4]]
