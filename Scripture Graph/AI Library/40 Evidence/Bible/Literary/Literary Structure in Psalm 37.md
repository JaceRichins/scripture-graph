# Literary Structure in Psalm 37

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In Hebrew, Psalm 37 is organized as an alphabetic acrostic, although the pattern is not visible in the English translation. This is strong evidence of deliberate literary organization and may reflect mnemonic or aesthetic design.

**Does not establish:** The acrostic does not establish Davidic authorship, a specific date, historical circumstances, or supernatural origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The acrostic may primarily serve memory, aesthetics, or a sense of completeness rather than encode an additional theological argument.
- Some features of the received arrangement could reflect editorial or scribal shaping rather than a single original compositional act.

**Scripture:** [[Psalm 37#^ps-37-1|Psalm 37:1-40]]

**Sources:**
- Robert Alter — *The Book of Psalms: A Translation with Commentary* (2007)
- Adele Berlin and Marc Zvi Brettler, editors — *The Jewish Study Bible, Second Edition* (2014)
