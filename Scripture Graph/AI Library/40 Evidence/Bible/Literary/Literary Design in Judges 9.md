# Literary Design in Judges 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of Jotham's reciprocal-fire pronouncement, the literal burning of Shechem's tower, the failed repetition at Thebez, the stone reversal, and the narrator's closing explanation is strong evidence that the extant chapter has been shaped as a coherent account of reciprocal retribution.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not establish the historical accuracy of every reported detail, a single compositional stage, or a particular date or author.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some correspondences may arise from editorial arrangement of earlier traditions rather than from a single original composition.
- The stone correspondence could be coincidental even though the fire and retribution correspondences are explicit.
- The concluding interpretation may impose coherence on events that developed through ordinary political conflict.
