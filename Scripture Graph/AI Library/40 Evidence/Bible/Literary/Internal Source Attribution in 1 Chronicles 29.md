# Internal Source Attribution in 1 Chronicles 29

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closing notice claims that accounts of David's reign were written in works associated with Samuel, Nathan, and Gad. This is evidence for the Chronicler's presentation of David's history as grounded in earlier prophetic records, but the notice alone cannot demonstrate the independence, date, contents, or survival of those works.

**Does not establish:** The notice does not establish that three independent manuscripts survive, nor does it independently verify every event narrated about David.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The titles may refer to distinct prophetic documents no longer extant.
- They may designate sections or forms of material incorporated into known biblical books.
- The notice may function as a conventional citation that lends authority to the account.
- The three designations may overlap rather than identify three wholly independent sources.
