# Narrative Prophecy-Outcome Correspondence in Jeremiah 38

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jeremiah 38 predicts Jerusalem's capture and, conditionally on Zedekiah's refusal to surrender, his capture and the city's burning; the following narrative records Jerusalem's fall, Zedekiah's capture, and the burning of the city. This strongly supports coherence between the oracle and the book's narrated outcome.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish supernatural foreknowledge, the oracle's precise date, or complete independence among the accounts.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prediction may have been recorded or edited after the outcome was known.
- Babylon's military superiority could have made the predicted result foreseeable without supernatural revelation.
- Jeremiah and Kings may preserve related literary or historical traditions rather than wholly independent testimony.
