# Internal Literary Pattern in 1 Samuel 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated conjunction of Saul's hostile measures, David's success, the LORD's presence, public love, and Saul's escalating fear is strong internal evidence that the chapter has been shaped around a reversal pattern: opposition meant to destroy David instead advances his standing.

**Does not establish:** The literary pattern does not by itself establish the historicity of every event, supernatural causation, or a particular theory of the chapter's composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The pattern may result from ordinary narrative selection rather than a deliberately formal literary design.
- Some repetitions may reflect the combination or editing of earlier traditions about David's rise.
