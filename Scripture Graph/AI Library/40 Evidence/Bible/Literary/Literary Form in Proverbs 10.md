# Literary Form in Proverbs 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's numerous balanced contrasts strongly support identifying antithetical parallelism as a principal literary technique. This form makes moral distinctions memorable, but by itself says little about the date or individual authorship of the sayings.

**Does not establish:** The literary form does not establish who composed or compiled the chapter, when that occurred, or whether every contrast is an exceptionless promise.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some individual verses may be better classified as comparison, consequence, or synthetic parallelism rather than strict antithetical parallelism.
