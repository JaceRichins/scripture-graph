# Internal Literary Structure in Numbers 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated burden language and the LORD's paired responses give the chapter strong narrative coherence: the leadership burden is answered by the seventy elders, while the demand for flesh is answered by quail followed by judgment.

**Does not establish:** This structural coherence does not by itself establish single authorship, the date of composition, or the historicity of the narrated events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence may result from editorial arrangement of originally separate traditions.
- Repeated vocabulary may arise naturally from the subject matter rather than from a deliberately designed literary structure.
