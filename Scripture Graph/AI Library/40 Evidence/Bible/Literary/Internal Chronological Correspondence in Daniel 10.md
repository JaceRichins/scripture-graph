# Internal Chronological Correspondence in Daniel 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Daniel's three full weeks of mourning correspond exactly to the messenger's twenty-one days of resistance by the prince of Persia. This is strong internal evidence that the narrative deliberately connects Daniel's sustained seeking with an unseen delay in the response.

**Does not establish:** The numerical correspondence does not independently establish the historical occurrence or metaphysical nature of the heavenly conflict.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The matching periods may primarily be a literary device designed to integrate the earthly and heavenly scenes.
- The correspondence does not by itself determine whether the narrated conflict should be read literally, symbolically, or both.
