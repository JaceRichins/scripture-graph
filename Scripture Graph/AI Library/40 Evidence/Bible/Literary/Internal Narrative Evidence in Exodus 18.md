# Internal Narrative Evidence in Exodus 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within this narrative, the diagnosis, proposed remedy, and Moses' implementation provide strong evidence that distributed adjudication is being commended as a wise response to an unsustainable workload.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the precise hierarchy of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens is a permanently binding ecclesiastical or civil blueprint.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may be descriptive of Israel's circumstances rather than a universal prescription for every institution.
- The numerical hierarchy may reflect conventional organizational language or an idealized arrangement.
- The reform addresses judicial access and workload; applying it to unrelated forms of leadership requires additional reasoning.
