# Internal Narrative Fulfillment in Jeremiah 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the chapter's own narrative, Hananiah's death in the seventh month directly and promptly corresponds to Jeremiah's prediction that Hananiah would die that year. This strongly supports the chapter's literary presentation of Jeremiah, rather than Hananiah, as the prophet whose warning is vindicated.

**Does not establish:** The internal correspondence does not independently establish the date of composition, the absence of retrospective shaping, or supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prediction and fulfillment are preserved in the same account rather than in independent contemporary sources.
- The account may have been composed or edited after Hananiah's death.
- Jeremiah may have made a successful prediction without this by itself demonstrating supernatural revelation.
