# Parallel-Narrative Chronology in John 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John places Pilate's presentation of Jesus at about the sixth hour, whereas Mark 15:25 assigns the crucifixion to the third hour. The surface difference is a genuine chronological difficulty, although approximate expressions, different timekeeping conventions, or different events being timed may affect the comparison.

**Does not establish:** The difference does not by itself establish either a successful harmonization or an irreconcilable contradiction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- John and Mark may use different systems for reckoning hours.
- One or both time statements may be deliberately approximate.
- John times the sentencing or presentation, while Mark times the crucifixion.
- Textual transmission or narrative-theological shaping may have contributed to the difference.
