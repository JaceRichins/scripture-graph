# Literary Pattern in Ezekiel 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Three closely patterned episodes recount rebellion, threatened fury, and divine restraint: Israel in Egypt, the first wilderness generation, and their children. The recurrence is strong internal evidence that the historical review has been deliberately organized around persistent rebellion and the preservation of the LORD's name.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish the historical completeness of the review, its compositional history, or the identity of every contributor to its final form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may partly reflect conventional prophetic formulas rather than a uniquely designed pattern.
- The pattern could reflect editorial arrangement as well as, or instead of, composition in a single setting.
