# Literary Structure in Psalm 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from the LORD's world, to the LORD's holy place, to the entrance of the LORD as King supplies evidence that the three sections have been deliberately arranged as a coherent whole: the universal owner is the holy God whom the righteous seek and the glorious King whom the gates receive.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish common authorship, an exact date, a specific ritual occasion, or the process by which the psalm reached its present form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Originally independent poetic units may have been combined by an editor who created or recognized thematic continuity.
- The perceived progression may partly reflect a reader's synthesis rather than a single author's compositional plan.
