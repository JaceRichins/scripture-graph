# Entity Disambiguation in Numbers 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Local genealogical context shows that Noah in this chapter is a daughter of Zelophehad and Sarah is a daughter of Asher; these names therefore must not be identified merely by surface matching with the patriarch Noah or Abraham's wife Sarah.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every repeated biblical name has a different bearer; the immediate genealogy must decide each case.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An index may intentionally group identical English name forms while retaining separate underlying entities.
- Translation and transliteration conventions can cause different Hebrew names to appear similar or identical in English.
