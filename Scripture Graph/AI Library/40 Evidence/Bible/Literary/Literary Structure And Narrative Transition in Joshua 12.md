# Literary Structure And Narrative Transition in Joshua 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's two introductory formulas, compact territorial descriptions, repeated tally, and concluding total provide strong internal evidence that Joshua 12 functions as a deliberate retrospective summary. Its emphasis on territory given for tribal possession also prepares for the allocation material that follows.

**Does not establish:** The summary form does not establish when the list was composed, whether it derives from eyewitness records, or whether every entry preserves an independent campaign.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The list may have originated as an independent administrative or archival tradition before being placed in its present narrative setting.
- Some of its transitional effect may result from later editorial arrangement rather than the design of a single author.
