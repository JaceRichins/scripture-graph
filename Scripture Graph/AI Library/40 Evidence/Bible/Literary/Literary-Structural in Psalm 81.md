# Literary-Structural in Psalm 81

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from commanded commemoration, to remembered deliverance, to exclusive allegiance, disobedience, judgment, and forfeited blessings strongly supports reading Psalm 81 as covenant exhortation. The more specific label 'covenant lawsuit' remains debatable because the psalm does not formally identify a judicial setting.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not establish the psalm's date, author, precise festival setting, or use in a formal covenant-renewal rite.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The sequence may be a general liturgical meditation on Israel's history rather than a formal covenant-renewal ceremony.
- The judicial features may reflect ordinary prophetic exhortation without constituting a distinct covenant-lawsuit genre.
