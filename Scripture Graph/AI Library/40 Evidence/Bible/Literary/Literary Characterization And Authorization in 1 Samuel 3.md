# Literary Characterization And Authorization in 1 Samuel 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter supplies cumulative internal evidence for its characterization of Samuel as an authorized prophet: divine initiative, receipt of a difficult oracle, complete delivery, continuing divine presence, dependable words, national recognition, and renewed revelation.

**Does not establish:** It establishes how the text authorizes Samuel within its narrative, not by itself the historical or divine origin of that authorization.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A narrator can construct a coherent authorization account to legitimate a revered leader.
- Literary coherence does not determine whether every narrated event occurred historically as described.
