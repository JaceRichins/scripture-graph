# Citation Of Extracanonical Literature in Jude 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The quotation in Jude 1:14-15 closely parallels 1 Enoch 1:9, part of the Jewish apocalyptic book of 1 Enoch, which is outside the Protestant and Catholic biblical canon (though the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church includes it). Jude is the only New Testament writer to name Enoch as the source of a direct quotation.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Jude regarded 1 Enoch as scripture in the same sense as the Old Testament; ancient authors (e.g., Paul in Acts 17:28 and Titus 1:12) cited non-scriptural sources for their true or useful content without treating them as canonical.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Jude may be drawing on a shared oral prophetic tradition attributed to Enoch rather than the specific literary text of 1 Enoch
- the wording could reflect a paraphrase or targum-like tradition rather than a fixed source text
