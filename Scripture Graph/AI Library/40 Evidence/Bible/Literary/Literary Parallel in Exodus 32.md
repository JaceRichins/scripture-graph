# Literary Parallel in Exodus 32

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The calf acclamation in Exodus 32 has a close verbal parallel in Jeroboam's calf narrative in 1 Kings 12. This strongly supports a literary or traditional relationship between the passages, but direction of dependence and compositional history cannot be determined from the verbal resemblance alone.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish which text was composed first, a specific author, or a complete theory of Israelite religious development.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later account may intentionally echo the Exodus tradition to condemn Jeroboam.
- Both passages may preserve a shared Israelite formula or tradition.
- Editorial shaping may have strengthened the verbal correspondence.
