# Literary Thematic Pattern in Proverbs 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence and intersection of speech, heart, correction, household, and way imagery provide moderately strong evidence that Proverbs 15 can be studied as a thematically coherent collection, even though it does not unfold as one continuous argument.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not establish single authorship, a precise compositional process, deliberate placement of every saying, or the historical date of the collection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence may result partly from later collection or arrangement rather than original composition as a unified chapter.
- Because wisdom sayings frequently reuse common themes, some clustering may arise from genre conventions rather than deliberate chapter-level design.
- Modern chapter divisions may encourage readers to perceive unity more strongly than the underlying collection warrants.
