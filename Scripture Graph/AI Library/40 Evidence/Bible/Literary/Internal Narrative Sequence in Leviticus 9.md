# Internal Narrative Sequence in Leviticus 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, the sequence from commanded ritual to divine glory and consuming fire strongly supports reading the final manifestation as divine ratification of the newly inaugurated priestly service.

**Does not establish:** The narrative framing does not independently establish the historical occurrence, supernatural mechanism, or date of the reported manifestation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fire may principally signify divine presence or holiness rather than approval alone.
- The episode may employ a conventional literary motif of heavenly fire to express acceptance.
- If a historical event underlies the account, the text does not permit reconstruction of its physical mechanism.
