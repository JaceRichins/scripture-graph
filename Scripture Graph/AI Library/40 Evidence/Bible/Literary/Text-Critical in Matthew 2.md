# Text-Critical in Matthew 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Hosea 11:1, quoted in Matthew 2:15 as 'Out of Egypt have I called my son,' refers in its original context to God's past deliverance of the nation of Israel from Egyptian bondage in the Exodus, not to a predicted future individual; Matthew's application to Jesus is a typological (sensus plenior) reading rather than a verbatim predictive fulfillment.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Hosea originally predicted Jesus, nor that Matthew's typological method is illegitimate; it clarifies the genre of the claimed 'fulfillment.'

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some interpreters hold that Hosea's wording carries a latent messianic resonance disclosed only retrospectively, making Matthew's use a legitimate 'fuller sense' rather than a reinterpretation
- Matthew may intend 'fulfilled' in a broad pattern/typological sense throughout his Gospel rather than as verbal prediction in each case
