# Literary Characterization in Acts 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between Saul's intended arrests, his immediate proclamation of Jesus, and subsequent threats against his own life provides strong textual evidence that Acts portrays a radical reversal of allegiance and vocation.

**Does not establish:** This literary reversal does not by itself prove every historical detail of the encounter or determine the best modern category for Saul's experience.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The episode may be described more precisely as a prophetic call or commission than as conversion from one religion to another.
- The narrative may compress a more gradual development in Saul's understanding and public activity.
