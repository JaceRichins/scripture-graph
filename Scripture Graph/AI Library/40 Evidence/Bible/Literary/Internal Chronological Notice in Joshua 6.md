# Internal Chronological Notice in Joshua 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The phrase stating that Rahab dwells in Israel 'even unto this day' indicates that the narrator speaks from a time later than the narrated conquest. It supplies evidence of temporal distance but does not identify how much time had elapsed.

**Does not establish:** The notice does not establish a precise composition date, the length of the interval, or whether Rahab herself was still living when the book reached its final form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The phrase may derive from an earlier source retained by a later compiler.
- The expression may be formulaic and therefore provide only weak leverage for dating the final form of Joshua.

**Scripture:** [[Joshua 6#^josh-6-25|Joshua 6:25]]
