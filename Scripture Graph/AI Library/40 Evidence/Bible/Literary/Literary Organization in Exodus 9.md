# Literary Organization in Exodus 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated demands, warning-and-fulfillment sequences, geographic and behavioral contrasts, and Pharaoh's confession followed by renewed resistance provide strong evidence that Exodus 9 has been deliberately shaped as a coherent literary narrative.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not establish historicity, eyewitness reporting, the narrative's date, single authorship, or the supernatural causation it reports.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some patterns may arise from conventional oral storytelling rather than composition by one author.
- A compiler or editor could have produced coherence from earlier materials.
- Repetition may partly reflect the conventional structure of plague narratives.
