# Literary-Form Evidence in Micah 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from summons and controversy, through a recital of divine benefits, to accusations and sentence is strong internal evidence that Micah 6 employs sustained judicial or covenant-lawsuit rhetoric.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the chapter records an actual court proceeding or that every detail derives from a fixed legal formula.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may use general prophetic disputation rather than conforming to a single formal lawsuit genre.
- Modern structural divisions and assignments of speakers are interpretive rather than explicitly marked in the text.
