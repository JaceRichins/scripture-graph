# Narrative-Purpose And Parable Structure in Luke 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of departure, reception of a kingdom, return, and subsequent accounting strongly supports reading the parable as correcting an expectation of immediate consummation and emphasizing responsible action during an interval. It does not supply a timetable for the kingdom's final appearance.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a date for the kingdom, the duration of the interval, or a one-to-one identification for every character and action.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parable may primarily teach stewardship and judgment rather than define a developed doctrine of eschatological delay.
- The departure and return may draw on familiar political experience without every detail functioning allegorically.
