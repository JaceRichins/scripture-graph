# Explicit Narrative Statement in 2 Samuel 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
David dedicated to the LORD the valuables brought by Joram together with silver and gold obtained from subdued peoples. This is strong textual evidence that the narrative presents at least part of the wealth of conquest as religiously dedicated rather than merely retained as David's personal property.

**Does not establish:** The dedication does not establish that the campaigns themselves were commanded by God, that all captured wealth was dedicated, or that no political benefit accrued to David.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Dedication could also function as royal legitimation or as an accounting designation for a state sanctuary treasury.
- The text does not specify when, where, or how the dedicated wealth was subsequently used.
