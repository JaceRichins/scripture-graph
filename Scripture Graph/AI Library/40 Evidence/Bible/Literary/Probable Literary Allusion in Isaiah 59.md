# Probable Literary Allusion in Isaiah 59

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The breastplate of righteousness and helmet of salvation in Ephesians 6:14–17 closely parallel the LORD's armor in Isaiah 59:17. This strongly supports an allusive relationship, although Ephesians adapts the imagery by placing God's armor on believers engaged in spiritual conflict.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallels do not establish a direct quotation or make the divine warrior and the armored believer identical figures.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages may draw independently on conventional armor imagery.
- Ephesians may combine Isaiah 59 with other scriptural warrior and armor passages rather than alluding exclusively to this chapter.
