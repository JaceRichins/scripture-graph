# Canonical Verbal Parallel in Proverbs 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The maxim in Proverbs 1:7 has close canonical parallels in Psalm 111:10 and Proverbs 9:10. This recurrence is strong evidence that fear of the Lord is a framing theme in biblical wisdom discourse and especially in Proverbs, but it does not by itself determine the literary dependence or authorship of the passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish which passage came first, common human authorship, or a precise date of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passages may independently employ a traditional wisdom maxim rather than directly quoting one another.
- Similar wording may reflect later editorial organization of a shared theological theme.
