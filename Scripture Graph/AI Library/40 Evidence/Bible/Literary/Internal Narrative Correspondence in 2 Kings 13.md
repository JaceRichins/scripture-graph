# Internal Narrative Correspondence in 2 Kings 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Elisha predicts that Jehoash will defeat Syria three times, and the chapter closes by reporting three victories and the recovery of Israelite cities. This is strong evidence of deliberate internal correspondence between the prophetic episode and the historical conclusion.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not establish when the prediction was recorded, the precise historical sequence of composition, or supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator or editor may have shaped the prophecy and fulfillment accounts together after the victories occurred.
- A traditional memory of three victories may have generated or influenced the enacted-prophecy account.
- The number three may have been preserved or stylized for literary symmetry.
