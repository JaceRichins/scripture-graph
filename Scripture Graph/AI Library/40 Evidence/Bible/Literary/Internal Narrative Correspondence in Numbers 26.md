# Internal Narrative Correspondence in Numbers 26

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Numbers' own narrative, the absence of the previously numbered men except Caleb and Joshua provides strong literary evidence that the announced wilderness judgment has reached fulfillment by the time of the Moab census.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical accuracy of the census totals or every reported wilderness death.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The correspondence may reflect deliberate literary or editorial shaping of the narrative.
- The census account alone does not independently verify the historical size or mortality of the wilderness population.
