# Canonical-Citation in Genesis 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Genesis 2:24 is cited as authoritative teaching on marriage elsewhere in scripture, including by Jesus in Matthew 19:5 and by Paul in Ephesians 5:31, showing the verse functioned as a foundational marriage text well beyond its narrative context.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish a doctrine of marriage's eternal duration; it shows only that the verse was treated as authoritative regarding marriage's origin and nature in later scripture.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 2#^gen-2-24|Genesis 2:24]], [[Matthew 19#^matt-19-5|Matthew 19:5]], [[Ephesians 5#^eph-5-31|Ephesians 5:31]]
