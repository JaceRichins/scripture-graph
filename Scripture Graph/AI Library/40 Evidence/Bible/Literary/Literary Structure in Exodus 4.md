# Literary Structure in Exodus 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit reversal from “they will not believe me” to “the people believed” strongly supports reading verse 31 as the resolution of Moses's opening objection. It is good evidence for deliberate narrative coherence, although the simple completion of a plot problem is an adequate alternative to a formal inclusio.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the chapter's date, single authorship, ancient historicity, or the historical occurrence of the signs.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may be ordinary plot resolution rather than a deliberately constructed formal inclusio.
- The final belief may chiefly advance the narrative into the confrontation with Pharaoh rather than emphasize literary symmetry.
