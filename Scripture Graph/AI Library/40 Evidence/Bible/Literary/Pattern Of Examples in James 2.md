# Pattern Of Examples in James 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
James's examples give strong evidence that 'works' includes concrete, costly responses rather than verbal profession alone: material aid for the needy, Abraham's offering of Isaac, and Rahab's reception and protection of the messengers.

**Does not establish:** The examples do not establish that human deeds operate independently of divine grace or that every deed has justifying force.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The examples illustrate works but do not exhaustively define every kind of work James may have in view.
- Abraham and Rahab may have been selected chiefly as traditional scriptural exemplars rather than to establish a systematic category of works.
