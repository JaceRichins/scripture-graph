# Literary-Theological Synthesis in Psalm 119

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close interweaving of declarations, petitions, laments, praise, and resolutions is strong internal evidence that the psalm treats engagement with divine law as a form of prayerful relationship, not merely the acquisition of legal information.

**Does not establish:** This literary pattern does not establish that prayer and commandment keeping are identical or that verbal devotion can replace ethical obedience.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The varied speech forms may reflect poetic convention without implying a single developed theology of scripture study.
- Some readers may emphasize covenant loyalty or wisdom instruction rather than prayerful relationship as the poem's controlling category.
