# Intrabook Literary Parallel in Judges 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The encounter resembles Gideon's commissioning in Judges 6: an initially unrecognized divine messenger, preparation of food or an offering, a miraculous fire-associated sign, recognition of the messenger, and fear connected with seeing the angel of the LORD. The repetitions form a credible literary parallel within Judges.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not prove common authorship, direct dependence, identical theology, or the historicity of either encounter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The similarities may arise from a conventional ancient messenger-encounter pattern rather than direct literary imitation.
- A compiler or editor may have shaped distinct traditions into a recurring pattern.
