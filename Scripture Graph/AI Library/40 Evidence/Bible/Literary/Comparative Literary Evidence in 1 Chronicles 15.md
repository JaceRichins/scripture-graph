# Comparative Literary Evidence in 1 Chronicles 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Comparison with the parallel account in 2 Samuel provides strong literary evidence that 1 Chronicles emphasizes ordered Levitical worship: it expands the narrative with named divisions, sanctification, prescribed carrying, skilled music, doorkeepers, and priestly trumpeters.

**Does not establish:** The comparison does not establish the exact compositional process, the historical reliability of every listed name, or the precise date of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The additional detail may derive from a fuller source rather than being composed solely to advance a theological program.
- Some differences may reflect the distinct scope and audience of each account rather than disagreement about the event.
- Narrative emphasis does not by itself determine which account preserves more complete historical detail.
