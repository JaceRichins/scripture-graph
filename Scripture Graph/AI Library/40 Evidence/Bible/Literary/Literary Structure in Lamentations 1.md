# Literary Structure in Lamentations 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In Hebrew, the chapter's twenty-two verses form an alphabetic acrostic, with successive verses beginning with successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet. This is strong evidence of deliberate formal organization, even though the pattern is not preserved visibly in the English translation.

**Does not establish:** The acrostic does not establish Jeremian authorship, a particular date, inspiration, or any single interpretation of why alphabetical order was chosen.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The acrostic may primarily aid composition, memory, or performance rather than symbolize comprehensive grief.
- Later editorial shaping could account for the form without identifying the original author or date.

**Scripture:** [[Lamentations 1#^lam-1-1|Lamentations 1:1-22]]
