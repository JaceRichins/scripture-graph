# Internal Literary Evidence in 1 Kings 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of the maternity dispute immediately after the divine grant, together with the conclusion that Israel recognized God's wisdom in Solomon, is strong internal literary evidence that the narrator intends the judgment to demonstrate fulfillment of the promised wisdom.

**Does not establish:** This narrative correspondence does not independently establish the episode's historicity, a supernatural cause for Solomon's ability, or the reliability of similar tests in real legal disputes.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may also function as royal legitimation or an idealized wisdom tale.
- Its literary function does not determine whether every reported detail is independently historical.
