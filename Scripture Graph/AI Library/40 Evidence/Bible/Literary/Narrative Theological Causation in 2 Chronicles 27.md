# Narrative Theological Causation in 2 Chronicles 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 6 strongly supports the conclusion that this narrator interprets Jotham's might as connected to his ordered conduct before God. Because the statement concerns Jotham's particular reign, it provides weak evidence for—and does not establish—an exceptionless rule that obedience always produces political, military, or material success.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every faithful person or ruler will receive military victory, wealth, political power, or freedom from suffering.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statement may summarize the Chronicler's theological interpretation of this reign rather than formulate a universal law.
- Jotham's military and building strength may also have involved political, economic, and administrative causes not described in this short account.
