# Scriptural Intertext in Lamentations 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The report that women cooked their own children closely parallels the siege curse of Deuteronomy 28:53 and the related lament in Lamentations 2:20. The correspondence supports reading Lamentations 4 within Israel's covenant-judgment vocabulary.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself prove direct literary dependence, the historicity of the reported act, or a specific author and date.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passages may independently describe a recognized horror of ancient siege warfare.
- Lamentations 4 may draw on a shared traditional image rather than directly quoting Deuteronomy.
- Literary correspondence alone cannot determine how literally every detail should be read.
