# Explicit Biblical Cross-Reference in Joshua 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
First Kings 16:34 explicitly presents Hiel's rebuilding of Jericho and the deaths of his firstborn and youngest son as occurring according to the word spoken through Joshua. This is strong evidence for an intentional intertextual fulfillment claim within the biblical narrative.

**Does not establish:** The literary connection by itself does not establish the independence or chronological priority of the prediction, nor does it settle the historical cause of the sons' deaths.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The author of Kings may have framed Hiel's family losses through the earlier Joshua tradition.
- The Joshua curse may have been formulated or transmitted with the later rebuilding episode in view.
