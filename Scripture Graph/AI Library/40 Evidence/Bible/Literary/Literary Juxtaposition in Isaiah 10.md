# Literary Juxtaposition in Isaiah 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The adjacent tree-felling and new-shoot images provide moderately strong literary evidence that Isaiah 10:33-34 and Isaiah 11:1 should be interpreted together as a contrast between the fall of the proud and the rise of the Davidic figure.

**Does not establish:** The juxtaposition alone does not establish the date, original compositional setting, or full messianic interpretation of Isaiah 11.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The juxtaposition may result from the final arrangement of originally distinct oracles.
- The forest and shoot images may be related thematically without every detail sharing the same historical referent.

**Scripture:** [[Isaiah 10#^isa-10-33|Isaiah 10:33-34]], [[Isaiah 11#^isa-11-1|Isaiah 11:1]]
