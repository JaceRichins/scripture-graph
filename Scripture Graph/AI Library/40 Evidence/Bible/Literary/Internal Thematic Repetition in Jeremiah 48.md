# Internal Thematic Repetition in Jeremiah 48

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit repetition of Moab's self-magnification against the LORD, reinforced by the concentrated pride vocabulary, strongly supports reading pride as a central rationale for the judgment.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that pride is the only charge against Moab, nor does it independently verify Moab's historical national psychology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Pride may be one prominent rationale among several rather than the single organizing cause.
- The repetition may partly reflect the incorporation of traditional Moab oracles.
