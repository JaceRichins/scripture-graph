# Literary Prediction-Fulfillment Correspondence in 1 Samuel 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The precise correspondence between the one-day death prediction in 1 Samuel 2:34 and the paired deaths in 1 Samuel 4:11 is strong evidence that the final-form narrative intentionally presents the battle as fulfillment of the judgment against Eli's house.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence alone does not establish when the prediction was recorded, supernatural foreknowledge, or the historical accuracy of every reported detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later narrator or editor could have formulated the earlier oracle in light of the remembered deaths.
- The account of the deaths could have been shaped to correspond with an existing oracle.
- Both passages could reflect a shared theological tradition rather than contemporaneous documentation.
