# Canonical Reception And Verbal Correspondence in Exodus 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The reuse of Exodus 24's covenant-blood language in the Last Supper traditions and Hebrews is strong evidence of the chapter's foundational importance for later biblical covenant theology.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself prove direct predictive prophecy, identical ritual meaning, or the historical truth of every event narrated.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Later authors may creatively appropriate Exodus language without claiming that its original human author foresaw their applications.
- Shared covenant vocabulary does not require identity between the Sinai ceremony and later Christian ordinances.
