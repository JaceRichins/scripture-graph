# Literary Structure in Leviticus 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The comprehensive list of offering laws in verses 37–38 is strong internal evidence that the chapter closes the sacrificial instruction collection begun in Leviticus 1. The colophon names burnt, grain, sin, trespass, consecration, and peace offerings and locates the command at Sinai.

**Does not establish:** The colophon does not establish single authorship, the date of composition, or that every regulation was practiced uniformly in every historical period.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The summary may have been supplied or shaped by an editor while still functioning as the collection's present literary conclusion.
- A closing summary identifies the present textual unit but does not by itself determine how or when its component laws were composed.
