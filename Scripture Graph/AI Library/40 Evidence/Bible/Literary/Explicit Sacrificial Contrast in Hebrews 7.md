# Explicit Sacrificial Contrast in Hebrews 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The word “once,” together with Jesus offering himself and being consecrated forever, is strong evidence that Hebrews presents his sacrifice as decisive and nonrepeatable in contrast to recurring priestly offerings.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself resolve later disputes about sacramental presence, memorial, or the application of Christ's sacrifice.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Christian traditions agree broadly on the once-for-all historical sacrifice but differ over how worship or sacramental rites participate in or represent it.
- Some details of the comparison may compress daily priestly service and the high priest's distinctive rites into one rhetorical contrast.

**Scripture:** [[Hebrews 7#^heb-7-27|Hebrews 7:27]], [[Hebrews 7#^heb-7-28|Hebrews 7:28]]
