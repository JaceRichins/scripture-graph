# Scriptural Intertext in Luke 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Jesus' report to John combines signs associated with Isaiah's restoration hopes, especially sight for the blind, hearing for the deaf, mobility for the lame, and good news for the poor. This strongly supports the interpretation that Jesus frames his works as signs of eschatological divine restoration.

**Does not establish:** The allusions do not by themselves settle every claim about Jesus' identity, the chronology of the traditions, or the historicity of each reported miracle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The list may draw upon a broad scriptural atmosphere rather than quote a single composite proof text.
- Some elements, especially cleansing lepers and raising the dead, are not supplied by the cited Isaiah passages.
- Readers may differ over whether the allusions identify Jesus explicitly as Messiah or more generally identify God's saving activity.
