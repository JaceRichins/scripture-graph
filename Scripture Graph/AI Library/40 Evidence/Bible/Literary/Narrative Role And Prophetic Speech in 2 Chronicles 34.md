# Narrative Role And Prophetic Speech in 2 Chronicles 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The royal delegation's consultation of Huldah and its return of her answer to Josiah are strong narrative evidence that she is treated as an authorized prophetic interpreter of the crisis.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish Huldah's broader office, jurisdiction, institutional rank, or whether her role was typical of prophetesses in Judah.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The episode may record an exceptional consultation rather than a general institutional pattern.
- The narrative does not explain why Huldah was chosen instead of any other prophet.
- The account's selection of Huldah does not reveal the full range of prophetic authorities then available.

**Scripture:** [[2 Chronicles 34#^2chr-34-20|2 Chronicles 34:20-28]]
