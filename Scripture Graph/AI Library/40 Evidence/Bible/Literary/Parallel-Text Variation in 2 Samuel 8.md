# Parallel-Text Variation in 2 Samuel 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closely parallel account in 1 Chronicles 18 differs at significant details: 2 Samuel reports seven hundred horsemen where Chronicles reports seven thousand, and the Valley of Salt reports differ in enemy identification and attribution. These variants are strong evidence that the surviving parallel forms should be compared critically rather than treated as identical or as independent corroboration.

**Does not establish:** The variants alone do not identify the earliest reading, prove that either complete account is historically unreliable, or justify a particular harmonization.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The numerical difference may result from scribal transmission rather than a distinct historical claim.
- The Valley of Salt differences may reflect variant source forms, editorial revision, textual corruption, or separate but related engagements.
- Some proposed harmonizations distinguish commanders, forces, or stages of one campaign, although the texts do not explicitly supply such a reconstruction.
