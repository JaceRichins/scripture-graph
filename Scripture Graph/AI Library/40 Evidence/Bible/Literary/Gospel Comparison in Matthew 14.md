# Gospel Comparison in Matthew 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Peter water-walking episode is absent from Mark's and John's parallel accounts of Jesus walking on the sea, suggesting it is unique to Matthew's telling of this shared event.

**Does not establish:** This does not establish that the Peter episode is unhistorical or a late literary invention; absence elsewhere is consistent with several explanations.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Mark and John may have known but omitted the detail rather than it being a later addition.
- Divergent oral traditions among early Christian communities could explain the difference without implying invention.
