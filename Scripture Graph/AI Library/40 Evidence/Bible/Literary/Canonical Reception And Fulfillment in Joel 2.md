# Canonical Reception And Fulfillment in Joel 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Canonical reception supplies substantial evidence for a layered Latter-day Saint reading of Joel 2:28-32: Acts applies the prophecy to Pentecost, while Joseph Smith—History reports that Moroni cited the same passage and located its fulfillment in the future relative to 1823. This supports an initial-and-further fulfillment model but does not by itself specify which details belong to each stage.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish an exhaustive chronology, identify every symbol with a single historical event, or prove that all interpreters must adopt the same model of multiple fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Peter may present Pentecost as the decisive fulfillment while some quoted cosmic details remain conventional prophetic imagery.
- Moroni may refer to a future completion or renewed application rather than denying any fulfillment at Pentecost.
- The two passages may apply Joel typologically to distinct events without implying one continuous fulfillment schedule.
