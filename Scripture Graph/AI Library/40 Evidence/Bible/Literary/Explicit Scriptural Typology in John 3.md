# Explicit Scriptural Typology in John 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 3 explicitly compares Moses lifting up the wilderness serpent with the necessary lifting up of the Son of Man, connecting belief in the Son with eternal life. This is strong textual evidence for a deliberate typological comparison, though it does not by itself define every intended correspondence between the serpent episode and Jesus.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish a complete theory of the Atonement or validate additional symbolic details not stated in the passage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The comparison may be narrowly rhetorical, emphasizing being lifted up and receiving life rather than authorizing detailed correspondences between every feature of the two events.
