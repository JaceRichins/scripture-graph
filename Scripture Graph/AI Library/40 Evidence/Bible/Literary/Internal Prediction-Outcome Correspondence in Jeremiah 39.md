# Internal Prediction-Outcome Correspondence in Jeremiah 39

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The correspondence between Jeremiah's warning in chapter 38 and the events in chapter 39 is strong evidence for the book's literary presentation of Jeremiah as a vindicated prophet. It is not, by itself, decisive historical evidence of supernatural foreknowledge because the dating and editorial history of the warning must be assessed independently.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the pre-event wording of every detail, supernatural prediction, or a particular theory of the book's composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The warning may have been shaped or expanded after Jerusalem's fall.
- An authentic pre-fall warning could have been preserved and later arranged to emphasize its fulfillment.
- Given the Babylonian siege, a disastrous outcome was foreseeable without supernatural knowledge.
