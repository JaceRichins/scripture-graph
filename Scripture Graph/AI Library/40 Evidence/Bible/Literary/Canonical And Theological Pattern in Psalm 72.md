# Canonical And Theological Pattern in Psalm 72

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of enduring reign, worldwide dominion, universal blessing, defense of the helpless, and parallels with later ideal-Davidic texts gives substantial support to a messianic reading within Christian interpretation.

**Does not establish:** These features do not prove that every line was originally intended as a direct prediction of Jesus Christ, nor do they exclude an initial historical royal application.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The universal and perpetual expressions can be understood as conventional royal hyperbole.
- The poem may first articulate hopes for Solomon or another historical Davidic king.
- Later readers may have reapplied an originally royal psalm to an anticipated Messiah.
