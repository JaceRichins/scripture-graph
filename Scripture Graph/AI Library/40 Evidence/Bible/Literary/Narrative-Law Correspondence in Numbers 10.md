# Narrative-Law Correspondence in Numbers 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The tribal sequence in Numbers 10 substantially corresponds to the camp order prescribed in Numbers 2: Judah's division leads, followed by Reuben's, Ephraim's, and finally Dan's. This is strong evidence of deliberate coordination between prescription and journey narrative.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish that the march occurred exactly as narrated, identify a date of composition, or prove single authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A narrator or editor may have shaped an idealized journey account to agree with the prescribed arrangement.
- Both chapters may preserve a common organizational tradition without having been composed together.
- Later harmonization could account for some of the correspondence.
