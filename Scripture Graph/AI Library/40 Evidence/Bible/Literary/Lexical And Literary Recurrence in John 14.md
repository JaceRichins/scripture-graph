# Lexical And Literary Recurrence in John 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The KJV's 'mansions' in verse 2 and 'abode' in verse 23 translate plural and singular forms of the same Greek noun, mone. This lexical recurrence supports a literary connection between dwelling in the Father's house and the Father and Son dwelling with the faithful disciple.

**Does not establish:** The lexical link does not by itself establish a detailed geography of heaven, graded kingdoms of glory, or a single timetable for all of Jesus's promises.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence may partly reflect ordinary dwelling vocabulary rather than a deliberately constructed inclusio.
- The two passages need not describe an identical event or location even though they share a noun.
