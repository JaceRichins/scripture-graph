# Internal Architectural Coherence in Exodus 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The measurements and pillar counts constitute strong internal evidence of a coordinated court plan: each one-hundred-cubit side has twenty pillars, the fifty-cubit west side has ten, and the fifty-cubit east side is partitioned into fifteen-, twenty-, and fifteen-cubit sections with three, four, and three pillars respectively.

**Does not establish:** The numerical coherence does not establish that the court was built, identify the date or author of the instructions, or prove that the measurements encode theological symbolism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coordination may reflect an ideal or schematic design rather than measurements taken from a historical structure.
- A compiler or editor could have harmonized inherited specifications.
- The numerical regularity may arise from ordinary practical planning without carrying additional symbolism.
