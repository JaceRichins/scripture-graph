# Explicit Scriptural Fulfillment Citation in Matthew 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew explicitly interprets Jesus's healings and exorcisms through Isaiah's servant language about bearing infirmities and sicknesses. This is strong textual evidence for Matthew's own theological reading of the healing ministry, although the precise relationship between this application, Isaiah's original context, and later doctrines of atonement remains interpretively debated.

**Does not establish:** The citation alone does not establish a complete theory of atonement, prove that every illness is removed in mortal life, or settle Isaiah 53's original historical referent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew may be applying Isaiah typologically rather than claiming a single exhaustive fulfillment.
- The citation may emphasize Jesus's healing ministry without defining how physical healing relates to forgiveness of sin or atonement.
- Differences among the Hebrew text, ancient translations, and Matthew's wording complicate claims based on exact verbal correspondence.
