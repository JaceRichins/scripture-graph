# Intra-Canonical Narrative Correspondence in 1 Samuel 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the canonical narrative, later episodes correspond to the judgment oracle: Eli's sons are killed and Eli dies after the ark is captured, while a still later narrator explicitly associates Abiathar's removal with fulfillment of the word concerning Eli's house at Shiloh. These are strong literary links within the narrative but are not independent historical corroboration of supernatural prediction.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the oracle's date, extra-textual historicity, or supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Later narrators or editors may have shaped the fulfillment accounts to correspond to the earlier oracle.
- The oracle may have been formulated or transmitted with knowledge of later events.
- The passages can demonstrate literary-theological coherence without supplying independent evidence for predictive revelation.
