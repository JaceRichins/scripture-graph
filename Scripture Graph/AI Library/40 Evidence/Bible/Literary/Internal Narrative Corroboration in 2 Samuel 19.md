# Internal Narrative Corroboration in 2 Samuel 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Mephibosheth's neglected feet, beard, and clothing from David's departure until his peaceful return provide internal narrative corroboration for his professed loyalty, but the signs are not decisive proof that every part of his accusation against Ziba is true.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Ziba lied, that Mephibosheth's account is complete, or that David's division of the land was a definitive judgment of guilt.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The neglected appearance could be a calculated display intended to support Mephibosheth's claim.
- The appearance could reflect distress or practical neglect without proving that Ziba deceived him.
- Mephibosheth may be sincere while still giving an incomplete account.
