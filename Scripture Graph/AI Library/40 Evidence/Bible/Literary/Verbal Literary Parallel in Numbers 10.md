# Verbal Literary Parallel in Numbers 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Numbers 10:35 and Psalm 68:1 share a close verbal pattern in which God arises, enemies are scattered, and those who hate him flee. This is strong evidence of a literary or traditional relationship between the expressions, but it does not identify the direction of dependence.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish literary priority, authorship, date, a specific compositional history, or the historicity of the wilderness journey.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Psalm 68 may adapt the saying preserved in Numbers.
- Numbers may reflect language also preserved or developed in the psalmic tradition.
- Both passages may draw upon a shared liturgical or sacred-war formula.
