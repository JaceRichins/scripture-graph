# Internal Narrative Evidence in Judges 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, fire consuming Gideon's food offering functions as evidence to Gideon that his visitor is an angel of the LORD. Gideon's ensuing fear, divine reassurance, and construction of an altar show that he understands the event as a genuine divine encounter.

**Does not establish:** The scene does not independently establish the event's historicity, nor does it establish that modern readers should seek the same kind of sign.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The episode may also serve as a literary authentication scene for the audience.
- A reader may accept the narrative function without treating the reported miracle as independently historically verified.
