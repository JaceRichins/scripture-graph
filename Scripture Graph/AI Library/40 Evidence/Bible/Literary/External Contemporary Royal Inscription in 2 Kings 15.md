# External Contemporary Royal Inscription in 2 Kings 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Tiglath-pileser III's inscriptions independently name Menahem of Samaria among rulers from whom payment or tribute was received. Under the standard identification of the biblical Pul with Tiglath-pileser III, this strongly corroborates the historical core of Menahem's Assyrian payment in verse 19.

**Does not establish:** This correspondence does not establish every detail of 2 Kings 15, the chapter's theological interpretation, or the accuracy of all its chronological data.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Assyrian and biblical accounts may preserve the same broad geopolitical event while independently shaping its details for royal and theological purposes.
- Royal inscriptions are propagandistic records and should not be treated as neutral descriptions.
- The inscriptions do not verify the biblical amount, collection method, or stated motive of confirming Menahem's kingdom.
