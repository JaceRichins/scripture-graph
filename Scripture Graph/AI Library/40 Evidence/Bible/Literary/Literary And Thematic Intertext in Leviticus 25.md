# Literary And Thematic Intertext in Leviticus 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared motifs of liberty, release, and an appointed favorable time make jubilee a plausible and widely recognized background for Isaiah 61:1–2 and Jesus's reading of that passage in Luke 4:18–21. The passages, however, do not explicitly identify the occasion as a calendrical jubilee.

**Does not establish:** The verbal and thematic relationship does not establish that Jesus announced a literal jubilee year or that every provision of Leviticus 25 is directly fulfilled in Luke 4.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah's liberty language can be read primarily against the return from exile rather than as a technical jubilee announcement.
- Luke may use Isaiah's language as a broader image of messianic salvation without asserting observance of the Levitical calendar.
