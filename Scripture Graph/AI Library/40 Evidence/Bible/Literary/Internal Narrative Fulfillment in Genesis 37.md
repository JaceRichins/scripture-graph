# Internal Narrative Fulfillment in Genesis 37

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within Genesis as a finished narrative, the brothers' later bowing before Joseph in Egypt strongly supports reading the sheaf dream as deliberate foreshadowing. It does not by itself prove that the dream was a supernatural revelation, because a narrative can be composed or shaped around its eventual outcome.

**Does not establish:** This correspondence alone does not establish supernatural prediction, a particular date of composition, or the historicity of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The dream may function as ordinary literary foreshadowing constructed with knowledge of the later plot.
- The account may preserve an older story subsequently arranged or edited to emphasize fulfillment.
- The brothers' bowing clearly corresponds to the first dream, while the precise fulfillment of every symbol in the second dream is less explicit.
