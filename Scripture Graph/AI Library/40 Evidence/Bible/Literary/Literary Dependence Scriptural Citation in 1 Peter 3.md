# Literary Dependence Scriptural Citation in 1 Peter 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
1 Peter 3:10-12 closely reproduces Psalm 34:12-16 in wording and order (refraining the tongue from evil, seeking peace, the Lord's eyes on the righteous and face against evildoers), constituting a direct literary quotation of the Psalm rather than a coincidental echo.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which textual form (Hebrew, Greek/LXX) of the Psalm Peter used, nor settle authorship questions about the epistle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording could reflect shared Jewish ethical/wisdom idiom circulating independently of direct literary use of the Psalm, though the close verbal sequence favors deliberate citation.
