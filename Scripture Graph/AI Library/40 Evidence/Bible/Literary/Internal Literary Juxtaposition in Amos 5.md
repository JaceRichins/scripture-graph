# Internal Literary Juxtaposition in Amos 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The immediate contrast between rejected feasts, offerings, and songs and the demand for flowing judgment and righteousness is strong internal evidence that worship severed from justice is unacceptable in this oracle.

**Does not establish:** This passage alone does not establish that Amos rejected every divinely authorized feast, sacrifice, musical practice, or assembly in all circumstances.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may reject Israel's cult more categorically rather than only worship disconnected from justice.
- The denunciation may particularly target compromised northern worship rather than every form of sacrifice.
- The absolute language may be prophetic hyperbole intended to establish moral priority.
