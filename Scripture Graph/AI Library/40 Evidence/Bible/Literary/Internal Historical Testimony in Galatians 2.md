# Internal Historical Testimony in Galatians 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Antioch episode is strong textual evidence that prominent early Christian leaders could disagree publicly in conduct and judgment over Jewish-Gentile relations.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a permanent schism, Peter's rejection of Christ, the absence of apostolic authority, or James's responsibility for Peter's withdrawal.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The dispute may have concerned inconsistent conduct under social pressure rather than disagreement about the gospel's underlying doctrine.
- Paul's account gives his own evaluation and does not preserve Peter's explanation or response.
- The people arriving from James need not have been acting with James's authorization.
