# Literary Structure in Ezekiel 25

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The consistent accusation-and-consequence structure is strong literary evidence that the chapter frames the nations' disasters as morally reasoned judgments for contempt, revenge, and destructive hostility.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the historical fulfillment, precise date, compositional history, or divine origin of the oracles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The structure may employ conventional prophetic judgment rhetoric rather than preserve the nations' motives as independently verified history.
- Some of the repeated formulas may reflect editorial organization of originally separate oracles.
