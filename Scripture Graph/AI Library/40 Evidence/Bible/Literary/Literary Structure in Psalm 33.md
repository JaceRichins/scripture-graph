# Literary Structure in Psalm 33

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from a call to praise, through reasons grounded in creation and providence, to a communal confession provides strong internal evidence that Psalm 33 functions as a coherent hymnic argument: confidence in God is offered as the practical response to his reliable word and governance.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not establish the identity of the author, the date of composition, or the precise historical setting.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some transitions may reflect conventional hymn forms rather than a tightly planned argument.
- Thematic coherence does not rule out composition from earlier poetic materials.
