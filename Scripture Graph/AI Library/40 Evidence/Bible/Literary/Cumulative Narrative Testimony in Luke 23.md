# Cumulative Narrative Testimony in Luke 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter repeatedly characterizes Jesus as innocent or righteous through testimony attributed to Pilate, the repentant malefactor, and the centurion. This convergence is strong evidence for Luke's deliberate literary and theological emphasis on Jesus's innocence, although it is not by itself independent historical corroboration of every reported declaration.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently prove the historicity or exact wording of each verdict, nor does it establish a general innocence of Roman administration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated verdicts may primarily serve Luke's theological and apologetic narrative aims.
- Multiple speakers within one authored narrative are not equivalent to multiple independent historical sources.
