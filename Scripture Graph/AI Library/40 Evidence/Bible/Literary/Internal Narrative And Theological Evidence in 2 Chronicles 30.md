# Internal Narrative And Theological Evidence in 2 Chronicles 30

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The narrative provides strong internal evidence that this chapter presents a heart prepared to seek God and Hezekiah's intercession as relevant to divine pardon in this exceptional case of ritual impurity: the LORD hears and heals the people.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that sincere intention normally dispenses with ordinances, purity requirements, or the law of Moses.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The episode may describe an exceptional emergency rather than a general principle governing ritual requirements.
- The decisive feature may be Hezekiah's authorized intercession rather than sincerity by itself.
- The text's emphasis on priestly sanctification and Mosaic procedure shows that ritual requirements remain important.
- The word healed may refer to removal of a specific divine affliction, ritual restoration, or a broader act of mercy; the chapter does not specify.
