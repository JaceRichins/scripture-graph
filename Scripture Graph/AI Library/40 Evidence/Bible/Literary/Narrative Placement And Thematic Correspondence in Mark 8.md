# Narrative Placement And Thematic Correspondence in Mark 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of the two-stage restoration of sight between the disciples' failure to perceive and Peter's partly correct but incomplete recognition of Jesus supports reading the healing as a narrative picture of gradual spiritual understanding. Peter sees that Jesus is the Christ but immediately resists the suffering that Jesus says his mission requires.

**Does not establish:** The juxtaposition does not prove that the evangelist intended every detail of the healing as an allegory of the disciples.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The healing may simply report an unusual gradual cure without symbolizing the disciples.
- Its placement may create suspense or emphasize Jesus' persistence rather than encode a discipleship metaphor.
- The correspondence with Peter's partial understanding may be thematic without making the healing an intentional enacted parable.
