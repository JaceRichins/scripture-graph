# Confession Reinforced By Prohibitions in Deuteronomy 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The confession that the LORD is one, reinforced by commands to serve him and not follow surrounding gods, strongly supports exclusive allegiance to the LORD. The precise semantic force of 'one LORD' should not be made to carry more than the surrounding passage establishes.

**Does not establish:** This verse alone does not settle every later metaphysical question about the nature of God or eliminate translation and interpretive questions surrounding the word 'one.'

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may emphasize that the LORD alone is Israel's God.
- The wording may emphasize the LORD's unity or uniqueness.
- Several compatible nuances may be present rather than one exhaustive definition.
