# Literary Progression in Isaiah 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The progression from emptied earth, through ruined celebration and inescapable judgment, to the Lord reigning in Zion supports reading the chapter as a purposeful theological movement from human disorder to divine rule.

**Does not establish:** It does not prove single authorship, date of composition, or a detailed end-time chronology.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The shifts may preserve several originally distinct prophetic or liturgical units.
- The movement may be thematic without constituting a tightly designed single structure.
