# Internal Literary And Ritual Evidence in Deuteronomy 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of inscribed stones, an altar and offerings, geographically assigned groups, loud proclamation, fixed formulas, and repeated communal responses provides strong internal evidence that the chapter presents a prescribed communal covenant ceremony.

**Does not establish:** The literary form does not by itself establish when, how, or whether the prescribed ceremony was historically enacted.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prescription may be a literary idealization rather than a record of a ceremony that was performed exactly as written.
- Some elements may have been combined or arranged during textual transmission.
