# Parallel Narrative Tradition in 2 Chronicles 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Both Chronicles and Kings associate Josiah's death with his encounter with Pharaoh Necho at Megiddo and his removal to Jerusalem. This convergence supports a shared core tradition, while differences in detail and probable literary dependence limit its value as independent corroboration.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish the exact sequence of Josiah's wounding and death or the divine origin of Necho's warning.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chronicles may be adapting Kings rather than preserving an independent witness.
- Both accounts may draw on a common written or oral source.
- Theological shaping may account for Chronicles' added warning, disguise, and battlefield dialogue.
