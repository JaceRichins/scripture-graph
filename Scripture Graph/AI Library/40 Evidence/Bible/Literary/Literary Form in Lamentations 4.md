# Literary Form in Lamentations 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In Hebrew, Lamentations 4 is an alphabetic poem comprising twenty-two one-verse stanzas. This is strong evidence of deliberate literary organization, even though the alphabetic pattern disappears in English translation.

**Does not establish:** The acrostic does not identify the author, establish a composition date, or prove any larger historical or theological conclusion.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The acrostic demonstrates careful composition but does not determine why the poet selected this form.
- Formal order may contain grief, aid memorization, convey completeness, or serve several purposes simultaneously.

**Scripture:** [[Lamentations 4#^lam-4-1|Lamentations 4:1-22]]

**Sources:**
- Adele Berlin — *Lamentations: A Commentary* (2002)
