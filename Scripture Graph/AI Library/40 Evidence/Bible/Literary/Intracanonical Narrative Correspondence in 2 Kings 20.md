# Intracanonical Narrative Correspondence in 2 Kings 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the completed Kings narrative, Isaiah's oracle anticipates later accounts of royal treasure and people being carried to Babylon. This is a strong final-form narrative correspondence, but the correspondence alone cannot date the oracle or prove that every later deportee was one of Hezekiah's direct descendants.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not independently establish the oracle's date, supernatural prediction, compositional history, or a precise one-to-one fulfillment of every detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may preserve an earlier warning subsequently understood as fulfilled.
- The passage may have received editorial shaping by writers who knew of the Babylonian deportations.
- Earlier tradition and later editorial framing may both contribute to the present form.

**Scripture:** [[2 Kings 20#^2kgs-20-17|2 Kings 20:17-18]], [[2 Kings 24#^2kgs-24-13|2 Kings 24:13-16]]
