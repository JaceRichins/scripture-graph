# Canonical Narrative Correspondence in Genesis 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Genesis 15 forecasts a sequence later narrated in Exodus: Abram's descendants reside in another land, experience servitude and affliction, face a divinely judged oppressor, and depart with possessions.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence does not independently prove the historical details, the date of composition, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- At the level of literary composition, Genesis 15 may have been shaped with knowledge of an established exodus tradition.
- The correspondence alone cannot determine when either passage was composed.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 15#^gen-15-13|Genesis 15:13-14]], [[Exodus 1#^ex-1-8|Exodus 1:8-14]], [[Exodus 12#^ex-12-35|Exodus 12:35-41]]
<!-- SG:END summary -->
