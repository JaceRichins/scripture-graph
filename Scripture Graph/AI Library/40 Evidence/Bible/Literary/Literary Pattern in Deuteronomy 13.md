# Literary Pattern in Deuteronomy 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of the proposed invitation to serve other gods, language about gods Israel has not known, and language of being drawn or thrust away gives the three cases thematic and verbal cohesion.

**Does not establish:** The verbal pattern does not establish a particular author, date, compositional history, or historical implementation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The cohesion may arise from conventional legal formulas rather than a specially designed literary arrangement.
- A compiler or editor could have standardized originally separate case laws.
