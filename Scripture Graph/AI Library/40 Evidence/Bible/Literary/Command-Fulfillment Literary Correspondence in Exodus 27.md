# Command-Fulfillment Literary Correspondence in Exodus 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Exodus 38:1-7 and 38:9-20 closely follow the altar and court specifications of Exodus 27:1-19 as construction reports. This correspondence is strong evidence that Exodus 38 is literarily designed to present fulfillment of the earlier commands.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the historicity, date, or authorship of the reported construction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both passages could have been derived from a shared written or oral template.
- An editor could have harmonized a construction account with the command account.
- Literary correspondence can present an ideal fulfillment without independently confirming that the construction occurred historically.
