# Internal Literary Structure in Ephesians 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The related praise refrains in verses 6, 12, and 14, together with the sustained attention to the Father, Christ, and the Spirit, provide substantial internal evidence that verses 3–14 form a deliberately organized unit. The pattern supports literary and theological coherence but does not prove a rigid three-part scheme.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish a formal doctrine of the Trinity, a particular later creed, or a uniquely correct subdivision of verses 3–14.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated language may be conventional praise rather than markers of a precise outline.
- References to the Father, Christ, and the Spirit overlap rather than forming completely separate sections.
- A proposed three-part arrangement may reflect a reader's theological categories more exactly than the passage's own boundaries.
