# Literary Structure in Joel 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated judicial vocabulary, reciprocal punishment, harvest imagery, cosmic disturbance, and the closing Zion frame provide strong internal evidence that the chapter is a coherent movement from accusation and judgment to sanctuary and restoration.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not by itself establish single authorship, the oracle's historical date, or a uniquely modern fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetitions may result from conventional prophetic vocabulary rather than deliberate structural design.
- The chapter may combine related oracle units that an editor arranged into their present sequence.
