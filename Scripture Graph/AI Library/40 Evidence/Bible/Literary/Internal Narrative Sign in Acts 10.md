# Internal Narrative Sign in Acts 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The circumcised believers' astonishment and their hearing the Gentiles speak with tongues and magnify God function as observable confirmation within the story that the gift of the Holy Ghost has also been poured upon Gentiles.

**Does not establish:** The narrative sign does not independently establish the historical occurrence or divine cause of the reported phenomenon.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may be shaped to parallel the earlier Pentecost narrative.
- Readers differ over whether speaking with tongues here describes known languages, ecstatic speech, or another vocal manifestation.
