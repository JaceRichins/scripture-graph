# Prophetic And Canonical Interpretation in Malachi 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Malachi 3:1-5 supplies substantial imagery used in Christian readings of the coming of Christ—arrival of the Lord, testing, purification, temple, and judgment—but the passage itself does not use the phrase 'Second Coming.' Its exclusive identification with that event is therefore an interpretation rather than a textual observation.

**Does not establish:** The future-oriented language alone does not establish the timing, number, or exclusive identity of its fulfillment.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The oracle may initially address divine judgment and temple reform anticipated in Malachi's postexilic setting.
- The passage may admit typological or multiple applications rather than one exhaustive fulfillment.
- Different Christian traditions identify the Lord's coming with the first advent, the Second Coming, or both.
