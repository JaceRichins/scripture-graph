# Historical Identification in Daniel 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The “vile person” of verses 21–35 is strongly identified with Antiochus IV Epiphanes. His irregular accession, political deceit, campaigns against Egypt, hostility to the Jewish covenant, suppression of sacrifice, sanctuary profanation, and persecution align closely with the chapter’s progression.

**Does not establish:** The identification does not establish that Antiochus exhausts every possible theological or typological application.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may be pre-event predictive prophecy about Antiochus IV.
- Antiochus may be both the immediate referent and a type of a later eschatological antagonist.
- Futurist interpretations may transfer some or all details after a proposed transition to a still-future ruler.
