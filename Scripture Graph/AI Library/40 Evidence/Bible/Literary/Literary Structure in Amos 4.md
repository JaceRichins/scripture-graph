# Literary Structure in Amos 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The fivefold refrain, the accumulating catalog of calamities, and the transition marked by 'Therefore' provide strong internal evidence for a deliberately organized rhetorical movement from warning to announced encounter.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not establish the historical occurrence, sequence, or supernatural causation of every calamity, nor does it independently establish the oracle's date or authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The calamities may be a thematic catalog rather than a chronological escalation.
- Repeated formulas may reflect conventional prophetic composition rather than a uniquely elaborate design.
