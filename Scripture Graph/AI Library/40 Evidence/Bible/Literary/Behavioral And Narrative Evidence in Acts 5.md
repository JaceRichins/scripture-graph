# Behavioral And Narrative Evidence in Acts 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The apostles' continued teaching after imprisonment, beating, and prohibition strongly supports the narrative theme of courageous endurance. Their persistence by itself, however, is not logically sufficient to prove that their religious claims were true.

**Does not establish:** Persistence establishes neither the historical accuracy of every narrated detail nor the truth of the apostles' theological claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Sincere commitment can motivate endurance even when a belief is mistaken.
- The narrative may emphasize courage to model ideal discipleship.
- Group solidarity and conflict with authorities can intensify commitment.
