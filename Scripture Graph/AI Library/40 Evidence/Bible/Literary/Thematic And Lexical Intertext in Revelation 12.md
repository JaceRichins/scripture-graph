# Thematic And Lexical Intertext in Revelation 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The conflict among a woman, a serpent, and the woman's seed resembles Genesis 3:15. Revelation's explicit identification of the dragon as the old serpent makes the connection substantial, although the chapter expands and transforms the earlier pattern.

**Does not establish:** The resemblance does not establish that Genesis 3:15 predicts every narrative detail in Revelation 12 or that both passages have only one referent.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Woman-serpent conflict may draw on broader symbolic traditions as well as Genesis.
- The woman's seed in Revelation is plural or collective in application, so it should not be mechanically equated with every proposed referent of the seed in Genesis.
