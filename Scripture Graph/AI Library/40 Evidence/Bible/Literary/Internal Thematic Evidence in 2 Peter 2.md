# Internal Thematic Evidence in 2 Peter 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides strong internal evidence that its concern is not merely incorrect doctrine but abusive conduct: the teachers are described as covetous, as making merchandise of others, as beguiling unstable souls, and as loving unrighteous gain.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the precise teachings, organization, or financial practices of the people being opposed.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The economic language may function partly as moral invective rather than as a description of a specific financial scheme.
- The named behaviors should not be assumed to characterize every person who teaches error.
