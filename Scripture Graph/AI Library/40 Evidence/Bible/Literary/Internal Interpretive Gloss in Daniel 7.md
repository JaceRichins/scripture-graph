# Internal Interpretive Gloss in Daniel 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Because the chapter itself explains the beasts and horns as political rulers or kingdoms, readings that treat these symbols as political powers have strong internal textual support; the internal explanation does not identify the specific historical powers.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish which four historical kingdoms are intended, the identity of the little horn, or the date and authorship of the vision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The symbols may represent dynastic regimes rather than isolated individual monarchs.
- A symbol may have a primary historical referent while also functioning typologically in later interpretation.

**Scripture:** [[Daniel 7#^dan-7-17|Daniel 7:17]], [[Daniel 7#^dan-7-23|Daniel 7:23]], [[Daniel 7#^dan-7-24|Daniel 7:24]]
