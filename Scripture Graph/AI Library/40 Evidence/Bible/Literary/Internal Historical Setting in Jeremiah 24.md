# Internal Historical Setting in Jeremiah 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Taken at face value, the political configuration named in the chapter supports a narrative setting after Jeconiah's deportation in 597 BCE but before Jerusalem's destruction in 586 BCE: Jeconiah has been carried to Babylon, while Zedekiah rules and a remnant remains in Jerusalem.

**Does not establish:** The setting does not by itself establish the date of the chapter's final composition, single authorship, or supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A later writer or editor could have supplied an accurate retrospective setting.
- The passage may preserve material from this period while reaching its final literary form later.
