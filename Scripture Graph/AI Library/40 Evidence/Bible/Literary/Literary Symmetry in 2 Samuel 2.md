# Literary Symmetry in 2 Samuel 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The numerical and verbal symmetry of the pool episode is strong evidence that this part of the narrative emphasizes reciprocal destruction: equal groups meet, every fighter grips a counterpart, and all fall before the conflict expands.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish equal moral responsibility for the entire civil conflict, equal total military strength, or that every movement occurred simultaneously.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The symmetry may primarily preserve the remembered convention of a representative combat rather than convey an ethical judgment.
- A stylized account may heighten symmetry beyond what an eyewitness could have observed precisely.
