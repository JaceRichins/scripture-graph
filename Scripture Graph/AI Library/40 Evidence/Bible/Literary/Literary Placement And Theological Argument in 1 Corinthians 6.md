# Literary Placement And Theological Argument in 1 Corinthians 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement of bodily resurrection between the declaration that the body is for the Lord and the discussion of sexual union provides strong evidence that Paul's sexual ethic is grounded in God's enduring purpose for the body, not merely in concern about temporary physical consequences.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish a complete Christian theology of sexuality or answer every disputed question about sexual conduct.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Resurrection may function primarily as reassurance about God's ownership and power rather than as a complete explanation of Paul's sexual ethic.
- Other premises in the passage, including union with Christ and divine purchase, also carry substantial argumentative weight.
