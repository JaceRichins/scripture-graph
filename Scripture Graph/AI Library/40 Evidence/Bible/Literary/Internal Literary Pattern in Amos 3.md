# Internal Literary Pattern in Amos 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of the lion image gives the chapter literary coherence: verse 4 considers a lion with prey, verse 8 equates the Lord's speech with a lion's roar, and verse 12 depicts Israel in the lion's mouth. This strongly supports reading verses 3–12 as a coordinated argument about warning and judgment.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the date, single authorship, compositional history, or historical fulfillment of the oracle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Lion imagery could recur because it is a conventional prophetic image rather than because the entire unit was designed around one motif.
- Later arrangement or editing could also account for some thematic coherence.
