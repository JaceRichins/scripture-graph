# Thematic Clustering in Proverbs 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The density of sayings about poverty, riches, unjust gain, work, greed, and generosity supports reading economic justice as a major concern of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The clustering does not establish a systematic economic theory, a rejection of wealth itself, or unified authorship of the sayings.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Economic concerns are common throughout Proverbs and need not constitute a deliberately unified argument here.
- The cluster may reflect editorial collection rather than composition as a single discourse.
