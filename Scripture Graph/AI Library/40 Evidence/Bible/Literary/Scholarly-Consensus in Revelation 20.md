# Scholarly-Consensus in Revelation 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Christian interpreters have long divided over how to read the "thousand years" of vv.4-7 — premillennial (a literal future earthly reign beginning at Christ's return), postmillennial (a golden age achieved through gospel progress before Christ's return), and amillennial (a symbolic description of the present church age or the intermediate state) — and the text of Revelation 20 itself does not settle which framework is correct.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which millennial framework (or the LDS reading reflected in the chapter heading) is the correct interpretation of the text; only that the text itself leaves the question open to serious, longstanding disagreement.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Amillennialists read the entire scene symbolically, denying any future literal thousand-year earthly reign.
- Postmillennialists locate the millennium's progress before, not after, Christ's return, inverting the sequence implied by a plain/literal LDS reading.
