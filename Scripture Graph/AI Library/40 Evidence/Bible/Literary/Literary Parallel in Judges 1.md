# Literary Parallel in Judges 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The extended verbal and narrative correspondences between Judges 1 and the territorial accounts in Joshua are strong evidence that the books preserve shared conquest and settlement traditions. The parallels alone do not determine the direction of literary dependence or the date and stages of composition.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not by themselves establish which text is earlier, a single author, a precise composition date, or the historical accuracy of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Judges may have drawn from Joshua in its present form.
- Joshua may have drawn from traditions also preserved in Judges.
- Both books may depend on shared oral or written sources.
- Later editors may have incorporated parallel material into one or both books.
