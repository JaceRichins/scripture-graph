# Literary And Narrative Evidence in Exodus 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated refrain, Miriam's timbrel, and the participation of the women provide moderately strong internal evidence that the chapter presents the victory song as communal and performative, not merely as private reflection.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the precise performance arrangement, the identity of the song's composer, or the historical date when its present form arose.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The scene may be literary staging intended to portray communal celebration without preserving an exact historical performance.
- The text does not specify whether Miriam's refrain was antiphonal with the entire preceding song or was a separate repetition.

**Scripture:** [[Exodus 15#^ex-15-1|Exodus 15:1]], [[Exodus 15#^ex-15-20|Exodus 15:20-21]]
