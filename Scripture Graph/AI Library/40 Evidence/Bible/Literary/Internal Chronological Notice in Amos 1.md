# Internal Chronological Notice in Amos 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The reference to an earthquake supplies an internal relative chronological marker: the vision is placed two years before an event apparently assumed to be recognizable to the intended audience.

**Does not establish:** The notice alone does not establish an exact modern date, the earthquake's magnitude, or the historical reliability of every oracle.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The superscription may preserve later editorial framing rather than wording written by Amos.
- The chapter itself does not identify the earthquake by an absolute date or provide independent corroboration.

**Scripture:** [[Amos 1#^amos-1-1|Amos 1:1]]
