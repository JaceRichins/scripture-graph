# Narrative Role Evidence in Judges 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Judges 4 provides strong textual evidence that this biblical narrative recognizes a woman as a prophetess and judge and depicts Israelites seeking her judgments. The narrower textual conclusion is secure, while broader historical or institutional conclusions require evidence beyond this chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the frequency of female leadership, a universal rule for religious offices, or the historical details of Deborah's career outside the narrative.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The title “judge” may describe Deborah's activity without defining a standardized political office.
- An exceptional narrative figure does not show how common female prophetic or judicial leadership was.
- The narrative portrayal alone cannot resolve questions about the episode's historical setting or composition.
