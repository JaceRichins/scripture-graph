# Thematic Concentration And Explicit Rationale in Exodus 22

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The stated needs of the poor debtor and the repeated assurance that God hears afflicted people provide substantial textual support for reading concern for vulnerable persons as integral to this legal collection, not merely incidental to it.

**Does not establish:** The passage does not establish that every Israelite law was egalitarian, that these commands were uniformly enforced, or that the chapter maps directly onto modern public policy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The provisions may be explained partly by maintaining social stability and communal cohesion.
- The protections are limited to the categories and circumstances named and should not be equated without qualification with modern social-welfare or human-rights systems.
