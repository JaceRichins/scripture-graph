# Canonical Reception And Verbal Parallel in Exodus 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Later New Testament texts reuse or interpret the language of covenant and blood found in Exodus 24:8. This is strong evidence for early Christian reception of the passage as a framework for understanding covenant inaugurated through blood, but it is not proof that every later Christian application was explicit in the original narrative.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the author or earliest audience of Exodus consciously understood the ceremony as a direct prediction of Jesus's words or later Christian ordinances.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The New Testament authors may be creatively rereading Exodus rather than merely restating its original historical meaning.
- Shared covenant language can support typological continuity without implying one-to-one correspondence between every ritual detail.
