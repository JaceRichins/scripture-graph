# Internal Literary Pattern in Jeremiah 34

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated use of "liberty" creates a clear judgment reversal: the people proclaim liberty and revoke it, so the Lord proclaims a destructive liberty for them to sword, pestilence, and famine. This is strong internal evidence of deliberate rhetorical irony in the passage.

**Does not establish:** The wordplay does not establish the historical circumstances, the identity of a particular composer or editor, or the chapter's precise compositional history.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition could partly reflect conventional prophetic diction rather than a uniquely constructed wordplay.
- The force of the repeated English term should not by itself be used to infer conclusions about authorship or compositional date.
