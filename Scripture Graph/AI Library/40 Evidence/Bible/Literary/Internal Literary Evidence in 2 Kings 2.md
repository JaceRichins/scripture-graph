# Internal Literary Evidence in 2 Kings 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, the fallen mantle, repeated Jordan crossing, and recognition by the sons of the prophets provide strong literary evidence that Elisha is presented as Elijah's successor.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the mechanics of priesthood transmission, a general succession ordinance, or the historicity of every narrated event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated miracle could demonstrate divine power through Elisha without defining a formal office-transfer procedure.
- The mantle may function primarily as a narrative symbol rather than as an ordinance or independently effective object.
