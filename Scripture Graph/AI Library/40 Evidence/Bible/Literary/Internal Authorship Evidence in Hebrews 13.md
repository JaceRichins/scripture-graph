# Internal Authorship Evidence in Hebrews 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closing identifies Timothy as the writer's brother, anticipates traveling with him, requests prayer for restoration to the recipients, and sends greetings involving people associated with Italy. These data support a writer personally known to the recipients and acquainted with Timothy, but they do not name Paul.

**Does not establish:** The closing does not establish Pauline authorship, the writer's precise location, or the identity and location of the recipients.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Paul could have written the letter and referred to Timothy as a brother and companion.
- Another early Christian leader from Timothy's wider network could fit the same evidence.
- The phrase concerning people from Italy can describe Italians away from Italy or greetings sent from within Italy, so it does not securely locate the writer.
