# Internal Literary Correspondence in 2 Kings 23

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The detailed correspondence between 1 Kings 13:2 and 2 Kings 23:15-18 is strong evidence that the Kings narrative intentionally links Josiah's act with the earlier Beth-el prophecy. How and when that correspondence arose remains a separate historical question.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence alone does not establish the dates of the two passages, their independence, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor could have shaped the earlier prophecy and later fulfillment account into correspondence.
- The prophecy could have been formulated or expanded retrospectively after Josiah's actions.
- A historical Josianic act and an older general oracle could have been joined and made more specific during transmission.
