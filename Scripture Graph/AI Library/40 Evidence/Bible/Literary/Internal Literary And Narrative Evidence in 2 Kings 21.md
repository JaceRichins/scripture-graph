# Internal Literary And Narrative Evidence in 2 Kings 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Later passages in Kings explicitly recall Manasseh's provocations and innocent blood when explaining why judgment against Judah was not averted. This is strong evidence that the book's final narrative gives Manasseh's reign a major causal role in its theological explanation of Jerusalem's fall.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical mechanism of Judah's fall, the date of the oracle, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The causal linkage may reflect retrospective theological or editorial shaping rather than a contemporaneous explanation.
- Other political, military, and economic causes of Judah's fall are outside this chapter's theological summary.
