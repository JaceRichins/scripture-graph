# Literary Design in John 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained coordination of seeing, knowing, testimony, judgment, and blindness provides strong evidence that the chapter is deliberately organized around a physical-to-spiritual sight motif.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence alone does not establish the episode’s historicity, the precise process of composition, or every theological inference drawn from the sign.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetition may arise naturally from an interrogation narrative rather than from a tightly planned literary scheme.
- The recognition sequence can be read as realistic character development without treating every occurrence of sight language as symbolic.
