# Repetition And Rhetorical Emphasis in Deuteronomy 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The concentrated repetition of the anti-merit formula provides strong internal evidence that preventing a self-righteous interpretation of the conquest is a deliberate purpose of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Deuteronomy treats conduct as irrelevant, nor does it independently prove the chapter's charge concerning the displaced nations.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may primarily serve covenantal accusation and communal humiliation rather than express a general doctrine of grace.
- The passage can reject comparative moral superiority without denying that obedience matters elsewhere in Deuteronomy.
