# Literary Structure in 2 Chronicles 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The itemized correspondence between the announced judgments and the immediately narrated disasters is strong evidence that the chapter has been deliberately organized as an indictment-and-fulfillment account.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence alone does not establish when the writing was composed, the historical mechanism of fulfillment, or supernatural foreknowledge.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An editor may have shaped received reports to correspond with the prophetic message.
- The prophetic wording may have been composed or expanded retrospectively after the reported disasters.
- Some correspondences could reflect conventional ancient descriptions of dynastic catastrophe rather than exact historical prediction.
