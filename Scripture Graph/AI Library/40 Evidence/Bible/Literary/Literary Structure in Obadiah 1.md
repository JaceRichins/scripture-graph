# Literary Structure in Obadiah 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated reversals organize the chapter: the high are brought down, trusted allies deceive, spectators become judged offenders, deeds return upon the doer, conquered Zion receives deliverance, and the Lord's kingdom concludes the oracle. The density and distribution of these reversals strongly support reading reciprocal reversal as a deliberate literary and theological principle.

**Does not establish:** The literary coherence does not establish the oracle's date, historical fulfillment, compositional unity, or divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some reversals may arise naturally from the subject of judgment rather than from a formal compositional design.
- The pattern need not constitute a rigid outline or technical poetic structure.
