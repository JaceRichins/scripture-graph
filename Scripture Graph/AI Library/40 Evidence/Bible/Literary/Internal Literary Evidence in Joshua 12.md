# Internal Literary Evidence in Joshua 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The catalogue is strong evidence that the book of Joshua presents Israel's campaigns as a large, ordered series of victories under Moses and Joshua. As a literary summary, however, it is not by itself proof that Israel immediately occupied every part of the named territories or eliminated all remaining resistance.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the archaeological chronology, the completeness of Israelite occupation, or the absence of later Canaanite resistance.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The list may summarize defeated political centers without claiming exhaustive settlement of their surrounding territories.
- Defeating a king may indicate the collapse of a particular coalition or city-state rather than permanent control of every locality.
- The catalogue may employ conventional victory-list rhetoric and therefore should not automatically be read as a modern territorial survey.
