# OnomasticHistorical in Romans 16

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Junia was a well-attested Roman feminine name in antiquity, while a corresponding masculine form 'Junias' is not clearly attested as an independent name apart from being hypothesized as a contraction; on this basis many text critics favor identifying Junia in Romans 16:7 as a woman.

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish the scope or nature of Junia's ministry role, only supports reading the name as feminine.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some early interpreters and later translators rendered the name as masculine 'Junias,' which may reflect assumptions about who could hold apostolic status rather than clear independent onomastic evidence for a male name

**Scripture:** [[Romans 16#^rom-16-7|Romans 16:7]]

**Sources:**
- Eldon Jay Epp — *Junia: The First Woman Apostle* (2005)
