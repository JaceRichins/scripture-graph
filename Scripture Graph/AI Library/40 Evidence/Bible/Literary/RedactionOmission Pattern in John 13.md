# RedactionOmission Pattern in John 13

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Unlike the Synoptic accounts of the Last Supper (which narrate Jesus instituting the bread and cup), John's Last Supper account in this chapter contains no institution of the sacrament and instead centers on the foot washing — a well-noted feature of John's version of the meal.

**Does not establish:** does not establish that John rejects or downplays the sacrament theologically; establishes only a literary/structural difference in where and how each Gospel narrates the supper.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Argument from silence: John may simply presuppose the institution as already known from the other Gospels/early Christian practice and choose not to repeat it, rather than making any theological statement by omitting it.
- John 6:51-58's language about eating Jesus' flesh and drinking his blood arguably already carries eucharistic meaning earlier in the Gospel, so the 'omission' in ch. 13 may be less significant than it first appears.
