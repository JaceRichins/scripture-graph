# Literary Form And Internal Organization in Joshua 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sustained sequence of directional movements, landmarks, regional headings, towns, villages, and numerical totals strongly supports reading the chapter as a territorial and administrative description rather than as a continuous battle narrative.

**Does not establish:** The detail does not by itself establish the list's date, archaeological accuracy, contemporaneity, or independence from later editorial work.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some components may have originated as separate boundary descriptions or settlement lists before being combined.
- An administrative literary form does not prove that the list was produced by a contemporary survey.
