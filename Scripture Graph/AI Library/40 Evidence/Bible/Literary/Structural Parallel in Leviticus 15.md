# Structural Parallel in Leviticus 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The closely corresponding procedures for a man's ongoing issue and a woman's prolonged blood issue support reading the chapter as a coordinated system governing bodily flows in both sexes.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not establish modern gender equality, a medical theory of bodily fluids, or identical practical consequences for both sexes.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The regulations are not identical in every detail, so structural correspondence should not be represented as complete symmetry.
- Parallel treatment of discharges does not by itself demonstrate equal social experience or equal burdens for men and women.
