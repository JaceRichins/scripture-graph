# Literary Prediction-Fulfillment Pattern in 1 Kings 14

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The close correspondence between Ahijah's announced sign and the narrated outcome is strong internal evidence that the chapter has been shaped to authenticate Ahijah as a prophet and to emphasize the efficacy of the LORD's word.

**Does not establish:** The literary correspondence alone does not independently establish supernatural foreknowledge, the date of composition, or the historical accuracy of every detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The account may have been composed or edited after the reported events so that prediction and fulfillment correspond closely.
- The fulfillment pattern can function as narrative theology whether or not every historical detail can be independently verified.
