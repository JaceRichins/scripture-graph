# Recurrent Literary Vocabulary in Jeremiah 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
A recurring medical vocabulary binds the chapter's accusations and lament: leaders heal the hurt only slightly, the people expect a time of health, the speaker shares the daughter's hurt, and the conclusion asks about balm, a physician, and unrecovered health. This pattern strongly supports reading false assurance and failed healing as a central literary theme.

**Does not establish:** The vocabulary does not establish single-stage composition, precise authorship, or that every occurrence was intentionally arranged by the same hand.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Related healing terms could have attracted originally separate oracles during compilation.
- The recurrence may reflect conventional prophetic language rather than a deliberately designed chapter.
