# Detailed Narrative Correspondence in Deuteronomy 9

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The cluster of matching persons, objects, actions, and sequence provides very strong evidence that Deuteronomy 9 and Exodus 32 refer to the same literary-traditional episode.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not determine the direction of literary dependence, the date of either account, or the historical factuality of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both accounts may draw on a shared earlier tradition rather than one text directly copying the other.
- Later literary shaping may account for some of the verbal and thematic correspondence.
