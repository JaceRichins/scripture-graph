# Historical Specificity in Jeremiah 51

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated naming of the kings of the Medes places the oracle within the historical-political horizon of powers expected to participate in Babylon's overthrow. This is strong evidence that the poem is oriented toward a concrete geopolitical fall, but by itself it is only moderate evidence for any precise date or theory of composition.

**Does not establish:** The references do not establish the oracle's exact date, single authorship, or supernatural prediction.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Medes may function as a conventional northern enemy rather than identify a narrowly dated event.
- The references could have been clarified or updated during later transmission.
- A prediction made before Babylon's fall and a composition shaped near or after the fall can both explain the specificity.
