# Parallel-Account Numerical Discrepancy in 2 Chronicles 36

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
In the supplied canonical wording, 2 Chronicles gives Jehoiachin's accession age as eight, whereas 2 Kings gives it as eighteen. This is a real textual discrepancy that should be acknowledged rather than silently harmonized.

**Does not establish:** The discrepancy does not by itself determine the original reading, explain how the variant arose, or settle broader claims about the historical reliability of either book.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A copying error may have altered a numeral in the transmission of one account.
- Some textual witnesses or translations may preserve eighteen in Chronicles.
- A proposed co-regency could be used to harmonize the ages, but the two verses themselves do not state one.

**Scripture:** [[2 Chronicles 36#^2chr-36-9|2 Chronicles 36:9]], [[2 Kings 24#^2kgs-24-8|2 Kings 24:8]]
