# Internal Predictive Correspondence in Acts 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within the narrative, Agabus's forecast corresponds substantially but not identically to subsequent events: Jews seize Paul and the Roman commander then binds him with two chains. The text does not explicitly say that Jews themselves bound Paul, so this is sequence-level rather than exact verbal fulfillment.

**Does not establish:** The internal correspondence alone does not establish supernatural foreknowledge, independent historical corroboration, or exact fulfillment in every detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The prediction may have been recorded or shaped after the arrest.
- The author may have arranged the account to emphasize correspondence.
- Given Paul's known conflicts, imprisonment could have been anticipated without supernatural knowledge.
- The agents of binding differ in the wording of the prediction and the narrated event.
