# Scriptural Reception And Reuse in Leviticus 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of the statutes-and-life formula in Ezekiel and Paul's explicit attribution of the principle to Moses provide strong evidence that Leviticus 18:5 became a significant scriptural formulation in later biblical argument.

**Does not establish:** The verbal connection alone does not settle larger debates about law, grace, covenant, or the precise meaning of life in each context.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Ezekiel and Paul may invoke a wider Torah formula rather than treating Leviticus 18:5 in isolation.
- Later authors employ the formula for different rhetorical purposes, so recurrence does not guarantee identical interpretation.
