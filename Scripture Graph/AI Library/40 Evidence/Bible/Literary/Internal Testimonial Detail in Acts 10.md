# Internal Testimonial Detail in Acts 10

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Peter appeals to eating and drinking with the risen Jesus as part of the witnesses' testimony. This concrete meal detail strengthens the passage's presentation of the resurrection appearances as embodied encounters rather than as a purely abstract doctrine.

**Does not establish:** The detail alone does not prove the historicity or supernatural character of Jesus's resurrection.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The meal detail could be a traditional or literary means of emphasizing bodily resurrection.
- A reported concrete detail does not independently verify the event it describes.
