# Internal Chronological Consistency in John 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
John 19:14 places Jesus still before Pilate, not yet crucified, 'about the sixth hour' on the day of preparation, whereas Mark 15:25 states Jesus was crucified at 'the third hour.' This three-hour discrepancy between the two Gospels' internal clocks is a long-recognized harmonization problem with no settled scholarly resolution.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish which account is historically more precise, or that either evangelist made an error rather than using a different time-reckoning convention.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Roman civil-time reckoning from midnight versus Jewish reckoning from sunrise could shift the stated hours relative to each other
- Ancient numeral confusion between similar-looking Greek letters for 'three' and 'six' may have produced a copying variant in one Gospel
- John may have arranged the timeline theologically so Jesus's sentencing/death coincides with the hour Passover lambs were being slain in the Temple
