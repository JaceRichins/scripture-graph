# Performance And Liturgical Form in Psalm 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated commands, questions, and answers in verses 7–10 are substantial internal evidence that the conclusion was suited to antiphonal or dramatic performance by more than one voice.

**Does not establish:** It does not identify the performers, prove an ark procession, specify a temple festival, or date the composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A single poet or reader can imitate dialogue without an actual multi-voice ceremony.
- The passage may have been composed for literary effect and only later used liturgically.
- The repetition may emphasize kingship rather than preserve a performance script.
