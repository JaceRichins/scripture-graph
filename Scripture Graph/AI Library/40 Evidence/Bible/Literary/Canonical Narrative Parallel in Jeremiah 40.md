# Canonical Narrative Parallel in Jeremiah 40

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Second Kings contains a close canonical parallel to Jeremiah's account of Gedaliah's appointment and his counsel that the remnant serve Babylon and remain in the land.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not establish two independent eyewitnesses, exact speech transcripts, or a complete reconstruction of the post-conquest administration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The accounts may reflect literary dependence rather than independent testimony.
- Both accounts may derive from a shared written or oral source.
- Parallel wording may result from later editorial coordination.
