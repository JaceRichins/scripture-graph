# Internal Literary Pattern in 2 Chronicles 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated appeals to written commands, established courses, the book of Moses, and earlier royal or cultic authorities strongly support the interpretation that the narrator intentionally frames this Passover as carefully ordered obedience.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the historical precision of every number, role, or procedural detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some repetitions may reflect conventional temple vocabulary rather than a specially designed literary emphasis.
- The pattern may reveal the narrator's theological program more directly than the precise form of the historical celebration.
