# Social And Ministry Participation in Philippians 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The description of the women as persons who laboured with the speaker in the gospel is strong textual evidence that women participated materially in the mission represented by this letter.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the precise duties, titles, ordination status, or institutional authority of Euodias and Syntyche.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The labor may have included hospitality, patronage, logistical support, evangelistic activity, or several kinds of service.
- The wording does not identify an ecclesiastical office or define the women's authority.

**Scripture:** [[Philippians 4#^philip-4-2|Philippians 4:2]], [[Philippians 4#^philip-4-3|Philippians 4:3]]
