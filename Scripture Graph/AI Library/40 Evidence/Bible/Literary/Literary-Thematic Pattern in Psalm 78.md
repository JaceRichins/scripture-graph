# Literary-Thematic Pattern in Psalm 78

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Repeated statements that Israel forgot or failed to remember, contrasted with God's remembrance of human frailty, provide strong textual evidence that memory is an organizing theological motif of the psalm.

**Does not establish:** The repeated vocabulary does not by itself establish a chiasm, a precise concentric outline, or a particular date of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence may arise naturally from the psalm's didactic subject rather than from a tightly designed formal structure.
- Some occurrences serve different immediate narrative functions and should not be forced into an exact symmetry.
