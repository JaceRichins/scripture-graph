# Explicit Canonical Fulfillment Claim in 1 Samuel 2

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The later removal of Abiathar from the priesthood is explicitly interpreted by the Kings narrator as fulfilling the LORD's word concerning Eli's house at Shiloh. This shows that the canonical narrative understands the judgment as extending beyond the deaths of Eli's immediate sons.

**Does not establish:** The literary cross-reference does not by itself establish the historical dating of either passage or identify every referent of the faithful-priest promise.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The Kings writer may be the one who retrospectively connected Abiathar's removal with the earlier oracle.
- The oracle may encompass several stages of fulfillment rather than one exhaustive event.

**Scripture:** [[1 Samuel 2#^1sam-2-30|1 Samuel 2:30-36]], [[1 Kings 2#^1kgs-2-26|1 Kings 2:26-27]]
