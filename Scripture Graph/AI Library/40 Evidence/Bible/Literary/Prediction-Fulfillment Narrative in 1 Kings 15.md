# Prediction-Fulfillment Narrative in 1 Kings 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The fulfillment formula in verse 29 is strong evidence that the narrator intends readers to understand the destruction of Jeroboam's house as fulfillment of the word spoken through Ahijah. Because the prophecy and fulfillment belong to the same literary history, this is not independent proof of supernatural foreknowledge.

**Does not establish:** It does not independently establish the date of Ahijah's oracle, supernatural prediction, or the external historicity of every narrated detail.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The fulfillment notice may reflect retrospective theological or editorial framing.
- The account may preserve an earlier prophetic tradition without providing independent evidence for when that tradition originated.
- Baasha's coup and purge can also be explained in ordinary dynastic and political terms.
