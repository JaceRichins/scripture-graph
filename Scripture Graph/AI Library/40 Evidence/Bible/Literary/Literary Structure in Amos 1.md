# Literary Structure in Amos 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The stable sequence of messenger formula, numerical indictment, named offense, and punishment—reinforced by the recurring fire-and-palaces language—is strong evidence that these oracles have been deliberately arranged as a literary series.

**Does not establish:** The pattern does not by itself establish the date, single authorship, historical fulfillment, or divine origin of the oracles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The regularity could partly reflect conventional prophetic formulas rather than composition of every unit at one time.
- An editor could have standardized or arranged originally separate oracles.
