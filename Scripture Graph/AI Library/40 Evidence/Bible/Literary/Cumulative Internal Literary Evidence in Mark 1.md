# Cumulative Internal Literary Evidence in Mark 1

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of the narrator's title, the heavenly voice, the unclean spirit's recognition, authoritative teaching, exorcisms, and healings is strong internal literary evidence that Mark 1 deliberately presents Jesus' identity through both testimony and enacted authority.

**Does not establish:** This literary convergence does not by itself establish the historicity of every episode, the mechanism of the healings, or the truth of the chapter's supernatural claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Individual scenes may have circulated separately before being arranged into the present narrative.
- The pattern demonstrates the evangelist's theological presentation even if readers disagree about the historical or metaphysical implications of the narrated events.
