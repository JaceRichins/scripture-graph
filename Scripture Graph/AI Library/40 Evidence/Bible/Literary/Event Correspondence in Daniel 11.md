# Event Correspondence in Daniel 11

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The ships of Chittim that force the northern ruler to withdraw are commonly connected with Roman intervention against Antiochus IV near Alexandria in 168 BCE. The correspondence explains why the ruler returns frustrated and redirects hostility toward the covenant community.

**Does not establish:** The term Chittim alone would not identify Rome; the strength of the identification comes from its position in the larger Antiochus sequence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Chittim can be a flexible designation for western maritime powers rather than a uniquely precise name for Rome.
- A typological or futurist reading may apply the naval intervention to a later western power.
