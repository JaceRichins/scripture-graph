# Narrative Comparison in Exodus 7

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The magicians' imitation is strong textual evidence that, within this narrative, an impressive act by itself does not identify the authoritative divine messenger. The decisive literary contrasts are command, announced purpose, control of the confrontation, and eventual outcome.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish how the magicians performed their acts, whether every reported transformation was physically identical, or a general rule that miracles cannot function as evidence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The magicians may be portrayed as using illusion or sleight of hand.
- The narrative may envision genuine occult power, possibly limited or permitted by God.
- Their replication may be a literary convention designed to delay Pharaoh's recognition.
- The text may be making a polemical claim about rival ritual specialists without describing the physical mechanism.
