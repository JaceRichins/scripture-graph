# Narrative-Geographical Structure in Acts 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The movement from Jerusalem into Judaea and Samaria is strong literary evidence that Acts 8 develops the geographical mission announced in Acts 1:8. It is a partial realization of that program rather than its completion, since the mission to the uttermost part of the earth continues through the rest of Acts.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence does not by itself establish the historicity of every reported event or that persecution was divinely caused.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The geographical sequence may reflect remembered chronology or ordinary travel routes as well as deliberate literary design.
- The correspondence could have been emphasized retrospectively by the author when arranging the narrative.
