# Internal Literary Contrast in Psalm 4

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The contrast between inward gladness, material abundance, and peaceful sleep strongly supports reading the conclusion as trust grounded in God rather than in favorable harvest conditions.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that material provision is unimportant, that the speaker faced immediate physical danger, or that faithful people are always protected from harm.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The agricultural comparison may chiefly describe the intensity of the speaker's joy rather than establish a general contrast between faith and prosperity.
- The psalm may presuppose actual deliverance or adequate material security that is not narrated.
