# Literary Coherence in Jeremiah 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The coordinated use of location, witnesses, spoken accusation, shattered vessel, and explanatory oracle provides strong internal evidence that the chapter is constructed as a coherent symbolic judgment scene.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not establish the episode's precise historical circumstances, single authorship, or the divine origin of the prophecy.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The coherence may reflect later editorial shaping of older prophetic materials.
- A well-constructed narrative does not demonstrate that every reported action occurred exactly as narrated.
