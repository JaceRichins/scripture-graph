# Argument From Narrated Scope And Silence in 2 Kings 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Although the first repair arrangement fails, the chapter never says that the priests stole or diverted the money. The silence makes claims of embezzlement substantially weaker than the narrower conclusion that the original arrangement did not produce the commanded repairs.

**Does not establish:** Narrative silence does not prove that no misuse occurred; it establishes only that theft is not an explicit accusation in this chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The narrator may have expected readers to infer priestly mismanagement without stating its exact form.
- The account may omit causes because its interest lies in the successful reorganization rather than in assigning blame.
- Insufficient revenue, competing priestly obligations, negligence, or administrative confusion could also explain the delay.
