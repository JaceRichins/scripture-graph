# Explicit Social And Ecclesial Inclusion in 1 Corinthians 12

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The inclusion of Jews, Gentiles, bond, and free within one Spirit-baptized body is strong evidence that inherited ethnic and social distinctions do not determine membership or spiritual standing within the body of Christ.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself demonstrate that all social distinctions disappeared in Corinth or supply a complete program for changing Roman social institutions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The statement may address equality of incorporation into Christ without directly prescribing the abolition of every social role.
- Paul's immediate concern is ecclesial unity, so extrapolation to every political or economic question requires caution.

**Scripture:** [[1 Corinthians 12#^1cor-12-13|1 Corinthians 12:13]]
