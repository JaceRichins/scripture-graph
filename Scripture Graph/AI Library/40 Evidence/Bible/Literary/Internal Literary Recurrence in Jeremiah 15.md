# Internal Literary Recurrence in Jeremiah 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated wall, conflict, divine-presence, and deliverance language provides strong internal literary evidence that Jeremiah 15:19–21 functions as a renewal or reapplication of Jeremiah's earlier commission after his lament.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish the passage's date, compositional history, single authorship, or the historicity of every narrated exchange.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence may reuse standard prophetic protection language without marking a formally distinct recommissioning scene.
- An editor may have linked independently transmitted passages through repeated vocabulary.
