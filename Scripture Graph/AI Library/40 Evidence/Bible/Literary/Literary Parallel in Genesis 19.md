# Literary Parallel in Genesis 19

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Genesis 19 and Judges 19 share a close narrative cluster: travelers receive lodging, townsmen surround the house and demand the male guest, a host offers women instead, and the threatened violence occurs at the doorway. The outcomes differ sharply, but the parallel is substantial.

**Does not establish:** The parallel alone does not determine which account is earlier, prove direct borrowing, or establish the historicity of either event.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both accounts may use a shared hospitality-violation type-scene.
- The similarities may reflect common social traditions rather than direct literary dependence.
- A later writer or editor may have deliberately shaped one account in light of the other.

**Scripture:** [[Genesis 19#^gen-19-1|Genesis 19:1-11]], [[Judges 19#^judg-19-15|Judges 19:15-26]]
