# Internal Literary Evidence in Leviticus 8

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The command at the beginning, the recurring conformity formulas, and the comprehensive obedience statement at the end constitute strong internal literary evidence that exact obedience is a central theme in the narrator's presentation of priestly consecration.

**Does not establish:** The literary pattern does not establish the ceremony's historicity, Mosaic authorship of the final text, or the divine origin of its commands.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions may partly reflect conventional priestly prose rather than a theme distinctive to this ceremony.
- An editor could have added or standardized the conformity formulas to emphasize obedience.
- Formulaic repetition may primarily organize the ritual sequence rather than make a separate theological argument.
