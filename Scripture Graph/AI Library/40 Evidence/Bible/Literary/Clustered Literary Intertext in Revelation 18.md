# Clustered Literary Intertext in Revelation 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Revelation 18 adapts several elements of Jeremiah 51's Babylon oracle: the summons for God's people to depart, Babylon's sudden fall, and an enacted sign in which a stone is cast into water to represent an irreversible sinking. The combination is strong evidence of sustained engagement with Jeremiah's oracle.

**Does not establish:** This dependence does not mean Revelation merely predicts a second fall of ancient Mesopotamian Babylon; the inherited imagery can be reapplied to another oppressive power.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Some verbal elements may have become conventional language for Babylon's fall.
- Revelation may employ Jeremiah through an already developed Jewish interpretive tradition.
