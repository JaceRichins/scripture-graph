# Repeated Literary-Theological Formula in Ezekiel 39

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated recognition formula provides strong textual evidence that revelation of the LORD's identity and rule is a central organizing concern of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** The refrain does not establish the historical identity of Gog, the date of fulfillment, or whether every narrated detail is intended literally.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetition may partly reflect a standard formula used throughout Ezekiel rather than a pattern uniquely designed for this chapter.
- Some repetition could result from the compilation or editorial shaping of related oracles.
