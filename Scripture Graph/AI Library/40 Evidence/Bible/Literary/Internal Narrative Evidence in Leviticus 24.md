# Internal Narrative Evidence in Leviticus 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The accused is held in custody while the community awaits the LORD's disclosed decision. Within the narrative's own presentation, this is strong evidence that the sentence is represented as a revealed ruling rather than as Moses' immediate personal reaction.

**Does not establish:** The narrative form does not independently establish the event's historicity or prove the divine origin of the law to readers who do not already accept the text's theological claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A literary author or editor could have framed the ruling as revelation in order to authorize an existing law.
- The delay may indicate uncertainty about the applicable penalty rather than uncertainty about whether the conduct was forbidden.
