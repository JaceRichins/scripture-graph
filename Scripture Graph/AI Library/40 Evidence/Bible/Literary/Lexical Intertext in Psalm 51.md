# Lexical Intertext in Psalm 51

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The Hebrew verb rendered "Create" in Psalm 51:10 is a form of ברא (brʾ), the verb used for God's creating in Genesis 1:1. This lexical connection supports reading the petition as a request for divine re-creation, although the shared verb alone does not require that interpretation.

**Does not establish:** The lexical correspondence does not prove a conscious Genesis allusion, creation ex nihilo within the heart, or any larger claim about the psalm's date or authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The psalmist may simply employ a conventional poetic synonym for divine renewal.
- Lexical reuse need not constitute a deliberate allusion to Genesis 1.
- The verb does not by itself imply creation ex nihilo.
