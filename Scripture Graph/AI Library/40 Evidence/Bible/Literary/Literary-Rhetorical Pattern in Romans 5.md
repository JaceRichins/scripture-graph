# Literary-Rhetorical Pattern in Romans 5

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated “much more” comparisons are strong textual evidence that the Adam–Christ parallel is deliberately asymmetrical: Christ's grace is presented not merely as matching the offence but as surpassing it.

**Does not establish:** This pattern does not establish universal salvation, a complete doctrine of inherited guilt, or any conclusion about the historical origin of Romans.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repetitions could be ordinary rhetorical amplification rather than evidence of a formally designed literary unit.
- The superiority of grace does not by itself determine how or when every individual participates in the gift.
