# Internal Political Theology in Deuteronomy 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The restrictions on royal accumulation, the required law copy, daily reading, and warning against elevation above fellow Israelites provide strong evidence for the chapter's literary-theological portrayal of limited and accountable kingship.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Israelite kings historically obeyed these limits, that the system was democratic, or when the royal law reached its present literary form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may be an exhortational ideal rather than a description of enforceable institutions.
- Some restrictions may function as a retrospective critique of Israelite royal practice.
