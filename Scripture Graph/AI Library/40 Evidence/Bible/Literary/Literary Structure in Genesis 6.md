# Literary Structure in Genesis 6

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The immediate movement from divine judgment to Noah's grace and character is strong internal evidence that Noah functions as the narrative pivot from universal corruption to preservation.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish why Noah found grace, whether grace or conduct has logical priority, or the account's historical origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The juxtaposition may be ordinary narrative sequence rather than an elaborate formal design.
