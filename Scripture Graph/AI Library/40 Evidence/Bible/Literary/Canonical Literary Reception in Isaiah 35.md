# Canonical Literary Reception in Isaiah 35

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 11:4–5 places the restoration of sight, hearing, and mobility among the works reported in answer to John the Baptist's question about Jesus. Its correspondence with Isaiah 35:5–6 is strong evidence that Matthew's presentation participates in an Isaianic restoration framework. It is weaker evidence for the more specific assertion that Isaiah 35 exclusively predicts that single episode.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that Isaiah 35 refers only to Jesus's mortal ministry, that all of the chapter was fulfilled in Matthew 11, or that no later or broader fulfillment is possible.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Matthew's list draws on a broader collection of Isaianic restoration passages rather than quoting Isaiah 35 alone.
- The correspondence may function typologically, presenting Jesus's works in scriptural categories without claiming that every feature of Isaiah 35 was fulfilled at that moment.
- Similar healing language can arise from the actual character of Jesus's reported ministry as well as deliberate literary allusion.
