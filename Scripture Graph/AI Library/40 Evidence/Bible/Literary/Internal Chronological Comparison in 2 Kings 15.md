# Internal Chronological Comparison in 2 Kings 15

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 30 dates Hoshea's accession to Jotham's twentieth year, while verse 33 assigns Jotham a reign of sixteen years. At minimum, these notices show that the synchronisms cannot safely be converted into a simple timeline without additional chronological or textual assumptions.

**Does not establish:** The mismatch does not by itself establish which reconstruction is correct, that the entire narrative is unreliable, or that a co-regency certainly occurred.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The twentieth year may count from the beginning of a co-regency rather than from Jotham's sole reign.
- Different accession-year conventions or calendars may contribute to the apparent mismatch.
- One of the chronological notices may reflect a scribal or transmission problem.
- The synchronism may use a regnal framework not fully preserved or explained in the chapter.
