# Baptismal And Triadic Language in Matthew 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Matthew 28:19 is direct textual evidence that this Gospel associates baptism with the singular 'name' of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.

**Does not establish:** The wording by itself does not define all later theological claims about the nature, equality, or unity of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The formula can be read liturgically without assuming that the verse articulates a complete metaphysical doctrine.
- Different Christian traditions interpret the relationship among the three named figures differently.
