# Rhetorical Evidence in D&C 123

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The vivid condemnatory language of verses 5-10 is strong evidence of the speaker's moral outrage and effort to motivate action, but it is not independent evidence proving the historical accusations it describes.

**Does not establish:** The intensity of the language does not independently verify particular perpetrators, damages, motives, or events.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Readers may understand the language as an authoritative moral assessment rather than primarily as motivational rhetoric.
- The rhetoric may reflect both genuine reported suffering and deliberate persuasive amplification.
