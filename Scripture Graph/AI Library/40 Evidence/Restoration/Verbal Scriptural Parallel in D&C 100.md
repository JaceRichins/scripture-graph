# Verbal Scriptural Parallel in D&C 100

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The statement that all things work together for good to those who walk uprightly is a close adaptation of Romans 8:28. D&C 100 applies that Pauline assurance specifically to upright conduct and the church's sanctification.

**Does not establish:** The parallel establishes textual affinity but does not by itself establish divine authorship or determine how much of Romans 8 should govern interpretation of D&C 100.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be an unmarked reuse of familiar King James language rather than a deliberate invitation to interpret all of Romans 8 as context.
- The phrase may have become part of general Christian discourse through repeated biblical use.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 100#^dc-100-15|D&C 100:15]], [[Romans 8#^rom-8-28|Romans 8:28]]
