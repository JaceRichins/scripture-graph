# Administrative And Literary Evidence in D&C 57

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Named officers, differentiated responsibilities, commercial arrangements, publishing assignments, and relocation instructions provide strong evidence that this chapter envisions Zion as an operational settlement project, not solely as an abstract spiritual ideal.

**Does not establish:** Administrative detail does not establish divine origin, prophetic authority, successful implementation, or the correctness of broader historical claims.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Administrative specificity can arise from an ordinary human settlement plan.
- The practical program and a spiritual conception of Zion need not be mutually exclusive.
