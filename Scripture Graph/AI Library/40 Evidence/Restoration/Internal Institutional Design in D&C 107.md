# Internal Institutional Design in D&C 107

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of consent, unanimity, ethical qualifications, appeal, and procedures for trying even the church president is strong textual evidence that the chapter's stated ideal of priesthood government includes accountability as well as authority.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that every historical decision followed these rules, nor that the system is democratic in the modern political sense.

**Alternative explanations:**
- These provisions may express institutional ideals without showing how consistently they were implemented.
- Equal-authority and appeal language can coexist with substantial centralization in the Presidency.
- The accountability mechanisms operate within a priesthood hierarchy rather than constituting modern representative democracy.
