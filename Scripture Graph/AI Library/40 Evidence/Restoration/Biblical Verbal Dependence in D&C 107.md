# Biblical Verbal Dependence in D&C 107

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sequence of virtues in D&C 107:30 and the promise in verse 31 closely follow the ordered virtue list and fruitfulness language of 2 Peter 1:5-8. The correspondence is strong evidence of literary reuse of that biblical passage.

**Does not establish:** The dependence does not by itself prove conscious quotation, plagiarism, historical provenance, or any specific theory of revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may deliberately quote or adapt 2 Peter.
- The sequence may have been reproduced from scriptural memory.
- A theological account may treat familiar biblical wording as the language through which revelation was given.
