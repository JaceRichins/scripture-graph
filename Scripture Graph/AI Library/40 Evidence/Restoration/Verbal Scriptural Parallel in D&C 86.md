# Verbal Scriptural Parallel in D&C 86

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 86:10 closely echoes Acts 3:21 by joining the restoration of all things with what the holy prophets have spoken since the world began. This is strong evidence of scriptural allusion or reuse.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not establish when or how the promised restoration is fulfilled, nor does it independently validate claims of priesthood lineage.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be a revealed restatement of Acts rather than literary borrowing.
- The phrase may have entered the revelation through Joseph Smith's biblical vocabulary.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 86#^dc-86-10|D&C 86:10]], [[Acts 3#^acts-3-21|Acts 3:21]]
