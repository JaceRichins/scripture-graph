# Literary Structure in D&C 84

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 6 begins a statement about the sons of Moses, but the thought is interrupted by an extended discussion and explicitly resumed in verse 31 with “as I said concerning the sons of Moses.” This securely identifies a large parenthetical or digressive unit in the present text. It offers only weak evidence for an extemporaneous or dictated compositional texture because a deliberate written digression is also plausible.

**Does not establish:** The structure does not establish the text's mode of production, date, or divine or human origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The interruption may be a deliberate literary parenthesis rather than a feature of oral composition.
- The structure may reflect later arrangement or editing rather than the initial delivery.
