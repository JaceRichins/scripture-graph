# Intra-Canonical Explanatory Coherence in D&C 103

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 103 makes success contingent on obedience and warns that polluted inheritances can again be lost; D&C 105:2-9 later explains the non-redemption of Zion in terms of transgression, insufficient obedience and unity, and a resulting need to wait. The later explanation is therefore conceptually consistent with conditions already stated in D&C 103.

**Does not establish:** The consistency does not prove advance prediction, divine causation of the outcome, or that participant disobedience was the only historical reason Zion was not restored.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The later revelation could be a retrospective theological explanation for an unsuccessful undertaking.
- Both sections could independently employ common covenant language in which blessing is conditional upon obedience.
- Canonical or editorial arrangement may heighten the appearance of a planned narrative progression.
