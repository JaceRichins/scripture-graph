# Internal Literary Coherence in D&C 128

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The recurrence of records at the administrative opening, in the interpretations of Revelation and Matthew, in the earthly-heavenly analogy, and in the final temple offering is strong internal evidence that recordkeeping is a deliberate organizing motif of the chapter.

**Does not establish:** Literary coherence does not establish the divine origin of the theology or the historical reality of the heavenly records and angelic visitations described.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The recurrence could partly result from the practical subject of the letter rather than a consciously designed literary structure.
- Later editorial arrangement could accentuate coherence, although the chapter itself presents the material as one letter.
