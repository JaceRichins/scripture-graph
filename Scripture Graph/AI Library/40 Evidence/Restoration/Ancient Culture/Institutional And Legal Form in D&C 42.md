# Institutional And Legal Form in D&C 42

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Detailed provisions concerning recognized ordination, deeds, stewardship, storehouse administration, officer support, witnesses, hearings, and jurisdiction are strong internal evidence that the chapter presents itself as operational communal law rather than solely as general devotional exhortation.

**Does not establish:** The internal form does not establish how consistently the law was implemented, how later communities interpreted it, or whether its claimed revelatory authority is historically demonstrable.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A text may prescribe detailed institutions without those provisions being fully implemented.
- The administrative detail could reflect developing practical needs rather than a single comprehensive design.
- Legal specificity alone says nothing about whether each rule was effective or just.
