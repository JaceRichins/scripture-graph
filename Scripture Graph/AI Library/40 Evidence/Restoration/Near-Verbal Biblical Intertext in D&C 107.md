# Near-Verbal Biblical Intertext in D&C 107

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The same extended sequence of uncommon virtues and the shared abundance-fruitfulness conclusion make literary reuse of 2 Peter 1:5-8 in D&C 107:30-31 highly probable.

**Does not establish:** The dependence does not establish independent ancient authorship, a particular theory of revelation, or whether the reuse was conscious.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage might reproduce the sequence through unconscious familiarity with biblical wording rather than deliberate quotation.
- A shared Christian virtue tradition could explain some individual terms, though it less readily explains their order and concluding language.
