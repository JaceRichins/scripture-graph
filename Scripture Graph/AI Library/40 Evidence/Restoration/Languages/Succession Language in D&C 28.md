# Succession Language in D&C 28

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The successor clause supplies clear evidence that the chapter's authority structure was not worded as permanently inseparable from Joseph personally, but it gives no procedure for identifying or appointing the eventual successor.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish which later claimant or succession procedure was legitimate.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The clause may contemplate divine appointment without specifying any human succession process.
- The antecedent of the plural pronoun in the clause is somewhat awkward, limiting conclusions about the exact office or keys transferred.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 28#^dc-28-7|D&C 28:7]]
