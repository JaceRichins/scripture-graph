# Time-Bounded Prophetic Language in D&C 84

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter makes an apparently time-bounded prediction that a temple would be built at the appointed place within 'this generation.' The wording creates a genuine interpretive and historical question because the intended scope of 'generation,' the identity of the promised house, and the conditions attached to the prediction are not explained within these verses.

**Does not establish:** The wording alone does not establish either fulfillment or failed prophecy; those conclusions require historical evidence and a defended interpretation of the prediction's terms.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Generation may be intended in a broader or nonstandard sense rather than as one ordinary human lifespan.
- The promise may be read as conditional upon the Saints' obedience even though the condition is not explicit in these verses.
- An interpreter may identify another sacred building or a future fulfillment with the promised house, though the immediate text points to the consecrated spot.
