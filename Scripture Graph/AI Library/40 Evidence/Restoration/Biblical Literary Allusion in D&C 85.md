# Biblical Literary Allusion in D&C 85

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The combination of putting forth a hand toward the ark of God and suffering death strongly supports a literary allusion in D&C 85:8 to Uzzah's death in the ark narrative.

**Does not establish:** The allusion does not identify the unnamed man, determine when the warning would be fulfilled, or prove a larger claim about the document's authorship.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording could employ a familiar biblical image without claiming a detailed correspondence to Uzzah.
- The allusion could be mediated through common English religious idiom rather than direct textual composition from 2 Samuel.
