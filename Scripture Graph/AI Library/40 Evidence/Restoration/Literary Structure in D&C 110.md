# Literary Structure in D&C 110

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated transitions after one vision closes and another opens provide strong internal evidence that verses 11-16 are arranged as a deliberate sequence of distinct commissions.

**Does not establish:** The narrative structure does not independently verify the historical occurrence, supernatural origin, or institutional meaning of the commissions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated transitions may be conventional visionary narration rather than a highly developed literary design.
- The present sequencing may reflect how the account was recorded or edited.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 110#^dc-110-11|D&C 110:11-16]]
