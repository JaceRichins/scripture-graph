# Doctrinal Identification in D&C 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 27:11 identifies 'Michael' with 'Adam' and titles him 'the ancient of days,' the same title given to the enthroned divine judge figure in Daniel 7. This reflects the doctrine, taught elsewhere in LDS scripture, that Adam and the archangel Michael are the same being.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Daniel's author(s) intended 'the Ancient of days' to refer to Adam or Michael; the equation depends on later LDS revelatory interpretation, not on Daniel's own context.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Mainstream biblical scholarship reads 'the Ancient of days' in Daniel 7 as a title for God/Yahweh himself as heavenly judge, not as a title later borne by a human patriarch such as Adam; Daniel's text gives no indication that this figure is to be identified with Adam.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 27#^dc-27-11|D&C 27:11]], [[Daniel 7#^dan-7-9|Daniel 7:9]]
