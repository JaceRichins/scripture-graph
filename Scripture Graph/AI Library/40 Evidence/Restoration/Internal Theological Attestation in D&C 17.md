# Internal Theological Attestation in D&C 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter contains an explicit first-person divine attestation that the translated book is true. This is central theological evidence within the revelation's own framework, but it is not independent corroboration for readers who do not already accept the revelation's divine authority.

**Does not establish:** The text's self-attestation does not independently prove the book's truth, the revelation's divine origin, or Joseph Smith's prophetic authority.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Believers may receive the declaration as revelation from Christ.
- A historical-critical reader may understand the divine voice as the text's asserted authority rather than independently verified speech.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 17#^dc-17-6|D&C 17:6]], [[D&C 17#^dc-17-9|D&C 17:9]]
