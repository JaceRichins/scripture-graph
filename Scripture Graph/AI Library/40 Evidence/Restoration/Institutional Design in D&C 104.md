# Institutional Design in D&C 104

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit separation of sacred proceeds from ordinary stewardship income strongly supports a reading in which the order distinguished restricted religious funds from general operating funds.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish compliance, protection against misuse, or direct equivalence with modern accounting categories.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The distinction may have been primarily ritual or symbolic rather than equivalent to modern fund accounting.
- The chapter does not provide enough operational detail to reconstruct every permitted transaction.
