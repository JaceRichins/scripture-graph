# Verbal Intertext in D&C 112

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The wording of D&C 112:29 closely parallels Mark 16:16 in linking belief and baptism with salvation and unbelief with condemnation. This is strong evidence of a literary relationship or use of a shared biblical formula, although the precise mode and claimed revelatory significance cannot be established by wording alone.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the direction or mechanism of dependence, the exhaustive conditions of salvation, or the historical authenticity of the revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may deliberately quote or paraphrase Mark.
- Both passages may employ a familiar Christian missionary formula.
- The correspondence may reflect the scriptural language available to the modern revelatory setting rather than a separately significant textual dependence.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 112#^dc-112-29|D&C 112:29]], [[Mark 16#^mark-16-16|Mark 16:16]]
