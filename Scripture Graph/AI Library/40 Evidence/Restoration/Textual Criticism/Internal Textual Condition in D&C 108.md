# Internal Textual Condition in D&C 108

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit condition "if you continue faithful" is strong textual evidence that the promise described in the surrounding verses is conditional upon continuing faithfulness; it is not evidence within the chapter that the promise was historically fulfilled.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish from this chapter alone whether or when the promised ordination and preaching commission occurred.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The condition may attach specifically to the promise in verses 4–6 rather than to every blessing mentioned in the revelation.
- The earlier declaration of forgiveness may concern Lyman's present standing and need not be grammatically dependent upon the later condition.
