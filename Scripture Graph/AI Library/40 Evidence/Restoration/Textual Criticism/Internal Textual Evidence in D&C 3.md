# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verses 17–20 are strong internal evidence for what the revelation claims about the plates' intended beneficiaries and purpose, but they are not independent external evidence that every named people existed historically or that any present population can be identified with them.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not establish external historicity, ancestry, geography, or a modern ethnic identification for the named groups.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Believing readers may accept the revelation itself as divine testimony and therefore assign it greater historical evidentiary weight.
- Critical readers may understand the named groups and preservation narrative within a nineteenth-century revelatory or compositional setting.
