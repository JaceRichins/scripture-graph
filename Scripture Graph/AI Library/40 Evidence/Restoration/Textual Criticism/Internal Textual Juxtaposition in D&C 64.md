# Internal Textual Juxtaposition in D&C 64

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The juxtaposition of required personal forgiveness with formal church accountability is strong internal evidence that the chapter distinguishes a forgiving disposition from the suspension of institutional judgment.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the complete procedure for church discipline, the outcome required in every case, or how these instructions map onto later institutional policies.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passages may address different kinds of offenses or different audiences rather than articulate a general distinction between personal and institutional responses.
- The precise meaning of being brought before the church depends on procedural context not supplied in this chapter.
