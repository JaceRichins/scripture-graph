# Verbal And Structural Intertextuality in D&C 122

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated “in perils” construction and the shared categories of robbers, sea, and false brethren make D&C 122:5 a close formal and lexical parallel to Paul's catalog in 2 Corinthians 11:26. This is strong evidence that the verse participates in recognizable King James Bible diction.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish whether the wording arose through conscious composition, revelatory accommodation, later recording practices, or another mechanism.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be a conscious Pauline allusion.
- The wording may reflect the revelator's or recorder's habitual immersion in King James Bible language rather than a deliberately marked quotation.
- Some similarity could result from the conventional language of danger catalogs, although the combined verbal parallels make pure coincidence less likely.
