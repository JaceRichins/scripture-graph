# Ethical-Administrative Textual Evidence in D&C 124

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The placement and specificity of the Nauvoo House rules provide strong textual evidence that economic administration is treated as morally accountable stewardship within the chapter, particularly where restricted funds, consent, restitution, and fair wages are concerned.

**Does not establish:** These regulations do not by themselves establish that the project was successfully completed, legally effective, or administered without later misconduct.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The detail may primarily answer immediate legal and practical needs rather than articulate a general theology of finance.
- Religious sanctions may function rhetorically to reinforce compliance with a specific communal project.
