# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 17

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As a text, D&C 17 is strong primary evidence that a specific witness experience and subsequent public testimony were being prescribed; because its language is prospective, the chapter alone is not evidence that the recipients later saw the objects or fulfilled the command.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the manifestation occurred, that every listed object was seen, or that the text is of divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The instructions could have been composed or finalized as part of a developing witness narrative.
- Later fulfillment must be assessed from later testimony and historical records rather than inferred from this prospective text.
