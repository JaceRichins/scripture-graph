# Internal Textual Instruction in D&C 58

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Within this specific Zion settlement program, the command to purchase land "lest" inheritance come through bloodshed is strong textual evidence that acquisition by purchase, rather than violent seizure, was the prescribed course.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a universal prohibition on war, prove that later participants consistently followed the instruction, or settle modern land-rights questions.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The warning may anticipate consequences of failing to purchase land without articulating a comprehensive moral prohibition on every use of force.
- The instruction concerns this settlement and cannot automatically be generalized into a complete doctrine of pacifism.
