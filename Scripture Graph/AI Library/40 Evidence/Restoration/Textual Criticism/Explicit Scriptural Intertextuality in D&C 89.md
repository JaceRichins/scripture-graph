# Explicit Scriptural Intertextuality in D&C 89

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The destroying-angel promise is explicit textual evidence of a deliberate Passover allusion: the revelation itself compares the promised deliverance to that of the children of Israel.

**Does not establish:** The allusion does not establish that every dietary instruction corresponds to a Passover element, that obedience guarantees immunity from all harm, or that the revelation has an ancient origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording could function mainly as familiar biblical rhetoric rather than prescribing a detailed one-to-one correspondence with every element of the Passover narrative.
- The analogy may emphasize divine protection generally without promising universal exemption from physical death.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 89#^dc-89-21|D&C 89:21]], [[Exodus 12#^ex-12-23|Exodus 12:23]]
