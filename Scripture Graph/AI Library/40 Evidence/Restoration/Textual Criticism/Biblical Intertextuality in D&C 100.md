# Biblical Intertextuality in D&C 100

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 100:15 has a close verbal affinity with Romans 8:28 in its statement that all things work together for good. D&C 100 adapts the surrounding formulation by referring to those who walk uprightly and by adding the sanctification of the church.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish a particular theory of composition, direct copying, divine origin, historical independence, or the interpretation of every phrase in D&C 100:15.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be a conscious allusion to Romans.
- It may reflect remembered King James Bible diction or broader Christian idiom.
- A believer may understand the biblical language as part of the revelation's revelatory register.
- The recorded wording may reflect transmission or editorial conventions.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 100#^dc-100-15|D&C 100:15]], [[Romans 8#^rom-8-28|Romans 8:28]]
