# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 128

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated integration of eyewitnesses, certified records, priesthood authority, earthly-heavenly correspondence, and a final temple book is strong internal textual evidence that the chapter treats accurate recording as integral to the ordinance system rather than as incidental clerical housekeeping.

**Does not establish:** The administrative system does not independently establish divine authorization, heavenly ratification, or the historical accuracy of every ordinance record.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The procedural detail may partly reflect the administrative difficulty of documenting a rapidly expanding practice.
- The record system may function as institutional quality control even apart from the chapter's theological explanation.
- The text expresses the author's own theological claims and is not external evidence that earthly entries receive heavenly recognition.
