# Textual Ambiguity in D&C 64

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 36 states that 'the rebellious are not of the blood of Ephraim,' tying continued standing in Zion to lineage language; this can be read either as describing literal descent or as figurative/adoptive covenant status, and the verse itself does not disambiguate which reading is intended.

**Does not establish:** The verse alone does not establish which reading — literal descent or figurative covenant adoption — the text intends; that determination depends on later doctrinal development outside this chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A literal-lineage reading, common in early-19th-century American religious thought about Israelite descent.
- A figurative/covenantal reading in which 'blood of Ephraim' denotes adopted covenant status conferred through faithfulness rather than genealogy, the reading favored in later official teaching on patriarchal blessings and adoption into the house of Israel.
