# Intertextual Verbal Parallel in D&C 43

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The lament that God repeatedly desired to gather the nations as a hen gathers her chickens but they refused is a close verbal and thematic parallel to Matthew 23:37 and to the expanded hen-gathering imagery in 3 Nephi 10:4–6.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish literary priority, independent antiquity, or the historical origin of the wording.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The revelation may deliberately adapt familiar biblical language from Matthew.
- The wording may reflect a broader scriptural tradition already reused in 3 Nephi.
- The parallel alone cannot determine whether one passage is directly dependent on another.
