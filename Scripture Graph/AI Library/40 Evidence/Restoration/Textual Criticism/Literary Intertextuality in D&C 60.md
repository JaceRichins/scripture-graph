# Literary Intertextuality in D&C 60

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The cluster of talents hidden or buried, possessions being taken away, jewels gathered in a coming day, missionaries traveling two by two, and dust shaken from the feet provides strong literary evidence that the English wording of D&C 60 participates in a King James Bible idiom.

**Does not establish:** These correspondences do not determine the mechanism of composition, establish a single source passage for the whole chapter, or independently prove or disprove divine revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may be a deliberate adaptation of biblical passages.
- The wording may reflect the revelator's habitual biblical vocabulary without a single consciously selected source.
- A believing interpretation may regard the biblical language as revelatory reuse rather than ordinary literary borrowing.
