# Textual Qualification in D&C 68

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter makes being moved upon by the Holy Ghost the stated condition under which an elder's speech is called scripture and the word or voice of the Lord. This is strong internal evidence against reading the passage as granting automatic scriptural status to every statement by an ordained speaker.

**Does not establish:** It does not define a canonization process, identify every inspired utterance, or establish that ordained leaders are generally infallible.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The condition may describe an inspired ideal without supplying hearers an objective procedure for determining when it has been met.
- Some readers may infer a presumption of inspiration for authorized missionary preaching, although the text does not state that every such utterance is inspired.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 68#^dc-68-3|D&C 68:3]], [[D&C 68#^dc-68-4|D&C 68:4]]
