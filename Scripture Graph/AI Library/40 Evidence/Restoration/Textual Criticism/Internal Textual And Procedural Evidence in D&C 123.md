# Internal Textual And Procedural Evidence in D&C 123

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The instructions to collect facts, identify participants, quantify damages, appoint a committee, obtain statements and affidavits, and preserve publications constitute strong evidence that the proposed appeal was intended to rest on an organized documentary record.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the truth of every accusation, the completeness of the resulting record, or the chapter's theological explanation of the oppression.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The procedures may partly reflect strategic preparation for public advocacy or governmental petitions.
- Affidavits and collected publications can document claims without guaranteeing that every claim or interpretation is accurate.
- The text specifies an intended program; by itself it does not show how completely or impartially that program was carried out.
