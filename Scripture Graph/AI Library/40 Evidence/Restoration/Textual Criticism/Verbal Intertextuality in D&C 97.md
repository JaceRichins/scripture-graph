# Verbal Intertextuality in D&C 97

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The wording about the ax, unfruitful trees, cutting down, and fire closely parallels biblical judgment sayings and is strong evidence that D&C 97 participates in the scriptural tree-fruit-fire tradition.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself determine a unique source, explain the mode of composition, establish ancient authorship, or verify the revelation's divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may arise from broad familiarity with biblical discourse rather than deliberate reuse of one particular verse.
- Several canonical passages share the same image, making a unique direct source difficult to identify.
- The phrasing may reflect the pervasive influence of King James Bible language on the revelation's English expression.
