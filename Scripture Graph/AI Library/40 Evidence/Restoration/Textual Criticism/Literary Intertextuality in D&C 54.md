# Literary Intertextuality in D&C 54

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clustered verbal correspondences with Revelation 22, Matthew 18, and Proverbs 8 are strong evidence that D&C 54 is deliberately expressed through biblical language and associations.

**Does not establish:** These literary correspondences do not by themselves establish divine origin, historical authenticity, or any particular theory of composition.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallels may reflect the speaker's immersion in King James Bible diction rather than deliberate allusions to each specific passage.
- Some wording may belong to a broadly shared nineteenth-century Christian scriptural register.
- The separate correspondences do not by themselves determine whether the composition was conscious, spontaneous, or understood as revelation.
