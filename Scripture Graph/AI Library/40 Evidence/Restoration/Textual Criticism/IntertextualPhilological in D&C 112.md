# IntertextualPhilological in D&C 112

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 112:25's 'upon my house shall it begin' closely parallels 1 Peter 4:17's statement that judgment 'must begin at the house of God.' The shared concept (judgment starting with the professing community rather than outsiders) and near-identical phrasing support literary/idiomatic dependence, though the passages differ in surrounding argument and purpose.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that the author consciously quoted or intended to invoke 1 Peter specifically, only that the language and concept are closely related.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Both texts may independently draw on a common stock biblical idiom about judgment beginning with God's own people, without one directly echoing the other.
- The parallel could be coincidental phrasing rather than deliberate allusion.
