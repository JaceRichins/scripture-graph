# Internal Primary Textual Evidence in D&C 115

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verse 4 is direct primary-text evidence that this revelation prescribes the designation The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints for the church in the last days.

**Does not establish:** It does not by itself establish uninterrupted historical usage of the full name, the date of composition, or the revelation's divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The verse establishes what the canonical text prescribes, but historical questions about when and how consistently the name was used require evidence outside this chapter.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 115#^dc-115-4|D&C 115:4]]
