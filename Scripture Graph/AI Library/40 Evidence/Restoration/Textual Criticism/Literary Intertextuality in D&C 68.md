# Literary Intertextuality in D&C 68

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The dense verbal and thematic correspondence between D&C 68:8–10 and the New Testament Great Commission strongly supports conscious reuse or restatement of biblical commissioning language.

**Does not establish:** The correspondence alone does not determine the mechanism of composition, prove historical independence, or adjudicate the revelation's divine origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The revelation may deliberately present a divine restatement of earlier scripture.
- The correspondence may reflect Joseph Smith's and his audience's immersion in King James Bible language.
- Both passages may employ a shared Christian missionary formula.
