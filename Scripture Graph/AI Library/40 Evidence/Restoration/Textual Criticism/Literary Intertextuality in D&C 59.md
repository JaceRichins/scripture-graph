# Literary Intertextuality in D&C 59

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The convergence of the love-commandment sequence, Decalogue-like prohibitions, the contrite-heart formula, the rest-and-works-follow promise, and the explicit appeal to the law and the prophets is strong evidence that D&C 59 is deliberately framed through biblical language and categories.

**Does not establish:** These intertexts do not by themselves establish divine revelation, ancient provenance, literary independence from the Bible, or any particular direction or mechanism of dependence.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The parallels may reflect direct or indirect use of the King James Bible.
- Some language may arise from broad Jewish and Christian scriptural tradition rather than a single targeted source.
- Individual parallels could be conventional religious diction, although the cluster makes coincidence unlikely.
