# Administrative Textual Evidence in D&C 124

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The stock limits, payment requirements, restricted use of funds, stockholder-consent rule, fourfold repayment provision, and wage instructions are strong evidence that the Nauvoo House is presented as an accountable economic stewardship rather than only as a symbolic religious ideal.

**Does not establish:** Administrative specificity does not establish divine origin, historical compliance, financial success, or freedom from later disputes.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The regulations may reflect practical corporate governance needs without carrying a broader theology of economic stewardship.
- Detailed rules demonstrate intended accountability but do not demonstrate that the rules were successfully implemented.
