# Explicit Scriptural Intertextuality in D&C 138

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The verbatim biblical quotations and explicit prophetic allusions provide strong evidence that the chapter is deliberately constructed as an interpretation and expansion of earlier scripture.

**Does not establish:** This literary dependence does not establish the divine origin, historical objectivity, or metaphysical truth of the reported vision.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The scriptural framing may reflect later literary shaping of the account rather than the precise sequence of the visionary experience.
- Intertextual expansion can arise through ordinary theological reflection and does not by itself require a supernatural explanation.
