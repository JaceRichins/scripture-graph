# Explicit Intratextual Causal Explanation in D&C 105

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
Verses 2–10 provide strong evidence for the chapter's own theological explanation of the delay—corporate disobedience and insufficient preparation—but they do not establish that these were the only historical causes affecting the expedition or displaced Saints.

**Does not establish:** The passage alone does not supply a complete historical explanation or show that every afflicted person was responsible for the delay.

**Alternative explanations:**
- External violence, legal conditions, logistics, disease, and political constraints may also have affected historical outcomes.
- The explanation may function pastorally or rhetorically in addition to making a causal claim.
