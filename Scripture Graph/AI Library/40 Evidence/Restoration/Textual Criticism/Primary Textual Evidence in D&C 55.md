# Primary Textual Evidence in D&C 55

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
As primary textual evidence, the chapter strongly supports the limited historical-theological claim that educational publishing for children was represented as part of divinely authorized church work.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish how many schools existed, which books were completed, how the program was administered, or the historical origin of the revelation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The instruction may describe a proposed program rather than one that was fully implemented.
- The phrase may concern a narrow curriculum assignment rather than a comprehensive philosophy of education.
- Calling the work divinely pleasing within the revelation does not by itself establish how later church schools understood their mission.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 55#^dc-55-4|D&C 55:4]]
