# Textual And Administrative Specificity in D&C 115

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The named recipients, specified location, phased schedule, financial constraint, construction standard, and provision for later expansion constitute strong textual evidence that the chapter presents itself as a concrete administrative directive rather than only a general devotional discourse.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish divine authorship, fulfillment of the construction program, or the historical outcome of the commands.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A text can adopt detailed administrative language as a literary or institutional composition.
- Specificity demonstrates the discourse's practical form but does not independently verify its divine source.
