# Literary Intertextuality in D&C 95

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The cluster of recognizable biblical and Book of Mormon expressions is good evidence that D&C 95 presents its temple command through an intentionally scriptural idiom joining election, final vineyard labor, divine action, and apostolic empowerment.

**Does not establish:** The intertextual pattern does not establish supernatural origin, ancient authorship, or any particular theory of how the revelation was produced.

**Alternative explanations:**
- A speaker immersed in scripture may reuse familiar language without constructing a tightly planned literary network.
- Some similarities may arise from the shared religious vocabulary of the King James Bible and early Latter-day Saint discourse.
- Readers may perceive greater thematic unity among the echoes than the text's producer intended.
