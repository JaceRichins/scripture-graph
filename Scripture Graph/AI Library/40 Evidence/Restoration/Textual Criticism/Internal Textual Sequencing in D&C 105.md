# Internal Textual Sequencing in D&C 105

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter's sequencing is strong internal evidence for distinguishing its immediate policy of restraint, legal redress, and peace from its conditional depiction of later force. This distinction clarifies the text but does not eliminate the ethical tension between the two programs.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish absolute pacifism, prove that the future martial scenario was figurative, or morally resolve every contemplated use of force.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The peace instructions may be chiefly a temporary strategy while the community lacks sufficient strength.
- The martial language may be rhetorical or symbolic rather than a concrete military plan.
- Peace and future coercion may have been understood by the text's speaker as compatible rather than contradictory.
