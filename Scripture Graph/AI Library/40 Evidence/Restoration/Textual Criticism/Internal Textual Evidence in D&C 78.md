# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 78

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The chapter provides strong textual evidence that its economic and administrative commands are framed as part of spiritual preparation, rather than as merely secular management.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish a complete economic system, a requirement of identical possessions, the program's historical implementation, or a universally applicable institutional form.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Spiritual language may authorize and motivate a practical institutional program without prescribing a general economic theology.
- The commands may be specific to this order and its historical participants rather than universally binding in the same administrative form.
