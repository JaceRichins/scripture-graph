# Verbal Intertextuality in D&C 21

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The near-verbatim 'gates of hell shall not prevail' wording is strong textual evidence that D&C 21:6 frames its promise in biblical language associated with Christ's declaration about his church in Matthew 16:18. It is only moderate evidence for a deliberate, specific allusion because the phrase was already memorable and widely available in the King James Bible.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel does not establish ancient authorship, historical fulfillment, institutional indefectibility, or that every later act of a church leader carries divine authority.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may reflect general familiarity with King James Bible idiom rather than a carefully constructed allusion to Matthew 16:18.
- The shared phrase alone does not determine whether 'you' in D&C 21:6 refers to particular hearers, the church collectively, or both.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 21#^dc-21-6|D&C 21:6]], [[Matthew 16#^matt-16-18|Matthew 16:18]]
