# Textual Correspondence in D&C 20

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The sacrament prayers in D&C 20:77 and 20:79 correspond nearly verbatim to Moroni 4:3 and 5:2. This is strong evidence of a direct literary or shared liturgical relationship between the texts, though verbal correspondence alone cannot determine the direction or historical origin of that relationship.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish the Book of Mormon's antiquity, determine which written form came first, or prove supernatural origin.

**Alternative explanations:**
- D&C 20 may directly adopt the prayers from the Book of Mormon.
- Both passages may preserve a common revealed or liturgical form.
- Editorial standardization could have increased the wording's similarity.
