# Intertextual Verbal And Thematic Parallel in D&C 75

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The clustering of house-to-house mission instructions, dust-shaking, judgment language, and the ask-and-knock formula is strong evidence that D&C 75 consciously employs language and patterns familiar from the King James New Testament.

**Does not establish:** The parallels do not establish divine origin, ancient authorship, historical fulfillment of the threatened judgments, or independence from the King James Bible.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The language may reflect direct reuse of familiar King James Bible expressions rather than an independent revelation reproducing an ancient missionary pattern.
- Some similarities may arise from a shared Christian missionary vocabulary, although the combined parallels are more specific than a general thematic resemblance.
