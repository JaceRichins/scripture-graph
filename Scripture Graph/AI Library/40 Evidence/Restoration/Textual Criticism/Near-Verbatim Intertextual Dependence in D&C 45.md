# Near-Verbatim Intertextual Dependence in D&C 45

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 45:7-8 closely reworks John 1:5 and John 1:11-12, recasting third-person narration about the Logos into first-person divine speech by Christ ('I came unto mine own, and mine own received me not... gave I power... to become the sons of God').

**Does not establish:** Does not by itself establish anything about the historicity of the Prologue of John or the source of the revelation beyond literary dependence on the KJV text Joseph Smith had access to.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording overlap could reflect generic KJV cadence rather than deliberate literary quotation, though the sequence and near-identical clauses make coincidence unlikely.
