# Internal Textual Synthesis in D&C 104

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The conjunction of divine ownership, individualized stewardship, accountability, aid to the poor, productive use of property, and commonly controlled treasuries is strong textual evidence that D&C 104 presents consecration as an integrated religious-economic ethic rather than as almsgiving alone.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that any modern economic system is identical to the revealed order or that every administrative detail is universally binding.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The provisions may primarily regulate a particular 1834 institution rather than define every enduring feature of consecration.
- Some features may reflect practical responses to debt, publishing needs, and property arrangements more than a comprehensive economic theory.
