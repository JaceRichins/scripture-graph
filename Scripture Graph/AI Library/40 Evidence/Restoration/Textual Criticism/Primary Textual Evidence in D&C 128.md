# Primary Textual Evidence in D&C 128

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 128 is strong primary textual evidence that the chapter itself integrates proxy baptism, priesthood authority, authenticated records, intergenerational perfection, restoration claims, and temple practice. It is not independent evidence that the asserted heavenly ratification or reported angelic manifestations occurred.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish divine authorship, heavenly recognition of recorded ordinances, the historical reality of the reported manifestations, or the correctness of every biblical interpretation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The theological synthesis may reflect the chapter's own interpretive construction rather than meanings required by the biblical passages it cites.
- The recording procedures may respond to practical institutional needs without demonstrating a corresponding heavenly record.
- Literary coherence can result from sustained composition or revision and does not establish supernatural origin.
