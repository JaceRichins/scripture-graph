# Scriptural Intertextuality in D&C 58

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The distinctive feast and refined-wine language in D&C 58:8 closely reuses Isaiah 25:6. The surrounding verses adapt that prophetic banquet imagery to Zion, the poor, all nations, and the marriage of the Lamb.

**Does not establish:** The verbal parallel alone does not establish supernatural origin, ancient authorship, or exclusive fulfillment of Isaiah 25 in nineteenth-century Missouri.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording may reflect Joseph Smith's familiarity with the King James Bible rather than a separately revealed allusion.
- The reuse may be primarily rhetorical and need not identify a single, exhaustive fulfillment of Isaiah's prophecy.
