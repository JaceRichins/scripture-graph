# Internal Textual Pattern in D&C 132

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated vocabulary of appointment, command, divine giving, law, word, and sealing is strong internal evidence that authorization is the chapter's organizing justificatory principle.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the claimed commands occurred, that the authority claim is divine, or that the resulting system is morally justified.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The repeated authorization language could be an ad hoc legal defense of already contested conduct rather than evidence of a prior, consistently applied principle.
- Different portions of the chapter may reflect accumulated instructions whose apparent unity results from compilation or redaction.
