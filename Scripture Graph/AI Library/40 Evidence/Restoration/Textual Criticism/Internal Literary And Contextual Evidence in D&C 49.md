# Internal Literary And Contextual Evidence in D&C 49

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The opening and closing mission commands bracket teachings on gospel ordinances, marriage, use of creation, and the Second Coming. This is strong internal evidence that the chapter presents these teachings as relevant to the Shaker mission, but it does not prove that every intervening statement answers a separately documented Shaker belief.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish the precise historical beliefs of the Shakers, a one-to-one correspondence between each correction and those beliefs, or the historical outcome of the mission.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The revelation may include a general doctrinal summary occasioned by the mission rather than a point-by-point response to Shaker teachings.
- Some teachings may address the missionaries or the wider church as much as the named audience.
