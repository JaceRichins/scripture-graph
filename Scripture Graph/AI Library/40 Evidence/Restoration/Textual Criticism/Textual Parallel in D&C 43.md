# Textual Parallel in D&C 43

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 43:24's lament that the Lord would have 'gathered you together as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, but ye would not' closely parallels wording attributed to Christ in Matthew 23:37 and in 3 Nephi 10:4-6, an overlap already confirmed by mechanical text-overlap indexing.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that the 3 Nephi occurrence is an authentic ancient utterance rather than a product of Joseph Smith's translation idiom; the parallel shows internal consistency of LDS scripture, not external/ancient authentication.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The hen-and-chickens metaphor is a natural pastoral image that could recur independently across texts without deliberate literary dependence.
- Joseph Smith, deeply familiar with the KJV, may have consciously echoed Matthew 23:37 in his own prophetic voice regardless of whether 3 Nephi's parallel usage reflects an ancient original.
