# Internal Textual Evidence in D&C 57

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated qualifications governing land acquisition provide strong textual evidence that the revealed program is conditional and procedural rather than an instruction for immediate, unlimited possession.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that the purchase program succeeded, that later land claims were legally or morally valid, or that the Saints would retain uninterrupted possession.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The qualifications may be ordinary prudential language rather than a deliberate theological limitation.
- A reader may distinguish the permanence of the promised inheritance from the contingent means prescribed for acquiring it.

**Scripture:** [[D&C 57#^dc-57-4|D&C 57:4]], [[D&C 57#^dc-57-5|D&C 57:5]], [[D&C 57#^dc-57-6|D&C 57:6]]
