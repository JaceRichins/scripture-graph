# Contextual And Lexical Evidence in D&C 71

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated temporal qualifications—mission for a season, further direction to come, and vindication in God's due time—are strong internal evidence for reading the commission as a bounded assignment whose outcomes need not be immediate.

**Does not establish:** It does not determine the mission's exact duration or rule out responsible later application of its principles.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The word season can function as an indefinite rhetorical expression rather than a sharply defined limit.
- Some readers may extend the promises typologically to later disciples despite their immediate addressees.
