# Literary Dependence Or Conscious Reuse in D&C 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The shared order and vocabulary provide strong evidence of a direct literary relationship between D&C 27:15-18 and the English biblical armor passage in Ephesians 6. This observation supports studying D&C 27 as an interpretive reuse of biblical language.

**Does not establish:** The parallel does not by itself establish a particular theory of revelation, historical authenticity, or the direction and mechanism of inspiration.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The wording could be described as revelatory restatement rather than ordinary literary composition.
- Some shared imagery might be mediated through broad familiarity with biblical language, although the sustained order makes coincidence implausible.
