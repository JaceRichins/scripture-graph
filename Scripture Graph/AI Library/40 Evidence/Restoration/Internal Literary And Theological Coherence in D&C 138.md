# Internal Literary And Theological Coherence in D&C 138

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The distinction between Christ's personal preaching and the preaching of commissioned messengers supplies a coherent internal answer to the narrator's stated timing problem and reconciles the chapter's claim that Christ did not personally enter the wicked assembly with Peter's broader statement that the gospel was preached to the dead.

**Does not establish:** Internal coherence does not establish the vision's divine origin, the objective geography of a spirit world, or that this is the only possible interpretation of 1 Peter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The distinction may represent a later theological harmonization of ambiguous New Testament passages.
- Other Christian interpretations understand 1 Peter's preaching language as proclamation to fallen spirits or as Christ preaching through Noah.
- Narrative coherence can result from careful composition without demonstrating supernatural revelation.
