# Near-Verbatim Quotation Applied Typologically in D&C 45

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
D&C 45:51-52's exchange—'What are these wounds in thine hands and in thy feet?' answered 'These wounds are the wounds with which I was wounded in the house of my friends'—closely parallels Zechariah 13:6, applying the prophecy explicitly to the resurrected Christ's wounds being recognized by the Jews.

**Does not establish:** Does not establish that Zechariah 13:6 was originally composed as messianic prophecy; that is an interpretive application, not a feature intrinsic to the Hebrew text's original context.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Zechariah 13:6 is disputed among biblical scholars as to whether it originally referred to a false prophet's self-inflicted wounds rather than a messianic figure; the messianic reading is a Christian (and here Latter-day Saint) interpretive layer, not the only historical-critical reading.
