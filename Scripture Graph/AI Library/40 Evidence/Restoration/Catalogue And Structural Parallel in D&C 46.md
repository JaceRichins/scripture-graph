# Catalogue And Structural Parallel in D&C 46

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The catalogue has an extensive textual and conceptual relationship with 1 Corinthians 12: both distribute different gifts through the Spirit for common benefit and include wisdom, knowledge, faith, healing, miracles, prophecy, discernment of spirits, tongues, and interpretation. D&C 46 also uses head-and-member language when explaining the gifts' coordination.

**Does not establish:** The relationship does not establish independent ancient origin, the direction or mechanism of inspiration, or that every term has precisely the same meaning in both chapters.

**Alternative explanations:**
- D&C 46 may be an explicit inspired adaptation of a familiar New Testament catalogue.
- Some overlap could reflect a shared Christian vocabulary of charismatic gifts, although the cumulative sequence and wording make mere coincidence implausible.
