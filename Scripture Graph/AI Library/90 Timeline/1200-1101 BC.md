# 1200-1101 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **1200 BC–1050 BC** — **Danite migration, conquest of Laish, and founding of the city of Dan** — Set in the pre-monarchic 'no king in Israel' era of Judges; the text gives no absolute date, so placement within the broad traditional span of the Judges period is approximate only. — [[Micah]] · [[Jonathan (Levite priest, son of Gershom)]] · [[the five Danite spies]] · [[Laish (renamed Dan)]] · [[Mount Ephraim]] · [[Judges 18]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1200 BC–1125 BC** — **Deborah and Barak's deliverance from Jabin of Hazor** — Placed within the pre-monarchic judges period based on the book's internal cumulative chronology of oppression and rest periods following Ehud; absolute dating is disputed among scholars and not fixed by external synchronism within the text. — [[Deborah]] · [[Barak]] · [[Sisera]] · [[Jael]] · [[Hazor]] · [[River Kishon]] · [[Judges 4]] *(approximate)*
