# AD 301-400 — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Book of Mormon lands
- **AD 321–AD 326** — **Mormon's childhood commission and early societal collapse** — The chapter heading assigns approximately A.D. 321–26; the narrative follows Mormon from about age ten through age fifteen and includes an intervening four-year peace. — [[Mormon]] · [[Ammaron]] · [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[hill Shim]] · [[Mormon 1]] *(approximate)*
- **AD 326** — **Mormon takes command at sixteen** — a boy general in a dying nation — and its historian — [[Mormon]] · [[Mormon 1]] · [[Mormon 2]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 326–AD 350** — **Mormon's early command and Nephite retreat, AD 326-350** — Derived from the chapter's own repeated year-count markers (verses 2, 9, 15, 16, 22, 28), which are internal to the Nephite record rather than externally corroborated. — [[Mormon]] · [[Aaron (Lamanite king)]] · [[Angola]] · [[Shem]] · [[Mormon 2]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 326–AD 350** — **Mormon's early command, record retrieval, and territorial treaty** — The chapter dates Mormon's first campaign to the 326th year and the territorial treaty to the 350th year; these are the narrative's own chronological notices. — [[Mormon]] · [[Ammaron]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Jashon]] · [[Shem]] · [[Mormon 2]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 350–AD 362** — **Nephite stand at Desolation and Mormon's refusal to lead** — Text gives explicit internal year counts: ten years of peace (v1), then 'three hundred and sixty years from the coming of Christ' (v4), with battles in the 361st and 362nd years (vv7-8). — [[Mormon]] · [[king of the Lamanites (unnamed)]] · [[Desolation]] · [[Mormon 3]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 360–AD 362** — **Preparation and battles at Desolation** — Mormon numbers the end of the ten-year interval as year 360 and dates the two battles to years 361 and 362. — [[Mormon]] · [[Nephites]] · [[Lamanites]] · [[Desolation]] · [[Mormon 3]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 363–AD 375** — **Nephite-Lamanite war and final military collapse** — The narrative explicitly marks years 363, 364, 366, 367, and 375 within its own chronological system. — [[Mormon]] · [[Nephites]] · [[Lamanites]] · [[Desolation]] · [[Teancum]] · [[Mormon 4]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 375** — **Mormon retrieves Ammaron's records from Shim** — The retrieval follows the chapter's year-375 campaign and Mormon's recognition that the land was about to be overthrown. — [[Mormon]] · [[Ammaron]] · [[hill Shim]] · [[Mormon 4]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 380–AD 384** — **Mormon abridges a thousand years onto gold plates** — he attaches the small plates whole — the wise purpose revealed — [[Mormon]] · [[Words of Mormon 1]] · [[3 Nephi 5]] · [[Mormon 6]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 384–AD 385** — **Final gathering and destruction at Cumorah** — Mormon 6:5 places the gathering after 384 years had passed; the official chapter heading dates the final destruction to about A.D. 385. — [[Mormon]] · [[Moroni]] · [[king of the Lamanites]] · [[Cumorah]] · [[Mormon 6]] *(approximate)*
- **AD 385** — **The last battle at Cumorah** — a nation ends; a record is buried to speak later — [[Mormon]] · [[Moroni (son of Mormon)]] · [[Cumorah]] · [[Mormon 6]] · [[Mormon 8]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 385–AD 421** — **Moroni wanders alone, finishing the record** — he abridges Ether, seals the book, and buries it in Cumorah — [[Moroni (son of Mormon)]] · [[Mormon 8]] · [[Ether 12]] · [[Moroni 10]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **AD 385** — **Mormon prepares the record for Moroni** — The official chapter heading dates the chapter to about A.D. 385, while verses 1–2 describe Mormon preparing to deliver the records to Moroni. — [[Mormon]] · [[Moroni]] · [[Words of Mormon 1]] *(approximate)*
- **AD 400** — **Moroni records the aftermath of Nephite destruction** — Mormon 8:6 states that four hundred years had passed since the coming of the Lord and Savior. — [[Moroni]] · [[Mormon]] · [[Cumorah]] · [[Mormon 8]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
