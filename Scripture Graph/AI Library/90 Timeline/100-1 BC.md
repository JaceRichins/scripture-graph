# 100-1 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **63 BC** — **Rome takes Judea** — Pompey enters Jerusalem — the stage set for the Gospels — [[Jerusalem]] *(historical)*
- **4 BC** — **The birth of Jesus Christ** — a night without darkness in the New World; a star over Bethlehem in the Old — [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Mary]] · [[Joseph (of Nazareth)]] · [[Bethlehem]] · [[Luke 2]] · [[Matthew 2]] · [[3 Nephi 1]] *(traditional dating)*
