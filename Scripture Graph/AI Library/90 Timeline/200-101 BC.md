# 200-101 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **167 BC–160 BC** — **The Maccabean revolt** — the temple rededicated — Hanukkah — [[Jerusalem]] *(historical)*

## In the Book of Mormon lands
- **200 BC** — **Mosiah discovers Zarahemla** — Nephites and Mulekites become one people — [[Mosiah I]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Omni 1]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **200 BC–187 BC** — **Zeniff's migration, settlement, and first reported war** — The official chapter heading assigns the narrative approximately 200–187 B.C.; internally, Mosiah 9:11 and 9:14 mark twelve years of settlement followed by war in the thirteenth year of Zeniff's reign. — [[Zeniff]] · [[King Laman]] · [[Land of Lehi-Nephi]] · [[Land of Shilom]] · [[Mosiah 9]] *(approximate)*
- **200 BC–160 BC** — **Zeniff's people return to the land of Nephi** — an over-zealous return that ends in bondage — [[Zeniff]] · [[Noah (BoM king)]] · [[Land of Nephi]] · [[Lehi-Nephi]] · [[Mosiah 9]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **160 BC–150 BC** — **Noah's reign and Abinadi's first warning** — The official chapter heading assigns the chapter approximately to 160-150 B.C.; the text itself supplies no absolute year. — [[King Noah]] · [[Abinadi]] · [[Zeniff]] · [[land of Shilom]] · [[land of Shemlon]] · [[Mosiah 11]] *(approximate)*
- **160 BC–124 BC** — **King Benjamin repels Lamanite invasion and restores peace** — Traditional Book of Mormon chronology places Benjamin's reign roughly in this range; Words of Mormon 1:12-18 describes wars and unrest occurring during that reign, prior to his final address recorded in Mosiah 2-6. — [[King Benjamin]] · [[Land of Nephi]] · [[Words of Mormon 1]] *(approximate)*
- **148 BC** — **Abinadi before King Noah's court** — one convert in the audience changes everything — [[Abinadi]] · [[Noah (BoM king)]] · [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Lehi-Nephi]] · [[Mosiah 12]] · [[Mosiah 13]] · [[Mosiah 17]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **148 BC** — **Abinadi executed and Alma preserves his words** — The official chapter heading places these events at approximately 148 B.C.; the chapter itself supplies no absolute date. — [[Abinadi]] · [[Alma]] · [[King Noah]] · [[Mosiah 17]] *(approximate)*
- **147 BC–145 BC** — **Alma baptizes at the waters of Mormon** — the church of Christ organized in the wilderness — [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Waters of Mormon]] · [[Mosiah 18]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **145 BC–121 BC** — **Alma's people in bondage; burdens made light** — the Lord strengthens their backs before opening the way — [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Amulon]] · [[Helam]] · [[Mosiah 23]] · [[Mosiah 24]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **130 BC** — **Amaleki gives the small plates to King Benjamin** — both record lines now travel together — [[Amaleki]] · [[King Benjamin]] · [[Omni 1]] · [[Words of Mormon 1]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **124 BC** — **King Benjamin's address from the tower** — a whole people takes Christ's name — [[King Benjamin]] · [[Mosiah II]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 2]] · [[Mosiah 3]] · [[Mosiah 4]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **124 BC** — **Benjamin addresses the people and announces Mosiah's reign** — The official chapter heading dates the address to about 124 B.C.; the chapter itself narrates Benjamin's announcement that Mosiah is king. — [[King Benjamin]] · [[Mosiah]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 2]] *(approximate)*
- **124 BC** — **King Benjamin teaches the converted multitude** — The official chapter heading places King Benjamin's continuing address at about 124 B.C. — [[King Benjamin]] · [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Mosiah 4]] *(approximate)*
- **124 BC** — **Benjamin's people enter a covenant** — The supplied official chapter heading dates the episode to about 124 B.C. — [[King Benjamin]] · [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Mosiah 5]] *(approximate)*
- **122 BC** — **Limhi's men find twenty-four gold plates** — a destroyed nation's story, waiting in the ruins — [[Limhi]] · [[Ammon (of Zarahemla)]] · [[Mosiah 8]] · [[Mosiah 21]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **121 BC** — **Limhi's people escape to Zarahemla** — the colony comes home — the storylines rejoin — [[Limhi]] · [[Ammon (of Zarahemla)]] · [[Gideon (BoM)]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 22]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **121 BC** — **Ammon reaches Limhi and announces contact with Zarahemla** — The official chapter heading dates the expedition, recognition scene, and Limhi's assembly to about 121 B.C. — [[Ammon]] · [[Limhi]] · [[Zeniff]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Land of Nephi]] · [[Mosiah 7]] *(approximate)*
- **120 BC** — **Alma's people delivered to Zarahemla** — all the people of Zarahemla hear all the stories at last — [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Mosiah II]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 24]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **120 BC** — **Reunification and church organization at Zarahemla** — The official chapter heading supplied with the text dates the events about 120 B.C.; the verses themselves do not state a year. — [[Mosiah]] · [[Alma]] · [[Limhi]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 25]] *(approximate)*
- **120 BC–100 BC** — **Alma establishes church discipline under Mosiah** — The official chapter heading assigns the narrated events broadly to about 120–100 B.C.; the chapter itself only locates them in Mosiah's reign. — [[Alma]] · [[King Mosiah]] · [[Abinadi]] · [[Land of Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 26]] *(approximate)*
