# 200-101 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **167 BC–160 BC** — **The Maccabean revolt** — the temple rededicated — Hanukkah — [[Jerusalem]] *(historical)*

## In the Book of Mormon lands
- **200 BC** — **Mosiah discovers Zarahemla** — Nephites and Mulekites become one people — [[Mosiah I]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Omni 1]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **200 BC–160 BC** — **Zeniff's people return to the land of Nephi** — an over-zealous return that ends in bondage — [[Zeniff]] · [[Noah (BoM king)]] · [[Land of Nephi]] · [[Lehi-Nephi]] · [[Mosiah 9]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **148 BC** — **Abinadi before King Noah's court** — one convert in the audience changes everything — [[Abinadi]] · [[Noah (BoM king)]] · [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Lehi-Nephi]] · [[Mosiah 12]] · [[Mosiah 13]] · [[Mosiah 17]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **147 BC–145 BC** — **Alma baptizes at the waters of Mormon** — the church of Christ organized in the wilderness — [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Waters of Mormon]] · [[Mosiah 18]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **145 BC–121 BC** — **Alma's people in bondage; burdens made light** — the Lord strengthens their backs before opening the way — [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Amulon]] · [[Helam]] · [[Mosiah 23]] · [[Mosiah 24]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **130 BC** — **Amaleki gives the small plates to King Benjamin** — both record lines now travel together — [[Amaleki]] · [[King Benjamin]] · [[Omni 1]] · [[Words of Mormon 1]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **124 BC** — **King Benjamin's address from the tower** — a whole people takes Christ's name — [[King Benjamin]] · [[Mosiah II]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 2]] · [[Mosiah 3]] · [[Mosiah 4]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **122 BC** — **Limhi's men find twenty-four gold plates** — a destroyed nation's story, waiting in the ruins — [[Limhi]] · [[Ammon (of Zarahemla)]] · [[Mosiah 8]] · [[Mosiah 21]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **121 BC** — **Limhi's people escape to Zarahemla** — the colony comes home — the storylines rejoin — [[Limhi]] · [[Ammon (of Zarahemla)]] · [[Gideon (BoM)]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 22]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
- **120 BC** — **Alma's people delivered to Zarahemla** — all the people of Zarahemla hear all the stories at last — [[Alma the Elder]] · [[Mosiah II]] · [[Zarahemla]] · [[Mosiah 24]] *(Book of Mormon internal dating)*
