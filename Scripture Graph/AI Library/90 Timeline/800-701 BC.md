# 800-701 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **796 BC–767 BC** — **Reign of Amaziah of Judah** — Conventional biblical chronology places Amaziah's 29-year reign in the early-to-mid 8th century BC, following his father Joash of Judah; exact dates vary by chronological scheme. — [[Amaziah]] · [[Joash (king of Israel)]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Chronicles 25]] *(traditional dating)*
- **760 BC–722 BC** — **Amos and Hosea warn the north** — justice like waters; love that will not let go — [[Amos]] · [[Hosea]] · [[Samaria]] · [[Amos 5]] · [[Hosea 11]] *(approximate)*
- **760 BC–750 BC** — **Amos's ministry under Jeroboam II, before the Uzziah earthquake** — Amos 1:1 dates the words to the overlapping reigns of Uzziah (Judah) and Jeroboam II (Israel), 'two years before the earthquake'; conventional scholarship places this in the decade before ca. 750 BC, though the exact year of the earthquake is not independently fixed by the text. — [[Amos]] · [[Uzziah]] · [[Jeroboam II]] · [[Tekoa]] · [[Amos 1]] *(approximate)*
- **760 BC–750 BC** — **Amos's prophetic ministry to the Northern Kingdom** — Conventionally dated to the reigns of Jeroboam II of Israel and Uzziah of Judah, per the book's superscription, roughly a generation before Israel's fall to Assyria in 722 BCE. — [[Amos]] · [[Israel (Northern Kingdom)]] · [[Amos 5]] *(traditional dating)*
- **750 BC–734 BC** — **Reign of Jotham of Judah** — Sixteen-year reign length given in 2 Chronicles 27:1, 8; absolute dates depend on disputed co-regency reconstructions with Uzziah and are not fixed by the text itself. — [[Jotham]] · [[Uzziah]] · [[Ahaz]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Chronicles 27]] *(approximate)*
- **740 BC–690 BC** — **Isaiah's ministry in Jerusalem** — the prophet Nephi quotes most — [[Isaiah]] · [[Hezekiah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Isaiah 6]] · [[Isaiah 53]] · [[2 Kings 19]] *(approximate)*
- **735 BC–715 BC** — **Reign of Ahaz king of Judah** — Reconstructed from regnal synchronisms with Pekah of Israel (2 Kings 16:1) and cross-checked against Assyrian tribute records; dates vary by roughly a decade across scholarly reconstructions due to possible co-regencies. — [[Ahaz]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Kings 16]] *(approximate)*
- **732 BC** — **Assyrian conquest of Damascus and death of Rezin** — Dated from Tiglath-pileser III's Assyrian royal annals recording the fall of Damascus, a date widely treated as secure and used to corroborate 2 Kings 16:9. — [[Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria]] · [[Rezin]] · [[Damascus]] · [[2 Kings 16]] *(historical)*
- **722 BC** — **Assyria destroys northern Israel** — the ten tribes scattered — the 'lost tribes' — [[Samaria]] · [[Assyria]] · [[2 Kings 17]] *(historical)*
- **722 BC** — **Assyrian conquest of Samaria (background referent of Ezek. 23:9-10)** — Widely dated Assyrian conquest of the northern kingdom's capital, treated in 2 Kings 17; Ezekiel 23:9-10 describes Aholah/Samaria being delivered to Assyrian lovers who slay her sons and daughters, matching this event's memory. — [[Assyrian conquerors]] · [[Samaria]] · [[Ezekiel 23]] *(historical)*
- **715 BC–686 BC** — **Hezekiah's reign and first-month temple reform** — Approximate range commonly cited from synchronistic reconstructions (e.g., Thiele-type chronologies) using Assyrian-period synchronisms; Hezekiah's exact accession date and any co-regency with Ahaz remain disputed among chronologists. — [[Hezekiah]] · [[David (king)]] · [[Aaron (brother of Moses)]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Chronicles 29]] *(historical)*
- **703 BC–700 BC** — **Hezekiah's illness, sign, and the Babylonian embassy** — Placed within Hezekiah's reign (commonly reconstructed c. 715-687 BC), correlated with the historically attested reigns of Merodach-baladan II over Babylon; exact placement of the illness within Hezekiah's 29-year reign relative to Sennacherib's 701 BC campaign is debated. — [[Hezekiah]] · [[Isaiah]] · [[Berodach-baladan]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Babylon]] · [[2 Kings 20]] *(approximate)*
- **701 BC** — **Jerusalem spared from Sennacherib** — the angel and the Assyrian camp — [[Hezekiah]] · [[Isaiah]] · [[Sennacherib]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Kings 19]] · [[Isaiah 37]] *(historical)*
