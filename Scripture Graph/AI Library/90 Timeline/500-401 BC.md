# 500-401 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **483 BC** — **Ahasuerus (Xerxes I) hosts his third-year feast at Susa** — Based on the widely accepted identification of Ahasuerus with Xerxes I of Persia (reigned 486-465 BC, per Achaemenid chronology); 'the third year of his reign' (Esther 1:3) places the feast circa 483 BC. — [[Ahasuerus (Xerxes I)]] · [[Vashti]] · [[Shushan (Susa)]] · [[Esther 1]] *(historical)*
- **474 BC** — **Esther saves her people** — for such a time as this — [[Esther]] · [[Mordecai]] · [[Haman]] · [[Susa]] · [[Esther 4]] *(approximate)*
- **458 BC–445 BC** — **Ezra reads the law; Nehemiah builds the wall** — a people rebuilt around a book — [[Ezra]] · [[Nehemiah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Ezra 7]] · [[Nehemiah 4]] · [[Nehemiah 8]] *(historical)*
- **458 BC** — **Ezra's Commission and Journey to Jerusalem** — Reckoned as the 7th year of Artaxerxes I (465-424 BC), the traditional/majority identification (Ezra 7:7-9), yielding 458 BC; a minority scholarly view identifies the king as Artaxerxes II, yielding 398 BC and reversing the traditional Ezra-before-Nehemiah order. — [[Ezra]] · [[Artaxerxes]] · [[Babylon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Ezra 7]] *(traditional dating)*
- **458 BC–398 BC** — **Ezra's caravan departs Babylon for Jerusalem** — Ezra 8:1 dates events to 'the reign of Artaxerxes the king' without a regnal year in this chapter; scholars dispute whether this is Artaxerxes I (c. 458 BCE) or Artaxerxes II (c. 398 BCE), so the range spans both proposals. — [[Ezra]] · [[Artaxerxes]] · [[Babylon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Ezra 8]] *(approximate)*
- **445 BC–433 BC** — **Covenant renewal and sealing under Nehemiah** — Placed within Nehemiah's first governorship of Yehud, conventionally dated from Artaxerxes I's 20th regnal year (Nehemiah 2:1) through his recall (Nehemiah 13:6); exact year of the sealing itself is not stated in the text. — [[Nehemiah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Nehemiah 10]] *(approximate)*
- **430 BC** — **Malachi — the Old Testament closes** — Elijah promised before the great and dreadful day — [[Malachi]] · [[Malachi 3]] · [[Malachi 4]] *(approximate)*
