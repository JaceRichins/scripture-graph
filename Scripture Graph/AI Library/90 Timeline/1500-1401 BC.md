# 1500-1401 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **1491 BC** — **Sinai covenant ceremony and Moses's ascent** — Traditional biblical chronology places the Exodus and the subsequent Sinai covenant in the same year the Israelites left Egypt, roughly three months after departure (cf. Exodus 19:1); exact year is disputed among chronological schemes (commonly given as either c. 1491 BC or c. 1446 BC). — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Joshua]] · [[Hur]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Exodus 24]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1450 BC–1449 BC** — **Twelve spies sent from Paran to scout Canaan** — Traditional conservative chronology places the Exodus c. 1446 BC; this episode falls in the second year of the wilderness journey, at Kadesh-Barnea, shortly before the 38-40 years of wandering that followed the people's unbelief. — [[Moses]] · [[Caleb]] · [[Joshua (Oshea)]] · [[Canaan]] · [[Hebron]] · [[Numbers 13]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC** — **The burning bush at Horeb** — I AM sends a shepherd back to Pharaoh — [[Moses]] · [[Horeb]] · [[Sinai]] · [[Exodus 3]] · [[Moses 1]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC** — **The Exodus from Egypt; the Red Sea** — traditional dating; some scholars place it ~1290 BC — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Red Sea]] · [[Exodus 12]] · [[Exodus 14]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC–1406 BC** — **Exodus and wilderness rebellions recalled in Psalm 106** — Traditional biblical chronology for the Exodus and forty-year wilderness period covering the events recounted in vv.7-33 (Red Sea, golden calf, Baal-peor, Meribah). — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Phinehas]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Canaan]] · [[Psalm 106]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC–1445 BC** — **The law given at Sinai** — the covenant written in stone — [[Moses]] · [[Sinai]] · [[Exodus 19]] · [[Exodus 20]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC–1444 BC** — **Aaron's Inaugural Sacrifices at the Wilderness Tabernacle** — Traditional biblical chronology places the Exodus and the subsequent Sinai tabernacle legislation, including Aaron's ordination (Leviticus 8) and inaugural sacrifices (Leviticus 9), in the mid-15th century BC under an early-Exodus dating scheme. — [[Aaron]] · [[Moses]] · [[Leviticus 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC–1444 BC** — **Consecration of the Levites for tabernacle service** — Numbers situates this consecration in the wilderness period shortly after the tabernacle's dedication (Numbers 7) and before the second Passover (Numbers 9:1-5); the absolute year depends on the traditional c. 1446 BC Exodus date, which is itself debated among scholars. — [[Aaron (brother of Moses)]] · [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Numbers 8]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC** — **The tabernacle raised** — a portable holy place for a moving people — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Sinai]] · [[Exodus 40]] · [[Leviticus 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC–1406 BC** — **Forty years in the wilderness** — a generation schooled by manna — [[Moses]] · [[Numbers 14]] · [[Deuteronomy 8]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1407 BC–1400 BC** — **Moses' Transjordan conquest and land grant to Reuben, Gad, half-Manasseh** — Within a traditional biblical chronology (Exodus c. 1446 BC), the defeat of Sihon and Og and the subsequent Transjordan land grant occurred near the end of the wilderness period, shortly before Moses' death and the conquest under Joshua. — [[Moses]] · [[Sihon]] · [[Og]] · [[Heshbon]] · [[Bashan]] · [[Joshua 13]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Jericho falls** — walls down at a shout — [[Joshua]] · [[Rahab]] · [[Jericho]] · [[Joshua 6]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Israel crosses the Jordan** — the waters stand; twelve stones remember — [[Joshua]] · [[Jordan River]] · [[Gilgal]] · [[Joshua 3]] · [[Joshua 4]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Moses delivers the Deuteronomic law on the plains of Moab** — Traditional biblical chronology places Moses' concluding addresses, including the refuge-city and witness laws, shortly before Israel's entry into Canaan; source-critical scholarship instead associates much of Deuteronomy's core with Josiah's 7th-century BCE reform. — [[Moses]] · [[Plains of Moab]] · [[Deuteronomy 19]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC–1400 BC** — **Command to renew the covenant at Gerizim and Ebal** — Traditional biblical chronology (based on 1 Kings 6:1) places Israel's entry into Canaan, and this anticipatory covenant-renewal command, near 1406 BC; the date is disputed among scholars who favor a later Exodus/conquest chronology. — [[Moses]] · [[Mount Ebal]] · [[Mount Gerizim]] · [[Deuteronomy 27]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Baal-peor apostasy recalled in Hosea 9:10** — Numbers 25 places Israel's apostasy with Baal-peor near the end of the wilderness period, traditionally dated to the close of the 40-year wandering before the conquest. — [[Israel]] · [[Baal-peor]] · [[Hosea 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC–1400 BC** — **Conquest and destruction of Jericho under Joshua** — Traditional dating derived from 1 Kings 6:1 (480 years before Solomon's 4th year); a later 'late-date' conquest (~1230-1200 BC) is also argued, and Jericho's archaeology remains disputed. — [[Joshua (son of Nun)]] · [[Rahab]] · [[Jericho]] · [[Joshua 6]] *(traditional dating)*
