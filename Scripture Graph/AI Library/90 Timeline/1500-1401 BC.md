# 1500-1401 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **1491 BC–1445 BC** — **Giving of the Ten Commandments at Sinai** — Traditional Latter-day Saint and Ussher-style chronologies place the Exodus and the subsequent giving of the law at Sinai (about three months later, per Exodus 19:1) within the 15th century BC; exact year is disputed among chronologies. — [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Exodus 20]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1491 BC** — **Sinai covenant ceremony and Moses's ascent** — Traditional biblical chronology places the Exodus and the subsequent Sinai covenant in the same year the Israelites left Egypt, roughly three months after departure (cf. Exodus 19:1); exact year is disputed among chronological schemes (commonly given as either c. 1491 BC or c. 1446 BC). — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Joshua]] · [[Hur]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Exodus 24]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1491 BC–1451 BC** — **Sinai lawgiving and tabernacle instructions** — LDS Bible Dictionary places Sinai lawgiving here; critical scholarship dates Exodus tradition variously (e.g., c. 1250 BC); some question a unified historical event. — [[Aaron]] · [[Moses]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Exodus 28]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1491 BC–1490 BC** — **Sinai covenant renewal after the golden calf** — Traditional LDS Bible chronology (following Ussher-style reckoning used in LDS Bible Dictionary conventions) places the Exodus and the Sinai covenant events around 1491 BC; internal biblical chronology and other scholarly datings vary by a century or more. — [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Joshua (son of Nun)]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Exodus 33]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1491 BC** — **Second tablets and covenant renewal at Mount Sinai** — Placed using the traditional LDS Bible Dictionary chronology for the Exodus (c. 1491 BC); occurs within the same encampment period at Sinai shortly after the golden-calf incident of Exodus 32. — [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Aaron (brother of Moses)]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Exodus 34]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1451 BC** — **Moses blesses the tribes of Israel before his death** — Traditional Bible-chronology dating places Moses' death, and this preceding blessing, roughly 40 years after a traditional Exodus date of c. 1491 BC; the Exodus date itself is disputed among scholars. — [[Moses]] · [[Joseph]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Deuteronomy 33]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1450 BC–1449 BC** — **Twelve spies sent from Paran to scout Canaan** — Traditional conservative chronology places the Exodus c. 1446 BC; this episode falls in the second year of the wilderness journey, at Kadesh-Barnea, shortly before the 38-40 years of wandering that followed the people's unbelief. — [[Moses]] · [[Caleb]] · [[Joshua (Oshea)]] · [[Canaan]] · [[Hebron]] · [[Numbers 13]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC** — **The burning bush at Horeb** — I AM sends a shepherd back to Pharaoh — [[Moses]] · [[Horeb]] · [[Sinai]] · [[Exodus 3]] · [[Moses 1]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC** — **The Exodus from Egypt; the Red Sea** — traditional dating; some scholars place it ~1290 BC — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Red Sea]] · [[Exodus 12]] · [[Exodus 14]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC–1350 BC** — **Caleb's line: Bezaleel to Judahite town-founding** — Bezaleel (1 Chronicles 2:20) is traditionally dated to the tabernacle's construction shortly after the Exodus; his kin later settle towns named in 2:50-55 during the conquest/judges period. — [[Caleb]] · [[Bezaleel]] · [[Hebron]] · [[Kirjath-jearim]] · [[1 Chronicles 2]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC–1445 BC** — **Israel's departure from Egypt and early wilderness journey** — Traditional biblical chronology (via 1 Kings 6:1) places the Exodus c. 1446 BC; many historians instead favor a 13th-century BC (Ramesside) date, and the event's historicity and precise date remain debated among scholars. — [[Moses]] · [[Joseph (of Egypt)]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Canaan]] · [[Exodus 13]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC–1250 BC** — **Israel's arrival at Sinai and the covenant theophany** — Occurs in the third month after the Exodus (19:1); absolute dating of the Exodus itself is disputed between an early date (~1446 BC, based on 1 Kings 6:1) and a late/Ramesside date (~13th century BC), so the range spans both scholarly positions rather than asserting one. — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Exodus 19]] *(approximate)*
- **1446 BC–1406 BC** — **Israel receives tabernacle and Sabbath instructions at Sinai** — Traditional biblical chronology (cf. 1 Kings 6:1) places the Sinai revelation shortly after the Exodus; scholars are divided between an early (~15th century BC) and late (~13th century BC) Exodus date. — [[Moses]] · [[Bezaleel]] · [[Aholiab]] · [[Mount Sinai]] · [[Exodus 31]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC–1406 BC** — **Exodus and wilderness rebellions recalled in Psalm 106** — Traditional biblical chronology for the Exodus and forty-year wilderness period covering the events recounted in vv.7-33 (Red Sea, golden calf, Baal-peor, Meribah). — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Phinehas]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Canaan]] · [[Psalm 106]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1446 BC–1445 BC** — **The law given at Sinai** — the covenant written in stone — [[Moses]] · [[Sinai]] · [[Exodus 19]] · [[Exodus 20]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC–1444 BC** — **Aaron's Inaugural Sacrifices at the Wilderness Tabernacle** — Traditional biblical chronology places the Exodus and the subsequent Sinai tabernacle legislation, including Aaron's ordination (Leviticus 8) and inaugural sacrifices (Leviticus 9), in the mid-15th century BC under an early-Exodus dating scheme. — [[Aaron]] · [[Moses]] · [[Leviticus 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC–1444 BC** — **Consecration of the Levites for tabernacle service** — Numbers situates this consecration in the wilderness period shortly after the tabernacle's dedication (Numbers 7) and before the second Passover (Numbers 9:1-5); the absolute year depends on the traditional c. 1446 BC Exodus date, which is itself debated among scholars. — [[Aaron (brother of Moses)]] · [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Numbers 8]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC** — **The tabernacle raised** — a portable holy place for a moving people — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Sinai]] · [[Exodus 40]] · [[Leviticus 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1445 BC–1406 BC** — **Forty years in the wilderness** — a generation schooled by manna — [[Moses]] · [[Numbers 14]] · [[Deuteronomy 8]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1410 BC–1400 BC** — **Moses delivers the Song of Moses on the plains of Moab** — Placed near the end of the 40-year wilderness period, shortly before Moses' death and Israel's crossing of the Jordan; exact date depends on disputed early vs. late Exodus chronology. — [[Moses]] · [[Aaron]] · [[Hoshea/Joshua]] · [[Moab]] · [[Mount Nebo]] · [[Deuteronomy 32]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1407 BC–1400 BC** — **Moses' Transjordan conquest and land grant to Reuben, Gad, half-Manasseh** — Within a traditional biblical chronology (Exodus c. 1446 BC), the defeat of Sihon and Og and the subsequent Transjordan land grant occurred near the end of the wilderness period, shortly before Moses' death and the conquest under Joshua. — [[Moses]] · [[Sihon]] · [[Og]] · [[Heshbon]] · [[Bashan]] · [[Joshua 13]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Jericho falls** — walls down at a shout — [[Joshua]] · [[Rahab]] · [[Jericho]] · [[Joshua 6]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Israel crosses the Jordan** — the waters stand; twelve stones remember — [[Joshua]] · [[Jordan River]] · [[Gilgal]] · [[Joshua 3]] · [[Joshua 4]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Moses's Farewell Discourse in the Plains of Moab** — Reckoned as the fortieth year after the Exodus per the internal date notice in Deuteronomy 1:3, shortly before Moses's death and Israel's crossing of the Jordan. — [[Moses]] · [[Moab]] · [[Deuteronomy 1]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Moses' covenant exhortation on the plains of Moab** — Traditional dating places this address shortly before Israel's crossing of the Jordan, roughly 40 years after a traditional Exodus date of c.1446 BC. — [[Moses]] · [[Moab]] · [[Deuteronomy 11]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Deuteronomy 15 sabbatical release laws given** — Narrative setting is Moses' address on the plains of Moab in the fortieth year after the Exodus, shortly before Israel entered Canaan (cf. Deuteronomy 1:3); scholars dispute the actual composition date of the legal core. — [[Egypt]] · [[Deuteronomy 15]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Moses delivers the Deuteronomic law on the plains of Moab** — Traditional biblical chronology places Moses' concluding addresses, including the refuge-city and witness laws, shortly before Israel's entry into Canaan; source-critical scholarship instead associates much of Deuteronomy's core with Josiah's 7th-century BCE reform. — [[Moses]] · [[Plains of Moab]] · [[Deuteronomy 19]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC–1400 BC** — **Command to renew the covenant at Gerizim and Ebal** — Traditional biblical chronology (based on 1 Kings 6:1) places Israel's entry into Canaan, and this anticipatory covenant-renewal command, near 1406 BC; the date is disputed among scholars who favor a later Exodus/conquest chronology. — [[Moses]] · [[Mount Ebal]] · [[Mount Gerizim]] · [[Deuteronomy 27]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Moses' covenant-renewal address on the plains of Moab** — Traditional biblical chronology places Moses' closing addresses shortly before Israel's crossing of the Jordan into Canaan, conventionally dated near 1406 BCE; the date is disputed among historians and depends on one's view of Exodus chronology. — [[Moses]] · [[Abraham]] · [[Isaac]] · [[Jordan River]] · [[Deuteronomy 30]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC** — **Baal-peor apostasy recalled in Hosea 9:10** — Numbers 25 places Israel's apostasy with Baal-peor near the end of the wilderness period, traditionally dated to the close of the 40-year wandering before the conquest. — [[Israel]] · [[Baal-peor]] · [[Hosea 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1406 BC–1400 BC** — **Conquest and destruction of Jericho under Joshua** — Traditional dating derived from 1 Kings 6:1 (480 years before Solomon's 4th year); a later 'late-date' conquest (~1230-1200 BC) is also argued, and Jericho's archaeology remains disputed. — [[Joshua (son of Nun)]] · [[Rahab]] · [[Jericho]] · [[Joshua 6]] *(traditional dating)*
