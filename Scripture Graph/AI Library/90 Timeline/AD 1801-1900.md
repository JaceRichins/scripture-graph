# AD 1801-1900 — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## The Restoration
- **AD 1820** — **The First Vision** — a boy's spring-morning prayer opens the dispensation — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sacred Grove]] · [[Palmyra]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1823** — **Moroni appears; the plates shown** — the buried record's author returns for it — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Moroni (son of Mormon)]] · [[Cumorah]] · [[Palmyra]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1827** — **Joseph receives the plates** — four years of schooling first — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Emma Smith]] · [[Cumorah]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1829** — **Priesthood restored by John the Baptist** — authority returns hand to head — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[John the Baptist]] · [[Susquehanna River]] · [[D&C 13]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1829** — **The translation by gift and power** — most of the book in about sixty-five working days — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Harmony]] · [[Fayette]] · [[D&C 3]] · [[D&C 10]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1829** — **The Three and the Eight see the plates** — an angel turns the leaves; eleven names sign forever — [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[David Whitmer]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Fayette]] · [[D&C 17]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **The Book of Mormon published; the Church organized** — Moroni's record speaks from the dust, 1,400 years on — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Palmyra]] · [[Fayette]] · [[D&C 20]] · [[D&C 21]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **Organization of the Church of Christ, April 6, 1830** — D&C 20:1 gives the explicit date: fourth month, sixth day, "one thousand eight hundred and thirty years" since Christ's coming. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[D&C 20]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **Revelation calling five early disciples to preach and exhort** — The section heading and Church historical records date this revelation to April 1830 at Fayette, New York, near the time of the Church's organization; this dating is not stated within the verses themselves. — [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Hyrum Smith]] · [[Samuel Smith]] · [[Joseph Smith Sr.]] · [[Fayette, New York]] · [[D&C 23]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **D&C 28 given, addressing Hiram Page revelations** — Dated September 1830 in the section heading and Church historical record, given near Fayette, New York, in connection with a conference addressing Hiram Page's claimed stone revelations. — [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Hiram Page]] · [[D&C 28]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **D&C 29 received: revelation on gathering, judgment** — Received through Joseph Smith at Fayette, New York, in September 1830, in the presence of six elders of the Church, per the section heading. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Fayette, New York]] · [[D&C 29]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **D&C 35 given to Joseph Smith and Sidney Rigdon** — Church history places this revelation in December 1830 in New York, shortly after Sidney Rigdon and Edward Partridge arrived from Ohio to meet Joseph Smith. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[New York]] · [[D&C 35]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Revelation on gathering to Kirtland, Ohio (D&C 38)** — Traditionally dated to the church conference at Fayette, New York, 2 January 1831, per the section's historical heading; commands the move to Ohio realized within weeks. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Fayette, New York]] · [[Ohio]] · [[D&C 38]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Revelation to James Covel given at Fayette, New York** — Section heading and historical record date this revelation to January 5, 1831, shortly before the Church's move to Kirtland, Ohio. — [[Jesus Christ]] · [[James Covel]] · [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Fayette, New York]] · [[Ohio]] · [[D&C 39]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **June 1831 conference and dispatch of elders to Missouri** — Traditional dating of D&C 52 to June 7, 1831, at the close of a conference in Ohio; the text itself refers forward to 'the next conference, which shall be held in Missouri' (v.2). — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[Edward Partridge]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Missouri]] · [[Ohio]] · [[D&C 52]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Colesville Saints directed from Thompson, Ohio to Missouri** — The revelation follows the collapse of a stewardship agreement on Leman Copley's land in Thompson, Ohio, and redirects the Colesville branch to journey west to Missouri; documented in the historical introduction to this revelation in the Joseph Smith Papers, Documents, Volume 1. — [[Newel Knight]] · [[Leman Copley]] · [[Missouri]] · [[D&C 54]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Revocation of Missouri travel assignments, Kirtland area** — The section heading and surrounding manuscript context place this revelation in September 1831, amid the early Missouri-gathering assignments given to Colesville-area Saints. — [[Thomas B. Marsh]] · [[Ezra Thayre]] · [[Selah J. Griffin]] · [[Newel Knight]] · [[Missouri]] · [[Thompson, Ohio]] · [[D&C 56]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Revelation given at Independence, Jackson County, Missouri** — Section heading and historical record date this revelation to July 20, 1831, given through Joseph Smith shortly after his arrival in Missouri. — [[Sidney Gilbert]] · [[Edward Partridge]] · [[William W. Phelps]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Independence (Missouri)]] · [[D&C 57]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Elders' return journey from Zion via St. Louis and Cincinnati** — Section dated August 8, 1831, at the land of Zion (Jackson County, Missouri), directing elders who had traveled there for the dedication of Zion to return to Ohio congregations. — [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Edward Partridge]] · [[St. Louis]] · [[Cincinnati]] · [[D&C 60]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Elders' return journey from Zion to Kirtland (Aug. 1831)** — The section heading dates this revelation to August 13, 1831, given during Joseph Smith's return journey from the dedication of Zion in Jackson County, Missouri, back toward Kirtland, Ohio. — [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Missouri]] · [[Kirtland, Ohio]] · [[D&C 62]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **D&C 63 given at Kirtland, Ohio** — Widely documented as received August 1, 1831, shortly after Joseph Smith's return from the first journey to Missouri; not stated within the verse text itself. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[Kirtland]] · [[D&C 63]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **D&C 64 given to Joseph Smith at Kirtland, Ohio** — Traditionally dated September 11, 1831, per the section's historical heading, during the early Kirtland/Zion consecration period. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Ezra Booth]] · [[Isaac Morley]] · [[Edward Partridge]] · [[Kirtland]] · [[D&C 64]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **D&C 65 received at Hiram, Ohio** — Section heading attributes this revelation to Joseph Smith at Hiram, Ohio, October 1831, during the period of Bible translation work there. — [[Hiram, Ohio]] · [[D&C 65]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **D&C 68 given at a conference of elders, Hiram, Ohio** — The section heading identifies this as a revelation given through Joseph Smith at a conference of high priests, in response to questions about the missionary calling of Orson Hyde and others, in November 1831. — [[Orson Hyde]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Zion]] · [[D&C 68]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **D&C 70: stewards appointed over the revelations** — Section heading dates this revelation to November 12, 1831, received through Joseph Smith at Hiram, Ohio. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Hiram, Ohio]] · [[D&C 70]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **Newel K. Whitney called and ordained bishop, Kirtland** — The chapter's official section heading and Church historical records date this revelation to Kirtland, Ohio, December 1831, coinciding with Whitney's ordination as bishop over the Church in Kirtland. — [[Newel K. Whitney]] · [[Kirtland, Ohio]] · [[D&C 72]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1832** — **Revelation concerning a counselor in the presidency** — The revelation manuscript is dated 15 March 1832; its surviving form addresses Jesse Gause, while the present canonical text names Frederick G. Williams. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Jesse Gause]] · [[Frederick G. Williams]] · [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Hiram, Ohio]] · [[D&C 81]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1832** — **Letter to W. W. Phelps on Zion's records (extract, D&C 85)** — Section heading dates this extract to a letter of Joseph Smith to William W. Phelps, Kirtland, Ohio, November 27, 1832, addressing record-keeping for the Church in Zion (Jackson County, Missouri); this dating is paratextual (from the heading) rather than drawn from the verse text itself. — [[Ezra (scribe, cited as precedent)]] · [[Zion]] · [[D&C 85]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1832–AD 1833** — **South Carolina Nullification Crisis** — South Carolina declared federal tariffs null within its borders and threatened armed resistance; the crisis was defused by the Force Bill and a compromise tariff in early 1833. — [[South Carolina]] · [[D&C 87]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1832–AD 1833** — **D&C 88 ('Olive Leaf') received at Kirtland, Ohio** — Per the section heading, verses 1-126 were received December 27-28, 1832, and verses 127-141 were added January 3, 1833, both at Kirtland, Ohio, during meetings connected to the School of the Prophets. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Kirtland, Ohio]] · [[D&C 88]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1833** — **D&C 90 received at Kirtland concerning First Presidency keys** — Section heading and internal content place this revelation in early 1833, addressing Joseph Smith, Sidney Rigdon, and Frederick G. Williams together as presiding officers. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[Frederick G. Williams]] · [[Zion]] · [[D&C 90]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1833** — **Revelation on Kirtland city-of-the-stake building lots** — Traditionally dated to 2 August 1833 in Kirtland, Ohio, per the section's historical heading in the Doctrine and Covenants, during the early Kirtland building and consecration period. — [[Hyrum Smith]] · [[Reynolds Cahoon]] · [[Jared Carter]] · [[Kirtland]] · [[D&C 94]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1833** — **D&C 97 given at Kirtland: school in Zion and temple commanded** — The section heading dates this revelation to August 2, 1833, given through Joseph Smith at Kirtland, Ohio. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Parley P. Pratt]] · [[Kirtland, Ohio]] · [[D&C 97]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1833** — **Mob destroys Independence press, tars Edward Partridge** — Church histories date the destruction of the Evening and Morning Star printing office and the tarring and feathering of Bishop Edward Partridge and Charles Allen to July 20, 1833, in Independence, Missouri. — [[Edward Partridge]] · [[Independence, Missouri]] · [[D&C 97]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1833** — **D&C 98 given at Kirtland amid Missouri persecutions** — Section heading identifies the revelation as received at Kirtland, Ohio, August 6, 1833, during the period of escalating mob violence against Latter-day Saints in Jackson County, Missouri. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Kirtland]] · [[D&C 98]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1834** — **Organization of the Church's first standing high council** — The minutes describe the organizing council as occurring 'this day' (v. 1); 1834 reflects the conventional Church-historical dating of this council, though the exact day is not stated in the verses themselves. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[Frederick G. Williams]] · [[D&C 102]] *(approximate)*
- **AD 1834** — **Fishing River Revelation defers Zion's redemption** — Traditionally dated June 22, 1834, near Fishing River, Clay County, Missouri, after Zion's Camp leaders determined not to force a crossing to redeem Jackson County following negotiations and rising tension with Missouri settlers. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Fishing River, Missouri]] · [[D&C 105]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1835** — **Declaration on governments and laws adopted at Kirtland** — Per the section's traditional heading, the declaration was adopted by unanimous vote of a general assembly of the Church at Kirtland, Ohio, in August 1835, for inclusion in that year's edition of the Doctrine and Covenants. — [[Kirtland, Ohio]] · [[D&C 134]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1836** — **The Kirtland Temple; keys restored** — Moses, Elias, and Elijah — Malachi's promise kept — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Elijah]] · [[Moses]] · [[Kirtland]] · [[D&C 110]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1836** — **Kirtland Temple dedication-week vision (D&C 110)** — Traditionally dated April 3, 1836, about a week after the Kirtland Temple's March 27, 1836 dedication, per Church historical records; the date is not stated within the verses of D&C 110 itself. — [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Elijah]] · [[Abraham (patriarch)]] · [[Kirtland Temple]] · [[D&C 110]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1836** — **Vision of the Celestial Kingdom, Kirtland Temple** — Traditionally dated to January 21, 1836, in the Kirtland Temple, shortly before the temple's formal dedication later that same month. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Adam]] · [[Abraham]] · [[Alvin Smith]] · [[Kirtland Temple]] · [[D&C 137]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1838–AD 1839** — **Winter in Liberty Jail** — a prison-temple: peace be unto thy soul — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Hyrum Smith]] · [[Liberty]] · [[D&C 121]] · [[D&C 122]] · [[D&C 123]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1839–AD 1846** — **Nauvoo the beautiful** — a city and temple from a swamp — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Brigham Young]] · [[Emma Smith]] · [[Nauvoo]] · [[D&C 124]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1843** — **Joseph Smith's instructions on marriage, prophecy, and spirit (1843)** — The internal date notation '(May 17th, 1843.)' in D&C 131:5 and the standard published section heading placing these instructions in 1843. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Ramus, Illinois]] · [[D&C 131]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1844** — **The martyrdom at Carthage** — sealed with blood — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Hyrum Smith]] · [[John Taylor]] · [[Carthage]] · [[D&C 135]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1846–AD 1847** — **The pioneer exodus west** — another Israel crosses another wilderness — [[Brigham Young]] · [[Winter Quarters]] · [[Salt Lake Valley]] · [[D&C 136]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1847** — **Camp of Israel organized at Winter Quarters for westward trek** — The revelation is dated January 14, 1847 in its section heading, given at Winter Quarters (present-day Nebraska) to organize pioneer companies for the journey to the Rocky Mountains following the 1846 expulsion from Nauvoo. — [[Ezra T. - **AD 1861–AD 1865** — **American Civil War, proposed fulfillment** — Commonly cited by Latter-day Saints as the war's onset since fighting began at Fort Sumter, South Carolina, though the chapter's 'all nations' language lacks an agreed discrete fulfillment. — [[South Carolina]] · [[D&C 87]] *(historical)*
