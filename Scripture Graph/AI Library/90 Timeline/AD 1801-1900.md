# AD 1801-1900 — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## The Restoration
- **AD 1820** — **The First Vision** — a boy's spring-morning prayer opens the dispensation — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sacred Grove]] · [[Palmyra]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1823** — **Moroni appears; the plates shown** — the buried record's author returns for it — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Moroni (son of Mormon)]] · [[Cumorah]] · [[Palmyra]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1827** — **Joseph receives the plates** — four years of schooling first — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Emma Smith]] · [[Cumorah]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1829** — **Priesthood restored by John the Baptist** — authority returns hand to head — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[John the Baptist]] · [[Susquehanna River]] · [[D&C 13]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1829** — **The translation by gift and power** — most of the book in about sixty-five working days — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Harmony]] · [[Fayette]] · [[D&C 3]] · [[D&C 10]] · [[Joseph Smith—History 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1829** — **The Three and the Eight see the plates** — an angel turns the leaves; eleven names sign forever — [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[David Whitmer]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Fayette]] · [[D&C 17]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **The Book of Mormon published; the Church organized** — Moroni's record speaks from the dust, 1,400 years on — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Palmyra]] · [[Fayette]] · [[D&C 20]] · [[D&C 21]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1830** — **Organization of the Church of Christ, April 6, 1830** — D&C 20:1 gives the explicit date: fourth month, sixth day, "one thousand eight hundred and thirty years" since Christ's coming. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[D&C 20]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1831** — **June 1831 conference and dispatch of elders to Missouri** — Traditional dating of D&C 52 to June 7, 1831, at the close of a conference in Ohio; the text itself refers forward to 'the next conference, which shall be held in Missouri' (v.2). — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Sidney Rigdon]] · [[Edward Partridge]] · [[Martin Harris]] · [[Missouri]] · [[Ohio]] · [[D&C 52]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1832–AD 1833** — **D&C 88 ('Olive Leaf') received at Kirtland, Ohio** — Per the section heading, verses 1-126 were received December 27-28, 1832, and verses 127-141 were added January 3, 1833, both at Kirtland, Ohio, during meetings connected to the School of the Prophets. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Kirtland, Ohio]] · [[D&C 88]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1833** — **D&C 98 given at Kirtland amid Missouri persecutions** — Section heading identifies the revelation as received at Kirtland, Ohio, August 6, 1833, during the period of escalating mob violence against Latter-day Saints in Jackson County, Missouri. — [[Joseph Smith]] · [[Kirtland]] · [[D&C 98]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1834** — **Fishing River Revelation defers Zion's redemption** — Traditionally dated June 22, 1834, near Fishing River, Clay County, Missouri, after Zion's Camp leaders determined not to force a crossing to redeem Jackson County following negotiations and rising tension with Missouri settlers. — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Fishing River, Missouri]] · [[D&C 105]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1836** — **The Kirtland Temple; keys restored** — Moses, Elias, and Elijah — Malachi's promise kept — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Oliver Cowdery]] · [[Elijah]] · [[Moses]] · [[Kirtland]] · [[D&C 110]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1836** — **Kirtland Temple dedication-week vision (D&C 110)** — Traditionally dated April 3, 1836, about a week after the Kirtland Temple's March 27, 1836 dedication, per Church historical records; the date is not stated within the verses of D&C 110 itself. — [[Jesus Christ]] · [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Elijah]] · [[Abraham (patriarch)]] · [[Kirtland Temple]] · [[D&C 110]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1838–AD 1839** — **Winter in Liberty Jail** — a prison-temple: peace be unto thy soul — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Hyrum Smith]] · [[Liberty]] · [[D&C 121]] · [[D&C 122]] · [[D&C 123]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1839–AD 1846** — **Nauvoo the beautiful** — a city and temple from a swamp — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Brigham Young]] · [[Emma Smith]] · [[Nauvoo]] · [[D&C 124]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1844** — **The martyrdom at Carthage** — sealed with blood — [[Joseph Smith Jr]] · [[Hyrum Smith]] · [[John Taylor]] · [[Carthage]] · [[D&C 135]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1846–AD 1847** — **The pioneer exodus west** — another Israel crosses another wilderness — [[Brigham Young]] · [[Winter Quarters]] · [[Salt Lake Valley]] · [[D&C 136]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1890** — **The Manifesto — Official Declaration 1** — a course change by revelation — [[Wilford Woodruff]] · [[Official Declaration 1]] *(historical)*
- **AD 1893** — **The Salt Lake Temple dedicated** — forty years of granite — [[Wilford Woodruff]] · [[Salt Lake City]] *(historical)*
