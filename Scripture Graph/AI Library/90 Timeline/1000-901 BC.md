# 1000-901 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **1000 BC** — **David brings the Ark of the Covenant to Jerusalem** — Conventional United Monarchy chronology places David's reign c. 1010-970 BC; this event follows shortly after his capture of Jerusalem in 2 Samuel 5. — [[David]] · [[Michal]] · [[Uzzah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Samuel 6]] *(traditional dating)*
- **975 BC–970 BC** — **David's Census, Plague, and Purchase of Ornan's Threshingfloor** — Placed near the close of David's reign in the biblical narrative sequence, shortly before his preparations for Solomon's temple (1 Chronicles 22); traditional regnal dating for David is approximately 1010-970 BC, but no fixed year is given in the text itself. — [[David (king)]] · [[Joab]] · [[Gad]] · [[Ornan the Jebusite]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 21]] *(traditional dating)*
- **972 BC–970 BC** — **David's material preparation and charge to Solomon for the temple** — Traditional biblical chronology places this charge near the end of David's roughly forty-year reign (c. 1010-970 BC), shortly before his death and before Solomon begins construction (1 Kings 6:1). — [[David (king)]] · [[Solomon]] · [[Moses (prophet)]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 22]] *(traditional dating)*
- **972 BC–970 BC** — **Solomon anointed co-regent king at Gihon** — Traditional biblical chronology places David's reign c. 1010-970 BCE; the events of this chapter occur near the end of David's life, shortly before his death. — [[David (king)]] · [[Solomon]] · [[Zadok]] · [[Nathan]] · [[Gihon]] · [[1 Kings 1]] *(traditional dating)*
- **972 BC–970 BC** — **Sheba's Revolt and Murder of Amasa** — Occurs immediately after Absalom's revolt near the end of David's traditionally dated reign (c. 1010-970 BCE); no internal date is given. — [[David]] · [[Joab]] · [[Sheba]] · [[Amasa]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Abel of Beth-maachah]] · [[2 Samuel 20]] *(approximate)*
- **971 BC** — **David commissions Solomon and delivers the temple pattern** — Placed near the end of David's reign, shortly before his death and Solomon's accession (cf. 1 Kings 2:10-12); David's reign is traditionally dated c. 1010-971 BC, so this assembly falls near its close. — [[David (king)]] · [[Solomon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 28]] *(traditional dating)*
- **971 BC–970 BC** — **Temple-fund offering and Solomon's public coronation** — Chapter narrates the freewill temple offering, David's final prayer and death, and Solomon's second, public anointing as one transitional episode at the close of David's reign. — [[David]] · [[Solomon]] · [[Zadok]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 29]] *(approximate)*
- **971 BC–931 BC** — **Traditional Solomonic authorship of Proverbs 10-22:16** — Proverbs 10:1 attributes this collection (including ch.17) to Solomon; traditional dating follows his reign. — [[Solomon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Proverbs 17]] *(traditional dating)*
- **970 BC–609 BC** — **Unbroken royal line, Solomon to Josiah** — 1 Chronicles 3:10-14 lists a single-son succession from Solomon through Josiah, spanning the united and divided monarchy period down to Josiah's death. — [[Solomon]] · [[Josiah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 3]] *(traditional dating)*
- **970 BC–960 BC** — **Solomon is publicly installed as king** — Approximate conventional dating near the end of David's reign; the chapter itself supplies no absolute year. — [[David]] · [[Solomon]] · [[Zadok]] · [[Nathan]] · [[Gihon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Kings 1]] *(approximate)*
- **970 BC–931 BC** — **Solomon's forty-year reign and death** — Conventional biblical chronology places Solomon's reign at roughly 970-931 BC based on the stated forty-year length (v. 42) correlated with regnal data elsewhere in Kings; the figure is a round number and precise absolute dating is disputed. — [[Solomon]] · [[Rehoboam]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Kings 11]] *(traditional dating)*
- **970 BC–966 BC** — **Solomon's early reign and the Gibeon dream** — Traditional biblical chronology places Solomon's accession c. 970 BCE, with 1 Kings 6:1 dating the start of temple construction to his fourth regnal year; the Gibeon dream is narrated as occurring before that construction begins. — [[Solomon]] · [[David]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Gibeon]] · [[1 Kings 3]] *(traditional dating)*
- **970 BC–967 BC** — **Solomon's accession and Gibeon theophany** — Traditional biblical chronology places the start of Solomon's reign at roughly 970 BCE, shortly after David's death; this episode occurs early in his reign per the chapter's own sequence. — [[Solomon]] · [[David]] · [[Moses]] · [[Gibeon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Chronicles 1]] *(traditional dating)*
- **969 BC–966 BC** — **Solomon-Hiram alliance and temple labor mobilization** — Traditional chronology places these preparations shortly before Solomon's fourth regnal year (1 Kings 6:1), commonly reckoned near 966 BCE, when temple construction formally began. — [[Solomon]] · [[Hiram (king of Tyre)]] · [[Adoniram]] · [[Tyre]] · [[Lebanon]] · [[1 Kings 5]] *(traditional dating)*
- **967 BC–947 BC** — **Solomon completes temple bronze/gold work and his own palace** — Traditional biblical chronology places Solomon's reign c. 970-931 BCE, with temple construction begun in his 4th year (1 Kings 6:1) and completed after seven years (1 Kings 6:38); this chapter's furnishings work and the subsequent thirteen-year palace project (1 Kings 7:1) would follow. — [[Solomon]] · [[Hiram (craftsman)]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Kings 7]] *(traditional dating)*
- **966 BC–959 BC** — **Solomon builds the first temple** — the glory fills the house — [[Solomon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Kings 6]] · [[1 Kings 8]] *(approximate)*
- **966 BC–959 BC** — **Construction of Solomon's Temple (foundation to completion)** — 1 Kings 6:1 dates the start to Solomon's 4th regnal year and 6:37-38 date the foundation to the 4th year (month Zif) and completion to the 11th year (month Bul), a seven-year span; absolute years follow conventional regnal chronology and are approximate. — [[Solomon]] · [[1 Kings 6]] *(traditional dating)*
- **966 BC–950 BC** — **Solomon completes the temple and palace (20th year)** — 1 Kings 6:1 dates the start of temple construction to Solomon's 4th regnal year; 1 Kings 9:10 states both the temple and palace were finished 'at the end of twenty years,' i.e. Solomon's 20th year, using traditional dates for his reign (c. 970-931 BCE). — [[Solomon]] · [[Hiram]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Tyre]] · [[1 Kings 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **959 BC** — **Dedication of Solomon's Temple and installation of the Ark** — Traditionally placed after temple construction was completed in Solomon's 11th regnal year (cf. 1 Kings 6:38), with the ark procession occurring at the feast of the seventh month. — [[Solomon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Chronicles 5]] *(traditional dating)*
- **950 BC–935 BC** — **Queen of Sheba's state visit to Solomon in Jerusalem** — Placed within Solomon's traditionally dated reign (c. 970-931 BC), narratively after the temple and palace building account of 1 Kings 6-9 — [[Solomon]] · [[Queen of Sheba]] · [[Hiram]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Sheba]] · [[1 Kings 10]] *(traditional dating)*
- **931 BC** — **The kingdom divides — Israel and Judah** — ten tribes north, two south — [[Rehoboam]] · [[Jeroboam]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Samaria]] · [[1 Kings 12]] *(historical)*
- **926 BC–925 BC** — **Shishak (Shoshenq I) invades Judah** — Correlated with 'the fifth year of king Rehoboam' (2 Chronicles 12:2); Shoshenq I's Canaan campaign is independently dated via Egyptian regnal chronology and commemorated on the Bubastite Portal relief at Karnak, providing one of the few fixed synchronisms between biblical and Egyptian chronology. — [[Rehoboam]] · [[Shishak (Shoshenq I)]] · [[Shemaiah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Egypt]] · [[2 Chronicles 12]] *(historical)*
