# 1000-901 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **975 BC–970 BC** — **David's Census, Plague, and Purchase of Ornan's Threshingfloor** — Placed near the close of David's reign in the biblical narrative sequence, shortly before his preparations for Solomon's temple (1 Chronicles 22); traditional regnal dating for David is approximately 1010-970 BC, but no fixed year is given in the text itself. — [[David (king)]] · [[Joab]] · [[Gad]] · [[Ornan the Jebusite]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 21]] *(traditional dating)*
- **971 BC–970 BC** — **Temple-fund offering and Solomon's public coronation** — Chapter narrates the freewill temple offering, David's final prayer and death, and Solomon's second, public anointing as one transitional episode at the close of David's reign. — [[David]] · [[Solomon]] · [[Zadok]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 29]] *(approximate)*
- **971 BC–931 BC** — **Traditional Solomonic authorship of Proverbs 10-22:16** — Proverbs 10:1 attributes this collection (including ch.17) to Solomon; traditional dating follows his reign. — [[Solomon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Proverbs 17]] *(traditional dating)*
- **970 BC–931 BC** — **Solomon's forty-year reign and death** — Conventional biblical chronology places Solomon's reign at roughly 970-931 BC based on the stated forty-year length (v. 42) correlated with regnal data elsewhere in Kings; the figure is a round number and precise absolute dating is disputed. — [[Solomon]] · [[Rehoboam]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Kings 11]] *(traditional dating)*
- **970 BC–966 BC** — **Solomon's early reign and the Gibeon dream** — Traditional biblical chronology places Solomon's accession c. 970 BCE, with 1 Kings 6:1 dating the start of temple construction to his fourth regnal year; the Gibeon dream is narrated as occurring before that construction begins. — [[Solomon]] · [[David]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Gibeon]] · [[1 Kings 3]] *(traditional dating)*
- **966 BC–959 BC** — **Solomon builds the first temple** — the glory fills the house — [[Solomon]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Kings 6]] · [[1 Kings 8]] *(approximate)*
- **966 BC–959 BC** — **Construction of Solomon's Temple (foundation to completion)** — 1 Kings 6:1 dates the start to Solomon's 4th regnal year and 6:37-38 date the foundation to the 4th year (month Zif) and completion to the 11th year (month Bul), a seven-year span; absolute years follow conventional regnal chronology and are approximate. — [[Solomon]] · [[1 Kings 6]] *(traditional dating)*
- **931 BC** — **The kingdom divides — Israel and Judah** — ten tribes north, two south — [[Rehoboam]] · [[Jeroboam]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Samaria]] · [[1 Kings 12]] *(historical)*
- **926 BC–925 BC** — **Shishak (Shoshenq I) invades Judah** — Correlated with 'the fifth year of king Rehoboam' (2 Chronicles 12:2); Shoshenq I's Canaan campaign is independently dated via Egyptian regnal chronology and commemorated on the Bubastite Portal relief at Karnak, providing one of the few fixed synchronisms between biblical and Egyptian chronology. — [[Rehoboam]] · [[Shishak (Shoshenq I)]] · [[Shemaiah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Egypt]] · [[2 Chronicles 12]] *(historical)*
