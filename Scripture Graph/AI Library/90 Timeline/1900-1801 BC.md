# 1900-1801 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **1900 BC–1890 BC** — **Isaac's Marriage to Rebekah** — Traditional harmonized biblical chronology (e.g. Ussher-style) places this shortly after Sarah's death, near Isaac's marriage at about age 40 (cf. Genesis 25:20); absolute dates are not independently confirmed. — [[Abraham]] · [[Isaac]] · [[Rebekah]] · [[Laban]] · [[Mesopotamia (city of Nahor)]] · [[Beer-lahai-roi]] · [[Genesis 24]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1900 BC–1801 BC** — **Jacob blessed by Isaac and flees to Haran** — Placed within the same patriarchal-era window used for the surrounding Isaac/Jacob/Esau narratives (Genesis 25-27); the text gives no absolute date, so this reflects a traditional reconstruction, not an internally fixed chronology. — [[Jacob]] · [[Isaac]] · [[Esau]] · [[Beth-el]] · [[Haran]] · [[Genesis 28]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1900 BC–1850 BC** — **Jacob adopts and blesses Ephraim and Manasseh in Egypt** — Placed near the end of Jacob's life in Egypt, shortly before his death (Genesis 49), within the traditional patriarchal chronology derived from Genesis genealogies. — [[Jacob (Israel)]] · [[Joseph (of Egypt)]] · [[Ephraim]] · [[Manasseh]] · [[Egypt]] · [[Genesis 48]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1898 BC** — **Covenant renewed, circumcision instituted at Abraham's age 99** — Text states Abraham was ninety-nine years old (Genesis 17:1, 17:24); year computed only under traditional biblical chronology reckonings, not independently datable. — [[Abraham (patriarch)]] · [[Sarah]] · [[Ishmael]] · [[Canaan]] · [[Genesis 17]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1880 BC–1040 BC** — **Judah's royal line: Pharez to David's birth** — Counts the generations named in 1 Chronicles 2:5,9-15 from Judah's grandson Pharez down to David, matched against the traditional dating of the patriarchal era and David's birth. — [[Judah]] · [[Boaz]] · [[Jesse]] · [[David]] · [[Bethlehem]] · [[1 Chronicles 2]] *(approximate)*
- **1870 BC** — **The binding of Isaac on Moriah** — the similitude offering — [[Abraham]] · [[Isaac]] · [[Moriah]] · [[Genesis 22]] *(approximate)*
- **1835 BC–1810 BC** — **Esau sells his birthright to Jacob** — The text places this 'when the boys grew' (25:27) but gives no specific age, so the timing relative to the twins' birth is broadly estimated rather than fixed. — [[Jacob]] · [[Esau]] · [[Genesis 25]] *(approximate)*
- **1835 BC–1825 BC** — **Death of Abraham at age 175** — Patriarchal narratives are traditionally placed in the Middle Bronze Age (~2000-1800 BC) based on internal genealogical ages (Genesis 25:7); no independent extrabiblical source confirms an exact date. — [[Abraham]] · [[Isaac]] · [[Ishmael]] · [[Cave of Machpelah]] · [[Genesis 25]] *(approximate)*
