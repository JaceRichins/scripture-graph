# 900-801 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **870 BC–848 BC** — **Jehoshaphat's reign and the En-gedi deliverance** — Derived from the regnal note in 2 Chronicles 20:31 (25-year reign, began at age 35) combined with conventional synchronistic chronology of the divided monarchy; absolute dates are disputed among chronologers and should be treated as approximate. — [[Jehoshaphat]] · [[Jahaziel]] · [[Ahaziah]] · [[Judah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Chronicles 20]] *(approximate)*
- **860 BC** — **Elijah on Carmel; fire answers** — then the still small voice at Horeb — [[Elijah]] · [[Ahab]] · [[Jezebel]] · [[Mount Carmel]] · [[1 Kings 18]] · [[1 Kings 19]] *(approximate)*
- **850 BC–800 BC** — **Elisha's ministry of miracles** — they that be with us are more — [[Elisha]] · [[2 Kings 2]] · [[2 Kings 5]] · [[2 Kings 6]] *(approximate)*
- **841 BC–814 BC** — **Reign of Jehu over Israel** — Twenty-eight-year reign given in 2 Kings 10:36; conventionally anchored by Jehu's tribute to Shalmaneser III depicted on the Black Obelisk, dated to the Assyrian king's 18th year (ca. 841 BC). — [[Jehu]] · [[Ahab]] · [[Jehonadab]] · [[Hazael]] · [[Samaria]] · [[Jezreel]] · [[2 Kings 10]] *(historical)*
- **835 BC–796 BC** — **Reign of Joash of Judah and temple repair** — Conventional biblical chronology (e.g., Thiele) places Joash's 40-year reign c. 835-796 BC; regnal synchronisms for this period are reconstructed from limited data and remain debated among scholars. — [[Joash]] · [[Jehoiada]] · [[Zechariah son of Jehoiada]] · [[Amaziah]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[2 Chronicles 24]] *(traditional dating)*
