## In the Old World
- **2350 BC** — **Noah and the Flood** — the earth cleansed — [[Noah]] · [[Genesis 7]] · [[Genesis 8]] · [[Moses 8]] *(traditional dating)*
- **2348 BC** — **The Genesis Flood (traditional dating)** — Derived from later chronological reckonings (e.g., Ussher) built on Masoretic genealogical data in Genesis 5 and 11; not an internally stated date and heavily disputed as history. — [[Noah]] · [[Shem]] · [[Ham]] · [[Japheth]] · [[Genesis 6]] *(traditional dating)*
- **2348 BC** — **Noahic covenant established after the Flood** — Traditional Ussher-style chronology places the Flood's end near 2348 BC; Genesis 9's covenant follows immediately upon the family's exit from the ark. Dating is a traditional construct, not stated numerically in the text itself. — [[Noah (patriarch)]] · [[Genesis 9]] *(traditional dating)*
