# 1100-1001 BC — what was happening

_All the world's scripture history in this century, side by side. Dating honesty: every entry says how its date is known._

## In the Old World
- **1100 BC–1050 BC** — **Days of Gibeah atrocity recalled in Hosea 9:9** — Judges 19-20 situates this civil war in the pre-monarchic judges period, conventionally dated to roughly the 12th-11th centuries BC. — [[Gibeah]] · [[Hosea 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1090 BC** — **Samuel called as a child** — speak, for thy servant heareth — [[Samuel]] · [[Eli]] · [[Shiloh]] · [[1 Samuel 3]] *(approximate)*
- **1050 BC–1010 BC** — **Reign of Saul and genealogy through Gibeon** — The repeated Saul genealogy (9:35-44, paralleling 8:29-38) sets up the narrative of Saul's reign and death beginning in 1 Chronicles 10. — [[Saul (king)]] · [[Jonathan (son of Saul)]] · [[1 Chronicles 9]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1050 BC–1020 BC** — **Samuel's Farewell Address at Saul's Coronation** — Traditional chronologies place Saul's accession and this address c. 1050-1020 BC; precise dating is uncertain because the regnal-year notice in 1 Samuel 13:1 is textually corrupt in the Masoretic Text. — [[Samuel]] · [[Saul]] · [[1 Samuel 12]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1050 BC–1030 BC** — **Israel's Demand for a King (transition from judges to monarchy)** — Conventional biblical chronology places Samuel's late judgeship and the elders' request for a king shortly before Saul's anointing, in the decades before Israel's first monarchy begins. — [[Samuel]] · [[Ramah]] · [[Beer-sheba]] · [[1 Samuel 8]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1050 BC** — **Saul anointed — Israel takes a king** — like all the nations — [[Saul]] · [[Samuel]] · [[1 Samuel 8]] · [[1 Samuel 10]] *(approximate)*
- **1030 BC–1010 BC** — **Saul's reign and the Michmash campaign against the Philistines** — Traditional approximate regnal dates for King Saul; the Michmash campaign of 1 Samuel 13-14 is narrated as occurring during his reign, prior to his rejection and David's rise. — [[Saul]] · [[Jonathan]] · [[Michmash]] · [[Gibeah]] · [[1 Samuel 14]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1025 BC** — **David and Goliath** — the battle is the LORD's — [[David]] · [[Goliath]] · [[Saul]] · [[Valley of Elah]] · [[1 Samuel 17]] *(approximate)*
- **1010 BC–970 BC** — **David reigns; Jerusalem the capital** — shepherd, psalmist, king — glory and wreckage — [[David]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Hebron]] · [[2 Samuel 5]] · [[Psalm 23]] *(approximate)*
- **1010 BC–970 BC** — **David's reign over all Israel (Hebron and Jerusalem)** — 1 Chronicles 29:27 gives 40 total years (7 in Hebron, 33 in Jerusalem); conventional biblical chronology (e.g. LDS Bible Dictionary) dates David's reign c. 1010-970 BC. — [[David]] · [[Hebron]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 29]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1010 BC–970 BC** — **David's forty-year reign, Hebron then Jerusalem** — 1 Chronicles 3:4 gives seven years six months in Hebron plus thirty-three years in Jerusalem, totaling forty years, matching the reign length given in 2 Samuel 5:4-5 and 1 Kings 2:11. — [[David (king)]] · [[Hebron]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[1 Chronicles 3]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1010 BC–1003 BC** — **David's reign as king of Judah at Hebron** — Conventional biblical chronology reckoning back from later regnal totals; disputed among scholars and not internally reconciled with Ish-bosheth's two-year reign noted in this same chapter. — [[David]] · [[Hebron]] · [[2 Samuel 2]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1010 BC–1002 BC** — **David's reign over Judah in Hebron, before uniting the kingdom** — 2 Samuel 5:5 states David reigned seven years and six months in Hebron over Judah before reigning over all Israel; 2 Samuel 3 falls within this period. Absolute dates follow conventional (not universally agreed) biblical chronology. — [[David]] · [[Abner]] · [[Joab]] · [[Hebron]] · [[2 Samuel 3]] *(traditional dating)*
- **1010 BC–970 BC** — **Traditional Davidic authorship of Psalm 69** — The psalm's canonical superscription and the LDS chapter heading attribute it to David; however vv35-36 presuppose Zion and Judah's cities need rebuilding, which some scholars take as evidence favoring a later, exilic/post-exilic composition instead. — [[David]] · [[Jerusalem]] · [[Psalm 69]] *(traditional dating)*
