# Psalms 49–86 Part 2 • Dr. John White • August 24-30 • Come, Follow Me

[[followHIM]] · 2026-08-20 · 49 min · source tier **A** · episode quality **75**
Guests: Dr. John White (Ethics and theology; dissertation on John Paul II; scholar of the Psalms and emb)

> Dr. John White, a recent BYU-convert scholar and ethicist formerly on faculty at Baylor, joins Hank Smith and John Bytheway to close their two-part discussion of Psalms 49–86, moving through Psalms 61–63 and 85–86 with attention to embodiment, ordered love, and Zion, interwoven with his own conversion story and the professional cost it carried.

## Timestamped outline

- **0:01** — Psalm 61: the rock higher than I: Dr. White frames Psalm 61 as divine refuge that transcends geography and circumstance, connecting it to Helaman 5:12 and sharing how a Mormon Tabernacle Choir arrangement of a related hymn drew him toward the Church. ([[Psalm 61]] · [[Helaman 5]])
- **4:16** — Psalm 62: 'only' God, six times: The hosts and guest note the sixfold repetition of 'only' in Psalm 62, reading it as a call to unshaken, repeatedly re-chosen trust in God alone rather than status or wealth. ([[Psalm 62]])
- **5:19** — Psalm 63: thirst, embodiment, and the argument from desire: Extended discussion of Psalm 63's bodily longing for God, including Hebrew imagery of thirst, C.S. Lewis's argument from desire, John Paul II's theology of the body, and a noted parallel to 2 Nephi 9:51's 'delight in fatness.' ([[Psalm 63]] · [[2 Nephi 9]] · [[Psalm 119]])
- **22:11** — Dr. White's conversion and its professional cost: Dr. White recounts his path to baptism (Sacred Grove, Moroni 10, the Grandin Press building), his decision to be baptized despite holding tenure at an institution reportedly barring LDS faculty, and the friction it caused with colleagues. ([[Moroni 10]] · [[Book of Mormon]])
- **31:43** — Psalm 85: mercy and truth kiss; Zion as place and cause: Discussion of Psalm 85's pairing of mercy/truth and righteousness/peace, linked to Alma 42's reconciliation of justice and mercy, Moses 7:62, and how the early Church's concept of Zion expanded from a physical place to a people and cause. ([[Psalm 85]] · [[Alma 42]] · [[Moses 7]] · [[Zion]])
- **39:35** — Psalm 86: unite my heart, consecration: Close reading of Psalm 86's structure and its plea to 'unite my heart,' connected to Neal A. Maxwell's teaching that consecration is 'the only surrender.' ([[Psalm 86]])
- **45:03** — Closing counsel: your view of God shapes your view of self: Dr. White closes with a caution against 'moralistic therapeutic deism' and an invitation to let the Psalms supply language for approaching a God who is emotionally present and involved. ([[Psalms]])
