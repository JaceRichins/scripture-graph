# What were the Mark Hofmann forgeries?

## Concise answer

<!-- SG:BEGIN concise-answer -->
Mark Hofmann's forgeries were modern documents, signatures, altered artifacts, currency, and invented provenances made to appear historically old. The most consequential Latter-day Saint examples were the purported Anthon transcript, the Martin Harris 'salamander letter,' the Joseph Smith III succession blessing, the Joseph Smith–Josiah Stowell letter, a Lucy Mack Smith letter, false witness letters, alleged original Book of Mormon manuscript pages, and a fabricated Grandin printing contract. His supposed McLellin Collection and damaging Oliver Cowdery history were different kinds of fraud: the first was a collection he sold but did not possess, and the second was an invented rumor. He also forged Americana and literary material, notably the Oath of a Freeman. Confession, court proceedings, and forensic evidence firmly establish the major items as modern frauds; the precise limits of his total output remain unsettled.
<!-- SG:END concise-answer -->

## Strongest supporting points

<!-- SG:BEGIN strongest-evidence -->
- **Itemized contemporary identification.** The 1987 notice *Fraudulent Documents from Forger Mark Hofmann Noted* identifies the core Latter-day Saint-related frauds, including the Anthon transcript, salamander letter, Joseph Smith III blessing, Stowell letter, Lucy Mack Smith letter, purported Book of Mormon manuscript leaves, witness letters, Grandin contract, and forged Joseph Smith signature.

- **Converging admissions and judicial evidence.** Hofmann pleaded guilty to two theft-by-deception counts as well as two murder counts, and his 1987 interviews contain extensive admissions about his documents and schemes. A plea or confession alone cannot classify every uncharged item, but these admissions strongly support the core catalog.

- **Item-specific document forensics.** Examiners identified artificial ink aging and microscopic cracking or “alligatoring” in examined documents. These results strongly establish modern manufacture for the tested items; they should not be generalized automatically to every artifact Hofmann handled.

- **Corroborating provenance and transaction patterns.** Fabricated ownership histories, interlocking supporting documents, financial records, and the nonexistent McLellin collection independently reinforce the fraud reconstruction.

- **Mixed ideological direction.** The catalog contains both provocative documents and apparently faith-promoting witness or manuscript material. This is checkable evidence against treating the entire output as a single-direction theological campaign.
<!-- SG:END strongest-evidence -->

## Strongest objections

<!-- SG:BEGIN objections -->
- **Prophetic discernment:** Church leaders accepted or transacted for several documents and did not identify the deception spiritually. A critic can reasonably argue that this is contrary evidence against expansive popular claims that prophets will reliably detect consequential fraud.

- **Institutional self-interest:** Official Church accounts describe the Church principally as a victim. Because the institution also faced embarrassment and allegations of suppression, those accounts cannot be the only sources used to evaluate motives, access restrictions, negotiations, or public-relations strategy.

- **Authentication was not universally helpless:** Hofmann fooled many experts, but the Tanners raised early doubts. Inadequate provenance controls, reliance on handwriting comparison, and examining documents separately contributed to the failure; sophistication alone does not excuse every acquisition decision.

- **Forgery does not erase uncomfortable history:** The salamander and Stowell letters were false, but their falsity does not prove Joseph Smith had no involvement with treasure seeking, seer stones, or folk-religious culture. Those subjects have authentic independent evidence and must be evaluated separately through [[Was Joseph Smith a treasure digger]] and [[Why did Joseph Smith use a seer stone]].

- **The full corpus is not closed:** Not every Hofmann-handled item was false, not every suspected object was tested alike, and later discoveries have occasionally raised new attribution questions. A neat total can overstate present knowledge.
<!-- SG:END objections -->

## Responses

<!-- SG:BEGIN responses -->
A thoughtful believing response begins by conceding the documented failure: leaders, historians, collectors, and examiners were deceived, and stronger acquisition safeguards were warranted. Latter-day Saint doctrine need not be framed as guaranteeing that a prophet recognizes every liar or forged object; therefore the case does not logically disprove all revelation. It does, however, weigh against exaggerated claims of automatic or infallible discernment.

Likewise, evidence that prominent documents were publicly discussed before the bombings weakens the broad accusation that every troubling item was purchased for permanent concealment. It does not settle every narrower dispute about motives, access, or managed disclosure. Institutional victimhood and institutional accountability can both be true.

Finally, believers need not defend the forged texts. Their proper historical weight is zero. Faith claims about [[Joseph Smith Jr]], [[Translation of the Book of Mormon]], succession, and the witnesses must stand or fall on authentic sources rather than on documents that happened to favor either belief or criticism.
<!-- SG:END responses -->

## Frameworks — how this can fit, and how it cannot

<!-- SG:BEGIN frameworks -->
**What the canon requires:** Nothing in the canon identifies Mark Hofmann, guarantees the authenticity of archival discoveries, or supplies a verdict on the salamander letter. The question is historical and forensic. Objections and defenses based on a supposed scriptural authentication therefore attach to assumptions brought to the case, not to the text of scripture.

- **Independently supported — modern-forgery model:** The major public-interest Hofmann documents are twentieth-century fabrications. Confession, judicial admissions, physical examination, provenance failures, and inter-document patterns converge on this conclusion. This is the controlling historical model.

- **Independently supported — mixed-corpus model:** Hofmann also traded authentic objects and altered genuine old materials. Each item therefore requires its own provenance and testing; 'handled by Hofmann' is not a sufficient final classification.

- **Independently supported — historical-independence model:** A forged source has no value as a period witness, but propositions it mentions may still be true or false on independent evidence. Thus the forged Stowell and salamander letters neither establish nor erase authentic evidence concerning treasure seeking; the forged witness letters neither establish nor erase the genuine witness record; and the forged succession blessing does not decide the succession dispute.

- **Plausible — fallible-leadership model:** Church leaders can hold genuine religious authority without receiving revelation about every transaction. This model fits the observable failure and common Latter-day Saint understandings of human fallibility, though the Hofmann evidence cannot independently prove its theology.

- **Possible and critically significant — failed-charism model:** If prophetic calling is defined as including dependable supernatural detection of major threats, the Hofmann episode is substantial contrary evidence. Its force is real against that strong premise but weaker against more limited accounts of revelation.

- **Disputed but possible — managed-disclosure model:** The Church acquired materials partly to preserve them and partly to control the handling of sensitive information. Public discussion of major documents before the bombings argues against total concealment, while private negotiations and archival restrictions leave narrower institutional questions open.

- **Ad hoc — genuine-documents conspiracy model:** One could claim that the major documents were authentic and that confession, forensic findings, and court proceedings formed a coordinated suppression effort. This requires dismissing multiple independent evidentiary streams without positive counterevidence and is therefore set aside.

- **Ad hoc — universal-exoneration model:** One could argue that because experts were fooled, every institutional decision was beyond criticism. That does not follow: early skepticism existed, acquisition controls can be evaluated, and successful deception does not make all judgment equally prudent.

Also set aside are lexical false matches: the Gospel of Mark evidence notes and the paintings of Heinrich Hofmann concern different subjects. The genuine McLellin papers must likewise not be conflated with Hofmann's nonexistent professed collection.
<!-- SG:END frameworks -->

## Honest assessment

<!-- SG:BEGIN assessment -->
**Established:** Hofmann created numerous Mormon, American-historical, and literary forgeries; the major Latter-day Saint documents attributed to him are modern frauds; his McLellin sale and Oliver Cowdery-history rumor concerned nonexistent materials; and his escalating fraud culminated in the murders of Steven Christensen and Kathleen Sheets. The false documents cannot responsibly be cited as evidence about early Church history.

**Open:** The complete number and location of Hofmann's productions, the status of every item that passed through his network, and some details of individual and institutional motives remain uncertain. Claims about suppression should distinguish publication, temporary restriction, acquisition motive, and permanent concealment rather than collapsing them into one accusation.

**Matter of interpretation or faith:** The episode plainly demonstrates human and institutional fallibility. Whether it disproves prophetic calling depends on what revelation and discernment are claimed to guarantee. Neither the forgeries nor their detection adjudicate the truth of the Restoration as a whole; they do require believers and critics alike to abandon arguments built on the forged documents.
<!-- SG:END assessment -->
