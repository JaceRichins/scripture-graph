# Love Your Enemies

**Speaker:** President Dallin H. Oaks · **Conference:** October 2020
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/general-conference/2020/10/17oaks?lang=eng

> Love Your Enemies  By President Dallin H. Oaks  First Counselor in the First Presidency  Knowing that we are all children of God gives us a vision of the worth of others and the ability to rise above prejudice.  The Lord’s teachings are for eternity and for all of God’s children. In this message I will give some examples from the United States, but the principles I teach are applicable everywhere…
