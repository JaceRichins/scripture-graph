# Deep in Our Heart

**Speaker:** Douglas D. Holmes · **Conference:** April 2020
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/general-conference/2020/04/16holmes?lang=eng

> Deep in Our Heart  By Douglas D. Holmes  Recently Released First Counselor in the Young Men General Presidency  The Lord is trying to help us—all of us—get His gospel deeper in our hearts.  Sisters and brothers, what a wonderful time we live in. As we celebrate the beginning of the Restoration, it is also appropriate to celebrate the ongoing Restoration that we are witnessing.
