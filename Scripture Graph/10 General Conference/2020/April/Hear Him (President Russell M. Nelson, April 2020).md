**Speaker:** President Russell M. Nelson · **Conference:** April 2020
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/general-conference/2020/04/45nelson?lang=eng

> Hear Him  By President Russell M. Nelson  President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints  Our Father knows that when we are surrounded by uncertainty and fear, what will help us the very most is to hear His Son.
