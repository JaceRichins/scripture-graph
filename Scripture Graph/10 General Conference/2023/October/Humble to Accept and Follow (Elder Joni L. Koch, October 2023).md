# Humble to Accept and Follow

**Speaker:** Elder Joni L. Koch · **Conference:** October 2023
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/general-conference/2023/10/32koch?lang=eng

> Humble to Accept and Follow  By Elder Joni L. Koch  Of the Seventy  Humility is a mandatory requirement for us to be ready to return to the presence of God.  In the fifth chapter of Alma, an introspective question is posed: “Could ye say, if ye were called to die at this time, within yourselves, that ye have been sufficiently humble?” That question implies that humility is a mandatory requirement…
