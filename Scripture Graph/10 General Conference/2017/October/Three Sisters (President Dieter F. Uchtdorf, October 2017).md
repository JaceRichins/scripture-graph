# Three Sisters

**Speaker:** President Dieter F. Uchtdorf · **Conference:** October 2017
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/general-conference/2017/10/three-sisters?lang=eng

> Three Sisters  By President Dieter F. Uchtdorf  Second Counselor in the First Presidency  We are responsible for our own discipleship, and it has little—if anything—to do with the way others treat us.  Dear sisters, dear friends, to begin general conference with a worldwide sisters’ session is significant and wonderful. Just imagine: sisters of all ages, backgrounds, nationalities, and languages …
