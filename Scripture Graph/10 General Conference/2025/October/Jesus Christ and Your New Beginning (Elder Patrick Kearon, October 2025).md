**Speaker:** Elder Patrick Kearon · **Conference:** October 2025
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/general-conference/2025/10/31kearon?lang=eng

> Jesus Christ and Your New Beginning  By Elder Patrick Kearon  Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles  All of us can have a new beginning through, and because of, Jesus Christ. Even you.
