# Honoring His Name

**Speaker:** Lisa L. Harkness · **Conference:** October 2019
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/general-conference/2019/10/32harkness?lang=eng

> Honoring His Name  By Lisa L. Harkness  First Counselor in the Primary General Presidency  With covenant identity and belonging, we are called by the name of Jesus Christ.  As parents excitedly await the birth of a child, they have the responsibility of choosing a name for their new baby. Perhaps when you were born, you received a name that was passed down in your family for generations. Or maybe…
