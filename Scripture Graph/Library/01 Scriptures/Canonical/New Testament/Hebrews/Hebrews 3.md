# Hebrews 3

**1** Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus; ^heb-3-1

**2** Who was faithful to him that appointed him, as also Moses was faithful in all his house. ^heb-3-2

**3** For this man was counted worthy of more glory than Moses, inasmuch as he who hath builded the house hath more honour than the house. ^heb-3-3

**4** For every house is builded by some man; but he that built all things is God. ^heb-3-4

**5** And Moses verily was faithful in all his house, as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were to be spoken after; ^heb-3-5

**6** But Christ as a son over his own house; whose house are we, if we hold fast the confidence and the rejoicing of the hope firm unto the end. ^heb-3-6

**7** Wherefore (as the Holy Ghost saith, To day if ye will hear his voice, ^heb-3-7

**8** Harden not your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the wilderness: ^heb-3-8

**9** When your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my works forty years. ^heb-3-9

**10** Wherefore I was grieved with that generation, and said, They do alway err in their heart; and they have not known my ways. ^heb-3-10

**11** So I sware in my wrath, They shall not enter into my rest.) ^heb-3-11

**12** Take heed, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from the living God. ^heb-3-12

**13** But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin. ^heb-3-13

**14** For we are made partakers of Christ, if we hold the beginning of our confidence steadfast unto the end; ^heb-3-14

**15** While it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in the provocation. ^heb-3-15

**16** For some, when they had heard, did provoke: howbeit not all that came out of Egypt by Moses. ^heb-3-16

**17** But with whom was he grieved forty years? was it not with them that had sinned, whose carcases fell in the wilderness? ^heb-3-17

**18** And to whom sware he that they should not enter into his rest, but to them that believed not? ^heb-3-18

**19** So we see that they could not enter in because of unbelief. ^heb-3-19
