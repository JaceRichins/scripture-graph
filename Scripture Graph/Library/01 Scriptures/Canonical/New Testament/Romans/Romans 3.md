# Romans 3

**1** What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision? ^rom-3-1

**2** Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God. ^rom-3-2

**3** For what if some did not believe? shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect? ^rom-3-3

**4** God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged. ^rom-3-4

**5** But if our unrighteousness commend the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is God unrighteous who taketh vengeance? (I speak as a man) ^rom-3-5

**6** God forbid: for then how shall God judge the world? ^rom-3-6

**7** For if the truth of God hath more abounded through my lie unto his glory; why yet am I also judged as a sinner? ^rom-3-7

**8** And not rather, (as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say,) Let us do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just. ^rom-3-8

**9** What then? are we better than they? No, in no wise: for we have before proved both Jews and Gentiles, that they are all under sin; ^rom-3-9

**10** As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one: ^rom-3-10

**11** There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God. ^rom-3-11

**12** They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one. ^rom-3-12

**13** Their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips: ^rom-3-13

**14** Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness: ^rom-3-14

**15** Their feet are swift to shed blood: ^rom-3-15

**16** Destruction and misery are in their ways: ^rom-3-16

**17** And the way of peace have they not known: ^rom-3-17

**18** There is no fear of God before their eyes. ^rom-3-18

**19** Now we know that what things soever the law saith, it saith to them who are under the law: that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God. ^rom-3-19

**20** Therefore by the deeds of the law there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law is the knowledge of sin. ^rom-3-20

**21** But now the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets; ^rom-3-21

**22** Even the righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all them that believe: for there is no difference: ^rom-3-22

**23** For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; ^rom-3-23

**24** Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus: ^rom-3-24

**25** Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God; ^rom-3-25

**26** To declare, I say, at this time his righteousness: that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus. ^rom-3-26

**27** Where is boasting then? It is excluded. By what law? of works? Nay: but by the law of faith. ^rom-3-27

**28** Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law. ^rom-3-28

**29** Is he the God of the Jews only? is he not also of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also: ^rom-3-29

**30** Seeing it is one God, which shall justify the circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith. ^rom-3-30

**31** Do we then make void the law through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the law. ^rom-3-31
