# Philippians 2

**1** If there be therefore any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any bowels and mercies, ^philip-2-1

**2** Fulfil ye my joy, that ye be likeminded, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind. ^philip-2-2

**3** Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves. ^philip-2-3

**4** Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others. ^philip-2-4

**5** Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus: ^philip-2-5

**6** Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: ^philip-2-6

**7** But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: ^philip-2-7

**8** And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross. ^philip-2-8

**9** Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name: ^philip-2-9

**10** That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth; ^philip-2-10

**11** And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father. ^philip-2-11

**12** Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. ^philip-2-12

**13** For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure. ^philip-2-13

**14** Do all things without murmurings and disputings: ^philip-2-14

**15** That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world; ^philip-2-15

**16** Holding forth the word of life; that I may rejoice in the day of Christ, that I have not run in vain, neither laboured in vain. ^philip-2-16

**17** Yea, and if I be offered upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I joy, and rejoice with you all. ^philip-2-17

**18** For the same cause also do ye joy, and rejoice with me. ^philip-2-18

**19** But I trust in the Lord Jesus to send Timotheus shortly unto you, that I also may be of good comfort, when I know your state. ^philip-2-19

**20** For I have no man likeminded, who will naturally care for your state. ^philip-2-20

**21** For all seek their own, not the things which are Jesus Christ's. ^philip-2-21

**22** But ye know the proof of him, that, as a son with the father, he hath served with me in the gospel. ^philip-2-22

**23** Him therefore I hope to send presently, so soon as I shall see how it will go with me. ^philip-2-23

**24** But I trust in the Lord that I also myself shall come shortly. ^philip-2-24

**25** Yet I supposed it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother, and companion in labour, and fellowsoldier, but your messenger, and he that ministered to my wants. ^philip-2-25

**26** For he longed after you all, and was full of heaviness, because that ye had heard that he had been sick. ^philip-2-26

**27** For indeed he was sick nigh unto death: but God had mercy on him; and not on him only, but on me also, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow. ^philip-2-27

**28** I sent him therefore the more carefully, that, when ye see him again, ye may rejoice, and that I may be the less sorrowful. ^philip-2-28

**29** Receive him therefore in the Lord with all gladness; and hold such in reputation: ^philip-2-29

**30** Because for the work of Christ he was nigh unto death, not regarding his life, to supply your lack of service toward me. ^philip-2-30
