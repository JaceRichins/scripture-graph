# Psalm 122

**1** I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the LORD. ^ps-122-1

**2** Our feet shall stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem. ^ps-122-2

**3** Jerusalem is builded as a city that is compact together: ^ps-122-3

**4** Whither the tribes go up, the tribes of the LORD, unto the testimony of Israel, to give thanks unto the name of the LORD. ^ps-122-4

**5** For there are set thrones of judgment, the thrones of the house of David. ^ps-122-5

**6** Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee. ^ps-122-6

**7** Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces. ^ps-122-7

**8** For my brethren and companions' sakes, I will now say, Peace be within thee. ^ps-122-8

**9** Because of the house of the LORD our God I will seek thy good. ^ps-122-9
