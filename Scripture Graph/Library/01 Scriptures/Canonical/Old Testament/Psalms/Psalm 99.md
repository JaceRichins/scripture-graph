# Psalm 99

**1** The LORD reigneth; let the people tremble: he sitteth between the cherubims; let the earth be moved. ^ps-99-1

**2** The LORD is great in Zion; and he is high above all the people. ^ps-99-2

**3** Let them praise thy great and terrible name; for it is holy. ^ps-99-3

**4** The king's strength also loveth judgment; thou dost establish equity, thou executest judgment and righteousness in Jacob. ^ps-99-4

**5** Exalt ye the LORD our God, and worship at his footstool; for he is holy. ^ps-99-5

**6** Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among them that call upon his name; they called upon the LORD, and he answered them. ^ps-99-6

**7** He spake unto them in the cloudy pillar: they kept his testimonies, and the ordinance that he gave them. ^ps-99-7

**8** Thou answeredst them, O LORD our God: thou wast a God that forgavest them, though thou tookest vengeance of their inventions. ^ps-99-8

**9** Exalt the LORD our God, and worship at his holy hill; for the LORD our God is holy. ^ps-99-9
