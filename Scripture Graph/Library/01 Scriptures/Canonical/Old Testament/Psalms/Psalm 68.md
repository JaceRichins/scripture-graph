# Psalm 68

**1** Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered: let them also that hate him flee before him. ^ps-68-1

**2** As smoke is driven away, so drive them away: as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God. ^ps-68-2

**3** But let the righteous be glad; let them rejoice before God: yea, let them exceedingly rejoice. ^ps-68-3

**4** Sing unto God, sing praises to his name: extol him that rideth upon the heavens by his name JAH, and rejoice before him. ^ps-68-4

**5** A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is God in his holy habitation. ^ps-68-5

**6** God setteth the solitary in families: he bringeth out those which are bound with chains: but the rebellious dwell in a dry land. ^ps-68-6

**7** O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people, when thou didst march through the wilderness; Selah: ^ps-68-7

**8** The earth shook, the heavens also dropped at the presence of God: even Sinai itself was moved at the presence of God, the God of Israel. ^ps-68-8

**9** Thou, O God, didst send a plentiful rain, whereby thou didst confirm thine inheritance, when it was weary. ^ps-68-9

**10** Thy congregation hath dwelt therein: thou, O God, hast prepared of thy goodness for the poor. ^ps-68-10

**11** The Lord gave the word: great was the company of those that published it. ^ps-68-11

**12** Kings of armies did flee apace: and she that tarried at home divided the spoil. ^ps-68-12

**13** Though ye have lien among the pots, yet shall ye be as the wings of a dove covered with silver, and her feathers with yellow gold. ^ps-68-13

**14** When the Almighty scattered kings in it, it was white as snow in Salmon. ^ps-68-14

**15** The hill of God is as the hill of Bashan; an high hill as the hill of Bashan. ^ps-68-15

**16** Why leap ye, ye high hills? this is the hill which God desireth to dwell in; yea, the LORD will dwell in it for ever. ^ps-68-16

**17** The chariots of God are twenty thousand, even thousands of angels: the Lord is among them, as in Sinai, in the holy place. ^ps-68-17

**18** Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led captivity captive: thou hast received gifts for men; yea, for the rebellious also, that the LORD God might dwell among them. ^ps-68-18

**19** Blessed be the Lord, who daily loadeth us with benefits, even the God of our salvation. Selah. ^ps-68-19

**20** He that is our God is the God of salvation; and unto GOD the Lord belong the issues from death. ^ps-68-20

**21** But God shall wound the head of his enemies, and the hairy scalp of such an one as goeth on still in his trespasses. ^ps-68-21

**22** The Lord said, I will bring again from Bashan, I will bring my people again from the depths of the sea: ^ps-68-22

**23** That thy foot may be dipped in the blood of thine enemies, and the tongue of thy dogs in the same. ^ps-68-23

**24** They have seen thy goings, O God; even the goings of my God, my King, in the sanctuary. ^ps-68-24

**25** The singers went before, the players on instruments followed after; among them were the damsels playing with timbrels. ^ps-68-25

**26** Bless ye God in the congregations, even the Lord, from the fountain of Israel. ^ps-68-26

**27** There is little Benjamin with their ruler, the princes of Judah and their council, the princes of Zebulun, and the princes of Naphtali. ^ps-68-27

**28** Thy God hath commanded thy strength: strengthen, O God, that which thou hast wrought for us. ^ps-68-28

**29** Because of thy temple at Jerusalem shall kings bring presents unto thee. ^ps-68-29

**30** Rebuke the company of spearmen, the multitude of the bulls, with the calves of the people, till every one submit himself with pieces of silver: scatter thou the people that delight in war. ^ps-68-30

**31** Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands unto God. ^ps-68-31

**32** Sing unto God, ye kingdoms of the earth; O sing praises unto the Lord; Selah: ^ps-68-32

**33** To him that rideth upon the heavens of heavens, which were of old; lo, he doth send out his voice, and that a mighty voice. ^ps-68-33

**34** Ascribe ye strength unto God: his excellency is over Israel, and his strength is in the clouds. ^ps-68-34

**35** O God, thou art terrible out of thy holy places: the God of Israel is he that giveth strength and power unto his people. Blessed be God. ^ps-68-35
