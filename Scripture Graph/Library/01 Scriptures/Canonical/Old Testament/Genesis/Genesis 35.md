# Genesis 35

**1** And God said unto Jacob, Arise, go up to Beth-el, and dwell there: and make there an altar unto God, that appeared unto thee when thou fleddest from the face of Esau thy brother. ^gen-35-1

**2** Then Jacob said unto his household, and to all that were with him, Put away the strange gods that are among you, and be clean, and change your garments: ^gen-35-2

**3** And let us arise, and go up to Beth-el; and I will make there an altar unto God, who answered me in the day of my distress, and was with me in the way which I went. ^gen-35-3

**4** And they gave unto Jacob all the strange gods which were in their hand, and all their earrings which were in their ears; and Jacob hid them under the oak which was by Shechem. ^gen-35-4

**5** And they journeyed: and the terror of God was upon the cities that were round about them, and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob. ^gen-35-5

**6** So Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan, that is, Beth-el, he and all the people that were with him. ^gen-35-6

**7** And he built there an altar, and called the place El-beth-el: because there God appeared unto him, when he fled from the face of his brother. ^gen-35-7

**8** But Deborah Rebekah's nurse died, and she was buried beneath Beth-el under an oak: and the name of it was called Allon-bachuth. ^gen-35-8

**9** And God appeared unto Jacob again, when he came out of Padan-aram, and blessed him. ^gen-35-9

**10** And God said unto him, Thy name is Jacob: thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name: and he called his name Israel. ^gen-35-10

**11** And God said unto him, I am God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins; ^gen-35-11

**12** And the land which I gave Abraham and Isaac, to thee I will give it, and to thy seed after thee will I give the land. ^gen-35-12

**13** And God went up from him in the place where he talked with him. ^gen-35-13

**14** And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where he talked with him, even a pillar of stone: and he poured a drink offering thereon, and he poured oil thereon. ^gen-35-14

**15** And Jacob called the name of the place where God spake with him, Beth-el. ^gen-35-15

**16** And they journeyed from Beth-el; and there was but a little way to come to Ephrath: and Rachel travailed, and she had hard labour. ^gen-35-16

**17** And it came to pass, when she was in hard labour, that the midwife said unto her, Fear not; thou shalt have this son also. ^gen-35-17

**18** And it came to pass, as her soul was in departing, (for she died) that she called his name Ben-oni: but his father called him Benjamin. ^gen-35-18

**19** And Rachel died, and was buried in the way to Ephrath, which is Beth-lehem. ^gen-35-19

**20** And Jacob set a pillar upon her grave: that is the pillar of Rachel's grave unto this day. ^gen-35-20

**21** And Israel journeyed, and spread his tent beyond the tower of Edar. ^gen-35-21

**22** And it came to pass, when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father's concubine: and Israel heard it. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve: ^gen-35-22

**23** The sons of Leah; Reuben, Jacob's firstborn, and Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Zebulun: ^gen-35-23

**24** The sons of Rachel; Joseph, and Benjamin: ^gen-35-24

**25** And the sons of Bilhah, Rachel's handmaid; Dan, and Naphtali: ^gen-35-25

**26** And the sons of Zilpah, Leah's handmaid; Gad, and Asher: these are the sons of Jacob, which were born to him in Padan-aram. ^gen-35-26

**27** And Jacob came unto Isaac his father unto Mamre, unto the city of Arbah, which is Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac sojourned. ^gen-35-27

**28** And the days of Isaac were an hundred and fourscore years. ^gen-35-28

**29** And Isaac gave up the ghost, and died, and was gathered unto his people, being old and full of days: and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him. ^gen-35-29
