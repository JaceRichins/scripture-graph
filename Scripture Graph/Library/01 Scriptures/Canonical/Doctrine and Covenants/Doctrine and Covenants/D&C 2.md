# D&C 2

**1** Behold, I will reveal unto you the Priesthood, by the hand of Elijah the prophet, before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord. ^dc-2-1

**2** And he shall plant in the hearts of the children the promises made to the fathers, and the hearts of the children shall turn to their fathers. ^dc-2-2

**3** If it were not so, the whole earth would be utterly wasted at his coming. ^dc-2-3
