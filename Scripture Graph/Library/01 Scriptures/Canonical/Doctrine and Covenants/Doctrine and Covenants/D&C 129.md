# D&C 129

**1** There are two kinds of beings in heaven, namely: Angels, who are resurrected personages, having bodies of flesh and bones— ^dc-129-1

**2** For instance, Jesus said: Handle me and see, for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me have. ^dc-129-2

**3** Secondly: the spirits of just men made perfect, they who are not resurrected, but inherit the same glory. ^dc-129-3

**4** When a messenger comes saying he has a message from God, offer him your hand and request him to shake hands with you. ^dc-129-4

**5** If he be an angel he will do so, and you will feel his hand. ^dc-129-5

**6** If he be the spirit of a just man made perfect he will come in his glory; for that is the only way he can appear— ^dc-129-6

**7** Ask him to shake hands with you, but he will not move, because it is contrary to the order of heaven for a just man to deceive; but he will still deliver his message. ^dc-129-7

**8** If it be the devil as an angel of light, when you ask him to shake hands he will offer you his hand, and you will not feel anything; you may therefore detect him. ^dc-129-8

**9** These are three grand keys whereby you may know whether any administration is from God. ^dc-129-9
