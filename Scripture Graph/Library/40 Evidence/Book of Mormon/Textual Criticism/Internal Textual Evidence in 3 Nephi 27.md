# Internal Textual Evidence in 3 Nephi 27

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The explicit definition in verses 13–21 is strong internal textual evidence that "my gospel" here includes both Christ's saving mission and a commanded human response, rather than functioning merely as an institutional label.

**Does not establish:** It does not establish that this passage exhaustively defines every doctrine or every scriptural use of the term gospel.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The passage may give an occasion-specific summary rather than an exhaustive definition of every use of the word gospel.
- Some readers may distinguish the gospel proper in verses 13–15 from the commanded response and consequences in verses 16–21.
