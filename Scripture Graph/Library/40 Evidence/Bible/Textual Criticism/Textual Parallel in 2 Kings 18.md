# Textual Parallel in 2 Kings 18

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The account of Sennacherib's delegation and Rab-shakeh's speech has an extensive textual parallel in Isaiah 36. This strongly supports a literary relationship or shared textual tradition between the passages.

**Does not establish:** The parallel by itself does not establish which version is earlier, whether either account is independent corroboration, or the historical accuracy of every reported speech.

**Alternative explanations:**
- Isaiah may depend directly or indirectly on Kings.
- Kings may depend directly or indirectly on an Isaianic account.
- Both passages may draw on a shared written or oral source.
- Later scribal transmission may have increased their verbal agreement.
