# External Historical Attestation in 2 Kings 24

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
A Babylonian chronicle independently reports that Nebuchadnezzar campaigned against the city of Judah, captured its king, appointed a king of his own choice, and received substantial tribute. This strongly corroborates the political core of the siege, Jehoiachin's removal, and Zedekiah's appointment in 2 Kings 24.

**Does not establish:** This corroboration does not establish divine causation, prophetic inspiration, the precise deportation totals, or the historical accuracy of every detail in the chapter.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The biblical author or a later editor could have drawn on court records or widely remembered political events.
- The Babylonian chronicle is itself a royal record with selective and potentially propagandistic presentation.
- Agreement on the campaign's political outline does not independently confirm every biblical detail or number.
