# Internal Literary Evidence in Exodus 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The repeated identification of God with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, together with the renewed promise of a land, is strong internal textual evidence that the chapter frames Israel's deliverance as a continuation of God's relationship with the ancestors.

**Does not establish:** This literary continuity does not by itself establish the episode's historicity, date of composition, or precise process of textual formation.

**Alternative explanations:**
- The ancestral references may also function as conventional narrative legitimation of Moses's authority.
- The framing could reflect literary or editorial shaping rather than preserving the wording of a single historical encounter.
