# Internal Numerical Consistency in Numbers 3

## Summary
<!-- SG:BEGIN summary -->
The redemption figures are arithmetically coordinated: 22,273 firstborn minus the stated aggregate of 22,000 Levites equals 273, and 273 at five shekels each equals the stated 1,365 shekels. This strongly supports deliberate numerical coordination in the final form of this calculation, but provides only weak support for the historical accuracy of the underlying census.

**Does not establish:** The calculation does not establish the census’s historicity, its date, its authorship, or the absence of textual development.

**Alternative explanations:**
- An author or editor could have constructed or coordinated the figures for literary and administrative coherence.
- The final redemption figures could be internally consistent even if the census numbers are schematic rather than historical.
- Later transmission or editing could have preserved the redemption equation while leaving the separate 300-person clan-total discrepancy.
