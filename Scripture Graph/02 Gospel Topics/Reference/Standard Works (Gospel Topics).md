# Standard Works

*Gospel Topics* · [source](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/standard-works?lang=eng)

The standard works are the volumes of scripture officially accepted by the Church—the Holy Bible, Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, and Pearl of Great Price.
