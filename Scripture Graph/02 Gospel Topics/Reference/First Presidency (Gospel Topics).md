# First Presidency

*Gospel Topics* · [source](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/first-presidency?lang=eng)

The First Presidency is the highest governing body of the Church. It is composed of the President of the Church, who is always the most senior living Apostle, and his counselors.
